About Words

Words is a free English word finder with full pages for 1000 of the most used English words. Each page gives every common sense of the word with a definition and examples, its synonyms and opposites, broader and narrower words, phrases built on it and its word family; 1000 pages also give the meaning in Hindi. The site also has six word lists and two guides written by us.

Where the content comes from

  • Definitions, example sentences and the relations between words (synonyms, opposites, kinds, word family) on the word pages and in the word finder come from WordNet 3.0, a lexical database of English made at Princeton University and freely licensed for any use. We have selected, arranged and lightly formatted the entries, and left out senses that are unsuitable for a general audience. Reference: Christiane Fellbaum (editor), WordNet: An Electronic Lexical Database, MIT Press, 1998. The data was downloaded on 12 November 2026.
  • Which words have their own page was decided from the usage counts that come with WordNet: the most used words whose entries are full enough to be worth a page.
  • The Hindi meanings, the word lists (words instead of "very", confused words, one-word substitutions, prefixes and suffixes, collective nouns, synonyms and antonyms) and the guides are our own writing, not taken from WordNet or any dictionary.

Limits you should know

WordNet describes mainly American English and was compiled some years ago, so a few definitions and spellings differ from current Indian usage, and very new words are missing. Its example sentences are short and sometimes old-fashioned. The Hindi meanings give only the commonest senses and have not yet been checked by a second editor. For examinations, follow your prescribed dictionary and textbook. If you find a mistake, please tell us.
