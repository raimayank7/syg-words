How to use

  1. Type your name or a short line above.
  2. Look through the styles and tap Copy on the one you like.
  3. Paste it where you want it, and check how it looks on the screen where people will see it.

These are special characters, not fonts: read how fancy text works and where not to use it first. For ideas for the name itself, try our username ideas and nickname ideas.

Questions

Will it work everywhere?

No. These are special characters. Most phones and computers show them, but some apps, games and forms change or reject them. Always test the result on the real screen.

Does it work for Hindi or other scripts?

No. Only the Latin letters A to Z and digits are styled. Other scripts stay as typed.

Is my text saved?

No. It is converted in your browser and not sent to us.
