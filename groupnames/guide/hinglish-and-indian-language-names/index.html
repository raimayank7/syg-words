Group names in Hinglish, Hindi and Punjabi

Many Indian groups are named in Hindi, Punjabi or another home language, and the writing is often English letters, called Hinglish for Hindi. This is not careless: it works on every phone, needs no special keyboard, and everyone in the group can read it. This page explains how to choose between Hinglish and native script, with examples from the lists on this site.

Hinglish: the easy choice

Names such as "Ghar Ki Baat", "Yaaron Ki Mehfil" and "Chai Pe Charcha" use English letters for Hindi words. The spelling is up to you, so be consistent inside the group. Keep words simple: "Dosti" is clearer than a rare Sanskrit word. Capital letters at the start of each word make a name easier to read.

Native script: the personal choice

A name written in Devanagari (घर की बात) or Gurmukhi (ਘਰ ਦੀ ਗੱਲ) feels more personal and is easy for those who read it. The risks are small but real: an older phone may lack a font, the name may be harder to search, and members who read only English cannot read it. A common solution is to write the name in both: "Ghar Ki Baat | घर की बात".

Mixing languages

Indians mix languages in speech, and group names can too: "Weekend Warriors Ka Adda". A name that combines an English word with a Hindi one is often the most natural: "Last Benchers Ka Group". Keep the grammar clear so the name reads well aloud.

Things to check

  • Does the name mean the same to every region? A word that is friendly in Delhi may be rude somewhere else.
  • Is it spelled the way the group says it? "Yaar" and "Yaaron" are both common; pick one.
  • Does it avoid slang that will confuse older members?
  • For Punjabi names, check the Gurmukhi spelling with a Punjabi reader if the name will be printed on anything.

Examples to adapt

Family: "Sharma Parivar". Friends: "Dosti Ka Adda". Neighbours: "Pados Ki Baat". Cousins: "Nani Ki Fauj". Wedding: "Shaadi Ka Ghar". All of these are in the lists on this site. Change the family name, the place or the year to make them yours.

Questions

Is Hinglish acceptable for a formal group?

For a school or office notice group, use clear English or standard Hindi. Hinglish suits social groups.

Can I use both scripts in one name?

Yes. "Ghar Ki Baat | घर की बात" works on most phones, and the English part is a fallback.
