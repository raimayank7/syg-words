Choosing a name that lasts

The best group names say what the group is for, sound like its members and fit on a phone screen. This site gives you a start: pick a theme, read the names aloud, change a word, a year or a place to make one your own, and put it to the group. The six step guide explains the method, and the description and rules guide helps once the group exists.

How the generator works

For each theme we wrote a list of ready names and two lists of words, such as "Happy" and "Family". The generator picks a ready name half the time and a pair of words the other half, so you see new combinations and some of our favourites. It does not check whether anyone uses a name, because chat apps do not need names to be unique.

Questions

Do group names need to be unique?

No. WhatsApp and other chat apps let any number of groups share a name. Choose one that tells your members what the group is for.

Are these names free to use?

Yes. They are our own writing and are yours to use. Avoid brand and public-body names so that people are not misled.
