About college batch group names

A college batch group often lasts for decades, from the first week of orientation to the twenty-year reunion. Its name carries the batch, the course and often a joke about the hostel or the canteen.

How to choose

  • Start with the batch year and course: "BCom 2024", "MBA 2026".
  • Add the hostel, canteen or college festival name if there is one.
  • A reunion group should have a name that can still be used at forty, so avoid slang that will age.
  • One group per purpose: batch, hostel, project team. Do not use one chat for everything.

What to avoid

Avoid names that single out a branch, a professor or a person from the batch.

Build your own

Words that suit college batch groups: Final-year, Hostel, Campus, Canteen, Placement, Sleepy, Brave, Backbench. Words to join them with: Batch, Gang, Club, Committee, Survivors, Warriors, Alumni, Crew. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What is a good name for a college batch group?

Batch year plus course plus a touch of your college life, for example "BCom 2024 Forever" or "Hostel Block C".

Should a reunion group get a new name?

Often yes: a name such as "Class Of Twenty Six" works for decades.
