About cousins group names

Cousin groups are often the liveliest in a family: they span cities, ages and houses, and they plan every wedding and trip. A cousin group name says "we grew up together" and can carry a touch of mischief that the main family group does not.

How to choose

  • Use the family or the grandparents' name: "Dada Ji Ke Pote", "Nani Ke Nawabs".
  • Count the cousins if the number is fun: "The Twelve", "Chhe Cousins".
  • Mention the village or the old house where everyone met.
  • Keep it neutral if the group has both age groups, so that the youngest do not feel left out.

What to avoid

Avoid anything that ranks the cousins (eldest, richest, cleverest) or that makes one branch of the family feel apart.

Build your own

Words that suit cousins groups: Cousin, Naughty, Wedding, Summer, Royal, Noisy, Happy, Old-house. Words to join them with: Club, Gang, Brigade, Squad, Fauj, Express, Circle, Mela. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Questions

What can we name a cousins WhatsApp group?

Use a name that points to your shared roots, such as the grandparents or the old house: "Dadi Ke Doston", "Nani Ki Fauj".

Is it fine to use a cheeky name?

Yes, if all members enjoy it. Remember that an older relative may be added later.
