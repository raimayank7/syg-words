About cricket and sports teams group names

A team group handles practice timings, match days and who brings the kit. A team name in a chat is also a badge, so players like one with a bit of spirit, a short form that fits on a jersey, and something that can be shouted.

How to choose

  • Use the team name and the season: "Eleven Strikers 2026".
  • Make a separate group for match-day logistics and one for banter.
  • Short names are easier for a jersey or a banner.
  • Check that the name does not copy an existing team in your league.

What to avoid

Avoid copying the names or logos of professional teams, and avoid names that insult opponents.

Build your own

Words that suit cricket and sports teams groups: Mighty, Fiery, Royal, Night, Sunday, Gully, Thunder, Swift. Words to join them with: XI, Strikers, Riders, Kings, Warriors, Titans, Club, Squad. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What is a good name for a cricket group?

The team name and season, such as "Eleven Strikers 2026", with a separate chat for banter.

Can we use a famous team's name?

It is better not to. Choose your own name so it is yours.
