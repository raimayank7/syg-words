About family group names

A family group is the busiest chat on most phones: birthdays, photos, forwards, and the news that someone has reached home. Its name is seen every day by grandparents and grandchildren alike, so it should be warm, short and easy to read in any script.

How to choose

  • Use the family name or the home's name: "The Sharma House" or "Sharma Parivar" is understood by every age.
  • Keep it readable by the oldest member. A name with many emojis or symbols may show as boxes on an older phone.
  • Name the place if the family is spread out: "Sharmas, Delhi to Dubai".
  • A small joke is fine if every member will laugh, but not one that belongs to only the young.

What to avoid

Avoid names that exclude someone, such as "The Boys" for a family with daughters, or that mention a private matter such as money or a dispute.

Build your own

Words that suit family groups: Happy, Big, Warm, Lucky, Golden, Cozy, Joyful, Loud. Words to join them with: Family, House, Clan, Home, Tribe, Circle, Nest, Parivar. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What is a good name for a family WhatsApp group?

One that the oldest and youngest member both understand: the family name plus a warm word, such as "Sharma Parivar" or "Home Sweet Group".

Should a family group name be in Hindi?

Use the language the family speaks at home. A name in Hindi written in English letters (Hinglish) is easy for everyone to read on any phone.
