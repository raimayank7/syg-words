About festivals and occasions group names

Festival groups are short-lived and very active: Diwali gifts, Eid greetings, Lohri gatherings, Christmas parties, Navratri garba nights. A name that carries the festival and the year keeps the chat clear and easy to archive.

How to choose

  • Festival and year: "Diwali 2026".
  • The people: "Diwali Ladies", "Colony Diwali Committee".
  • Use greetings with care: only use a festival name that your group celebrates.
  • Rename or leave the group when the festival ends.

What to avoid

Avoid mixing festivals in a way that might offend, and avoid any political or divisive message in the name.

Build your own

Words that suit festivals and occasions groups: Festive, Happy, Colourful, Bright, Sweet, Joyful, Grand, Lucky. Words to join them with: Utsav, Mela, Committee, Team, Party, Squad, Club, Crew. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What is a good name for a festival group?

The festival and the year, plus the people: "Diwali 2026", "Colony Diwali Committee".

Should I keep the group after the festival?

You can rename it to the next festival or leave it; an archive is fine.
