About fitness and walking group names

A fitness group runs on encouragement: who has walked, who has done yoga, who needs a nudge. A good name has energy and a sense of habit, and is not shy about a joke at the expense of the snooze button.

How to choose

  • Say the activity: "Morning Walkers", "Yoga At Dawn".
  • Add a time or place to attract nearby people.
  • A goal in the name keeps spirits up: "10,000 Steps Club".
  • Keep the tone encouraging; no body shaming.

What to avoid

Avoid names about weight loss promises or body comparison, and avoid medical claims.

Build your own

Words that suit fitness and walking groups: Early, Fit, Happy, Sweaty, Daily, Brave, Strong, Sunny. Words to join them with: Walkers, Runners, Club, Squad, Gang, Challenge, Buddies, Crew. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What is a good fitness group name?

The activity plus energy: "Morning Walkers", "10,000 Steps Club", "Sweat Squad".

Can we name a group for weight loss?

It is kinder to name the habit (walking, yoga) than the weight.
