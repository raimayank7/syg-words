About food and recipe groups group names

A food group is for recipes, restaurant finds and the endless question "what shall we cook today". Its name can be playful, with a hint of spice, and should make people hungry.

How to choose

  • Use a food word: chai, tiffin, thali, masala, mithai.
  • Add the group's speciality: "Biryani Lovers", "Baking Club".
  • A recipe-swap group may like a title that sounds like a cookbook.
  • Name the city if it is for restaurants: "Ludhiana Foodies".

What to avoid

Avoid brand names of restaurants or products, and avoid health claims about food.

Build your own

Words that suit food and recipe groups groups: Spicy, Tasty, Crispy, Sunday, Hungry, Homemade, Street, Sweet. Words to join them with: Foodies, Kitchen, Club, Brigade, Thali, Masala, Table, Gang. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What is a good name for a recipe group?

A food word plus a bit of fun: "Tiffin Talks", "Masala Mix", "The Recipe Exchange".

Can we use a restaurant's name?

Better not; use your own name and avoid trademarks.
