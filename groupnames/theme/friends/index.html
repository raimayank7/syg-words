About friends group names

A friends group is where the jokes live, so the name is the first joke. The best ones are short, a little silly, and mean something only to the people in the chat. They are also read many times a day, so a name that is funny once but tiring the tenth time will be changed soon.

How to choose

  • Use a shared memory: the place you met, a trip, a nickname, a favourite phrase.
  • Short is better. Names under 25 characters are shown in full on most phones.
  • A running gag can be updated, so choose something that can grow.
  • Agree the name with the group. A name only one person likes will not last.

What to avoid

Avoid names that mock a member, mention someone's weight, caste, religion or looks, or that would embarrass the group if a parent or a boss saw them.

Build your own

Words that suit friends groups: Crazy, Late, Lazy, Epic, Silly, Secret, Midnight, Funky. Words to join them with: Squad, Gang, Club, Crew, Yaars, Mehfil, Bunch, Adda. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Questions

What are funny WhatsApp group names for friends?

Names about your habits work best: "Plan Cancel Karo", "The Late Replies", "Seen And Ignored". Pick one that matches what your group really does.

How do we choose a name everyone likes?

Ask for three suggestions each, pick the top two by a thumbs-up vote, and use the funnier one for a month.
