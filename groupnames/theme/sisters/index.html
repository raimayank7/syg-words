About sisters, brothers and siblings group names

Siblings' groups are small and personal: a few people who share parents, a past and a hundred inside jokes. Their names tend to be affectionate, a little teasing, and often in the home language.

How to choose

  • Use the shared birth order or number: "The Three Sisters".
  • Add a nickname or a childhood memory.
  • Use the home language, since the group is private.
  • A name can be changed often; let each sibling take a turn.

What to avoid

Avoid names that compare siblings or bring up old arguments.

Build your own

Words that suit sisters, brothers and siblings groups: Little, Naughty, Big, Original, Funny, Lucky, Loud, Sweet. Words to join them with: Sisters, Brothers, Siblings, Gang, Fauj, Squad, Toli, Duo. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What can siblings name their group?

Something teasing and warm in the home language: "Behnon Ka Adda", "Partners In Crime".

Should parents be in the siblings' group?

That is for the siblings to decide; a separate parents group is often easier.
