About startups and small business group names

A startup group is where a small team decides, argues and celebrates. A shop owner, a freelancer or a founder uses the group for orders, suppliers and customers. The name should be clear to the team and professional to the outside world, because customers may be added to some of these groups.

How to choose

  • Business name plus function: "Sharma Traders Orders", "Founders Circle".
  • Keep a customer group (announcements only) separate from the team group.
  • Name groups by the job: "Suppliers", "Delivery Updates", "Customer Support".
  • Check that the name does not mislead customers about who runs it.

What to avoid

Avoid grand claims such as "number one" and avoid names that suggest official status you do not have.

Build your own

Words that suit startups and small business groups: Smart, Prime, Local, Early, Growing, Trusted, Fresh, Daily. Words to join them with: Traders, Desk, Team, Circle, Hub, Network, Club, Updates. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Questions

How should a business WhatsApp group be named?

Business name plus purpose: "Sharma Traders Orders" or "Delivery Updates".

Should customers be in the same group as staff?

No. Keep them in a separate announcements group.
