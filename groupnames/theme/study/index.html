About study and book groups group names

A study group works best when its name states the subject and the goal. A book group wants a name that suggests reading, and perhaps a regular meeting. In both cases, a good name helps new members understand what the group is for.

How to choose

  • Subject and exam first: "CA Foundation Group", "UPSC Prelims 2026".
  • Add the schedule if it is regular: "Sunday Book Club".
  • Keep a notes-only group separate from the discussion.
  • Do not use the name of a coaching institute or publisher unless you belong to it.

What to avoid

Avoid promising results ("100 percent selection") and avoid names that suggest copied papers or answer sharing.

Build your own

Words that suit study and book groups groups: Daily, Focused, Curious, Bright, Early, Silent, Sunday, Smart. Words to join them with: Study Circle, Book Club, Learners, Readers, Warriors, Hub, Buddies, Room. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What should a study group be named?

The subject and the exam or goal, for example "NEET Revision Circle" or "UPSC Prelims 2026".

How do we name a book club?

Use a reading word and your meeting day, such as "The Sunday Book Club".
