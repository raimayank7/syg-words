About trips and travel group names

A trip group is born to plan one journey and then lives on as an album. The name usually has the destination and the year, plus a joke about the plan. It is the group in which the photos are shared, so people look at the name often.

How to choose

  • Destination and year first: "Goa 2026", "Manali Winter Trip".
  • Add a theme or an inside joke: "No Plan Goa".
  • A trip group with a deadline should have a clear purpose in the description: dates, budget, who pays.
  • Rename it after the trip to something like "Goa 2026 Photos" so it stays tidy.

What to avoid

Avoid names that promise things you cannot do (luxury, surprise) and avoid anything that reveals a plan for a person's surprise party.

Build your own

Words that suit trips and travel groups: Wild, Lazy, Mountain, Beach, Road, Budget, Winter, Summer. Words to join them with: Trippers, Travellers, Explorers, Gang, Squad, Riders, Nomads, Crew. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then add a year, a place or a nickname that only your group knows.

Want something different? The name generator mixes words from this theme into new ideas, and the guide to naming a group has six steps for any group.

Questions

What should a trip group be named?

The destination, the year, and a joke: "Goa 2026", "No Plan Goa".

Should I rename the group after the trip?

Yes. "Goa 2026 Photos" tells people what the group is now for.
