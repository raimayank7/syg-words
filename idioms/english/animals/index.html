Animal idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

16 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

People have lived beside animals for thousands of years, and each animal earned a character in the language. The fox is clever, the bee is busy, the horse is strong and the fish is helpless out of water. English idioms borrow these characters. When you know what the animal stands for, the idiom opens up.

Hindi does the same thing with different animals, which is why a word for word translation rarely works. English says "raining cats and dogs"; Hindi says "मूसलाधार बारिश". So do not translate the animal. Translate the idea. The Hindi line under each idiom on this page gives the idea in the words people really use.

Animal idioms are lively and suit stories, speeches and conversation. In a formal letter or an answer in a science paper they can sound out of place, so choose with care.

  • Let the cat out of the bag

    MeaningTo tell a secret by mistake.

    Hindiभेद खोल देना

    ExampleWe planned a surprise party, but Rohan let the cat out of the bag.

  • Raining cats and dogs

    MeaningRaining very heavily.

    Hindiमूसलाधार बारिश होना

    ExampleTake an umbrella. It is raining cats and dogs.

  • Kill two birds with one stone

    MeaningTo finish two jobs with one action.

    Hindiएक तीर से दो निशाने लगाना, एक पंथ दो काज

    ExampleI walked to the market, so I got my exercise and the vegetables: two birds with one stone.

  • The elephant in the room

    MeaningA big problem everyone knows about but nobody wants to talk about.

    Hindiवह बड़ी बात जिस पर कोई बोलना नहीं चाहता

    ExampleThe cost of the trip was the elephant in the room at our family meeting.

  • Hold your horses

    MeaningWait; do not be in such a hurry.

    Hindiज़रा ठहरो, जल्दबाज़ी मत करो

    ExampleHold your horses! We have not even decided the date yet.

  • A fish out of water

    MeaningSomeone who feels uncomfortable in a new place.

    Hindiबेगानी जगह में असहज होना

    ExampleOn my first day in the big city school I felt like a fish out of water.

  • Straight from the horse's mouth

    MeaningFrom the person who knows it first-hand.

    Hindiसीधे असली स्रोत से

    ExampleThe holiday is confirmed. I heard it straight from the horse's mouth, the principal.

  • Busy as a bee

    MeaningVery busy and active.

    Hindiबहुत व्यस्त रहना

    ExampleMother has been busy as a bee since morning because guests are coming.

  • Cry wolf

    MeaningTo raise a false alarm so often that nobody believes you later.

    Hindiझूठा शोर मचाना

    ExampleIf you cry wolf about being ill every Monday, no one will believe you when you really are.

  • Take the bull by the horns

    MeaningTo face a difficult problem directly and bravely.

    Hindiमुश्किल का डटकर सामना करना

    ExampleShe took the bull by the horns and spoke to the manager herself.

  • The early bird catches the worm

    MeaningThe person who starts early gets the best chance.

    Hindiजो जल्दी उठता है वही पाता है

    ExampleI reached the counter at seven and got the first token. The early bird catches the worm.

  • A wild goose chase

    MeaningA long search for something that cannot be found.

    Hindiबेकार की भागदौड़

    ExampleLooking for that old shop turned into a wild goose chase. It closed years ago.

  • Let sleeping dogs lie

    MeaningDo not disturb a situation that is calm now.

    Hindiगड़े मुर्दे न उखाड़ना

    ExampleThey have stopped arguing. Let sleeping dogs lie and do not bring it up again.

  • Smell a rat

    MeaningTo feel that something is wrong or dishonest.

    Hindiदाल में कुछ काला लगना

    ExampleThe price was too low to be true, and I smelt a rat.

  • A dark horse

    MeaningSomeone whose ability is not known and who may surprise everyone.

    Hindiछुपा रुस्तम

    ExampleNobody expected her to win. She was the dark horse of the tournament.

  • Crocodile tears

    MeaningFalse tears; pretended sadness.

    Hindiघड़ियाली आँसू बहाना

    ExampleHe shed crocodile tears when the rival team lost.
