Body idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

16 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

The body gives English more idioms than any other subject. We think with the head, feel with the heart, work with the hands and stand on our feet, so it is natural that these words slipped into everyday sayings. The trick with body idioms is that the picture is usually close to the meaning. Someone who "keeps an eye" on a bag really is looking at it now and then. Someone who gets "cold feet" has feet that will not carry them forward.

Read the idiom, picture it, then read the meaning. If the picture and the meaning join in your mind, you will not forget it. Most of the idioms on this page are safe in both speech and school writing. A few, such as "pull someone's leg", are friendly and informal: use them in a letter to a friend or in a story, not in a formal application.

  • Keep an eye on

    MeaningTo watch something or someone carefully for a while.

    Hindiनज़र रखना, ध्यान रखना

    ExamplePlease keep an eye on my bag while I buy the tickets.

  • Cost an arm and a leg

    MeaningTo be very expensive.

    Hindiबहुत महँगा होना

    ExampleThe new phone looks good, but it costs an arm and a leg.

  • Pull someone's leg

    MeaningTo joke with someone by telling them something untrue.

    Hindiमज़ाक करना, खिंचाई करना

    ExampleRelax, there is no test today. I was only pulling your leg.

  • Get cold feet

    MeaningTo suddenly feel too nervous to do something you had planned.

    Hindiऐन मौके पर घबरा जाना

    ExampleHe wanted to sing on stage but got cold feet at the last minute.

  • Learn by heart

    MeaningTo memorise something completely.

    Hindiरट लेना, कंठस्थ करना

    ExampleOur teacher asked us to learn the poem by heart.

  • Give someone a hand

    MeaningTo help someone.

    Hindiहाथ बँटाना, मदद करना

    ExampleCould you give me a hand with these boxes?

  • Head over heels

    MeaningCompletely, deeply (usually in love).

    Hindiपूरी तरह डूबा हुआ

    ExampleShe is head over heels in love with her new puppy.

  • Turn a blind eye

    MeaningTo pretend not to notice something wrong.

    Hindiदेखकर भी अनदेखा करना

    ExampleThe guard turned a blind eye to the boys who came in late.

  • See eye to eye

    MeaningTo agree with someone.

    Hindiएक राय होना

    ExampleMy brother and I do not see eye to eye on which channel to watch.

  • On the tip of my tongue

    MeaningAlmost remembered, but not quite.

    Hindiज़बान पर होना पर याद न आना

    ExampleHer name is on the tip of my tongue. Give me a second.

  • Keep your chin up

    MeaningTo stay cheerful in a hard time.

    Hindiहिम्मत बनाए रखना

    ExampleKeep your chin up. One bad result does not decide your future.

  • Have your hands full

    MeaningTo be very busy.

    Hindiबहुत व्यस्त होना

    ExampleWith two small children and a shop to run, she has her hands full.

  • A pain in the neck

    MeaningA person or thing that is very annoying.

    Hindiसिरदर्द बन जाना

    ExampleFilling the same form three times is a pain in the neck.

  • Face the music

    MeaningTo accept the unpleasant result of what you did.

    Hindiअपने किए का नतीजा भुगतना

    ExampleI broke the window, so I went home to face the music.

  • Break a leg

    MeaningGood luck (said to a performer before a show).

    Hindiशुभकामना देना

    ExampleYour dance is next. Break a leg!

  • Lend an ear

    MeaningTo listen with attention and kindness.

    Hindiध्यान से सुनना

    ExampleA good friend lends an ear when you are upset.
