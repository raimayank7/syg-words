Colour idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

Every language paints its feelings, but not with the same colours. In English, green is the colour of envy, blue of sadness and red of anger or danger. In Hindi, anger also turns the face red ("लाल-पीला होना"), but sadness has no blue. So a colour idiom has to be learnt as a whole, not guessed from the colour.

Some colour idioms have a story behind them. A "red-letter day" comes from old calendars, in which festivals and holy days were printed in red. A "white elephant" is said to come from a tale about kings who gifted a rare white elephant that was costly to keep and could not be put to work. Stories like these are worth a minute of your time because they tie the meaning to the words.

These idioms are short and vivid, and examiners like to ask them. Learn them in pairs of opposites where you can: "black and white" (clear) against "a grey area" (unclear), and "green signal" against "caught red-handed".

  • Out of the blue

    MeaningSuddenly and unexpectedly.

    Hindiअचानक, बिना किसी अंदेशे के

    ExampleOut of the blue, my old classmate called me after ten years.

  • Green with envy

    MeaningVery jealous.

    Hindiजलन से भर जाना

    ExampleHis friends were green with envy when they saw his new cycle.

  • Caught red-handed

    MeaningCaught while doing something wrong.

    Hindiरंगे हाथों पकड़े जाना

    ExampleThe boy was caught red-handed taking mangoes from the orchard.

  • A white lie

    MeaningA small lie told to be polite or kind.

    Hindiकिसी का दिल रखने के लिए बोला गया छोटा झूठ

    ExampleI told a white lie and said the burnt toast was tasty.

  • In black and white

    MeaningIn writing or print; very clear.

    Hindiलिखित रूप में, साफ़-साफ़

    ExampleDo not trust a spoken promise. Get the offer in black and white.

  • A red-letter day

    MeaningA very happy and important day.

    Hindiयादगार और ख़ुशी का दिन

    ExampleThe day our village got its first bus was a red-letter day.

  • See red

    MeaningTo become very angry.

    Hindiग़ुस्से से लाल होना

    ExampleFather saw red when he found the scratch on the new car.

  • Feeling blue

    MeaningFeeling sad.

    Hindiउदास होना

    ExampleShe has been feeling blue since her best friend moved to another city.

  • Green signal

    MeaningPermission to begin something.

    Hindiहरी झंडी मिलना

    ExampleThe principal gave the green signal for the science fair.

  • A golden opportunity

    MeaningA very good chance that may not come again.

    Hindiसुनहरा मौका

    ExampleThe free coaching camp is a golden opportunity for young players.

  • A grey area

    MeaningA matter that is not clear or has no fixed rule.

    Hindiधुँधला या अस्पष्ट मामला

    ExampleWhether a guest may use the library is a grey area in the rules.

  • With flying colours

    MeaningWith great success.

    Hindiशानदार सफलता के साथ

    ExampleShe passed the board examination with flying colours.

  • A white elephant

    MeaningSomething costly to keep that is of little use.

    Hindiसफ़ेद हाथी

    ExampleThe huge empty stadium has become a white elephant for the town.

  • Black sheep

    MeaningA member of a family or group who is seen as a disgrace or as different.

    Hindiपरिवार की लीक से हटकर चलने वाला

    ExampleHe was called the black sheep of the family until his music made him famous.

  • Roll out the red carpet

    MeaningTo give a guest a special welcome.

    Hindiशानदार स्वागत करना

    ExampleThe school rolled out the red carpet for its old students.
