Feelings idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

"I am happy" and "I am angry" are correct, but they are flat. Idioms give feelings a shape. A happy person is "over the moon". A nervous person has "butterflies in the stomach". An angry person "hits the roof". With these phrases your speech and stories sound alive.

This page is arranged from happy feelings to difficult ones. When you write a story, try to show a feeling with one idiom instead of three adjectives. "Meera had butterflies in her stomach as she walked to the stage" tells the reader more than "Meera was very, very nervous".

A word about kindness: idioms such as "lose your temper" describe behaviour, and it is fair to use them. Avoid joking phrases about someone's mind or health; they hurt people and have no place in good writing.

  • Over the moon

    MeaningExtremely pleased and happy.

    Hindiख़ुशी से झूम उठना

    ExampleMy parents were over the moon when I got the scholarship.

  • Butterflies in the stomach

    MeaningA nervous feeling before doing something.

    Hindiघबराहट से पेट में हलचल होना

    ExampleI had butterflies in my stomach before my first speech.

  • Lose your temper

    MeaningTo become angry suddenly.

    Hindiआपा खो देना

    ExampleThe driver lost his temper in the traffic jam.

  • Jump for joy

    MeaningTo be very happy and excited.

    Hindiख़ुशी से उछल पड़ना

    ExampleThe children jumped for joy when the picnic was announced.

  • Down in the dumps

    MeaningSad and without energy.

    Hindiमन उदास और बुझा-बुझा होना

    ExampleHe has been down in the dumps since his team lost the final.

  • A weight off your shoulders

    MeaningA feeling of relief when a worry ends.

    Hindiसिर से बोझ उतरना

    ExampleHanding in the project was a weight off my shoulders.

  • Scared stiff

    MeaningSo frightened that you cannot move.

    Hindiडर के मारे जम जाना

    ExampleI was scared stiff when the lights went out in the lift.

  • Hit the roof

    MeaningTo become extremely angry.

    Hindiआगबबूला होना

    ExampleMother hit the roof when she saw mud on the new carpet.

  • Get on someone's nerves

    MeaningTo annoy someone.

    Hindiकिसी को खिझाना, नाक में दम करना

    ExampleThe dripping tap is getting on my nerves.

  • A heart of gold

    MeaningA very kind nature.

    Hindiसोने जैसा दिल

    ExampleOur watchman has a heart of gold; he feeds every stray dog in the lane.

  • Have a change of heart

    MeaningTo change your opinion or feeling.

    Hindiमन बदल जाना

    ExampleHe refused at first but had a change of heart and joined the team.

  • Mixed feelings

    MeaningTo feel both happy and sad about something.

    Hindiमिली-जुली भावनाएँ

    ExampleI have mixed feelings about leaving school: excited and a little sad.

  • Burst into tears

    MeaningTo suddenly start crying.

    Hindiफूट-फूटकर रो पड़ना

    ExampleThe little girl burst into tears when her balloon flew away.

  • Keep your cool

    MeaningTo stay calm in a difficult moment.

    Hindiशांत रहना, ठंडे दिमाग़ से काम लेना

    ExampleThe pilot kept her cool and landed the plane safely.

  • Take something to heart

    MeaningTo be deeply affected by a remark.

    Hindiकिसी बात को दिल पर ले लेना

    ExampleIt was only a joke. Do not take it to heart.
