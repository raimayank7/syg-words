Food idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

16 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

Food idioms are some of the easiest to remember because everybody eats. Many of them come from the kitchen and the dining table of old England, so the foods are bread, butter, cake, tea and beans rather than roti, ghee and dal. The ideas, however, are the same everywhere. Something easy is "a piece of cake" in English and "बाएँ हाथ का खेल" in Hindi.

A helpful way to study this page is to group the idioms by what they say. Some describe how easy or hard a thing is (a piece of cake, a hard nut to crack). Some describe people (the apple of my eye, a couch potato). Some describe money and work (bread and butter, bring home the bacon). Make three small lists in your notebook and add one sentence about your own life to each idiom. A sentence about yourself stays in memory far longer than a sentence from a book.

  • A piece of cake

    MeaningSomething very easy to do.

    Hindiबाएँ हाथ का खेल

    ExampleThe maths test was a piece of cake because I had practised every sum.

  • Spill the beans

    MeaningTo tell secret information.

    Hindiराज़ खोल देना

    ExampleDo not spill the beans about the gift before her birthday.

  • The apple of someone's eye

    MeaningA person who is loved very much.

    Hindiआँखों का तारा

    ExampleThe youngest grandchild is the apple of her grandfather's eye.

  • Bread and butter

    MeaningThe main way a person earns a living.

    Hindiरोज़ी-रोटी

    ExampleTailoring is his bread and butter; singing is only a hobby.

  • Cry over spilt milk

    MeaningTo be upset about something that cannot be changed now.

    Hindiबीती बात पर पछताना

    ExampleThe marks are out. There is no use crying over spilt milk; prepare for the next exam.

  • Take it with a pinch of salt

    MeaningDo not believe something completely.

    Hindiकिसी बात को पूरा सच न मानना

    ExampleHe loves to exaggerate, so take his stories with a pinch of salt.

  • Butter someone up

    MeaningTo praise someone too much to get a favour.

    Hindiमक्खन लगाना, चापलूसी करना

    ExampleHe is buttering up the teacher because he wants extra marks.

  • A hard nut to crack

    MeaningA difficult problem or a person who is hard to deal with.

    Hindiटेढ़ी खीर

    ExampleThe last question in the paper was a hard nut to crack.

  • Not my cup of tea

    MeaningNot something I like or am good at.

    Hindiमेरे बस या पसंद की चीज़ नहीं

    ExampleHorror films are not my cup of tea. I prefer comedies.

  • In a nutshell

    MeaningIn a few words; briefly.

    Hindiसंक्षेप में, सार में

    ExampleIn a nutshell, we need more time and two more people.

  • Have a lot on your plate

    MeaningTo have a great deal of work or many worries.

    Hindiसिर पर बहुत काम होना

    ExampleWith exams and the wedding at home, I have a lot on my plate this month.

  • Sell like hot cakes

    MeaningTo be sold quickly and in large numbers.

    Hindiहाथों-हाथ बिकना

    ExampleThe new kites sold like hot cakes before Makar Sankranti.

  • Couch potato

    MeaningA lazy person who sits and watches television for hours.

    Hindiदिन भर टीवी के आगे पड़ा रहने वाला

    ExampleGo and play outside. Do not become a couch potato in the holidays.

  • Bite off more than you can chew

    MeaningTo take on more work than you can manage.

    Hindiअपनी क्षमता से ज़्यादा काम ले लेना

    ExampleI joined three clubs at once and soon saw that I had bitten off more than I could chew.

  • Full of beans

    MeaningLively and full of energy.

    Hindiजोश और फुर्ती से भरा

    ExampleThe children were full of beans after the long weekend.

  • Icing on the cake

    MeaningSomething extra that makes a good thing even better.

    Hindiसोने पर सुहागा

    ExampleWe won the match, and my catch being shown on the screen was the icing on the cake.
