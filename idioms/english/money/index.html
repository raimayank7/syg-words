Money idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

Idioms about money are heard at home, in shops and in offices, so they are useful to know well before you start earning. They talk about three things: having money, lacking money, and what something is worth.

Notice how often these idioms compare money to a living or natural thing. Money "does not grow on trees". A careful person "saves for a rainy day". A person in debt is "in the red", a phrase that comes from the old habit of writing losses in red ink in account books, while profits were written in black.

These idioms are common in newspapers and in business English, so they also help when you read the money pages or prepare for an interview. One warning: an idiom is a way of speaking, not advice. "Penny wise and pound foolish" describes a habit; it does not tell you how to manage your savings.

  • Save for a rainy day

    MeaningTo keep money aside for a time of need.

    Hindiबुरे वक़्त के लिए बचाकर रखना

    ExampleMy grandmother always tells us to save a little for a rainy day.

  • Money does not grow on trees

    MeaningMoney is limited and must not be wasted.

    Hindiपैसे पेड़ पर नहीं उगते

    ExampleWe cannot buy a new game every month. Money does not grow on trees.

  • Break the bank

    MeaningTo cost too much.

    Hindiजेब पर बहुत भारी पड़ना

    ExampleA picnic in the park will be fun and will not break the bank.

  • Make ends meet

    MeaningTo earn just enough to pay for what you need.

    Hindiगुज़ारा चलाना

    ExampleAfter the rent went up, the family found it hard to make ends meet.

  • Tighten your belt

    MeaningTo spend less because you have less money.

    Hindiख़र्च में कटौती करना

    ExampleFather's shop was closed for a month, so we all tightened our belts.

  • Born with a silver spoon in the mouth

    MeaningBorn into a rich family.

    Hindiअमीर घर में पैदा होना

    ExampleHe was born with a silver spoon in his mouth and never had to worry about fees.

  • Penny wise and pound foolish

    MeaningCareful about small amounts but careless about large ones.

    Hindiअशर्फ़ियाँ लुटें और कोयलों पर मुहर

    ExampleHe walked two kilometres to save ten rupees and then lost his wallet: penny wise and pound foolish.

  • Worth its weight in gold

    MeaningExtremely useful or valuable.

    Hindiबहुत क़ीमती और काम का

    ExampleA neighbour who helps in an emergency is worth their weight in gold.

  • Pay through the nose

    MeaningTo pay far too much for something.

    Hindiज़रूरत से बहुत ज़्यादा दाम चुकाना

    ExampleWe booked late and paid through the nose for the train tickets.

  • In the red

    MeaningOwing money; making a loss.

    Hindiघाटे में होना

    ExampleThe small shop was in the red for two years before it started to earn.

  • Foot the bill

    MeaningTo pay the cost of something.

    Hindiख़र्च उठाना

    ExampleUncle footed the bill for the whole family dinner.

  • Time is money

    MeaningTime is valuable, so do not waste it.

    Hindiसमय बहुत क़ीमती है

    ExampleLet us start the meeting on the dot. Time is money.

  • A penny for your thoughts

    MeaningA friendly way to ask what someone is thinking.

    Hindiक्या सोच रहे हो?

    ExampleYou have been quiet all evening. A penny for your thoughts?

  • From rags to riches

    MeaningFrom being very poor to being rich.

    Hindiग़रीबी से अमीरी तक पहुँचना

    ExampleHer journey from a roadside stall to a chain of bakeries is a real rags to riches story.

  • Cut corners

    MeaningTo do something badly or cheaply to save time or money.

    Hindiकाम में कंजूसी या लापरवाही करना

    ExampleThe builder cut corners, and the roof began to leak in the first monsoon.
