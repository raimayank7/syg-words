School and learning idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

These idioms come straight from school life: books, classes, tests and teachers. Students can use them the same day, in a diary entry, a letter to a friend or a speech on Teachers' Day.

Some carry a small lesson about learning itself. "Practice makes perfect" and "learn from your mistakes" are simple truths that every coach repeats. "Do not judge a book by its cover" warns us not to decide about people from their looks. Because they are short and wise, such idioms make good opening or closing lines for an essay.

A few are informal. "Bookworm" and "teacher's pet" are playful words to use among friends; they can sting if said unkindly. "Pass with flying colours" and "cover a lot of ground" are safe anywhere.

  • Hit the books

    MeaningTo begin studying seriously.

    Hindiपढ़ाई में जुट जाना

    ExampleThe match is over. Now I must hit the books for tomorrow's test.

  • A bookworm

    MeaningA person who loves reading.

    Hindiकिताबी कीड़ा

    ExampleMy sister is a bookworm; she finishes a novel every week.

  • Brainstorm

    MeaningTo think of many ideas together, quickly.

    Hindiमिलकर नए-नए विचार सोचना

    ExampleLet us brainstorm names for our class magazine.

  • Practice makes perfect

    MeaningDoing something again and again makes you good at it.

    Hindiकरत-करत अभ्यास के जड़मति होत सुजान

    ExampleYour handwriting will improve. Practice makes perfect.

  • Do not judge a book by its cover

    MeaningDo not decide about someone or something from the outside.

    Hindiऊपरी रूप देखकर राय न बनाना

    ExampleThe old shop looks plain but sells the best sweets. Do not judge a book by its cover.

  • Pass with flying colours

    MeaningTo pass with very high marks or great success.

    Hindiबहुत अच्छे अंकों से पास होना

    ExampleAll four friends passed the driving test with flying colours.

  • Teach someone a lesson

    MeaningTo punish someone so that they do not repeat a mistake.

    Hindiसबक़ सिखाना

    ExampleMissing the bus taught him a lesson about getting up late.

  • Learn from your mistakes

    MeaningTo use past errors to do better.

    Hindiअपनी ग़लतियों से सीखना

    ExampleI learnt from my mistakes and checked every answer this time.

  • Draw a blank

    MeaningTo fail to remember or find something.

    Hindiकुछ याद न आना

    ExampleI knew the formula at home, but in the exam I drew a blank.

  • Cover a lot of ground

    MeaningTo deal with a large amount of work or many subjects.

    Hindiबहुत सारा काम या पाठ निपटाना

    ExampleWe covered a lot of ground in today's revision class.

  • Put your thinking cap on

    MeaningTo think seriously about a problem.

    Hindiदिमाग़ लगाना

    ExamplePut your thinking caps on, children. Here is a riddle.

  • Teacher's pet

    MeaningA student who is the teacher's favourite.

    Hindiशिक्षक का चहेता

    ExampleHe is called the teacher's pet because he answers first every time.

  • Know something inside out

    MeaningTo know something very thoroughly.

    Hindiकिसी चीज़ को रग-रग से जानना

    ExampleShe knows the map of India inside out.

  • A quick learner

    MeaningSomeone who learns new things fast.

    Hindiजल्दी सीख लेने वाला

    ExampleThe new boy is a quick learner and picked up Hindi in months.

  • From A to Z

    MeaningIncluding everything, from beginning to end.

    Hindiशुरू से आख़िर तक, सब कुछ

    ExampleThe guide explains cricket rules from A to Z.
