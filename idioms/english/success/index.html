Success and failure idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

Life in idioms looks like a game. You can "hit the jackpot" or "miss the boat". You can be "on a winning streak" or go "back to square one". Many of these idioms come from sport, card games and sailing, where winning and losing are easy to see.

They are especially useful for speeches and essays about goals, hard work and learning from mistakes. A farewell speech sounds warmer with a line such as "you have passed with flying colours, and the sky is the limit". A report on a failed plan sounds honest with "the idea fell flat, so we are back to square one".

Try to learn them as a pair: one for a good result and one for a bad result. In the list below, the idioms of success come first and the idioms of failure after them, so you can match them yourself.

  • The sky is the limit

    MeaningThere is no limit to what can be achieved.

    Hindiतरक़्क़ी की कोई सीमा नहीं

    ExampleWith your talent and hard work, the sky is the limit.

  • Hit the jackpot

    MeaningTo have a big success or great luck.

    Hindiबड़ी सफलता या क़िस्मत हाथ लगना

    ExampleWe hit the jackpot with our stall: every plate was sold by noon.

  • Bear fruit

    MeaningTo give good results after effort.

    Hindiमेहनत का फल मिलना, रंग लाना

    ExampleMonths of practice bore fruit when the team reached the finals.

  • Come a long way

    MeaningTo make great progress.

    Hindiबहुत तरक़्क़ी करना

    ExampleHe could not read a year ago. He has come a long way.

  • A feather in your cap

    MeaningAn achievement to be proud of.

    Hindiउपलब्धियों में एक और नगीना

    ExampleWinning the state medal is another feather in her cap.

  • Win hands down

    MeaningTo win very easily.

    Hindiआसानी से जीत जाना

    ExampleOur school won the quiz hands down.

  • Turn over a new leaf

    MeaningTo begin to behave in a better way.

    Hindiनई और बेहतर शुरुआत करना

    ExampleAfter the warning he turned over a new leaf and never missed a class.

  • Miss the boat

    MeaningTo lose a chance by being too slow.

    Hindiमौका हाथ से निकल जाना

    ExampleThe form closed yesterday. I am afraid you have missed the boat.

  • Back to square one

    MeaningBack at the beginning, with nothing gained.

    Hindiफिर वहीं पहुँच जाना जहाँ से चले थे

    ExampleThe glue did not hold, and we were back to square one.

  • Fall flat

    MeaningTo fail to have the effect that was wanted.

    Hindiबेअसर रह जाना

    ExampleHis joke fell flat; nobody in the hall laughed.

  • Throw in the towel

    MeaningTo stop trying; to accept defeat.

    Hindiहार मान लेना

    ExampleThe puzzle was hard, but she refused to throw in the towel.

  • A close shave

    MeaningA narrow escape from danger or failure.

    Hindiबाल-बाल बचना

    ExampleThe cycle missed the pothole by an inch. That was a close shave.

  • Learn the hard way

    MeaningTo learn from a painful experience.

    Hindiठोकर खाकर सीखना

    ExampleI learnt the hard way to save my work every few minutes.

  • Go down the drain

    MeaningTo be wasted or lost.

    Hindiपानी में चला जाना, बेकार हो जाना

    ExampleAll our decoration went down the drain when it rained on the pandal.

  • Try your luck

    MeaningTo attempt something that may or may not succeed.

    Hindiक़िस्मत आज़माना

    ExampleI have never painted before, but I will try my luck in the contest.
