Talking and secrets idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

We spend much of the day talking, so English has a rich store of idioms about talk itself: speaking plainly, hiding things, listening and keeping promises. They help you describe a conversation without repeating "he said" and "she said".

Several come in useful pairs. To keep a secret is to "keep it under your hat"; to reveal it is to "let the cat out of the bag". To speak directly is to "call a spade a spade"; to avoid the point is to "beat around the bush". Learning them as opposites doubles what you remember.

Idioms about truth and promises matter in formal life too. "Keep your word", "read between the lines" and "word of mouth" are used in newspapers and in business. They are a safe choice for essays and for the spoken round of an examination.

  • Speak your mind

    MeaningTo say honestly what you think.

    Hindiखुलकर अपनी बात कहना

    ExampleDo not be afraid to speak your mind in the meeting.

  • Keep it under your hat

    MeaningTo keep something secret.

    Hindiबात अपने तक ही रखना

    ExampleI have been chosen as captain, but keep it under your hat until Monday.

  • Call a spade a spade

    MeaningTo say the truth plainly, even if it is unpleasant.

    Hindiखरी-खरी कहना

    ExampleShe calls a spade a spade and told us the plan was weak.

  • Read between the lines

    MeaningTo understand a meaning that is not said openly.

    Hindiअनकही बात समझ लेना

    ExampleHis letter was cheerful, but reading between the lines I knew he was homesick.

  • Word of mouth

    MeaningPeople telling other people about something.

    Hindiज़ुबानी प्रचार, एक से दूसरे तक बात पहुँचना

    ExampleThe small dhaba became famous by word of mouth.

  • Keep your word

    MeaningTo do what you promised.

    Hindiवादा निभाना

    ExampleHe kept his word and returned the book on Friday.

  • Actions speak louder than words

    MeaningWhat you do matters more than what you say.

    Hindiकथनी से करनी भली

    ExampleDo not just promise to help. Actions speak louder than words.

  • Hear it on the grapevine

    MeaningTo hear news as a rumour passed from person to person.

    Hindiउड़ती-उड़ती ख़बर सुनना

    ExampleI heard on the grapevine that a new teacher is joining.

  • Get straight to the point

    MeaningTo talk about the main thing at once.

    Hindiसीधे मुद्दे पर आना

    ExampleWe have little time, so let me get straight to the point.

  • Speak of the devil

    MeaningSaid when a person appears just as you are talking about them.

    Hindiनाम लिया और हाज़िर

    ExampleSpeak of the devil! We were just talking about you.

  • Easier said than done

    MeaningSimple to talk about but hard to do.

    Hindiकहना आसान, करना मुश्किल

    ExampleWaking up at five every day is easier said than done.

  • Put in a good word

    MeaningTo say something good about a person to help them.

    Hindiकिसी की सिफ़ारिश करना

    ExamplePlease put in a good word for me with the coach.

  • Lost for words

    MeaningUnable to speak because of surprise or strong feeling.

    Hindiकुछ कहते न बनना

    ExampleWhen they sang for my birthday, I was lost for words.

  • Have the last word

    MeaningTo make the final remark in an argument.

    Hindiआख़िरी बात अपनी रखना

    ExampleMy little sister always wants to have the last word.

  • Talk someone into something

    MeaningTo persuade someone to do something.

    Hindiकिसी को मना लेना, राज़ी कर लेना

    ExampleMy friends talked me into joining the drama club.
