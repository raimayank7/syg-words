Time idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

English treats time almost like money: we spend it, save it, waste it and run out of it. Many time idioms follow from this one idea. Others come from clocks and bells, such as "around the clock" and "saved by the bell".

These idioms are among the most useful for daily speech, because we talk about being early, late and busy every day. They are also safe in formal writing. "In the nick of time", "sooner or later" and "for the time being" can all appear in an essay, a report or a letter without sounding too casual.

When you practise, link each idiom to a moment in your own day. What did you finish "in the nick of time" this week? What happens "once in a blue moon" in your family? Say the sentence aloud. Idioms are learnt by the ear and the mouth as much as by the eye.

  • Once in a blue moon

    MeaningVery rarely.

    Hindiकभी-कभार, ईद का चाँद होना

    ExampleWe eat out once in a blue moon; mostly we cook at home.

  • In the nick of time

    MeaningAt the last possible moment; just in time.

    Hindiऐन वक़्त पर

    ExampleThe ambulance arrived in the nick of time.

  • Better late than never

    MeaningDoing something late is better than not doing it at all.

    Hindiदेर आए दुरुस्त आए

    ExampleHe began to learn swimming at forty. Better late than never.

  • Beat the clock

    MeaningTo finish something before the time is over.

    Hindiसमय रहते काम पूरा कर लेना

    ExampleWe beat the clock and handed in the project an hour early.

  • Call it a day

    MeaningTo stop working for the day.

    Hindiआज के लिए काम बंद करना

    ExampleWe have painted two rooms. Let us call it a day.

  • Around the clock

    MeaningAll day and all night, without stopping.

    Hindiदिन-रात, चौबीसों घंटे

    ExampleDoctors worked around the clock during the flood.

  • Kill time

    MeaningTo do something unimportant while you wait.

    Hindiवक़्त काटना

    ExampleThe train was late, so we killed time by playing word games.

  • At the eleventh hour

    MeaningAt the very last moment.

    Hindiआख़िरी घड़ी में

    ExampleThe chief guest cancelled at the eleventh hour.

  • Time flies

    MeaningTime passes very quickly.

    Hindiवक़्त पंख लगाकर उड़ता है

    ExampleTime flies. It feels like yesterday that we joined this school.

  • A race against time

    MeaningA situation in which something must be done very fast.

    Hindiसमय से होड़

    ExampleGetting the stage ready before the guests came was a race against time.

  • Sooner or later

    MeaningAt some time in the future, certainly.

    Hindiदेर-सवेर

    ExampleSooner or later the truth comes out.

  • For the time being

    MeaningFor now; until something changes.

    Hindiफ़िलहाल

    ExampleFor the time being, classes will be held in the library.

  • Behind the times

    MeaningOld-fashioned; not aware of new ways.

    Hindiज़माने से पीछे होना

    ExampleGrandfather says he is behind the times, but he pays every bill on his phone.

  • Take your time

    MeaningDo not hurry.

    Hindiआराम से करो, जल्दी नहीं है

    ExampleTake your time and read the question twice before you answer.

  • Saved by the bell

    MeaningRescued from trouble at the last moment by something outside.

    Hindiऐन मौके पर बच जाना

    ExampleThe teacher was about to ask me, but the period ended. Saved by the bell!
