Weather and nature idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

15 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

Britain, where many English idioms were born, has weather that changes several times a day. Perhaps that is why rain, clouds, storms and ice appear in so many sayings. Bad weather stands for trouble, and clear weather stands for peace. "A storm in a teacup" is a small trouble made to look big. "Every cloud has a silver lining" promises that trouble carries some good inside it.

Nature idioms use the same pictures: the sea is wide and risky, ice is thin and dangerous, fire spreads fast. These pictures are easy to see in the mind, which makes this a good first page for a young learner.

One of them needs care. "Under the weather" means slightly unwell, such as with a cold or a headache. Do not use it for a serious illness; that would sound careless.

  • Under the weather

    MeaningFeeling slightly ill.

    Hindiतबीयत कुछ ढीली होना

    ExampleI am a bit under the weather today, so I will rest at home.

  • Every cloud has a silver lining

    MeaningSomething good can be found in every bad situation.

    Hindiहर मुश्किल में कुछ अच्छा छिपा होता है

    ExampleI missed the bus but met an old friend at the stop. Every cloud has a silver lining.

  • A storm in a teacup

    MeaningA lot of anger or worry about something small.

    Hindiराई का पहाड़, बात का बतंगड़

    ExampleTheir fight over a pencil was a storm in a teacup.

  • Break the ice

    MeaningTo make people who have just met feel relaxed.

    Hindiझिझक तोड़ना, बातचीत शुरू करना

    ExampleThe teacher told a funny story to break the ice on the first day.

  • The tip of the iceberg

    MeaningA small part of a much bigger problem.

    Hindiबड़ी समस्या का छोटा-सा हिस्सा

    ExampleThe two late buses are only the tip of the iceberg; the whole timetable needs fixing.

  • On cloud nine

    MeaningExtremely happy.

    Hindiख़ुशी से फूला न समाना

    ExampleShe was on cloud nine when her painting won the first prize.

  • Come rain or shine

    MeaningWhatever happens; in any weather.

    Hindiचाहे जो हो जाए

    ExampleThe postman comes to our lane every morning, come rain or shine.

  • Add fuel to the fire

    MeaningTo make a bad situation worse.

    Hindiआग में घी डालना

    ExampleShouting back at him will only add fuel to the fire.

  • A drop in the ocean

    MeaningA very small amount compared with what is needed.

    Hindiऊँट के मुँह में जीरा

    ExampleOur hundred rupees was a drop in the ocean, but we gave it gladly.

  • Go with the flow

    MeaningTo accept things as they come instead of fighting them.

    Hindiहालात के साथ चलना

    ExampleThe plan kept changing, so we decided to go with the flow.

  • Calm before the storm

    MeaningA quiet time just before trouble or great activity.

    Hindiतूफ़ान से पहले की शांति

    ExampleThe empty hall at six in the morning was the calm before the storm of the wedding.

  • Steal someone's thunder

    MeaningTo take the attention or praise that someone else should get.

    Hindiकिसी की वाहवाही ख़ुद लूट लेना

    ExampleHe announced his own news at her farewell and stole her thunder.

  • Skating on thin ice

    MeaningDoing something risky that may get you into trouble.

    Hindiख़तरे से खेलना

    ExampleYou are skating on thin ice by coming late every day this week.

  • Out of the woods

    MeaningOut of danger or difficulty.

    Hindiख़तरे से बाहर होना

    ExampleThe fever is down, but the doctor says he is not out of the woods yet.

  • Beat around the bush

    MeaningTo avoid saying something directly.

    Hindiघुमा-फिराकर बात करना

    ExampleStop beating around the bush and tell me how much it costs.
