Work and effort idioms in English, with Hindi meaning

16 idioms. Each has a plain meaning, the meaning in Hindi and a sentence of our own. Copy one or send it on WhatsApp.

These are the idioms of the classroom, the playing field and the office. They describe how people start a job, how hard they try and how they work together. You will hear them from teachers, coaches and managers, and you will need them in interviews, where a line such as "I am ready to go the extra mile" says a great deal in few words.

Many of them come from older ways of working. "Burn the midnight oil" belongs to the days of oil lamps. "Back to the drawing board" comes from the large boards on which engineers drew their plans. "Learn the ropes" comes from sailing ships, where a new sailor had to learn which rope moved which sail.

Use them honestly. An idiom cannot stand in for a real example. In an interview, say the idiom and then prove it with something you actually did.

  • Burn the midnight oil

    MeaningTo study or work late into the night.

    Hindiदेर रात तक मेहनत करना

    ExampleShe burnt the midnight oil for a month before the entrance test.

  • Go the extra mile

    MeaningTo do more than what is expected.

    Hindiज़रूरत से आगे बढ़कर मेहनत करना

    ExampleOur class teacher goes the extra mile and stays back for weak students.

  • Hit the nail on the head

    MeaningTo say exactly the right thing.

    Hindiबिलकुल सही बात कहना

    ExampleYou hit the nail on the head: the real problem is poor planning.

  • Back to the drawing board

    MeaningTo start planning again because the first plan failed.

    Hindiनए सिरे से योजना बनाना

    ExampleThe model bridge collapsed, so it was back to the drawing board for our team.

  • Get the ball rolling

    MeaningTo start an activity.

    Hindiकाम की शुरुआत करना

    ExampleLet me get the ball rolling with the first question.

  • Pull your socks up

    MeaningTo make an effort to improve.

    Hindiकमर कसना, सुधरने की ठानना

    ExampleYou must pull your socks up if you want to pass in science.

  • Leave no stone unturned

    MeaningTo try every possible way.

    Hindiकोई कसर न छोड़ना

    ExampleThe police left no stone unturned to find the lost child.

  • Learn the ropes

    MeaningTo learn how a job is done.

    Hindiकाम के तौर-तरीके सीखना

    ExampleIt took me a week to learn the ropes at the shop.

  • In the same boat

    MeaningIn the same difficult situation as others.

    Hindiएक ही नाव में सवार होना

    ExampleNone of us has finished the homework. We are all in the same boat.

    MeaningTo depend on only one plan or chance.

    Hindiसब कुछ एक ही दाँव पर लगा देना

    ExampleApply to three colleges. Do not put all your eggs in one basket.

    MeaningTo accept something hard and get on with it.

    Hindiकड़वा घूँट पीकर काम करना

    Hindiछिपा हुआ वरदान

    MeaningIt is now your turn to decide or act.

  • Rome was not built in a day
