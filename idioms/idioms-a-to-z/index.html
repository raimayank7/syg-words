This is the complete index of the site: 184 English idioms in alphabetical order, followed by 162 Hindi muhavare in the order of the Hindi alphabet. Each line shows the saying and a short meaning. Tap it to open its theme page at that entry, where you will find the fuller meaning, the meaning in the other language and an example sentence.

English idioms are sorted by their first main word, ignoring "a" and "the", so "a piece of cake" is under P. If you remember only one word of an idiom, the search box on the home page is quicker: it looks inside the idiom, its reading and its meaning.
