Your name in Urdu

How your name looks in Urdu, how the tool writes it, and what to check.

The result is written by sound and is approximate. No native reader has checked it. Do not use it on official documents; for anything permanent, ask a reader of the script.

Urdu is written from right to left in a form of the Arabic script, in the sloping Nastaliq style. Unlike Hindi, it writes mostly consonants and the long vowels, and leaves short vowels out, so a name in Urdu shows less than the name in Devanagari. A reader who knows the name fills in the missing vowels.

The tool follows that custom. It writes each consonant with its Urdu letter, uses alif, ye and vav for long vowels, writes a final a as the small ہ, and leaves out short vowels in the middle of the name. The result is a plain, readable skeleton, but Urdu spelling of names also depends on the Persian and Arabic forms of the name, which a short tool cannot know. For a gift, a document or a signboard, have a reader check it.

Urdu spelling of names depends on Persian and Arabic forms and on the missing short vowels. This is a first draft, not a final spelling.

Write my name

How the tool writes it

  • Letters are chosen by sound, using the Urdu letters پ, چ, گ and ک where the English sound is p, ch, g or k.
  • Aspirated sounds written "kh", "gh", "th", "dh", "bh", "ph" and "jh" are written with a main letter plus ھ.
  • Long vowels "aa", "ee", "oo" are written with alif, ye and vav. Short vowels in the middle of a word are left out, as Urdu spelling does.
  • A word that starts with a vowel begins with alif, and a final "a" is written as ہ.

How this script was checked

Each consonant checked against the Unicode letter name (for example SHEEN for sh); vowel handling follows the usual practice of leaving out short vowels, which is our rule, not a table. The check covered 23 items; it found no disagreement.

A check like this finds slips in letters and sounds. It cannot say whether a native reader would spell the name this way, so the results stay approximate.

Check it with a reader

Read the result aloud to someone who reads Urdu. If they say the name as you do, the spelling works. If they would write it differently, ask which spelling they prefer and use that. The guide on documents and tattoos says when a check is essential.

Questions

How do I write my name in Urdu?

Type the name on the tool page and read the Urdu card, then have an Urdu reader check the vowels.

Why are some vowels missing?

Urdu spelling leaves out short vowels. They are understood from the word.
