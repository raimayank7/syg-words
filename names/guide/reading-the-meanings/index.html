How to read name meanings: what they can and cannot tell you

The meanings on this site are taken from the English Wiktionary, a dictionary written by volunteers. They tell you what the word means in the language of the name. That is useful, but it is not the same as the meaning a family intends. This page explains the difference.

Word meaning and name meaning

When a name is also an ordinary word, the dictionary gives the word's meaning: Ankush is a goad, a tool used to guide an elephant, and also means control or restraint. The family giving the name Ankush may think of strength, or of a family elder with that name. The dictionary cannot tell you that.

Several senses

Many words have more than one sense, and the first is not always the one parents intend. We show up to three senses and, for a few names, a note on the person or place the name refers to, as given in the dictionary.

Spelling

The same name can be spelled several ways in English letters (Abhay, Abhaya). The spelling shown is the one the dictionary gives first. Use the spelling your family uses.

Origin

When the dictionary states where the name comes from (Sanskrit, Persian, Arabic), we show it. An empty origin means the dictionary does not say, not that the name has none.

When to ask

For a religious or ritual use of a name, ask a priest, imam or granthi. For a family name, ask the oldest relative you can: the story behind a name is part of its meaning.

About the source

The data is from the English Wiktionary and is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. Wiktionary does not cover every name, and some entries are short. If you find a mistake on this site, please tell us, and consider correcting the Wiktionary entry itself.

Questions

Why does a name show a meaning that looks odd?

Because the dictionary gives the meaning of the word, which can have several senses. The sense the family means is usually the first or second.

Is a name with no meaning here meaningless?

No. It only means we found no meaning in the dictionary for that name.
