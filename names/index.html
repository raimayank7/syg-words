    How to use this site

    Type a name or a meaning in the search box: "light" finds every name whose word means light. Or open a language and read down the list. Each card shows the name in its own script, the English spelling, what the word means, and where it comes from. When you have a short list, read the checklist for choosing a name and ask an elder in the family about the customs you may want to follow.
