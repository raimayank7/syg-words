Arabic boy names are usually real words: an adjective, a noun or a verb. Many describe a hoped-for quality, such as noble, bright or generous, and some are the names of prophets or companions in the Islamic tradition.

Wiktionary gives the meaning of the Arabic word, which is the meaning of the name for most families.

Before you choose

  • Check the Latin spelling: Arabic names have several forms in English.
  • Ask a family elder or imam about religious names.
  • Pick a name you can say in your own accent.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Abbas (grim, glowering, frowning, stern, scowling, surly), Adam (human), Adel (just, fair, equitable), Adham (shady, darkened much, almost black), Ahmed (to show oneself praiseworthy). Each card gives the name in its own script and in English letters, the meanings the dictionary gives for the word, a note if the name also refers to a person or place, and the origin if it is stated.
