Arabic girl names often mean beauty, hope, light or a flower: Amal means hope, Bushra means good news. Most are ordinary Arabic words.

Wiktionary lists the meaning of the word; where a verb is given, the name usually comes from its action or its noun.

Before you choose

  • Check the spelling used in English documents.
  • Say it with the surname.
  • Ask an elder if the name is religious in origin.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Abir (a certain mixture of perfumes compounded with saffron), Alia (elevation, highness), Amal (to hope for), Bushra (news), Farah (to be happy, to be glad, to be merry, to be joyful, to exult, to rejoice). Each card gives the name in its own script and in English letters, the meanings the dictionary gives for the word, a note if the name also refers to a person or place, and the origin if it is stated.
