Bengali families often give two names: a daak naam (pet name) used at home and a bhalo naam (good name) used in school and on documents. The good name is usually Sanskrit or Arabic in origin and carries a meaning such as light, river or hope.

The list below shows names that have a Wiktionary entry with a meaning. Bengali spelling varies in English letters, for example Subhas and Shubhash.

Before you choose

  • Decide both names: the official one and the one used at home.
  • Check the English spelling used by relatives.
  • Say it with the surname: Bengali surnames can be long.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Abed (worshipful, devout), Abhishek (anointing), Abir (a hued (originally red), scented dust, especially used during the Holi festival), Adam (man), Aftab (the sun). Each card gives the name in its own script and in English letters, the meanings the dictionary gives for the word, a note if the name also refers to a person or place, and the origin if it is stated. The guide to reading name meanings explains why the dictionary meaning and the family meaning can differ.
