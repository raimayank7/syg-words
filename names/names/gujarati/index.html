Gujarati names are mainly Sanskrit, with some Persian and Arabic names in Muslim and Parsi communities. Many families name children after a god or a virtue, and Gujarati homes often add a family name or father's name after the given name.

The list below covers names with a Wiktionary entry that states a meaning.

Before you choose

  • Check the order used on documents: given name, father's name, surname.
  • Pick a spelling that works in English and Gujarati.
  • Read the meaning, then ask a grandparent for the family story.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Aarti (aarti), Aftab (sun), Akshay (inexhaustible), Ali (female friend), Amir (noble). Each card gives the name in its own script and in English letters, the meanings the dictionary gives for the word, a note if the name also refers to a person or place, and the origin if it is stated. The guide to reading name meanings explains why the dictionary meaning and the family meaning can differ.
