Most Hindi boy names are Sanskrit words that mean a quality, a god, a hero or a hope for the child: fearless, victorious, bright, gentle. Many come from the epics, so a family may name a son after Arjuna, Ram or Angad, and many are plain words, like Ankush (a goad) or Amber (sky).

In many Hindu families the name is chosen at the naming ceremony, the namkaran, held some days after the birth, and some families pick the first letter with the help of a priest or an astrologer. That is a family custom, not a rule, and nothing on this page depends on it.

Before you choose

  • Say the name aloud with the surname: some pairs run together.
  • Check how it reads in English letters: one Hindi name can be spelled Abhay or Abhaya.
  • Look at the meaning below, then ask an elder: the family may have a meaning of its own.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Abhay (fearless), Abhigyan (recognition, remembrance, recollection), Abhijit (conquered), Abhilash (desire, longing), Abhimanyu (Abhimanyu, the son of Arjuna and Subhadra, in Mahabharata). Each card gives the name in its own script and in English letters, the meanings the dictionary gives for the word, a note if the name also refers to a person or place, and the origin if it is stated.
