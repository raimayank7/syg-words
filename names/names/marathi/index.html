Marathi names are mostly Sanskrit and often follow a naming ceremony on the twelfth day, when the child is named by the family. Many families pass down a grandparent's name, and Marathi people often use a middle name for the father.

The list shows names with a Wiktionary entry that states a meaning.

Before you choose

  • Check the use of the father's name as a middle name.
  • Spell it in English the way relatives do.
  • Ask an elder for the family's reason for a name.

Reading this list
