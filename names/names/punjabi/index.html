Punjabi names are given in Hindu, Sikh and Muslim families and so draw on Sanskrit, Gurbani and Arabic or Persian. Sikh families traditionally take the first letter of the name from the hukamnama read at the gurdwara after a birth, and add Singh or Kaur to complete it.

The names listed here are those with a Wiktionary entry that gives a meaning. Many Punjabi names, especially those drawn from Gurbani, are not covered, and the list is short.

Before you choose

  • Check whether the family wants a name that works for a boy or girl with Singh or Kaur.
  • Spelling in Gurmukhi and in English letters may differ.
  • For a Sikh naming, the gurdwara can help with the first letter.
