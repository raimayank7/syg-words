Sanskrit is the source of most Hindu names. A Sanskrit name is usually a compound of two meaningful words, so it carries a whole phrase: Arjuna (bright, clear), Abhimanyu (fearless spirit).

This list is short because only some Sanskrit given names have a Wiktionary entry that states a meaning.

Before you choose

  • Look for compounds: the parts often explain the whole.
  • Check the long-vowel spelling: Sanskrit marks length (a and aa differ).
  • Ask a Sanskrit teacher if you plan to use the name in a ritual.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Arjuna (white; clear; bright), Asita (black, dark), Axar (imperishable, lasting), Bhagiratha (a king from the Ikshvaku dynasty, who is said to have brought the river Ganga from the heavens to the earth), Bhavani (Name of a Hindu goddess associated with goddess Durga, identified in later times with Parvati (she is the wife of Shiva) within the tradition of Shaivism).
