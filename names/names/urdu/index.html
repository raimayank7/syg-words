Urdu names combine Arabic, Persian and Turkic words and are used among Muslims in South Asia. Many mean a virtue or a quality and some carry a reference to the Quran or to a family of the Prophet.

The list below shows names with a Wiktionary entry that states a meaning. Spelling in English letters varies widely (Mohammad, Muhammad, Mohd).

Before you choose

  • Check the preferred English spelling before documents are made.
  • Read the meaning and ask an imam or elder if the name is of religious origin.
  • Say it with the family name.

Reading this list

The first names in this list are Abhishek (sprinkling, anointing), Abid (godly, pious), Adam (man), Aftab (Sun), Akhtar (star). Each card gives the name in its own script and in English letters, the meanings the dictionary gives for the word, a note if the name also refers to a person or place, and the origin if it is stated. The guide to reading name meanings explains why the dictionary meaning and the family meaning can differ.
