A name for the person, not for the list

A nickname works because it belongs to one person and a few others. Use these ideas as a start: read them aloud, shorten one until it is easy and warm to say, and ask the person before you use it. The guide to choosing a nickname gives the method, keeping nicknames kind says what to leave out, and naming a pet covers dogs, cats and small pets.

How the generator works

For each theme we wrote a list of ready names and two lists of words. The generator picks a ready name half the time and a pair of words the other half, so you see new combinations and some of our favourites.

Questions

Are these names free to use?

Yes, they are our own writing. Choose one the person likes.

Can a nickname be changed?

Of course. Many people have several in a lifetime.
