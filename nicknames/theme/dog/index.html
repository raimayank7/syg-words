About dog names

A good dog name is one or two syllables, ends in a clear vowel or a hard sound, and is easy to call across a park. Dogs respond best to names that sound different from your usual commands.

How to choose

  • Choose one or two syllables: Bruno, Leo, Coco.
  • Avoid names that sound like commands, such as "No" or "Sit".
  • Try it in a call across the garden.
  • Use a name you are happy to say in public for fifteen years.

What to avoid

Avoid names that sound like cruelty or a human relative, and avoid famous brand names.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Little, Big, Lucky, Golden, Happy, Brave, Clever, Sunny. Words to join them with: Moti, Sheru, Bruno, Biscuit, Peanut, Mango, Shadow, Buddy. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then shorten it until it is easy and warm to say.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guides on choosing a nickname, keeping nicknames kind and naming a pet explain what to check first.

Questions

What is a good dog name?

One or two syllables, easy to call and different from your commands.

Can I rename an adult dog?

Yes. Use the new name with treats and it usually sticks within a week or two.
