How the finder works

The finder looks up how the word sounds in the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, takes the sounds from the last stressed vowel to the end, and lists the 23,151 words in our list that end with the same sounds and begin that ending differently. Near rhymes keep the vowel and end in a similar sound. See how rhymes work for the rules.

For songs, poems and school

Rhyming for songs and poems shows how writers use perfect and near rhymes, and teaching rhyme is for parents and teachers. The pages of rhyme sounds list every word with a common ending.

Questions

Does it work for Hindi?

No, English only. Hindi rhyme (qafiya) works on different sounds and is not covered here.

Why does a word I know not show?

The list holds about 23,151 common words. Rare words, names and abbreviations are left out; the finder then guesses from how the word ends in spelling and says so.
