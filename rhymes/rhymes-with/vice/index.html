How vice sounds

"vice" has 1 syllable and its rhyming part is the sounds AY (as in "hide"), S (as in "sea") (the capital letters are sound codes used by the dictionary). A perfect rhyme must match all of them and must start the rhyming part with a different sound, which is why nice is a rhyme and not a repeat.

12 of the 17 perfect rhymes have one syllable, which are the easiest to use at the end of a line.

How the rhymes are spelled

The same sound is written in different ways. Among the perfect rhymes for vice, the commonest word endings are "-ice" (13), "-ise" (3). Do not choose a rhyme by its spelling alone: always say both words aloud.

Using a near rhyme

Near rhymes keep the vowel and end in a similar sound (for example m, n and ng, or p and b). Songwriters use them when a perfect rhyme would sound forced. The near rhymes for vice are listed above in the second list.
