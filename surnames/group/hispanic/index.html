This page lists the 100 surnames with the highest share of people who are of Hispanic origin (any race), among surnames held by at least 20,000 people in the 2010 Census (so that the percentages rest on a large enough group). The highest share is for Barajas, at 96.46% of its 45,153 people.

The percentages describe how a group of people with one surname identified themselves in 2010. They do not describe the origin of the name, and they say nothing certain about any individual: a person with any of these surnames may belong to another group. The six groups used here are explained in the guide to the percentages.

Names held by fewer than 20,000 people are left out of this page, which means that very rare surnames with a high share do not appear. To check any surname, use the search.
