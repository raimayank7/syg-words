A checklist for choosing a channel, page or podcast name

A channel name is a small brand. It appears on every video, post and episode, and changing it later can lose followers or confuse people. Use this checklist before you decide. It works for video channels, photo pages, podcasts and Telegram channels.

1. Does it say what you do?

A viewer should be able to guess the topic from the name. "Tadka Tales" hints at cooking, "Circuit Chai" at electronics. A name that gives no clue works only if you plan to promote it heavily.

2. Can people say it and spell it?

When someone hears the name in a video or from a friend, can they find it? Test by saying it to three people and asking them to type it. Avoid unusual spellings, silent letters and symbols.

3. Is it free of trademarks?

Do not use the name of a brand, a film, a song, an app or a game in your channel name. It can lead to the name being taken down. A short search for the name on the app and a general web search will show obvious conflicts. We cannot give legal advice; if the channel will be a business, ask a professional about registering the name.

4. Is it close to a bigger channel?

A name that is one letter away from a famous channel looks like a copy and may be reported. Choose something clearly different.

5. Will it still fit in five years?

Do not put a year, an age, a city or a single product in the name if you may change. A name like "Cooking With Riya" survives a change of recipes; "Riya 2026 Diet Plan" does not.

6. Does the handle match?

Try to use the same handle on the apps you plan to use. If it is taken somewhere, make a small variation that you can use everywhere, instead of three unrelated handles.

7. Is it respectful?

Avoid names that mock groups of people, make health promises or suggest official status. Think of how the name would look in a news story about your channel.

Next step

Choose three names from the lists on this site or from your own ideas, apply the checklist, and ask five people which one they remember best a day later.

Questions

Should the channel name be the same on every app?

It helps people find you. If one app has the name taken, add a short word such as your first name, and use that choice everywhere.

Can I change a channel name later?

Usually yes, but you may lose some recognition. Choose carefully the first time.
