Username Ideas

How to choose a username you will not regret

A username is the name people use to find you, tag you and remember you. You will probably keep it for years, so it is worth five minutes of thought. This guide gives a short method and the mistakes to avoid.

1. Decide where it will be used

A name for a game can be playful. A name for a work profile, a job application or an exam portal should be plain, ideally close to your real name. If you will use one name everywhere, make it the plain one and keep fun names for games.

2. Make it easy to say and spell

Say the name aloud to a friend and ask them to spell it back. If they cannot, simplify. Avoid swapping letters for numbers (such as 4 for A), long strings of underscores, and spellings that look like another word. Names with a rare spelling are hard to type on a phone and hard to find in search.

3. Keep it private

Do not put your phone number, date of birth, address, school or the name of a child in a username. Strangers can see it and it can be used to guess passwords or find you. A year is fine if it is not your birth year.

4. Make it able to grow

Names with a place, a job or an age (for example "Delhi_Student_21") become wrong when life changes. Choose words that stay true: a mood, a colour, an animal, an interest.

5. Check it, and have a backup

Usernames are unique on each app, so the one you want may be taken. We cannot check this for you, and a website that says it can is not showing the app's own records. Open the app and try the name. Have two or three alternatives ready, such as adding a short word, not a long number.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using the same username and password everywhere.
  • Choosing a name that copies a famous person, which can get the account suspended.
  • Putting a joke in a name you will use for work.
  • Making a name so long that it is cut off in a list.

Questions

Should my username be my real name?

For work and study, yes or something close to it. For games and social fun, an invented name is fine and gives more privacy.

What if the username I want is taken?

Add a short meaningful word, change one word or invent a new one. Avoid adding many random numbers.
