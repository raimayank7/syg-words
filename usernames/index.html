Choosing a name that lasts

A good username is short, easy to say and spell, and safe to share. A good channel name tells people what you make. This site gives you a start: pick a theme, read the ideas aloud, change a word, and try the result on the app. Usernames are unique on each app, so the first choice is often taken; have two or three ready. The guide to choosing a username explains the method, username safety lists what to keep out of a name, and the channel name checklist covers YouTube, Instagram and podcasts.

How the generator works

For each theme we wrote a list of ready names and two lists of words. The generator picks a ready name half the time and a pair of words the other half, so you see new combinations and some of our favourites. It does not check whether a name is taken, because only the app can do that.

Questions

Can you tell me if a username is available?

No. Only the app knows. Try the name on the app, and keep two or three alternatives ready.

Are these names free to use?

Yes, they are our own writing. Avoid brand names, famous people and official bodies so that you do not mislead anyone.
