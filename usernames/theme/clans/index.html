About clan and guild names

A clan or guild name is shared by many players, so it should be easy to chant, look good as a short tag and avoid copying anyone. The ideas here are our own and work for any game or community.

How to choose

  • Choose a name and a two to four letter tag that fit together.
  • Pick a theme: animals, weather, mythology, places.
  • Say it in chat: "I am from ..." should sound natural.
  • Agree the name with the whole clan and write the rules in the description.

What to avoid

Avoid copying famous clans, professional teams or brand names. Avoid names with hate or violence.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Iron, Silent, Thunder, Night, Crimson, Desert, Storm, Phoenix, Frost, Shadow, Golden, Royal. Words to join them with: Legion, Order, Pack, Brigade, Council, Wolves, Syndicate, Guard, Clan, Realm, Hawks, Rangers. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

What makes a good clan name?

Easy to say, easy to shorten into a tag, and not copied from an existing clan.

How long should the tag be?

Two to four letters is common, but each game sets its own limit.
