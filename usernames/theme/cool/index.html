About cool usernames

A cool username is short, easy to say and easy to remember, and it still sounds like you in two years. The ideas below use strong words and simple sounds, with no long strings of numbers.

How to choose

  • Say it aloud. If you stumble over it, so will your friends.
  • Two short words usually beat one long one.
  • Use a number only if it means something to you, and not your birth year.
  • Check that the spelling is easy to type on a phone.

What to avoid

Avoid your phone number, birth date, school or home address in a username, and avoid copying the name of a well-known person.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Silver, Night, Blue, Quiet, Iron, Swift, Ghost, Cobalt, Pixel, Solar, Arctic, Lunar. Words to join them with: Falcon, Pilot, Ember, Storm, Leaf, Harbor, River, Wolf, Nomad, Drift, Fox, Raven. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

What makes a username cool?

Short, easy to say and easy to spell, with a picture in it: "SilverFalcon" is clearer than a string of random letters.

Can I check if a username is taken?

Only on the app itself. We cannot check, and no site that does it for you can be fully trusted.
