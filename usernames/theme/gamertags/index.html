About gamer names

A gamer name is your badge in every match. It should look strong on a scoreboard, fit in the space the game allows, and still be easy for teammates to say over voice chat. These ideas are for any game and are our own words.

How to choose

  • Choose a name that is easy to say aloud over voice chat.
  • Use a simple spelling; odd letters make it hard for friends to find you.
  • Keep it short enough to fit a scoreboard.
  • Do not copy a famous streamer or player; it confuses people and some games take action.

What to avoid

Avoid names with abuse or hate, and avoid personal details such as your real full name or phone number.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Silent, Thunder, Shadow, Crimson, Nova, Iron, Arctic, Ghost, Blaze, Steel, Night, Storm. Words to join them with: Scout, Wolf, Strike, Viper, Blade, Phantom, Ranger, Hunter, Falcon, Fury, King, Raven. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

How do I choose a good gamer name?

Short, easy to say, easy to spell and not copied from a streamer.

Can I use these in any game?

Yes, they are only words. Each game has its own length and character rules, so adjust to fit.
