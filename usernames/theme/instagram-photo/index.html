About photography page names

A photography page name should be elegant and simple: a studio or a person, and a light word. It should not compete with the photos, and it should be easy to find when a client asks for the handle.

How to choose

  • Use your name or studio plus the word photography or frames.
  • A short handle looks better under a picture.
  • Light, frame, lens and moment are common words, so add a twist.
  • Say what you shoot: weddings, portraits, nature.

What to avoid

Avoid a name that copies a well-known studio, and avoid claims of awards you do not hold.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Golden, Candid, Quiet, Pure, Little, Sunlit, Nature, Street, Wedding, Memory, Blink, Light. Words to join them with: Frames, Lens, Studio, Stories, Moments, Shutter, Collective, Diary, Makers, Chronicles, Light, Soul. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

Should I use my own name for a photography page?

Yes, for portraits and weddings, clients often search by name. Add the word photography so the page is easy to find.

Is a studio name better?

A studio name looks bigger and can grow into a team.
