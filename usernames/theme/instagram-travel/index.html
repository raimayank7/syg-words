About instagram travel page names

A travel page name should sound like a journey. It should be short enough for a handle, clear enough for strangers, and have room to grow as the places change. Use a word for wandering, the road, or the map.

How to choose

  • Use a word for travel: roam, wander, trail, road, map.
  • A place name limits you; a mood name does not.
  • A handle with fewer symbols is easier to tag.
  • Check for similar pages so you are not mistaken for someone else.

What to avoid

Avoid claiming to be an official tourism board, and avoid brand names.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Wander, Roam, Trail, Road, Backpack, Map, Hills, Passport, Slow, Offbeat, Monsoon, Weekend. Words to join them with: Diaries, Stories, Moments, Trails, Seeker, Journal, Tales, Pages, Gang, Dreams, Nights, Rani. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

Should a travel page name include a place?

Better not, so you can travel anywhere. A mood word like "wander" or "roam" lasts longer.

Can I use a hill station name?

You can mention it, but a general name is more flexible.
