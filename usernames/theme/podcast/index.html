About podcast names

A podcast name is heard before it is read, so it should be easy to say and spell, tell listeners the topic, and stand out in a list. It will go on your cover art and in every conversation about the show.

How to choose

  • Say it aloud, and ask a friend to spell it after hearing it once.
  • A topic word helps listeners find you.
  • Keep it under four words.
  • Search podcast apps for similar names before you commit.

What to avoid

Avoid names of other podcasts or media companies, and avoid claims such as "India's number one".

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Chai, Evening, Small-town, Curious, Slow, Midnight, Sunday, Desi, Unfiltered, Simple, Daily, Real. Words to join them with: Podcast, Adda, Talks, Corner, Hour, Sessions, Baatein, Table, Room, Mic, Stories, Banter. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

What makes a good podcast name?

It is easy to say and spell, hints at the topic and does not copy another show.

Should it be in Hindi or English?

Use the language of the show. A mixed name like "Chai Pe Podcast" suits shows that mix both.
