About vlog and lifestyle channel names

A vlog channel is about a person, so the name usually has a personality in it: a nickname, a place or a way of seeing the day. It should be easy to remember and to say when someone tells a friend about you.

How to choose

  • Use your name, a nickname or a place.
  • Add a word for the type of content: diaries, days, life.
  • Keep it short; viewers will say it aloud.
  • Make sure the name can grow if your videos change.

What to avoid

Avoid anything that gives your home address or your children's names.

Build your own

Words that fit this theme: Daily, Small-town, Weekend, Little, Simple, Humble, Everyday, Slow, Village, City, Student, Family. Words to join them with: Diaries, Days, Stories, Moments, Vlogs, Life, Mornings, Journey, Baatein, Dose, Roam, Notes. Put one from the first list in front of one from the second, then try the result on the app, because the first choice is often taken.

The name generator mixes these words into new ideas, and the guide to choosing a username and username safety explain what to check first.

Questions

Should a vlog channel use my name?

It can, and it builds a personal brand, but a topic name is easier to share if you plan to change your content.

Is it safe to use my city?

A city is usually fine. Avoid your street, building or school.
