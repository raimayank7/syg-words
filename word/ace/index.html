Ace: meaning, synonyms and examples

Ace is used as a noun (6 senses), verb (4 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The smallest whole number or a numeral representing this number.

In Hindi
इक्का; उस्ताद
Synonyms
one, single, unity, adept, champion, sensation, maven, mavin, virtuoso, genius

Ace as a noun

  1. The smallest whole number or a numeral representing this number.

    “He has the one but will need a two and three to go with it.” “They had lunch at one.”

    Synonyms: one, single, unity · A kind of: digit · Kinds: monad, singleton

  2. One of four playing cards in a deck having a single pip on its face.

    A kind of: playing card · Kinds: ace of clubs, ace of diamonds, ace of hearts, ace of spades

  3. Someone who is dazzlingly skilled in any field.

    Synonyms: adept, champion, sensation, maven, mavin, virtuoso, genius, hotshot · A kind of: expert · Kinds: track star

  4. Proteolytic enzyme that converts angiotensin I into angiotensin II.

    Synonyms: angiotensin converting enzyme, angiotensin-converting enzyme · A kind of: protease

  5. A major strategic headquarters of NATO; safeguards an area extending from Norway to Turkey.

    A kind of: headquarters

  6. A serve that the receiver is unable to reach.

    A kind of: serve

Ace as a verb

  1. Succeed at easily.

    “She sailed through her exams.” “You will pass with flying colors.” “She nailed her astrophysics course.”

    Synonyms: breeze through, pass with flying colors, sweep through, sail through, nail · A kind of: pass

  2. Score an ace against.

    “He aced his opponents.”

    A kind of: score

  3. Play (a hole) in one stroke.

    A kind of: play

  4. Serve an ace against (someone).

    A kind of: serve

Ace as an adjective

  1. Of the highest quality.

    “An ace reporter.” “A crack shot.” “A first-rate golfer.”

    Synonyms: crack, first-rate, super, tiptop, topnotch, top-notch, tops · Similar: superior

Phrases and compounds with "ace"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • deuce-ace (noun) The cardinal number that is the sum of one and one and one.
  • ace of clubs (noun) The ace in the club suit.
  • ace of hearts (noun) The ace in the heart suit.
  • ace of spades (noun) The ace in the spade suit; sometimes taken as a portent of death.
  • ace of diamonds (noun) The ace in the diamond suit.
