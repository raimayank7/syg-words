Administration: meaning, synonyms and examples

Administration is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A method of tending to or managing the affairs of a some group of people (especially the group's business affairs).

Administration as a noun

  1. A method of tending to or managing the affairs of a some group of people (especially the group's business affairs).

    Synonyms: disposal · A kind of: management · Kinds: conducting, line management, organization, running, polity

  2. The persons (or committees or departments etc.) who make up a body for the purpose of administering something.

    “He claims that the present administration is corrupt.” “The governance of an association is responsible to its members.” “He quickly became recognized as a member of the establishment.”

    Synonyms: governance, governing body, establishment, brass, organization, organisation · A kind of: body · Kinds: top brass, executive, judiciary, county council, government officials, management

  3. The act of administering medication.

    Synonyms: giving medication · A kind of: medication · Kinds: drip feed

  4. The tenure of a president.

    “Things were quiet during the Eisenhower administration.”

    Synonyms: presidency, presidential term · A kind of: tenure

  5. The act of governing; exercising authority.

    “Regulations for the governing of state prisons.” “He had considerable experience of government.”

    Synonyms: government, governing, governance, government activity · A kind of: social control · Kinds: misgovernment, legislation, trust busting

  6. The act of meting out justice according to the law.

    Synonyms: judicature · A kind of: justice

Phrases and compounds with "administration"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bush administration (noun) The executive under President George H. W. Bush.
  • carter administration (noun) The executive under President Carter.
  • reagan administration (noun) The executive under President Reagan.
  • clinton administration (noun) The executive under President Clinton.
  • letters of administration (noun) Legal document naming someone to administer an estate when no executor has been named.
  • technology administration (noun) An agency in the Department of Commerce that works with United States industries to promote competitiveness and maximize the impact of technology on economic growth.
  • small business administration (noun) An independent agency of the United States government that protects the interests of small businesses and ensures that they receive a fair share of government contracts.
  • federal housing administration (noun) The federal agency in the Department of Housing and Urban Development that insures residential mortgages.
  • social security administration (noun) An independent government agency responsible for the Social Security system.
  • general services administration (noun) A central management agency that sets Federal policy for Federal procurement and real property management and information resources management.
  • master in business administration (noun) A master's degree in business.
  • transportation security administration (noun) An agency established in 2001 to safeguard United States transportation systems and insure safe air travel.
  • national archives and records administration (noun) The independent agency that oversees management of federal government records including presidential libraries and historic collections.
  • national aeronautics and space administration (noun) An independent agency of the United States government responsible for aviation and spaceflight.
  • occupational safety and health administration (noun) A government agency in the Department of Labor to maintain a safe and healthy work environment.
  • national oceanic and atmospheric administration (noun) An agency in the Department of Commerce that maps the oceans and conserves their living resources; predicts changes to the earth's environment; provides weather reports and forecasts floods and hurricanes and other natural disasters related to weather.
