Advance: meaning, synonyms and examples

Advance is used as a verb (12 senses), noun (6 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move forward, also in the metaphorical sense.

In Hindi
आगे बढ़ना; अग्रिम
Synonyms
progress, pass on, move on, march on, go on, throw out, boost, supercharge, promote, further
Opposites
recede, back, retreat
Word family
advancement, advancer

Advance as a verb

  1. Move forward, also in the metaphorical sense.

    “Time marches on.”

    Synonyms: progress, pass on, move on, march on, go on · Opposite: recede · A kind of: travel · Kinds: forge, penetrate, creep up, encroach, press on, string, pass, close in

  2. Bring forward for consideration or acceptance.

    “Advance an argument.”

    Synonyms: throw out · A kind of: propose

  3. Increase or raise.

    “Boost the voltage in an electrical circuit.”

    Synonyms: boost, supercharge · A kind of: increase

  4. Contribute to the progress or growth of.

    “I am promoting the use of computers in the classroom.”

    Synonyms: promote, boost, further, encourage · A kind of: support · Kinds: connive at, foster, spur, help, carry, feed, contribute

  5. Cause to move forward.

    “Can you move the car seat forward?”

    Synonyms: bring forward · Opposite: back · A kind of: move · Kinds: nose

  6. Obtain advantages, such as points, etc.

    “The home team was gaining ground.” “After defeating the Knicks, the Blazers pulled ahead of the Lakers in the battle for the number-one playoff berth in the Western Conference.”

    Synonyms: gain, win, pull ahead, make headway, get ahead, gain ground · Kinds: steal, score

  7. Develop in a positive way.

    “He progressed well in school.” “My plants are coming along.” “Plans are shaping up.”

    Synonyms: progress, come on, come along, get on, get along, shape up · A kind of: develop · Kinds: climb, leapfrog

  8. Develop further.

    “We are advancing technology every day.”

    A kind of: better

  9. Give a promotion to or assign to a higher position.

    “John was kicked upstairs when a replacement was hired.” “Women tend not to advance in the major law firms.” “I got promoted after many years of hard work.”

    Synonyms: promote, upgrade, kick upstairs, raise, elevate · A kind of: delegate · Kinds: tenure, bring up, spot promote, ennoble, prefer, brevet

  10. Pay in advance.

    “Can you advance me some money?”

    A kind of: lend

  11. Move forward.

    “We have to advance clocks and watches when we travel eastward.”

    Synonyms: set ahead · A kind of: set

  12. Rise in rate or price.

    “The stock market gained 24 points today.”

    Synonyms: gain · A kind of: wax

Advance as a noun

  1. A movement forward.

    “He listened for the progress of the troops.”

    Synonyms: progress, progression · A kind of: change of location · Kinds: headway

  2. A change for the better; progress in development.

    Synonyms: improvement, betterment · A kind of: transformation · Kinds: revival, refinement, adjustment, conservation, recovery

  3. A tentative suggestion designed to elicit the reactions of others.

    “She rejected his advances.”

    Synonyms: overture, approach, feeler · A kind of: suggestion

  4. The act of moving forward (as toward a goal).

    Synonyms: progress, progression, procession, advancement, forward motion, onward motion · Opposite: retreat · A kind of: motion · Kinds: push, career, march, plain sailing, leapfrog

  5. An amount paid before it is earned.

    Synonyms: cash advance · A kind of: sum

  6. Increase in price or value.

    “The news caused a general advance on the stock market.”

    Synonyms: rise · A kind of: increase

Advance as an adjective

  1. Being ahead of time or need.

    “Gave advance warning.” “Was beforehand with her report.”

    Synonyms: beforehand · Similar: early

  2. Situated ahead or going before.

    “An advance party.” “At that time the most advanced outpost was still east of the Rockies.”

    Synonyms: advanced, in advance · Similar: front

Phrases and compounds with "advance"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in advance (adverb) Ahead of time; in anticipation.
  • cash advance (noun) An amount paid before it is earned.
  • film advance (noun) A mechanism for advancing film in a camera or projector.
  • spark advance (noun) The timing of ignition relative to the position of the piston in an internal-combustion engine.
  • patterned advance (noun) A series with a definite pattern of advance.
  • annuity in advance (noun) An annuity paid in a series of more or less equal payments at the beginning of equally spaced periods.
  • advance death benefit (noun) A percentage of death benefits paid directly to policy holders having a short life expectancy (usually 6 months).
