Age: meaning, synonyms and examples

Age is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: How long something has existed.

In Hindi
उम्र; युग
Synonyms
historic period, eld, long time, years, old age, geezerhood, senesce, get on, mature, maturate
Opposites
rejuvenate

Age as a noun

  1. How long something has existed.

    “It was replaced because of its age.”

    A kind of: property · Kinds: chronological age, bone age, developmental age, fetal age, mental age, oldness, newness, oldness

  2. An era of history having some distinctive feature.

    “We live in a litigious age.”

    Synonyms: historic period · A kind of: era · Kinds: antiquity, golden age, reign, turn of the century

  3. A time of life (usually defined in years) at which some particular qualification or power arises.

    “She was now of school age.” “Tall for his eld.”

    Synonyms: eld · A kind of: time of life · Kinds: majority, minority, voting age

  4. A prolonged period of time.

    “We've known each other for ages.” “I haven't been there for years and years.”

    Synonyms: long time, years · A kind of: time period · Kinds: eon, blue moon, year dot

  5. A late time of life.

    “Old age is not for sissies.” “He's showing his years.” “Age hasn't slowed him down at all.”

    Synonyms: old age, years, eld, geezerhood · A kind of: time of life · Kinds: dotage

Age as a verb

  1. Begin to seem older; get older.

    “The death of his wife caused him to age fast.”

    A kind of: develop

  2. Grow old or older.

    “She aged gracefully.” “We age every day--what a depressing thought!” “Young men senesce.”

    Synonyms: senesce, get on, mature, maturate · A kind of: develop · Kinds: turn, fossilize, dote

  3. Make older.

    “The death of his child aged him tremendously.”

    Opposite: rejuvenate · A kind of: change

Phrases and compounds with "age"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • of age (adjective) Having attained a specific age; (`aged' is pronounced as one syllable).
  • age-old (adjective) Belonging to or lasting from times long ago.
  • ice age (noun) Any period of time during which glaciers covered a large part of the earth's surface.
  • old age (noun) A late time of life.
  • age norm (noun) The average age at which particular performances are expected to appear.
  • bone age (noun) A person's age measured by matching their bone development (as shown by X rays) with bone development of an average person of known chronological age.
  • iron age (noun) (archeology) the period following the Bronze Age; characterized by rapid spread of iron tools and weapons.
  • jazz age (noun) The 1920s in the United States characterized in the novels of F. Scott Fitzgerald as a period of wealth, youthful exuberance, and carefree hedonism.
  • age class (noun) People in the same age range.
  • age group (noun) A group of people having approximately the same age.
  • age limit (noun) Regulation establishing the maximum age for doing something or holding some position.
  • fetal age (noun) The age of an embryo counting from the time of fertilization.
  • legal age (noun) The age at which persons are considered competent to manage their own affairs.
  • space age (noun) The age beginning with the first space travel; from 1957 to the present.
  • stone age (noun) (archeology) the earliest known period of human culture, characterized by the use of stone implements.
  • age of man (noun) Last 2 million years.
