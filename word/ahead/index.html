Ahead: meaning, synonyms and examples

Ahead is used as an adverb (7 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: At or in the front.

In Hindi
आगे
Synonyms
in front, before, forward, onward, onwards, forwards, forrader, in advance, beforehand, out front
Opposites
back

Ahead as an adverb

  1. At or in the front.

    “I see the lights of a town ahead.” “The road ahead is foggy.” “Staring straight ahead.”

    Synonyms: in front, before

  2. Toward the future; forward in time.

    “I like to look ahead in imagination to what the future may bring.” “I look forward to seeing you.”

    Synonyms: forward · Opposite: back

  3. In a forward direction.

    “Go ahead.” “The train moved ahead slowly.” “The boat lurched ahead.”

    Synonyms: onward, onwards, forward, forwards, forrader

  4. Ahead of time; in anticipation.

    “When you pay ahead (or in advance) you receive a discount.” “We like to plan ahead.” “Should have made reservations beforehand.”

    Synonyms: in advance, beforehand

  5. To a more advanced or advantageous position.

    “A young man sure to get ahead.” “Pushing talented students ahead.”

  6. To a different or a more advanced time (meaning advanced either toward the present or toward the future).

    “Moved the appointment ahead from Tuesday to Monday.” “Pushed the deadline ahead from Tuesday to Wednesday.”

  7. Leading or ahead in a competition.

    “The horse was three lengths ahead going into the home stretch.” “Ahead by two pawns.” “Our candidate is in the lead in the polls.”

    Synonyms: out front, in the lead

Ahead as an adjective

  1. Having the leading position or higher score in a contest.

    “He is ahead by a pawn.” “The leading team in the pennant race.”

    Synonyms: in the lead, leading · Similar: up

Phrases and compounds with "ahead"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go ahead (verb) Proceed (with a plan of action).
  • go-ahead (noun) A signal to proceed.
  • get ahead (verb) Obtain advantages, such as points, etc.
  • set ahead (verb) Move forward.
  • dead ahead (adverb) Exactly ahead or in front.
  • plow ahead (verb) Proceed (with a plan of action).
  • pull ahead (verb) Obtain advantages, such as points, etc.
  • thrust ahead (verb) Push one's way.
  • ahead of time (adverb) Before the usual time or the time expected.
  • ahead of the game (adverb) In an advantageous position.
