Ask: meaning, synonyms and examples

Ask is used as a verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Inquire about.

In Hindi
पूछना, माँगना
Synonyms
inquire, enquire, require, expect, necessitate, postulate, need, take, involve, call for
Word family
asker

Ask as a verb

  1. Inquire about.

    “I asked about their special today.” “He had to ask directions several times.”

    Synonyms: inquire, enquire · A kind of: communicate · Kinds: pry, consult

  2. Make a request or demand for something to somebody.

    “She asked him for a loan.”

    A kind of: request · Kinds: request, solicit

  3. Direct or put; seek an answer to.

    “Ask a question.”

    A kind of: give voice

  4. Consider obligatory; request and expect.

    “We require our secretary to be on time.” “Aren't we asking too much of these children?” “I expect my students to arrive in time for their lessons.”

    Synonyms: require, expect · A kind of: demand · Kinds: call

  5. Address a question to and expect an answer from.

    “Ask your teacher about trigonometry.” “The children asked me about their dead grandmother.”

    A kind of: address · Kinds: question, interrogate

  6. Require as useful, just, or proper.

    “It takes nerve to do what she did.” “Success usually requires hard work.” “This job asks a lot of patience and skill.”

    Synonyms: necessitate, postulate, need, require, take, involve, call for, demand · Kinds: claim, govern, draw, cost, cry out for, compel

  7. Require or ask for as a price or condition.

    “He is asking $200 for the table.” “The kidnappers are asking a million dollars in return for the release of their hostage.”

    A kind of: demand

Phrases and compounds with "ask"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • ask in (verb) Ask to enter.
  • ask for (verb) Increase the likelihood of.
  • ask out (verb) Make a date.
  • ask over (verb) Invite someone to one's house.
  • ask round (verb) Invite someone to one's house.
  • ask for it (verb) Persist with actions or an attitude despite the probability that it will cause trouble.
  • ask jeeves (noun) A widely used search engine accepting plain English questions or phrases or terms.
  • ask for trouble (verb) Persist with actions or an attitude despite the probability that it will cause trouble.
