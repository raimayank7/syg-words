Away: meaning, synonyms and examples

Away is used as an adverb (11 senses) and adjective (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: From a particular thing or place or position (`forth' is obsolete).

In Hindi
दूर
Synonyms
off, forth, out, aside, by, outside
Opposites
home
Word family
awayness

Away as an adverb

  1. From a particular thing or place or position (`forth' is obsolete).

    “Ran away from the lion.” “Wanted to get away from there.” “Sent the children away to boarding school.”

    Synonyms: off, forth

  2. From one's possession.

    “He gave out money to the poor.” “Gave away the tickets.”

    Synonyms: out

  3. Out of the way (especially away from one's thoughts).

    “Brush the objections aside.” “Pushed all doubts away.”

    Synonyms: aside

  4. Out of existence.

    “The music faded away.” “Tried to explain away the affair of the letter.” “Idled the hours away.”

  5. At a distance in space or time.

    “The boat was 5 miles off (or away).” “The party is still 2 weeks off (or away).” “Away back in the 18th century.”

    Synonyms: off

  6. Indicating continuing action; continuously or steadily.

    “He worked away at the project for more than a year.” “The child kept hammering away as if his life depended on it.”

  7. So as to be removed or gotten rid of.

    “Cleared the mess away.” “The rotted wood had to be cut away.”

  8. Freely or at will.

    “Fire away!”

  9. In or into a proper place (especially for storage or safekeeping).

    “Put the toys away.” “Her jewels are locked away in a safe.” “Filed the letter away.”

  10. In a different direction.

    “Turn aside.” “Turn away one's face.” “Glanced away.”

    Synonyms: aside

  11. In reserve; not for immediate use.

    “Started setting aside money to buy a car.” “Put something by for her old age.” “Has a nest egg tucked away for a rainy day.”

    Synonyms: aside, by

Away as an adjective

  1. Not present; having left.

    “He's away right now.” “You must not allow a stranger into the house when your mother is away.”

    Similar: absent

  2. Used of an opponent's ground.

    “An away game.”

    Opposite: home

  3. (of a baseball pitch) on the far side of home plate from the batter.

    “The pitch was away (or wide).” “An outside pitch.”

    Synonyms: outside · Similar: inaccurate

Phrases and compounds with "away"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go away (verb) Move away from a place into another direction.
  • cut away (verb) Move quickly to another scene or focus when filming.
  • die away (verb) Become less in amount or intensity.
  • eat away (verb) Remove soil or rock.
  • ebb away (verb) Flow back or recede.
  • get away (verb) Run away from confinement.
  • lay away (verb) Save up as for future use.
  • peg away (verb) Work doggedly or persistently.
  • put away (verb) Place in a place where something cannot be removed or someone cannot escape.
  • run away (verb) Flee; take to one's heels; cut and run.
  • yap away (verb) Talk incessantly and tiresomely.
  • away game (noun) A game played away from home.
  • back away (verb) Make a retreat from an earlier commitment or activity.
  • bear away (verb) Remove from a certain place, environment, or mental or emotional state; transport into a new location or state.
  • cart away (verb) Take away by means of a vehicle.
  • cast away (verb) Throw or cast away.
