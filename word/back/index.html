Back: meaning, synonyms and examples

Back is used as an adverb (6 senses), noun (9 senses), verb (9 senses) and adjective (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: In or to or toward a former location.

In Hindi
पीठ; पीछे, वापस
Synonyms
backward, backwards, rearward, rearwards, dorsum, rear, spinal column, vertebral column, spine, backbone
Opposites
ahead, front, advance, veer
Word family
backing, backer

Back as an adverb

  1. In or to or toward a former location.

    “She went back to her parents' house.”

  2. At or to or toward the back or rear.

    “He moved back.” “Tripped when he stepped backward.” “She looked rearward out the window of the car.”

    Synonyms: backward, backwards, rearward, rearwards

  3. In or to or toward an original condition.

    “He went back to sleep.”

  4. In or to or toward a past time.

    “Set the clocks back an hour.” “Never look back.” “Lovers of the past looking fondly backward.”

    Synonyms: backward · Opposite: ahead

  5. In reply.

    “He wrote back three days later.”

  6. In repayment or retaliation.

    “We paid back everything we had borrowed.” “He hit me and I hit him back.” “I was kept in after school for talking back to the teacher.”

Back as a noun

  1. The posterior part of a human (or animal) body from the neck to the end of the spine.

    “His back was nicely tanned.”

    Synonyms: dorsum · A kind of: body part

  2. The side that goes last or is not normally seen.

    “He wrote the date on the back of the photograph.”

    Synonyms: rear · A kind of: side · Kinds: stern, tail

  3. The part of something that is furthest from the normal viewer.

    “He stood at the back of the stage.” “It was hidden in the rear of the store.”

    Synonyms: rear · Opposite: front · A kind of: position

  4. (football) a person who plays in the backfield.

    A kind of: football player · Kinds: flanker back, quarterback, tailback, wingback

  5. The series of vertebrae forming the axis of the skeleton and protecting the spinal cord.

    “The fall broke his back.”

    Synonyms: spinal column, vertebral column, spine, backbone, rachis · A kind of: skeletal structure · Kinds: notochord, chine

  6. The protective covering on the front, back, and spine of a book.

    “The book had a leather binding.”

    Synonyms: binding, book binding, cover · A kind of: protective covering · Kinds: half binding, three-quarter binding

  7. The part of a garment that covers the back of your body.

    “They pinned a `kick me' sign on his back.”

    A kind of: cloth covering

  8. A support that you can lean against while sitting.

    “The back of the dental chair was adjustable.”

    Synonyms: backrest · A kind of: support · Kinds: cantle, ladder-back

  9. (American football) the position of a player on a football team who is stationed behind the line of scrimmage.

    A kind of: position · Kinds: linebacker, fullback, halfback

Back as a verb

  1. Be behind; approve of.

    “He plumped for the Labor Party.” “I backed Kennedy in 1960.”

    Synonyms: endorse, indorse, plump for, plunk for, support · A kind of: approve · Kinds: guarantee, champion

  2. Travel backward.

    “Back into the driveway.” “The car backed up and hit the tree.”

    A kind of: travel · Kinds: back out

  3. Give support or one's approval to.

    “I'll second that motion.” “I can't back this plan.” “Endorse a new project.”

    Synonyms: second, endorse, indorse · A kind of: support

  4. Cause to travel backward.

    “Back the car into the parking spot.”

    Opposite: advance

  5. Support financial backing for.

    “Back this enterprise.”

    A kind of: finance

  6. Be in back of.

    “My garage backs their yard.”

    Opposite: front · A kind of: lie

  7. Shift to a counterclockwise direction.

    “The wind backed.”

    Opposite: veer · A kind of: switch

  8. Establish as valid or genuine.

    “Can you back up your claims?”

    Synonyms: back up · A kind of: confirm

  9. Strengthen by providing with a back or backing.

    A kind of: strengthen

Back as an adjective

  1. Related to or located at the back.

    “The back yard.” “The back entrance.”

    Opposite: front · Similar: backmost, rear

  2. Located at or near the back of an animal.

    “Back (or hind) legs.” “The hinder part of a carcass.”

    Synonyms: hind, hinder · Similar: posterior

  3. Of an earlier date.

    “Back issues of the magazine.”

    Similar: noncurrent

Phrases and compounds with "back"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • back up (verb) Give moral or psychological support, aid, or courage to.
  • go back (verb) Belong to an earlier time.
  • back end (noun) The side of an object that is opposite its front.
  • back off (verb) Move backwards from a certain position.
  • back out (verb) Move out of a space backwards.
  • back saw (noun) A handsaw that is stiffened by metal reinforcement along the upper edge.
  • buy back (verb) Buy what had previously been sold, lost, or given away.
  • cut back (verb) Return in time.
  • get back (verb) Recover something or somebody that appeared to be lost.
  • pay back (verb) Act or give recompense in recognition of someone's behavior or actions.
  • put back (verb) Put something back where it belongs.
  • set back (verb) Hold back to a later time.
  • set-back (noun) Structure where a wall or building narrows abruptly.
  • sit back (verb) Settle into a comfortable sitting position.
  • win back (verb) Recover something or somebody that appeared to be lost.
  • back away (verb) Make a retreat from an earlier commitment or activity.
