Ball: meaning, synonyms and examples

Ball is used as a noun (12 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Round object that is hit or thrown or kicked in games.

In Hindi
गेंद
Synonyms
musket ball, globe, orb, testis, testicle, orchis, ballock, bollock, nut, egg

Ball as a noun

  1. Round object that is hit or thrown or kicked in games.

    “The ball travelled 90 mph on his serve.” “The mayor threw out the first ball.” “The ball rolled into the corner pocket.”

    A kind of: game equipment · Kinds: baseball, basketball, billiard ball, bocce ball, bowl, bowling ball, cricket ball, croquet ball

  2. A solid projectile that is shot by a musket.

    “They had to carry a ramrod as well as powder and ball.”

    Synonyms: musket ball · A kind of: shot

  3. An object with a spherical shape.

    “A ball of fire.”

    Synonyms: globe, orb · A kind of: sphere · Kinds: crystal ball, mothball, time-ball, fireball, fireball, globule, spherule, pellet

  4. The people assembled at a lavish formal dance.

    “The ball was already emptying out before the fire alarm sounded.”

    A kind of: dance

  5. One of the two male reproductive glands that produce spermatozoa and secrete androgens.

    “She kicked him in the balls and got away.”

    Synonyms: testis, testicle, orchis, ballock, bollock, nut, egg · A kind of: male reproductive gland · Kinds: cobblers, undescended testis

  6. A spherical object used as a plaything.

    “He played with his rubber ball in the bathtub.”

    A kind of: plaything · Kinds: beach ball, snowball

  7. United States comedienne best known as the star of a popular television program (1911-1989).

  8. A compact mass.

    “A ball of mud caught him on the shoulder.”

    Synonyms: clod, glob, lump, clump, chunk · A kind of: agglomeration · Kinds: clot, gob, clew

  9. A lavish dance requiring formal attire.

    Synonyms: formal · A kind of: dance · Kinds: cotillion, masked ball, promenade

  10. A more or less rounded anatomical body or mass.

    “The ball at the base of the thumb.” “He stood on the balls of his feet.”

    A kind of: structure

  11. The game of baseball.

    A kind of: baseball · Kinds: daisy cutter

  12. A pitch that is not in the strike zone.

    “He threw nine straight balls before the manager yanked him.”

    A kind of: pitch

Ball as a verb

  1. Form into a ball by winding or rolling.

    “Ball wool.”

    A kind of: wind

Phrases and compounds with "ball"

  • ball up (verb) Make a mess of, destroy or ruin.
  • no ball (noun) Unlawfully delivered ball in cricket.
  • ball boy (noun) A boy who retrieves balls for tennis players.
  • cue ball (noun) The ball that the billiard player or pool player strikes with his cue.
  • fly ball (noun) (baseball) a hit that flies up in the air.
  • gum ball (noun) A ball of chewing gum with a coating of colored sugar.
  • net ball (noun) A serve that strikes the net before falling into the receiver's court; the ball must be served again.
  • tea ball (noun) A kitchen utensil consisting of a perforated metal ball for making tea.
  • ball club (noun) A team of professional baseball players who play and travel together.
  • ball cock (noun) Floating ball that controls level in a water tank.
  • ball fern (noun) Feathery fern of tropical Asia and Malaysia.
  • ball game (noun) A field game played with a ball (especially baseball).
  • ball gown (noun) The most formal gown; worn to a ball.
  • ball hawk (noun) A team athlete who is skilled at stealing or catching the ball.
  • ball over (verb) Surprise greatly; knock someone's socks off.
  • dirt ball (noun) A person who has a nasty or unethical character undeserving of respect.
