Band: meaning, synonyms and examples

Band is used as a noun (13 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An unofficial association of people or groups.

In Hindi
पट्टी; बैंड
Synonyms
set, circle, lot, banding, stria, striation, stripe, dance band, dance orchestra, isthmus

Band as a noun

  1. An unofficial association of people or groups.

    “The smart set goes there.” “They were an angry lot.”

    Synonyms: set, circle, lot · A kind of: social group · Kinds: car pool, clique, cohort, conspiracy, horsy set, jet set, party

  2. Instrumentalists not including string players.

    A kind of: musical organization · Kinds: marching band, brass band, concert band

  3. A stripe or stripes of contrasting color.

    “Chromosomes exhibit characteristic bands.” “The black and yellow banding of bees and wasps.”

    Synonyms: banding, stria, striation · A kind of: stripe · Kinds: collar

  4. An adornment consisting of a strip of a contrasting color or material.

    Synonyms: banding, stripe · A kind of: adornment · Kinds: cigar band, hatband, neckband, pinstripe

  5. A group of musicians playing popular music for dancing.

    Synonyms: dance band, dance orchestra · A kind of: musical organization · Kinds: big band, jazz band, rock group, steel band

  6. A range of frequencies between two limits.

    A kind of: range · Kinds: frequency band

  7. A thin flat strip of flexible material that is worn around the body or one of the limbs (especially to decorate the body).

    A kind of: strip · Kinds: armband, armlet, backband, bellyband, collar, collar, garter, girdle

  8. A cord-like tissue connecting two larger parts of an anatomical structure.

    Synonyms: isthmus · A kind of: tissue

  9. Jewelry consisting of a circlet of precious metal (often set with jewels) worn on the finger.

    “She had rings on every finger.” “He noted that she wore a wedding band.”

    Synonyms: ring · A kind of: jewelry · Kinds: annulet, engagement ring, mourning ring, ringlet, signet ring, wedding ring

  10. A driving belt in machinery.

    A kind of: belt

  11. A thin flat strip or loop of flexible material that goes around or over something else, typically to hold it together or as a decoration.

    A kind of: strip, loop

  12. A strip of material attached to the leg of a bird to identify it (as in studies of bird migration).

    Synonyms: ring · A kind of: strip

  13. A restraint put around something to hold it together.

    A kind of: restraint · Kinds: brake band, collet, ligature, withe

Band as a verb

  1. Bind or tie together, as with a band.

    A kind of: tie

  2. Attach a ring to the foot of, in order to identify.

    “Ring birds.” “Band the geese to observe their migratory patterns.”

    Synonyms: ring · A kind of: attach

Phrases and compounds with "band"

  • jazz band (noun) A small band of jazz musicians.
  • rock band (noun) A band of musicians who play rock'n'roll music.
  • brake band (noun) A band that can be tightened around a shaft to stop its rotation.
  • brass band (noun) A group of musicians playing only brass and percussion instruments.
  • cigar band (noun) A narrow paper band around a cigar.
  • dance band (noun) A group of musicians playing popular music for dancing.
  • steel band (noun) A band that plays instruments made from the heads of oil drums (Caribbean Islands).

  • wrist band (noun) A band or bracelet worn around the wrist.
  • rubber band (noun) A narrow band of elastic rubber used to hold things (such as papers) together.
  • band oneself (verb) Attach oneself to a group.
