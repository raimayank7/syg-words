Bear: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bear is used as a verb (12 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Have.

In Hindi
भालू; सहना
Synonyms
give birth, deliver, birth, have, digest, endure, stick out, stomach, stand, tolerate
Opposites
bull
Word family
bearable, bearer, bearing

Bear as a verb

  1. Have.

    “Bear a resemblance.” “Bear a signature.”

    A kind of: have · Kinds: carry

  2. Cause to be born.

    “My wife had twins yesterday!”

    Synonyms: give birth, deliver, birth, have · A kind of: produce · Kinds: twin, drop, foal, cub, kitten, lamb, litter, whelp

  3. Put up with something or somebody unpleasant.

    “I cannot bear his constant criticism.” “The new secretary had to endure a lot of unprofessional remarks.” “He learned to tolerate the heat.”

    Synonyms: digest, endure, stick out, stomach, stand, tolerate, support, brook · A kind of: permit · Kinds: accept, stand for, bear up, take lying down, take a joke, sit out, pay

  4. Move while holding up or supporting.

    “Bear gifts.” “Bear a heavy load.” “Bear news.”

    A kind of: transport · Kinds: frogmarch

  5. Bring forth, "The apple tree bore delicious apples this year".

    “The apple tree bore delicious apples this year.” “The unidentified plant bore gorgeous flowers.”

    Synonyms: turn out · A kind of: make · Kinds: spin off, seed, crop, overbear, fruit

  6. Take on as one's own the expenses or debts of another person.

    “I'll accept the charges.” “She agreed to bear the responsibility.”

    Synonyms: take over, accept, assume · A kind of: take · Kinds: face the music

  7. Contain or hold; have within.

    “The canteen holds fresh water.” “This can contains water.”

    Synonyms: hold, carry, contain · A kind of: include · Kinds: retain, enclose

  8. Bring in.

    “Interest-bearing accounts.” “How much does this savings certificate pay annually?”

    Synonyms: yield, pay · A kind of: gain · Kinds: net, pay off

  9. Have on one's person.

    “He wore a red ribbon.” “Bear a scar.”

    Synonyms: wear · A kind of: have

  10. Behave in a certain manner.

    “She carried herself well.” “He bore himself with dignity.” “They conducted themselves well during these difficult times.”

    Synonyms: behave, acquit, deport, conduct, comport, carry · A kind of: hold, act · Kinds: fluster, assert, deal, walk around, pose

  11. Have rightfully; of rights, titles, and offices.

    “She bears the title of Duchess.” “He held the governorship for almost a decade.”

    Synonyms: hold · A kind of: have

  12. Support or hold in a certain manner.

    “She holds her head high.” “He carried himself upright.”

    Synonyms: hold, carry · Kinds: sling, stoop, piggyback, poise, behave

Bear as a noun

  1. Massive plantigrade carnivorous or omnivorous mammals with long shaggy coats and strong claws.

    A kind of: carnivore · Kinds: bear cub, brown bear, bruin, ice bear, sloth bear

  2. An investor with a pessimistic market outlook; an investor who expects prices to fall and so sells now in order to buy later at a lower price.

    Opposite: bull · A kind of: investor
