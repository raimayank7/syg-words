Bed: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bed is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A piece of furniture that provides a place to sleep.

In Hindi
बिस्तर, पलंग
Synonyms
bottom, seam, layer, go to bed, turn in, crawl in, kip down, hit the hay, hit the sack, sack out

Bed as a noun

  1. A piece of furniture that provides a place to sleep.

    “He sat on the edge of the bed.” “The room had only a bed and chair.”

    A kind of: bedroom furniture · Kinds: berth, built-in bed, bunk, bunk bed, cot, couch, deathbed, double bed

  2. A plot of ground in which plants are growing.

    “The gardener planted a bed of roses.”

    A kind of: plot · Kinds: asparagus bed, flowerbed, hotbed, seedbed, turnip bed

  3. A depression forming the ground under a body of water.

    “He searched for treasure on the ocean bed.”

    Synonyms: bottom · A kind of: natural depression · Kinds: lake bed, ocean floor, riverbed, streambed

  4. (geology) a stratum of rock (especially sedimentary rock).

    “They found a bed of sandstone.”

    A kind of: stratum · Kinds: ore bed

  5. A stratum of ore or coal thick enough to be mined with profit.

    “He worked in the coal beds.”

    Synonyms: seam · A kind of: stratum · Kinds: coal seam

  6. Single thickness of usually some homogeneous substance.

    “Slices of hard-boiled egg on a bed of spinach.”

    Synonyms: layer · A kind of: artifact · Kinds: backing, blanket, course, cushion, interlayer, lift, overlay, ply

  7. The flat surface of a printing press on which the type form is laid in the last stage of producing a newspaper or magazine or book etc.

    A kind of: surface

  8. A foundation of earth or rock supporting a road or railroad track.

    “The track bed had washed away.”

    A kind of: foundation · Kinds: railroad bed, roadbed

Bed as a verb

  1. Furnish with a bed.

    “The inn keeper could bed all the new arrivals.”

    A kind of: supply · Kinds: bunk

  2. Place (plants) in a prepared bed of soil.

    A kind of: plant

  3. Put to bed.

    “The children were bedded at ten o'clock.”

    A kind of: put

  4. Prepare for sleep.

    “I usually turn in at midnight.” “He goes to bed at the crack of dawn.”

    Synonyms: go to bed, turn in, crawl in, kip down, hit the hay, hit the sack, sack out, go to sleep · Kinds: bed down

  • bed down (verb) Go to bed.

  • bunk bed (noun) Beds built one above the other.
  • camp bed (noun) A small bed that folds up for storage or transport.

  • rose bed (noun) A flower bed in which roses are growing.
  • sofa bed (noun) A sofa that can be converted into a bed.

  • bed check (noun) A check that everyone is in bed by the time they should be.
  • bed cover (noun) Decorative cover for a bed.
