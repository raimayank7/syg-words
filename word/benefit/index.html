Benefit: meaning, synonyms and examples

Benefit is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Financial assistance in time of need.

In Hindi
लाभ, फ़ायदा
Synonyms
welfare, profit, gain, do good
Word family
beneficial, beneficent

Benefit as a noun

  1. Financial assistance in time of need.

    A kind of: payment · Kinds: stock option, cost-of-living benefit, death benefit, disability benefit, sick benefit, fringe benefit

  2. Something that aids or promotes well-being.

    “For the benefit of all.”

    Synonyms: welfare · A kind of: good · Kinds: advantage, sake

  3. A performance to raise money for a charitable cause.

    A kind of: performance · Kinds: benefit concert

Benefit as a verb

  1. Derive a benefit from.

    “She profited from his vast experience.”

    Synonyms: profit, gain · A kind of: get · Kinds: cash in on, profiteer, capitalize, pyramid, net

  2. Be beneficial for.

    “This will do you good.”

    Synonyms: do good · A kind of: help

Phrases and compounds with "benefit"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • tax benefit (noun) A tax deduction that is granted in order to encourage a particular type of commercial activity.
  • sick benefit (noun) Money paid (by the government) to someone who is too ill to work.
  • benefit album (noun) Concept album compiling a performer's work or work supporting some worthy cause.
  • death benefit (noun) Insurance or pension money payable to a beneficiary of a deceased.
  • fringe benefit (noun) An incidental benefit awarded for certain types of employment (especially if it is regarded as a right).
  • benefit concert (noun) A concert given for the benefit of some charitable cause.
  • sickness benefit (noun) Money paid (by the government) to someone who is too ill to work.
  • benefit of clergy (noun) Sanction by a religious rite.
  • disability benefit (noun) Insurance benefits paid in case of disability.
  • retirement benefit (noun) A monthly payment made to someone who is retired from work.
  • advance death benefit (noun) A percentage of death benefits paid directly to policy holders having a short life expectancy (usually 6 months).
  • cost-benefit analysis (noun) An analysis of the cost effectiveness of different alternatives in order to see whether the benefits outweigh the costs.
  • supplementary benefit (noun) Benefits paid to bring incomes up to minimum levels established by law.
  • cost-of-living benefit (noun) A benefit that goes to anyone whose money receipts increase automatically as prices rise.
