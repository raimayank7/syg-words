Best: meaning, synonyms and examples

Best is used as an adjective (2 senses), adverb (3 senses), noun (3 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: (superlative of `good') having the most positive qualities.

In Hindi
सबसे अच्छा
Synonyms
better, topper, outdo, outflank, trump, scoop
Opposites
worst

Best as an adjective

  1. (superlative of `good') having the most positive qualities.

    “The best film of the year.” “The best solution.” “The best time for planting.”

    Opposite: worst · Similar: champion, high-grade, first, go-to-meeting, optimum, primo

  2. (comparative and superlative of `well') wiser or more advantageous and hence advisable.

    “It would be better to speak to him.” “The White House thought it best not to respond.”

    Synonyms: better · Similar: advisable

Best as an adverb

  1. In a most excellent way or manner.

    “He played best after a couple of martinis.”

  2. It would be sensible.

    “You'd best stay at home.”

  3. From a position of superiority or authority.

    “Father knows best.” “I know better.”

    Synonyms: better

Best as a noun

  1. The supreme effort one can make.

    “They did their best.”

    Opposite: worst · A kind of: attempt

  2. The person who is most outstanding or excellent; someone who tops all others.

    “He could beat the best of them.”

    Synonyms: topper · A kind of: person

  3. Canadian physiologist (born in the United States) who assisted F. G. Banting in research leading to the discovery of insulin (1899-1978).

Best as a verb

  1. Get the better of.

    “The goal was to best the competition.”

    Synonyms: outdo, outflank, trump, scoop · A kind of: beat · Kinds: outmaneuver

Phrases and compounds with "best"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at best (adverb) Under the best of conditions.
  • best man (noun) The principal groomsman at a wedding.
  • had best (verb) Act in one's own or everybody's best interest.
  • best-known (adjective) Most familiar or renowned.
  • best-loved (adjective) Preferred above all others and treated with partiality.
  • level best (noun) The greatest possible degree.
  • at the best (adverb) Under the best of conditions.
  • best friend (noun) The one friend who is closest to you.
  • best of all (adverb) Especially fortunate.
  • best seller (noun) A book that has had a large and rapid sale.
  • second best (noun) The competitor who finishes second.
  • second-best (adjective) Next to the best.
  • sunday best (noun) The best attire you have which is worn to church on Sunday.
  • best-selling (adjective) Selling in great numbers.
  • get the best (verb) Overcome, usually through no fault or weakness of the person that is overcome.
  • do one's best (verb) Perform a task as well as possible.
