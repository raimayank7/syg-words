Big: meaning, synonyms and examples

Big is used as an adjective (12 senses) and adverb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Above average in size or number or quantity or magnitude or extent.

In Hindi
बड़ा
Synonyms
large, bad, prominent, heavy, boastful, braggart, bragging, braggy, cock-a-hoop, crowing
Opposites
little, small
Word family
bigness

Big as an adjective

  1. Above average in size or number or quantity or magnitude or extent.

    “A large city.” “Set out for the big city.” “A large sum.”

    Synonyms: large · Opposite: little · Similar: ample, astronomic, bear-sized, bigger, biggish, blown-up

  2. Significant.

    “Graduation was a big day in his life.”

    Similar: important

  3. Very intense.

    “A bad headache.” “In a big rage.” “Had a big (or bad) shock.”

    Synonyms: bad · Similar: intense

  4. Loud and firm.

    “A big voice.” “Big bold piano sounds.”

    Similar: loud

  5. Conspicuous in position or importance.

    “A big figure in the movement.” “Big man on campus.” “He's very large in financial circles.”

    Synonyms: large, prominent · Similar: conspicuous

  6. Prodigious.

    “Big spender.” “Big eater.” “Heavy investor.”

    Synonyms: heavy · Similar: intemperate

  7. Exhibiting self-importance.

    “Big talk.”

    Synonyms: boastful, braggart, bragging, braggy, cock-a-hoop, crowing, self-aggrandizing, self-aggrandising · Similar: proud

  8. Feeling self-importance.

    “Too big for his britches.” “Had a swelled head.” “He was swelled with pride.”

    Synonyms: swelled, vainglorious · Similar: proud

  9. (of animals) fully developed.

    “An adult animal.” “A grown woman.”

    Synonyms: adult, full-grown, fully grown, grown, grownup · Similar: mature

  10. Marked by intense physical force.

    “A big wind.”

    Similar: heavy

  11. Generous and understanding and tolerant.

    “A heart big enough to hold no grudges.” “That's very big of you to be so forgiving.” “A large and generous spirit.”

    Synonyms: large, magnanimous · Similar: generous

  12. Given or giving freely.

    “Was a big tipper.” “The bounteous goodness of God.” “Bountiful compliments.”

    Synonyms: bighearted, bounteous, bountiful, freehanded, handsome, giving, liberal, openhanded · Similar: generous

Big as an adverb

  1. Extremely well.

    “His performance went over big.”

  2. In a boastful manner.

    “He talked big all evening.”

    Synonyms: boastfully, vauntingly, large

  3. On a grand scale.

    “Think big.”

    Opposite: small

  4. In a major way.

    “The play failed big at the box office.”
