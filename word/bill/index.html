Bill: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bill is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A statute in draft before it becomes law.

In Hindi
बिल; विधेयक
Synonyms
measure, account, invoice, note, government note, bank bill, banker's bill, bank note, banknote, greenback

Bill as a noun

  1. A statute in draft before it becomes law.

    “They held a public hearing on the bill.”

    Synonyms: measure · A kind of: legal document · Kinds: appropriation bill, bill of attainder, bottle bill, farm bill, trade bill

  2. An itemized statement of money owed for goods shipped or services rendered.

    “He paid his bill and left.” “Send me an account of what I owe.”

    Synonyms: account, invoice · A kind of: statement · Kinds: electric bill, hotel bill, medical bill, phone bill, reckoning, tax bill, check

  3. A piece of paper money (especially one issued by a central bank).

    “He peeled off five one-thousand-zloty notes.”

    Synonyms: note, government note, bank bill, banker's bill, bank note, banknote, greenback · A kind of: paper money · Kinds: silver certificate, hundred dollar bill, fifty dollar bill, twenty dollar bill, tenner, fiver, two dollar bill, dollar

  4. The entertainment offered at a public presentation.

    A kind of: program

  5. An advertisement (usually printed on a page or in a leaflet) intended for wide distribution.

    “He mailed the circular to all subscribers.”

    Synonyms: circular, handbill, broadside, broadsheet, flier, flyer, throwaway · A kind of: ad · Kinds: stuffer

  6. A sign posted in a public place as an advertisement.

    “A poster advertised the coming attractions.”

    Synonyms: poster, posting, placard, notice, card · A kind of: sign · Kinds: show bill, flash card

  7. A list of particulars (as a playbill or bill of fare).

    A kind of: list · Kinds: bill of entry, bill of goods, menu, playbill

  8. A long-handled saw with a curved blade.

    “He used a bill to prune branches off of the tree.”

    Synonyms: billhook · A kind of: saw

  9. A brim that projects to the front to shade the eyes.

    “He pulled down the bill of his cap and trudged ahead.”

    Synonyms: peak, eyeshade, visor, vizor · A kind of: brim

  10. Horny projecting mouth of a bird.

    Synonyms: beak, neb, nib, pecker · A kind of: mouth · Kinds: cere

Bill as a verb

  1. Demand payment.

    “Will I get charged for this service?” “We were billed for 4 nights in the hotel, although we stayed only 3 nights.”

    Synonyms: charge · A kind of: account · Kinds: levy, tithe, assess, undercharge, surcharge, invoice

  2. Advertise especially by posters or placards.

    “He was billed as the greatest tenor since Caruso.”

    A kind of: advertise

  3. Publicize or announce by placards.

    Synonyms: placard · A kind of: post

Phrases and compounds with "bill"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • t-bill (noun) A short-term obligation that is not interest-bearing (it is purchased at a discount); can be traded on a discount basis for 91 days.
  • tax bill (noun) Money owed for taxes.
  • bank bill (noun) A piece of paper money (especially one issued by a central bank).
  • farm bill (noun) A statute that would regulate farm production and prices.
  • show bill (noun) A poster advertising a show or play.
  • time bill (noun) A draft payable at a specified future date.
  • true bill (noun) An indictment endorsed by a grand jury.
  • twin bill (noun) Two games instead of one (especially in baseball when the same two teams play two games on the same day).
  • bill gates (noun) United States computer entrepreneur whose software company made him the youngest multi-billionaire in the history of the United States (born in 1955).
  • bill haley (noun) United States rock singer who was one of the first to popularize rock'n'roll music (1925-1981).
  • hotel bill (noun) Statement of charges for staying in a hotel.
  • phone bill (noun) Statement of charges for telephone service.
  • sight bill (noun) A draft payable on presentation.
  • trade bill (noun) A statute that would regulate foreign trade.
  • bill poster (noun) Someone who pastes up bills or placards on walls or billboards.
  • bottle bill (noun) A statute that would require merchants to reclaim used bottles.
