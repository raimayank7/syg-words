Bit: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bit is used as a noun (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: A small piece or quantity of something.

In Hindi
थोड़ा-सा; टुकड़ा
Synonyms
spot, chip, flake, fleck, scrap, moment, mo, minute, second, piece
Word family
bitty

Bit as a noun

  1. A small piece or quantity of something.

    “A spot of tea.” “A bit of paper.” “A bit of lint.”

    Synonyms: spot · A kind of: small indefinite quantity

  2. A small fragment of something broken off from the whole.

    “A bit of rock caught him in the eye.”

    Synonyms: chip, flake, fleck, scrap · A kind of: fragment · Kinds: matchwood, scale, scurf, splinter

  3. An indefinitely short time.

    “Wait just a moment.” “In a mo.” “It only takes a minute.”

    Synonyms: moment, mo, minute, second · A kind of: time · Kinds: blink of an eye

  4. An instance of some kind.

    “It was a nice piece of work.” “He had a bit of good luck.”

    Synonyms: piece · A kind of: case

  5. Piece of metal held in horse's mouth by reins and used to control the horse while riding.

    “The horse was not accustomed to a bit.”

    A kind of: stable gear · Kinds: bar bit, bridoon, curb, snaffle

  6. A unit of measurement of information (from binary + digit); the amount of information in a system having two equiprobable states.

    “There are 8 bits in a byte.”

    A kind of: unit of measurement · Kinds: parity bit

  7. A small amount of solid food; a mouthful.

    “All they had left was a bit of bread.”

    Synonyms: morsel, bite · Kinds: chew, crumb, sop

  8. A small fragment.

    “Overheard snatches of their conversation.”

    Synonyms: snatch · A kind of: fragment

  9. A short theatrical performance that is part of a longer program.

    “He did his act three times every evening.” “She had a catchy little routine.” “It was one of the best numbers he ever did.”

    Synonyms: act, routine, number, turn · A kind of: performance · Kinds: show-stopper

  10. The part of a key that enters a lock and lifts the tumblers.

    A kind of: part

  11. The cutting part of a drill; usually pointed and threaded and is replaceable in a brace or bitstock or drill press.

    “He looked around for the right size bit.”

    A kind of: cutting implement · Kinds: bur, center bit, chamfer bit, counterbore, drilling bit, expansion bit, pilot bit, spade bit

Phrases and compounds with "bit"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • a bit (adverb) To a small degree; somewhat.
  • bar bit (noun) A bit for horses that is a solid bar of metal.
  • bit part (noun) A small role.
  • bore bit (noun) A drill for penetrating rock.
  • core bit (noun) A hollow drilling bit that is the cutting part of a core drill; allows core samples to be taken.
  • curb bit (noun) A horse's bit with an attached chain or strap to check the horse.
  • rock bit (noun) A drill bit that has hardened rotating rollers.
  • bit field (noun) A field containing only binary characters.
  • blade bit (noun) A drilling bit with cutting edges usually hardened against wear.
  • check bit (noun) (computer science) a bit that is used in an error detection procedure in which a 0 or 1 is added to each group of bits so that it will have either an odd number of 1's or an even number of 1's; e.g., if the parity is odd then any group of bits that arrives with an even number of 1's must contain an error.
  • cross bit (noun) A rock drill having cruciform cutting edges; used in mining.
  • drill bit (noun) A bit used in drilling for oil.
  • every bit (adverb) To the same degree (often followed by `as').
  • pilot bit (noun) A small bit that drills a first hole to guide a larger drill.
  • spade bit (noun) A thin bit with a center point and cutting edges on either side.
  • twist bit (noun) A bit or drill having deep helical grooves.
