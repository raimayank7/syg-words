Blow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Blow is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: A powerful stroke with the fist or a weapon.

In Hindi
फूँकना; झटका
Synonyms
bump, reverse, reversal, setback, black eye, shock, gust, blast, puff, float
Word family
blowy, blowing, blower

Blow as a noun

  1. A powerful stroke with the fist or a weapon.

    “A blow on the head.”

    A kind of: stroke · Kinds: backhander, clip, knock, thwack, smack, smacker, knockdown, knockout

  2. An impact (as from a collision).

    “The bump threw him off the bicycle.”

    Synonyms: bump · A kind of: impact · Kinds: jolt, concussion, rap, knock, pounding, sideswipe, slap

  3. An unfortunate happening that hinders or impedes; something that is thwarting or frustrating.

    Synonyms: reverse, reversal, setback, black eye · A kind of: happening · Kinds: whammy

  4. An unpleasant or disappointing surprise.

    “It came as a shock to learn that he was injured.”

    Synonyms: shock · A kind of: surprise · Kinds: blip

  5. A strong current of air.

    “The tree was bent almost double by the gust.”

    Synonyms: gust, blast · A kind of: wind · Kinds: bluster, sandblast, puff

  6. Forceful exhalation through the nose or mouth.

    “He gave his nose a loud blow.” “He blew out all the candles with a single puff.”

    Synonyms: puff · A kind of: exhalation · Kinds: insufflation

Blow as a verb

  1. Exhale hard.

    “Blow on the soup to cool it down.”

    A kind of: exhale · Kinds: pant, puff, insufflate

  2. Be blowing or storming.

    “The wind blew from the West.”

    Kinds: breeze, set in, waft, storm, squall, bluster

  3. Free of obstruction by blowing air through.

    “Blow one's nose.”

    A kind of: exhaust

  4. Be in motion due to some air or water current.

    “The leaves were blowing in the wind.” “The boat drifted on the lake.” “The sailboat was adrift on the open sea.”

    Synonyms: float, drift, be adrift · A kind of: travel · Kinds: waft, tide, stream

  5. Make a sound as if blown.

    “The whistle blew.”

    A kind of: sound

  6. Shape by blowing.

    “Blow a glass vase.”

    A kind of: shape

  7. Make a mess of, destroy or ruin.

    “I botched the dinner and we had to eat out.” “The pianist screwed up the difficult passage in the second movement.”

    Synonyms: botch, bodge, bumble, fumble, botch up, muff, flub, screw up · A kind of: fail

  8. Spend thoughtlessly; throw away.

    “He wasted his inheritance on his insincere friends.” “You squandered the opportunity to get and advanced degree.”

    Synonyms: waste, squander · A kind of: use · Kinds: burn

  9. Spend lavishly or wastefully on.

    “He blew a lot of money on his new home theater.”

    A kind of: spend

  10. Sound by having air expelled through a tube.

    “The trumpets blew.”

    A kind of: sound

  11. Play or sound a wind instrument.

    “She blew the horn.”

    A kind of: sound

  12. Cause air to go in, on, or through.

    “Blow my hair dry.”

    A kind of: send · Kinds: blast

  13. Cause to move by means of an air current.

    “The wind blew the leaves around in the yard.”

    A kind of: move · Kinds: whiff

  14. Spout moist air from the blowhole.

    “The whales blew.”

    A kind of: spurt

Phrases and compounds with "blow"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • blow up (verb) Cause to burst with a violent release of energy.
  • by-blow (noun) The illegitimate offspring of unmarried parents.
  • blow fly (noun) Large usually hairy metallic blue or green fly; lays eggs in carrion or dung or wounds.
  • blow gas (noun) The gas leaving a generator during a blow period.
  • blow off (verb) Come off due to an explosion or other strong force.
  • blow out (verb) Melt, break, or become otherwise unusable.
  • blow-dry (verb) Dry hair with a hair dryer.
  • joe blow (noun) A hypothetical average man.
  • low blow (noun) Unscrupulous abuse.
  • blow over (verb) Disappear gradually.
  • blow tube (noun) A tube that directs air or gas into a flame to concentrate heat.
  • blow drier (noun) A hand-held electric blower that can blow warm air onto the hair; used for styling hair.
  • blow dryer (noun) A hand-held electric blower that can blow warm air onto the hair; used for styling hair.
  • blow a fuse (verb) Get very angry and fly into a rage.
  • blow-by-blow (adjective) Providing great detail.
  • strike a blow (verb) Affect adversely.
