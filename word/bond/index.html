Bond: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bond is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: An electrical force linking atoms.

In Hindi
बंधन; बॉन्ड
Synonyms
chemical bond, bond certificate, alliance, bail, bail bond, shackle, hamper, trammel, attachment, bond paper
Word family
bondable

Bond as a noun

  1. An electrical force linking atoms.

    Synonyms: chemical bond · A kind of: attraction · Kinds: covalent bond, cross-link, hydrogen bond, ionic bond, metallic bond, peptide bond

  2. A certificate of debt (usually interest-bearing or discounted) that is issued by a government or corporation in order to raise money; the issuer is required to pay a fixed sum annually until maturity and then a fixed sum to repay the principal.

    Synonyms: bond certificate · A kind of: debt instrument, security · Kinds: bond issue, convertible bond, corporate bond, coupon bond, government bond, junk bond, municipal bond, noncallable bond

  3. A connection based on kinship or marriage or common interest.

    “The shifting alliances within a large family.” “Their friendship constitutes a powerful bond between them.”

    Synonyms: alliance · A kind of: connection · Kinds: silver cord

  4. (criminal law) money that must be forfeited by the bondsman if an accused person fails to appear in court for trial.

    “The judge set bail at $10,000.” “A $10,000 bond was furnished by an alderman.”

    Synonyms: bail, bail bond · A kind of: recognizance

  5. A restraint that confines or restricts freedom (especially something used to tie down or restrain a prisoner).

    Synonyms: shackle, hamper, trammel · A kind of: restraint · Kinds: ball and chain, fetter, handcuff, irons

  6. A connection that fastens things together.

    Synonyms: attachment · A kind of: connection · Kinds: ligament

  7. A superior quality of strong durable white writing paper; originally made for printing documents.

    Synonyms: bond paper · A kind of: writing paper

  8. United States civil rights leader who was elected to the legislature in Georgia but was barred from taking his seat because he opposed the Vietnam War (born 1940).

  9. British secret operative 007 in novels by Ian Fleming.

  10. The property of sticking together (as of glue and wood) or the joining of surfaces of different composition.

    “The mutual adhesiveness of cells.” “A heated hydraulic press was required for adhesion.”

    Synonyms: adhesiveness, adhesion, adherence · A kind of: stickiness

Bond as a verb

  1. Stick to firmly.

    “Will this wallpaper adhere to the wall?”

    Synonyms: adhere, hold fast, bind, stick, stick to · A kind of: attach · Kinds: bind

  2. Create social or emotional ties.

    “The grandparents want to bond with the child.”

    Synonyms: bind, tie, attach · A kind of: relate · Kinds: fixate, befriend

  3. Issue bonds on.

    A kind of: mortgage

  4. Bring together in a common cause or emotion.

    “The death of their child had drawn them together.”

    Synonyms: bring together, draw together · A kind of: unite

Phrases and compounds with "bond"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bail bond (noun) (criminal law) money that must be forfeited by the bondsman if an accused person fails to appear in court for trial.
  • junk bond (noun) A (speculative) bond with a credit rating of BB or lower; issued for leveraged buyouts and other takeovers by companies with questionable credit.
  • bond issue (noun) Bonds sold by a corporation or government agency at a particular time and identifiable by date of maturity.
  • bond paper (noun) A superior quality of strong durable white writing paper; originally made for printing documents.
  • ionic bond (noun) A chemical bond in which one atom loses an electron to form a positive ion and the other atom gains an electron to form a negative ion.
  • james bond (noun) British secret operative 007 in novels by Ian Fleming.
  • bearer bond (noun) A bond issued with detachable coupons that must be presented to the issuer for interest payments.
  • bond rating (noun) An evaluation by a rating company of the probability that a particular bond issue will default.
  • coupon bond (noun) A bond issued with detachable coupons that must be presented to the issuer for interest payments.
  • dative bond (noun) A covalent bond in which both electrons are provided by one of the atoms.
  • double bond (noun) A covalent bond in which two pairs of electrons are shared between two atoms.
  • julian bond (noun) United States civil rights leader who was elected to the legislature in Georgia but was barred from taking his seat because he opposed the Vietnam War (born 1940).
  • letter bond (noun) A bond that has not been registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission and cannot be sold to the general public.
  • surety bond (noun) A bond given to protect the recipient against loss in case the terms of a contract are not filled; a surety company assumes liability for nonperformance.
  • bond servant (noun) Someone bound to labor without wages.
  • bond trading (noun) Trading in bonds (usually by a broker on the floor of an exchange).
