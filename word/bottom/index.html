Bottom: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bottom is used as a noun (7 senses), verb (3 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The lower side of anything.

In Hindi
तल, निचला भाग
Synonyms
underside, undersurface, nates, arse, butt, backside, bum, buns, can, fundament
Opposites
top, side

Bottom as a noun

  1. The lower side of anything.

    Synonyms: underside, undersurface · A kind of: side · Kinds: base, bilge, heel, sole, underbelly

  2. The lowest part of anything.

    “They started at the bottom of the hill.”

    A kind of: region · Kinds: foot, base, rock bottom

  3. The fleshy part of the human body that you sit on.

    “He deserves a good kick in the butt.” “Are you going to sit on your fanny and do nothing?”

    Synonyms: nates, arse, butt, backside, bum, buns, can, fundament · A kind of: body part

  4. The second half of an inning; while the home team is at bat.

    Synonyms: bottom of the inning · Opposite: top

  5. A depression forming the ground under a body of water.

    “He searched for treasure on the ocean bed.”

    Synonyms: bed · A kind of: natural depression · Kinds: lake bed, ocean floor, riverbed, streambed

  6. Low-lying alluvial land near a river.

    Synonyms: bottomland · A kind of: land

  7. A cargo ship.

    “They did much of their overseas trade in foreign bottoms.”

    Synonyms: freighter, merchantman, merchant ship · A kind of: cargo ship

Bottom as a verb

  1. Provide with a bottom or a seat.

    “Bottom the chairs.”

    A kind of: supply

  2. Strike the ground, as with a ship's bottom.

    A kind of: hit

  3. Come to understand.

    Synonyms: penetrate, fathom · A kind of: understand

Bottom as an adjective

  1. Situated at the bottom or lowest position.

    “The bottom drawer.”

    Opposite: side, top · Similar: bottommost, inferior, nether

  2. The lowest rank.

    “Bottom member of the class.”

    Similar: worst

Phrases and compounds with "bottom"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at bottom (adverb) In reality.
  • bottom-up (adjective) Of an approach to a problem that begins with details and works up to the highest conceptual level.
  • bottom dog (noun) A person of low status.
  • bottom out (verb) Reach the low point.
  • bottom rot (noun) Fungous disease of lettuce that first rots lower leaves and spreads upward.
  • sea bottom (noun) The bottom of a sea or ocean.
  • bell-bottom (adjective) (of trousers) having legs that flare at the bottom.
  • bottom fish (noun) Fish that live on the sea bottom (particularly the commercially important gadoid fish like cod and haddock, or flatfish like flounder).
  • bottom line (noun) The last line in an audit; the line that shows profit or loss.
  • flat-bottom (adjective) Having a flat bottom.
  • lake bottom (noun) The bottom of a lake.
  • rock bottom (noun) The absolute bottom.
  • rock-bottom (adjective) Well below normal (especially in price).
  • bottom quark (noun) A quark with a charge of -1/3 and a mass about 10,000 times that of an electron.
  • bottom round (noun) Cut from the round; suitable for pot roast.
  • false bottom (noun) A horizontal structure that partitions a ship or box (especially one built close to the actual bottom).
