Boy: meaning, synonyms and examples

Boy is used as a noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A youthful male person.

In Hindi
लड़का
Synonyms
male child, son
Opposites

Word family
boyhood

Boy as a noun

  1. A youthful male person.

    “The baby was a boy.” “She made the boy brush his teeth every night.” “Most soldiers are only boys in uniform.”

    Synonyms: male child · Opposite: girl · Kinds: altar boy, ball boy, bat boy, cub, farm boy, plowboy, schoolboy, shop boy

  2. A friendly informal reference to a grown man.

    “He likes to play golf with the boys.”

    A kind of: man · Kinds: broth of a boy, one of the boys

  3. A male human offspring.

    “Their son became a famous judge.” “His boy is taller than he is.”

    Synonyms: son · Opposite: girl · A kind of: male offspring · Kinds: mother's boy

Phrases and compounds with "boy"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bat boy (noun) (baseball) a boy who takes care of bats and other baseball equipment.
  • old boy (noun) A familiar term of address for a man.
  • ball boy (noun) A boy who retrieves balls for tennis players.
  • city boy (noun) A city dweller with sophisticated manners and clothing.
  • farm boy (noun) A boy who has grown up on a farm.
  • poor boy (noun) A large sandwich made of a long crusty roll split lengthwise and filled with meats and cheese (and tomato and onion and lettuce and condiments); different names are used in different sections of the United States.
  • shop boy (noun) A young male shop assistant.
  • wolf boy (noun) A male person assumed to have been raised by wolves.
  • altar boy (noun) A boy serving as an acolyte.
  • boy scout (noun) A boy who is a member of the Boy Scouts.
  • cabin boy (noun) A young man acting as a servant on a ship.
  • sonny boy (noun) A male child (a familiar term of address to a boy).
  • teddy boy (noun) A tough youth of 1950's and 1960's wearing Edwardian style clothes.
  • water boy (noun) An assistant who supplies drinking water.
  • barrow-boy (noun) A hawker of fruit and vegetables from a barrow.
  • boy scouts (noun) An international (but decentralized) movement started in 1908 in England with the goal of teaching good citizenship to boys.
