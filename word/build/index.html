Build: meaning, synonyms and examples

Build is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make by combining materials and parts.

In Hindi
बनाना, निर्माण करना
Synonyms
construct, make, build up, work up, progress, establish, ramp up, physique, body-build, habitus
Word family
builder, building

Build as a verb

  1. Make by combining materials and parts.

    “This little pig made his house out of straw.” “Some eccentric constructed an electric brassiere warmer.”

    Synonyms: construct, make · A kind of: make · Kinds: customize, revet, dry-wall, lock, wattle, frame, rebuild, groin

  2. Form or accumulate steadily.

    “Resistance to the manager's plan built up quickly.” “Pressure is building up at the Indian-Pakistani border.”

    Synonyms: build up, work up, progress · A kind of: develop

  3. Build or establish something abstract.

    “Build a reputation.”

    Synonyms: establish · A kind of: make

  4. Improve the cleansing action of.

    “Build detergents.”

    A kind of: better

  5. Order, supervise, or finance the construction of.

    “The government is building new schools in this state.”

    A kind of: oversee

  6. Give form to, according to a plan.

    “Build a modern nation.” “Build a million-dollar business.”

    A kind of: develop

  7. Be engaged in building.

    “These architects build in interesting and new styles.”

    A kind of: create

  8. Found or ground.

    “Build a defense on nothing but the accused person's reputation.”

    A kind of: establish

  9. Bolster or strengthen.

    “We worked up courage.” “Build up confidence.” “Ramp up security in the airports.”

    Synonyms: build up, work up, ramp up · A kind of: increase

  10. Develop and grow.

    “Suspense was building right from the beginning of the opera.”

    A kind of: intensify

Build as a noun

  1. Constitution of the human body.

    Synonyms: physique, body-build, habitus · A kind of: bodily property · Kinds: lankiness, dumpiness, body type

  2. Alternative names for the body of a human being.

    “Leonardo studied the human body.” “He has a strong physique.” “The spirit is willing but the flesh is weak.”

    Synonyms: human body, physical body, material body, soma, figure, physique, anatomy, shape · A kind of: body · Kinds: person, juvenile body, adult body, male body, female body

Phrases and compounds with "build"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • build in (verb) Make something an integral part of something else.
  • build on (verb) Be based on; of theories and claims, for example.
  • build up (verb) Enlarge, develop, or increase by degrees or in stages.
  • body-build (noun) Constitution of the human body.
  • build upon (verb) Be based on; of theories and claims, for example.
  • hand-build (verb) Make without a potter's wheel.
