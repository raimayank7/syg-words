Burst: meaning, synonyms and examples

Burst is used as a verb (8 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Come open suddenly and violently, as if from internal pressure.

In Hindi
फटना
Synonyms
split, break open, break, erupt, explode, abound, bristle, collapse, bust, explosion
Word family
burster

Burst as a verb

  1. Come open suddenly and violently, as if from internal pressure.

    “The bubble burst.”

    Synonyms: split, break open · A kind of: break · Kinds: pop, blow, stave

  2. Force out or release suddenly and often violently something pent up.

    “Break into tears.” “Erupt in anger.”

    Synonyms: break, erupt · A kind of: express emotion

  3. Burst outward, usually with noise.

    “The champagne bottle exploded.”

    Synonyms: explode · A kind of: change integrity · Kinds: crump, go off, erupt

  4. Move suddenly, energetically, or violently.

    “He burst out of the house into the cool night.”

    A kind of: jump

  5. Be in a state of movement or action.

    “The room abounded with screaming children.” “The garden bristled with toddlers.”

    Synonyms: abound, bristle · A kind of: have

  6. Emerge suddenly.

    “The sun burst into view.”

    A kind of: emerge

  7. Cause to burst.

    “The ice broke the pipe.”

    Synonyms: collapse · Kinds: pop

  8. Break open or apart suddenly and forcefully.

    “The dam burst.”

    Synonyms: bust · A kind of: break · Kinds: shatter

Burst as a noun

  1. The act of exploding or bursting.

    “The explosion of the firecrackers awoke the children.”

    Synonyms: explosion · A kind of: change of integrity · Kinds: detonation, fulmination

  2. Rapid simultaneous discharge of firearms.

    “Our fusillade from the left flank caught them by surprise.”

    Synonyms: fusillade, salvo, volley · A kind of: fire

  3. A sudden flurry of activity (often for no obvious reason).

    “A burst of applause.” “A fit of housecleaning.”

    Synonyms: fit · A kind of: activity · Kinds: fits and starts

  4. A sudden intense happening.

    “An outburst of heavy rain.” “A burst of lightning.”

    Synonyms: outburst, flare-up · A kind of: happening · Kinds: rush

Phrases and compounds with "burst"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • burst out (verb) Give sudden release to an expression.
  • burst upon (verb) Spring suddenly.
  • burst forth (verb) Jump out from a hiding place and surprise (someone).
  • burst in on (verb) Spring suddenly.
