Care: meaning, synonyms and examples

Care is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Feel concern or interest.

In Hindi
देखभाल; परवाह
Synonyms
give care, wish, like, manage, deal, handle, worry, attention, aid, tending
Word family
caring

Care as a verb

  1. Feel concern or interest.

    “I really care about my work.” “I don't care.”

    A kind of: feel for

  2. Provide care for.

    “The nurse was caring for the wounded.”

    Synonyms: give care · A kind of: help · Kinds: attend, mother, nurse

  3. Prefer or wish to do something.

    “Do you care to try this dish?” “Would you like to come along to the movies?”

    Synonyms: wish, like · A kind of: desire · Kinds: please

  4. Be in charge of, act on, or dispose of.

    “I can deal with this crew of workers.” “This blender can't handle nuts.” “She managed her parents' affairs after they got too old.”

    Synonyms: manage, deal, handle · A kind of: control · Kinds: administer, organize, work, come to grips, dispose of, take care, coordinate, juggle

  5. Be concerned with.

    “I worry about my grades.”

    Synonyms: worry · A kind of: mind · Kinds: brood

Care as a noun

  1. The work of providing treatment for or attending to someone or something.

    “No medical care was required.” “The old car needs constant attention.”

    Synonyms: attention, aid, tending · A kind of: work · Kinds: hair care, maternalism, babysitting, pet sitting, dental care, first aid, treatment, incubation

  2. Judiciousness in avoiding harm or danger.

    “He exercised caution in opening the door.” “He handled the vase with care.”

    Synonyms: caution, precaution, forethought · A kind of: judiciousness

  3. An anxious feeling.

    “Care had aged him.” “They hushed it up out of fear of public reaction.”

    Synonyms: concern, fear · A kind of: anxiety

  4. A cause for feeling concern.

    “His major care was the illness of his wife.”

    A kind of: predicament

  5. Attention and management implying responsibility for safety.

    “He is in the care of a bodyguard.”

    Synonyms: charge, tutelage, guardianship · A kind of: protection · Kinds: due care, foster care, great care, providence, slight care

  6. Activity involved in maintaining something in good working order.

    “He wrote the manual on car care.”

    Synonyms: maintenance, upkeep · A kind of: repair · Kinds: camera care, car care, overhaul, pump priming, scheduled maintenance, steam fitting

Phrases and compounds with "care"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • car care (noun) Keeping a car in good working order.
  • care for (verb) Have a liking, fondness, or taste (for).
  • day care (noun) Childcare during the day while parents work.
  • due care (noun) The care that a reasonable man would exercise under the circumstances; the standard for determining legal duty.
  • give care (verb) Provide care for.
  • hair care (noun) Care for the hair: the activity of washing or cutting or curling or arranging the hair.
  • skin care (noun) Care for the skin.
  • take care (verb) Be careful, prudent, or watchful.
  • care-laden (adjective) Burdened by cares.
  • child care (noun) A service involving care for other people's children.
  • great care (noun) More attention and consideration than is normally bestowed by prudent persons.
  • camera care (noun) Keeping a camera in good working order.
  • care a hang (verb) Show no concern or interest; always used in the negative.
  • dental care (noun) Care for the teeth.
  • foster care (noun) Supervised care for delinquent or neglected children usually in an institution or substitute home.
  • health care (noun) Social insurance for the ill and injured.
