Carry: meaning, synonyms and examples

Carry is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Move while supporting, either in a vehicle or in one's hands or on one's body.

In Hindi
ले जाना, उठाना
Synonyms
transport, pack, take, impart, conduct, transmit, convey, channel, express, hold
Word family
carrier, carriage

Carry as a verb

  1. Move while supporting, either in a vehicle or in one's hands or on one's body.

    “You must carry your camping gear.” “Carry the suitcases to the car.” “This train is carrying nuclear waste.”

    Synonyms: transport · A kind of: move · Kinds: shoulder, port, bear, bucket, return, pipe in, port, porter

  2. Have with oneself; have on one's person.

    “She always takes an umbrella.” “I always carry money.” “She packs a gun when she goes into the mountains.”

    Synonyms: pack, take · A kind of: have

  3. Transmit or serve as the medium for transmission.

    “Sound carries well over water.” “The airwaves carry the sound.” “Many metals conduct heat.”

    Synonyms: impart, conduct, transmit, convey, channel · A kind of: bring · Kinds: wash up, pipe in, bring in, retransmit

  4. Serve as a means for expressing something.

    “The painting of Mary carries motherly love.” “His voice carried a lot of anger.”

    Synonyms: convey, express · A kind of: communicate · Kinds: quantify

  5. Bear or be able to bear the weight, pressure,or responsibility of.

    “His efforts carried the entire project.” “How many credits is this student carrying?” “We carry a very large mortgage.”

    A kind of: hold

  6. Support or hold in a certain manner.

    “She holds her head high.” “He carried himself upright.”

    Synonyms: hold, bear · Kinds: sling, stoop, piggyback, poise, behave

  7. Contain or hold; have within.

    “The canteen holds fresh water.” “This can contains water.”

    Synonyms: hold, bear, contain · A kind of: include · Kinds: retain, enclose

  8. Extend to a certain degree.

    “Carry too far.” “She carries her ideas to the extreme.”

    A kind of: effect

  9. Continue or extend.

    “The civil war carried into the neighboring province.” “The disease extended into the remote mountain provinces.”

    Synonyms: extend · A kind of: continue

  10. Be necessarily associated with or result in or involve.

    “This crime carries a penalty of five years in prison.”

    A kind of: imply

  11. Win in an election.

    “The senator carried his home state.”

    A kind of: win

  12. Include, as on a list.

    “How many people are carried on the payroll?”

    A kind of: include

  13. Behave in a certain manner.

    “She carried herself well.” “He bore himself with dignity.” “They conducted themselves well during these difficult times.”

    Synonyms: behave, acquit, bear, deport, conduct, comport · A kind of: hold, act · Kinds: fluster, assert, deal, walk around, pose

  14. Have on hand.

    “Do you carry kerosene heaters?”

    Synonyms: stock, stockpile · A kind of: have

Carry as a noun

  1. The act of carrying something.

    A kind of: transportation · Kinds: packing, piggyback, fireman's carry, portage, porterage

Phrases and compounds with "carry"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • carry on (verb) Direct the course of; manage or control.
  • carry off (verb) Be successful; achieve a goal.
  • carry out (verb) Put in effect.
  • carry away (verb) Remove from a certain place, environment, or mental or emotional state; transport into a new location or state.
  • carry back (verb) Deduct a loss or an unused credit from taxable income for a prior period.
  • carry over (verb) Transfer or persist from one stage or sphere of activity to another.
  • carry-over (noun) Application of a skill learned in one situation to a different but similar situation.
  • carry weight (verb) Have influence to a specified degree.
  • carry forward (verb) Transfer from one time period to the next.
  • carry through (verb) Put in effect.
  • carry-forward (noun) The accumulated and undivided profits of a corporation after provision has been made for dividends and reserves.
  • carry-the can (verb) Accept the unpleasant consequences of one's actions.
  • cash-and-carry (adjective) Sold for cash and the customer carries it away (no delivery service).
  • fireman's carry (noun) The act of carrying a person over your shoulder.
