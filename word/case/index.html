Case: meaning, synonyms and examples

Case is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An occurrence of something.

In Hindi
मामला; डिब्बा
Synonyms
instance, example, event, lawsuit, suit, cause, causa, subject, guinea pig, caseful
Word family
casing

Case as a noun

  1. An occurrence of something.

    “It was a case of bad judgment.” “Another instance occurred yesterday.” “But there is always the famous example of the Smiths.”

    Synonyms: instance, example · A kind of: happening · Kinds: humiliation, piece, time

  2. A special set of circumstances.

    “In that event, the first possibility is excluded.” “It may rain in which case the picnic will be canceled.”

    Synonyms: event · A kind of: circumstance

  3. A comprehensive term for any proceeding in a court of law whereby an individual seeks a legal remedy.

    “The family brought suit against the landlord.”

    Synonyms: lawsuit, suit, cause, causa · A kind of: proceeding · Kinds: civil suit, class action, countersuit, criminal suit, moot, paternity suit

  4. The actual state of things.

    “That was not the case.”

    A kind of: fact

  5. A portable container for carrying several objects.

    “The musicians left their instrument cases backstage.”

    A kind of: container · Kinds: baggage, briefcase, cardcase, cigarette case, compact, dispatch case, glasses case, gun case

  6. A person requiring professional services.

    “A typical case was the suburban housewife described by a marriage counselor.”

    A kind of: person · Kinds: client, patient, referral, welfare case

  7. A person who is subjected to experimental or other observational procedures; someone who is an object of investigation.

    “The subjects for this investigation were selected randomly.” “The cases that we studied were drawn from two different communities.”

    Synonyms: subject, guinea pig · A kind of: person

  8. A problem requiring investigation.

    “Perry Mason solved the case of the missing heir.”

    A kind of: problem

  9. A statement of facts and reasons used to support an argument.

    “He stated his case clearly.”

    A kind of: argument

  10. The quantity contained in a case.

    Synonyms: caseful · A kind of: containerful

  11. Nouns or pronouns or adjectives (often marked by inflection) related in some way to other words in a sentence.

    Synonyms: grammatical case · A kind of: grammatical category · Kinds: nominative, oblique

  12. A specific state of mind that is temporary.

    “A case of the jitters.”

    A kind of: state of mind

  13. A person of a specified kind (usually with many eccentricities).

    “A real character.” “A strange character.” “A friendly eccentric.”

    Synonyms: character, eccentric, type · A kind of: adult

  14. A specific size and style of type within a type family.

    Synonyms: font, fount, typeface, face · A kind of: type · Kinds: unicameral script, bicameral script, typewriter font, proportional font, font cartridge, boldface, italic, screen font

Case as a verb

  1. Look over, usually with the intention to rob.

    “They men cased the housed.”

    A kind of: inspect

  2. Enclose in, or as if in, a case.

    “My feet were encased in mud.”

    Synonyms: encase, incase · A kind of: enclose · Kinds: pack, box, sack, crate

Phrases and compounds with "case"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in case (adverb) If there happens to be need.
  • case law (noun) A system of jurisprudence based on judicial precedents rather than statutory laws.
  • gun case (noun) A case for storing a gun.
  • nut case (noun) A whimsically eccentric person.
  • case load (noun) The number of cases handled in a given period of time (as by a court or agency).
  • case shot (noun) A metallic cylinder packed with shot and used as ammunition in a firearm.
  • gear case (noun) The shell (metal casing) in which a train of gears is sealed.
  • test case (noun) A representative legal action whose outcome is likely to become a precedent.
  • tool case (noun) A box or chest or cabinet for holding hand tools.
  • wing case (noun) Either of the horny front wings in beetles and some other insects which cover and protect the functional hind wings.
  • case knife (noun) A metal blade with a handle; used as cutlery.
  • case study (noun) A careful study of some social unit (as a corporation or division within a corporation) that attempts to determine what factors led to its success or failure.
  • index case (noun) The earliest documented case of a disease that is included in an epidemiological study.
  • spore case (noun) Organ containing or producing spores.
  • watch case (noun) The metal case in which the works of a watch are housed.
  • dative case (noun) The category of nouns serving as the indirect object of a verb.
