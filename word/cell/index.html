Cell: meaning, synonyms and examples

Cell is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any small compartment.

In Hindi
कोशिका; कोठरी
Synonyms
electric cell, cadre, cellular telephone, cellular phone, cellphone, mobile phone, cubicle, jail cell, prison cell
Word family
cellular

Cell as a noun

  1. Any small compartment.

    “The cells of a honeycomb.”

    A kind of: compartment

  2. (biology) the basic structural and functional unit of all organisms; they may exist as independent units of life (as in monads) or may form colonies or tissues as in higher plants and animals.

    A kind of: living thing · Kinds: recombinant, blastomere, flagellated cell, fiber, beta cell, somatic cell, blastema, zygote

  3. A device that delivers an electric current as the result of a chemical reaction.

    Synonyms: electric cell · A kind of: electrical device · Kinds: electrolytic cell, fuel cell, solar cell, storage cell, voltaic cell

  4. A small unit serving as part of or as the nucleus of a larger political movement.

    Synonyms: cadre · A kind of: political unit · Kinds: sleeper cell

  5. A hand-held mobile radiotelephone for use in an area divided into small sections, each with its own short-range transmitter/receiver.

    Synonyms: cellular telephone, cellular phone, cellphone, mobile phone · A kind of: radiotelephone

  6. Small room in which a monk or nun lives.

    Synonyms: cubicle · A kind of: room

  7. A room where a prisoner is kept.

    Synonyms: jail cell, prison cell · A kind of: room · Kinds: bullpen, cooler, dungeon, guardroom, hold, sweatbox

Phrases and compounds with "cell"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • b cell (noun) A lymphocyte derived from bone marrow that provides humoral immunity; it recognizes free antigen molecules in solution and matures into plasma cells that secrete immunoglobulin (antibodies) that inactivate the antigens.
  • t cell (noun) A small lymphocyte developed in the thymus; it orchestrates the immune system's response to infected or malignant cells.
  • air cell (noun) A tiny sac for holding air in the lungs; formed by the terminal dilation of tiny air passageways.
  • dry cell (noun) A small Leclanche cell containing no free liquid; the electrolyte is a paste and the negative zinc pole forms the container of the cell; used in flashlights, portable radios, etc.
  • egg cell (noun) The female reproductive cell; the female gamete.
  • fat cell (noun) Cells composed of fat.
  • rod cell (noun) A visual receptor cell that is sensitive to dim light.
  • wet cell (noun) A primary voltaic cell having a liquid electrolyte.
  • beta cell (noun) A cell that produces insulin in the isles of Langerhans in the pancreas.
  • bone cell (noun) A cell that is part of a bone.
  • cell wall (noun) A rigid layer of polysaccharides enclosing the membrane of plant and prokaryotic cells; maintains the shape of the cell and serves as a protective barrier.
  • cell-free (adjective) Lacking cells.
  • cell-like (adjective) Resembling a cell.
  • cone cell (noun) A visual receptor cell in the retina that is sensitive to bright light and to color.
  • fuel cell (noun) Cell that produces electricity by oxidation of fuel (hydrogen and oxygen or zinc and air); often used in electric cars.
  • germ cell (noun) A spermatozoon or an ovum; a cell responsible for transmitting DNA to the next generation.
