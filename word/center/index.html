Center: meaning, synonyms and examples

Center is used as a noun (14 senses), verb (3 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An area that is approximately central within some larger region.

In Hindi
केंद्र
Synonyms
centre, middle, heart, eye, center field, centerfield, midpoint, kernel, substance, core
Opposites
right, left
Word family
central, centric, centrical, centering

Center as a noun

  1. An area that is approximately central within some larger region.

    “It is in the center of town.” “They ran forward into the heart of the struggle.” “They were in the eye of the storm.”

    Synonyms: centre, middle, heart, eye · A kind of: area · Kinds: center stage, city center, storm center, financial center, hub, inner city, medical center, midfield

  2. The piece of ground in the outfield directly ahead of the catcher.

    “He hit the ball to deep center.”

    Synonyms: center field, centerfield · A kind of: tract

  3. A building dedicated to a particular activity.

    “They were raising money to build a new center for research.”

    Synonyms: centre · A kind of: building · Kinds: burn center, call center, community center, conference center, control center, research center, service club, settlement house

  4. A point equidistant from the ends of a line or the extremities of a figure.

    Synonyms: centre, midpoint · A kind of: point · Kinds: bight, center of curvature, bowels, bull's eye, center of gravity, center of mass, core, navel

  5. The choicest or most essential or most vital part of some idea or experience.

    “The gist of the prosecutor's argument.” “The heart and soul of the Republican Party.” “The nub of the story.”

    Synonyms: kernel, substance, core, centre, essence, gist, heart, heart and soul · A kind of: content · Kinds: bare bones, hypostasis, quiddity, quintessence, stuff

  6. The object upon which interest and attention focuses.

    “His stories made him the center of the party.”

    Synonyms: centre, center of attention, centre of attention · A kind of: object · Kinds: conversation piece, crosshairs, cynosure, eye-catcher

  7. A cluster of nerve cells governing a specific bodily process.

    “In most people the speech center is in the left hemisphere.”

    Synonyms: centre, nerve center, nerve centre · A kind of: neural structure · Kinds: auditory center, rhinencephalon, superior colliculus, inferior colliculus, respiratory center

  8. The middle of a military or naval formation.

    “They had to reinforce the center.”

    A kind of: formation

  9. (basketball) the person who plays center on a basketball team.

    A kind of: basketball player

  10. (football) the person who plays center on the line of scrimmage and snaps the ball to the quarterback.

    “The center fumbled the handoff.”

    Synonyms: snapper · A kind of: lineman

  11. A place where some particular activity is concentrated.

    “They received messages from several centers.”

    Synonyms: centre · A kind of: place · Kinds: nerve center, capital

  12. Politically moderate persons; centrists.

    A kind of: class

  13. (ice hockey) the person who plays center on a hockey team.

    A kind of: hockey player

  14. The sweet central portion of a piece of candy that is enclosed in chocolate or some other covering.

    Synonyms: centre · A kind of: sweet

Center as a verb

  1. Center upon.

    “Her entire attention centered on her children.” “Our day revolved around our work.”

    Synonyms: focus on, center on, revolve around, revolve about, concentrate on · A kind of: refer

  2. Direct one's attention on something.

    “Please focus on your studies and not on your hobbies.”

    Synonyms: concentrate, focus, centre, pore, rivet · A kind of: think · Kinds: steep, recall, think, zoom in, listen

  3. Move into the center.

    “That vase in the picture is not centered.”

    Synonyms: centre · A kind of: move

Center as an adjective

  1. Equally distant from the extremes.

    Synonyms: halfway, middle, midway · Similar: central

  2. Of or belonging to neither the right nor the left politically or intellectually.

    Opposite: right, left · Similar: centrist

Phrases and compounds with "center"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • center on (verb) Center upon.
  • center bit (noun) A bit with a sharp center point for guidance and two side cutters.
  • off-center (adjective) Situated away from the center or axis.
  • burn center (noun) A center where patients with severe burns can be treated.
  • call center (noun) A center equipped to handle a large volume of telephone calls (especially for taking orders or serving customers).
  • center line (noun) A line that bisects a plane figure.
  • city center (noun) The central part of a city.
  • dead center (noun) The position of a crank when it is in line with the connecting rod and not exerting torque.
  • center field (noun) The piece of ground in the outfield directly ahead of the catcher.
  • center punch (verb) Make a small hole in something as a guide for a drill.
  • center stage (noun) A position of prominence or importance.
  • civic center (noun) A center where the members of a community can gather for social or cultural activities.
  • nerve center (noun) A center that provides information and control.
  • storm center (noun) A center of trouble or disturbance.
  • urban center (noun) A large and densely populated urban area; may include several independent administrative districts.
  • center spread (noun) The spread at the center of a magazine.
