Cloud: meaning, synonyms and examples

Cloud is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any collection of particles (e.g., smoke or dust) or gases that is visible.

In Hindi
बादल
Synonyms
swarm, overcast, obscure, befog, becloud, obnubilate, haze over, fog, mist, defile
Word family
cloudy, clouding

Cloud as a noun

  1. Any collection of particles (e.g., smoke or dust) or gases that is visible.

    A kind of: physical phenomenon · Kinds: coma, nebula, aerosol, cosmic dust, dust cloud

  2. A visible mass of water or ice particles suspended at a considerable altitude.

    A kind of: atmospheric phenomenon · Kinds: cirrocumulus, cirrostratus, cirrus, cloud bank, contrail, cumulonimbus, cumulus, nacreous cloud

  3. Out of touch with reality.

    “His head was in the clouds.”

    A kind of: unreality

  4. A cause of worry or gloom or trouble.

    “The only cloud on the horizon was the possibility of dissent by the French.”

    A kind of: gloom

  5. Suspicion affecting your reputation.

    “After that mistake he was under a cloud.”

    A kind of: suspicion

  6. A group of many things in the air or on the ground.

    “A swarm of insects obscured the light.” “Clouds of blossoms.” “It discharged a cloud of spores.”

    Synonyms: swarm · A kind of: group · Kinds: infestation

Cloud as a verb

  1. Make overcast or cloudy.

    “Fall weather often overcasts our beaches.”

    Synonyms: overcast · A kind of: darken · Kinds: fog up, haze

  2. Make less visible or unclear.

    “The stars are obscured by the clouds.” “The big elm tree obscures our view of the valley.”

    Synonyms: obscure, befog, becloud, obnubilate, haze over, fog, mist · A kind of: hide · Kinds: overshadow

  3. Billow up in the form of a cloud.

    “The smoke clouded above the houses.”

    A kind of: billow

  4. Make gloomy or depressed.

    “Their faces were clouded with sadness.”

    A kind of: affect

  5. Place under suspicion or cast doubt upon.

    “Sully someone's reputation.”

    Synonyms: defile, sully, corrupt, taint · A kind of: mar

  6. Make less clear.

    “The stroke clouded memories of her youth.”

    A kind of: change

  7. Colour with streaks or blotches of different shades.

    Synonyms: mottle, dapple · A kind of: spot · Kinds: harlequin, stipple

  8. Make milky or dull.

    “The chemical clouded the liquid to which it was added.”

    A kind of: dull

Phrases and compounds with "cloud"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • cloud up (verb) Become covered with clouds.
  • red cloud (noun) Leader of the Oglala who resisted the development of a trail through Wyoming and Montana by the United States government (1822-1909).
  • war cloud (noun) An ominous sign that war threatens.
  • cloud bank (noun) A layer of clouds seen from a distance.
  • cloud nine (noun) A state of extreme happiness.
  • cloud over (verb) Become covered with clouds.
  • dust cloud (noun) A cloud of dust suspended in the air.
  • oort cloud (noun) (astronomy) a hypothetical huge collection of comets orbiting the sun far beyond the orbit of Pluto; perturbations (as by other stars) can upset a comet's orbit and may send it tumbling toward the sun.
  • rain cloud (noun) A dark grey cloud bearing rain.
  • cloud cover (noun) The state of the sky when it is covered by clouds.
  • cloud grass (noun) Spanish grass with light feathery panicles grown for dried bouquets.
  • saint cloud (noun) A town in central Minnesota on the Mississippi River; granite quarries.
  • storm cloud (noun) A heavy dark cloud presaging rain or a storm.
  • cirrus cloud (noun) A wispy white cloud (usually of fine ice crystals) at a high altitude (4 to 8 miles).
  • cloud seeder (noun) A person who seeds clouds.
  • nimbus cloud (noun) A dark grey cloud bearing rain.
