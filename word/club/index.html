Club: meaning, synonyms and examples

Club is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A team of professional baseball players who play and travel together.

In Hindi
क्लब; डंडा
Synonyms
baseball club, ball club, nine, social club, society, guild, gild, lodge, order, clubhouse
Word family
clubby

Club as a noun

  1. A team of professional baseball players who play and travel together.

    “Each club played six home games with teams in its own division.”

    Synonyms: baseball club, ball club, nine · A kind of: baseball team

  2. A formal association of people with similar interests.

    “He joined a golf club.” “They formed a small lunch society.” “Men from the fraternal order will staff the soup kitchen today.”

    Synonyms: social club, society, guild, gild, lodge, order · A kind of: association · Kinds: athenaeum, bookclub, chapter, chess club, country club, fraternity, glee club, golf club

  3. Stout stick that is larger at one end.

    “He carried a club in self defense.” “He felt as if he had been hit with a club.”

    A kind of: stick · Kinds: bat, bludgeon, cudgel, knobkerrie, lathi, truncheon

  4. A building that is occupied by a social club.

    “The clubhouse needed a new roof.”

    Synonyms: clubhouse · A kind of: building

  5. Golf equipment used by a golfer to hit a golf ball.

    Synonyms: golf club, golf-club · A kind of: golf equipment · Kinds: iron, wood

  6. A playing card in the minor suit that has one or more black trefoils on it.

    “He led a small club.” “Clubs were trumps.”

    A kind of: playing card

  7. A spot that is open late at night and that provides entertainment (as singers or dancers) as well as dancing and food and drink.

    “Don't expect a good meal at a cabaret.” “The gossip columnist got his information by visiting nightclubs every night.” “He played the drums at a jazz club.”

    Synonyms: cabaret, nightclub, night club, nightspot · A kind of: spot · Kinds: honkytonk, supper club

Club as a verb

  1. Unite with a common purpose.

    “The two men clubbed together.”

    A kind of: unite

  2. Gather and spend time together.

    “They always club together.”

    A kind of: meet

  3. Strike with a club or a bludgeon.

    Synonyms: bludgeon · A kind of: hit

  4. Gather into a club-like mass.

    “Club hair.”

    A kind of: gather
