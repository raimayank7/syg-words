Clutch: meaning, synonyms and examples

Clutch is used as a verb (3 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Take hold of; grab.

In Hindi
कसकर पकड़ना; क्लच
Synonyms
seize, prehend, cling to, hold close, hold tight, get hold of, clasp, clench, clutches, grasp

Clutch as a verb

  1. Take hold of; grab.

    “The sales clerk quickly seized the money on the counter.” “She clutched her purse.” “The mother seized her child by the arm.”

    Synonyms: seize, prehend · A kind of: take · Kinds: nab, rack, claw, collar, get, collar, clasp, grip

  2. Hold firmly, usually with one's hands.

    “She clutched my arm when she got scared.”

    Synonyms: cling to, hold close, hold tight · A kind of: hold · Kinds: cuddle

  3. Affect.

    “Fear seized the prisoners.” “The patient was seized with unbearable pains.” “He was seized with a dreadful disease.”

    Synonyms: seize, get hold of · A kind of: overwhelm

Clutch as a noun

  1. The act of grasping.

    “He released his clasp on my arm.” “He has a strong grip for an old man.” “She kept a firm hold on the railing.”

    Synonyms: clasp, clench, clutches, grasp, grip, hold · A kind of: grasping · Kinds: chokehold, embrace, wrestling hold

  2. A tense critical situation.

    “He is a good man in the clutch.”

    A kind of: temporary state

  3. A number of birds hatched at the same time.

    A kind of: brood

  4. A collection of things or persons to be handled together.

    Synonyms: batch · A kind of: collection · Kinds: schmeer

  5. A woman's strapless purse that is carried in the hand.

    Synonyms: clutch bag · A kind of: bag

  6. A pedal or lever that engages or disengages a rotating shaft and a driving mechanism.

    “He smoothely released the clutch with one foot and stepped on the gas with the other.”

    Synonyms: clutch pedal · A kind of: pedal

  7. A coupling that connects or disconnects driving and driven parts of a driving mechanism.

    “This year's model has an improved clutch.”

    A kind of: coupling · Kinds: freewheel, friction clutch

Phrases and compounds with "clutch"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • clutch bag (noun) A woman's strapless purse that is carried in the hand.
  • cone clutch (noun) A friction clutch in which the frictional surfaces are cone-shaped.
  • disk clutch (noun) A friction clutch in which the frictional surfaces are disks.
  • slip clutch (noun) A friction clutch that will slip when the torque is too great.
  • clutch pedal (noun) A pedal or lever that engages or disengages a rotating shaft and a driving mechanism.
  • friction clutch (noun) A clutch in which one part turns the other by the friction between them.
  • cone friction clutch (noun) A friction clutch in which the frictional surfaces are cone-shaped.
  • slip friction clutch (noun) A friction clutch that will slip when the torque is too great.
