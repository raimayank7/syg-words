Coast: meaning, synonyms and examples

Coast is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The shore of a sea or ocean.

In Hindi
समुद्र-तट
Synonyms
seashore, seacoast, sea-coast, slide, glide
Word family
coastal, coaster

Coast as a noun

  1. The shore of a sea or ocean.

    Synonyms: seashore, seacoast, sea-coast · A kind of: shore · Kinds: littoral, landfall, seaside, tideland

  2. A slope down which sleds may coast.

    “When it snowed they made a coast on the golf course.”

    A kind of: slope

  3. The area within view.

    “The coast is clear.”

    A kind of: view

  4. The act of moving smoothly along a surface while remaining in contact with it.

    “His slide didn't stop until the bottom of the hill.” “The children lined up for a coast down the snowy slope.”

    Synonyms: slide, glide · A kind of: motion · Kinds: skid, snowboarding

Coast as a verb

  1. Move effortlessly; by force of gravity.

    A kind of: glide · Kinds: freewheel

Phrases and compounds with "coast"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • sea-coast (noun) The shore of a sea or ocean.
  • coast lily (noun) Orange-flowered lily of Pacific coast of United States.
  • east coast (noun) The eastern seaboard of the United States (especially the strip between Boston and Washington D.C.).
  • gold coast (noun) A republic in West Africa on the Gulf of Guinea.
  • gulf coast (noun) A seashore of the Gulf of Mexico.
  • west coast (noun) The western seaboard of the United States from Washington to southern California.
  • coast range (noun) A string of mountain ranges along the Pacific coast of North America from southeastern Alaska to Lower California.
  • ivory coast (noun) A republic in western Africa on the Gulf of Guinea; one of the most prosperous and politically stable countries in Africa.
  • adelie coast (noun) A costal region of Antarctica to the south of Australia; noted for its large colonies of penguins.
  • coast banksia (noun) Shrubby tree with silky foliage and spikes of cylindrical yellow nectarous flowers.
  • coast redwood (noun) Lofty evergreen of United States coastal foothills from Oregon to Big Sur; it flourishes in wet, rainy, foggy habitats.
  • pacific coast (noun) A coast of the Pacific Ocean.
  • atlantic coast (noun) A coast of the Atlantic Ocean.
  • coast boykinia (noun) Plant with leaves mostly at the base and openly branched clusters of small white flowers; western North America.
  • coast live oak (noun) Highly variable often shrubby evergreen oak of coastal zone of western North America having small thick usually spiny-toothed dark-green leaves.
  • coast polypody (noun) Stiff leathery-leaved fern of western North America having ovate fronds parted to the midrib.
