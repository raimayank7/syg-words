Color: meaning, synonyms and examples

Color is used as a noun (8 senses), verb (6 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A visual attribute of things that results from the light they emit or transmit or reflect.

In Hindi
रंग
Synonyms
colour, coloring, colouring, vividness, coloration, colouration, people of color, people of colour, semblance, gloss
Opposites
colorlessness, discolor, black-and-white
Word family

Color as a noun

  1. A visual attribute of things that results from the light they emit or transmit or reflect.

    “A white color is made up of many different wavelengths of light.”

    Synonyms: colour, coloring, colouring · Opposite: colorlessness · A kind of: visual property · Kinds: primary color, heather mixture, mottle, shade, chromatic color, achromatic color, coloration, complexion

  2. Interest and variety and intensity.

    “The Puritan Period was lacking in color.” “The characters were delineated with exceptional vividness.”

    Synonyms: colour, vividness · A kind of: interest

  3. The timbre of a musical sound.

    “The recording fails to capture the true color of the original music.”

    Synonyms: colour, coloration, colouration · A kind of: timbre

  4. A race with skin pigmentation different from the white race (especially Blacks).

    Synonyms: colour, people of color, people of colour · A kind of: race

  5. An outward or token appearance or form that is deliberately misleading.

    “He hoped his claims would have a semblance of authenticity.” “He tried to give his falsehood the gloss of moral sanction.” “The situation soon took on a different color.”

    Synonyms: semblance, gloss, colour · A kind of: appearance · Kinds: color of law, simulacrum, face value, guise, disguise, verisimilitude

  6. Any material used for its color.

    “She used a different color for the trim.”

    Synonyms: coloring material, colouring material, colour · A kind of: material · Kinds: paint, indicator, mordant, dye, tincture, hematochrome, pigment, pigment

  7. (physics) the characteristic of quarks that determines their role in the strong interaction.

    “Each flavor of quarks comes in three colors.”

    Synonyms: colour · A kind of: kind

  8. The appearance of objects (or light sources) described in terms of a person's perception of their hue and lightness (or brightness) and saturation.

    Synonyms: colour · A kind of: appearance

Color as a verb

  1. Add color to.

    “The child colored the drawings.” “Fall colored the trees.” “Colorize black and white film.”

    Synonyms: colorize, colorise, colourise, colourize, colour, color in, colour in · Opposite: discolor · A kind of: change · Kinds: blackwash, motley, polychrome, azure, purple, aurify, verdigris, pinkify

  2. Affect as in thought or feeling.

    “My personal feelings color my judgment in this case.” “The sadness tinged his life.”

    Synonyms: tinge, colour, distort · A kind of: affect

  3. Modify or bias.

    “His political ideas color his lectures.”

    Synonyms: colour · A kind of: influence

  4. Decorate with colors.

    “Color the walls with paint in warm tones.”

    Synonyms: colour, emblazon · A kind of: decorate · Kinds: miniate

  5. Give a deceptive explanation or excuse for.

    “Color a lie.”

    Synonyms: colour, gloss · A kind of: apologize

  6. Change color, often in an undesired manner.

    “The shirts discolored.”

    Synonyms: discolor, discolour, colour · A kind of: change · Kinds: blush, pale, tan, sunburn, whiten, blacken, turn, silver

Color as an adjective

  1. Having or capable of producing colors.

    “Color film.” “He rented a color television.” “Marvelous color illustrations.”

    Synonyms: colour · Opposite: black-and-white

Phrases and compounds with "color"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • color in (verb) Add color to.
  • color tv (noun) A television that transmits images in color.
  • color bar (noun) Barrier preventing blacks from participating in various activities with whites.
  • off-color (adjective) In violation of good taste even verging on the indecent.
  • oil color (noun) Oil paint containing pigment that is used by an artist.
  • vat color (noun) A water-insoluble dye that is applied by reducing the dye to an alkaline form, applying the dye, then regenerating the insoluble dye by oxidation in the material; used for dyeing cotton.
  • color code (noun) System using colors to designate classifications.
  • color line (noun) Barrier preventing blacks from participating in various activities with whites.
  • color tube (noun) A television tube that displays images in full color.
  • color wash (noun) A wash of whitewash or other water-base paint tinted with a colored pigment.
  • food color (noun) A digestible substance used to give color to food.
  • skin color (noun) The coloring of a person's face.
  • basic color (noun) A dye that is considered to be a base because the chromophore is part of a positive ion.
  • color chart (noun) A chart displaying colors.
  • color force (noun) (physics) the interaction that binds protons and neutrons together in the nuclei of atoms; mediated by gluons.
  • color guard (noun) A ceremonial escort for the (regimental) colors.
