Column: meaning, synonyms and examples

Column is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A line of units following one after another.

In Hindi
स्तंभ, खंभा; कॉलम
Synonyms
chromatography column, tower, pillar, editorial, newspaper column
Word family
columnar, columnist

Column as a noun

  1. A line of units following one after another.

    A kind of: file

  2. A vertical glass tube used in column chromatography; a mixture is poured in the top and washed through a stationary substance where components of the mixture are adsorbed selectively to form colored bands.

    Synonyms: chromatography column · A kind of: tube

  3. A vertical array of numbers or other information.

    “He added a column of numbers.”

    A kind of: array

  4. Anything that approximates the shape of a column or tower.

    “The test tube held a column of white powder.” “A tower of dust rose above the horizon.” “A thin pillar of smoke betrayed their campsite.”

    Synonyms: tower, pillar · A kind of: shape · Kinds: columella, hoodoo

  5. An article giving opinions or perspectives.

    Synonyms: editorial, newspaper column · A kind of: article · Kinds: agony column

  6. A vertical cylindrical structure standing alone and not supporting anything (such as a monument).

    Synonyms: pillar · A kind of: structure · Kinds: obelisk, totem pole

  7. (architecture) a tall vertical cylindrical structure standing upright and used to support a structure.

    Synonyms: pillar · A kind of: upright · Kinds: atlas, caryatid, newel, pilaster, pile, support column

  8. A page or text that is vertically divided.

    “The newspaper devoted several columns to the subject.” “The bookkeeper used pages that were divided into columns.”

    A kind of: text

  9. Any tubular or pillar-like supporting structure in the body.

    A kind of: skeletal structure

  • lally column (noun) Support column consisting of a steel cylinder filled with concrete.
  • spinal column (noun) The series of vertebrae forming the axis of the skeleton and protecting the spinal cord.
  • support column (noun) A column that supports a heavy weight.

  • chromatography column (noun) A vertical glass tube used in column chromatography; a mixture is poured in the top and washed through a stationary substance where components of the mixture are adsorbed selectively to form colored bands.
  • column chromatography (noun) Chromatography that uses selective adsorption by a column of powders.
