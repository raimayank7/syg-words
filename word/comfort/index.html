Comfort: meaning, synonyms and examples

Comfort is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A state of being relaxed and feeling no pain.

In Hindi
आराम; दिलासा
Synonyms
comfortableness, consolation, solace, ease, quilt, comforter, puff, soothe, console
Opposites
discomfort
Word family
comforter

Comfort as a noun

  1. A state of being relaxed and feeling no pain.

    “He is a man who enjoys his comfort.” “She longed for the comfortableness of her armchair.”

    Synonyms: comfortableness · Opposite: discomfort · A kind of: condition · Kinds: relief, solace, coziness, convenience

  2. A feeling of freedom from worry or disappointment.

    A kind of: pleasure · Kinds: consolation, relief

  3. The act of consoling; giving relief in affliction.

    “His presence was a consolation to her.”

    Synonyms: consolation, solace · A kind of: relief

  4. A freedom from financial difficulty that promotes a comfortable state.

    “A life of luxury and ease.” “He had all the material comforts of this world.”

    Synonyms: ease · A kind of: affluence · Kinds: lap of luxury

  5. Satisfaction or physical well-being provided by a person or thing.

    “His friendship was a comfort.” “A padded chair was one of the room's few comforts.”

    A kind of: gratification

  6. Bedding made of two layers of cloth filled with stuffing and stitched together.

    Synonyms: quilt, comforter, puff · A kind of: bedclothes · Kinds: eiderdown, patchwork

  7. Assistance, such as that provided to an enemy or to a known criminal.

    “It gave comfort to the enemy.”

    A kind of: aid

Comfort as a verb

  1. Give moral or emotional strength to.

    Synonyms: soothe, console, solace · Kinds: calm, still

  2. Lessen pain or discomfort; alleviate.

    “Ease the pain in your legs.”

    Synonyms: ease · A kind of: relieve

Phrases and compounds with "comfort"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • cold comfort (noun) Very limited consolation or empathy.
  • comfort food (noun) Food that is simply prepared and gives a sense of wellbeing; typically food with a high sugar or carbohydrate content that is associated with childhood or with home cooking.
  • comfort station (noun) A toilet that is available to the public.
  • louis comfort tiffany (noun) United States artist who developed Tiffany glass (1848-1933).
