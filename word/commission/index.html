Commission: meaning, synonyms and examples

Commission is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A special group delegated to consider some matter.

In Hindi
आयोग; कमीशन
Synonyms
committee, commissioning, deputation, delegation, delegacy, mission, charge, direction, military commission, perpetration
Word family
commissioner, commit, commissioning

Commission as a noun

  1. A special group delegated to consider some matter.

    “A committee is a group that keeps minutes and loses hours.”

    Synonyms: committee · A kind of: administrative unit · Kinds: blue ribbon commission, board, election commission, fairness commission, planning commission, conservancy, select committee, subcommittee

  2. A fee for services rendered based on a percentage of an amount received or collected or agreed to be paid (as distinguished from a salary).

    “He works on commission.”

    A kind of: fee

  3. The act of granting authority to undertake certain functions.

    Synonyms: commissioning · A kind of: authorization · Kinds: mandate

  4. The state of being in good working order and ready for operation.

    “Put the ships into commission.” “The motor was out of commission.”

    A kind of: operation

  5. A group of representatives or delegates.

    Synonyms: deputation, delegation, delegacy, mission · A kind of: organization · Kinds: diplomatic mission, embassy

  6. A formal statement of a command or injunction to do something.

    “The judge's charge to the jury.”

    Synonyms: charge, direction · A kind of: command · Kinds: misdirection

  7. An official document issued by a government and conferring on the recipient the rank of an officer in the armed forces.

    Synonyms: military commission · A kind of: certificate

  8. The act of committing a crime.

    Synonyms: perpetration, committal · A kind of: crime

  9. A special assignment that is given to a person or group.

    “A confidential mission to London.” “His charge was deliver a message.”

    Synonyms: mission, charge · A kind of: assignment · Kinds: fool's errand, mission impossible

Commission as a verb

  1. Put into commission; equip for service; of ships.

    A kind of: equip

  2. Place an order for.

    A kind of: order

  3. Charge with a task.

    A kind of: authorize

Phrases and compounds with "commission"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • commission plan (noun) A municipal government that combines legislative and executive authority in the members of a commission.
  • high commission (noun) An embassy of one British Commonwealth country to another.
  • ecosoc commission (noun) A commission of the Economic and Social Council of the United Nations.
  • zoning commission (noun) A commission delegated to supervise the zoning of areas for residential or commercial use.
  • election commission (noun) A commission delegated to supervise an election.
  • fairness commission (noun) A commission delegated to ensure opportunities for the expression of opposing views.
  • military commission (noun) An official document issued by a government and conferring on the recipient the rank of an officer in the armed forces.
  • planning commission (noun) A commission delegated to propose plans for future activities and developments.
  • population commission (noun) The commission of the Economic and Social Council of the United Nations that is concerned with population control.
  • blue ribbon commission (noun) An independent and exclusive commission of nonpartisan statesmen and experts formed to investigate some important governmental issue.
  • postal rate commission (noun) An independent federal agency that recommends changes in postal rates.
  • statistical commission (noun) The commission of the Economic and Social Council of the United Nations that is concerned with statistical data from member nations.
  • atomic energy commission (noun) A former executive agency (from 1946 to 1974) that was responsible for research into atomic energy and its peacetime uses in the United States.
  • federal trade commission (noun) An independent agency of the United States federal government that maintains fair and free competition; enforces federal antitrust laws; educates the public about identity theft.
  • commission on human rights (noun) The commission of the Economic and Social Council of the United Nations that is concerned with human rights.
  • nuclear regulatory commission (noun) An independent federal agency created in 1974 to license and regulate nuclear power plants.
