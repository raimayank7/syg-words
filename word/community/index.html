Community: meaning, synonyms and examples

Community is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A group of people living in a particular local area.

In Hindi
समुदाय
Synonyms
community of interests, residential district, residential area, biotic community
Word family
communal

Community as a noun

  1. A group of people living in a particular local area.

    “The team is drawn from all parts of the community.”

    A kind of: gathering · Kinds: parish, convent, house, speech community, neighborhood, village, hamlet, horde

  2. Common ownership.

    “They shared a community of possessions.”

    A kind of: ownership

  3. A group of nations having common interests.

    “They hoped to join the NATO community.”

    A kind of: world organization

  4. Agreement as to goals.

    “The preachers and the bootleggers found they had a community of interests.”

    Synonyms: community of interests · A kind of: agreement

  5. A district where people live; occupied primarily by private residences.

    Synonyms: residential district, residential area · A kind of: district · Kinds: planned community, uptown, suburb, exurbia, tenement district, warren

  6. (ecology) a group of interdependent organisms inhabiting the same region and interacting with each other.

    Synonyms: biotic community · A kind of: group · Kinds: biome

Phrases and compounds with "community"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • community chest (noun) A charity supported by individual subscriptions; defrays the demands on a community for social welfare.
  • legal community (noun) The body of individuals qualified to practice law in a particular jurisdiction.
  • biotic community (noun) (ecology) a group of interdependent organisms inhabiting the same region and interacting with each other.
  • community center (noun) A center where the members of a community can gather for social or cultural activities.
  • speech community (noun) People sharing a given language or dialect.
  • bedroom community (noun) A community where many commuters live.
  • community college (noun) A nonresidential junior college offering a curriculum fitted to the needs of the community.
  • community service (noun) A service that is performed for the benefit of the public or its institutions.
  • medical community (noun) The body of individuals who are qualified to practice medicine.
  • planned community (noun) A residential district that is planned for a certain class of residents.
  • business community (noun) The body of individuals who manage businesses.
  • community property (noun) Property and income belonging jointly to a married couple.
  • european community (noun) An international organization of European countries formed after World War II to reduce trade barriers and increase cooperation among its members.
  • retirement community (noun) A planned community for residents who have retired from an active working life.
  • community of scholars (noun) The body of individuals holding advanced academic degrees.
  • community of interests (noun) Agreement as to goals.
