Concern: meaning, synonyms and examples

Concern is used as a verb (2 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be relevant to.

In Hindi
चिंता; संबंध
Synonyms
refer, pertain, relate, come to, bear on, touch, touch on, have-to doe with, interest, occupy
Opposites
unconcern

Concern as a verb

  1. Be relevant to.

    “There were lots of questions referring to her talk.” “My remark pertained to your earlier comments.”

    Synonyms: refer, pertain, relate, come to, bear on, touch, touch on, have-to doe with · Kinds: focus on, apply, involve, matter to

  2. Be on the mind of.

    “I worry about the second Germanic consonant shift.”

    Synonyms: interest, occupy, worry

Concern as a noun

  1. Something that interests you because it is important or affects you.

    “The safety of the ship is the captain's concern.”

    A kind of: interest · Kinds: worldly concern, matter, personal business, part, point of honor

  2. An anxious feeling.

    “Care had aged him.” “They hushed it up out of fear of public reaction.”

    Synonyms: care, fear · A kind of: anxiety

  3. A feeling of sympathy for someone or something.

    “She felt strong concern for those less fortunate.”

    Opposite: unconcern · A kind of: sympathy · Kinds: solicitude, softheartedness

  4. Something or someone that causes anxiety; a source of unhappiness.

    “New York traffic is a constant concern.” “It's a major worry.”

    Synonyms: worry, headache, vexation · A kind of: negative stimulus · Kinds: bugaboo, burden, business

  5. A commercial or industrial enterprise and the people who constitute it.

    “He bought his brother's business.” “A small mom-and-pop business.” “A racially integrated business concern.”

    Synonyms: business, business concern, business organization, business organisation · A kind of: enterprise · Kinds: agency, brokerage, carrier, chain, firm, franchise, manufacturer, partnership

Phrases and compounds with "concern"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • self-concern (noun) Concern for your own interests and welfare.
  • banking concern (noun) A financial institution that accepts deposits and channels the money into lending activities.
  • earthly concern (noun) The concerns of this life as distinguished from heaven and the afterlife.
  • worldly concern (noun) The concerns of this life as distinguished from heaven and the afterlife.
  • business concern (noun) A commercial or industrial enterprise and the people who constitute it.
  • printing concern (noun) A company that does commercial printing.
  • packaging concern (noun) A company that packages goods for sale or shipment or storage.
