Conclusion: meaning, synonyms and examples

Conclusion is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A position or opinion or judgment reached after consideration.

In Hindi
निष्कर्ष
Synonyms
decision, determination, stopping point, finale, finis, finish, last, close, ending, ratiocination
Word family
conclude

Conclusion as a noun

  1. A position or opinion or judgment reached after consideration.

    “A decision unfavorable to the opposition.” “His conclusion took the evidence into account.” “Satisfied with the panel's determination.”

    Synonyms: decision, determination · A kind of: judgment · Kinds: predetermination

  2. An intuitive assumption.

    “Jump to a conclusion.”

    A kind of: assumption · Kinds: non sequitur

  3. The temporal end; the concluding time.

    “The stopping point of each round was signaled by a bell.” “The market was up at the finish.” “They were playing better at the close of the season.”

    Synonyms: stopping point, finale, finis, finish, last, close · A kind of: end

  4. Event whose occurrence ends something.

    “His death marked the ending of an era.” “When these final episodes are broadcast it will be the finish of the show.”

    Synonyms: ending, finish · A kind of: happening · Kinds: end, result, foregone conclusion, destruction, omega, stop, victory, defeat

  5. The proposition arrived at by logical reasoning (such as the proposition that must follow from the major and minor premises of a syllogism).

    Synonyms: ratiocination · A kind of: proposition

  6. The act of ending something.

    “The termination of the agreement.”

    Synonyms: termination, ending · A kind of: change of state · Kinds: demonetization, release, completion, retirement, relinquishment, dissolution, overthrow, adjournment

  7. A final settlement.

    “The conclusion of a business deal.” “The conclusion of the peace treaty.”

    A kind of: settlement

  8. The last section of a communication.

    “In conclusion I want to say...”

    Synonyms: end, close, closing, ending · A kind of: section · Kinds: anticlimax, epilogue, epilogue, peroration, finale

  9. The act of making up your mind about something.

    “The burden of decision was his.” “He drew his conclusions quickly.”

    Synonyms: decision, determination · A kind of: choice · Kinds: appointment, call, move, casting lots, resolution

Phrases and compounds with "conclusion"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in conclusion (adverb) The item at the end.
  • conclusion of law (noun) A finding as to the applicability of a rule of law to particular facts.
  • foregone conclusion (noun) An inevitable ending.
