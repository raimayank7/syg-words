Condition: meaning, synonyms and examples

Condition is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A state at a particular time.

In Hindi
हालत; शर्त
Synonyms
status, precondition, stipulation, circumstance, consideration, shape, term, experimental condition, discipline, train
Word family
conditioning, conditioner

Condition as a noun

  1. A state at a particular time.

    “A condition (or state) of disrepair.” “The current status of the arms negotiations.”

    Synonyms: status · A kind of: state · Kinds: diversity, anchorage, health, mode, niche, noise conditions, participation, prepossession

  2. An assumption on which rests the validity or effect of something else.

    Synonyms: precondition, stipulation · A kind of: premise · Kinds: boundary condition, provision

  3. A mode of being or form of existence of a person or thing.

    “The human condition.”

    A kind of: state · Kinds: stratification, ordinary, roots, lysogeny, fortune, amphidiploidy, diploidy, haploidy

  4. Information that should be kept in mind when making a decision.

    “Another consideration is the time it would take.”

    Synonyms: circumstance, consideration · A kind of: information · Kinds: justification, mitigating circumstance

  5. The state of (good) health (especially in the phrases `in condition' or `in shape' or `out of condition' or `out of shape').

    Synonyms: shape · A kind of: good health · Kinds: fitness

  6. An illness, disease, or other medical problem.

    “A heart condition.” “A skin condition.”

    A kind of: illness

  7. (usually plural) a statement of what is required as part of an agreement.

    “The contract set out the conditions of the lease.” “The terms of the treaty were generous.”

    Synonyms: term · A kind of: statement

  8. The procedure that is varied in order to estimate a variable's effect by comparison with a control condition.

    Synonyms: experimental condition · A kind of: procedure

Condition as a verb

  1. Establish a conditioned response.

    A kind of: teach

  2. Develop (children's) behavior by instruction and practice; especially to teach self-control.

    “Parents must discipline their children.” “Is this dog trained?”

    Synonyms: discipline, train, check · A kind of: develop · Kinds: mortify

  3. Specify as a condition or requirement in a contract or agreement; make an express demand or provision in an agreement.

    “The will stipulates that she can live in the house for the rest of her life.” “The contract stipulates the dates of the payments.”

    Synonyms: stipulate, qualify, specify · A kind of: contract · Kinds: provide

  4. Put into a better state.

    “He conditions old cars.”

    A kind of: better · Kinds: recondition

  5. Apply conditioner to in order to make smooth and shiny.

    “I condition my hair after washing it.”

    A kind of: shampoo

Phrases and compounds with "condition"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in condition (adjective) Physically fit.
  • air-condition (verb) Control the humidity and temperature of.
  • eye condition (noun) The condition of the optical properties of the eye.
  • toxic condition (noun) The physiological state produced by a poison or other toxic substance.
  • mental condition (noun) (psychology) a mental condition in which the qualities of a state are relatively constant even though the state itself may be dynamic.
  • control condition (noun) A standard against which other conditions can be compared in a scientific experiment.
  • weather condition (noun) The atmospheric conditions that comprise the state of the atmosphere in terms of temperature and wind and clouds and precipitation.
  • boundary condition (noun) (mathematics) a condition specified for the solution to a set of differential equations.
  • economic condition (noun) The condition of the economy.
  • physical condition (noun) The condition or state of the body or bodily functions.
  • sanitary condition (noun) The state of sanitation (clean or dirty).
  • essential condition (noun) A prerequisite.
  • financial condition (noun) The condition of (corporate or personal) finances.
  • hereditary condition (noun) A disease or disorder that is inherited genetically.
  • atmospheric condition (noun) The atmospheric conditions that comprise the state of the atmosphere in terms of temperature and wind and clouds and precipitation.
  • experimental condition (noun) The procedure that is varied in order to estimate a variable's effect by comparison with a control condition.
