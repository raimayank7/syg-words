Continue: meaning, synonyms and examples

Continue is used as a verb (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Continue a certain state, condition, or activity.

In Hindi
जारी रखना
Synonyms
go on, proceed, go along, keep, carry on, uphold, bear on, preserve, go forward, retain
Opposites
discontinue
Word family
continuation, continuance

Continue as a verb

  1. Continue a certain state, condition, or activity.

    “Keep on working!” “We continued to work into the night.” “Keep smiling.”

    Synonyms: go on, proceed, go along, keep · Opposite: discontinue · A kind of: act · Kinds: hold, run on, ride

  2. Continue talking.

    “I know it's hard,.” “But there is no choice.” “Carry on--pretend we are not in the room.”

    Synonyms: go on, carry on, proceed · A kind of: talk · Kinds: segue, ramble on

  3. Keep or maintain in unaltered condition; cause to remain or last.

    “Preserve the peace in the family.” “Continue the family tradition.” “Carry on the old traditions.”

    Synonyms: uphold, carry on, bear on, preserve · Opposite: discontinue · A kind of: keep · Kinds: resume, persevere, perpetuate, prolong, mummify

  4. Move ahead; travel onward in time or space.

    “We proceeded towards Washington.” “She continued in the direction of the hills.” “We are moving ahead in time now.”

    Synonyms: proceed, go forward · A kind of: travel · Kinds: head, trace, roar, limp, wander

  5. Allow to remain in a place or position or maintain a property or feature.

    “We cannot continue several servants any longer.” “She retains a lawyer.” “The family's fortune waned and they could not keep their household staff.”

    Synonyms: retain, keep, keep on · A kind of: prolong

  6. Do something repeatedly and showing no intention to stop.

    “We continued our research into the cause of the illness.” “The landlord persists in asking us to move.”

    Synonyms: persist in · A kind of: act

  7. Continue after an interruption.

    “The demonstration continued after a break for lunch.”

    A kind of: act

  8. Continue in a place, position, or situation.

    “After graduation, she stayed on in Cambridge as a student adviser.” “Stay with me, please.” “Despite student protests, he remained Dean for another year.”

    Synonyms: stay, stay on, remain · A kind of: be · Kinds: bide, hold over

  9. Span an interval of distance, space or time.

    “The war extended over five years.” “The period covered the turn of the century.” “My land extends over the hills on the horizon.”

    Synonyms: cover, extend · A kind of: be · Kinds: cross, sweep, overlap, spread, ridge, dot

  10. Exist over a prolonged period of time.

    “The bad weather continued for two more weeks.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: carry, prevail, stand
