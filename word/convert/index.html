Convert: meaning, synonyms and examples

Convert is used as a verb (11 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Change from one system to another or to a new plan or policy.

In Hindi
बदलना, रूपांतरित करना
Synonyms
change over, change, exchange, commute, win over, convince
Word family
conversion, converter, convertible, convertor

Convert as a verb

  1. Change from one system to another or to a new plan or policy.

    “We converted from 220 to 110 Volt.”

    Synonyms: change over · A kind of: change · Kinds: transduce, metricize, decimalize, float

  2. Change the nature, purpose, or function of something.

    “Convert lead into gold.” “Convert hotels into jails.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: caramelize, rasterize, humify, verbalize, sporulate, novelize, deaden, opalize

  3. Change religious beliefs, or adopt a religious belief.

    “She converted to Buddhism.”

    A kind of: change

  4. Exchange or replace with another, usually of the same kind or category.

    “Could you convert my dollars into pounds?” “He changed his name.” “Convert centimeters into inches.”

    Synonyms: change, exchange, commute · A kind of: replace · Kinds: rectify, utilize, capitalize, launder, break

  5. Cause to adopt a new or different faith.

    “The missionaries converted the Indian population.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: catholicize, proselytize

  6. Score an extra point or points after touchdown by kicking the ball through the uprights or advancing the ball into the end zone.

    “Smith converted and his team won.”

    A kind of: score

  7. Complete successfully.

    “Score a penalty shot or free throw.”

    A kind of: score

  8. Score (a spare).

    A kind of: score

  9. Make (someone) agree, understand, or realize the truth or validity of something.

    “He had finally convinced several customers of the advantages of his product.”

    Synonyms: win over, convince · A kind of: persuade · Kinds: disarm

  10. Exchange a penalty for a less severe one.

    Synonyms: commute, exchange · A kind of: change

  11. Change in nature, purpose, or function; undergo a chemical change.

    “The substance converts to an acid.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: caramelize, malt, immobilize, melanize

Convert as a noun

  1. A person who has been converted to another religious or political belief.

    A kind of: person · Kinds: proselyte
