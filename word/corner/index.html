Corner: meaning, synonyms and examples

Corner is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A place off to the side of an area.

In Hindi
कोना
Synonyms
nook, street corner, turning point, recess, recession, niche, box, quoin, tree

Corner as a noun

  1. A place off to the side of an area.

    “He tripled to the rightfield corner.” “The southeastern corner of the Mediterranean.”

    A kind of: area

  2. The point where two lines meet or intersect.

    “The corners of a rectangle.”

    A kind of: point · Kinds: canthus

  3. An interior angle formed by two meeting walls.

    “A piano was in one corner of the room.”

    Synonyms: nook · A kind of: area · Kinds: amen corner, chimney corner

  4. The intersection of two streets.

    “Standing on the corner watching all the girls go by.”

    Synonyms: street corner, turning point · A kind of: intersection · Kinds: blind corner

  5. The point where three areas or surfaces meet or intersect.

    “The corners of a cube.”

    A kind of: point

  6. A small concavity.

    Synonyms: recess, recession, niche · A kind of: concave shape · Kinds: pharyngeal recess

  7. A temporary monopoly on a kind of commercial trade.

    “A corner on the silver market.”

    A kind of: monopoly

  8. A predicament from which a skillful or graceful escape is impossible.

    “His lying got him into a tight corner.”

    Synonyms: box · A kind of: predicament

  9. A projecting part where two sides or edges meet.

    “He knocked off the corners.”

    A kind of: part

  10. A remote area.

    A kind of: area

  11. (architecture) solid exterior angle of a building; especially one formed by a cornerstone.

    Synonyms: quoin · A kind of: structure

Corner as a verb

  1. Gain control over.

    “Corner the gold market.”

    A kind of: control

  2. Force a person or an animal into a position from which he cannot escape.

    Synonyms: tree · A kind of: steer

  3. Turn a corner.

    “The car corners.”

    A kind of: turn
