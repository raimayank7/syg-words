Correct: meaning, synonyms and examples

Correct is used as a verb (8 senses) and adjective (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make right or correct.

In Hindi
सही; सुधारना
Synonyms
rectify, right, compensate, redress, chastise, castigate, objurgate, chasten, counterbalance, make up
Opposites
falsify, incorrect
Word family
correction, corrective, corrigible, correctness

Correct as a verb

  1. Make right or correct.

    “Correct the mistakes.” “Rectify the calculation.”

    Synonyms: rectify, right · Opposite: falsify · A kind of: change by reversal · Kinds: rectify, debug

  2. Make reparations or amends for.

    “Right a wrongs done to the victims of the Holocaust.”

    Synonyms: right, compensate, redress · A kind of: change · Kinds: over-correct, expiate

  3. Censure severely.

    “She chastised him for his insensitive remarks.”

    Synonyms: chastise, castigate, objurgate, chasten · A kind of: call on the carpet · Kinds: flame

  4. Adjust for.

    “Engineers will work to correct the effects or air resistance.”

    Synonyms: compensate, counterbalance, make up, even out, even off, even up · A kind of: balance · Kinds: carry, cover

  5. Punish in order to gain control or enforce obedience.

    “The teacher disciplined the pupils rather frequently.”

    Synonyms: discipline, sort out · A kind of: punish

  6. Go down in value.

    “The stock market corrected.” “Prices slumped.”

    Synonyms: decline, slump · A kind of: descend

  7. Alter or regulate so as to achieve accuracy or conform to a standard.

    “Adjust the clock, please.” “Correct the alignment of the front wheels.”

    Synonyms: adjust, set · A kind of: change · Kinds: proportion, modulate, temper, tune, calibrate, tune, time, trim

  8. Treat a defect.

    “The new contact lenses will correct for his myopia.”

    A kind of: treat

Correct as an adjective

  1. Free from error; especially conforming to fact or truth.

    “The correct answer.” “The correct version.” “The right answer.”

    Synonyms: right · Opposite: incorrect · Similar: accurate, letter-perfect, straight

  2. Socially right or correct.

    “It isn't right to leave the party without saying goodbye.” “Correct behavior.”

    Synonyms: right · Similar: proper

  3. In accord with accepted standards of usage or procedure.

    “What's the right word for this?” “The right way to open oysters.”

    Synonyms: right · Similar: proper

  4. Correct in opinion or judgment.

    “Time proved him right.”

    Synonyms: right · Similar: right-minded

Phrases and compounds with "correct"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • over-correct (verb) Make excessive corrections for fear of making an error.
  • politically correct (adjective) Exhibiting political correctness.
