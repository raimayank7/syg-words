Cost: meaning, synonyms and examples

Cost is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The total spent for goods or services including money and time and labor.

In Hindi
क़ीमत, लागत
Synonyms
monetary value, price, toll, be
Word family
costly

Cost as a noun

  1. The total spent for goods or services including money and time and labor.

    A kind of: outgo · Kinds: expense, capital expenditure, payment, ransom, cost overrun, cost of living, borrowing cost, distribution cost

  2. The property of having material worth (often indicated by the amount of money something would bring if sold).

    “The fluctuating monetary value of gold and silver.” “He puts a high price on his services.” “He couldn't calculate the cost of the collection.”

    Synonyms: monetary value, price · A kind of: value · Kinds: average cost, marginal cost, expensiveness, assessment, inexpensiveness

  3. Value measured by what must be given or done or undergone to obtain something.

    “The cost in human life was enormous.” “The price of success is hard work.” “What price glory?”

    Synonyms: price, toll · A kind of: value · Kinds: death toll

Cost as a verb

  1. Be priced at.

    “These shoes cost $100.”

    Synonyms: be · A kind of: be · Kinds: set back

  2. Require to lose, suffer, or sacrifice.

    “This mistake cost him his job.”

    A kind of: necessitate

Phrases and compounds with "cost"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • low-cost (adjective) That you have the financial means for.
  • cost-plus (adjective) Determining payment based on the actual cost of production plus an agreed-upon fee or rate of profit.
  • unit cost (noun) Calculated cost for a given unit of a product.
  • fixed cost (noun) A periodic charge that does not vary with business volume (as insurance or rent or mortgage payments etc.).
  • at any cost (adverb) Regardless of the cost involved.
  • cost ledger (noun) Ledger showing the accumulated costs classified in various ways.
  • average cost (noun) Total cost for all units bought (or produced) divided by the number of units.
  • capital cost (noun) The opportunity cost of the funds employed as the result of an investment decision; the rate of return that a business could earn if it chose another investment with equivalent risk.
  • cost cutting (noun) The act of cutting costs.
  • cost overrun (noun) Excess of cost over budget.
  • carrying cost (noun) The opportunity cost of unproductive assets; the expense incurred by ownership.
  • cost analysis (noun) Breaking down the costs of some operation and reporting on each factor separately.
  • cost increase (noun) An increase in cost.
  • handling cost (noun) The cost of handling (especially the cost of packaging and mailing an order).
  • marginal cost (noun) The increase or decrease in costs as a result of one more or one less unit of output.
  • borrowing cost (noun) The cost of borrowing something.
