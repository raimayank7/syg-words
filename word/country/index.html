Country: meaning, synonyms and examples

Country is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A politically organized body of people under a single government.

In Hindi
देश; देहात
Synonyms
state, nation, land, commonwealth, res publica, body politic, rural area, area

Country as a noun

  1. A politically organized body of people under a single government.

    “The state has elected a new president.” “African nations.” “Students who had come to the nation's capitol.”

    Synonyms: state, nation, land, commonwealth, res publica, body politic · A kind of: political unit · Kinds: commonwealth country, developing country, foreign country, rogue state, suzerain, sea power, world power, city state

  2. The territory occupied by a nation.

    “He returned to the land of his birth.” “He visited several European countries.”

    Synonyms: state, land · A kind of: administrative district · Kinds: banana republic, fatherland, buffer state, kingdom, tax haven, sultanate

  3. The people who live in a nation or country.

    “A statement that sums up the nation's mood.” “The news was announced to the nation.” “The whole country worshipped him.”

    Synonyms: nation, land · A kind of: people

  4. An area outside of cities and towns.

    “His poetry celebrated the slower pace of life in the country.”

    Synonyms: rural area · A kind of: geographical area · Kinds: backwoods, farmland, countryside, scrubland, weald, wold

  5. A particular geographical region of indefinite boundary (usually serving some special purpose or distinguished by its people or culture or geography).

    “It was a mountainous area.” “Bible country.”

    Synonyms: area · A kind of: region · Kinds: arena, high country, bed ground, broadcast area, center, corner, corner, disaster area

Phrases and compounds with "country"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • up-country (adverb) To or in the interior of a country or region.
  • old country (noun) The country of origin of an immigrant.
  • back country (noun) A remote and undeveloped area.
  • country club (noun) A suburban club for recreation and socializing.
  • country-bred (adjective) Rough and uncouth.
  • high country (noun) An area lying above the piedmont but below the timberline.
  • west country (noun) The southwestern part of England (including Cornwall and Devon and Somerset).
  • asian country (noun) Any one of the nations occupying the Asian continent.
  • country house (noun) A house (usually large and impressive) on an estate in the country.
  • country music (noun) A simple style of folk music heard mostly in the southern United States; usually played on stringed instruments.
  • country store (noun) A retail store serving a sparsely populated region; usually stocked with a wide variety of merchandise.
  • country-dance (noun) A type of folk dance in which couples are arranged in sets or face one another in a line.
  • country-style (adjective) Typical of the country.
  • cross country (noun) A long race run over open country.
  • cross-country (adverb) Not following tracks or roads.
  • balkan country (noun) Any one of the countries on the Balkan Peninsula.
