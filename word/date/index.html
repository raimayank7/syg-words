Date: meaning, synonyms and examples

Date is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: The specified day of the month.

In Hindi
तारीख़; खजूर
Synonyms
day of the month, escort, appointment, engagement, particular date, date stamp, go steady, go out, see
Word family
dating

Date as a noun

  1. The specified day of the month.

    “What is the date today?”

    Synonyms: day of the month · A kind of: day · Kinds: maturity, birthday

  2. A participant in a date.

    “His date never stopped talking.”

    Synonyms: escort · A kind of: companion · Kinds: blind date

  3. A meeting arranged in advance.

    “She asked how to avoid kissing at the end of a date.”

    Synonyms: appointment, engagement · A kind of: meeting · Kinds: blind date, double date

  4. A particular but unspecified point in time.

    “They hoped to get together at an early date.”

    Synonyms: particular date · A kind of: point

  5. The present.

    “They are up to date.” “We haven't heard from them to date.”

    A kind of: present

  6. The particular day, month, or year (usually according to the Gregorian calendar) that an event occurred.

    “He tried to memorizes all the dates for his history class.”

    A kind of: calendar day, calendar month · Kinds: epoch

  7. A particular day specified as the time something happens.

    “The date of the election is set by law.”

    A kind of: day · Kinds: future date, rain date, sell-by date

  8. Sweet edible fruit of the date palm with a single long woody seed.

    A kind of: edible fruit

Date as a verb

  1. Go on a date with.

    “Tonight she is dating a former high school sweetheart.”

    A kind of: go out · Kinds: double-date

  2. Stamp with a date.

    “The package is dated November 24.”

    Synonyms: date stamp · A kind of: date, stamp

  3. Assign a date to; determine the (probable) date of.

    “Scientists often cannot date precisely archeological or prehistorical findings.”

    A kind of: determine · Kinds: chronologize, misdate

  4. Date regularly; have a steady relationship with.

    “Did you know that she is seeing an older man?” “He is dating his former wife again!”

    Synonyms: go steady, go out, see · A kind of: consort

  5. Provide with a dateline; mark with a date.

    “She wrote the letter on Monday but she dated it Saturday so as not to reveal that she procrastinated.”

    A kind of: supply · Kinds: dateline, date

Phrases and compounds with "date"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • to date (adverb) Prior to the present time.
  • date bar (noun) Fruit bar containing chopped dates.
  • due date (noun) The date on which an obligation must be repaid.
  • date back (verb) Belong to an earlier time.
  • date from (verb) Belong to an earlier time.
  • date line (noun) An imaginary line on the surface of the earth following (approximately) the 180th meridian.
  • date palm (noun) Tall tropical feather palm tree native to Syria bearing sweet edible fruit.
  • date plum (noun) An Asiatic persimmon tree cultivated for its small yellow or purplish-black edible fruit much valued by Afghan tribes.
  • date-mark (verb) Mark with a date and place.
  • rain date (noun) An alternative date set for some outdoor event in case it rains on the appointed date.
  • blind date (noun) A participant in a blind date (someone you meet for the first time when you have a date with them).
  • date bread (noun) Bread containing chopped dates.
  • date stamp (verb) Stamp with a date.
  • up-to-date (adjective) Reflecting the latest information or changes.
  • double date (noun) A date in which two couples participate.
  • double-date (verb) Go out on a date with a partner and another couple.
