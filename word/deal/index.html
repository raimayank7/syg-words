Deal: meaning, synonyms and examples

Deal is used as a verb (13 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Act on verbally or in some form of artistic expression.

In Hindi
सौदा; निपटना
Synonyms
cover, treat, handle, plow, address, consider, take, look at, cope, get by
Word family
dealings, dealing, dealer

Deal as a verb

  1. Act on verbally or in some form of artistic expression.

    “This book deals with incest.” “The course covered all of Western Civilization.” “The new book treats the history of China.”

    Synonyms: cover, treat, handle, plow, address · A kind of: broach · Kinds: theologize, discourse

  2. Take into consideration for exemplifying purposes.

    “Take the case of China.” “Consider the following case.”

    Synonyms: consider, take, look at · A kind of: think about · Kinds: contemplate, dally, abstract, warm to

  3. Take action with respect to (someone or something).

    “How are we going to deal with this problem?” “The teacher knew how to deal with these lazy students.”

    A kind of: act

  4. Come to terms with.

    “We got by on just a gallon of gas.” “They made do on half a loaf of bread every day.”

    Synonyms: cope, get by, make out, make do, contend, grapple, manage · A kind of: act · Kinds: improvise, fend, hack, scrape along, meet

  5. Administer or bestow, as in small portions.

    “Administer critical remarks to everyone present.” “Dole out some money.” “Shell out pocket money for the children.”

    Synonyms: distribute, administer, mete out, parcel out, lot, dispense, shell out, deal out · A kind of: give · Kinds: assign, reallot, deal, give

  6. Do business; offer for sale as for one's livelihood.

    “She deals in gold.” “The brothers sell shoes.”

    Synonyms: sell, trade · A kind of: transact · Kinds: deal, black marketeer, pyramid, market, peddle

  7. Be in charge of, act on, or dispose of.

    “I can deal with this crew of workers.” “This blender can't handle nuts.” “She managed her parents' affairs after they got too old.”

    Synonyms: manage, care, handle · A kind of: control · Kinds: administer, organize, work, come to grips, dispose of, take care, coordinate, juggle

  8. Behave in a certain way towards others.

    “He deals fairly with his employees.”

    A kind of: behave

  9. Distribute cards to the players in a game.

    “Who's dealing?”

    A kind of: distribute · Kinds: misdeal

  10. Direct the course of; manage or control.

    “You cannot conduct business like this.”

    Synonyms: conduct, carry on · A kind of: manage · Kinds: racketeer

  11. Give out as one's portion or share.

    Synonyms: share, divvy up, portion out, apportion · A kind of: distribute

  12. Give (a specific card) to a player.

    “He dealt me the Queen of Spades.”

    A kind of: pass

  13. Sell.

    “Deal hashish.”

    A kind of: deal

Deal as a noun

  1. A particular instance of buying or selling.

    “It was a package deal.” “I had no further trade with him.” “He's a master of the business deal.”

    Synonyms: trade, business deal · A kind of: transaction · Kinds: arms deal, penny ante

  2. An agreement between parties (usually arrived at after discussion) fixing obligations of each.

    “He made a bargain with the devil.” “He rose to prominence through a series of shady deals.”

    Synonyms: bargain · A kind of: agreement

  3. (often followed by `of') a large number or amount or extent.

    “A batch of letters.” “A deal of trouble.” “A lot of money.”

    Synonyms: batch, flock, good deal, great deal, hatful, heap, lot, mass · A kind of: large indefinite quantity · Kinds: flood, haymow

  4. A plank of softwood (fir or pine board).

    A kind of: board

  5. Wood that is easy to saw (from conifers such as pine or fir).

    Synonyms: softwood · A kind of: wood · Kinds: pulpwood

  6. The cards held in a card game by a given player at any given time.

    “I didn't hold a good hand all evening.” “He kept trying to see my hand.”

    Synonyms: hand · A kind of: collection · Kinds: bridge hand, poker hand

  7. The type of treatment received (especially as the result of an agreement).

    “He got a good deal on his car.”

    A kind of: result · Kinds: fair deal, raw deal

  8. The act of distributing playing cards.

    “The deal was passed around the table clockwise.”

    A kind of: distribution · Kinds: misdeal

  9. The act of apportioning or distributing something.

    “The captain was entrusted with the deal of provisions.”

    A kind of: allotment · Kinds: new deal

Phrases and compounds with "deal"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • big deal (noun) An important influential person.
  • deal out (verb) Administer or bestow, as in small portions.
  • new deal (noun) The economic policy of F. D. Roosevelt.
  • raw deal (noun) Unfair treatment.
  • arms deal (noun) A deal to provide military arms.
  • fair deal (noun) Fair treatment.
  • good deal (noun) (often followed by `of') a large number or amount or extent.
  • great deal (noun) (often followed by `of') a large number or amount or extent.
  • a good deal (adverb) To a very great degree or extent.
  • square deal (noun) Fair treatment.
  • a great deal (adverb) To a very great degree or extent.
  • business deal (noun) A particular instance of buying or selling.
