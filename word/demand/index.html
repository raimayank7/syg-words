Demand: meaning, synonyms and examples

Demand is used as a verb (6 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Request urgently and forcefully.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites

Word family

Demand as a verb

  1. Request urgently and forcefully.

    “The victim's family is demanding compensation.” “The boss demanded that he be fired immediately.” “She demanded to see the manager.”

    A kind of: request · Kinds: want, ask, clamor, dun, ask

  2. Require as useful, just, or proper.

    “It takes nerve to do what she did.” “Success usually requires hard work.” “This job asks a lot of patience and skill.”

    Synonyms: necessitate, ask, postulate, need, require, take, involve, call for · Kinds: claim, govern, draw, cost, cry out for, compel

  3. Claim as due or just.

    “The bank demanded payment of the loan.”

    Synonyms: exact · A kind of: claim · Kinds: command, call

  4. Lay legal claim to.

    A kind of: claim

  5. Summon to court.

    A kind of: summon

  6. Ask to be informed of.

    “I demand an explanation.”

    A kind of: request

Demand as a noun

  1. An urgent or peremptory request.

    “His demands for attention were unceasing.”

    A kind of: request · Kinds: challenge, ultimatum, insistence, call, requisition, call, margin call, wage claim

  2. The ability and desire to purchase goods and services.

    “The automobile reduced the demand for buggywhips.” “The demand exceeded the supply.”

    Opposite: supply · A kind of: economic process · Kinds: consumption

  3. Required activity.

    “The requirements of his work affected his health.” “There were many demands on his time.”

    Synonyms: requirement · A kind of: duty

  4. The act of demanding.

    “The kidnapper's exorbitant demands for money.”

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: exaction, claim

  5. A condition requiring relief.

    “She satisfied his need for affection.” “God has no need of men to accomplish His work.” “There is a demand for jobs.”

    Synonyms: need · A kind of: condition · Kinds: lack, necessity

Phrases and compounds with "demand"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in demand (adjective) Greatly desired.
  • demand loan (noun) A loan that is repayable on demand.
  • demand note (noun) A note payable on demand.
  • demand deposit (noun) A bank deposit from which withdrawals can be made without notice.
  • demand feeding (noun) Feeding a baby or animal whenever it shows a need.
  • demand-pull inflation (noun) Inflation caused by an increase in demand or in the supply of money.
  • demand for explanation (noun) A challenge to defend what someone has said.
  • demand for identification (noun) As by a sentry.
