Disk: meaning, synonyms and examples

Disk is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Something with a round shape resembling a flat circular plate.

In Hindi
चकती, डिस्क
Synonyms
disc, saucer, phonograph record, phonograph recording, record, platter, magnetic disk, magnetic disc, harrow
Word family
diskette

Disk as a noun

  1. Something with a round shape resembling a flat circular plate.

    “The moon's disk hung in a cloudless sky.”

    Synonyms: disc, saucer · A kind of: round shape · Kinds: intervertebral disc, point

  2. A flat circular plate.

    Synonyms: disc · A kind of: plate, circle · Kinds: acetate disk, brake disk, deadeye, diaphragm, discus, planchet, puck, token

  3. Sound recording consisting of a disk with a continuous groove; used to reproduce music by rotating while a phonograph needle tracks in the groove.

    Synonyms: phonograph record, phonograph recording, record, disc, platter · A kind of: sound recording · Kinds: seventy-eight

  4. (computer science) a memory device consisting of a flat disk covered with a magnetic coating on which information is stored.

    Synonyms: magnetic disk, magnetic disc, disc · A kind of: memory device · Kinds: diskette, hard disc

Disk as a verb

  1. Draw a harrow over (land).

    Synonyms: harrow · A kind of: plow

Phrases and compounds with "disk"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • ram disk (noun) (computer science) a virtual drive that is created by setting aside part of the random-access memory to use as if it were a group of sectors.
  • disk file (noun) (computer science) a computer file stored on a magnetic disk and identified by a unique label.
  • disk pack (noun) (computer science) a portable pack of magnetic disks used in a disk storage device.
  • hard disk (noun) A rigid magnetic disk mounted permanently in a drive unit.
  • brake disk (noun) A disk or plate that is fixed to the wheel; pressure is applied to it by the brake pads.
  • disk brake (noun) Hydraulic brake in which friction is applied to both sides of a spinning disk by the brake pads.
  • disk cache (noun) A cache that stores copies of frequently used disk sectors in random access memory (RAM) so they can be read without accessing the slower disk.
  • disk drive (noun) Computer hardware that holds and spins a magnetic or optical disk and reads and writes information on it.
  • disk error (noun) Error resulting from malfunction of a magnetic disk.
  • disk shape (noun) The roundness of a 2-dimensional figure.
  • disk space (noun) (computer science) the space available on a magnetic disk.
  • fixed disk (noun) A rigid magnetic disk mounted permanently in a drive unit.
  • optic disk (noun) The point where the optic nerve enters the retina; not sensitive to light.
  • disk access (noun) Memory access to the computer disk on which information is stored.
  • disk clutch (noun) A friction clutch in which the frictional surfaces are disks.
  • disk harrow (noun) A harrow with a series of disks set on edge at an angle.
