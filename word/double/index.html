Double: meaning, synonyms and examples

Double is used as a verb (6 senses), adjective (7 senses), noun (5 senses) and adverb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Increase twofold.

In Hindi
दोगुना, दोहरा
Synonyms
duplicate, double over, double up, reduplicate, repeat, replicate, dual, twofold, two-fold, treble
Opposites
single
Word family
doubling

Double as a verb

  1. Increase twofold.

    “The population doubled within 50 years.”

    Synonyms: duplicate · A kind of: multiply · Kinds: redouble, geminate

  2. Hit a two-base hit.

    A kind of: hit

  3. Bend over or curl up, usually with laughter or pain.

    “He doubled and vomited violently.”

    Synonyms: double over, double up · A kind of: bend

  4. Do double duty; serve two purposes or have two functions.

    “She doubles as his wife and secretary.”

    A kind of: function

  5. Bridge: make a demand for (a card or suit).

    A kind of: bid

  6. Make or do or perform again.

    “He could never replicate his brilliant performance of the magic trick.”

    Synonyms: duplicate, reduplicate, repeat, replicate · A kind of: reproduce · Kinds: replicate, recapitulate, reduplicate

Double as an adjective

  1. Having more than one decidedly dissimilar aspects or qualities.

    “A double (or dual) role for an actor.” “The office of a clergyman is twofold; public preaching and private influence.” “Every episode has its double and treble meaning.”

    Synonyms: dual, twofold, two-fold, treble, threefold, three-fold · Similar: multiple

  2. Consisting of or involving two parts or components usually in pairs.

    “An egg with a double yolk.” “A double (binary) star.” “Double doors.”

    Synonyms: dual, duple · Similar: multiple

  3. Twice as great or many.

    “Ate a double portion.” “The dose is doubled.” “A twofold increase.”

    Synonyms: doubled, twofold, two-fold · Similar: multiple

  4. Used of flowers having more than the usual number of petals in crowded or overlapping arrangements.

    “Double chrysanthemums have many rows of petals and are usually spherical or hemispherical.”

    Opposite: single

  5. Used of homologous chromosomes associated in pairs in synapsis.

    Synonyms: bivalent

  6. Large enough for two.

    “A double bed.” “A double room.”

    Similar: large

  7. Having two meanings with intent to deceive.

    “A sly double meaning.” “Spoke with forked tongue.”

    Synonyms: forked · Similar: equivocal

Double as a noun

  1. A base hit on which the batter stops safely at second base.

    “He hit a double to deep centerfield.”

    Synonyms: two-base hit, two-bagger, two-baser · A kind of: base hit · Kinds: line-drive double

  2. A stand-in for movie stars to perform dangerous stunts.

    “His first job in Hollywood was as a double for Clark Gable.”

    Synonyms: stunt man, stunt woman · A kind of: stand-in

  3. Someone who closely resembles a famous person (especially an actor).

    “He could be Gingrich's double.” “She's the very image of her mother.”

    Synonyms: image, look-alike · A kind of: person · Kinds: ringer

  4. A quantity that is twice as great as another.

    “36 is the double of 18.”

    A kind of: multiple

  5. Raising the stakes in a card game by a factor of 2.

    “I decided his double was a bluff.”

    Synonyms: doubling

Double as an adverb

  1. Downward and forward.

    “He was bent double with pain.”

  2. Two together.

    “Some people sleep better double.”

  3. To double the degree.

    “She was doubly rewarded.” “His eyes were double bright.”

    Synonyms: doubly, twice
