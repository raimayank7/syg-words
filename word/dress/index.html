Dress: meaning, synonyms and examples

Dress is used as a verb (14 senses), noun (3 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Put on clothes.

In Hindi
पोशाक; कपड़े पहनना
Synonyms
get dressed, clothe, enclothe, garb, raiment, tog, garment, habilitate, fit out, apparel
Opposites
undress
Word family
dresser, dressing, dressy

Dress as a verb

  1. Put on clothes.

    “We had to dress quickly.” “Dress the patient.” “Can the child dress by herself?”

    Synonyms: get dressed · Opposite: undress · A kind of: change · Kinds: overdress, dress, dress down, prink, bundle up, wear, dandify, change

  2. Provide with clothes or put clothes on.

    “Parents must feed and dress their child.”

    Synonyms: clothe, enclothe, garb, raiment, tog, garment, habilitate, fit out · Opposite: undress · A kind of: change state · Kinds: prim, cover, jacket, frock, shirt, habit, vesture, overdress

  3. Put a finish on.

    “Dress the surface smooth.”

    A kind of: finish

  4. Dress in a certain manner.

    “She dresses in the latest Paris fashion.” “He dressed up in a suit and tie.”

    Synonyms: dress up · A kind of: dress · Kinds: vest

  5. Dress or groom with elaborate care.

    “She likes to dress when going to the opera.”

    Synonyms: preen, primp, plume · A kind of: overdress

  6. Arrange in ranks.

    “Dress troops.”

    Synonyms: line up · A kind of: position

  7. Decorate (food), as with parsley or other ornamental foods.

    Synonyms: trim, garnish · A kind of: decorate

  8. Provide with decoration.

    “Dress the windows.”

    Synonyms: decorate · A kind of: arrange

  9. Put a dressing on.

    “Dress the salads.”

    A kind of: put on

  10. Cultivate, tend, and cut back the growth of.

    “Dress the plants in the garden.”

    Synonyms: snip, clip, crop, trim, lop, prune, cut back · A kind of: thin out · Kinds: shear, poll, top, disbud

  11. Cut down rough-hewn (lumber) to standard thickness and width.

    A kind of: pare

  12. Convert into leather.

    “Dress the tanned skins.”

    A kind of: convert

  13. Apply a bandage or medication to.

    “Dress the victim's wounds.”

    A kind of: treat · Kinds: poultice, bandage

  14. Give a neat appearance to.

    “Groom the dogs.” “Dress the horses.”

    Synonyms: groom, curry · A kind of: fancify

Dress as a noun

  1. A one-piece garment for a woman; has skirt and bodice.

    Synonyms: frock · A kind of: woman's clothing · Kinds: caftan, chemise, coatdress, cocktail dress, dirndl, gown, jumper, kirtle

  2. Clothing of a distinctive style or for a particular occasion.

    “Formal attire.” “Battle dress.”

    Synonyms: attire, garb · A kind of: clothing · Kinds: ao dai, costume, costume, costume, costume, disguise, ecclesiastical attire, finery

  3. Clothing in general.

    “She was refined in her choice of apparel.” “He always bought his clothes at the same store.” “Fastidious about his dress.”

    Synonyms: apparel, wearing apparel, clothes · A kind of: clothing · Kinds: workwear

Dress as an adjective

  1. Suitable for formal occasions.

    “Formal wear.” “A full-dress uniform.” “Dress shoes.”

    Synonyms: full-dress · Similar: formal

  2. (of an occasion) requiring formal clothes.

    “A dress dinner.” “A full-dress ceremony.”

    Synonyms: full-dress · Similar: formal

Phrases and compounds with "dress"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • dress up (verb) Put on special clothes to appear particularly appealing and attractive.
  • dress hat (noun) A man's hat with a tall crown; usually covered with silk or with beaver fur.
  • dress code (noun) A set of rules specifying the correct manner of dress while on the premises of the institution (or specifying what manner of dress is prohibited).
  • dress down (verb) Censure severely or angrily.
  • dress rack (noun) A rack used primarily to display dresses for sale in a store.
  • dress ship (verb) Decorate a ship with flags.
  • dress shop (noun) A shop that sells women's clothes and jewelry.
  • dress suit (noun) Formalwear consisting of full evening dress for men.
  • full dress (noun) Formalwear consisting of full evening dress for men.
  • full-dress (adjective) Suitable for formal occasions.
  • dress blues (noun) A dress uniform for formal occasions.
  • dress shirt (noun) A man's white shirt (with a starch front) for evening wear (usually with a tuxedo).
  • fancy dress (noun) A costume worn as a disguise at a masquerade party.
  • battle dress (noun) A military uniform designed for field service.
  • dinner dress (noun) A gown for evening wear.
  • diving dress (noun) A weighted and hermetically sealed garment supplied with air; worn by underwater divers.
