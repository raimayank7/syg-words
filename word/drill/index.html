Drill: meaning, synonyms and examples

Drill is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make a hole, especially with a pointed power or hand tool.

In Hindi
बरमा; अभ्यास
Synonyms
bore, exercise, practice, practise, practice session, recitation
Word family
electric drill, drilling

Drill as a verb

  1. Make a hole, especially with a pointed power or hand tool.

    “Don't drill here, there's a gas pipe.” “Drill a hole into the wall.” “Drill for oil.”

    Synonyms: bore · A kind of: cut · Kinds: spud, counter-drill, trepan

  2. Train in the military, e.g., in the use of weapons.

    A kind of: train

  3. Learn by repetition.

    “We drilled French verbs every day.” “Pianists practice scales.”

    Synonyms: exercise, practice, practise · A kind of: learn

  4. Teach by repetition.

    A kind of: teach · Kinds: hammer in, inculcate

  5. Undergo military training or do military exercises.

    A kind of: train

Drill as a noun

  1. A tool with a sharp point and cutting edges for making holes in hard materials (usually rotating rapidly or by repeated blows).

    A kind of: tool · Kinds: auger, bore bit, brace and bit, core drill, dentist's drill, hand drill, power drill, reamer

  2. Similar to the mandrill but smaller and less brightly colored.

    A kind of: baboon

  3. Systematic training by multiple repetitions.

    “Practice makes perfect.”

    Synonyms: exercise, practice, practice session, recitation · A kind of: training · Kinds: fire drill, manual of arms, military drill, rehearsal, rehearsal, review, scrimmage, shadowboxing

  4. (military) the training of soldiers to march (as in ceremonial parades) or to perform the manual of arms.

    A kind of: training

Phrases and compounds with "drill"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • drill in (verb) Teach by drills and repetition.
  • drill bit (noun) A bit used in drilling for oil.
  • drill rig (noun) Rig used in drilling for oil or gas.
  • drill rod (noun) Carbon steel used for rock drills and dowels.
  • burr drill (noun) A high speed drill that dentists use to cut into teeth.
  • core drill (noun) A drill that removes a cylindrical core from the drill hole.
  • drill hole (noun) A hole or passage made by a drill; usually made for exploratory purposes.
  • drill site (noun) Workplace that is the site of a drill hole.
  • drill-like (adjective) Resembling a drill.
  • fire drill (noun) An exercise intended to train people in duties and escape procedures to be followed in case of fire.
  • hand drill (noun) A small portable drill held and operated by hand.
  • rock drill (noun) A drill for penetrating rock.
  • star drill (noun) A steel rock drill with a star-shaped point that is used for making holes in stones or masonry; it is operated by hitting the end with a hammer while rotating it between blows.
  • drill press (noun) A machine tool with a separate, upright stand; an electric drill is pressed into the work automatically or with a hand lever.
  • drill steel (noun) Carbon steel used for rock drills and dowels.
  • power drill (noun) A power tool for drilling holes into hard materials.
