Earth: meaning, synonyms and examples

Earth is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The 3rd planet from the sun; the planet we live on.

In Hindi
पृथ्वी; मिट्टी
Synonyms
world, globe, ground, land, dry land, solid ground, terra firma, worldly concern, earthly concern
Word family
earthling, earthy, earthing

Earth as a noun

  1. The 3rd planet from the sun; the planet we live on.

    “The Earth moves around the sun.” “He sailed around the world.”

    Synonyms: world, globe

  2. The loose soft material that makes up a large part of the land surface.

    “They dug into the earth outside the church.”

    Synonyms: ground · A kind of: material · Kinds: moraine, diatomaceous earth, saprolite, soil

  3. The solid part of the earth's surface.

    “The plane turned away from the sea and moved back over land.” “The earth shook for several minutes.” “He dropped the logs on the ground.”

    Synonyms: land, dry land, ground, solid ground, terra firma · A kind of: object · Kinds: archipelago, beachfront, cape, coastal plain, floor, foreland, forest, island

  4. Once thought to be one of four elements composing the universe (Empedocles).

    A kind of: element

  5. The concerns of this life as distinguished from heaven and the afterlife.

    “They consider the church to be independent of the world.”

    Synonyms: worldly concern, earthly concern, world · A kind of: concern

  6. A connection between an electrical device and a large conducting body, such as the earth (which is taken to be at zero voltage).

    Synonyms: ground · A kind of: connection

Earth as a verb

  1. Hide in the earth like a hunted animal.

    A kind of: hide

  2. Connect to the earth.

    “Earth the circuit.”

    A kind of: ground

Phrases and compounds with "earth"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • earth up (verb) Block with earth, as after a landslide.
  • on earth (adverb) Used with question words to convey surprise.
  • earth god (noun) A god of fertility and vegetation.
  • earth wax (noun) A waxy mineral that is a mixture of hydrocarbons and occurs in association with petroleum; some varieties are used in making ceresin and candles.
  • earth-god (noun) A god of fertility and vegetation.
  • earth-ball (noun) Any of various highly prized edible subterranean fungi of the genus Tuber; grow naturally in southwestern Europe.
  • rare earth (noun) Any element of the lanthanide series (atomic numbers 57 through 71).
  • earth color (noun) A colored mineral used as a pigment.
  • earth almond (noun) European sedge having small edible nutlike tubers.
  • earth mother (noun) The earth conceived of as the female principle of fertility.
  • earth tremor (noun) A small earthquake.
  • earth-tongue (noun) Any club-shaped fungus of the genus Geoglossum.
  • down-to-earth (adjective) Sensible and practical.
  • earth goddess (noun) A goddess of fertility and vegetation.
  • earth science (noun) Any of the sciences that deal with the earth or its parts.
  • earth-goddess (noun) A goddess of fertility and vegetation.
