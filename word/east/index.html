East: meaning, synonyms and examples

East is used as a noun (5 senses), adjective (1 sense) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The cardinal compass point that is at 90 degrees.

In Hindi
पूर्व
Synonyms
due east, eastward
Opposites
west

East as a noun

  1. The cardinal compass point that is at 90 degrees.

    Synonyms: due east, eastward · A kind of: cardinal compass point

  2. The countries of Asia.

  3. The region of the United States lying to the north of the Ohio River and to the east of the Mississippi River.

  4. The direction corresponding to the eastward cardinal compass point.

    A kind of: direction

  5. A location in the eastern part of a country, region, or city.

    A kind of: location

East as an adjective

  1. Situated in or facing or moving toward the east.

    Opposite: west · Similar: eastbound, easterly, easterly, eastern, easternmost, eastside

East as an adverb

  1. To, toward, or in the east.

    “We travelled east for several miles.” “Located east of Rome.”

Phrases and compounds with "east"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • due east (noun) The cardinal compass point that is at 90 degrees.
  • east sea (noun) An arm of the Pacific between China and Japan.
  • far east (noun) A popular expression for the countries of eastern Asia (usually including China and Mongolia and Taiwan and Japan and Korea and Indochina and eastern Siberia).
  • east side (noun) The side that is on the east.
  • east wind (noun) A wind from the east.
  • nor'-east (noun) The compass point midway between north and east; at 45 degrees.
  • sou'-east (noun) The compass point midway between south and east; at 135 degrees.
  • east coast (noun) The eastern seaboard of the United States (especially the strip between Boston and Washington D.C.).
  • east india (noun) A group of islands in the Indian and Pacific Oceans between Asia and Australia.
  • east river (noun) A tidal strait separating Manhattan and the Bronx from Queens and Brooklyn.
  • east timor (noun) A former Portuguese colony that was annexed by Indonesia in 1976; voted for independence from Indonesia in 1999 and in May 2002 became an independent nation.
  • east-sider (noun) A resident of the east side of Manhattan in New York City.
  • north-east (adverb) To, toward, or in the northeast.
  • south-east (adverb) To, toward, or in the southeast.
  • east africa (noun) A geographical area in eastern Africa.
  • east anglia (noun) A region of eastern England that was formerly a kingdom.
