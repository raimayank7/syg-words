Exercise: meaning, synonyms and examples

Exercise is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: The activity of exerting your muscles in various ways to keep fit.

In Hindi
व्यायाम; अभ्यास
Synonyms
exercising, physical exercise, physical exertion, workout, use, usage, utilization, utilisation, employment, practice
Word family
exerciser, exercising

Exercise as a noun

  1. The activity of exerting your muscles in various ways to keep fit.

    “The doctor recommended regular exercise.” “He did some exercising.” “The physical exertion required by his work kept him fit.”

    Synonyms: exercising, physical exercise, physical exertion, workout · A kind of: effort · Kinds: cardiopulmonary exercise, gymnastic exercise, kick up, set, bodybuilding, calisthenics, isometrics, isotonic exercise

  2. The act of using.

    “Skilled in the utilization of computers.”

    Synonyms: use, usage, utilization, utilisation, employment · A kind of: activity · Kinds: practice, play, misuse, exploitation, recycling, application

  3. Systematic training by multiple repetitions.

    “Practice makes perfect.”

    Synonyms: practice, drill, practice session, recitation · A kind of: training · Kinds: fire drill, manual of arms, military drill, rehearsal, rehearsal, review, scrimmage, shadowboxing

  4. A task performed or problem solved in order to develop skill or understanding.

    “You must work the examples at the end of each chapter in the textbook.”

    Synonyms: example · A kind of: lesson

  5. (usually plural) a ceremony that involves processions and speeches.

    “Academic exercises.”

    A kind of: ceremony · Kinds: commencement

Exercise as a verb

  1. Put to use.

    “Exert one's power or influence.”

    Synonyms: exert · A kind of: use

  2. Carry out or practice; as of jobs and professions.

    “Practice law.”

    Synonyms: practice, practise, do · Kinds: shamanize

  3. Give a workout to.

    “Some parents exercise their infants.” “My personal trainer works me hard.” “Work one's muscles.”

    Synonyms: work, work out · A kind of: work · Kinds: warm up

  4. Do physical exercise.

    “She works out in the gym every day.”

    Synonyms: work out · Kinds: limber up, train, tumble, tone, weight-lift

  5. Learn by repetition.

    “We drilled French verbs every day.” “Pianists practice scales.”

    Synonyms: drill, practice, practise · A kind of: learn

Phrases and compounds with "exercise"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • arm exercise (noun) Exercise designed to strengthen the arm muscles.
  • exercise set (noun) Several exercises intended to be done in series.
  • leg exercise (noun) Exercise designed to strengthen the leg muscles.
  • back exercise (noun) Exercise designed to strengthen the back muscles.
  • exercise bike (noun) An exercise device resembling a stationary bike.
  • neck exercise (noun) Exercise designed to strengthen the neck muscles.
  • exercise device (noun) A device designed to provide exercise for the user.
  • aerobic exercise (noun) Exercise that increases the need for oxygen.
  • stomach exercise (noun) An exercise designed to strengthen the abdominal muscles.
  • isotonic exercise (noun) Exercise in which opposing muscles contract and there is controlled movement (tension is constant while the lengths of the muscles change).
  • physical exercise (noun) The activity of exerting your muscles in various ways to keep fit.
  • anaerobic exercise (noun) Exercise that builds muscles through tension.
  • gymnastic exercise (noun) (gymnastics) an exercise designed to develop and display strength and agility and balance (usually performed with or on some gymnastic apparatus).
  • isometric exercise (noun) Muscle-building exercises (or a system of musclebuilding exercises) involving muscular contractions against resistance without movement (the muscles contracts but the length of the muscle does not change).
  • graduation exercise (noun) An academic exercise in which diplomas are conferred.
  • commencement exercise (noun) An academic exercise in which diplomas are conferred.
