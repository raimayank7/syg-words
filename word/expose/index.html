Expose: meaning, synonyms and examples

Expose is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Expose or make accessible to some action or influence.

In Hindi
उजागर करना
Synonyms
unwrap, disclose, let on, bring out, reveal, discover, divulge, break, give away, let out
Word family
exposure

Expose as a verb

  1. Expose or make accessible to some action or influence.

    “Expose your students to art.” “Expose the blanket to sunshine.”

    A kind of: subject · Kinds: ventilate, sun, air out, overexpose, underexpose

  2. Make known to the public information that was previously known only to a few people or that was meant to be kept a secret.

    “The auction house would not disclose the price at which the van Gogh had sold.” “The actress won't reveal how old she is.” “Bring out the truth.”

    Synonyms: unwrap, disclose, let on, bring out, reveal, discover, divulge, break · A kind of: tell · Kinds: blackwash, muckrake, blow, out, spring, betray, confide, leak

  3. To show, make visible or apparent.

    “The Metropolitan Museum is exhibiting Goya's works this month.” “Why don't you show your nice legs and wear shorter skirts?” “National leaders will have to display the highest skills of statesmanship.”

    Synonyms: exhibit, display · A kind of: show · Kinds: open, produce, hold up, bench, flaunt, brandish, model, model

  4. Remove all or part of one's clothes to show one's body.

    “Uncover your belly.” “The man exposed himself in the subway.”

    Synonyms: uncover · Kinds: unwrap, undrape, unclothe, bare, unmask, unveil

  5. Disclose to view as by removing a cover.

    “The curtain rose to disclose a stunning set.”

    Synonyms: disclose · A kind of: uncover · Kinds: face

  6. Put in a dangerous, disadvantageous, or difficult position.

    Synonyms: queer, scupper, endanger, peril · A kind of: affect · Kinds: compromise

  7. Expose to light, of photographic film.

    A kind of: subject · Kinds: overexpose, underexpose

  8. Expose while ridiculing; especially of pretentious or false claims and ideas.

    “The physicist debunked the psychic's claims.”

    Synonyms: debunk · A kind of: ridicule · Kinds: unmask

  9. Abandon by leaving out in the open air.

    “The infant was exposed by the teenage mother.” “After Christmas, many pets get abandoned.”

    A kind of: abandon

Expose as a noun

  1. The exposure of an impostor or a fraud.

    “He published an expose of the graft and corruption in city government.”

    Synonyms: unmasking · A kind of: exposure
