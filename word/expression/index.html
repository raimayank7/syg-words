Expression: meaning, synonyms and examples

Expression is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: The feelings expressed on a person's face.

In Hindi
अभिव्यक्ति; हाव-भाव
Synonyms
look, aspect, facial expression, face, manifestation, reflection, reflexion, verbal expression, verbalism, saying
Word family
express

Expression as a noun

  1. The feelings expressed on a person's face.

    “A sad expression.” “A look of triumph.” “An angry face.”

    Synonyms: look, aspect, facial expression, face · A kind of: countenance · Kinds: leer, sparkle

  2. Expression without words.

    “Tears are an expression of grief.” “The pulse is a reflection of the heart's condition.”

    Synonyms: manifestation, reflection, reflexion · A kind of: demonstration · Kinds: lamentation, act, effusion

  3. The communication (in speech or writing) of your beliefs or opinions.

    “Expressions of good will.” “He helped me find verbal expression for my ideas.” “The idea was immediate but the verbalism took hours.”

    Synonyms: verbal expression, verbalism · A kind of: communication · Kinds: articulation, cold turkey, congratulation

  4. A word or phrase that particular people use in particular situations.

    “Pardon the expression.”

    Synonyms: saying, locution · A kind of: speech · Kinds: logion, calque, advice and consent, ambiguity, shucks, tongue twister, anatomical reference, southernism

  5. The style of expressing yourself.

    “He suggested a better formulation.” “His manner of expression showed how much he cared.”

    Synonyms: formulation · A kind of: expressive style · Kinds: sentimentalism, honorific, archaism, boilerplate, colloquialism, parlance, wording

  6. A group of symbols that make a mathematical statement.

    Synonyms: formula · A kind of: mathematical statement · Kinds: sentential function, primitive, exponential expression

  7. (genetics) the process of expressing a gene.

    A kind of: organic process

  8. A group of words that form a constituent of a sentence and are considered as a single unit.

    “I concluded from his awkward constructions that he was a foreigner.”

    Synonyms: construction, grammatical construction · A kind of: constituent · Kinds: adjunct, clause, complement, involution, phrase, predicator

  9. The act of forcing something out by squeezing or pressing.

    A kind of: squeeze

Phrases and compounds with "expression"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gene expression (noun) Conversion of the information encoded in a gene first into messenger RNA and then to a protein.
  • self-expression (noun) The expression of one's individuality (usually through creative activities).
  • facial expression (noun) A gesture executed with the facial muscles.
  • verbal expression (noun) The communication (in speech or writing) of your beliefs or opinions.
  • turn of expression (noun) A distinctive spoken or written expression.
  • indirect expression (noun) An indirect way of expressing something.
  • idiomatic expression (noun) An expression whose meanings cannot be inferred from the meanings of the words that make it up.
  • exponential expression (noun) A mathematical expression consisting of a constant (especially e) raised to some power.
  • parenthetical expression (noun) An expression in parentheses.
