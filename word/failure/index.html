Failure: meaning, synonyms and examples

Failure is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: An act that fails.

In Hindi
असफलता
Synonyms
loser, nonstarter, unsuccessful person, bankruptcy
Opposites
success
Word family
fail

Failure as a noun

  1. An act that fails.

    “His failure to pass the test.”

    A kind of: nonaccomplishment · Kinds: failing, naught, loss, backsliding, error, out, nonconformity, default

  2. An event that does not accomplish its intended purpose.

    “The surprise party was a complete failure.”

    Opposite: success · A kind of: happening · Kinds: downfall, flame-out, malfunction, miscarriage, miss, flop, malformation, breakdown

  3. Lack of success.

    “He felt that his entire life had been a failure.” “That year there was a crop failure.”

    Opposite: success · A kind of: fortune · Kinds: bankruptcy, bank failure, crop failure, dead duck

  4. A person with a record of failing; someone who loses consistently.

    Synonyms: loser, nonstarter, unsuccessful person · A kind of: unfortunate · Kinds: bankrupt, flash in the pan, flop, underdog

  5. An unexpected omission.

    “He resented my failure to return his call.” “The mechanic's failure to check the brakes.”

    A kind of: omission · Kinds: disappointment, breach, copout

  6. Inability to discharge all your debts as they come due.

    “The company had to declare bankruptcy.” “Fraudulent loans led to the failure of many banks.”

    Synonyms: bankruptcy · A kind of: insolvency

  7. Loss of ability to function normally.

    “Kidney failure.”

    A kind of: disorder · Kinds: heart failure, renal failure

Phrases and compounds with "failure"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bank failure (noun) The inability of a bank to meet its credit obligations.
  • crop failure (noun) The failure of crops to produce a marketable surplus.
  • brake failure (noun) Brakes fail to stop a vehicle.
  • heart failure (noun) Inability of the heart to pump enough blood to sustain normal bodily functions.
  • power failure (noun) Equipment failure resulting when the supply of power fails.
  • renal failure (noun) Inability of the kidneys to excrete wastes and to help maintain the electrolyte balance.
  • engine failure (noun) Engine does not run.
  • kidney failure (noun) Inability of the kidneys to excrete wastes and to help maintain the electrolyte balance.
  • coronary failure (noun) Inability of the heart to pump enough blood to sustain normal bodily functions.
  • equipment failure (noun) A cessation of normal operation.
  • acute renal failure (noun) Renal failure associated with burns or other trauma or with acute infection or obstruction of the urinary tract.
  • circulatory failure (noun) Failure of the cardiovascular system to supply adequate amounts of blood to body tissues.
  • acute kidney failure (noun) Renal failure associated with burns or other trauma or with acute infection or obstruction of the urinary tract.
  • chronic renal failure (noun) Renal failure that can result from a variety of systemic disorders.
  • chronic kidney failure (noun) Renal failure that can result from a variety of systemic disorders.
  • congestive heart failure (noun) Inability to pump enough blood to avoid congestion in the tissues.
