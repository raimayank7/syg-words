Far: meaning, synonyms and examples

Far is used as an adverb (5 senses) and adjective (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: To a considerable degree; very much.

Far as an adverb

  1. To a considerable degree; very much.

    “A far far better thing that I do.” “Felt far worse than yesterday.” “Eyes far too close together.”

  2. At or to or from a great distance in space.

    “He traveled far.” “Strayed far from home.” “Sat far away from each other.”

  3. At or to a certain point or degree.

    “I can only go so far before I have to give up.” “How far can we get with this kind of argument?”

  4. Remote in time.

    “If we could see far into the future.” “All that happened far in the past.”

  5. To an advanced stage or point.

    “A young man who will go very far.”

Far as an adjective

  1. Located at a great distance in time or space or degree.

    “We come from a far country.” “Far corners of the earth.” “The far future.”

    Opposite: near · Similar: cold, distant, distant, faraway, farther, farthermost

  2. Being of a considerable distance or length.

    “A far trek.”

    Similar: long

  3. Being the animal or vehicle on the right or being on the right side of an animal or vehicle.

    “The horse on the right is the far horse.” “The right side is the far side of the horse.”

    Similar: right

  4. Beyond a norm in opinion or actions.

    “The far right.”

    Similar: immoderate

Phrases and compounds with "far"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • by far (adverb) By a considerable margin.
  • go far (verb) Succeed in a big way; get to the top.
  • so far (adverb) Used in negative statement to describe a situation that has existed up to this point or up to the present time.
  • far cry (noun) Distance estimated in terms of the audibility of a cry.
  • far-off (adjective) Very far away in space or time.
  • far-out (adjective) Informal terms; strikingly unconventional.
  • far east (noun) A popular expression for the countries of eastern Asia (usually including China and Mongolia and Taiwan and Japan and Korea and Indochina and eastern Siberia).
  • far left (adjective) Radical or extremely liberal.
  • thus far (adverb) Used in negative statement to describe a situation that has existed up to this point or up to the present time.
  • far-famed (adjective) Widely known and esteemed.
  • far-flung (adjective) Distributed over a considerable extent.
  • far-right (adjective) Extremely conservative.
  • in so far (adverb) To the degree or extent that.
  • far and away (adverb) By a considerable margin.
  • far and near (adverb) Over great areas or distances; everywhere.
  • far and wide (adverb) Over great areas or distances; everywhere.
