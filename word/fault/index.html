Fault: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fault is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A wrong action attributable to bad judgment or ignorance or inattention.

In Hindi
दोष, ग़लती
Synonyms
mistake, error, defect, flaw, demerit, faulting, geological fault, shift, fracture, break
Word family
faulty

Fault as a noun

  1. A wrong action attributable to bad judgment or ignorance or inattention.

    “He made a bad mistake.” “She was quick to point out my errors.” “I could understand his English in spite of his grammatical faults.”

    Synonyms: mistake, error · A kind of: nonaccomplishment · Kinds: blot, confusion, incursion, miscalculation, distortion, slip, offside, oversight

  2. An imperfection in an object or machine.

    “A flaw caused the crystal to shatter.” “If there are any defects you should send it back to the manufacturer.”

    Synonyms: defect, flaw · A kind of: imperfection · Kinds: blister, bug, hole

  3. The quality of being inadequate or falling short of perfection.

    “They discussed the merits and demerits of her novel.” “He knew his own faults much better than she did.”

    Synonyms: demerit · A kind of: worth

  4. (geology) a crack in the earth's crust resulting from the displacement of one side with respect to the other.

    “They built it right over a geological fault.” “He studied the faulting of the earth's crust.”

    Synonyms: faulting, geological fault, shift, fracture, break · A kind of: crack · Kinds: inclined fault, strike-slip fault

  5. (electronics) equipment failure attributable to some defect in a circuit (loose connection or insulation failure or short circuit etc.).

    “It took much longer to find the fault than to fix it.”

    A kind of: breakdown

  6. Responsibility for a bad situation or event.

    “It was John's fault.”

    A kind of: responsibility

  7. (sports) a serve that is illegal (e.g., that lands outside the prescribed area).

    “He served too many double faults.”

    A kind of: serve · Kinds: double fault, footfault

Fault as a verb

  1. Put or pin the blame on.

    Synonyms: blame · A kind of: charge

Phrases and compounds with "fault"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at fault (adjective) Deserving blame.
  • fault line (noun) (geology) line determined by the intersection of a geological fault and the earth's surface.
  • find fault (verb) Harass with constant criticism.
  • to a fault (adverb) To a degree exceeding normal or proper limits.
  • common fault (noun) An inclined fault in which the hanging wall appears to have slipped downward relative to the footwall.
  • denali fault (noun) A major open geological fault in Alaska.
  • double fault (noun) (tennis) two successive faults in serving resulting in the loss of the point.
  • normal fault (noun) An inclined fault in which the hanging wall appears to have slipped downward relative to the footwall.
  • thrust fault (noun) A geological fault in which the upper side appears to have been pushed upward by compression.
  • gravity fault (noun) An inclined fault in which the hanging wall appears to have slipped downward relative to the footwall.
  • reverse fault (noun) A geological fault in which the upper side appears to have been pushed upward by compression.
  • inclined fault (noun) A geological fault in which one side is above the other.
  • geological fault (noun) (geology) a crack in the earth's crust resulting from the displacement of one side with respect to the other.
  • overthrust fault (noun) A geological fault in which the upper side appears to have been pushed upward by compression.
  • san andreas fault (noun) A major geological fault in California; runs from San Diego to San Francisco; the source of serious earthquakes.
  • strike-slip fault (noun) A geological fault in which one of the adjacent surfaces appears to have moved horizontally.
