Filter: meaning, synonyms and examples

Filter is used as a verb (3 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Remove by passing through a filter.

In Hindi
छन्नी; छानना
Synonyms
filtrate, strain, separate out, filter out, percolate, sink in, permeate, trickle, dribble
Word family
filtration, filtrate

Filter as a verb

  1. Remove by passing through a filter.

    “Filter out the impurities.”

    Synonyms: filtrate, strain, separate out, filter out · A kind of: separate

  2. Pass through.

    “Water permeates sand easily.”

    Synonyms: percolate, sink in, permeate · A kind of: penetrate · Kinds: infiltrate, infiltrate

  3. Run or flow slowly, as in drops or in an unsteady stream.

    “Water trickled onto the lawn from the broken hose.” “Reports began to dribble in.”

    Synonyms: trickle, dribble · A kind of: run · Kinds: leach

Filter as a noun

  1. Device that removes something from whatever passes through it.

    A kind of: device · Kinds: air filter, bacteria bed, coffee filter, drain basket, filter bed, fuel filter, light filter, oil filter

  2. An electrical device that alters the frequency spectrum of signals passing through it.

    A kind of: electrical device · Kinds: high-pass filter, low-pass filter

Phrases and compounds with "filter"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air filter (noun) A filter that removes dust from the air that passes through it.
  • filter bed (noun) Filter consisting of a layer of sand or gravel for filtering water.
  • filter out (verb) Remove by passing through a filter.
  • filter tip (noun) An air filter on the end of a cigarette; contains material that filters the smoke.
  • oil filter (noun) A filter that removes impurities from the oil used to lubricate an internal-combustion engine.
  • fuel filter (noun) A filter in the fuel line that screens out dirt and rust particles from the fuel.
  • filter paper (noun) A porous unsized paper used for filtering.
  • light filter (noun) A transparent filter that reduces the light (or some wavelengths of the light) passing through it.
  • water filter (noun) A filter to remove impurities from the water supply.
  • coffee filter (noun) Filter (usually of paper) that passes the coffee and retains the coffee grounds.
  • filter-tipped (adjective) Of a cigar or cigarette; having a tip designed to filter the smoke.
  • low-pass filter (noun) A filter that passes frequencies below a certain value and attenuates frequencies above that value.
  • high-pass filter (noun) A filter that passes frequencies above a certain value and attenuates frequencies below that value.
  • filter-tipped cigarette (noun) A cigarette with a filter tip.
