Floor: meaning, synonyms and examples

Floor is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The inside lower horizontal surface (as of a room, hallway, tent, or other structure).

In Hindi
फ़र्श; मंज़िल
Synonyms
flooring, level, storey, story, base, trading floor, shock, ball over, blow out of the water, take aback

Floor as a noun

  1. The inside lower horizontal surface (as of a room, hallway, tent, or other structure).

    “They needed rugs to cover the bare floors.” “We spread our sleeping bags on the dry floor of the tent.”

    Synonyms: flooring · A kind of: horizontal surface · Kinds: bell deck, dance floor, floorboard, parquet, truck bed

  2. A structure consisting of a room or set of rooms at a single position along a vertical scale.

    “What level is the office on?”

    Synonyms: level, storey, story · A kind of: structure · Kinds: basement, ground floor, loft, loft, mezzanine

  3. A lower limit.

    “The government established a wage floor.”

    Synonyms: base · A kind of: control · Kinds: price floor, wage floor

  4. The ground on which people and animals move about.

    “The fire spared the forest floor.”

    A kind of: land

  5. The bottom surface of any lake or other body of water.

    A kind of: surface

  6. The lower inside surface of any hollow structure.

    “The floor of the pelvis.” “The floor of the cave.”

    A kind of: surface

  7. The occupants of a floor.

    “The whole floor complained about the lack of heat.”

    A kind of: gathering

  8. The parliamentary right to address an assembly.

    “The chairman granted him the floor.”

    A kind of: right

  9. The legislative hall where members debate and vote and conduct other business.

    “There was a motion from the floor.”

    A kind of: hall

  10. A large room in a exchange where the trading is done.

    “He is a floor trader.”

    Synonyms: trading floor · A kind of: room

Floor as a verb

  1. Surprise greatly; knock someone's socks off.

    “I was floored when I heard that I was promoted.”

    Synonyms: shock, ball over, blow out of the water, take aback · A kind of: surprise · Kinds: startle

  2. Knock down with force.

    “He decked his opponent.”

    Synonyms: deck, coldcock, dump, knock down · A kind of: beat

Phrases and compounds with "floor"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • floor wax (noun) A preparation containing wax and used to polish and preserve the finish of floors.
  • fly floor (noun) A narrow raised platform at the side of a stage in a theater; stagehands can work the ropes controlling equipment in the flies.
  • sea floor (noun) The bottom of a sea or ocean.
  • floor lamp (noun) A lamp that stands on the floor.
  • floor plan (noun) Scale drawing of a horizontal section through a building at a given level; contrasts with elevation.
  • floor show (noun) A series of acts at a night club.
  • shop floor (noun) Workplace consisting of the part of a factory housing the machines.
  • wage floor (noun) Floor below which wages are not allowed to fall.
  • dance floor (noun) A bare floor polished for dancing.
  • first floor (noun) The floor of a building that is at or nearest to the level of the ground around the building.
  • floor board (noun) A board in the floor.
  • floor cover (noun) A covering for a floor.
  • floor joist (noun) Joist that supports a floor.
  • ocean floor (noun) The bottom of a sea or ocean.
  • price floor (noun) Floor below which prices are not allowed to fall.
  • floor leader (noun) The legislator who organizes his party's strategy.
