Flux: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flux is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: The rate of flow of energy or particles across a given surface.

In Hindi
प्रवाह
Synonyms
fluxion, state of flux, magnetic field, magnetic flux, flux density, flow, liquefy, liquify, blend, mix
Word family
fluxion

Flux as a noun

  1. The rate of flow of energy or particles across a given surface.

    A kind of: rate · Kinds: neutron flux, radiant flux

  2. A flow or discharge.

    Synonyms: fluxion · A kind of: flow

  3. A substance added to molten metals to bond with impurities that can then be readily removed.

    A kind of: chemical · Kinds: soldering flux

  4. Excessive discharge of liquid from a cavity or organ (as in watery diarrhea).

    A kind of: pathology

  5. A state of uncertainty about what should be done (usually following some important event) preceding the establishment of a new direction of action.

    “The flux following the death of the emperor.”

    Synonyms: state of flux · A kind of: state

  6. The lines of force surrounding a permanent magnet or a moving charged particle.

    Synonyms: magnetic field, magnetic flux · A kind of: field · Kinds: magnetosphere, solar magnetic field

  7. (physics) the number of changes in energy flow across a given surface per unit area.

    Synonyms: flux density · A kind of: density

  8. In constant change.

    “His opinions are in flux.” “The newness and flux of the computer industry.”

    A kind of: change

Flux as a verb

  1. Move or progress freely as if in a stream.

    “The crowd flowed out of the stadium.”

    Synonyms: flow · A kind of: move · Kinds: ripple, transpire

  2. Become liquid or fluid when heated.

    “The frozen fat liquefied.”

    Synonyms: liquefy, liquify · A kind of: change integrity · Kinds: condense, dissolve, fuse

  3. Mix together different elements.

    “The colors blend well.”

    Synonyms: blend, mix, conflate, commingle, immix, fuse, coalesce, meld · A kind of: change integrity · Kinds: gauge, absorb, melt, blend in, accrete, conjugate, admix, alloy

Phrases and compounds with "flux"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • flux unit (noun) A measure of the strength of a magnetic field per unit area.
  • flux density (noun) (physics) the number of changes in energy flow across a given surface per unit area.
  • neutron flux (noun) The rate of flow of neutrons; the number of neutrons passing through a unit area in unit time.
  • radiant flux (noun) The rate of flow of radiant energy (electromagnetic waves).
  • luminous flux (noun) The rate of flow of light energy.
  • magnetic flux (noun) A measure of the strength of a magnetic field over a given area.
  • state of flux (noun) A state of uncertainty about what should be done (usually following some important event) preceding the establishment of a new direction of action.
  • soldering flux (noun) Flux applied to surfaces that are to be joined by soldering; flux cleans the surfaces and results in a better bond.
  • flux applicator (noun) An applicator for applying flux (as in soldering).
  • flux density unit (noun) A measure of the amount of flux per unit of cross sectional area.
  • luminous flux unit (noun) A measure of luminous flux per unit area.
  • magnetic flux unit (noun) A measure of the strength of a magnetic field per unit area.
  • magnetic flux density (noun) The amount of magnetic flux in a unit area perpendicular to the direction of magnetic flow.
