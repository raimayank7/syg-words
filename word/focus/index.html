Focus: meaning, synonyms and examples

Focus is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: The concentration of attention or energy on something.

In Hindi
केंद्र; ध्यान केंद्रित करना
Synonyms
focusing, focussing, focal point, direction, centering, nidus, stress, concentrate, center, centre
Opposites
blur
Word family
focal, focussing, focusing

Focus as a noun

  1. The concentration of attention or energy on something.

    “The focus of activity shifted to molecular biology.” “He had no direction in his life.”

    Synonyms: focusing, focussing, focal point, direction, centering · A kind of: concentration · Kinds: particularism

  2. Maximum clarity or distinctness of an image rendered by an optical system.

    “In focus.” “Out of focus.”

    A kind of: distinctness

  3. Maximum clarity or distinctness of an idea.

    “The controversy brought clearly into focus an important difference of opinion.”

    A kind of: clarity

  4. A central point or locus of an infection in an organism.

    “The focus of infection.”

    Synonyms: focal point, nidus · A kind of: point

  5. Special emphasis attached to something.

    “The stress was more on accuracy than on speed.”

    Synonyms: stress · A kind of: emphasis

  6. A point of convergence of light (or other radiation) or a point from which it diverges.

    Synonyms: focal point · A kind of: point

  7. A fixed reference point on the concave side of a conic section.

    A kind of: point

Focus as a verb

  1. Direct one's attention on something.

    “Please focus on your studies and not on your hobbies.”

    Synonyms: concentrate, center, centre, pore, rivet · A kind of: think · Kinds: steep, recall, think, zoom in, listen

  2. Cause to converge on or toward a central point.

    “Focus the light on this image.”

    Opposite: blur · A kind of: sharpen · Kinds: refocus

  3. Bring into focus or alignment; to converge or cause to converge; of ideas or emotions.

    Synonyms: concenter, concentre, focalize, focalise · A kind of: align · Kinds: refocus

  4. Become focussed or come into focus.

    “The light focused.”

    Synonyms: focalize, focalise · Opposite: blur · A kind of: adjust

  5. Put (an image) into focus.

    “Please focus the image; we cannot enjoy the movie.”

    Synonyms: focalize, focalise, sharpen · Opposite: blur · A kind of: adjust · Kinds: refocus
