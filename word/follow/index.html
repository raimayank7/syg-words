Follow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Follow is used as a verb (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: To travel behind, go after, come after.

In Hindi
पीछे चलना, पालन करना
Synonyms
postdate, fall out, travel along, comply, abide by, come after, conform to, adopt, espouse, take after
Opposites
precede
Word family
follower, following

Follow as a verb

  1. To travel behind, go after, come after.

    “The ducklings followed their mother around the pond.” “Please follow the guide through the museum.”

    Opposite: precede · A kind of: travel · Kinds: lag, tailgate, shadow, carry

  2. Be later in time.

    “Tuesday always follows Monday.”

    Synonyms: postdate

  3. Come as a logical consequence; follow logically.

    “It follows that your assertion is false.” “The theorem falls out nicely.”

    Synonyms: fall out · A kind of: result

  4. Travel along a certain course.

    “Follow the road.” “Follow the trail.”

    Synonyms: travel along · A kind of: travel · Kinds: heel, ascend

  5. Act in accordance with someone's rules, commands, or wishes.

    “He complied with my instructions.” “You must comply or else!” “Follow these simple rules.”

    Synonyms: comply, abide by · A kind of: obey · Kinds: oblige, toe the line, conform to

  6. Come after in time, as a result.

    “A terrible tsunami followed the earthquake.”

    Synonyms: come after · A kind of: result

  7. Behave in accordance or in agreement with.

    “Follow a pattern.” “Follow my example.”

    Synonyms: conform to · A kind of: imitate · Kinds: go by

  8. Be next.

    “Mary plays best, with John and Sue following.”

    A kind of: be

  9. Choose and follow; as of theories, ideas, policies, strategies or plans.

    “She followed the feminist movement.” “The candidate espouses Republican ideals.”

    Synonyms: adopt, espouse · A kind of: choose · Kinds: adhere

  10. To bring something about at a later time than.

    “She followed dinner with a brandy.” “He followed his lecture with a question and answer period.”

  11. Imitate in behavior; take as a model.

    “Teenagers follow their friends in everything.”

    Synonyms: take after · A kind of: imitate

  12. Follow, discover, or ascertain the course of development of something.

    “We must follow closely the economic development is Cuba.” “Trace the student's progress.”

    Synonyms: trace · A kind of: analyze

  13. Follow with the eyes or the mind.

    “Keep an eye on the baby, please!” “The world is watching Sarajevo.” “She followed the men with the binoculars.”

    Synonyms: watch, observe, watch over, keep an eye on · A kind of: check · Kinds: keep tabs on, guard, invigilate

  14. Be the successor (of).

    “Carter followed Ford.” “Will Charles succeed to the throne?”

    Synonyms: succeed, come after · Kinds: accede, supplant

Phrases and compounds with "follow"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • follow up (verb) Pursue to a conclusion or bring to a successful issue.
  • follow-on (noun) An immediate second innings forced on a cricket team scoring a prescribed number of runs fewer than its opponents in the first innings.
  • follow-up (noun) A piece of work that exploits or builds on earlier work.
  • follow out (verb) Pursue to a conclusion or bring to a successful issue.
  • follow suit (verb) Do what someone else is doing.
  • follow up on (verb) Carry further or advance.
  • follow through (verb) Carry a stroke to natural completion after hitting or releasing a ball.
  • follow-through (noun) Carrying some project or intention to full completion.
