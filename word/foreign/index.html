Foreign: meaning, synonyms and examples

Foreign is used as an adjective (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Of concern to or concerning the affairs of other nations (other than your own).

Foreign as an adjective

  1. Of concern to or concerning the affairs of other nations (other than your own).

    “Foreign trade.” “A foreign office.”

    Opposite: domestic · Similar: abroad, external

  2. Relating to or originating in or characteristic of another place or part of the world.

    “Foreign nations.” “A foreign accent.” “On business in a foreign city.”

    Synonyms: strange · Opposite: native · Similar: adventive, alien, nonnative, established, foreign-born, imported

  3. Not contained in or deriving from the essential nature of something.

    “An economic theory alien to the spirit of capitalism.” “The mysticism so foreign to the French mind and temper.” “Jealousy is foreign to her nature.”

    Synonyms: alien · Similar: extrinsic

  4. Not belonging to that in which it is contained; introduced from an outside source.

    “Water free of extraneous matter.” “Foreign particles in milk.”

    Synonyms: extraneous · Similar: adulterating

Phrases and compounds with "foreign"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • foreign aid (noun) Aid (such as economic or military assistance) provided to one nation by another.
  • foreign bill (noun) A bill of exchange that is drawn in one country and made payable in another.
  • foreign-born (adjective) Of persons born in another area or country than that lived in.
  • foreign agent (noun) A spy for a foreign country.
  • foreign draft (noun) A bill of exchange that is drawn in one country and made payable in another.
  • foreign legion (noun) A military unit composed of foreign volunteers who serve the state.
  • foreign office (noun) The government department in charge of foreign relations.
  • foreign policy (noun) A policy governing international relations.
  • foreign country (noun) Any state of which one is not a citizen.
  • foreign mission (noun) An organization of missionaries in a foreign land sent to carry on religious work.
  • foreign service (noun) The part of the State Department that supplies diplomats for the United States embassies and consulates around the world.
  • foreign exchange (noun) The system by which one currency is exchanged for another; enables international transactions to take place.
  • foreign minister (noun) A government minister for foreign relations.
  • foreign correspondent (noun) A journalist who sends news reports and commentary from a foreign country for publication or broadcast.
  • french foreign legion (noun) A former foreign legion in the French army that was used for military duties outside of France.
  • french foreign office (noun) The French department in charge of foreign affairs; referred to familiarly by its address in Paris.
