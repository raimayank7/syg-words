Game: meaning, synonyms and examples

Game is used as a noun (11 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A contest with rules to determine a winner.

In Hindi
खेल
Synonyms
plot, secret plan, biz, crippled, halt, halting, lame, gimpy, gamy, gamey
Word family
gamy, gamey, gameness

Game as a noun

  1. A contest with rules to determine a winner.

    “You need four people to play this game.”

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: curling, bowling, pall-mall, athletic game, child's game, card game, table game, parlor game

  2. A single play of a sport or other contest.

    “The game lasted two hours.”

    A kind of: contest · Kinds: away game, home game, exhibition game, nightcap, twin bill, playoff game, cup tie

  3. An amusement or pastime.

    “They played word games.” “He thought of his painting as a game that filled his empty time.” “His life was all fun and games.”

    A kind of: diversion · Kinds: catch, party game, computer game, pinball, guessing game, ducks and drakes, mind game, paper chase

  4. Animal hunted for food or sport.

    A kind of: animal · Kinds: big game, game bird

  5. (tennis) a division of play during which one player serves.

    A kind of: part

  6. (games) the score at a particular point or the score needed to win.

    “The game is 6 all.” “He is serving for the game.”

    A kind of: score

  7. The flesh of wild animals that is used for food.

    A kind of: meat · Kinds: venison, buffalo, rabbit

  8. A secret scheme to do something (especially something underhand or illegal).

    “They concocted a plot to discredit the governor.” “I saw through his little game from the start.”

    Synonyms: plot, secret plan · A kind of: scheme · Kinds: counterplot, intrigue, conspiracy

  9. The game equipment needed in order to play a particular game.

    “The child received several games for his birthday.”

    A kind of: game equipment · Kinds: puzzle

  10. Your occupation or line of work.

    “He's in the plumbing game.” “She's in show biz.”

    Synonyms: biz · A kind of: occupation

  11. Frivolous or trifling behavior.

    “For actors, memorizing lines is no game.” “For him, life is all fun and games.”

Game as an adjective

  1. Disabled in the feet or legs.

    “A crippled soldier.” “A game leg.”

    Synonyms: crippled, halt, halting, lame, gimpy · Similar: unfit

  2. Willing to face danger.

    Synonyms: gamy, gamey, gritty, mettlesome, spirited, spunky · Similar: brave

Phrases and compounds with "game"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go game (noun) A board game for two players who place counters on a grid; the object is to surround and so capture the opponent's counters.
  • big game (noun) Large animals that are hunted for sport.
  • day game (noun) A game played in daylight.
  • end game (noun) The final stages of an extended process of negotiation.
  • game law (noun) A regulation intended to manage or preserve game animals.
  • war game (noun) A simulation of a military operation intended to train military commanders or to demonstrate a situation or to test a proposed strategy.
  • away game (noun) A game played away from home.
  • ball game (noun) A field game played with a ball (especially baseball).
  • card game (noun) A game played with playing cards.
  • crap game (noun) Playing craps.
  • fair game (noun) A person who is the aim of an attack (especially a victim of ridicule or exploitation) by some hostile person or influence.
  • game bird (noun) Any bird (as grouse or pheasant) that is hunted for sport.
  • game fish (noun) Any fish providing sport for the angler.
  • game fowl (noun) Any of several breeds reared for cockfighting.
  • game plan (noun) (figurative) a carefully thought out strategy for achieving an objective in war or politics or business or personal affairs.
  • game room (noun) A recreation room for noisy activities (parties or children's play etc).
