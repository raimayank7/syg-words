Green: meaning, synonyms and examples

Green is used as an adjective (5 senses), noun (8 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Of the color between blue and yellow in the color spectrum; similar to the color of fresh grass.

Green as an adjective

  1. Of the color between blue and yellow in the color spectrum; similar to the color of fresh grass.

    “A green tree.” “Green fields.” “Green paint.”

    Synonyms: greenish, light-green, dark-green · Similar: chromatic

  2. Concerned with or supporting or in conformity with the political principles of the Green Party.

  3. Not fully developed or mature; not ripe.

    “Unripe fruit.” “Fried green tomatoes.” “Green wood.”

    Synonyms: unripe, unripened, immature · Opposite: ripe · Similar: unaged

  4. Looking pale and unhealthy.

    “You're looking green.” “Green around the gills.”

    Similar: ill

  5. Naive and easily deceived or tricked.

    “At that early age she had been gullible and in love.”

    Synonyms: fleeceable, gullible · Similar: naive

Green as a noun

  1. Green color or pigment; resembling the color of growing grass.

    Synonyms: greenness, viridity · A kind of: chromatic color · Kinds: greenishness, sea green, sage green, bottle green, chrome green, emerald, olive green, yellow green

  2. A piece of open land for recreational use in an urban area.

    “They went for a walk in the park.”

    Synonyms: park, commons, common · A kind of: tract · Kinds: amusement park, village green

  3. United States labor leader who was president of the American Federation of Labor from 1924 to 1952 and who led the struggle with the Congress of Industrial Organizations (1873-1952).

  4. An environmentalist who belongs to the Green Party.

    A kind of: environmentalist

  5. A river that rises in western Wyoming and flows southward through Utah to become a tributary of the Colorado River.

  6. An area of closely cropped grass surrounding the hole on a golf course.

    “The ball rolled across the green and into the bunker.”

    Synonyms: putting green, putting surface · A kind of: site

  7. Any of various leafy plants or their leaves and stems eaten as vegetables.

    Synonyms: greens, leafy vegetable · A kind of: vegetable · Kinds: chop-suey greens, sprout, beet green, chard, salad green, lamb's-quarter, wild spinach, turnip greens

  8. Street names for ketamine.

    Synonyms: jet, super acid, honey oil, cat valium · A kind of: ketamine

Green as a verb

  1. Turn or become green.

    “The trees are greening.”

    A kind of: discolor

Phrases and compounds with "green"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • green ash (noun) A variety of red ash having glossy branchlets and lower leaf surfaces.
  • green bay (noun) A city of eastern Wisconsin on an arm of Lake Michigan.
  • green pea (noun) Fresh pea.
  • green tea (noun) Tea leaves that have been steamed and dried without fermenting.
  • pea green (noun) A shade of green tinged with yellow.
  • pea-green (adjective) Of a moderate slightly yellowish-green color.
  • sea green (noun) The property of a moderate green color resembling the waters of the sea.
  • sea-green (adjective) Of the color of the sea; bluish green.
  • beet green (noun) Young leaves of the beetroot.
  • blue green (noun) A blue-green color or pigment.
  • blue-green (adjective) Of a bluish shade of green.
  • dark-green (adjective) Of the color between blue and yellow in the color spectrum; similar to the color of fresh grass.
  • gray-green (adjective) Of green tinged with grey.
  • green bean (noun) Immature bean pod eaten as a vegetable.
  • green card (noun) A card that identifies the bearer as an alien with permanent resident status in the United States.
  • green corn (noun) A corn plant developed in order to have young ears that are sweet and suitable for eating.
