Heat: meaning, synonyms and examples

Heat is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A form of energy that is transferred by a difference in temperature.

In Hindi
गर्मी, ताप
Synonyms
heat energy, hotness, high temperature, warmth, passion, heating system, heating plant, heating, heat up, inflame
Opposites
cool
Word family
heatable, heater, heating

Heat as a noun

  1. A form of energy that is transferred by a difference in temperature.

    Synonyms: heat energy · A kind of: energy · Kinds: geothermal energy, heat of dissociation, heat of formation, heat of solution, latent heat, specific heat

  2. The presence of heat.

    Synonyms: hotness, high temperature · A kind of: temperature · Kinds: calefaction, fieriness, torridity, warmth, white heat

  3. The sensation caused by heat energy.

    Synonyms: warmth · A kind of: temperature

  4. The trait of being intensely emotional.

    Synonyms: warmth, passion · A kind of: emotionality · Kinds: fieriness

  5. A preliminary race in which the winner advances to a more important race.

    A kind of: race

  6. Utility to warm a building.

    “The heating system wasn't working.” “They have radiant heating.”

    Synonyms: heating system, heating plant, heating · A kind of: utility · Kinds: central heating, gas heat, panel heating, steam heat

Heat as a verb

  1. Make hot or hotter.

    “The sun heats the oceans.” “Heat the water on the stove.”

    Synonyms: heat up · Opposite: cool · A kind of: change · Kinds: crispen, scald, soak, calcine, preheat, overheat, sear, broil

  2. Provide with heat.

    “Heat the house.”

    A kind of: supply · Kinds: steam-heat

  3. Arouse or excite feelings and passions.

    “The ostentatious way of living of the rich ignites the hatred of the poor.” “The refugees' fate stirred up compassion around the world.” “Wake old feelings of hatred.”

    Synonyms: inflame, stir up, wake, ignite, fire up · A kind of: arouse · Kinds: ferment

  4. Gain heat or get hot.

    “The room heated up quickly.”

    Synonyms: hot up, heat up · Opposite: cool · A kind of: change state · Kinds: overheat, fry

Phrases and compounds with "heat"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • heat up (verb) Gain heat or get hot.
  • gas heat (noun) Heating system that burns natural gas.
  • heat ray (noun) A ray that produces a thermal effect.
  • red heat (noun) The heat or the color of fire.
  • dead heat (noun) A tie in a race.
  • heat lamp (noun) Electric heater consisting of a high-power incandescent lamp that emits infrared radiation.
  • heat pump (noun) Apparatus that extracts heat from a liquid that is at a higher temperature than its surroundings; can be used to transfer heat from a reservoir outside in order to heat a building.
  • heat rash (noun) Obstruction of the sweat ducts during high heat and humidity.
  • heat sink (noun) A metal conductor specially designed to conduct (and radiate) heat.
  • heat unit (noun) A unit of measurement for work.
  • heat wave (noun) A wave of unusually hot weather.
  • blood heat (noun) Temperature of the body; normally 98.6 F or 37 C in humans; usually measured to obtain a quick evaluation of a person's health.
  • heat flash (noun) A flash of intense heat (as released by an atomic explosion).
  • steam heat (noun) A heating system in which steam is generated in boilers and piped to radiators.
  • steam-heat (verb) Heat by means of steam.
  • total heat (noun) (thermodynamics) a thermodynamic quantity equal to the internal energy of a system plus the product of its volume and pressure.
