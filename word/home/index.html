Home: meaning, synonyms and examples

Home is used as a noun (9 senses), adverb (3 senses), adjective (3 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Where you live at a particular time.

In Hindi
घर
Synonyms
place, dwelling, domicile, abode, habitation, dwelling house, home plate, home base, plate, base
Opposites
away
Word family
homely, homy, homey

Home as a noun

  1. Where you live at a particular time.

    “Deliver the package to my home.” “He doesn't have a home to go to.” “Your place or mine?”

    Synonyms: place · A kind of: residence · Kinds: home away from home

  2. Housing that someone is living in.

    “He built a modest dwelling near the pond.” “They raise money to provide homes for the homeless.”

    Synonyms: dwelling, domicile, abode, habitation, dwelling house · A kind of: housing · Kinds: cliff dwelling, condominium, fixer-upper, hearth, hermitage, homestead, house, lake dwelling

  3. The country or state or city where you live.

    “Canadian tariffs enabled United States lumber companies to raise prices at home.” “His home is New Jersey.”

    A kind of: location

  4. (baseball) base consisting of a rubber slab where the batter stands; it must be touched by a base runner in order to score.

    “He ruled that the runner failed to touch home.”

    Synonyms: home plate, home base, plate · A kind of: base

  5. The place where you are stationed and from which missions start and end.

    Synonyms: base · A kind of: location

  6. Place where something began and flourished.

    “The United States is the home of basketball.”

    A kind of: beginning

  7. An environment offering affection and security.

    “Home is where the heart is.” “There's no place like home.”

    A kind of: environment

  8. A social unit living together.

    “He moved his family to Virginia.” “I waited until the whole house was asleep.” “The teacher asked how many people made up his home.”

    Synonyms: family, household, house, menage · A kind of: unit · Kinds: broken home, nuclear family, extended family, foster family, foster home

  9. An institution where people are cared for.

    “A home for the elderly.”

    Synonyms: nursing home, rest home · A kind of: institution

Home as an adverb

  1. At or to or in the direction of one's home or family.

    “He stays home on weekends.” “After the game the children brought friends home for supper.” “I'll be home tomorrow.”

  2. On or to the point aimed at.

    “The arrow struck home.”

  3. To the fullest extent; to the heart.

    “Drove the nail home.” “Drove his point home.” “His comments hit home.”

Home as an adjective

  1. Used of your own ground.

    “A home game.”

    Opposite: away

  2. Relating to or being where one lives or where one's roots are.

    “My home town.”

  3. Inside the country.

    “The British Home Office has broader responsibilities than the United States Department of the Interior.” “The nation's internal politics.”

    Synonyms: interior, internal, national · Similar: domestic

Home as a verb

  1. Provide with, or send to, a home.

    A kind of: house

  2. Return home accurately from a long distance.

    “Homing pigeons.”

    A kind of: return
