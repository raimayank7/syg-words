House: meaning, synonyms and examples

House is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A dwelling that serves as living quarters for one or more families.

In Hindi
घर, मकान
Synonyms
firm, business firm, sign of the zodiac, star sign, sign, mansion, planetary house, family, household, home
Word family
housing

House as a noun

  1. A dwelling that serves as living quarters for one or more families.

    “He has a house on Cape Cod.” “She felt she had to get out of the house.”

    A kind of: dwelling, building · Kinds: beach house, boarding house, bungalow, cabin, chalet, chapterhouse, country house, detached house

  2. The members of a business organization that owns or operates one or more establishments.

    “He worked for a brokerage house.”

    Synonyms: firm, business firm · A kind of: business · Kinds: corporation, accounting firm, consulting firm, publisher, dealer, law firm, auction house

  3. The members of a religious community living together.

    A kind of: community

  4. The audience gathered together in a theatre or cinema.

    “The house applauded.” “He counted the house.”

    A kind of: audience

  5. An official assembly having legislative powers.

    “A bicameral legislature has two houses.”

    A kind of: legislature

  6. Aristocratic family line.

    “The House of York.”

    A kind of: family · Kinds: royalty

  7. Play in which children take the roles of father or mother or children and pretend to interact like adults.

    “The children were playing house.”

    A kind of: play

  8. (astrology) one of 12 equal areas into which the zodiac is divided.

    Synonyms: sign of the zodiac, star sign, sign, mansion, planetary house · A kind of: region

  9. A social unit living together.

    “He moved his family to Virginia.” “I waited until the whole house was asleep.” “The teacher asked how many people made up his home.”

    Synonyms: family, household, home, menage · A kind of: unit · Kinds: broken home, nuclear family, extended family, foster family, foster home

  10. A building where theatrical performances or motion-picture shows can be presented.

    “The house was full.”

    Synonyms: theater, theatre · A kind of: building · Kinds: arena theater, cinema, dinner theater, home theater, little theater, music hall, opera

  11. A building in which something is sheltered or located.

    “They had a large carriage house.”

    A kind of: building · Kinds: bathhouse, courthouse, field house, icehouse, meat house, oast house, pump house, smokehouse

House as a verb

  1. Contain or cover.

    “This box houses the gears.”

    A kind of: accommodate

  2. Provide housing for.

    “The immigrants were housed in a new development outside the town.”

    Synonyms: put up, domiciliate · A kind of: shelter · Kinds: rehouse, home, lodge, chamber, take in

Phrases and compounds with "house"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • us house (noun) The lower legislative house of the United States Congress.
  • bee house (noun) A shed containing a number of beehives.
  • dog house (noun) Outbuilding that serves as a shelter for a dog.
  • house cat (noun) Any domesticated member of the genus Felis.
  • house fly (noun) Common fly that frequents human habitations and spreads many diseases.
  • nut house (noun) Pejorative terms for an insane asylum.
  • row house (noun) A house that is one of a row of identical houses situated side by side and sharing common walls.
  • sod house (noun) A house built of sod or adobe laid in horizontal courses.
  • card-house (noun) An unstable construction with playing cards.
  • coal house (noun) A shed for storing coal.
  • deck-house (noun) A superstructure on the upper deck of a ship.
  • frat house (noun) A house used as a residence by a chapter of a fraternity.
  • full house (noun) A poker hand with 3 of a kind and a pair.
  • hash house (noun) An inexpensive restaurant.
  • house dick (noun) A private detective employed by a hotel or retail store.
  • house wren (noun) Common American wren that nests around houses.
