Interior: meaning, synonyms and examples

Interior is used as a noun (3 senses) and adjective (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: The region that is inside of something.

In Hindi
भीतरी भाग
Synonyms
inside, home, internal, national, inner, midland, upcountry
Opposites
exterior
Word family
interiorize

Interior as a noun

  1. The region that is inside of something.

    Synonyms: inside · A kind of: region · Kinds: midland, midst, penetralia

  2. The inner or enclosed surface of something.

    Synonyms: inside · A kind of: surface · Kinds: belly

  3. The United States federal department charged with conservation and the development of natural resources; created in 1849.

    A kind of: executive department

Interior as an adjective

  1. Situated within or suitable for inside a building.

    “An interior scene.” “Interior decoration.” “An interior bathroom without windows.”

    Opposite: exterior · Similar: indoor, inside

  2. Inside the country.

    “The British Home Office has broader responsibilities than the United States Department of the Interior.” “The nation's internal politics.”

    Synonyms: home, internal, national · Similar: domestic

  3. Located inward.

    “Beethoven's manuscript looks like a bloody record of a tremendous inner battle.” “She thinks she has no soul, no interior life, but the truth is that she has no access to it.” “An internal sense of rightousness.”

    Synonyms: inner, internal · Similar: inward

  4. Inside and toward a center.

    “Interior regions of the earth.”

    Similar: internal

  5. Of or coming from the middle of a region or country.

    “Upcountry districts.”

    Synonyms: midland, upcountry · Similar: inland
