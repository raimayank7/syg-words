Issue: meaning, synonyms and examples

Issue is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: Prepare and issue for public distribution or sale.

In Hindi
मुद्दा; जारी करना; अंक
Synonyms
publish, bring out, put out, release, supply, emerge, come out, come forth, go forth, egress
Opposites
recall
Word family
issuer, issuing

Issue as a verb

  1. Prepare and issue for public distribution or sale.

    “Publish a magazine or newspaper.”

    Synonyms: publish, bring out, put out, release · A kind of: publicize · Kinds: edit

  2. Circulate or distribute or equip with.

    “Issue a new uniform to the children.” “Supply blankets for the beds.”

    Synonyms: supply · Opposite: recall · A kind of: distribute · Kinds: unblock, reissue, free

  3. Bring out an official document (such as a warrant).

    A kind of: communicate

  4. Come out of.

    “Water issued from the hole in the wall.” “The words seemed to come out by themselves.”

    Synonyms: emerge, come out, come forth, go forth, egress · Kinds: pop out, radiate, leak, escape, fall, debouch, come out

  5. Make out and issue.

    “Write out a check.” “Cut a ticket.” “Please make the check out to me.”

    Synonyms: write out, make out, cut · A kind of: write · Kinds: check

Issue as a noun

  1. An important question that is in dispute and must be settled.

    “The issue could be settled by requiring public education for everyone.” “Politicians never discuss the real issues.”

    A kind of: content · Kinds: gut issue, paramount issue, pocketbook issue, quodlibet

  2. One of a series published periodically.

    “She found an old issue of the magazine in her dentist's waiting room.”

    Synonyms: number · A kind of: periodical · Kinds: edition

  3. Some situation or event that is thought about.

    “He kept drifting off the topic.” “He had been thinking about the subject for several years.” “It is a matter for the police.”

    Synonyms: topic, subject, matter · A kind of: content · Kinds: area, blind spot, remit, res judicata

  4. The act of providing an item for general use or for official purposes (usually in quantity).

    “A new issue of stamps.” “The last issue of penicillin was over a month ago.”

    Synonyms: issuing, issuance · A kind of: provision · Kinds: stock issue

  5. Supplies (as food or clothing or ammunition) issued by the government.

    Synonyms: military issue, government issue · A kind of: store

  6. The income or profit arising from such transactions as the sale of land or other property.

    “The average return was about 5%.”

    Synonyms: return, take, takings, proceeds, yield, payoff · A kind of: income · Kinds: economic rent, payback

  7. A phenomenon that follows and is caused by some previous phenomenon.

    “The magnetic effect was greater when the rod was lengthwise.” “His decision had depressing consequences for business.” “He acted very wise after the event.”

    Synonyms: consequence, effect, outcome, result, event, upshot · A kind of: phenomenon · Kinds: offspring, aftereffect, aftermath, bandwagon effect, brisance, butterfly effect, by-product, change

  8. The immediate descendants of a person.

    “She was the mother of many offspring.” “He died without issue.”

    Synonyms: offspring, progeny · A kind of: relative · Kinds: baby, child, firstborn, grandchild, successor

  9. The becoming visible.

    “Not a day's difference between the emergence of the andrenas and the opening of the willow catkins.”

    Synonyms: emergence, egress · A kind of: beginning · Kinds: eruption, dissilience

  10. An opening that permits escape or release.

    “He blocked the way out.” “The canyon had only one issue.”

    Synonyms: exit, outlet, way out · A kind of: opening · Kinds: outfall

  11. The act of issuing printed materials.

    Synonyms: publication · A kind of: printing

Phrases and compounds with "issue"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gut issue (noun) An issue that elicits strong emotional reactions.
  • hot issue (noun) Newly issued stock that is in great public demand.
  • non-issue (noun) The state of something that has outlived its relevance.
  • bond issue (noun) Bonds sold by a corporation or government agency at a particular time and identifiable by date of maturity.
  • take issue (verb) Be of different opinions.
  • issue forth (verb) Come forth.
  • stock issue (noun) (corporation law) the authorization and delivery of shares of stock for sale to the public or the shares thus offered at a particular time.
  • rights issue (noun) An offering of common stock to existing shareholders who hold subscription rights or pre-emptive rights that entitle them to buy newly issued shares at a discount from the price at which they will be offered to the public later.
  • military issue (noun) Supplies (as food or clothing or ammunition) issued by the government.
  • paramount issue (noun) An issue whose settlement is more important than anything else; and issue that must be settled before anything else can be settled.
  • government issue (noun) Supplies (as food or clothing or ammunition) issued by the government.
  • hot-button issue (noun) An issue that elicits strong emotional reactions.
  • pocketbook issue (noun) An issue whose settlement will affect financial resources.
  • bread-and-butter issue (noun) An issue whose settlement will affect financial resources.
