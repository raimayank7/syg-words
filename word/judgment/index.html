Judgment: meaning, synonyms and examples

Judgment is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: An opinion formed by judging something.

In Hindi
निर्णय, फ़ैसला
Synonyms
judgement, mind, assessment, judicial decision, judging, opinion, legal opinion, sound judgment, sound judgement, perspicacity
Word family
judgmental, judge

Judgment as a noun

  1. An opinion formed by judging something.

    “He was reluctant to make his judgment known.” “She changed her mind.”

    Synonyms: judgement, mind · A kind of: opinion · Kinds: decision

  2. The act of judging or assessing a person or situation or event.

    “They criticized my judgment of the contestants.”

    Synonyms: judgement, assessment · A kind of: act · Kinds: justice, adjudication, disapproval, evaluation, estimate, logistic assessment, value judgment

  3. (law) the determination by a court of competent jurisdiction on matters submitted to it.

    Synonyms: judgement, judicial decision · A kind of: due process · Kinds: reversal, affirmation, confession of judgment, default judgment, non prosequitur, final judgment, judgment in personam, judgment in rem

  4. The cognitive process of reaching a decision or drawing conclusions.

    Synonyms: judgement, judging · A kind of: decision making · Kinds: prejudgment

  5. The legal document stating the reasons for a judicial decision.

    “Opinions are usually written by a single judge.”

    Synonyms: opinion, legal opinion, judgement · A kind of: legal document · Kinds: concurring opinion, dissenting opinion, majority opinion, obiter dictum

  6. The capacity to assess situations or circumstances shrewdly and to draw sound conclusions.

    Synonyms: judgement, sound judgment, sound judgement, perspicacity · A kind of: trait · Kinds: objectivity, subjectivity

  7. The mental ability to understand and discriminate between relations.

    Synonyms: sagacity, sagaciousness, judgement, discernment · A kind of: wisdom · Kinds: eye, common sense, judiciousness, discretion, injudiciousness

Phrases and compounds with "judgment"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • judgment day (noun) (New Testament) day at the end of time following Armageddon when God will decree the fates of all individual humans according to the good and evil of their earthly lives.
  • judgment lien (noun) Lien on a debtor's property that is granted to a creditor by court judgment; lien may be enforced by having the sheriff seize the property and hold a sheriff's sale.
  • last judgment (noun) (New Testament) day at the end of time following Armageddon when God will decree the fates of all individual humans according to the good and evil of their earthly lives.
  • pass judgment (verb) Form a critical opinion of.
  • final judgment (noun) A judgment disposing of the case before the court; after the judgment (or an appeal from it) is rendered all that remains is to enforce the judgment.
  • sound judgment (noun) The capacity to assess situations or circumstances shrewdly and to draw sound conclusions.
  • value judgment (noun) An assessment that reveals more about the values of the person making the assessment than about the reality of what is assessed.
  • day of judgment (noun) (New Testament) day at the end of time following Armageddon when God will decree the fates of all individual humans according to the good and evil of their earthly lives.
  • judgment in rem (noun) A judgment pronounced on the status of some particular subject or property or thing (as opposed to one pronounced on persons).
  • default judgment (noun) A judgment entered in favor of the plaintiff when the defendant defaults (fails to appear in court).
  • summary judgment (noun) A judgment rendered by the court prior to a verdict because no material issue of fact exists and one party or the other is entitled to a judgment as a matter of law.
  • cognovit judgment (noun) A judgment entered after a written confession by the debtor without the expense of ordinary legal proceedings.
  • personal judgment (noun) A judgment rendered against an individual (or corporation) for the payment of money damages.
  • judgment by default (noun) A judgment entered in favor of the plaintiff when the defendant defaults (fails to appear in court).
  • judgment in personam (noun) A judgment rendered against an individual (or corporation) for the payment of money damages.
  • unfavorable judgment (noun) Disapproval expressed by pointing out faults or shortcomings.
