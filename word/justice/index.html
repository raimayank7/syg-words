Justice: meaning, synonyms and examples

Justice is used as a noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: The quality of being just or fair.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites
injustice

Justice as a noun

  1. The quality of being just or fair.

    Synonyms: justness · Opposite: injustice · A kind of: righteousness, natural virtue · Kinds: fairness, right

  2. Judgment involved in the determination of rights and the assignment of rewards and punishments.

    A kind of: judgment · Kinds: administration

  3. A public official authorized to decide questions brought before a court of justice.

    Synonyms: judge, jurist · A kind of: official, adjudicator · Kinds: alcalde, chief justice, doge, justiciar, magistrate, ordinary, praetor, recorder

  4. The United States federal department responsible for enforcing federal laws (including the enforcement of all civil rights legislation); created in 1870.

    A kind of: executive department

Phrases and compounds with "justice"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • do justice (verb) Bring out fully or to advantage.
  • chief justice (noun) The judge who presides over a supreme court.
  • poetic justice (noun) An outcome in which virtue triumphs over vice (often ironically).
  • court of justice (noun) A tribunal that is presided over by a magistrate or by one or more judges who administer justice according to the laws.
  • justice department (noun) The United States federal department responsible for enforcing federal laws (including the enforcement of all civil rights legislation); created in 1870.
  • justice of the peace (noun) A local magistrate with limited powers.
  • department of justice (noun) The United States federal department responsible for enforcing federal laws (including the enforcement of all civil rights legislation); created in 1870.
  • fugitive from justice (noun) Someone who is sought by law officers; someone trying to elude justice.
  • obstruction of justice (noun) Impeding those who seek justice in a court (as by trying to influence or intimidate any juror or witness or officer of the court); can result in a finding of contempt of court.
  • bureau of justice statistics (noun) The agency in the Department of Justice that is the primary source of criminal justice statistics for federal and local policy makers.
  • department of justice canada (noun) An agency of the Canadian government that provides litigation and legal advice and opinions to the government.
  • national institute of justice (noun) The law enforcement agency that is the research and development branch of the Department of Justice.
  • international court of justice (noun) A court established to settle disputes between members of the United Nations.
  • united nations crime prevention and criminal justice (noun) The United Nations office responsible for crime prevention and criminal justice and law reform.
