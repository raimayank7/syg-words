King: meaning, synonyms and examples

King is used as a noun (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: A male sovereign; ruler of a kingdom.

In Hindi
राजा
Synonyms
male monarch, queen, world-beater, baron, big businessman, business leader, magnate, mogul, power, top executive
Opposites
queen
Word family
kingdom, kingly, kingship

King as a noun

  1. A male sovereign; ruler of a kingdom.

    Synonyms: male monarch · Opposite: queen · A kind of: sovereign

  2. A competitor who holds a preeminent position.

    Synonyms: queen, world-beater · A kind of: rival

  3. A very wealthy or powerful businessman.

    “An oil baron.”

    Synonyms: baron, big businessman, business leader, magnate, mogul, power, top executive, tycoon · A kind of: businessman · Kinds: oil tycoon

  4. Preeminence in a particular category or group or field.

    “The lion is the king of beasts.”

    A kind of: eminence

  5. United States woman tennis player (born in 1943).

  6. United States guitar player and singer of the blues (born in 1925).

  7. United States charismatic civil rights leader and Baptist minister who campaigned against the segregation of Blacks (1929-1968).

  8. A checker that has been moved to the opponent's first row where it is promoted to a piece that is free to move either forward or backward.

    A kind of: checker

  9. One of the four playing cards in a deck bearing the picture of a king.

    A kind of: face card

  10. (chess) the weakest but the most important piece.

    A kind of: chessman

Phrases and compounds with "king"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • king nut (noun) Hickory of the eastern United States resembling the shagbark but having a much larger nut.
  • sea king (noun) A Viking pirate chief.
  • sun king (noun) King of France from 1643 to 1715; his long reign was marked by the expansion of French influence in Europe and by the magnificence of his court and the Palace of Versailles (1638-1715).
  • king crab (noun) Meat of large cold-water crab; mainly leg meat.
  • king fern (noun) Fern of rain forests of tropical Australia and New Zealand and South Africa.
  • king john (noun) Youngest son of Henry II; King of England from 1199 to 1216; succeeded to the throne on the death of his brother Richard I; lost his French possessions; in 1215 John was compelled by the barons to sign the Magna Carta (1167-1216).
  • king lear (noun) The hero of William Shakespeare's tragedy who was betrayed and mistreated by two of his scheming daughters.
  • king post (noun) Post connecting the crossbeam to the apex of a triangular truss.
  • king-size (adjective) Extra large.
  • king cobra (noun) Large cobra of southeastern Asia and the East Indies; the largest venomous snake; sometimes placed in genus Naja.
  • king devil (noun) European hawkweed introduced into northeastern United States; locally troublesome weeds.
  • king james (noun) The first Stuart to be king of England and Ireland from 1603 to 1625 and king of Scotland from 1567 to 1625; he was the son of Mary Queen of Scots and he succeeded Elizabeth I; he alienated the British Parliament by claiming the divine right of kings (1566-1625).
  • king snake (noun) Any of numerous nonvenomous North American constrictors; feed on other snakes and small mammals.
  • king-sized (adjective) Extra large.
  • march king (noun) A United States bandmaster and composer of military marches (1854-1932).
  • king arthur (noun) A legendary king of the Britons (possibly based on a historical figure in the 6th century but the story has been retold too many times to be sure); said to have led the Knights of the Round Table at Camelot.
