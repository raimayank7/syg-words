Language: meaning, synonyms and examples

Language is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A systematic means of communicating by the use of sounds or conventional symbols.

In Hindi
भाषा
Synonyms
linguistic communication, speech, speech communication, spoken communication, spoken language, voice communication, oral communication, lyric, words, linguistic process

Language as a noun

  1. A systematic means of communicating by the use of sounds or conventional symbols.

    “He taught foreign languages.” “The language introduced is standard throughout the text.” “The speed with which a program can be executed depends on the language in which it is written.”

    Synonyms: linguistic communication · A kind of: communication · Kinds: usage, dead language, words, source language, object language, sign language, artificial language, metalanguage

  2. (language) communication by word of mouth.

    “His speech was garbled.” “He uttered harsh language.” “He recorded the spoken language of the streets.”

    Synonyms: speech, speech communication, spoken communication, spoken language, voice communication, oral communication · A kind of: auditory communication · Kinds: words, pronunciation, conversation, discussion, saying, non-standard speech, idiolect, monologue

  3. The text of a popular song or musical-comedy number.

    “His compositions always started with the lyrics.” “He wrote both words and music.” “The song uses colloquial language.”

    Synonyms: lyric, words · A kind of: text · Kinds: love lyric

  4. The cognitive processes involved in producing and understanding linguistic communication.

    “He didn't have the language to express his feelings.”

    Synonyms: linguistic process · A kind of: higher cognitive process · Kinds: reading

  5. The mental faculty or power of vocal communication.

    “Language sets homo sapiens apart from all other animals.”

    Synonyms: speech · A kind of: faculty

  6. A system of words used to name things in a particular discipline.

    “Legal terminology.” “Biological nomenclature.” “The language of sociology.”

    Synonyms: terminology, nomenclature · A kind of: word · Kinds: markup language, toponymy

Phrases and compounds with "language"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • body language (noun) Communication via the movements or attitudes of the body.
  • dead language (noun) A language that is no longer learned as a native language.
  • language area (noun) A large cortical area (in the left hemisphere in most people) containing all the centers associated with language.
  • language unit (noun) One of the natural units into which linguistic messages can be analyzed.
  • language zone (noun) A large cortical area (in the left hemisphere in most people) containing all the centers associated with language.
  • sign language (noun) Language expressed by visible hand gestures.
  • sign-language (adjective) Used of the language of the deaf.
  • tone language (noun) A language in which different tones distinguish different meanings.
  • first language (noun) One's native language; the language learned by children and passed from one generation to the next.
  • hmong language (noun) A language of uncertain affiliation spoken by the Hmong.
  • kadai language (noun) A family of Sino-Tibetan languages spoken in southeastern Asia.
  • mayan language (noun) A family of American Indian languages spoken by Maya.
  • query language (noun) A source language consisting of procedural operators that invoke functions to be executed.
  • tonal language (noun) A language in which different tones distinguish different meanings.
  • altaic language (noun) A group of related languages spoken in Asia and southeastern Europe.
  • arabic language (noun) The Semitic language of the Arabs; spoken in a variety of dialects.
