Late: meaning, synonyms and examples

Late is used as an adjective (7 senses) and adverb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Being or occurring at an advanced period of time or after a usual or expected time.

Opposites

Late as an adjective

  1. Being or occurring at an advanced period of time or after a usual or expected time.

    “Late evening.” “Late 18th century.” “A late movie.”

    Opposite: early, middle · Similar: advanced, after-hours, latish, posthumous

  2. After the expected or usual time; delayed.

    “A belated birthday card.” “I'm late for the plane.” “The train is late.”

    Synonyms: belated, tardy · Similar: unpunctual

  3. Of the immediate past or just previous to the present time.

    “A late development.” “Their late quarrel.” “His recent trip to Africa.”

    Synonyms: recent · Similar: past

  4. Having died recently.

    “Her late husband.”

    Similar: dead

  5. Of a later stage in the development of a language or literature; used especially of dead languages.

    “Late Greek.”

    Opposite: early, middle

  6. At or toward an end or late period or stage of development.

    “The late phase of feudalism.” “A later symptom of the disease.” “Later medical science could have saved the child.”

    Synonyms: later · Opposite: early · Similar: advanced, tardive

  7. (used especially of persons) of the immediate past.

    “The former president.” “Our late President is still very active.” “The previous occupant of the White House.”

    Synonyms: former, previous · Similar: past

Late as an adverb

  1. Later than usual or than expected.

    “The train arrived late.” “We awoke late.” “The children came late to school.”

    Synonyms: belatedly, tardily · Opposite: early

  2. To an advanced time.

    “Deep into the night.” “Talked late into the evening.”

    Synonyms: deep

  3. At an advanced age or stage.

    “She married late.” “Undertook the project late in her career.”

  4. In the recent past.

    “He was in Paris recently.” “Lately the rules have been enforced.” “As late as yesterday she was fine.”

    Synonyms: recently, lately, of late, latterly

Phrases and compounds with "late"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • of late (adverb) In the recent past.
  • late greek (noun) The Greek language in the 3rd to 8th centuries.
  • late latin (noun) The form of Latin written between the 3rd and 8th centuries.
  • sleep late (verb) Sleep later than usual or customary.
  • late blight (noun) Blight in which symptoms appear late in the growing season especially a disease of solanaceous plants caused by the fungus Phytophthora infestans.
  • late-blooming (adjective) Of plants that bloom during the autumn.
  • late-ripening (adjective) Of plants that ripen in the fall.
  • late-flowering (adjective) Of plants that bloom during the autumn.
  • late-night hour (noun) The latter part of night.
  • late purple aster (noun) A variety of aster.
  • late-spring-blooming (adjective) Of plants that bloom during the spring.
