Leaf: meaning, synonyms and examples

Leaf is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: The main organ of photosynthesis and transpiration in higher plants.

In Hindi
पत्ता
Synonyms
leafage, foliage, folio, flick, flip, thumb, riffle, riff
Word family
leafy, leaflet, leafage

Leaf as a noun

  1. The main organ of photosynthesis and transpiration in higher plants.

    Synonyms: leafage, foliage · A kind of: plant organ · Kinds: cataphyll, floral leaf, dandelion green, pitcher, sporophyll, blade, amplexicaul leaf, greenery

  2. A sheet of any written or printed material (especially in a manuscript or book).

    Synonyms: folio · A kind of: sheet · Kinds: flyleaf, interleaf, page

  3. Hinged or detachable flat section (as of a table or door).

    A kind of: section · Kinds: drop-leaf

Leaf as a verb

  1. Look through a book or other written material.

    “He thumbed through the report.” “She leafed through the volume.”

    Synonyms: flick, flip, thumb, riffle, riff · A kind of: peruse

  2. Turn over pages.

    “Leaf through a book.” “Leaf a manuscript.”

    A kind of: turn

  3. Produce leaves, of plants.

    A kind of: grow

Phrases and compounds with "leaf"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bay leaf (noun) Dried leaf of the bay laurel.
  • fig leaf (noun) A leaf from a fig tree.
  • leaf bud (noun) A bud from which leaves (but not flowers) develop.
  • leaf bug (noun) Small bright-colored insect that feeds on plant juices.
  • leaf fat (noun) Fat lining the abdomen and kidneys in hogs which is used to make lard.
  • tea leaf (noun) Dried leaves of the tea shrub; used to make tea.
  • drop-leaf (noun) A hinged leaf on a table that can be raised and supported by a bracket.
  • gold leaf (noun) A very thin form of gold foil.
  • leaf beet (noun) Beet lacking swollen root; grown as a vegetable for its edible leaves and stalks.
  • leaf cast (noun) A disease of conifers causing the needles to fall.
  • leaf form (noun) Any of the various shape that leaves of plants can assume.
  • leaf lard (noun) Fat lining the abdomen and kidneys in hogs which is used to make lard.
  • leaf mold (noun) Soil composed mainly of decaying leaves.
  • leaf node (noun) (botany) the small swelling that is the part of a plant stem from which one or more leaves emerge.
  • leaf soil (noun) Soil composed mainly of decaying leaves.
  • leaf-book (noun) An unbound manuscript of some ancient classic (as distinguished from a scroll).
