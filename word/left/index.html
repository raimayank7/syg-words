Left: meaning, synonyms and examples

Left is used as an adjective (4 senses), noun (5 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Being or located on or directed toward the side of the body to the west when facing north.

In Hindi
बायाँ; बचा हुआ
Synonyms
leftover, left over, odd, remaining, unexpended, left-hand, left wing, left hand, left field, leftfield
Opposites
right, center

Left as an adjective

  1. Being or located on or directed toward the side of the body to the west when facing north.

    “My left hand.” “Left center field.” “The left bank of a river is bank on your left side when you are facing downstream.”

    Opposite: right · Similar: left-hand, leftmost, near, port

  2. Not used up.

    “Leftover meatloaf.” “She had a little money left over so she went to a movie.” “Some odd dollars left.”

    Synonyms: leftover, left over, odd, remaining, unexpended · Similar: unexhausted

  3. Intended for the left hand.

    “I rarely lose a left-hand glove.”

    Synonyms: left-hand · Similar: left-handed

  4. Of or belonging to the political or intellectual left.

    Opposite: center, right · Similar: far left, leftish, leftist, liberal

Left as a noun

  1. Location near or direction toward the left side; i.e. the side to the north when a person or object faces east.

    “She stood on the left.”

    Opposite: right · A kind of: position · Kinds: stage left

  2. Those who support varying degrees of social or political or economic change designed to promote the public welfare.

    Synonyms: left wing · A kind of: faction

  3. The hand that is on the left side of the body.

    “Jab with your left.”

    Synonyms: left hand · A kind of: hand

  4. The piece of ground in the outfield on the catcher's left.

    “The batter flied out to left.”

    Synonyms: left field, leftfield · A kind of: tract

  5. A turn toward the side of the body that is on the north when the person is facing east.

    “Take a left at the corner.”

    A kind of: turn

Left as an adverb

  1. Toward or on the left; also used figuratively.

    “He looked right and left.” “The political party has moved left.”

    Opposite: right
