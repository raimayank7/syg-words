Legal: meaning, synonyms and examples

Legal is used as an adjective (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Established by or founded upon law or official or accepted rules.

Legal as an adjective

  1. Established by or founded upon law or official or accepted rules.

    Opposite: illegal · Similar: court-ordered, judicial, jural, lawful, ratified, statutory

  2. Of or relating to jurisprudence.

    “Legal loophole.”

  3. Having legal efficacy or force.

    “A sound title to the property.”

    Synonyms: sound, effectual · Similar: valid

  4. Relating to or characteristic of the profession of law.

    “The legal profession.”

  5. Allowed by official rules.

    “A legal pass receiver.”

    Similar: eligible

Phrases and compounds with "legal"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • legal age (noun) The age at which persons are considered competent to manage their own affairs.
  • legal fee (noun) A fee paid for legal service.
  • legal code (noun) A code of laws adopted by a state or nation.
  • legal duty (noun) Acts which the law requires be done or forborne.
  • legal brief (noun) A document stating the facts and points of law of a client's case.
  • legal fraud (noun) Comprises all acts or omissions or concealments involving breach of equitable or legal duty or trust or confidence.
  • legal power (noun) (law) the right and power to interpret and apply the law.
  • legal right (noun) A right based in law.
  • legal action (noun) A judicial proceeding brought by one party against another; one party prosecutes another for a wrong done or for protection of a right or for prevention of a wrong.
  • legal expert (noun) A legal scholar versed in civil law or the law of nations.
  • legal injury (noun) Any harm or injury resulting from a violation of a legal right.
  • legal ouster (noun) The expulsion of someone (such as a tenant) from the possession of land by process of law.
  • legal status (noun) A status defined by law.
  • legal system (noun) A system for interpreting and enforcing the laws.
  • legal tender (noun) Something that can be used as an official medium of payment.
  • legal holiday (noun) Authorized by law and limiting work or official business.
