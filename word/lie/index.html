Lie: meaning, synonyms and examples

Lie is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be located or situated somewhere; occupy a certain position.

In Hindi
झूठ बोलना; लेटना
Synonyms
dwell, consist, lie in, rest, lie down, prevarication
Opposites
stand, sit
Word family
liar, lying

Lie as a verb

  1. Be located or situated somewhere; occupy a certain position.

    A kind of: be · Kinds: nestle, intervene, top, mediate, ride, lap, localize, slant

  2. Be lying, be prostrate; be in a horizontal position.

    “The sick man lay in bed all day.” “The books are lying on the shelf.”

    Opposite: stand, sit · Kinds: sun, sprawl, recumb, overlie, lie awake, repose, bask

  3. Originate (in).

    “The problems dwell in the social injustices in this country.”

    Synonyms: dwell, consist, lie in · A kind of: exist

  4. Be and remain in a particular state or condition.

    “Lie dormant.”

    A kind of: be

  5. Tell an untruth; pretend with intent to deceive.

    “Don't lie to your parents.” “She lied when she told me she was only 29.”

    A kind of: misinform · Kinds: romance, perjure, fib

  6. Have a place in relation to something else.

    “The fate of Bosnia lies in the hands of the West.” “The responsibility rests with the Allies.”

    Synonyms: rest · A kind of: be

  7. Assume a reclining position.

    “Lie down on the bed until you feel better.”

    Synonyms: lie down · A kind of: change posture · Kinds: stretch, charge, prostrate

Lie as a noun

  1. A statement that deviates from or perverts the truth.

    Synonyms: prevarication · A kind of: falsehood · Kinds: fib, jactitation, whopper, white lie

  2. Norwegian diplomat who was the first Secretary General of the United Nations (1896-1968).

  3. Position or manner in which something is situated.

    A kind of: position

Phrases and compounds with "lie"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • lie in (verb) Originate (in).
  • lie-in (noun) A long stay in bed in the morning.
  • lie low (verb) Keep a low profile, try to be inconspicuous.
  • lie down (verb) Assume a reclining position.
  • lie-abed (noun) A person who stays in bed until a relatively late hour.
  • lie about (verb) Hang around idly.
  • lie awake (verb) Lie without sleeping.
  • white lie (noun) An unimportant lie (especially one told to be tactful or polite).
  • lie around (verb) Hang around idly.
  • trygve lie (noun) Norwegian diplomat who was the first Secretary General of the United Nations (1896-1968).
  • lie dormant (verb) Be inactive, as if asleep.
  • lie in wait (verb) Wait in hiding to attack.
  • lie detector (noun) A polygraph that records bodily changes sometimes associated with lying.
  • trygve halvden lie (noun) Norwegian diplomat who was the first Secretary General of the United Nations (1896-1968).
