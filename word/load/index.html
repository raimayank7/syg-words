Load: meaning, synonyms and examples

Load is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Weight to be borne or conveyed.

In Hindi
बोझ; लादना
Synonyms
loading, burden, cargo, lading, freight, payload, shipment, consignment, encumbrance, incumbrance
Word family
loader, loading

Load as a noun

  1. Weight to be borne or conveyed.

    Synonyms: loading, burden · A kind of: weight · Kinds: burthen, dead load, live load, millstone, overload, overload

  2. A quantity that can be processed or transported at one time.

    “The system broke down under excessive loads.”

    Synonyms: loading · A kind of: indefinite quantity · Kinds: trainload

  3. Goods carried by a large vehicle.

    Synonyms: cargo, lading, freight, loading, payload, shipment, consignment · A kind of: merchandise

  4. The power output of a generator or power plant.

    A kind of: electrical power

  5. An onerous or difficult concern.

    “The burden of responsibility.” “That's a load off my mind.”

    Synonyms: burden, encumbrance, incumbrance, onus · A kind of: concern · Kinds: dead weight, fardel, imposition

  6. A deposit of valuable ore occurring within definite boundaries separating it from surrounding rocks.

    Synonyms: lode · A kind of: deposit · Kinds: mother lode

  7. The front part of a guided missile or rocket or torpedo that carries the nuclear or explosive charge or the chemical or biological agents.

    Synonyms: warhead, payload · A kind of: explosive

  8. Electrical device to which electrical power is delivered.

    A kind of: electrical device

Load as a verb

  1. Fill or place a load on.

    “Load a car.” “Load the truck with hay.”

    Synonyms: lade, laden, load up · A kind of: fill · Kinds: pack, overload, reload, stack

  2. Provide (a device) with something necessary.

    “He loaded his gun carefully.” “Load the camera.”

    Synonyms: charge · A kind of: fill · Kinds: recharge

  3. Transfer from a storage device to a computer's memory.

    A kind of: transfer

  4. Put (something) on a structure or conveyance.

    “Load the bags onto the trucks.”

    A kind of: put

  5. Corrupt, debase, or make impure by adding a foreign or inferior substance; often by replacing valuable ingredients with inferior ones.

    Synonyms: adulterate, stretch, dilute, debase · A kind of: corrupt · Kinds: water down, sophisticate

Phrases and compounds with "load"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • load up (verb) Fill or place a load on.
  • case load (noun) The number of cases handled in a given period of time (as by a court or agency).
  • dead load (noun) A constant load on a structure (e.g. a bridge) due to the weight of the supported structure itself.
  • live load (noun) A variable load on a structure (e.g. a bridge) such as moving traffic.
  • load down (verb) Load with a pack.
  • load line (noun) Waterlines to show the level the water should reach when the ship is properly loaded.
  • work load (noun) Work that a person is expected to do in a specified time.
  • get a load (verb) Look at with attention.
  • load factor (noun) (aeronautics) the ratio of an external load to the weight of the aircraft (measured in g).
  • load-bearing (adjective) Capable of bearing a structural load.
  • load-shedding (noun) Cutting off the electric current on certain lines when the demand becomes greater than the supply.
