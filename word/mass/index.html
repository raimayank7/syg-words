Mass: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mass is used as a noun (8 senses), verb (1 sense) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The property of a body that causes it to have weight in a gravitational field.

In Hindi
द्रव्यमान; ढेर; जन
Synonyms
batch, deal, flock, good deal, great deal, hatful, heap, lot, mess, mickle

Mass as a noun

  1. The property of a body that causes it to have weight in a gravitational field.

    A kind of: fundamental quantity, physical property · Kinds: body, biomass, critical mass, rest mass, relativistic mass, bulk, gravitational mass, inertial mass

  2. (often followed by `of') a large number or amount or extent.

    “A batch of letters.” “A deal of trouble.” “A lot of money.”

    Synonyms: batch, deal, flock, good deal, great deal, hatful, heap, lot · A kind of: large indefinite quantity · Kinds: flood, haymow

  3. An ill-structured collection of similar things (objects or people).

    A kind of: collection · Kinds: logjam, shock

  4. (Roman Catholic Church and Protestant Churches) the celebration of the Eucharist.

    A kind of: religious ceremony

  5. A body of matter without definite shape.

    “A huge ice mass.”

    A kind of: body · Kinds: coprolith, drift, mat, pulp

  6. The common people generally.

    “Separate the warriors from the mass.” “Power to the people.”

    Synonyms: multitude, masses, hoi polloi, people, the great unwashed · A kind of: group · Kinds: audience, following

  7. The property of something that is great in magnitude.

    “It is cheaper to buy it in bulk.” “He received a mass of correspondence.” “The volume of exports.”

    Synonyms: bulk, volume · A kind of: magnitude · Kinds: dollar volume

  8. A musical setting for a Mass.

    “They played a Mass composed by Beethoven.”

    A kind of: religious music

Mass as a verb

  1. Join together into a mass or collect or form a mass.

    “Crowds were massing outside the palace.”

    A kind of: crowd · Kinds: press

Mass as an adjective

  1. Formed of separate units gathered into a mass or whole.

    “Aggregate expenses include expenses of all divisions combined for the entire year.” “The aggregated amount of indebtedness.”

    Synonyms: aggregate, aggregated, aggregative · Similar: collective

Phrases and compounds with "mass"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air mass (noun) A large body of air with uniform characteristics horizontally.
  • ice mass (noun) A large mass of ice.
  • low mass (noun) A Mass recited without music.
  • high mass (noun) A solemn and elaborate Mass with music.
  • land mass (noun) A large continuous extent of land.
  • mass card (noun) (Roman Catholic Church) a card sent to a bereaved family that says the sender has arranged for a Mass to be said in memory of the deceased.
  • mass noun (noun) A noun that does not form plurals.
  • mass unit (noun) A unit of measurement for mass.
  • rest mass (noun) (physics) the mass of a body as measured when the body is at rest relative to an observer, an inherent property of the body.
  • atomic mass (noun) (chemistry) the mass of an atom of a chemical element expressed in atomic mass units.
  • mass action (noun) (neurology) the principle that the cortex of the brain operates as a coordinated system with large masses of neural tissue involved in all complex functioning.
  • mass defect (noun) The amount by which the mass of an atomic nucleus is less than the sum of the masses of its constituent particles.
  • mass energy (noun) (physics) the mass of a body regarded relativistically as energy.
  • mass medium (noun) (usually plural) transmissions that are disseminated widely to the public.
  • mass number (noun) The sum of the number of neutrons and protons in an atomic nucleus.
  • mass culture (noun) The culture that is widely disseminated via the mass media.
