Medium: meaning, synonyms and examples

Medium is used as a noun (11 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A means or instrumentality for storing or communicating information.

In Hindi
माध्यम; मध्यम
Synonyms
culture medium, spiritualist, sensitive, mass medium, metier, average, intermediate
Word family
medial

Medium as a noun

  1. A means or instrumentality for storing or communicating information.

    A kind of: instrumentality · Kinds: vehicle, paper, film, print media, storage medium, telecommunication, album

  2. The surrounding environment.

    “Fish require an aqueous medium.”

    A kind of: environment

  3. An intervening substance through which signals can travel as a means for communication.

    A kind of: communication · Kinds: ether, air

  4. (bacteriology) a nutrient substance (solid or liquid) that is used to cultivate micro-organisms.

    Synonyms: culture medium · A kind of: food · Kinds: agar

  5. A liquid with which pigment is mixed by a painter.

    A kind of: liquid · Kinds: megilp

  6. (biology) a substance in which specimens are preserved or displayed.

    A kind of: substance · Kinds: contrast medium

  7. An intervening substance through which something is achieved.

    “The dissolving medium is called a solvent.”

    A kind of: substance · Kinds: solvent

  8. A state that is intermediate between extremes; a middle position.

    “A happy medium.”

    A kind of: state

  9. Someone who serves as an intermediary between the living and the dead.

    “He consulted several mediums.”

    Synonyms: spiritualist, sensitive · A kind of: psychic

  10. (usually plural) transmissions that are disseminated widely to the public.

    Synonyms: mass medium · A kind of: transmission · Kinds: broadcast medium

  11. An occupation for which you are especially well suited.

    “In law he found his true metier.”

    Synonyms: metier · A kind of: occupation

Medium as an adjective

  1. Around the middle of a scale of evaluation.

    “An orange of average size.” “Intermediate capacity.”

    Synonyms: average, intermediate · Similar: moderate

  2. (meat) cooked until there is just a little pink meat inside.

    Similar: cooked

Phrases and compounds with "medium"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • mass medium (noun) (usually plural) transmissions that are disseminated widely to the public.
  • medium wave (noun) A radio wave with a wavelength between 100 and 1000 meters (a frequency between 300 kilohertz and 3000 kilohertz).
  • medium-size (adjective) Intermediate in size.
  • medium steel (noun) Steel with 0.15-0.3% carbon.
  • medium-large (adjective) Of anything that is large but not the largest.
  • medium-sized (adjective) Intermediate in size.
  • culture medium (noun) (bacteriology) a nutrient substance (solid or liquid) that is used to cultivate micro-organisms.
  • storage medium (noun) A medium for storing information.
  • contrast medium (noun) A substance that is opaque to x-rays; when administered it allows a radiologist to examine the organ or tissue it fills.
  • magnetic medium (noun) Any storage medium in which different patterns of magnetization are used to represent stored bits or bytes of information.
  • broadcast medium (noun) A medium that disseminates via telecommunications.
  • campanula medium (noun) European biennial widely cultivated for its blue or violet or white flowers.
  • medium frequency (noun) 300 to 3000 kilohertz.
  • dispersing medium (noun) (of colloids) a substance in which another is colloidally dispersed.
  • dispersion medium (noun) (of colloids) a substance in which another is colloidally dispersed.
  • medium of exchange (noun) Anything that is generally accepted as a standard of value and a measure of wealth in a particular country or region.
