Metal: meaning, synonyms and examples

Metal is used as a noun (2 senses), adjective (1 sense) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Any of several chemical elements that are usually shiny solids that conduct heat or electricity and can be formed into sheets etc.

Synonyms
metallic element, alloy, metallic
Word family
metallic

Metal as a noun

  1. Any of several chemical elements that are usually shiny solids that conduct heat or electricity and can be formed into sheets etc.

    Synonyms: metallic element · A kind of: chemical element · Kinds: heavy metal, base metal, noble metal, aluminum, americium, antimony, barium, berkelium

  2. A mixture containing two or more metallic elements or metallic and nonmetallic elements usually fused together or dissolving into each other when molten.

    “Brass is an alloy of zinc and copper.”

    Synonyms: alloy · A kind of: mixture · Kinds: heavy metal, oroide, amalgam, fusible metal, electrum, pewter, pinchbeck, pot metal

Metal as an adjective

  1. Containing or made of or resembling or characteristic of a metal.

    “A metallic compound.” “Metallic luster.” “The strange metallic note of the meadow lark, suggesting the clash of vibrant blades.”

    Synonyms: metallic · Similar: all-metal, aluminiferous, antimonial, argentiferous, auriferous, bimetal

Metal as a verb

  1. Cover with metal.

    A kind of: coat

Phrases and compounds with "metal"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • all-metal (adjective) Consisting completely of metal.
  • metal bar (noun) Metal that is cast in the shape of a block for convenient handling.
  • metal saw (noun) Saw used with one hand for cutting metal.
  • pot metal (noun) Cast iron used for making cooking wares.
  • base metal (noun) A metal that is common and not considered precious.
  • bell metal (noun) Bronze with 3 or 4 parts copper to 1 part tin; used in making bells.
  • grid metal (noun) A kind of hard lead that is used for grids in storage batteries.
  • metal drum (noun) A cylindrical metal container used for shipping or storage of liquids.
  • metal wood (noun) Golf wood with a metal head instead of the traditional wooden head.
  • road metal (noun) Broken rock used for repairing or making roads.
  • shot metal (noun) An alloy that is 98% lead and 2% arsenic; used in making small shot.
  • type metal (noun) An alloy of tin and lead and antimony used to make printing type.
  • heavy metal (noun) A metal of relatively high density (specific gravity greater than about 5) or of high relative atomic weight (especially one that is poisonous like mercury or lead).
  • metal glove (noun) A glove of armored leather; protects the hand.
  • metal money (noun) Coins collectively.
  • metal screw (noun) Screw made of metal.
