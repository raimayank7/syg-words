Mount: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mount is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Attach to a support.

In Hindi
चढ़ना; पर्वत
Synonyms
wax, climb, rise, hop on, mount up, get on, jump on, climb on, bestride, climb up
Word family
mounting, mounter

Mount as a verb

  1. Attach to a support.

    “They mounted the aerator on a floating.”

    A kind of: attach · Kinds: remount

  2. Go up or advance.

    “Sales were climbing after prices were lowered.”

    Synonyms: wax, climb, rise · A kind of: increase · Kinds: jump, advance

  3. Fix onto a backing, setting, or support.

    “Mount slides for macroscopic analysis.”

    A kind of: fix

  4. Put up or launch.

    “Mount a campaign against pornography.”

    A kind of: initiate

  5. Get up on the back of.

    “Mount a horse.”

    Synonyms: hop on, mount up, get on, jump on, climb on, bestride · A kind of: move · Kinds: remount

  6. Go upward with gradual or continuous progress.

    “Did you ever climb up the hill behind your house?”

    Synonyms: climb, climb up, go up · A kind of: rise · Kinds: scale, escalade, ramp, mountaineer, ride

  7. Prepare and supply with the necessary equipment for execution or performance.

    “Mount a theater production.” “Mount an attack.” “Mount a play.”

    Synonyms: put on · A kind of: organize · Kinds: rerun

Mount as a noun

  1. A lightweight horse kept for riding only.

    Synonyms: saddle horse, riding horse · A kind of: horse · Kinds: palfrey, warhorse, prancer, hack, cow pony, quarter horse, buckskin, crowbait

  2. The act of climbing something.

    “It was a difficult climb to the top.”

    Synonyms: climb · A kind of: rise · Kinds: scaling, clamber, mountain climbing, rock climbing

  3. A land mass that projects well above its surroundings; higher than a hill.

    Synonyms: mountain · A kind of: natural elevation · Kinds: alp, ben, seamount, volcano

  4. A mounting consisting of a piece of metal (as in a ring or other jewelry) that holds a gem in place.

    “The diamond was in a plain gold mount.”

    Synonyms: setting · A kind of: mounting · Kinds: pave

  5. Something forming a back that is added for strengthening.

    Synonyms: backing · A kind of: layer, strengthener

Phrases and compounds with "mount"

  • mount adams (noun) A mountain peak in southwestern Washington in the Cascade Range (12,307 feet high).
  • mount asama (noun) A volcano in central Honshu near Nagano; one of the largest volcanoes in Japan (8,340 feet).
  • mount athos (noun) An autonomous area in northeastern Greece that is the site of several Greek Orthodox monasteries founded in the tenth century.
  • mount garmo (noun) The highest mountain peak in the Pamir Mountains; near the Chinese border in northeastern Tajikistan (24,590 feet high).
  • mount logan (noun) A mountain peak in the St. Elias Range in the southwestern Yukon Territory in Canada (19,850 feet high).
  • mount sinai (noun) A mountain peak in the southern Sinai Peninsula (7,500 feet high); it is believed to be the peak on which Moses received the Ten Commandments.
  • mount ararat (noun) The mountain peak that Noah's ark landed on as the waters of the great flood receded.
  • mount carmel (noun) A mountain range in northwestern Israel near the Mediterranean coast.
  • mount elbert (noun) The highest peak in the Rocky Mountains in central Colorado (14,431 feet high).
  • mount ranier (noun) A mountain peak in central Washington; highest peak in the Cascade Range; (14,410 feet high).
  • mount shasta (noun) A volcanic mountain peak in the Cascade Range in northern California (14,162 feet high).
  • mount tacoma (noun) A mountain peak in central Washington; highest peak in the Cascade Range; (14,410 feet high).
  • mount vernon (noun) The former residence of George Washington in northeastern Virginia overlooking the Potomac river.
