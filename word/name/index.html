Name: meaning, synonyms and examples

Name is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A language unit by which a person or thing is known.

In Hindi
नाम
Synonyms
gens, figure, public figure, epithet, call, identify, nominate, make, appoint, constitute
Word family
namer, naming

Name as a noun

  1. A language unit by which a person or thing is known.

    “His name really is George Washington.” “Those are two names for the same thing.”

    A kind of: language unit · Kinds: agnomen, assumed name, eponym, eponym, filename, patronymic, matronymic, street name

  2. A person's reputation.

    “He wanted to protect his good name.”

    A kind of: repute

  3. Family based on male descent.

    “He had no sons and there was no one to carry on his name.”

    Synonyms: gens · A kind of: family

  4. A well-known or notable person.

    “They studied all the great names in the history of France.” “She is an important figure in modern music.”

    Synonyms: figure, public figure · A kind of: important person

  5. By the sanction or authority of.

    “Halt in the name of the law.”

    A kind of: sanction

  6. A defamatory or abusive word or phrase.

    Synonyms: epithet · A kind of: defamation · Kinds: smear word

Name as a verb

  1. Assign a specified (usually proper) proper name to.

    “They named their son David.” “The new school was named after the famous Civil Rights leader.”

    Synonyms: call · A kind of: label · Kinds: baptize, refer, style, dub, rename, entitle, term, tag

  2. Give the name or identifying characteristics of; refer to by name or some other identifying characteristic property.

    “Many senators were named in connection with the scandal.” “The almanac identifies the auspicious months.”

    Synonyms: identify · A kind of: denote · Kinds: enumerate, number, announce

  3. Charge with a function; charge to be.

    “She was named Head of the Committee.” “She was made president of the club.”

    Synonyms: nominate, make · A kind of: appoint · Kinds: rename

  4. Create and charge with a task or function.

    “Nominate a committee.”

    Synonyms: appoint, nominate, constitute · A kind of: establish · Kinds: pack, co-opt

  5. Mention and identify by name.

    “Name your accomplices!”

    A kind of: specify

  6. Make reference to.

    “His name was mentioned in connection with the invention.”

    Synonyms: mention, advert, bring up, cite, refer · A kind of: think of · Kinds: commend, speak of the devil, remember, quote, touch on, invoke, namedrop, raise

  7. Identify as in botany or biology, for example.

    Synonyms: identify, discover, key, key out, distinguish, describe

  8. Give or make a list of; name individually; give the names of.

    “List the states west of the Mississippi.”

    Synonyms: list · A kind of: enumerate

  9. Determine or distinguish the nature of a problem or an illness through a diagnostic analysis.

    Synonyms: diagnose · A kind of: analyze · Kinds: explore

Phrases and compounds with "name"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in name (adverb) By title or repute though not in fact.
  • name day (noun) The feast day of a saint whose name one bears.
  • name tag (noun) A tag showing the name of the person who wears it.
  • pen name (noun) An author's pseudonym.
  • pet name (noun) A name of endearment (especially one using a diminutive suffix).
  • file name (noun) (computer science) the name given to a computer file in order to distinguish it from other files; may contain an extension that indicates the type of file.
  • last name (noun) The name used to identify the members of a family (as distinguished from each member's given name).
  • name part (noun) The role of the character after whom the play is named.
  • part name (noun) A word that names a part of a larger whole.
  • brand name (noun) A name given to a product or service.
  • false name (noun) A name that has been assumed temporarily.
  • first name (noun) The name that precedes the surname.
  • given name (noun) The name that precedes the surname.
  • place name (noun) The name by which a geographical place is known.
  • stage name (noun) The pseudonym of an actor.
  • trade name (noun) A name given to a product or service.
