Near: meaning, synonyms and examples

Near is used as an adjective (6 senses), adverb (2 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Not far distant in time or space or degree or circumstances.

In Hindi
पास, नज़दीक
Synonyms
close, nigh, cheeseparing, penny-pinching, skinny, dear, good, approximate, about, almost
Opposites
far
Word family
nearness

Near as an adjective

  1. Not far distant in time or space or degree or circumstances.

    “Near neighbors.” “In the near future.” “They are near equals.”

    Synonyms: close, nigh · Opposite: far · Similar: adjacent, nearby, warm, hot

  2. Being on the left side.

    “The near or nigh horse is the one on the left.” “The animal's left side is its near or nigh side.”

    Synonyms: nigh · Similar: left

  3. Closely resembling the genuine article.

    “A dress of near satin.”

    Similar: artificial

  4. Giving or spending with reluctance.

    “Our cheeseparing administration.” “Very close (or near) with his money.” “A penny-pinching miserly old man.”

    Synonyms: cheeseparing, close, penny-pinching, skinny · Similar: stingy

  5. With or in a close or intimate relationship.

    “A good friend.” “My sisters and brothers are near and dear.”

    Synonyms: dear, good · Similar: close

  6. Very close in resemblance.

    “Sketched in an approximate likeness.” “A near likeness.”

    Synonyms: approximate · Similar: close

Near as an adverb

  1. Near in time or place or relationship.

    “As the wedding day drew near.” “Stood near the door.” “Don't shoot until they come near.”

    Synonyms: nigh, close

  2. (of actions or states) slightly short of or not quite accomplished; all but.

    “The job is (just) about done.” “The baby was almost asleep when the alarm sounded.” “We're almost finished.”

    Synonyms: about, almost, most, nearly, nigh, virtually, well-nigh

Near as a verb

  1. Move towards.

    “We were approaching our destination.” “They are drawing near.” “The enemy army came nearer and nearer.”

    Synonyms: approach, come on, go up, draw near, draw close, come near · A kind of: come · Kinds: drive up, bear down on, edge in, close, push

Phrases and compounds with "near"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • come near (verb) Almost do or experience something.
  • draw near (verb) Move towards.
  • near gale (noun) Wind moving 32-38 knots; 7 on the Beaufort scale.
  • near miss (noun) An accidental collision that is narrowly avoided.
  • near thing (noun) Something that barely avoids failure or disaster.
  • near-blind (adjective) Having greatly reduced vision.
  • near vision (noun) Vision for objects 2 feet or closer to the viewer.
  • far and near (adverb) Over great areas or distances; everywhere.
  • near-death experience (noun) The experience of being close to death but surviving.
