Nucleus: meaning, synonyms and examples

Nucleus is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A part of the cell containing DNA and RNA and responsible for growth and reproduction.

In Hindi
नाभिक, केंद्रक
Synonyms
cell nucleus, karyon, core, core group, lens nucleus
Word family
nucleate

Nucleus as a noun

  1. A part of the cell containing DNA and RNA and responsible for growth and reproduction.

    Synonyms: cell nucleus, karyon · A kind of: organelle · Kinds: pronucleus, subthalamic nucleus

  2. The positively charged dense center of an atom.

    A kind of: center

  3. A small group of indispensable persons or things.

    “Five periodicals make up the core of their publishing program.”

    Synonyms: core, core group · A kind of: set · Kinds: cadre

  4. (astronomy) the center of the head of a comet; consists of small solid particles of ice and frozen gas that vaporizes on approaching the sun to form the coma and tail.

    A kind of: center

  5. Any histologically identifiable mass of neural cell bodies in the brain or spinal cord.

    A kind of: neural structure · Kinds: dentate nucleus

  6. The central structure of the lens that is surrounded by the cortex.

    Synonyms: lens nucleus · A kind of: structure

Phrases and compounds with "nucleus"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • cell nucleus (noun) A part of the cell containing DNA and RNA and responsible for growth and reproduction.
  • lens nucleus (noun) The central structure of the lens that is surrounded by the cortex.
  • nucleus niger (noun) A layer of deeply pigmented grey matter in the midbrain; associated with the striate body; is involved in metabolic disturbances associated with Parkinson's disease and with Huntington's disease.
  • benzene nucleus (noun) A closed chain of 6 carbon atoms with hydrogen atoms attached.
  • caudate nucleus (noun) A tail-shaped basal ganglion located in a lateral ventricle of the brain.
  • dentate nucleus (noun) A large laminar nucleus of grey matter within the white matter of each cerebral hemisphere.
  • lentiform nucleus (noun) A basal ganglion shaped like a lens and including the outer reddish putamen and the inner pale yellow pallidum.
  • amygdaloid nucleus (noun) An almond-shaped neural structure in the anterior part of the temporal lobe of the cerebrum; intimately connected with the hypothalamus and the hippocampus and the cingulate gyrus; as part of the limbic system it plays an important role in motivation and emotional behavior.
  • lenticular nucleus (noun) A basal ganglion shaped like a lens and including the outer reddish putamen and the inner pale yellow pallidum.
  • subthalamic nucleus (noun) An oval mass of grey matter located in the caudal part of the subthalamus; associated with the striate body.
