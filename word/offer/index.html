Offer: meaning, synonyms and examples

Offer is used as a verb (13 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make available or accessible, provide or furnish.

In Hindi
प्रस्ताव; पेश करना
Synonyms
proffer, volunteer, extend, bid, tender, offer up, put up, provide, propose, declare oneself
Word family
offerer, offeror, offering

Offer as a verb

  1. Make available or accessible, provide or furnish.

    “The conference center offers a health spa.” “The hotel offers private meeting rooms.”

    A kind of: supply

  2. Present for acceptance or rejection.

    “She offered us all a cold drink.”

    Synonyms: proffer · A kind of: give · Kinds: give, tender

  3. Agree freely.

    “She volunteered to drive the old lady home.” “I offered to help with the dishes but the hostess would not hear of it.”

    Synonyms: volunteer · A kind of: act

  4. Put forward for consideration.

    “He offered his opinion.”

    A kind of: project

  5. Offer verbally.

    “Extend my greetings.” “He offered his sympathy.”

    Synonyms: extend · A kind of: give

  6. Make available for sale.

    “The stores are offering specials on sweaters this week.”

    A kind of: market · Kinds: put up

  7. Propose a payment.

    “The Swiss dealer offered $2 million for the painting.”

    Synonyms: bid, tender · Kinds: by-bid, subscribe, overbid, underbid, outbid, underbid

  8. Produce or introduce on the stage.

    “The Shakespeare Company is offering `King Lear' this month.”

    A kind of: produce

  9. Present as an act of worship.

    “Offer prayers to the gods.”

    Synonyms: offer up · A kind of: worship · Kinds: sacrifice

  10. Mount or put up.

    “Put up a good fight.” “Offer resistance.”

    Synonyms: put up, provide · A kind of: engage

  11. Make available; provide.

    “Extend a loan.” “The bank offers a good deal on new mortgages.”

    Synonyms: extend · A kind of: supply

  12. Ask (someone) to marry you.

    “He popped the question on Sunday night.” “She proposed marriage to the man she had known for only two months.” “The old bachelor finally declared himself to the young woman.”

    Synonyms: propose, declare oneself, pop the question · A kind of: request

  13. Threaten to do something.

    “I offered to leave the committee if they did not accept my proposal.”

    A kind of: threaten

Offer as a noun

  1. The verbal act of offering.

    “A generous offer of assistance.”

    Synonyms: offering · A kind of: speech act

  2. Something offered (as a proposal or bid).

    “Noteworthy new offerings for investors included several index funds.”

    Synonyms: offering · A kind of: message · Kinds: contract offer, marriage proposal, counteroffer, bid, prospectus, tender offer, reward, rights offering

  3. A usually brief attempt.

    “He took a crack at it.” “I gave it a whirl.”

    Synonyms: crack, fling, go, pass, whirl · A kind of: attempt

Phrases and compounds with "offer"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • offer up (verb) Present as an act of worship.
  • offer price (noun) (stock market) the price at which a broker is willing to sell a certain security.
  • tender offer (noun) An offer to buy shares in a corporation (usually above the market price) for cash or securities or both.
  • contract offer (noun) Offer by an employer to contract to pay an employee at a given rate.
  • marriage offer (noun) An offer of marriage.
