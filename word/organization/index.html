Organization: meaning, synonyms and examples

Organization is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: A group of people who work together.

In Hindi
संगठन
Synonyms
organisation, arrangement, system, administration, governance, governing body, establishment, brass, constitution, formation
Word family
organize

Organization as a noun

  1. A group of people who work together.

    Synonyms: organisation · A kind of: social group · Kinds: adhocracy, affiliate, bureaucracy, nongovernmental organization, fiefdom, line of defense, line organization, association

  2. An organized structure for arranging or classifying.

    “He changed the arrangement of the topics.” “The facts were familiar but it was in the organization of them that he was original.” “He tried to understand their system of classification.”

    Synonyms: arrangement, organisation, system · A kind of: structure · Kinds: classification system, contrivance, coordinate system, data structure, design, distribution, genetic map, kinship system

  3. The persons (or committees or departments etc.) who make up a body for the purpose of administering something.

    “He claims that the present administration is corrupt.” “The governance of an association is responsible to its members.” “He quickly became recognized as a member of the establishment.”

    Synonyms: administration, governance, governing body, establishment, brass, organisation · A kind of: body · Kinds: top brass, executive, judiciary, county council, government officials, management

  4. The act of organizing a business or an activity related to a business.

    “He was brought in to supervise the organization of a new department.”

    Synonyms: organisation · A kind of: administration · Kinds: nonprofit organization, rationalization, reorganization, self-organization, syndication

  5. An ordered manner; orderliness by virtue of being methodical and well organized.

    “His compulsive organization was not an endearing quality.” “We can't do it unless we establish some system around here.”

    Synonyms: organisation, system · A kind of: orderliness

  6. The activity or result of distributing or disposing persons or things properly or methodically.

    “His organization of the work force was very efficient.”

    Synonyms: organisation · A kind of: activity · Kinds: randomization, systematization, order, listing, territorialization

  7. The act of forming or establishing something.

    “The constitution of a PTA group last year.” “It was the establishment of his reputation.” “He still remembers the organization of the club.”

    Synonyms: constitution, establishment, formation, organisation · A kind of: beginning · Kinds: unionization, collectivization, communization, federation, colonization

Phrases and compounds with "organization"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • organization man (noun) An employee who sacrifices his own individuality for the good of an organization.
  • line organization (noun) The organizational structure of activities contributing directly to the organization's output.
  • news organization (noun) An agency to collects news reports for newspapers and distributes it electronically.
  • self-organization (noun) Organizing yourself (especially organizing your own labor union).
  • organization chart (noun) A chart showing the lines of responsibility between departments of a large organization.
  • world organization (noun) An international alliance involving many different countries.
  • global organization (noun) An international alliance involving many different countries.
  • social organization (noun) The people in a society considered as a system organized by a characteristic pattern of relationships.
  • musical organization (noun) An organization of musicians who perform together.
  • organization expense (noun) The cost (over a period of five years) of organizing a new corporation or partnership.
  • business organization (noun) A commercial or industrial enterprise and the people who constitute it.
  • financial organization (noun) An institution (public or private) that collects funds (from the public or other institutions) and invests them in financial assets.
  • nonprofit organization (noun) An organization chartered for other than profit-making activities.
  • world trade organization (noun) An international organization based in Geneva that monitors and enforces rules governing global trade.
  • paramilitary organization (noun) A group of civilians organized in a military fashion (especially to operate in place of or to assist regular army troops).
  • professional organization (noun) An organization of and for professional people.
