Paper: meaning, synonyms and examples

Paper is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A material made of cellulose pulp derived mainly from wood or rags or certain grasses.

In Hindi
काग़ज़; अख़बार
Synonyms
composition, report, theme, newspaper, newspaper publisher, wallpaper
Word family
papery, paperer, papering

Paper as a noun

  1. A material made of cellulose pulp derived mainly from wood or rags or certain grasses.

    A kind of: material · Kinds: card, confetti, sheet, art paper, blotting paper, blueprint paper, carbon paper, cardboard

  2. An essay (especially one written as an assignment).

    “He got an A on his composition.”

    Synonyms: composition, report, theme · A kind of: essay · Kinds: term paper

  3. A daily or weekly publication on folded sheets; contains news and articles and advertisements.

    “He read his newspaper at breakfast.”

    Synonyms: newspaper · A kind of: press · Kinds: daily, gazette, school newspaper, tabloid

  4. A medium for written communication.

    “The notion of an office running without paper is absurd.”

    A kind of: medium

  5. A scholarly article describing the results of observations or stating hypotheses.

    “He has written many scientific papers.”

    A kind of: article

  6. A business firm that publishes newspapers.

    “Murdoch owns many newspapers.”

    Synonyms: newspaper, newspaper publisher · A kind of: publisher

  7. The physical object that is the product of a newspaper publisher.

    “When it began to rain he covered his head with a newspaper.”

    Synonyms: newspaper · A kind of: product

Paper as a verb

  1. Cover with paper.

    “Paper the box.”

    A kind of: cover

  2. Cover with wallpaper.

    Synonyms: wallpaper · A kind of: cover

Phrases and compounds with "paper"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • on paper (adverb) As written or printed.
  • art paper (noun) A high-quality paper (usually having a filler of china clay).
  • paper bag (noun) A bag made of paper or plastic for holding customer's purchases.
  • paper cup (noun) A disposable cup made of paper; for holding drinks.
  • rag paper (noun) Paper made partly or wholly from rags.
  • tar paper (noun) A heavy paper impregnated with tar and used as part of a roof for waterproofing.
  • wax paper (noun) Paper that has been waterproofed by treatment with wax or paraffin.
  • bond paper (noun) A superior quality of strong durable white writing paper; originally made for printing documents.
  • exam paper (noun) A written examination.
  • laid paper (noun) Writing paper having a watermark of fine lines running across the grain.
  • paper clip (noun) A wire or plastic clip for holding sheets of paper together.
  • paper doll (noun) A piece of paper cut or folded into the shape of a human being.
  • paper feed (noun) A device for inserting sheets of paper into a printer or typewriter.
  • paper gold (noun) Reserve assets in the International Monetary Fund; designed to supplement reserves of gold and convertible currencies used to maintain stability in the foreign exchange market.
  • paper loss (noun) An unrealized loss on an investment calculated by subtracting the current market price from the investor's cost.
  • paper mill (noun) A mill where paper is manufactured.
