Part: meaning, synonyms and examples

Part is used as a noun (12 senses), adverb (1 sense) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Something determined in relation to something that includes it.

In Hindi
हिस्सा, भाग
Synonyms
portion, component part, component, constituent, piece, region, function, office, role, character
Word family
partial, parting, partitive

Part as a noun

  1. Something determined in relation to something that includes it.

    “He wanted to feel a part of something bigger than himself.” “I read a portion of the manuscript.” “The smaller component is hard to reach.”

    Synonyms: portion, component part, component, constituent · A kind of: relation · Kinds: substance, language unit, item, butt, basis, detail, unit, member

  2. Something less than the whole of a human artifact.

    “The rear part of the house.” “Glue the two parts together.”

    Synonyms: portion · A kind of: object · Kinds: appendage, bit, bottleneck, bulb, butt, component, cutout, foible

  3. A portion of a natural object.

    “They analyzed the river into three parts.” “He needed a piece of granite.”

    Synonyms: piece · A kind of: thing · Kinds: body part, corpus, acicula, base, corner, craton, cutting, fragment

  4. That which concerns a person with regard to a particular role or situation.

    “It requires vigilance on our part.” “They resisted every effort on his part.”

    A kind of: concern

  5. The extended spatial location of something.

    “The farming regions of France.” “Religions in all parts of the world.” “Regions of outer space.”

    Synonyms: region · A kind of: location · Kinds: atmosphere, biosphere, depth, interplanetary space, interstellar space, heliosphere, intergalactic space, deep space

  6. The actions and activities assigned to or required or expected of a person or group.

    “The function of a teacher.” “The government must do its part.” “Play its role.”

    Synonyms: function, office, role · A kind of: duty · Kinds: capacity, hat, portfolio, stead, second fiddle

  7. An actor's portrayal of someone in a play.

    “She played the part of Desdemona.”

    Synonyms: character, role, theatrical role, persona · A kind of: portrayal · Kinds: bit part, heavy, hero, ingenue, title role, villain

  8. Assets belonging to or due to or contributed by an individual person or group.

    “He wanted his share in cash.”

    Synonyms: share, portion, percentage · A kind of: assets · Kinds: tranche, dispensation, dole, way, ration, allowance, slice, split

  9. One of the portions into which something is regarded as divided and which together constitute a whole.

    “The written part of the exam.” “The finance section of the company.” “The BBC's engineering division.”

    Synonyms: section, division · A kind of: concept · Kinds: frame, beginning, middle, end, high point, component, chukker, inning

  10. A line of scalp that can be seen when sections of hair are combed in opposite directions.

    “His part was right in the middle.”

    Synonyms: parting · A kind of: line

  11. The melody carried by a particular voice or instrument in polyphonic music.

    “He tried to sing the tenor part.”

    Synonyms: voice · A kind of: tune · Kinds: primo, secondo, voice part, accompaniment, bass

  12. The part played by a person in bringing about a result.

    “I am proud of my contribution in advancing the project.” “They all did their share of the work.”

    Synonyms: contribution, share · A kind of: attempt · Kinds: end

Part as an adverb

  1. In part; in some degree; not wholly.

    “I felt partly to blame.” “He was partially paralyzed.”

    Synonyms: partially, partly

Part as a verb

  1. Go one's own way; move apart.

    “The friends separated after the party.”

    Synonyms: separate, split · A kind of: move · Kinds: disperse, break up, diffract

  2. Discontinue an association or relation; go different ways.

    “The business partners broke over a tax question.” “The couple separated after 25 years of marriage.” “My friend and I split up.”

    Synonyms: separate, split up, split, break, break up · Kinds: give the axe, disunify, disassociate, break with, divorce, secede, break

  3. Leave.

    “The family took off for Florida.”

    Synonyms: depart, start, start out, set forth, set off, set out, take off · A kind of: leave · Kinds: take off, roar off, blaze, sally forth

  4. Come apart.

    “The two pieces that we had glued separated.”

    Synonyms: separate, divide · A kind of: change · Kinds: subdivide, polarize, calve, chip, disjoin, detach, segregate, segment

  5. Force, take, or pull apart.

    “He separated the fighting children.” “Moses parted the Red Sea.”

    Synonyms: separate, disunite, divide · A kind of: move · Kinds: compartmentalize, polarize, sequester, disjoin, disjoint, disconnect, cut, tear

Phrases and compounds with "part"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bit part (noun) A small role.
  • car part (noun) A component of an automobile.
  • two-part (adjective) Involving two parts or elements.
  • auto part (noun) A component of an automobile.
  • bass part (noun) The lowest part in polyphonic music.
  • body part (noun) Any part of an organism such as an organ or extremity.
  • good part (noun) A place of especial strength.
  • name part (noun) The role of the character after whom the play is named.
  • part name (noun) A word that names a part of a larger whole.
  • part with (verb) Give up what is not strictly needed.
  • part-time (adjective) Involving less than the standard or customary time for an activity.
  • take part (verb) Share in something.
  • weak part (noun) A place of especial vulnerability.
  • after part (noun) The rear part of a ship.
  • fifth part (noun) One part in five equal parts.
  • part music (noun) Vocal music for several voices in independent parts (usually performed without accompaniment).
