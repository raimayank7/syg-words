Particular: meaning, synonyms and examples

Particular is used as an adjective (6 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Unique or specific to a person or thing or category.

In Hindi
ख़ास, विशेष
Synonyms
peculiar, special, especial, exceptional, finical, finicky, fussy, picky, specific, detail
Opposites
general, universal
Word family
particularity

Particular as an adjective

  1. Unique or specific to a person or thing or category.

    “The particular demands of the job.” “Has a particular preference for Chinese art.” “A peculiar bond of sympathy between them.”

    Synonyms: peculiar, special · Similar: specific

  2. Separate and distinct from others of the same group or category.

    “Interested in one particular artist.” “A man who wishes to make a particular woman fall in love with him.”

    Similar: specific

  3. Surpassing what is common or usual or expected.

    “He paid especial attention to her.” “Exceptional kindness.” “A matter of particular and unusual importance.”

    Synonyms: especial, exceptional, special · Similar: uncommon

  4. First and most important.

    “His special interest is music.” “She gets special (or particular) satisfaction from her volunteer work.”

    Synonyms: special · Similar: primary

  5. Exacting especially about details.

    “A finicky eater.” “Fussy about clothes.” “Very particular about how her food was prepared.”

    Synonyms: finical, finicky, fussy, picky · Similar: fastidious

  6. Providing specific details or circumstances.

    “A particular description of the room.”

    Similar: careful

Particular as a noun

  1. A fact about some part (as opposed to general).

    “He always reasons from the particular to the general.”

    Synonyms: specific · Opposite: general · A kind of: fact

  2. A small part that can be considered separately from the whole.

    “It was perfect in all details.”

    Synonyms: detail, item · A kind of: part · Kinds: highlight

  3. (logic) a proposition that asserts something about some (but not all) members of a class.

    Synonyms: particular proposition · Opposite: universal · A kind of: proposition

Phrases and compounds with "particular"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in particular (adverb) Specifically or especially distinguished from others.
  • particular date (noun) A particular but unspecified point in time.
  • particular baptist (noun) Group of Baptist congregations believing the teachings of the French theologian John Calvin who believed in strict predetermination.
  • particular proposition (noun) (logic) a proposition that asserts something about some (but not all) members of a class.
