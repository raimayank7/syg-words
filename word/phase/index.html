Phase: meaning, synonyms and examples

Phase is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any distinct time period in a sequence of events.

In Hindi
चरण
Synonyms
stage, form, phase angle

Phase as a noun

  1. Any distinct time period in a sequence of events.

    “We are in a transitional stage in which many former ideas must be revised or rejected.”

    Synonyms: stage · A kind of: time period · Kinds: diakinesis, diplotene, leptotene, pachytene, phase of cell division, zygotene, chapter, incubation

  2. (physical chemistry) a distinct state of matter in a system; matter that is identical in chemical composition and physical state and separated from other material by the phase boundary.

    “The reaction occurs in the liquid phase of the system.”

    Synonyms: form · A kind of: state of matter · Kinds: dispersed phase, dispersing phase

  3. A particular point in the time of a cycle; measured from some arbitrary zero and expressed as an angle.

    Synonyms: phase angle · A kind of: point

  4. (astronomy) the particular appearance of a body's state of illumination (especially one of the recurring shapes of the part of Earth's moon that is illuminated by the sun).

    “The full phase of the moon.”

    A kind of: appearance · Kinds: phase of the moon

Phase as a verb

  1. Arrange in phases or stages.

    “Phase a withdrawal.”

    A kind of: stage

  2. Adjust so as to be in a synchronized condition.

    “He phased the intake with the output of the machine.”

    A kind of: synchronize

Phrases and compounds with "phase"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • phase ii (noun) A clinical trial on more persons than in phase I; intended to evaluate the efficacy of a treatment for the condition it is intended to treat; possible side effects are monitored.
  • phase in (verb) Introduce gradually.
  • phase out (verb) Terminate gradually.
  • phase-out (noun) The act or instance of a planned discontinuation.
  • phase angle (noun) A particular point in the time of a cycle; measured from some arbitrary zero and expressed as an angle.
  • phase space (noun) (physics) an ideal space in which the coordinate dimensions represent the variables that are required to describe a system or substance.
  • luteal phase (noun) The second half of the menstrual cycle after ovulation; the corpus luteum secretes progesterone which prepares the endometrium for the implantation of an embryo; if fertilization does not occur then menstrual flow begins.
  • phase change (noun) A change from one state (solid or liquid or gas) to another without a change in chemical composition.
  • fertile phase (noun) The time in the menstrual cycle when fertilization is most likely to be possible (7 days before to 7 days after ovulation).
  • dispersed phase (noun) (of colloids) a substance in the colloidal state.
  • menstrual phase (noun) The phase of the menstrual cycle during which the lining of the uterus is shed (the first day of menstrual flow is considered day 1 of the menstrual cycle).
  • secretory phase (noun) The second half of the menstrual cycle after ovulation; the corpus luteum secretes progesterone which prepares the endometrium for the implantation of an embryo; if fertilization does not occur then menstrual flow begins.
  • dispersing phase (noun) (of colloids) a substance in which another is colloidally dispersed.
  • phase modulation (noun) Modulation of the phase of the carrier wave.
  • phase transition (noun) A change from one state (solid or liquid or gas) to another without a change in chemical composition.
  • phase of the moon (noun) A time when the Moon presents a particular recurring appearance.
