Physical: meaning, synonyms and examples

Physical is used as an adjective (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Involving the body as distinguished from the mind or spirit.

In Hindi
शारीरिक; भौतिक
Synonyms

Opposites

Word family
physicality, physicalness, physics

Physical as an adjective

  1. Involving the body as distinguished from the mind or spirit.

    “Physical exercise.” “Physical suffering.” “Was sloppy about everything but her physical appearance.”

    Opposite: mental · Similar: animal, bodily, material, personal, physiologic, somatogenic

  2. Relating to the sciences dealing with matter and energy; especially physics.

    “Physical sciences.” “Physical laws.”

  3. Having substance or material existence; perceptible to the senses.

    “A physical manifestation.” “Surrounded by tangible objects.”

    Similar: material

  4. According with material things or natural laws (other than those peculiar to living matter).

    “A reflex response to physical stimuli.”

    Similar: natural

  5. Characterized by energetic bodily activity.

    “A very physical dance performance.”

    Similar: energetic

  6. Impelled by physical force especially against resistance.

    “Forcible entry.” “A real cop would get physical.” “Strong-arm tactics.”

    Synonyms: forcible, strong-arm · Similar: forceful

  7. Concerned with material things.

    “Physical properties.” “The physical characteristics of the earth.” “The physical size of a computer.”

    Similar: material

Phrases and compounds with "physical"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • physical body (noun) Alternative names for the body of a human being.
  • physical value (noun) Cost of reproducing physical property minus various allowances (especially depreciation).
  • physical change (noun) A change from one state (solid or liquid or gas) to another without a change in chemical composition.
  • physical entity (noun) An entity that has physical existence.
  • physical object (noun) A tangible and visible entity; an entity that can cast a shadow.
  • physical ability (noun) The ability to perform some physical act; contrasting with mental ability.
  • physical contact (noun) The act of touching physically.
  • physical fitness (noun) Good physical condition; being in shape or in condition.
  • physical process (noun) A sustained phenomenon or one marked by gradual changes through a series of states.
  • physical science (noun) The physical properties, phenomena, and laws of something.
  • physical therapy (noun) Therapy that uses physical agents: exercise and massage and other modalities.
  • physical exercise (noun) The activity of exerting your muscles in various ways to keep fit.
  • physical exertion (noun) The activity of exerting your muscles in various ways to keep fit.
  • physical pendulum (noun) Pendulum consisting of an actual object allowed to rotate freely around a horizontal axis.
  • physical property (noun) Any property used to characterize matter and energy and their interactions.
  • physical topology (noun) The appearance of the network.
