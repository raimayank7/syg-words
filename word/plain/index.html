Plain: meaning, synonyms and examples

Plain is used as an adjective (7 senses), noun (2 senses), verb (1 sense) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Clearly revealed to the mind or the senses or judgment.

In Hindi
सादा; मैदान
Synonyms
apparent, evident, manifest, patent, unmistakable, unpatterned, sheer, unmingled, unmixed, unvarnished
Opposites
fancy, patterned
Word family
plainness

Plain as an adjective

  1. Clearly revealed to the mind or the senses or judgment.

    “The effects of the drought are apparent to anyone who sees the parched fields.” “Evident hostility.” “Manifest disapproval.”

    Synonyms: apparent, evident, manifest, patent, unmistakable · Similar: obvious

  2. Not elaborate or elaborated; simple.

    “Plain food.” “Stuck to the plain facts.” “A plain blue suit.”

    Opposite: fancy · Similar: austere, bare, chaste, dry, dry, featureless

  3. Lacking patterns especially in color.

    Synonyms: unpatterned · Opposite: patterned · Similar: solid-colored

  4. Not mixed with extraneous elements.

    “Plain water.” “Not an unmixed blessing.”

    Synonyms: sheer, unmingled, unmixed · Similar: pure

  5. Free from any effort to soften to disguise.

    “The plain and unvarnished truth.” “The unvarnished candor of old people and children.”

    Synonyms: unvarnished · Similar: direct

  6. Lacking embellishment or ornamentation.

    “A plain hair style.” “Unembellished white walls.” “Functional architecture featuring stark unornamented concrete.”

    Synonyms: bare, spare, unembellished, unornamented · Similar: unadorned

  7. Lacking in physical beauty or proportion.

    “A homely child.” “Several of the buildings were downright homely.” “A plain girl with a freckled face.”

    Synonyms: homely · Similar: unattractive

Plain as a noun

  1. Extensive tract of level open land.

    “They emerged from the woods onto a vast open plain.” “He longed for the fields of his youth.”

    Synonyms: field, champaign · A kind of: land · Kinds: flat, floodplain, llano, moor, peneplain, snowfield, steppe, tundra

  2. A basic knitting stitch.

    Synonyms: knit, knit stitch, plain stitch · A kind of: knitting stitch

Plain as a verb

  1. Express complaints, discontent, displeasure, or unhappiness.

    “My mother complains all day.” “She has a lot to kick about.”

    Synonyms: complain, kick, sound off, quetch, kvetch · Kinds: nag, backbite, whine, murmur, grouch, protest, repine, gripe

Plain as an adverb

  1. Unmistakably (`plain' is often used informally for `plainly').

    “The answer is obviously wrong.” “She was in bed and evidently in great pain.” “He was manifestly too important to leave off the guest list.”

    Synonyms: obviously, evidently, manifestly, patently, apparently, plainly

Phrases and compounds with "plain"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • salt plain (noun) A flat expanse of salt left by the evaporation of a body of salt water.
  • flood plain (noun) A low plain adjacent to a river that is formed chiefly of river sediment and is subject to flooding.
  • plain flour (noun) Flour that does not contain a raising agent.
  • plain weave (noun) A basic style of weave in which the weft and warp threads intertwine alternately to produce a checkerboard effect.
  • plain-woven (adjective) (of cloth) made in plain weave.
  • plain stitch (noun) A basic knitting stitch.
  • plain turkey (noun) Popular Australian game bird.
  • coastal plain (noun) A plain adjacent to a coast.
  • plain clothes (noun) Ordinary clothing as distinguished from uniforms, work clothes, clerical garb, etc.
  • plain sailing (noun) Easy unobstructed progress.
  • alluvial plain (noun) A flat resulting from repeated deposits of alluvial material by running water.
  • plain wanderer (noun) Small Australian bird related to the button quail; classified as wading bird but inhabits plains.
  • nullarbor plain (noun) A vast arid plain of southern Australia stretching inland from the Great Australian Bight; has sparse vegetation and no surface water and is almost uninhabited; the site of a major rocket research center.
  • serengeti plain (noun) A vast plain in Tanzania to the west of the Great Rift Valley known for its wildlife.
