Plant: meaning, synonyms and examples

Plant is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Buildings for carrying on industrial labor.

In Hindi
पौधा; कारख़ाना; रोपना
Synonyms
works, industrial plant, flora, plant life, set, implant, engraft, embed, imbed, establish
Word family
plantal, plantlet, planter, planting

Plant as a noun

  1. Buildings for carrying on industrial labor.

    “They built a large plant to manufacture automobiles.”

    Synonyms: works, industrial plant · A kind of: building complex · Kinds: bottling plant, factory, mint, packinghouse, recycling plant, refinery, saltworks, sewage disposal plant

  2. (botany) a living organism lacking the power of locomotion.

    Synonyms: flora, plant life · A kind of: organism · Kinds: phytoplankton, microflora, crop, endemic, holophyte, non-flowering plant, plantlet, wilding

  3. An actor situated in the audience whose acting is rehearsed but seems spontaneous to the audience.

    A kind of: actor

  4. Something planted secretly for discovery by another.

    “The police used a plant to trick the thieves.” “He claimed that the evidence against him was a plant.”

    A kind of: contrivance

Plant as a verb

  1. Put or set (seeds, seedlings, or plants) into the ground.

    “Let's plant flowers in the garden.”

    Synonyms: set · A kind of: put · Kinds: root, puddle, checkrow, bed, dibble, afforest, replant, tree

  2. Fix or set securely or deeply.

    “He planted a knee in the back of his opponent.” “The dentist implanted a tooth in the gum.”

    Synonyms: implant, engraft, embed, imbed · A kind of: insert · Kinds: pot, nest, bury

  3. Set up or lay the groundwork for.

    “Establish a new department.”

    Synonyms: establish, found, constitute, institute · A kind of: initiate · Kinds: fix, appoint

  4. Place into a river.

    “Plant fish.”

    A kind of: stock

  5. Place something or someone in a certain position in order to secretly observe or deceive.

    “Plant a spy in Moscow.” “Plant bugs in the dissident's apartment.”

    A kind of: put

  6. Put firmly in the mind.

    “Plant a thought in the students' minds.”

    Synonyms: implant · A kind of: communicate

Phrases and compounds with "plant"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air plant (noun) Plant that derives moisture and nutrients from the air and rain; usually grows on another plant but not parasitic on it.
  • bog plant (noun) A semiaquatic plant that grows in soft wet land; most are monocots: sedge, sphagnum, grasses, cattails, etc; possibly heath.
  • gas plant (noun) Eurasian perennial herb with white flowers that emit flammable vapor in hot weather.
  • gum plant (noun) Any of various western American plants of the genus Grindelia having resinous leaves and stems formerly used medicinally; often poisonous to livestock.
  • ice plant (noun) Old World annual widely naturalized in warm regions having white flowers and fleshy foliage covered with hairs that resemble ice.
  • pea plant (noun) A leguminous plant of the genus Pisum with small white flowers and long green pods containing edible green seeds.
  • pie plant (noun) Long cultivated hybrid of Rheum palmatum; stems often cooked in pies or as sauce or preserves.
  • plant bug (noun) Small bright-colored insect that feeds on plant juices.
  • pot plant (noun) A plant suitable for growing in a flowerpot (especially indoors).
  • sun plant (noun) Widely cultivated in many varieties for its fleshy moss-like foliage and profusion of brightly colored flowers.
  • wax plant (noun) Succulent climber of southern Asia with umbels of pink and white star-shaped flowers.
  • yam plant (noun) Any of a number of tropical vines of the genus Dioscorea many having edible tuberous roots.
  • bean plant (noun) Any of various leguminous plants grown for their edible seeds and pods.
  • beef plant (noun) South American plant having green to purple or red branches with green to purple ornamental foliage and spikes of insignificant woolly flowers with dry membranous bracts.
  • burn plant (noun) Very short-stemmed plant with thick leaves with soothing mucilaginous juice; leaves develop spiny margins with maturity; native to Mediterranean region; grown widely in tropics and as houseplants.
  • lead plant (noun) Shrub of sandy woodlands and stream banks of western United States having hoary pinnate flowers and dull-colored racemose flowers; thought to indicate the presence of lead ore.
