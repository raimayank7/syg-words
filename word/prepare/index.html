Prepare: meaning, synonyms and examples

Prepare is used as a verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make ready or suitable or equip in advance for a particular purpose or for some use, event, etc.

In Hindi
तैयार करना
Synonyms
fix, set up, ready, gear up, set, cook, make, organize, organise, devise
Word family
preparative, preparatory, preparation

Prepare as a verb

  1. Make ready or suitable or equip in advance for a particular purpose or for some use, event, etc.

    “Get the children ready for school!” “Prepare for war.” “I was fixing to leave town after I paid the hotel bill.”

    Synonyms: fix, set up, ready, gear up, set · A kind of: change · Kinds: socialize, provide, cram, precondition, mount, set up, winterize, summerize

  2. Prepare for eating by applying heat.

    “Cook me dinner, please.” “Can you make me an omelette?” “Fix breakfast for the guests, please.”

    Synonyms: cook, fix, ready, make · A kind of: create from raw material · Kinds: preserve, deglaze, scallop, put on, devil, precook, whip up, concoct

  3. To prepare verbally, either for written or spoken delivery.

    “Prepare a report.” “Prepare a speech.”

    A kind of: make · Kinds: rough in

  4. Arrange by systematic planning and united effort.

    “Machinate a plot.” “Organize a strike.” “Devise a plan to take over the director's office.”

    Synonyms: organize, organise, devise, get up, machinate · A kind of: initiate · Kinds: mount, lay, set up, sandwich, spatchcock, embattle

  5. Educate for a future role or function.

    “He is grooming his son to become his successor.” “The prince was prepared to become King one day.” “They trained him to be a warrior.”

    Synonyms: groom, train · A kind of: educate · Kinds: qualify

  6. Create by training and teaching.

    “The old master is training world-class violinists.” “We develop the leaders for the future.”

    Synonyms: train, develop, educate · A kind of: teach · Kinds: retrain, drill, housebreak, toilet-train

  7. Lead up to and soften by sounding the dissonant note in it as a consonant note in the preceding chord.

    “Prepare the discord in bar 139.”

    A kind of: sound

  8. Undergo training or instruction in preparation for a particular role, function, or profession.

    “She is training to be a teacher.” “He trained as a legal aid.”

    Synonyms: train · A kind of: learn · Kinds: apprentice, retrain, drill

Phrases and compounds with "prepare"
