Private: meaning, synonyms and examples

Private is used as an adjective (4 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Confined to particular persons or groups or providing privacy.

In Hindi
निजी
Synonyms
individual, secret, buck private, common soldier
Opposites
public
Word family
privateness

Private as an adjective

  1. Confined to particular persons or groups or providing privacy.

    “A private place.” “Private discussions.” “Private lessons.”

    Opposite: public · Similar: clannish, cloistered, close, closed-door, confidential, confidential

  2. Concerning things deeply private and personal.

    “Private correspondence.” “Private family matters.”

    Similar: personal

  3. Concerning one person exclusively.

    “We all have individual cars.” “Each room has a private bath.”

    Synonyms: individual · Similar: personal

  4. Not expressed.

    “Secret (or private) thoughts.”

    Synonyms: secret · Similar: inward

Private as a noun

  1. An enlisted man of the lowest rank in the Army or Marines.

    “Our prisoner was just a private and knew nothing of value.”

    Synonyms: buck private, common soldier · A kind of: enlisted man

Phrases and compounds with "private"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in private (adverb) Kept private or confined to those intimately concerned.
  • private eye (noun) Someone who can be employed as a detective to collect information.
  • buck private (noun) An enlisted man of the lowest rank in the Army or Marines.
  • private line (noun) A telephone line serving a single subscriber.
  • private road (noun) A road leading up to a private house.
  • private school (noun) A school established and controlled privately and supported by endowment and tuition.
  • private treaty (noun) A sale of property at a price agreed on by the seller and buyer without an intervening agency.
  • private citizen (noun) A citizen who does not hold any official or public position.
  • private nuisance (noun) A nuisance that interferes with your interest in and private use and enjoyment of your land.
  • private practice (noun) The practice of a profession independently and not as an employee.
  • private property (noun) Movable property (as distinguished from real estate).
  • private detective (noun) Someone who can be employed as a detective to collect information.
  • private enterprise (noun) An economy that relies chiefly on market forces to allocate goods and resources and to determine prices.
  • private foundation (noun) A charity that does not receive a major part of its support from the public.
  • private instructor (noun) A person who gives private instruction (as in singing, acting, etc.).
  • private-enterprise (adjective) Subscribing to capitalistic competition.
