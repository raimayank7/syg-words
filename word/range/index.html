Range: meaning, synonyms and examples

Range is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: An area in which something acts or operates or has power or control: "the range of a supersonic jet".

In Hindi
सीमा, दायरा; पर्वतमाला
Synonyms
scope, reach, orbit, compass, ambit, mountain range, range of mountains, chain, mountain chain, chain of mountains
Word family
rangy

Range as a noun

  1. An area in which something acts or operates or has power or control: "the range of a supersonic jet".

    “The range of a supersonic jet.” “A piano has a greater range than the human voice.” “The ambit of municipal legislation.”

    Synonyms: scope, reach, orbit, compass, ambit · A kind of: extent · Kinds: approximate range, confines, contrast, internationality, latitude, horizon, sweep, gamut

  2. The limits within which something can be effective.

    “Range of motion.” “He was beyond the reach of their fire.”

    Synonyms: reach · A kind of: limit · Kinds: earshot, view, rifle range

  3. A large tract of grassy open land on which livestock can graze.

    “They used to drive the cattle across the open range every spring.” “He dreamed of a home on the range.”

    A kind of: tract · Kinds: home range

  4. A series of hills or mountains.

    “The valley was between two ranges of hills.” “The plains lay just beyond the mountain range.”

    Synonyms: mountain range, range of mountains, chain, mountain chain, chain of mountains · A kind of: geological formation

  5. A place for shooting (firing or driving) projectiles of various kinds.

    “The army maintains a missile range in the desert.” “Any good golf club will have a range where you can practice.”

    A kind of: facility · Kinds: practice range, rifle range, test range

  6. A variety of different things or activities.

    “He answered a range of questions.” “He was impressed by the range and diversity of the collection.”

    A kind of: assortment · Kinds: band

  7. (mathematics) the set of values of the dependent variable for which a function is defined.

    “The image of f(x) = x^2 is the set of all non-negative real numbers if the domain of the function is the set of all real numbers.”

    Synonyms: image, range of a function · A kind of: set

  8. The limit of capability.

    “Within the compass of education.”

    Synonyms: compass, reach, grasp · A kind of: capability · Kinds: sight

  9. A kitchen appliance used for cooking food.

    “Dinner was already on the stove.”

    Synonyms: stove, kitchen stove, kitchen range, cooking stove · A kind of: kitchen appliance · Kinds: charcoal burner, cookstove, electric range, gas range, potbelly

Range as a verb

  1. Change or be different within limits.

    “Estimates for the losses in the earthquake range as high as $2 billion.” “Interest rates run from 5 to 10 percent.” “The instruments ranged from tuba to cymbals.”

    Synonyms: run · A kind of: be

  2. Move about aimlessly or without any destination, often in search of food or employment.

    “The gypsies roamed the woods.” “Roving vagabonds.” “The cattle roam across the prairie.”

    Synonyms: roll, wander, swan, stray, tramp, roam, cast, ramble · A kind of: travel · Kinds: maunder, gallivant

  3. Have a range; be capable of projecting over a certain distance, as of a gun.

    “This gun ranges over two miles.”

    Kinds: carry

  4. Range or extend over; occupy a certain area.

    “The plants straddle the entire state.”

    Synonyms: straddle · A kind of: constitute · Kinds: spread-eagle

  5. Lay out orderly or logically in a line or as if in a line.

    “Lay out the clothes.” “Lay out the arguments.”

    Synonyms: array, lay out, set out · A kind of: arrange · Kinds: compart

  6. Feed as in a meadow or pasture.

    “The herd was grazing.”

    Synonyms: crop, browse, graze, pasture · A kind of: feed

  7. Let eat.

    “Range the animals in the prairie.”

    A kind of: feed

  8. Assign a rank or rating to.

    “How would you rank these students?” “The restaurant is rated highly in the food guide.”

    Synonyms: rate, rank, order, grade, place · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: superordinate, shortlist, seed, reorder, subordinate, prioritize, sequence, downgrade

Phrases and compounds with "range"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • range in (verb) Direct onto a point or target, especially by automatic navigational aids.
  • gas range (noun) A range with gas rings and an oven for cooking with gas.
  • free-range (adjective) Of livestock and domestic poultry; permitted to graze or forage rather than being confined to a feedlot.
  • golf range (noun) A practice range for practicing golf shots.
  • home range (noun) The area in which an animal normally ranges.
  • long-range (adjective) Involving an extended span of time.
  • range hood (noun) Exhaust hood over a kitchen range.
  • range pole (noun) Surveying instrument consisting of a straight rod painted in bands of alternate red and white each one foot wide; used for sightings by surveyors.
  • test range (noun) A range for conducting tests.
  • coast range (noun) A string of mountain ranges along the Pacific coast of North America from southeastern Alaska to Lower California.
  • rifle range (noun) The distance that a rifle bullet will carry.
  • short-range (adjective) Relating to the near future.
  • teton range (noun) A mountain range in northwest Wyoming; contains the Grand Teton.
  • alaska range (noun) A mountain range in south central Alaska; contains Mount McKinley.
  • firing range (noun) A practice range for target practice.
  • mesabi range (noun) A range of hills in northeastern Minnesota where rich iron ore deposits were discovered in 1887.
