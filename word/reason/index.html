Reason: meaning, synonyms and examples

Reason is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A rational motive for a belief or action.

In Hindi
कारण; तर्क
Synonyms
ground, understanding, intellect, rationality, reasonableness, cause, grounds, reason out, conclude, argue
Word family
reasoner, reasoning

Reason as a noun

  1. A rational motive for a belief or action.

    “The reason that war was declared.” “The grounds for their declaration.”

    Synonyms: ground · A kind of: rational motive · Kinds: occasion, score, why

  2. An explanation of the cause of some phenomenon.

    “The reason a steady state was never reached was that the back pressure built up too slowly.”

    A kind of: explanation

  3. The capacity for rational thought or inference or discrimination.

    “We are told that man is endowed with reason and capable of distinguishing good from evil.”

    Synonyms: understanding, intellect · A kind of: faculty

  4. The state of having good sense and sound judgment.

    “His rationality may have been impaired.” “He had to rely less on reason than on rousing their emotions.”

    Synonyms: rationality, reasonableness · A kind of: sanity

  5. A justification for something existing or happening.

    “He had no cause to complain.” “They had good reason to rejoice.”

    Synonyms: cause, grounds · A kind of: justification

  6. A fact that logically justifies some premise or conclusion.

    “There is reason to believe he is lying.”

    A kind of: fact

Reason as a verb

  1. Decide by reasoning; draw or come to a conclusion.

    “We reasoned that it was cheaper to rent than to buy a house.”

    Synonyms: reason out, conclude · A kind of: think · Kinds: induce, deduce, syllogize, find, deduce, gather, generalize

  2. Present reasons and arguments.

    Synonyms: argue · A kind of: present · Kinds: re-argue, expostulate, defend

  3. Think logically.

    “The children must learn to reason.”

    A kind of: think · Kinds: rationalize away, theorize, theorize, ratiocinate, calculate, categorize, speculate

Phrases and compounds with "reason"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • reason out (verb) Decide by reasoning; draw or come to a conclusion.
  • age of reason (noun) A movement in Europe from about 1650 until 1800 that advocated the use of reason and individualism instead of tradition and established doctrine.
