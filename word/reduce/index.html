Reduce: meaning, synonyms and examples

Reduce is used as a verb (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: Cut down on; make a reduction in.

In Hindi
कम करना, घटाना
Synonyms
cut down, cut back, trim, trim down, trim back, cut, bring down, come down, boil down, shrink
Opposites
blow up, gain
Word family
reductive, reduction, reducible, reducer, reductant, reducing

Reduce as a verb

  1. Cut down on; make a reduction in.

    “Reduce your daily fat intake.” “The employer wants to cut back health benefits.”

    Synonyms: cut down, cut back, trim, trim down, trim back, cut, bring down · A kind of: decrease · Kinds: shorten, spill, quench, retrench, slash, thin out, thin, take away

  2. Make less complex.

    “Reduce a problem to a single question.”

    A kind of: simplify · Kinds: abbreviate

  3. Bring to humbler or weaker state or condition.

    A kind of: demote

  4. Simplify the form of a mathematical equation of expression by substituting one term for another.

    A kind of: substitute

  5. Lower in grade or rank or force somebody into an undignified situation.

    “She reduced her niece to a servant.”

    A kind of: take down

  6. Be the essential element.

    “The proposal boils down to a compromise.”

    Synonyms: come down, boil down · A kind of: become

  7. Reduce in size; reduce physically.

    “Hot water will shrink the sweater.” “Can you shrink this image?”

    Synonyms: shrink · A kind of: decrease · Kinds: reduce, reef, miniaturize, depopulate, downsize, contract

  8. Lessen and make more modest.

    “Reduce one's standard of living.”

    A kind of: impoverish

  9. Make smaller.

    “Reduce an image.”

    Synonyms: scale down · Opposite: blow up · A kind of: shrink

  10. To remove oxygen from a compound, or cause to react with hydrogen or form a hydride, or to undergo an increase in the number of electrons.

    Synonyms: deoxidize, deoxidise · A kind of: change · Kinds: benficiate, pole

  11. Narrow or limit.

    “Reduce the influx of foreigners.”

    Synonyms: tighten · A kind of: restrict

  12. Put down by force or intimidation.

    “The government quashes any attempt of an uprising.” “China keeps down her dissidents very efficiently.” “The rich landowners subjugated the peasants working the land.”

    Synonyms: repress, quash, keep down, subdue, subjugate · A kind of: oppress

  13. Undergo meiosis.

    “The cells reduce.”

    A kind of: separate

  14. Reposition (a broken bone after surgery) back to its normal site.

    A kind of: reposition
