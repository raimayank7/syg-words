Report: meaning, synonyms and examples

Report is used as a verb (6 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: To give an account or representation of in words.

In Hindi
रिपोर्ट; सूचना देना
Synonyms
describe, account, cover, study, written report, news report, story, write up, report card, composition
Word family
reporter, reportage, reporting

Report as a verb

  1. To give an account or representation of in words.

    “Discreet Italian police described it in a manner typically continental.”

    Synonyms: describe, account · A kind of: inform

  2. Announce as the result of an investigation or experience or finding.

    “Dozens of incidents of wife beatings are reported daily in this city.” “The team reported significant advances in their research.”

    A kind of: announce

  3. Announce one's presence.

    “I report to work every day at 9 o'clock.”

    A kind of: announce · Kinds: check in, check out, clock out

  4. Make known to the authorities.

    “One student reported the other to the principal.”

    A kind of: inform

  5. Be responsible for reporting the details of, as in journalism.

    “Snow reported on China in the 1950's.” “The cub reporter covered New York City.”

    Synonyms: cover · A kind of: inform

  6. Complain about; make a charge against.

    “I reported her to the supervisor.”

    A kind of: complain

Report as a noun

  1. A written document describing the findings of some individual or group.

    “This accords with the recent study by Hill and Dale.”

    Synonyms: study, written report · A kind of: document · Kinds: assay, case study, white book, blue book, green paper, progress report, position paper, medical report

  2. The act of informing by verbal report.

    “He heard reports that they were causing trouble.” “By all accounts they were a happy couple.”

    Synonyms: account · A kind of: informing · Kinds: summarization, indirect discourse, direct discourse, megillah, debriefing, anecdote, narration, gossip

  3. A short account of the news.

    “The report of his speech.” “The story was on the 11 o'clock news.” “The account of his speech that was given on the evening news made the governor furious.”

    Synonyms: news report, story, account, write up · A kind of: news · Kinds: newsletter, bulletin, dispatch, urban legend, exclusive

  4. A sharp explosive sound (especially the sound of a gun firing).

    “They heard a violent report followed by silence.”

    A kind of: noise

  5. A written evaluation of a student's scholarship and deportment.

    “His father signed his report card.”

    Synonyms: report card · A kind of: information

  6. An essay (especially one written as an assignment).

    “He got an A on his composition.”

    Synonyms: composition, paper, theme · A kind of: essay · Kinds: term paper

  7. The general estimation that the public has for a person.

    “He acquired a reputation as an actor before he started writing.” “He was a person of bad report.”

    Synonyms: reputation · A kind of: estimate

Phrases and compounds with "report"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • report out (verb) Return a bill after consideration and revision to a legislative body.
  • news report (noun) A short account of the news.
  • report card (noun) A written evaluation of a student's scholarship and deportment.
  • medical report (noun) A report of the results of a medical examination of a patient.
  • written report (noun) A written document describing the findings of some individual or group.
  • earnings report (noun) A financial statement that gives operating results for a specific period.
  • progress report (noun) A report of work accomplished during a specified time period.
  • attestation report (noun) A consulting service in which a CPA expresses a conclusion about the reliability of a written statement that is the responsibility of someone else.
  • self-report inventory (noun) A personality inventory in which a person is asked which of a list of traits and characteristics describe her or him or to indicate which behaviors and hypothetical choices he or she would make.
  • self-report personality inventory (noun) A personality inventory in which a person is asked which of a list of traits and characteristics describe her or him or to indicate which behaviors and hypothetical choices he or she would make.
