Rest: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rest is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: Something left after other parts have been taken away.

In Hindi
आराम; बाक़ी
Synonyms
remainder, balance, residual, residue, residuum, ease, repose, relaxation, respite, relief
Opposites
be active
Word family
rester

Rest as a noun

  1. Something left after other parts have been taken away.

    “There was no remainder.” “He threw away the rest.” “He took what he wanted and I got the balance.”

    Synonyms: remainder, balance, residual, residue, residuum · A kind of: part · Kinds: leftover

  2. Freedom from activity (work or strain or responsibility).

    “Took his repose by the swimming pool.”

    Synonyms: ease, repose, relaxation · A kind of: inactivity · Kinds: bedrest, laziness, lie-in, quiescence, leisure

  3. A pause for relaxation.

    “People actually accomplish more when they take time for short rests.”

    Synonyms: respite, relief, rest period · A kind of: pause · Kinds: breath

  4. A state of inaction.

    “A body will continue in a state of rest until acted upon.”

    A kind of: inaction

  5. Euphemisms for death (based on an analogy between lying in a bed and in a tomb).

    “She was laid to rest beside her husband.” “They had to put their family pet to sleep.”

    Synonyms: eternal rest, sleep, eternal sleep, quietus · A kind of: death

  6. A support on which things can be put.

    “The gun was steadied on a special rest.”

    A kind of: support · Kinds: armrest, chin rest, headrest

  7. A musical notation indicating a silence of a specified duration.

    A kind of: musical notation · Kinds: whole rest, half rest, quarter rest

Rest as a verb

  1. Not move; be in a resting position.

    A kind of: be · Kinds: kneel, stand

  2. Take a short break from one's activities in order to relax.

    Synonyms: breathe, catch one's breath, take a breather · A kind of: pause

  3. Give a rest to.

    “He rested his bad leg.” “Rest the dogs for a moment.”

    Kinds: blow

  4. Have a place in relation to something else.

    “The fate of Bosnia lies in the hands of the West.” “The responsibility rests with the Allies.”

    Synonyms: lie · A kind of: be

  5. Be at rest.

    Opposite: be active · A kind of: recumb · Kinds: sleep, drowse

  6. Stay the same; remain in a certain state.

    “The dress remained wet after repeated attempts to dry it.” “Rest assured.” “Stay alone.”

    Synonyms: stay, remain · A kind of: be · Kinds: keep out, sit tight, stick together, stand, keep, be

  7. Be inherent or innate in.

    Synonyms: reside, repose · A kind of: inhere in

  8. Put something in a resting position, as for support or steadying.

    “Rest your head on my shoulder.”

    A kind of: put

  9. Sit, as on a branch.

    “The birds perched high in the tree.”

    Synonyms: perch, roost · A kind of: sit

  10. Rest on or as if on a pillow.

    “Pillow your head.”

    Synonyms: pillow · A kind of: put

  11. Be inactive, refrain from acting.

    “The committee is resting over the summer.”

    Kinds: hibernate

Phrases and compounds with "rest"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at rest (adjective) In a state of repose or especially sleep.
  • rest on (verb) Rest on for support.
  • bed rest (noun) Confinement to bed continuously (as in the case of some sick or injured persons).
  • gun rest (noun) Wale at the top of the side of boat; topmost planking of a wooden vessel.
  • rest day (noun) A day set aside for rest.
  • chin rest (noun) A rest on which a violinist can place the chin.
  • half rest (noun) A musical rest having the time value of half a whole rest or equal in duration to two beats in common time.
  • rest area (noun) Designated paved area beside a main road where cars can stop temporarily.
  • rest home (noun) An institution where people are cared for.
  • rest mass (noun) (physics) the mass of a body as measured when the body is at rest relative to an observer, an inherent property of the body.
  • rest stop (noun) Designated paved area beside a main road where cars can stop temporarily.
  • rest-cure (noun) Rest as a medical treatment for stress or anxiety etc.
  • rest house (noun) A building used for shelter by travelers (especially in areas where there are no hotels).
  • whole rest (noun) A musical rest equal in duration to four beats in common time.
  • day of rest (noun) A day set aside for rest.
  • lay to rest (verb) Place in a grave or tomb.
