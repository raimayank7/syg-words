Reverse: meaning, synonyms and examples

Reverse is used as a verb (5 senses), noun (6 senses) and adjective (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Change to the contrary.

In Hindi
उलटा; पीछे करना
Synonyms
change by reversal, turn, turn back, invert, overrule, overturn, override, overthrow, revoke, annul
Opposites
obverse, forward
Word family
reversible, reversal

Reverse as a verb

  1. Change to the contrary.

    “The trend was reversed.” “The tides turned against him.” “Public opinion turned when it was revealed that the president had an affair with a White House intern.”

    Synonyms: change by reversal, turn · A kind of: change · Kinds: interchange, turn the tables, commutate, switch over, correct, falsify, permute, metamorphose

  2. Turn inside out or upside down.

    Synonyms: turn back, invert · A kind of: change

  3. Rule against.

    “The Republicans were overruled when the House voted on the bill.”

    Synonyms: overrule, overturn, override, overthrow · A kind of: rule

  4. Cancel officially.

    “He revoked the ban on smoking.” “Lift an embargo.” “Vacate a death sentence.”

    Synonyms: revoke, annul, lift, countermand, repeal, overturn, rescind, vacate · A kind of: cancel · Kinds: renege

  5. Reverse the position, order, relation, or condition of.

    “When forming a question, invert the subject and the verb.”

    Synonyms: invert · A kind of: reorder

Reverse as a noun

  1. A relation of direct opposition.

    “We thought Sue was older than Bill but just the reverse was true.”

    Synonyms: contrary, opposite · A kind of: opposition

  2. The gears by which the motion of a machine can be reversed.

    Synonyms: reverse gear · A kind of: gear

  3. An unfortunate happening that hinders or impedes; something that is thwarting or frustrating.

    Synonyms: reversal, setback, blow, black eye · A kind of: happening · Kinds: whammy

  4. The side of a coin or medal that does not bear the principal design.

    Synonyms: verso · Opposite: obverse · A kind of: side · Kinds: tail

  5. (American football) a running play in which a back running in one direction hands the ball to a back running in the opposite direction.

    A kind of: run · Kinds: double reverse

  6. Turning in the opposite direction.

    Synonyms: reversion, reversal, turnabout, turnaround · A kind of: change of direction · Kinds: about-face, u-turn

Reverse as an adjective

  1. Directed or moving toward the rear.

    “A rearward glance.” “A rearward movement.”

    Synonyms: rearward · Similar: backward

  2. Of the transmission gear causing backward movement in a motor vehicle.

    “In reverse gear.”

    Opposite: forward

  3. Reversed (turned backward) in order or nature or effect.

    Synonyms: inverse · Similar: backward

Phrases and compounds with "reverse"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • reverse gear (noun) The gears by which the motion of a machine can be reversed.
  • reverse hang (noun) A hang with the arms extended in back.
  • reverse fault (noun) A geological fault in which the upper side appears to have been pushed upward by compression.
  • reverse split (noun) A decrease in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.
  • double reverse (noun) (American football) a running play in which a first reverse is followed by a second reverse.
  • reverse osmosis (noun) (chemistry) a method of producing pure water; a solvent passes through a semipermeable membrane in a direction opposite to that for natural osmosis when it is subjected to a hydrostatic pressure greater than the osmotic pressure.
  • reverse lightning (noun) Atmospheric discharges (lasting 10 msec) bursting from the tops of giant storm clouds in blue cones that widen as they flash upward.
  • piked reverse hang (noun) A reverse hang performed on the rings.
  • reverse stock split (noun) A decrease in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.
  • reverse transcriptase (noun) A polymerase that catalyzes the formation of DNA using RNA as a template; found especially in retroviruses.
  • reverse polish notation (noun) A parenthesis-free notation for forming mathematical expressions in which each operator follows its operands.
