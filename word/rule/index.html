Rule: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rule is used as a noun (12 senses) and verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: A principle or condition that customarily governs behavior.

In Hindi
नियम; शासन करना
Synonyms
regulation, convention, normal, pattern, formula, prescript, linguistic rule, principle, dominion, ruler
Word family
ruler, ruling

Rule as a noun

  1. A principle or condition that customarily governs behavior.

    “It was his rule to take a walk before breakfast.” “Short haircuts were the regulation.”

    Synonyms: regulation · A kind of: concept · Kinds: restriction, guidepost, cy pres, working principle

  2. Something regarded as a normative example.

    “The convention of not naming the main character.” “Violence is the rule not the exception.” “His formula for impressing visitors.”

    Synonyms: convention, normal, pattern, formula · A kind of: practice · Kinds: mores, code of conduct, universal

  3. Prescribed guide for conduct or action.

    Synonyms: prescript · A kind of: direction · Kinds: bylaw, rubric, order, rule of evidence, principle, golden rule, dictate, regulation

  4. (linguistics) a rule describing (or prescribing) a linguistic practice.

    Synonyms: linguistic rule · A kind of: concept · Kinds: universal, grammatical rule, morphological rule

  5. A basic generalization that is accepted as true and that can be used as a basis for reasoning or conduct.

    “Their principles of composition characterized all their works.”

    Synonyms: principle · A kind of: generalization · Kinds: pillar, yang, yin, feng shui

  6. The duration of a monarch's or government's power.

    “During the rule of Elizabeth.”

    A kind of: duration · Kinds: regency

  7. Dominance or power through legal authority.

    “France held undisputed dominion over vast areas of Africa.” “The rule of Caesar.”

    Synonyms: dominion · A kind of: dominance · Kinds: paramountcy, raj, reign, suzerainty

  8. Directions that define the way a game or sport is to be conducted.

    “He knew the rules of chess.”

    A kind of: direction · Kinds: ground rule

  9. Any one of a systematic body of regulations defining the way of life of members of a religious order.

    “The rule of St. Dominic.”

    A kind of: direction

  10. A rule or law concerning a natural phenomenon or the function of a complex system.

    “The principle of the conservation of mass.” “The principle of jet propulsion.” “The right-hand rule for inductive fields.”

    Synonyms: principle · A kind of: law · Kinds: mass-energy equivalence, principle of equivalence, principle of liquid displacement, principle of superposition, principle of superposition, mass-action principle, localization of function

  11. (mathematics) a standard procedure for solving a class of mathematical problems.

    “He determined the upper bound with Descartes' rule of signs.” “He gave us a general formula for attacking polynomials.”

    Synonyms: formula · A kind of: procedure · Kinds: metarule, algorithm, heuristic, recursion

  12. Measuring stick consisting of a strip of wood or metal or plastic with a straight edge that is used for drawing straight lines and measuring lengths.

    Synonyms: ruler · A kind of: measuring stick · Kinds: carpenter's rule, foot rule, meterstick, yardstick

Rule as a verb

  1. Exercise authority over; as of nations.

    “Who is governing the country now?”

    Synonyms: govern · A kind of: control · Kinds: throne, misgovern, dictate, reign

  2. Decide with authority.

    Synonyms: decree · A kind of: decide · Kinds: overrule, rule out

  3. Be larger in number, quantity, power, status or importance.

    “Money reigns supreme here.” “Hispanics predominate in this neighborhood.”

    Synonyms: predominate, dominate, reign, prevail · Kinds: override, overarch, preponderate

  4. Decide on and make a declaration about.

    “Find someone guilty.”

    Synonyms: find · A kind of: pronounce

  5. Have an affinity with; of signs of the zodiac.

    A kind of: attach to

  6. Mark or draw with a ruler.

    “Rule the margins.”

    A kind of: draw

  7. Keep in check.

    “Rule one's temper.”

    Synonyms: harness, rein · A kind of: restrict

Phrases and compounds with "rule"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rule in (verb) Include or exclude by determining judicially or in agreement with rules.
  • gag rule (noun) A rule for limiting or ending debate in a deliberative body.
  • rule out (verb) Make impossible, especially beforehand.
  • foot rule (noun) A ruler one foot long.
  • home rule (noun) Self-government in local matters by a city or county that is part of a national government.
  • rule book (noun) A collection of rules or prescribed standards on the basis of which decisions are made.
  • self-rule (noun) Government of a political unit by its own people.
  • board rule (noun) A measure used in computing board feet.
  • plumb rule (noun) A plumb line attached to a narrow board.
  • slide rule (noun) Analog computer consisting of a handheld instrument used for rapid calculations; have been replaced by pocket calculators.
  • golden rule (noun) Any important rule.
  • ground rule (noun) (baseball) a special rule (as in baseball) dealing with situations that arise due to the nature of the playing grounds.
  • rule of law (noun) A state of order in which events conform to the law.
  • hearsay rule (noun) A rule that declares not admissible as evidence any statement other than that by a witness.
  • miranda rule (noun) The rule that police (when interrogating you after an arrest) are obliged to warn you that anything you say may be used as evidence and to read you your constitutional rights (the right to a lawyer and the right to remain silent until advised by a lawyer).
  • one-man rule (noun) A form of government in which the ruler is an absolute dictator (not restricted by a constitution or laws or opposition etc.).
