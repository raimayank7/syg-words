Say: meaning, synonyms and examples

Say is used as a verb (11 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Express in words.

In Hindi
कहना
Synonyms
state, tell, allege, aver, suppose, read, order, enjoin, pronounce, articulate
Word family
saying

Say as a verb

  1. Express in words.

    “He said that he wanted to marry her.” “Tell me what is bothering you.” “State your opinion.”

    Synonyms: state, tell · A kind of: express · Kinds: present, misstate, answer, precede, announce, articulate, get out, declare

  2. Report or maintain.

    “He alleged that he was the victim of a crime.” “He said it was too late to intervene in the war.” “The registrar says that I owe the school money.”

    Synonyms: allege, aver · A kind of: assert · Kinds: plead

  3. Express a supposition.

    “Let us say that he did not tell the truth.” “Let's say you had a lot of money--what would you do?”

    Synonyms: suppose · A kind of: speculate

  4. Have or contain a certain wording or form.

    “The passage reads as follows.” “What does the law say?”

    Synonyms: read · A kind of: have

  5. Give instructions to or direct somebody to do something with authority.

    “I said to him to go home.” “She ordered him to do the shopping.” “The mother told the child to get dressed.”

    Synonyms: order, tell, enjoin · A kind of: request · Kinds: direct, instruct, command, call, warn

  6. Speak, pronounce, or utter in a certain way.

    “She pronounces French words in a funny way.” “I cannot say `zip wire'.” “Can the child sound out this complicated word?”

    Synonyms: pronounce, articulate, enounce, sound out, enunciate · Kinds: twang, devoice, raise, lilt, palatalize, nasalize, nasalize, mispronounce

  7. Communicate or express nonverbally.

    “What does this painting say?” “Did his face say anything about how he felt?”

    A kind of: convey

  8. Utter aloud.

    “She said `Hello' to everyone in the office.”

    A kind of: express

  9. State as one's opinion or judgement; declare.

    “I say let's forget this whole business.”

  10. Recite or repeat a fixed text.

    “Say grace.” “She said her `Hail Mary'.”

    A kind of: recite

  11. Indicate.

    “The clock says noon.”

    A kind of: read

Say as a noun

  1. The chance to speak.

    “Let him have his say.”

    A kind of: opportunity

Phrases and compounds with "say"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • say-so (noun) One chap's arbitrary assertion.
  • as we say (adverb) In a manner of speaking.
  • say hey kid (noun) United States baseball player (born in 1931).
  • say farewell (verb) Say good-bye or bid farewell.
  • never-say-die (adjective) Impossible to subdue.
  • that is to say (adverb) As follows.
