Scratch: meaning, synonyms and examples

Scratch is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Cause friction.

In Hindi
खरोंच; खुजलाना
Synonyms
rub, fray, fret, chafe, scrape, scratch up, itch, cancel, call off, scrub
Word family
scratcher, scratchy

Scratch as a verb

  1. Cause friction.

    “My sweater scratches.”

    Synonyms: rub, fray, fret, chafe · A kind of: touch

  2. Cut the surface of; wear away the surface of.

    Synonyms: scrape, scratch up · A kind of: incise · Kinds: claw, scuff

  3. Scrape or rub as if to relieve itching.

    “Don't scratch your insect bites!”

    Synonyms: rub, itch · A kind of: irritate

  4. Postpone indefinitely or annul something that was scheduled.

    “Call off the engagement.” “Cancel the dinner party.” “We had to scrub our vacation plans.”

    Synonyms: cancel, call off, scrub

  5. Remove by erasing or crossing out or as if by drawing a line.

    “Please strike this remark from the record.” “Scratch that remark.”

    Synonyms: strike, expunge, excise · A kind of: delete

  6. Gather (money or other resources) together over time.

    “She had scraped together enough money for college.” “They scratched a meager living.”

    Synonyms: scrape, scrape up, come up · A kind of: roll up · Kinds: nickel-and-dime

  7. Carve, cut, or etch into a material or surface.

    “Engrave a pen.” “Engraved the trophy cupt with the winner's.” “The lovers scratched their names into the bark of the tree.”

    Synonyms: engrave, grave, inscribe · A kind of: carve · Kinds: character

Scratch as a noun

  1. An abraded area where the skin is torn or worn off.

    Synonyms: abrasion, scrape, excoriation · A kind of: wound · Kinds: graze, rope burn

  2. A depression scratched or carved into a surface.

    Synonyms: incision, prick, slit, dent · A kind of: depression · Kinds: score

  3. Informal terms for money.

    Synonyms: boodle, bread, cabbage, clams, dinero, dough, gelt, kale · A kind of: money

  4. A competitor who has withdrawn from competition.

    A kind of: rival

  5. A line indicating the location of the start of a race or a game.

    Synonyms: start, starting line, scratch line · A kind of: line

  6. Dry mash for poultry.

    Synonyms: chicken feed · A kind of: mash

  7. Poor handwriting.

    Synonyms: scribble, scrawl, cacography · A kind of: handwriting · Kinds: chicken scratch, squiggle

  8. (golf) a handicap of zero strokes.

    “A golfer who plays at scratch should be able to achieve par on a course.”

    A kind of: handicap

  9. An indication of damage.

    Synonyms: scrape, scar, mark · A kind of: blemish

Phrases and compounds with "scratch"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • scratch up (verb) Cut the surface of; wear away the surface of.
  • scratch awl (noun) A sharp-pointed awl for marking wood or metal to be cut.
  • scratch off (verb) Write quickly.
  • scratch out (verb) Strike or cancel by or as if by rubbing or crossing out.
  • scratch pad (noun) Pad for preliminary or hasty writing or notes or sketches etc.
  • from scratch (adverb) From the beginning, especially without relying on resources or other advantages.
  • scratch line (noun) A line indicating the location of the start of a race or a game.
  • scratch race (noun) A race in which all contestants start from scratch (on equal terms).
  • scratch test (noun) A test to determine allergic sensitivity to various substances by applying them to scratches in the skin.
  • scratch along (verb) Manage one's existence barely.
  • scratch paper (noun) Pad for preliminary or hasty writing or notes or sketches etc.
  • scratch sheet (noun) A racing publication giving information on horses and the outcomes of horse races.
  • chicken scratch (noun) Cramped or illegible handwriting.
  • cat scratch disease (noun) A disease thought to be transmitted to humans by a scratch from a cat.
