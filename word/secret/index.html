Secret: meaning, synonyms and examples

Secret is used as an adjective (11 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Not open or public; kept private or not revealed.

In Hindi
रहस्य, राज़; गुप्त
Synonyms
clandestine, cloak-and-dagger, hole-and-corner, hugger-mugger, hush-hush, surreptitious, undercover, underground, unavowed, private

Secret as an adjective

  1. Not open or public; kept private or not revealed.

    “A secret formula.” “Secret ingredients.” “Secret talks.”

    Similar: concealed

  2. Conducted with or marked by hidden aims or methods.

    “Clandestine intelligence operations.” “Cloak-and-dagger activities behind enemy lines.” “Hole-and-corner intrigue.”

    Synonyms: clandestine, cloak-and-dagger, hole-and-corner, hugger-mugger, hush-hush, surreptitious, undercover, underground · Similar: covert

  3. Not openly made known.

    “A secret marriage.” “A secret bride.”

    Synonyms: unavowed · Similar: unacknowledged

  4. Communicated covertly.

    “Their secret signal was a wink.” “Secret messages.”

    Similar: covert

  5. Not expressed.

    “Secret (or private) thoughts.”

    Synonyms: private · Similar: inward

  6. Designed to elude detection.

    “A hidden room or place of concealment such as a priest hole.” “A secret passage.” “The secret compartment in the desk.”

    Synonyms: hidden · Similar: concealed

  7. Hidden from general view or use.

    “A privy place to rest and think.” “A secluded romantic spot.” “A secret garden.”

    Synonyms: privy, secluded · Similar: private

  8. (of information) given in confidence or in secret.

    “This arrangement must be kept confidential.” “Their secret communications.”

    Synonyms: confidential · Similar: private

  9. Indulging only covertly.

    Similar: covert

  10. Having an import not apparent to the senses nor obvious to the intelligence; beyond ordinary understanding.

    “Mysterious symbols.” “The mystical style of Blake.” “Occult lore.”

    Synonyms: mysterious, mystic, mystical, occult, orphic · Similar: esoteric

  11. The next to highest level of official classification for documents.

    Similar: classified

Secret as a noun

  1. Something that should remain hidden from others (especially information that is not to be passed on).

    “The combination to the safe was a secret.” “He tried to keep his drinking a secret.”

    A kind of: information · Kinds: confidence, open secret, trade secret

  2. Information known only to a special group.

    “The secret of Cajun cooking.”

    Synonyms: arcanum · A kind of: information · Kinds: esoterica, cabala, password

  3. Something that baffles understanding and cannot be explained.

    “How it got out is a mystery.” “It remains one of nature's secrets.”

    Synonyms: mystery, enigma, closed book · A kind of: perplexity

Phrases and compounds with "secret"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in secret (adverb) In secrecy; not openly.
  • top-secret (adjective) The highest official level of classification of documents.
  • open secret (noun) Something that is supposed to be secret but is generally known.
  • secret code (noun) A secret method of writing.
  • secret plan (noun) A secret scheme to do something (especially something underhand or illegal).
  • secret agent (noun) A person secretly employed in espionage for a government.
  • trade secret (noun) A secret (method or device or formula) that gives a manufacturer an advantage over the competition.
  • secret ballot (noun) A vote in which each person's choice is secret but the totaled votes are public.
  • secret police (noun) A police force that operates in secrecy (usually against persons suspected of treason or sedition).
  • secret service (noun) The United States intelligence agency that protects current and former presidents and vice presidents and their immediate families and protects distinguished foreign visitors; detects and apprehends counterfeiters; suppresses forgery of government securities and documents.
  • secret society (noun) A society that conceals its activities from nonmembers.
  • secret writing (noun) A piece of writing in code or cipher.
  • secret approval (noun) (law) tacit approval of someone's wrongdoing.
  • us secret service (noun) The United States intelligence agency that protects current and former presidents and vice presidents and their immediate families and protects distinguished foreign visitors; detects and apprehends counterfeiters; suppresses forgery of government securities and documents.
  • secret intelligence service (noun) The government agency in the United Kingdom that is responsible for internal security and counterintelligence overseas.
  • united states secret service (noun) The United States intelligence agency that protects current and former presidents and vice presidents and their immediate families and protects distinguished foreign visitors; detects and apprehends counterfeiters; suppresses forgery of government securities and documents.
