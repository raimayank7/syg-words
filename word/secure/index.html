Secure: meaning, synonyms and examples

Secure is used as a verb (6 senses) and adjective (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Get by special effort.

In Hindi
सुरक्षित
Synonyms
procure, fasten, fix, guarantee, ensure, insure, assure, plug, stop up, batten
Opposites
insecure
Word family
securer, security, secureness

Secure as a verb

  1. Get by special effort.

    “He procured extra cigarettes even though they were rationed.”

    Synonyms: procure · A kind of: obtain · Kinds: suborn, copyright, patent, engage

  2. Cause to be firmly attached.

    “Fasten the lock onto the door.” “She fixed her gaze on the man.”

    Synonyms: fasten, fix · A kind of: attach · Kinds: strap, tie, rig, bitt, latch, garter, chain, cable

  3. Assure payment of.

    A kind of: guarantee · Kinds: certify

  4. Make certain of.

    “This nest egg will ensure a nice retirement for us.” “Preparation will guarantee success!”

    Synonyms: guarantee, ensure, insure, assure · Kinds: doom, make

  5. Fill or close tightly with or as if with a plug.

    “Plug the hole.” “Stop up the leak.”

    Synonyms: plug, stop up · A kind of: close · Kinds: stopper, tampon, chink, cork

  6. Furnish with battens.

    “Batten ships.”

    Synonyms: batten, batten down · A kind of: strengthen

Secure as an adjective

  1. Free from fear or doubt; easy in mind.

    “He was secure that nothing will be held against him.”

    Synonyms: unafraid, untroubled · Opposite: insecure

  2. Free from danger or risk.

    “Secure from harm.” “His fortune was secure.” “Made a secure place for himself in his field.”

    Opposite: insecure · Similar: assured, firm, fail-safe, sure

  3. Not likely to fail or give way.

    “The lock was secure.” “A secure foundation.” “A secure hold on her wrist.”

    Opposite: insecure · Similar: steady, tight

  4. Immune to attack; incapable of being tampered with.

    “An impregnable fortress.” “Fortifications that made the frontier inviolable.” “A secure telephone connection.”

    Synonyms: impregnable, inviolable, strong, unassailable, unattackable · Similar: invulnerable

  5. Financially sound.

    “A good investment.” “A secure investment.”

    Synonyms: dependable, good, safe · Similar: sound
