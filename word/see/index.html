See: meaning, synonyms and examples

See is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Perceive by sight or have the power to perceive by sight.

In Hindi
देखना
Synonyms
understand, realize, realise, witness, find, visualize, visualise, envision, project, fancy
Word family
seer, seeing

See as a verb

  1. Perceive by sight or have the power to perceive by sight.

    “You have to be a good observer to see all the details.” “Can you see the bird in that tree?” “He is blind--he cannot see.”

    A kind of: perceive · Kinds: glimpse, see, catch sight, behold

  2. Perceive (an idea or situation) mentally.

    “Now I see!” “I just can't see your point.” “Does she realize how important this decision is?”

    Synonyms: understand, realize, realise · Kinds: perceive, appreciate

  3. Perceive or be contemporaneous with.

    “We found Republicans winning the offices.” “You'll see a lot of cheating in this school.” “The 1960's saw the rebellion of the younger generation against established traditions.”

    Synonyms: witness, find · A kind of: experience · Kinds: catch

  4. Imagine; conceive of; see in one's mind.

    “I can't see him on horseback!” “I can see what will happen.” “I can see a risk in this strategy.”

    Synonyms: visualize, visualise, envision, project, fancy, figure, picture, image · A kind of: imagine

  5. Deem to be.

    “She views this quite differently from me.” “I consider her to be shallow.” “I don't see the situation quite as negatively as you do.”

    Synonyms: consider, reckon, view, regard · A kind of: think · Kinds: expect, receive, reconsider, reconsider, include, consider, call, like

  6. Get to know or become aware of, usually accidentally.

    “I learned that she has two grown-up children.” “I see that you have been promoted.”

    Synonyms: learn, hear, get word, get wind, pick up, find out, get a line, discover · Kinds: get the goods, wise up, trip up, ascertain, discover

  7. See or watch.

    “View a show on television.” “This program will be seen all over the world.” “View an exhibition.”

    Synonyms: watch, view, catch, take in · A kind of: watch · Kinds: visualize, spectate, preview

  8. Come together.

    “I'll probably see you at the meeting.” “How nice to see you again!”

    Synonyms: meet, run into, encounter, run across, come across · Kinds: intersect

  9. Find out, learn, or determine with certainty, usually by making an inquiry or other effort.

    “I want to see whether she speaks French.” “See whether it works.” “Find out if he speaks Russian.”

    Synonyms: determine, check, find out, ascertain, watch, learn · Kinds: test

  10. Be careful or certain to do something; make certain of something.

    “He verified that the valves were closed.” “See that the curtains are closed.” “Control the quality of the product.”

    Synonyms: check, insure, see to it, ensure, control, ascertain, assure · A kind of: verify · Kinds: proofread, control, double-check, cross-check, cinch, spot-check, cover

  11. Go to see for professional or business reasons.

    “You should see a lawyer.” “We had to see a psychiatrist.”

    A kind of: visit

  12. Go to see for a social visit.

    “I went to see my friend Mary the other day.”

    A kind of: visit

  13. Go to see a place, as for entertainment.

    “We went to see the Eiffel Tower in the morning.”

    Synonyms: visit · A kind of: tour · Kinds: take in

  14. Take charge of or deal with.

    “Could you see about lunch?” “I must attend to this matter.” “She took care of this business.”

    Synonyms: attend, take care, look · A kind of: care · Kinds: minister, tend

See as a noun

  1. The seat within a bishop's diocese where his cathedral is located.

    A kind of: seat
