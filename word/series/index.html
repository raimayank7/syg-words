Series: meaning, synonyms and examples

Series is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Similar things placed in order or happening one after another.

In Hindi
श्रृंखला
Synonyms
serial, serial publication
Word family
serial

Series as a noun

  1. Similar things placed in order or happening one after another.

    “They were investigating a series of bank robberies.”

    A kind of: ordering · Kinds: chain, cordon, course, cycle, electromotive series, hierarchy, nexus, progression

  2. A serialized set of programs.

    “A comedy series.” “The Masterworks concert series.”

    Synonyms: serial · A kind of: broadcast · Kinds: soap opera, tetralogy

  3. A periodical that appears at scheduled times.

    Synonyms: serial, serial publication · A kind of: periodical · Kinds: semiweekly, weekly, semimonthly, monthly, quarterly, bimonthly, biweekly

  4. (sports) several contests played successively by the same teams.

    “The visiting team swept the series.”

    A kind of: contest · Kinds: home stand

  5. (electronics) connection of components in such a manner that current flows first through one and then through the other.

    “The voltage divider consisted of a series of fixed resistors.”

    A kind of: connection

  6. A group of postage stamps having a common theme or a group of coins or currency selected as a group for study or collection.

    “The Post Office issued a series commemorating famous American entertainers.” “His coin collection included the complete series of Indian-head pennies.”

    A kind of: group

  7. (mathematics) the sum of a finite or infinite sequence of expressions.

    A kind of: polynomial · Kinds: power series, convergence, divergence, geometric series, exponential series

Phrases and compounds with "series"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gi series (noun) Diagnostic tests of the alimentary canal; usually involves inserting a contrast medium (such as barium sulfate) and taking an X-ray.
  • in series (adjective) Of or relating to the sequential performance of multiple operations.
  • time series (noun) A series of values of a variable at successive times.
  • power series (noun) The sum of terms containing successively higher integral powers of a variable.
  • world series (noun) Series that constitutes the playoff for the baseball championship.
  • alkane series (noun) A series of non-aromatic saturated hydrocarbons with the general formula CnH(2n+2).
  • fourier series (noun) The sum of a series of trigonometric expressions; used in the analysis of periodic functions.
  • methane series (noun) A series of non-aromatic saturated hydrocarbons with the general formula CnH(2n+2).
  • ordered series (noun) An ordered reference standard.
  • series circuit (noun) A circuit having its parts connected serially.
  • actinide series (noun) (chemistry) a series of 15 radioactive elements with increasing atomic numbers from actinium to lawrencium.
  • paraffin series (noun) A series of non-aromatic saturated hydrocarbons with the general formula CnH(2n+2).
  • geometric series (noun) A geometric progression written as a sum.
  • lanthanide series (noun) The rare-earth elements with atomic numbers 57 through 71; having properties similar to lanthanum.
  • exponential series (noun) A series derived from the expansion of an exponential expression.
  • electromotive series (noun) A serial arrangement of metallic elements or ions according to their electrode potentials determined under specified conditions; the order shows the tendency of one metal to reduce the ions of any other metal below it in the series.
