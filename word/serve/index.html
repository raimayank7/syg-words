Serve: meaning, synonyms and examples

Serve is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Serve a purpose, role, or function.

In Hindi
सेवा करना, परोसना
Synonyms
function, service, help, serve up, dish out, dish up, dish, serve well, do, attend to
Word family
server, serving, servant, servitor

Serve as a verb

  1. Serve a purpose, role, or function.

    “The tree stump serves as a table.” “The female students served as a control group.” “This table would serve very well.”

    Synonyms: function · A kind of: suffice · Kinds: prelude, act as

  2. Do duty or hold offices; serve in a specific function.

    “He served as head of the department for three years.” “She served in Congress for two terms.”

    A kind of: work · Kinds: sit, staff, act, rotate, officiate, caddie, represent

  3. Contribute or conduce to.

    “The scandal served to increase his popularity.”

    A kind of: effect · Kinds: help

  4. Be used by; as of a utility.

    “The sewage plant served the neighboring communities.” “The garage served to shelter his horses.”

    Synonyms: service · A kind of: function

  5. Help to some food; help with food or drink.

    “I served him three times, and after that he helped himself.”

    Synonyms: help · A kind of: provide

  6. Provide (usually but not necessarily food).

    “We serve meals for the homeless.” “She dished out the soup at 8 P.M.” “The entertainers served up a lively show.”

    Synonyms: serve up, dish out, dish up, dish · A kind of: provide · Kinds: plank

  7. Devote (part of) one's life or efforts to, as of countries, institutions, or ideas.

    “She served the art of music.” “He served the church.” “Serve the country.”

    A kind of: work

  8. Promote, benefit, or be useful or beneficial to.

    “Art serves commerce.” “Their interests are served.” “The lake serves recreation.”

    Synonyms: serve well · A kind of: foster

  9. Spend time in prison or in a labor camp.

    “He did six years for embezzlement.”

    Synonyms: do · A kind of: spend · Kinds: admit

  10. Work for or be a servant to.

    “May I serve you?” “She attends the old lady in the wheelchair.” “Can you wait on our table, please?”

    Synonyms: attend to, wait on, attend, assist · A kind of: help · Kinds: valet, fag

  11. Deliver a warrant or summons to someone.

    “He was processed by the sheriff.”

    Synonyms: process, swear out · A kind of: deliver · Kinds: subpoena, wash

  12. Be sufficient; be adequate, either in quality or quantity.

    “A few words would answer.” “This car suits my purpose well.” “Will $100 do?”

    Synonyms: suffice, do, answer · A kind of: satisfy · Kinds: tide over, go a long way, serve, qualify, go around

  13. Do military service.

    “She served in Vietnam.” “My sons never served, because they are short-sighted.”

    A kind of: work

  14. Mate with.

    “Male animals serve the females for breeding purposes.”

    Synonyms: service · Kinds: stand

Serve as a noun

  1. (sports) a stroke that puts the ball in play.

    “His powerful serves won the game.”

    Synonyms: service · A kind of: tennis stroke · Kinds: ace, fault, let

Phrases and compounds with "serve"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • serve up (verb) Provide (usually but not necessarily food).
  • serve well (verb) Promote, benefit, or be useful or beneficial to.
  • break of serve (noun) (tennis) a score consisting of winning a game when your opponent was serving.
  • drop one's serve (verb) Lose a game in which one is serving.
  • first-come-first-serve (adjective) Not accepting reservations.

Word family
