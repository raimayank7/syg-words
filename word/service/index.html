Service: meaning, synonyms and examples

Service is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Work done by one person or group that benefits another.

In Hindi
सेवा
Synonyms
religious service, divine service, military service, armed service, avail, help, table service, servicing, serve, serving
Opposites
disservice
Word family
servicing

Service as a noun

  1. Work done by one person or group that benefits another.

    “Budget separately for goods and services.”

    A kind of: work · Kinds: consulting service, facility, national service, utility

  2. An act of help or assistance.

    “He did them a service.”

    Opposite: disservice · A kind of: aid · Kinds: childcare, community service, community service, help desk, seating

  3. The act of public worship following prescribed rules.

    “The Sunday service.”

    Synonyms: religious service, divine service · A kind of: religious ceremony · Kinds: church service, devotional, prayer meeting, chapel service, committal service, none, vesper, watch night

  4. A company or agency that performs a public service; subject to government regulation.

    A kind of: company · Kinds: utility

  5. Employment in or work for another.

    “He retired after 30 years of service.”

    A kind of: employment

  6. A force that is a branch of the armed forces.

    Synonyms: military service, armed service · A kind of: force · Kinds: army, navy, coastguard, air force

  7. Canadian writer (born in England) who wrote about life in the Yukon Territory (1874-1958).

  8. A means of serving.

    “Of no avail.” “There's no help for it.”

    Synonyms: avail, help · A kind of: helpfulness

  9. Tableware consisting of a complete set of articles (silver or dishware) for use at table.

    Synonyms: table service · A kind of: tableware · Kinds: dinner service, place setting

  10. The act of mating by male animals.

    “The bull was worth good money in servicing fees.”

    Synonyms: servicing · A kind of: coupling

  11. (law) the acts performed by an English feudal tenant for the benefit of his lord which formed the consideration for the property granted to him.

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: socage, military service, knight's service

  12. (sports) a stroke that puts the ball in play.

    “His powerful serves won the game.”

    Synonyms: serve · A kind of: tennis stroke · Kinds: ace, fault, let

  13. The act of delivering a writ or summons upon someone.

    “He accepted service of the subpoena.”

    Synonyms: serving, service of process · A kind of: delivery

  14. Periodic maintenance on a car or machine.

    “It was time for an overhaul on the tractor.”

    Synonyms: overhaul, inspection and repair · A kind of: care · Kinds: interim overhaul

Service as a verb

  1. Be used by; as of a utility.

    “The sewage plant served the neighboring communities.” “The garage served to shelter his horses.”

    Synonyms: serve · A kind of: function

  2. Make fit for use.

    “Service my truck.” “The washing machine needs to be serviced.”

    A kind of: tune

  3. Mate with.

    “Male animals serve the females for breeding purposes.”

    Synonyms: serve · Kinds: stand

Phrases and compounds with "service"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bus service (noun) A public utility providing local transportation.
  • gas service (noun) A public utility that provides gas.
  • ill service (noun) An act intended to help that turns out badly.
  • lip service (noun) An expression of agreement that is not supported by real conviction.
  • service cap (noun) A cap with a flat circular top and a visor.
  • service fee (noun) A percentage of a bill (as at a hotel or restaurant) added in payment for service.
  • service man (noun) A skilled worker whose job is to repair things.
  • tea service (noun) A set of china or silverware for serving tea.
  • curb service (noun) Service provided to customers who remain in their vehicles.
  • mail service (noun) The system whereby messages are transmitted via the post office.
  • self-service (noun) The practice of serving yourself (as in a grocery or cafeteria).
  • service area (noun) Place on a highway providing garage services and eating and toilet facilities.
  • service book (noun) A book setting forth the forms of church service.
  • service call (noun) A trip made by a repairman to visit the location of something in need of service.
  • service club (noun) A club of professional or business people organized for their coordination and active in public services.
  • service door (noun) An entrance intended for the use of servants or for delivery of goods and removal of refuse.
