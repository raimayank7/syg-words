Short: meaning, synonyms and examples

Short is used as an adjective (11 senses), noun (3 senses), adverb (7 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Primarily temporal sense; indicating or being or seeming to be limited in duration.

In Hindi
छोटा, नाटा
Synonyms
little, inadequate, poor, unretentive, forgetful, light, scant, shortsighted, unforesightful, myopic
Opposites
long, tall
Word family
shortness

Short as an adjective

  1. Primarily temporal sense; indicating or being or seeming to be limited in duration.

    “A short life.” “A short flight.” “A short holiday.”

    Opposite: long · Similar: abbreviated, brief, clipped, fleeting, short and sweet, short-dated

  2. (primarily spatial sense) having little length or lacking in length.

    “Short skirts.” “Short hair.” “The board was a foot short.”

    Opposite: long · Similar: abbreviated, close, curtal, sawed-off, shortish, short-range

  3. Low in stature; not tall.

    “He was short and stocky.” “Short in stature.” “A short smokestack.”

    Synonyms: little · Opposite: tall · Similar: chunky, compact, half-length, pint-size, short-stalked, squab

  4. Not sufficient to meet a need.

    “An inadequate income.” “A poor salary.” “Money is short.”

    Synonyms: inadequate, poor · Similar: insufficient

  5. (of memory) deficient in retentiveness or range.

    “A short memory.”

    Synonyms: unretentive, forgetful

  6. Not holding securities or commodities that one sells in expectation of a fall in prices.

    “A short sale.” “Short in cotton.”

    Opposite: long

  7. Of speech sounds or syllables of relatively short duration.

    “The English vowel sounds in `pat', `pet', `pit', `pot', putt' are short.”

    Opposite: long

  8. Less than the correct or legal or full amount often deliberately so.

    “A light pound.” “A scant cup of sugar.” “Regularly gives short weight.”

    Synonyms: light, scant · Similar: insufficient

  9. Lacking foresight or scope.

    “A short view of the problem.” “Shortsighted policies.” “Shortsighted critics derided the plan.”

    Synonyms: shortsighted, unforesightful, myopic · Similar: improvident

  10. Tending to crumble or break into flakes due to a large amount of shortening.

    “Shortbread is a short crumbly cookie.” “A short flaky pie crust.”

    Similar: breakable

  11. Marked by rude or peremptory shortness.

    “Try to cultivate a less brusque manner.” “A curt reply.” “The salesgirl was very short with him.”

    Synonyms: brusque, brusk, curt · Similar: discourteous

Short as a noun

  1. The location on a baseball field where the shortstop is stationed.

    A kind of: tract

  2. Accidental contact between two points in an electric circuit that have a potential difference.

    Synonyms: short circuit · A kind of: contact

  3. The fielding position of the player on a baseball team who is stationed between second and third base.

    Synonyms: shortstop · A kind of: position

Short as an adverb

  1. Quickly and without warning.

    “He stopped suddenly.”

    Synonyms: abruptly, suddenly, dead

  2. Without possessing something at the time it is contractually sold.

    “He made his fortune by selling short just before the crash.”

  3. Clean across.

    “The car's axle snapped short.”

  4. At some point or distance before a goal is reached.

    “He fell short of our expectations.”

  5. So as to interrupt.

    “She took him up short before he could continue.”

  6. At a disadvantage.

    “I was caught short.”

    Synonyms: unawares

  7. In a curt, abrupt and discourteous manner.

    “He told me curtly to get on with it.” “He talked short with everyone.” “He said shortly that he didn't like it.”

    Synonyms: curtly, shortly

Short as a verb

  1. Cheat someone by not returning him enough money.

    Synonyms: short-change · A kind of: victimize

  2. Create a short circuit in.

    Synonyms: short-circuit · A kind of: make

Phrases and compounds with "short"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in short (adverb) In a concise manner; in a few words.
  • cut short (verb) Interrupt before its natural or planned end.
  • for short (adverb) As an abbreviation.
  • run short (verb) To be spent or finished.
  • short ton (noun) A United States unit of weight equivalent to 2000 pounds.
  • short-run (adjective) Relating to or extending over a limited period.
  • come short (verb) Fail to meet (expectations or standards).
  • fall short (verb) Fail to meet (expectations or standards).
  • sell short (verb) Sell securities or commodities or foreign currency that is not actually owned by the seller, who hopes to cover (buy back) the sold items at a lower price and thus to earn a profit.
  • short aria (noun) A short aria.
  • short bone (noun) A bone that is of approximately equal dimension in all directions.
  • short iron (noun) An iron with a short shaft and pitched face; for hitting short high shots.
  • short line (noun) A transportation system that operates over relatively short distances.
  • short list (noun) A list of applicants winnowed from a longer list who have been deemed suitable and from which the successful person will be chosen.
  • short ribs (noun) Cut of beef containing rib ends near the sternum.
  • short sale (noun) Sale of securities or commodity futures not owned by the seller (who hopes to buy them back later at a lower price).
