Sign: meaning, synonyms and examples

Sign is used as a verb (8 senses), noun (11 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Mark with one's signature; write one's name (on).

In Hindi
चिह्न, संकेत; हस्ताक्षर करना
Synonyms
subscribe, ratify, contract, sign on, sign up, signal, signalize, signalise, bless, mark
Word family
signature, signer, signing, signify

Sign as a verb

  1. Mark with one's signature; write one's name (on).

    “She signed the letter and sent it off.” “Please sign here.”

    Synonyms: subscribe · A kind of: write · Kinds: rubricate

  2. Approve and express assent, responsibility, or obligation.

    “All parties ratified the peace treaty.” “Have you signed your contract yet?”

    Synonyms: ratify · A kind of: validate

  3. Be engaged by a written agreement.

    “The soprano signed to sing the new opera.”

    A kind of: contract · Kinds: undersign, ink, autograph, initial, countersign, execute, endorse

  4. Engage by written agreement.

    “They signed two new pitchers for the next season.”

    Synonyms: contract, sign on, sign up · A kind of: hire · Kinds: contract out

  5. Communicate silently and non-verbally by signals or signs.

    “He signed his disapproval with a dismissive hand gesture.” “The diner signaled the waiters to bring the menu.”

    Synonyms: signal, signalize, signalise · A kind of: communicate · Kinds: wigwag, semaphore, heliograph, flag, whistle

  6. Place signs, as along a road.

    “Sign an intersection.” “This road has been signed.”

    A kind of: put

  7. Communicate in sign language.

    “I don't know how to sign, so I could not communicate with my deaf cousin.”

    A kind of: communicate

  8. Make the sign of the cross over someone in order to call on God for protection; consecrate.

    Synonyms: bless · A kind of: gesticulate

Sign as a noun

  1. A perceptible indication of something not immediately apparent (as a visible clue that something has happened).

    “He showed signs of strain.” “They welcomed the signs of spring.”

    Synonyms: mark · A kind of: clue · Kinds: token

  2. A public display of a message.

    “He posted signs in all the shop windows.”

    A kind of: communication · Kinds: poster, street sign, signpost

  3. Any nonverbal action or gesture that encodes a message.

    “Signals from the boat suddenly stopped.”

    Synonyms: signal, signaling · A kind of: communication · Kinds: recording, phone number, starting signal, storm signal, radio beam, ticktack, time signal, dog-ear

  4. Structure displaying a board on which advertisements can be posted.

    “The highway was lined with signboards.”

    Synonyms: signboard · A kind of: structure · Kinds: billboard, sandwich board, scoreboard, shingle

  5. (astrology) one of 12 equal areas into which the zodiac is divided.

    Synonyms: sign of the zodiac, star sign, mansion, house, planetary house · A kind of: region

  6. (medicine) any objective evidence of the presence of a disorder or disease.

    “There were no signs of asphyxiation.”

    A kind of: evidence · Kinds: vital sign

  7. Having an indicated pole (as the distinction between positive and negative electric charges).

    “He got the polarity of the battery reversed.” “Charges of opposite sign.”

    Synonyms: polarity · A kind of: opposition · Kinds: positivity, negativity

  8. An event that is experienced as indicating important things to come.

    “He hoped it was an augury.” “It was a sign from God.”

    Synonyms: augury, foretoken, preindication · A kind of: experience · Kinds: war cloud, omen

  9. A gesture that is part of a sign language.

    A kind of: gesture

  10. A fundamental linguistic unit linking a signifier to that which is signified.

    “The bond between the signifier and the signified is arbitrary.”

    A kind of: language unit

  11. A character indicating a relation between quantities.

    “Don't forget the minus sign.”

    A kind of: mathematical notation · Kinds: equal sign, plus sign, minus sign, radical sign

Sign as an adjective

  1. Used of the language of the deaf.

    Synonyms: gestural, signed, sign-language · Similar: communicative

Phrases and compounds with "sign"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • v sign (noun) A sign (for victory); making a V with the index and middle fingers.
  • co-sign (verb) Sign jointly.
  • sign in (verb) Announce one's arrival, e.g. at hotels or airports.
  • sign on (verb) Engage by written agreement.
  • sign up (verb) Engage by written agreement.
  • sign off (verb) Cease broadcasting; get off the air; as of radio stations.
  • high sign (noun) A silent signal of warning or recognition.
  • plus sign (noun) A sign indicating the operation of addition.
  • sign away (verb) Formally assign ownership of.
  • sign over (verb) Formally assign ownership of.
  • star sign (noun) (astrology) one of 12 equal areas into which the zodiac is divided.
  • equal sign (noun) A sign indicating that the quantities on either side are equal.
  • minus sign (noun) A sign indicating the operation of subtraction.
  • pound sign (noun) A symbol for a unit of currency (especially for the pound sterling in Great Britain).
  • vital sign (noun) Sign of life; usually an indicator of a person's general physical condition.
  • dollar sign (noun) A symbol of commercialism or greed.
