Silver: meaning, synonyms and examples

Silver is used as an adjective (4 senses), noun (5 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Made from or largely consisting of silver.

In Hindi
चाँदी
Synonyms
silvern, silvery, argent, silverish, eloquent, facile, fluent, silver-tongued, smooth-spoken, ash grey
Word family
silvery

Silver as an adjective

  1. Made from or largely consisting of silver.

    “Silver bracelets.”

    Similar: metallic

  2. Having the white lustrous sheen of silver.

    “A land of silver (or silvern) rivers where the salmon leap.” “Repeated scrubbings have given the wood a silvery sheen.”

    Synonyms: silvern, silvery · Similar: bright

  3. Of lustrous grey; covered with or tinged with the color of silver.

    “Silvery hair.”

    Synonyms: argent, silvery, silverish · Similar: achromatic

  4. Expressing yourself readily, clearly, effectively.

    “Able to dazzle with his facile tongue.” “Silver speech.”

    Synonyms: eloquent, facile, fluent, silver-tongued, smooth-spoken · Similar: articulate

Silver as a noun

  1. A soft white precious univalent metallic element having the highest electrical and thermal conductivity of any metal; occurs in argentite and in free form; used in coins and jewelry and tableware and photography.

    A kind of: noble metal, conductor · Kinds: coin silver

  2. Coins made of silver.

    A kind of: precious metal

  3. A light shade of grey.

    Synonyms: ash grey, ash gray, silver grey, silver gray · A kind of: gray

  4. Silverware eating utensils.

    Synonyms: flatware · A kind of: silverware

  5. A trophy made of silver (or having the appearance of silver) that is usually awarded for winning second place in a competition.

    Synonyms: silver medal · A kind of: trophy

Silver as a verb

  1. Coat with a layer of silver or a silver amalgam.

    “Silver the necklace.”

    A kind of: plate

  2. Make silver in color.

    “Her worries had silvered her hair.”

    A kind of: color

  3. Turn silver.

    “The man's hair silvered very attractively.”

    A kind of: discolor

Phrases and compounds with "silver"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • silver age (noun) (classical mythology) the second age of the world, characterized by opulence and irreligion; by extension, a period secondary in achievement to a golden age.
  • silver ash (noun) Any of various timber trees of the genus Flindersia.
  • silver fir (noun) Any of various true firs having leaves white or silvery white beneath.
  • silver fox (noun) Red fox in the color phase when its pelt is tipped with white.
  • silver oak (noun) Small slender tree with usually entire grey-green pendulous leaves and white or cream-colored flowers; northern Australia.
  • silver-tip (noun) Powerful brownish-yellow bear of the uplands of western North America.
  • coin silver (noun) A silver of the degree of purity established for making legal silver coins.
  • silver bell (noun) Any of various deciduous trees of the genus Halesia having white bell-shaped flowers.
  • silver city (noun) A town in southwestern New Mexico.
  • silver cord (noun) The emotional bond between a mother and her offspring.
  • silver fern (noun) Tropical American fern having fronds with white undersides.
  • silver gray (noun) A light shade of grey.
  • silver grey (noun) A light shade of grey.
  • silver hake (noun) Found off Atlantic coast of North America.
  • silver lace (noun) Shrubby perennial of the Canary Islands having white flowers and leaves and hairy stems covered with dustlike down; sometimes placed in genus Chrysanthemum.
  • silver lime (noun) Large tree native to eastern Europe and Asia Minor having leaves with white tomentum on the under side; widely cultivated as an ornamental.
