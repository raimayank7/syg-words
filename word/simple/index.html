Simple: meaning, synonyms and examples

Simple is used as an adjective (7 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Having few parts; not complex or complicated or involved.

In Hindi
सरल, सादा
Synonyms
elementary, uncomplicated, unproblematic, bare, mere, childlike, wide-eyed, round-eyed, dewy-eyed, dim-witted
Opposites
complex, compound
Word family
simpleness, simplicity

Simple as an adjective

  1. Having few parts; not complex or complicated or involved.

    “A simple problem.” “Simple mechanisms.” “A simple design.”

    Opposite: complex · Similar: simplex, simplistic, unanalyzable, uncomplicated

  2. Easy and not involved or complicated.

    “An elementary problem in statistics.” “Elementary, my dear Watson.” “A simple game.”

    Synonyms: elementary, uncomplicated, unproblematic · Similar: easy

  3. Apart from anything else; without additions or modifications.

    “Only the bare facts.” “Shocked by the mere idea.” “The simple passage of time was enough.”

    Synonyms: bare, mere · Similar: plain

  4. Exhibiting childlike simplicity and credulity.

    “Childlike trust.” “Dewy-eyed innocence.” “Listened in round-eyed wonder.”

    Synonyms: childlike, wide-eyed, round-eyed, dewy-eyed · Similar: naive

  5. Lacking mental capacity and subtlety.

    Synonyms: dim-witted, simple-minded · Similar: retarded

  6. (botany) of leaf shapes; of leaves having no divisions or subdivisions.

    Synonyms: unsubdivided · Opposite: compound · Similar: acerate, acuminate, apiculate, caudate, cordate, cuneate

  7. Unornamented.

    “A simple country schoolhouse.” “Her black dress--simple to austerity.”

    Similar: plain

Simple as a noun

  1. Any herbaceous plant having medicinal properties.

    A kind of: herb

  2. A person lacking intelligence or common sense.

    Synonyms: simpleton · A kind of: person · Kinds: airhead, dimwit, dingbat, dumbbell, fool, idiot, idiot savant, nebbish

Phrases and compounds with "simple"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fee simple (noun) A fee without limitation to any class of heirs; they can sell it or give it away.
  • simple eye (noun) An eye having a single lens.
  • simple leaf (noun) A leaf that is not divided into parts.
  • simple fruit (noun) An indehiscent fruit derived from a single ovary having one or many seeds within a fleshy wall or pericarp: e.g. grape; tomato; cranberry.
  • simple sugar (noun) A sugar (like sucrose or fructose) that does not hydrolyse to give other sugars; the simplest group of carbohydrates.
  • simple phobia (noun) Any phobia (other than agoraphobia) associated with relatively simple well-defined stimuli.
  • simple pistil (noun) Consists of one carpel.
  • simple-minded (adjective) Lacking subtlety and insight.
  • simple absence (noun) An absence seizure without other complications; followed by 3-per-sec brainwave spikes.
  • simple machine (noun) A device for overcoming resistance at one point by applying force at some other point.
  • simple protein (noun) A protein that yields only amino acids when hydrolyzed.
  • simple fraction (noun) The quotient of two integers.
  • simple fracture (noun) An uncomplicated fracture in which the broken bones to not pierce the skin.
  • simple interest (noun) Interest paid on the principal alone.
  • simple pendulum (noun) A hypothetical pendulum suspended by a weightless frictionless thread of constant length.
  • simple sentence (noun) A sentence having no coordinate clauses or subordinate clauses.
