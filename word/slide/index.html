Slide: meaning, synonyms and examples

Slide is used as a verb (3 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move obliquely or sideways, usually in an uncontrolled manner.

In Hindi
फिसलना
Synonyms
skid, slip, slue, slew, slither, microscope slide, swoop, playground slide, sliding board, glide
Word family
slider

Slide as a verb

  1. Move obliquely or sideways, usually in an uncontrolled manner.

    “The wheels skidded against the sidewalk.”

    Synonyms: skid, slip, slue, slew · A kind of: glide · Kinds: submarine, skid, side-slip

  2. To pass or move unobtrusively or smoothly.

    “They slid through the wicket in the big gate.”

    Synonyms: slither · A kind of: travel

  3. Move smoothly along a surface.

    A kind of: move

Slide as a noun

  1. A small flat rectangular piece of glass on which specimens can be mounted for microscopic study.

    Synonyms: microscope slide · A kind of: plate glass

  2. (geology) the descent of a large mass of earth or rocks or snow etc.

    A kind of: descent · Kinds: avalanche, landslide

  3. (music) rapid sliding up or down the musical scale.

    “The violinist was indulgent with his swoops and slides.”

    Synonyms: swoop · A kind of: glissando

  4. Plaything consisting of a sloping chute down which children can slide.

    Synonyms: playground slide, sliding board · A kind of: plaything

  5. The act of moving smoothly along a surface while remaining in contact with it.

    “His slide didn't stop until the bottom of the hill.” “The children lined up for a coast down the snowy slope.”

    Synonyms: glide, coast · A kind of: motion · Kinds: skid, snowboarding

  6. A transparency mounted in a frame; viewed with a slide projector.

    Synonyms: lantern slide · A kind of: foil

  7. Sloping channel through which things can descend.

    Synonyms: chute, slideway, sloping trough · A kind of: gutter · Kinds: coal chute, runway, water chute

  • slide action (noun) Action mechanism in a modern rifle or shotgun; a back and forward motion of a sliding lever ejects the empty shell case and cocks the firearm and loads a new round.

  • slide fastener (noun) A fastener for locking together two toothed edges by means of a sliding tab.
  • slide projector (noun) Projector that projects an enlarged image of a slide onto a screen.
  • microscope slide (noun) A small flat rectangular piece of glass on which specimens can be mounted for microscopic study.
  • playground slide (noun) Plaything consisting of a sloping chute down which children can slide.
