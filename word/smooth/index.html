Smooth: meaning, synonyms and examples

Smooth is used as an adjective (8 senses), verb (3 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Having a surface free from roughness or bumps or ridges or irregularities.

In Hindi
चिकना
Synonyms
politic, suave, bland, fluent, fluid, liquid, legato, placid, quiet, still
Opposites
rough, roughen
Word family
smoothness, smoother

Smooth as an adjective

  1. Having a surface free from roughness or bumps or ridges or irregularities.

    “Smooth skin.” “A smooth tabletop.” “Smooth fabric.”

    Opposite: rough · Similar: creaseless, even-textured, fast, fine-textured, glassy, seamless

  2. Smoothly agreeable and courteous with a degree of sophistication.

    “He was too politic to quarrel with so important a personage.” “The manager pacified the customer with a smooth apology for the error.”

    Synonyms: politic, suave, bland · Similar: diplomatic

  3. Of the margin of a leaf shape; not broken up into teeth.

    Opposite: rough · Similar: entire, repand, sinuate, undulate, unnotched

  4. Smooth and unconstrained in movement.

    “A long, smooth stride.” “The fluid motion of a cat.” “The liquid grace of a ballerina.”

    Synonyms: fluent, fluid, liquid · Similar: graceful

  5. (music) without breaks between notes; smooth and connected.

    “A legato passage.”

    Synonyms: legato

  6. Of motion that runs or flows or proceeds without jolts or turbulence.

    “A smooth ride.”

    Opposite: rough

  7. Lacking obstructions or difficulties.

    “The bill's path through the legislature was smooth and orderly.”

    Similar: easy

  8. (of a body of water) free from disturbance by heavy waves.

    “A ribbon of sand between the angry sea and the placid bay.” “The quiet waters of a lagoon.” “A lake of tranquil blue water reflecting a tranquil blue sky.”

    Synonyms: placid, quiet, still, tranquil, unruffled · Similar: calm

Smooth as a verb

  1. Make smooth or smoother, as if by rubbing.

    “Smooth the surface of the wood.”

    Synonyms: smoothen · Opposite: roughen · A kind of: change surface · Kinds: strip, launch, rake, plane, float, sandpaper, sandblast, file

  2. Make (a surface) shine.

    “Shine the silver, please.” “Polish my shoes.”

    Synonyms: polish, smoothen, shine · A kind of: fancify · Kinds: slick, buff, gloss

  3. Free from obstructions.

    “Smooth the way towards peace negotiations.”

    Synonyms: smooth out · A kind of: rid

Smooth as a noun

  1. The act of smoothing.

    “He gave his hair a quick smooth.”

    A kind of: accomplishment

Phrases and compounds with "smooth"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • smooth out (verb) Free from obstructions.
  • smooth over (verb) Treat hurriedly or avoid dealing with properly.
  • smooth alder (noun) Common shrub of the eastern United States with smooth bark.
  • smooth aster (noun) A variety of aster.
  • smooth plane (noun) A small plane for finish work.
  • smooth sumac (noun) Common nonpoisonous shrub of eastern North America with waxy compound leaves and green paniculate flowers followed by red berries.
  • smooth-faced (adjective) Lacking hair on the face.
  • smooth muscle (noun) A muscle that contracts without conscious control and found in walls of internal organs such as stomach and intestine and bladder and blood vessels (excluding the heart).
  • smooth-bodied (adjective) Having a smooth body.
  • smooth-haired (adjective) Having hair that feels smooth.
  • smooth-shaven (adjective) Closely shaved recently.
  • smooth-spoken (adjective) Expressing yourself readily, clearly, effectively.
  • smooth dogfish (noun) Small bottom-dwelling shark found along both Atlantic coasts.
  • smooth woodsia (noun) Rock-inhabiting fern of Arctic and subarctic Europe to eastern Asia.
  • smooth-shelled (adjective) Having a smooth shell.
  • smooth-skinned (adjective) Having smooth skin.
