Spin: meaning, synonyms and examples

Spin is used as a verb (8 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Revolve quickly and repeatedly around one's own axis.

In Hindi
घूमना; कातना
Synonyms
spin around, whirl, reel, gyrate, birl, twirl, spin out, twist, twisting, tailspin
Word family
spinner, spinning

Spin as a verb

  1. Revolve quickly and repeatedly around one's own axis.

    “The dervishes whirl around and around without getting dizzy.”

    Synonyms: spin around, whirl, reel, gyrate · A kind of: revolve · Kinds: whirligig

  2. Stream in jets, of liquids.

    “The creek spun its course through the woods.”

    A kind of: stream · Kinds: centrifuge

  3. Cause to spin.

    “Spin a coin.”

    Synonyms: whirl, birl, twirl · A kind of: rotate

  4. Make up a story.

    “Spin a yarn.”

    A kind of: fabricate

  5. Form a web by making a thread.

    “Spiders spin a fine web.”

    A kind of: create from raw material

  6. Work natural fibers into a thread.

    “Spin silk.”

    A kind of: twist

  7. Twist and turn so as to give an intended interpretation.

    “The President's spokesmen had to spin the story to make it less embarrassing.”

    A kind of: present · Kinds: sugarcoat

  8. Prolong or extend.

    “Spin out a visit.”

    Synonyms: spin out · A kind of: prolong

Spin as a noun

  1. A swift whirling motion (usually of a missile).

    A kind of: rotation · Kinds: backspin, topspin

  2. The act of rotating rapidly.

    “He gave the crank a spin.” “It broke off after much twisting.”

    Synonyms: twirl, twist, twisting, whirl · A kind of: rotation · Kinds: pirouette, birling

  3. A short drive in a car.

    “He took the new car for a spin.”

    A kind of: drive

  4. Rapid descent of an aircraft in a steep spiral.

    Synonyms: tailspin · A kind of: acrobatics

  5. A distinctive interpretation (especially as used by politicians to sway public opinion).

    “The campaign put a favorable spin on the story.”

    A kind of: rendition

  • spin-off (noun) A product made during the manufacture of something else.
  • spin drier (noun) A clothes dryer that uses centrifugal motion to dry the clothes that are put into it.

  • spin around (verb) Revolve quickly and repeatedly around one's own axis.
  • spin doctor (noun) A public relations person who tries to forestall negative publicity by publicizing a favorable interpretation of the words or actions of a company or political party or famous person.
  • spin the plate (noun) A game in which something round (as a plate) is spun on edge and the name of a player is called; the named player must catch the spinning object before it falls or pay a forfeit.
  • spin the bottle (noun) A game in which a player spins a bottle and kisses the person that it points to when it stops spinning.
  • spin the platter (noun) A game in which something round (as a plate) is spun on edge and the name of a player is called; the named player must catch the spinning object before it falls or pay a forfeit.
  • electron spin resonance (noun) Microwave spectroscopy in which there is resonant absorption of radiation by a paramagnet.
