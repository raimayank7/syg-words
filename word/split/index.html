Split: meaning, synonyms and examples

Split is used as a verb (5 senses), noun (11 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Separate into parts or portions.

In Hindi
चीरना, बाँटना
Synonyms
divide, split up, separate, dissever, carve up, cleave, rive, part, break, break up
Word family
splitter

Split as a verb

  1. Separate into parts or portions.

    “Divide the cake into three equal parts.” “The British carved up the Ottoman Empire after World War I.”

    Synonyms: divide, split up, separate, dissever, carve up · A kind of: change integrity · Kinds: subdivide, format, sectionalize, triangulate, unitize, lot, parcel, sliver

  2. Separate or cut with a tool, such as a sharp instrument.

    “Cleave the bone.”

    Synonyms: cleave, rive · A kind of: tear · Kinds: maul, laminate

  3. Discontinue an association or relation; go different ways.

    “The business partners broke over a tax question.” “The couple separated after 25 years of marriage.” “My friend and I split up.”

    Synonyms: separate, part, split up, break, break up · Kinds: give the axe, disunify, disassociate, break with, divorce, secede, break

  4. Go one's own way; move apart.

    “The friends separated after the party.”

    Synonyms: separate, part · A kind of: move · Kinds: disperse, break up, diffract

  5. Come open suddenly and violently, as if from internal pressure.

    “The bubble burst.”

    Synonyms: burst, break open · A kind of: break · Kinds: pop, blow, stave

Split as a noun

  1. Extending the legs at right angles to the trunk (one in front and the other in back).

    A kind of: acrobatic stunt

  2. A bottle containing half the usual amount.

    A kind of: bottle

  3. A promised or claimed share of loot or money.

    “He demanded his split before they disbanded.”

    A kind of: share

  4. A lengthwise crack in wood.

    “He inserted the wedge into a split in the log.”

    A kind of: crack

  5. An opening made forcibly as by pulling apart.

    “There was a rip in his pants.” “She had snags in her stockings.”

    Synonyms: rip, rent, snag, tear · A kind of: opening

  6. An old Croatian city on the Adriatic Sea.

  7. A dessert of sliced fruit and ice cream covered with whipped cream and cherries and nuts.

    A kind of: frozen dessert · Kinds: banana split

  8. (tenpin bowling) a divided formation of pins left standing after the first bowl.

    “He was winning until he got a split in the tenth frame.”

    A kind of: formation

  9. An increase in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.

    “They announced a two-for-one split of the common stock.”

    Synonyms: stock split, split up · A kind of: increase

  10. The act of rending or ripping or splitting something.

    “He gave the envelope a vigorous rip.”

    Synonyms: rent, rip · A kind of: tear

  11. Division of a group into opposing factions.

    “Another schism like that and they will wind up in bankruptcy.”

    Synonyms: schism · A kind of: division

Split as an adjective

  1. Having been divided; having the unity destroyed.

    “Congress...gave the impression of...a confusing sum of disconnected local forces.” “A league of disunited nations.” “A fragmented coalition.”

    Synonyms: disconnected, disunited, fragmented · Similar: divided

  2. (especially of wood) cut or ripped longitudinally with the grain.

    “We bought split logs for the fireplace.”

    Similar: cut

Phrases and compounds with "split"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • split up (noun) An increase in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.
  • split run (noun) A print run of a newspaper during which some articles or advertisements are changed to produce a different edition.
  • split-pea (noun) Dried hulled pea; used in soup.
  • split down (noun) A decrease in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.
  • split rail (noun) A rail that is split from a log.
  • split shift (noun) A working shift divided into two periods of time with several hours in between.
  • stock split (noun) An increase in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.
  • banana split (noun) A banana split lengthwise and topped with scoops of ice cream and sauces and nuts and whipped cream.
  • split second (noun) A very short time (as the time it takes the eye to blink or the heart to beat).
  • split ticket (noun) A ballot cast by a voter who votes for candidates from more than one party.
  • lickety split (adverb) Without delay.
  • reverse split (noun) A decrease in the number of outstanding shares of a corporation without changing the shareholders' equity.
  • split decision (noun) A boxing decision in which the judges are not unanimous.
  • split-pea soup (noun) Made of stock and split peas with onions carrots and celery.
  • split infinitive (noun) An infinitive with an adverb between `to' and the verb (e.g., `to boldly go').
  • split personality (noun) A relatively rare dissociative disorder in which the usual integrity of the personality breaks down and two or more independent personalities emerge.
