Square: meaning, synonyms and examples

Square is used as a noun (8 senses), verb (8 senses), adjective (6 senses) and adverb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: (geometry) a plane rectangle with four equal sides and four right angles; a four-sided regular polygon.

In Hindi
वर्ग; चौक
Synonyms
foursquare, second power, public square, lame, square toes, square up, feather, straight, hearty, satisfying
Opposites
round
Word family
squareness

Square as a noun

  1. (geometry) a plane rectangle with four equal sides and four right angles; a four-sided regular polygon.

    “You can compute the area of a square if you know the length of its sides.”

    Synonyms: foursquare · A kind of: rectangle, regular polygon · Kinds: quadrate

  2. The product of two equal terms.

    “Nine is the second power of three.” “Gravity is inversely proportional to the square of the distance.”

    Synonyms: second power · A kind of: number

  3. An open area at the meeting of two or more streets.

    Synonyms: public square · A kind of: tract · Kinds: plaza

  4. Something approximating the shape of a square.

    A kind of: shape

  5. Someone who doesn't understand what is going on.

    Synonyms: lame · A kind of: simpleton

  6. A formal and conservative person with old-fashioned views.

    Synonyms: square toes · A kind of: conservative

  7. Any artifact having a shape similar to a plane geometric figure with four equal sides and four right angles.

    “A checkerboard has 64 squares.”

    A kind of: artifact

  8. A hand tool consisting of two straight arms at right angles; used to construct or test right angles.

    “The carpenter who built this room must have lost his square.”

    A kind of: hand tool · Kinds: carpenter's square, try square

Square as a verb

  1. Make square.

    “Square the circle.” “Square the wood with a file.”

    Synonyms: square up · A kind of: shape

  2. Raise to the second power.

    A kind of: multiply

  3. Cause to match, as of ideas or acts.

    A kind of: adjust

  4. Position so as to be square.

    “He squared his shoulders.”

    A kind of: position · Kinds: square up

  5. Be compatible with.

    “One idea squares with another.”

    A kind of: match

  6. Pay someone and settle a debt.

    “I squared with him.”

    A kind of: settle

  7. Turn the paddle; in canoeing.

    Synonyms: feather · A kind of: paddle

  8. Turn the oar, while rowing.

    Synonyms: feather · A kind of: row

Square as an adjective

  1. Having four equal sides and four right angles or forming a right angle.

    “A square peg in a round hole.” “A square corner.”

    Opposite: round · Similar: quadrate, right-angled, squared, squarish

  2. Characterized by honesty and fairness.

    “Straight dealing.” “A square deal.”

    Synonyms: straight · Similar: aboveboard, guileless, straightarrow

  3. Providing abundant nourishment.

    “A hearty meal.” “Good solid food.” “Ate a substantial breakfast.”

    Synonyms: hearty, satisfying, solid, substantial · Similar: wholesome

  4. Leaving no balance.

    “My account with you is now all square.”

    Similar: paid

  5. Without evasion or compromise.

    “A square contradiction.” “He is not being as straightforward as it appears.”

    Synonyms: straightforward, straight · Similar: direct

  6. Rigidly conventional or old-fashioned.

    Synonyms: straight · Similar: conventional

Square as an adverb

  1. In a straight direct way.

    “Looked him squarely in the eye.” “Ran square into me.”

    Synonyms: squarely

  2. In a square shape.

    “A squarely cut piece of paper.” “Folded the sheet of paper square.”

    Synonyms: squarely

  3. Firmly and solidly.

    “Hit the ball squarely.” “The bat met the ball squarely.” “Planted his great bulk square before his enemy.”

    Synonyms: squarely
