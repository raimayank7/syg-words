Stamp: meaning, synonyms and examples

Stamp is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Walk heavily.

In Hindi
मुहर; डाक टिकट
Synonyms
stomp, stump, pigeonhole, stereotype, emboss, boss, cast, mold, mould, impression
Word family
stamper

Stamp as a verb

  1. Walk heavily.

    “The men stomped through the snow in their heavy boots.”

    Synonyms: stomp, stump · A kind of: walk

  2. To mark, or produce an imprint in or on something.

    “A man whose name is permanently stamped on our maps.”

    A kind of: impress · Kinds: date, rubberstamp, postmark

  3. Reveal clearly as having a certain character.

    “His playing stamps him as a Romantic.”

    A kind of: qualify

  4. Affix a stamp to.

    “Are the letters properly stamped?”

    A kind of: affix · Kinds: meter

  5. Treat or classify according to a mental stereotype.

    “I was stereotyped as a lazy Southern European.”

    Synonyms: pigeonhole, stereotype · A kind of: classify

  6. Destroy or extinguish as if by stamping with the foot.

    “Stamp fascism into submission.” “Stamp out tyranny.”

  7. Form or cut out with a mold, form, or die.

    “Stamp needles.”

    A kind of: shape

  8. Crush or grind with a heavy instrument.

    “Stamp fruit extract the juice.”

    A kind of: squash

  9. Raise in a relief.

    “Embossed stationery.”

    Synonyms: emboss, boss · A kind of: impress · Kinds: block

Stamp as a noun

  1. The distinctive form in which a thing is made.

    “Pottery of this cast was found throughout the region.”

    Synonyms: cast, mold, mould · A kind of: solid

  2. A type or class.

    “More men of his stamp are needed.”

    A kind of: class

  3. A symbol that is the result of printing or engraving.

    “He put his stamp on the envelope.”

    Synonyms: impression · A kind of: symbol · Kinds: imprint, seal

  4. A small adhesive token stuck on a letter or package to indicate that that postal fees have been paid.

    Synonyms: postage, postage stamp · A kind of: token

  5. Something that can be used as an official medium of payment.

    Synonyms: tender, legal tender · A kind of: medium of exchange · Kinds: food stamp

  6. A small piece of adhesive paper that is put on an object to show that a government tax has been paid.

    Synonyms: revenue stamp · A kind of: sheet

  7. Machine consisting of a heavy bar that moves vertically for pounding or crushing ores.

    Synonyms: pestle · A kind of: machine

  8. A block or die used to imprint a mark or design.

    A kind of: die

  9. A device incised to make an impression; used to secure a closing or to authenticate documents.

    Synonyms: seal · A kind of: device · Kinds: bulla, cachet, great seal, handstamp, signet

Phrases and compounds with "stamp"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • stamp act (noun) An act passed by the British Parliament in 1756 that raised revenue from the American Colonies by a duty in the form of a stamp required on all newspapers and legal or commercial documents; opposition by the Colonies resulted in the repeal of the act in 1766.
  • stamp out (verb) End or extinguish by forceful means.
  • stamp pad (noun) A block of absorbent material saturated with ink; used to transfer ink evenly to a rubber stamp.
  • stamp tax (noun) A tax collected by requiring a stamp to be purchased and attached (usually on documents or publications).
  • date stamp (verb) Stamp with a date.
  • food stamp (noun) A government-issued stamp that can be used in exchange for food.
  • stamp down (verb) To put down by force or authority.
  • stamp duty (noun) A tax collected by requiring a stamp to be purchased and attached (usually on documents or publications).
  • stamp mill (noun) A mill in which ore is crushed with stamps.
  • stamp album (noun) An album for stamps.
  • rubber stamp (noun) A stamp (usually made of rubber) for imprinting a mark or design by hand.
  • stamp dealer (noun) A dealer in stamps (whose customers are stamp collectors).
  • postage stamp (noun) A small adhesive token stuck on a letter or package to indicate that that postal fees have been paid.
  • revenue stamp (noun) A small piece of adhesive paper that is put on an object to show that a government tax has been paid.
  • stamp battery (noun) A series of stamps operated in one mortar for crushing ores.
  • trading stamp (noun) A token resembling a stamp given by a retailer to a buyer; the token is redeemable for articles on a special list.
