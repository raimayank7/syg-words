Star: meaning, synonyms and examples

Star is used as a noun (8 senses), verb (3 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: (astronomy) a celestial body of hot gases that radiates energy derived from thermonuclear reactions in the interior.

In Hindi
तारा; सितारा
Synonyms
ace, adept, champion, sensation, maven, mavin, virtuoso, genius, hotshot, superstar
Word family
starry, stardom

Star as a noun

  1. (astronomy) a celestial body of hot gases that radiates energy derived from thermonuclear reactions in the interior.

    A kind of: celestial body · Kinds: binary star, fixed star, giant star, lodestar, multiple star, neutron star, nova, red dwarf

  2. Someone who is dazzlingly skilled in any field.

    Synonyms: ace, adept, champion, sensation, maven, mavin, virtuoso, genius · A kind of: expert · Kinds: track star

  3. Any celestial body visible (as a point of light) from the Earth at night.

    A kind of: celestial body · Kinds: starlet

  4. An actor who plays a principal role.

    Synonyms: principal, lead · A kind of: actor · Kinds: co-star, film star, idol, television star

  5. A plane figure with 5 or more points; often used as an emblem.

    A kind of: plane figure · Kinds: hexagram, asterism, pentacle

  6. A performer who receives prominent billing.

    Synonyms: headliner · A kind of: performer

  7. A star-shaped character * used in printing.

    Synonyms: asterisk · A kind of: character

  8. The topology of a network whose components are connected to a hub.

    Synonyms: star topology · A kind of: topology

Star as a verb

  1. Feature as the star.

    “The movie stars Dustin Hoffman as an autistic man.”

    A kind of: have · Kinds: co-star

  2. Be the star in a performance.

    A kind of: perform · Kinds: co-star

  3. Mark with an asterisk.

    “Linguists star unacceptable sentences.”

    Synonyms: asterisk · A kind of: mark

Star as an adjective

  1. Indicating the most important performer or role.

    “The leading man.” “Prima ballerina.” “Prima donna.”

    Synonyms: leading, prima, starring, stellar · Similar: major

Phrases and compounds with "star"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • co-star (noun) One of two actors who are given equal status as stars in a play or film.
  • tv star (noun) A star in a television show.
  • bog star (noun) Plant having ovate leaves in a basal rosette and white starlike flowers netted with green.
  • dog star (noun) The brightest star in the sky; in Canis Major.
  • sea star (noun) Echinoderms characterized by five arms extending from a central disk.
  • blue star (noun) Subshrubs of southeastern United States forming slow-growing clumps and having blue flowers in short terminal cymes.
  • film star (noun) A star who plays leading roles in the cinema.
  • pole star (noun) The brightest star in Ursa Minor; at the end of the handle of the Little Dipper; the northern axis of the earth points toward it.
  • rock star (noun) A famous singer of rock music.
  • star sign (noun) (astrology) one of 12 equal areas into which the zodiac is divided.
  • fixed star (noun) Any star in the Ptolemaic theory of planetary motion.
  • flare star (noun) A red dwarf star in which luminosity can change several magnitudes in a few minutes.
  • giant star (noun) A very bright star of large diameter and low density (relative to the Sun).
  • movie star (noun) A star who plays leading roles in the cinema.
  • north star (noun) The brightest star in Ursa Minor; at the end of the handle of the Little Dipper; the northern axis of the earth points toward it.
  • opera star (noun) Singer of lead role in an opera.
