Step: meaning, synonyms and examples

Step is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any maneuver made as part of progress toward a goal.

In Hindi
कदम; सीढ़ी
Synonyms
measure, footstep, pace, stride, stair, gradation, stone's throw, footfall, tone, whole tone

Step as a noun

  1. Any maneuver made as part of progress toward a goal.

    “The situation called for strong measures.” “The police took steps to reduce crime.”

    Synonyms: measure · A kind of: maneuver · Kinds: countermeasure, shark repellent, precaution

  2. The distance covered by a step.

    “He stepped off ten paces from the old tree and began to dig.”

    Synonyms: footstep, pace, stride · A kind of: indefinite quantity

  3. The act of changing location by raising the foot and setting it down.

    “He walked with unsteady steps.”

    A kind of: locomotion · Kinds: pace, pas, trip, sidestep, footstep

  4. Support consisting of a place to rest the foot while ascending or descending a stairway.

    “He paused on the bottom step.”

    Synonyms: stair · A kind of: support · Kinds: corbel step

  5. Relative position in a graded series.

    “Always a step behind.” “Subtle gradations in color.” “Keep in step with the fashions.”

    Synonyms: gradation · A kind of: rank · Kinds: cut

  6. A short distance.

    “It's only a step to the drugstore.”

    Synonyms: stone's throw · A kind of: small indefinite quantity

  7. The sound of a step of someone walking.

    “He heard footsteps on the porch.”

    Synonyms: footfall, footstep · A kind of: sound · Kinds: tramp

  8. A musical interval of two semitones.

    Synonyms: tone, whole tone, whole step · A kind of: interval

  9. A mark of a foot or shoe on a surface.

    “The police made casts of the footprints in the soft earth outside the window.”

    Synonyms: footprint, footmark · A kind of: mark · Kinds: footprint evidence

  10. A solid block joined to the beams in which the heel of a ship's mast or capstan is fixed.

    A kind of: block

  11. A sequence of foot movements that make up a particular dance.

    “He taught them the waltz step.”

    Synonyms: dance step · A kind of: locomotion · Kinds: moonwalk, chasse, glissade

Step as a verb

  1. Shift or move by taking a step.

    “Step back.”

    A kind of: travel · Kinds: backpedal

  2. Put down or press the foot, place the foot.

    “For fools rush in where angels fear to tread.” “Step on the brake.”

    Synonyms: tread · A kind of: travel · Kinds: step on

  3. Cause (a computer) to execute a single command.

    A kind of: run

  4. Treat badly.

    “This boss abuses his workers.” “She is always stepping on others to get ahead.”

    Synonyms: mistreat, maltreat, abuse, ill-use, ill-treat · A kind of: treat · Kinds: kick around

  5. Furnish with steps.

    “The architect wants to step the terrace.”

    A kind of: supply

  6. Move with one's feet in a specific manner.

    “Step lively.”

    A kind of: travel

  7. Walk a short distance to a specified place or in a specified manner.

    “Step over to the blackboard.”

    A kind of: walk

  8. Place (a ship's mast) in its step.

    A kind of: put

  9. Measure (distances) by pacing.

    “Step off ten yards.”

    Synonyms: pace · A kind of: quantify

  10. Move or proceed as if by steps into a new situation.

    “She stepped into a life of luxury.” “He won't step into his father's footsteps.”

    A kind of: move

Phrases and compounds with "step"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • step in (verb) Get involved, so as to alter or hinder an action, or through force or threat of force.
  • step on (verb) Place or press the foot on.
  • step up (verb) Increase in extent or intensity.
  • step-in (noun) Short underpants for women or children (usually used in the plural).
  • step-up (noun) The act of increasing something.
  • one-step (noun) An early ballroom dance; precursor to the fox-trot.
  • step out (verb) Go outside a room or building for a short period of time.
  • two-step (noun) A ballroom dance in duple meter; marked by sliding steps.
  • crow step (noun) (architecture) a step on the top of a gable wall.
  • half step (noun) The musical interval between adjacent keys on a keyboard instrument.
  • keep step (verb) Maintain the same pace.
  • step down (verb) Give up or retire from a position.
  • step-down (noun) The act of decreasing or reducing something.
  • dance step (noun) A sequence of foot movements that make up a particular dance.
  • first step (noun) The first of a series of actions.
  • goose step (noun) A manner of marching with legs straight and swinging high.
