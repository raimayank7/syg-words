Strength: meaning, synonyms and examples

Strength is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: The property of being physically or mentally strong.

In Hindi
ताक़त, शक्ति
Synonyms
military capability, military strength, military posture, posture, force, forcefulness, forte, strong suit, long suit, metier
Opposites
weakness

Strength as a noun

  1. The property of being physically or mentally strong.

    “Fatigue sapped his strength.”

    Opposite: weakness · A kind of: property · Kinds: good part, brawn, might, vigor, robustness, huskiness, stoutness, sturdiness

  2. Capability in terms of personnel and materiel that affect the capacity to fight a war.

    “We faced an army of great strength.” “Politicians have neglected our military posture.”

    Synonyms: military capability, military strength, military posture, posture · A kind of: capability · Kinds: sea power, firepower

  3. Physical energy or intensity.

    “He hit with all the force he could muster.” “It was destroyed by the strength of the gale.” “A government has not the vitality and forcefulness of a living man.”

    Synonyms: force, forcefulness · A kind of: intensity · Kinds: brunt, momentum, energy

  4. An asset of special worth or utility.

    “Cooking is his forte.”

    Synonyms: forte, strong suit, long suit, metier, specialty, speciality, strong point · A kind of: asset · Kinds: green thumb

  5. The power to induce the taking of a course of action or the embracing of a point of view by means of argument or entreaty.

    “The strength of his argument settled the matter.”

    Synonyms: persuasiveness · A kind of: power · Kinds: convincingness

  6. The amount of energy transmitted (as by acoustic or electromagnetic radiation).

    “He adjusted the intensity of the sound.” “They measured the station's signal strength.”

    Synonyms: intensity, intensity level · A kind of: magnitude · Kinds: radio brightness, threshold level, field strength, candlepower, acoustic power, half-intensity

  7. Capacity to produce strong physiological or chemical effects.

    “The toxin's potency.” “The strength of the drinks.”

    Synonyms: potency, effectiveness · A kind of: power

  8. The condition of financial success.

    “The strength of the company's stock in recent weeks.”

    Opposite: weakness · A kind of: prosperity

  9. Permanence by virtue of the power to resist stress or force.

    “They advertised the durability of their products.”

    Synonyms: lastingness, durability, enduringness · A kind of: permanence · Kinds: tensile strength, indestructibility, continuity, changelessness, everlastingness

Phrases and compounds with "strength"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • full-strength (adjective) Without water.
  • field strength (noun) The vector sum of all the forces exerted by an electrical or magnetic field (on a unit mass or unit charge or unit magnetic pole) at a given point in the field.
  • tensile strength (noun) The strength of material expressed as the greatest longitudinal stress it can bear without tearing apart.
  • military strength (noun) Capability in terms of personnel and materiel that affect the capacity to fight a war.
  • tower of strength (noun) A person who can be relied on to give a great deal of support and comfort.
  • pillar of strength (noun) A person who can be relied on to give a great deal of support and comfort.
  • field strength unit (noun) An electromagnetic unit of magnetic intensity.
  • high-strength brass (noun) A brass with from 1-4% manganese to harden it.
  • industrial-strength (adjective) Extremely strong or concentrated or durable.
  • magnetic field strength (noun) The amount of magnetic flux in a unit area perpendicular to the direction of magnetic flow.
