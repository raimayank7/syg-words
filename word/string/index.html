String: meaning, synonyms and examples

String is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: A lightweight cord.

In Hindi
धागा, डोरी
Synonyms
twine, bowed stringed instrument, train, drawstring, drawing string, cosmic string, chain, strand, thread, draw
Opposites
unstring
Word family
stringer

String as a noun

  1. A lightweight cord.

    Synonyms: twine · A kind of: cord · Kinds: chalk line, packthread

  2. Stringed instruments that are played with a bow.

    “The strings played superlatively well.”

    Synonyms: bowed stringed instrument · A kind of: stringed instrument · Kinds: bass fiddle, cello, viol, viola, violin

  3. A tightly stretched cord of wire or gut, which makes sound when plucked, struck, or bowed.

    A kind of: cord · Kinds: snare

  4. A sequentially ordered set of things or events or ideas in which each successive member is related to the preceding.

    “A string of islands.” “Train of mourners.” “A train of thought.”

    Synonyms: train · A kind of: series

  5. A linear sequence of symbols (characters or words or phrases).

    A kind of: sequence · Kinds: extension, string of words, substring

  6. A tie consisting of a cord that goes through a seam around an opening.

    “He pulled the drawstring and closed the bag.”

    Synonyms: drawstring, drawing string · A kind of: tie · Kinds: purse string

  7. A tough piece of fiber in vegetables, meat, or other food (especially the tough fibers connecting the two halves of a bean pod).

    A kind of: fiber

  8. (cosmology) a hypothetical one-dimensional subatomic particle having a concentration of energy and the dynamic properties of a flexible loop.

    Synonyms: cosmic string · A kind of: elementary particle

  9. A collection of objects threaded on a single strand.

    A kind of: collection · Kinds: beads

  10. A necklace made by a stringing objects together.

    “A string of beads.” “A strand of pearls.”

    Synonyms: chain, strand · A kind of: necklace

String as a verb

  1. Thread on or as if on a string.

    “String pearls on a string.” “The child drew glass beads on a string.” “Thread dried cranberries.”

    Synonyms: thread, draw · A kind of: arrange · Kinds: bead, wire

  2. Add as if on a string.

    “String these ideas together.” “String up these songs and you'll have a musical.”

    Synonyms: string up · A kind of: add

  3. Move or come along.

    Synonyms: string along · A kind of: advance

  4. Stretch out or arrange like a string.

    A kind of: arrange

  5. String together; tie or fasten with a string.

    “String the package.”

    A kind of: fasten

  6. Remove the stringy parts of.

    “String beans.”

    A kind of: remove

  7. Provide with strings.

    “String my guitar.”

    Opposite: unstring · A kind of: change

  • second-string (adjective) Being a replacement or substitute for a regular member of a team.
  • string cheese (noun) Cheese formed in long strings twisted together.
