Strong: meaning, synonyms and examples

Strong is used as an adjective (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Having strength or power greater than average or expected.

In Hindi
मज़बूत, ताक़तवर
Synonyms
potent, stiff, impregnable, inviolable, secure, unassailable, unattackable, solid, substantial, warm
Opposites
weak

Strong as an adjective

  1. Having strength or power greater than average or expected.

    “A strong radio signal.” “Strong medicine.” “A strong man.”

    Opposite: weak · Similar: beardown, beefed-up, brawny, bullnecked, bullocky, fortified

  2. Not faint or feeble.

    “A strong odor of burning rubber.”

    Similar: intense

  3. Having or wielding force or authority.

    “Providing the ground soldier with increasingly potent weapons.”

    Synonyms: potent · Similar: powerful

  4. Having a strong physiological or chemical effect.

    “A potent toxin.” “A potent cup of tea.” “A stiff drink.”

    Synonyms: potent, stiff · Similar: equipotent, multipotent

  5. Immune to attack; incapable of being tampered with.

    “An impregnable fortress.” “Fortifications that made the frontier inviolable.” “A secure telephone connection.”

    Synonyms: impregnable, inviolable, secure, unassailable, unattackable · Similar: invulnerable

  6. Of good quality and condition; solidly built.

    “A solid foundation.” “Several substantial timber buildings.”

    Synonyms: solid, substantial · Similar: sound

  7. Of verbs not having standard (or regular) inflection.

    “`sing' is a strong verb.”

    Similar: irregular

  8. Freshly made or left.

    “A warm trail.” “The scent is warm.”

    Synonyms: warm · Similar: fresh

  9. Strong and sure.

    “A firm grasp.” “Gave a strong pull on the rope.”

    Synonyms: firm · Similar: forceful
