Substance: meaning, synonyms and examples

Substance is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: The real physical matter of which a person or thing consists.

In Hindi
पदार्थ; सार
Synonyms
kernel, core, center, centre, essence, gist, heart, heart and soul, inwardness, marrow
Word family
substantial

Substance as a noun

  1. The real physical matter of which a person or thing consists.

    “DNA is the substance of our genes.”

    A kind of: matter, part · Kinds: body substance, protoplasm, chemistry, material, phlogiston, mixture, atom, chemical element

  2. The choicest or most essential or most vital part of some idea or experience.

    “The gist of the prosecutor's argument.” “The heart and soul of the Republican Party.” “The nub of the story.”

    Synonyms: kernel, core, center, centre, essence, gist, heart, heart and soul · A kind of: content · Kinds: bare bones, hypostasis, quiddity, quintessence, stuff

  3. The idea that is intended.

    “What is the meaning of this proverb?”

    Synonyms: meaning · A kind of: idea · Kinds: semantics, significance, tenor, undertone, reference, reference, connotation

  4. Material of a particular kind or constitution.

    “The immune response recognizes invading substances.”

    A kind of: physical entity

  5. Considerable capital (wealth or income).

    “He is a man of means.”

    Synonyms: means · A kind of: capital · Kinds: pocketbook, wherewithal

  6. What a communication that is about something is about.

    Synonyms: message, content, subject matter · A kind of: communication · Kinds: body, corker, reminder, request, memorial, latent content, subject, digression

  7. A particular kind or species of matter with uniform properties.

    “Shigella is one of the most toxic substances known to man.”

    A kind of: matter · Kinds: food, nutrient, philosopher's stone, allergen, assay, pyrogen, pyrogen, hydrocolloid
