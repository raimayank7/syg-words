Suit: meaning, synonyms and examples

Suit is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A set of garments (usually including a jacket and trousers or skirt) for outerwear all of the same fabric and color.

In Hindi
सूट; अनुकूल होना
Synonyms
suit of clothes, lawsuit, case, cause, causa, courtship, wooing, courting, accommodate, fit
Word family
sue

Suit as a noun

  1. A set of garments (usually including a jacket and trousers or skirt) for outerwear all of the same fabric and color.

    “They buried him in his best suit.”

    Synonyms: suit of clothes · A kind of: garment · Kinds: business suit, pinstripe, slack suit, zoot suit

  2. A comprehensive term for any proceeding in a court of law whereby an individual seeks a legal remedy.

    “The family brought suit against the landlord.”

    Synonyms: lawsuit, case, cause, causa · A kind of: proceeding · Kinds: civil suit, class action, countersuit, criminal suit, moot, paternity suit

  3. (slang) a businessman dressed in a business suit.

    “All the suits care about is the bottom line.”

    A kind of: businessman

  4. A man's courting of a woman; seeking the affections of a woman (usually with the hope of marriage).

    “Its was a brief and intense courtship.”

    Synonyms: courtship, wooing, courting · A kind of: entreaty · Kinds: bundling

  5. A petition or appeal made to a person of superior status or rank.

    A kind of: entreaty

  6. Playing card in any of four sets of 13 cards in a pack; each set has its own symbol and color.

    “A flush is five cards in the same suit.” “In bridge you must follow suit.” “What suit is trumps?”

    A kind of: playing card · Kinds: major suit, minor suit, trump

Suit as a verb

  1. Be agreeable or acceptable to.

    “This suits my needs.”

    Synonyms: accommodate, fit · A kind of: meet

  2. Be agreeable or acceptable.

    “This time suits me.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: agree

  3. Accord or comport with.

    “This kind of behavior does not suit a young woman!”

    Synonyms: befit, beseem · A kind of: match

  4. Enhance the appearance of.

    “Mourning becomes Electra.” “This behavior doesn't suit you!”

    Synonyms: become · A kind of: fancify

Phrases and compounds with "suit"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • g suit (noun) Worn by fliers and astronauts to counteract the forces of gravity and acceleration.
  • cat suit (noun) A tight-fitting garment of stretchy material that covers the body from the shoulders to the thighs (and may have long sleeves or legs reaching down to the ankles); worn by ballet dancers and acrobats for practice or performance.
  • gym suit (noun) Clothes prescribed for wear while participating in gymnastic exercise.
  • wet suit (noun) A close-fitting garment made of a permeable material; worn in cold water (as by skin divers) to retain body heat.
  • body suit (noun) A tight-fitting garment of stretchy material that covers the body from the shoulders to the thighs (and may have long sleeves or legs reaching down to the ankles); worn by ballet dancers and acrobats for practice or performance.
  • jump suit (noun) One-piece garment fashioned after a parachutist's uniform.
  • long suit (noun) In a hand, the suit having the most cards.
  • tank suit (noun) A woman's one-piece bathing suit.
  • test suit (noun) A representative legal action whose outcome is likely to become a precedent.
  • zoot suit (noun) A flashy suit of extreme cut.
  • civil suit (noun) A lawsuit alleging violations of civil law by the defendant.
  • dress suit (noun) Formalwear consisting of full evening dress for men.
  • major suit (noun) (bridge) a suit of superior scoring value, either spades or hearts.
  • minor suit (noun) ( bridge) a suit of inferior scoring value, either diamonds or clubs.
  • pants suit (noun) A pair of pants and a matching jacket worn by women.
  • slack suit (noun) Casual dress consisting of slacks and matching jacket.
