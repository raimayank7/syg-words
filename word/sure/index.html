Sure: meaning, synonyms and examples

Sure is used as an adjective (9 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Having or feeling no doubt or uncertainty; confident and assured.

In Hindi

Opposites

Sure as an adjective

  1. Having or feeling no doubt or uncertainty; confident and assured.

    “Felt certain of success.” “Was sure (or certain) she had seen it.” “Was very sure in his beliefs.”

    Synonyms: certain · Opposite: unsure · Similar: convinced

  2. Exercising or taking care great enough to bring assurance.

    “Be certain to disconnect the iron when you are through.” “Be sure to lock the doors.”

    Synonyms: certain · Similar: careful

  3. Certain to occur; destined or inevitable.

    “He was certain to fail.” “His fate is certain.” “In this life nothing is certain but death and taxes.”

    Synonyms: certain · Similar: bound, doomed, foreordained, in for

  4. Physically secure or dependable.

    “A sure footing.” “Was on sure ground.”

    Similar: secure

  5. Reliable in operation or effect.

    “A quick and certain remedy.” “A sure way to distinguish the two.” “Wood dust is a sure sign of termites.”

    Synonyms: certain · Similar: reliable

  6. (of persons) worthy of trust or confidence.

    “A sure (or trusted) friend.”

    Synonyms: trusted · Similar: trustworthy

  7. Infallible or unfailing.

    “A sure (or true) sign of one's commitment.”

    Similar: true

  8. Certain not to fail.

    “A sure hand on the throttle.”

    Similar: steady

  9. Impossible to doubt or dispute.

    “Indisputable (or sure) proof.”

    Synonyms: indisputable · Similar: certain

Sure as an adverb

  1. Definitely or positively (`sure' is sometimes used informally for `surely').

    “The results are surely encouraging.” “She certainly is a hard worker.” “It's going to be a good day for sure.”

    Synonyms: surely, certainly, for sure, for certain, sure enough, sure as shooting

Phrases and compounds with "sure"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • for sure (adverb) Definitely or positively (`sure' is sometimes used informally for `surely').
  • make sure (verb) Make a point of doing something; act purposefully and intentionally.
  • sure-fire (adjective) Certain to be successful.
  • sure thing (noun) Something that is certain.
  • to be sure (adverb) Admittedly.
  • sure enough (adverb) As supposed or expected.
  • sure-enough (adjective) (used informally especially for emphasis).
  • sure-footed (adjective) Not liable to stumble or fall.
  • sure-handed (adjective) Proficient and confident in performance.
  • sure as shooting (adverb) Definitely or positively (`sure' is sometimes used informally for `surely').
