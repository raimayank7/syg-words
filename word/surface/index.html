Surface: meaning, synonyms and examples

Surface is used as a noun (6 senses), adjective (1 sense) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: The outer boundary of an artifact or a material layer constituting or resembling such a boundary.

Opposites

Word family
surficial, surfacing

Surface as a noun

  1. The outer boundary of an artifact or a material layer constituting or resembling such a boundary.

    “There is a special cleaner for these surfaces.” “The cloth had a pattern of red dots on a white surface.”

    A kind of: artifact · Kinds: bed, board, face, ground, horizontal surface, meniscus, miter, plaster

  2. The extended two-dimensional outer boundary of a three-dimensional object.

    “They skimmed over the surface of the water.” “A brush small enough to clean every dental surface.” “The sun has no distinct surface.”

    A kind of: boundary · Kinds: interface, hard palate, palate, side, celestial sphere, end, inside, outside

  3. The outermost level of the land or sea.

    “Earthquakes originate far below the surface.” “Three quarters of the Earth's surface is covered by water.”

    A kind of: layer · Kinds: floor, floor

  4. A superficial aspect as opposed to the real nature of something.

    “It was not what it appeared to be on the surface.”

    A kind of: aspect

  5. Information that has become public.

    “All the reports were out in the open.” “The facts had been brought to the surface.”

    Synonyms: open · A kind of: public knowledge

  6. A device that provides reactive force when in motion relative to the surrounding air; can lift or control a plane in flight.

    Synonyms: airfoil, aerofoil, control surface · A kind of: device · Kinds: aileron, elevator, flap, horizontal stabilizer, rotor blade, rudder, spoiler, spoiler

Surface as an adjective

  1. On the surface.

    “Surface materials of the moon.”

    Opposite: subsurface, overhead · Similar: aboveground, grade-constructed, opencast

Surface as a verb

  1. Come to the surface.

    Synonyms: come up, rise up, rise · A kind of: ascend · Kinds: emerge, resurface, bubble up, well

  2. Put a coat on; cover the surface of; furnish with a surface.

    “Coat the cake with chocolate.”

    Synonyms: coat · A kind of: cover · Kinds: rubberize, anodize, gelatinize, skim, stucco, egg, encrust, dredge

  3. Appear or become visible; make a showing.

    “She turned up at the funeral.” “I hope the list key is going to surface again.”

    Synonyms: come on, come out, turn up, show up · A kind of: appear

Phrases and compounds with "surface"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • hard surface (verb) Cover with asphalt or a similar surface.
  • road surface (noun) The paved surface of a paved roadway.
  • surface area (noun) The extent of a 2-dimensional surface enclosed within a boundary.
  • surface fire (noun) A forest fire that burns only the surface litter and undergrowth.
  • surface gage (noun) Gauge consisting of a scriber mounted on an adjustable stand; used to test the accuracy of plane surfaces.
  • surface lift (noun) A ski tow that pulls skiers up a slope without lifting them off the ground.
  • surface mail (noun) Mail that is sent by land or sea.
  • surface mine (verb) Extract (ore) from a strip-mine.
  • surface ship (noun) A warship that operates on the surface of the water.
  • surface soil (noun) The layer of soil on the surface.
  • surface-mine (verb) Extract (ore) from a strip-mine.
  • work surface (noun) A horizontal surface for supporting objects used in working or playing games.
  • paved surface (noun) A level horizontal surface covered with paving material.
  • surface gauge (noun) Gauge consisting of a scriber mounted on an adjustable stand; used to test the accuracy of plane surfaces.
  • surface noise (noun) Noise produced by the friction of the stylus of a record player moving over the rotating record.
  • upper surface (noun) The side that is uppermost.
