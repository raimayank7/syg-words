Tap: meaning, synonyms and examples

Tap is used as a verb (11 senses) and noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Cut a female screw thread with a tap.

In Hindi
नल; थपथपाना
Synonyms
tip, exploit, wiretap, intercept, bug, rap, knock, pink, tapdance, solicit
Word family
tapper, tapping, tapster

Tap as a verb

  1. Cut a female screw thread with a tap.

    A kind of: cut

  2. Draw from or dip into to get something.

    “Tap one's memory.” “Tap a source of money.”

    A kind of: withdraw

  3. Strike lightly.

    “He tapped me on the shoulder.”

    Synonyms: tip · A kind of: strike · Kinds: percuss

  4. Draw from; make good use of.

    “We must exploit the resources we are given wisely.”

    Synonyms: exploit · A kind of: use · Kinds: maximize, harness, mine, quarry, overexploit, commercialize, milk

  5. Tap a telephone or telegraph wire to get information.

    “The FBI was tapping the phone line of the suspected spy.” “Is this hotel room bugged?”

    Synonyms: wiretap, intercept, bug · A kind of: listen in

  6. Furnish with a tap or spout, so as to be able to draw liquid from it.

    A kind of: supply

  7. Make light, repeated taps on a surface.

    “He was tapping his fingers on the table impatiently.”

    Synonyms: rap, knock, pink · A kind of: sound

  8. Walk with a tapping sound.

    A kind of: walk

  9. Dance and make rhythmic clicking sounds by means of metal plates nailed to the sole of the dance shoes.

    “Glover tapdances better than anybody.”

    Synonyms: tapdance · A kind of: dance

  10. Pierce in order to draw a liquid from.

    “Tap a maple tree for its syrup.”

    A kind of: pierce

  11. Make a solicitation or entreaty for something; request urgently or persistently.

    “Henry IV solicited the Pope for a divorce.” “My neighbor keeps soliciting money for different charities.”

    Synonyms: solicit, beg · A kind of: request · Kinds: quest, canvass, lobby

Tap as a noun

  1. The sound made by a gentle blow.

    Synonyms: pat, rap · A kind of: sound · Kinds: pitter-patter

  2. A gentle blow.

    Synonyms: rap, strike · A kind of: blow

  3. A faucet for drawing water from a pipe or cask.

    Synonyms: water faucet, water tap, hydrant · A kind of: faucet, plumbing fixture

  4. A small metal plate that attaches to the toe or heel of a shoe (as in tap dancing).

    A kind of: plate

  5. A tool for cutting female (internal) screw threads.

    A kind of: tool

  6. A plug for a bunghole in a cask.

    Synonyms: spigot · A kind of: plug

  7. The act of tapping a telephone or telegraph line to get information.

    Synonyms: wiretap · A kind of: investigation

  8. A light touch or stroke.

    Synonyms: pat, dab · A kind of: touch

Phrases and compounds with "tap"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • on tap (adjective) (of liquids) available by turning a tap.
  • tap out (verb) Beat out a rhythm.
  • tap-off (noun) The act of starting a basketball game with a jump ball.
  • tap dance (noun) A dance step tapped out audibly with the feet.
  • tap water (noun) Water directly from the spigot.
  • water tap (noun) A faucet for drawing water from a pipe or cask.
  • spinal tap (noun) Removal by centesis of fluid from the subarachnoid space of the lumbar region of the spinal cord for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.
  • tap dancer (noun) A dancer who sounds out rhythms by using metal taps on the toes and heels of the shoes.
  • tap wrench (noun) A wrench for turning a tap to create an internal screw thread.
  • tap dancing (noun) A dance step tapped out audibly with the feet.
