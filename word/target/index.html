Target: meaning, synonyms and examples

Target is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A reference point to shoot at.

In Hindi
लक्ष्य, निशाना
Synonyms
mark, prey, quarry, fair game, target area, butt, aim, object, objective, place

Target as a noun

  1. A reference point to shoot at.

    “His arrow hit the mark.”

    Synonyms: mark · A kind of: reference point · Kinds: clout, drogue

  2. A person who is the aim of an attack (especially a victim of ridicule or exploitation) by some hostile person or influence.

    “He fell prey to muggers.” “Everyone was fair game.” “The target of a manhunt.”

    Synonyms: prey, quarry, fair game · A kind of: victim

  3. The location of the target that is to be hit.

    Synonyms: target area · A kind of: topographic point

  4. Sports equipment consisting of an object set up for a marksman or archer to aim at.

    Synonyms: butt · A kind of: sports equipment · Kinds: clay pigeon

  5. The goal intended to be attained (and which is believed to be attainable).

    “The sole object of her trip was to see her children.”

    Synonyms: aim, object, objective · A kind of: goal · Kinds: grail, business, point, thing

Target as a verb

  1. Intend (something) to move towards a certain goal.

    “He aimed his fists towards his opponent's face.” “Criticism directed at her superior.” “Direct your anger towards others, not towards yourself.”

    Synonyms: aim, place, direct, point · A kind of: aim · Kinds: address, range in

Phrases and compounds with "target"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • target area (noun) The location of the target that is to be hit.
  • radar target (noun) A radar echo displayed so as to show the position of a reflecting surface.
  • target organ (noun) (radiology) organ intended to receive the therapeutic dose of a radioactive substance.
  • target range (noun) A practice range for target practice.
  • water-target (noun) Aquatic plant with floating oval leaves and purple flowers; in lakes and slow-moving streams; suitable for aquariums.
  • dead on target (adjective) Accurately placed or thrown.
  • target company (noun) A company that has been chosen as attractive for takeover by a potential acquirer.
  • target program (noun) A fully compiled or assembled program ready to be loaded into the computer.
  • target-hunting (adjective) Guided automatically toward the target.
  • takeover target (noun) A company that has been chosen as attractive for takeover by a potential acquirer.
  • target language (noun) The language into which a text written in another language is to be translated.
  • target practice (noun) Practice in shooting at targets.
  • target acquisition system (noun) A shipboard system for the detection and identification and location of a target with enough detail to permit effective weapon employment.
