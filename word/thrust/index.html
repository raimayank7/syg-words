Thrust: meaning, synonyms and examples

Thrust is used as a verb (8 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Push forcefully.

In Hindi
धक्का, ज़ोर
Synonyms
stuff, shove, squeeze, lunge, hurl, hurtle, force, pierce, push up, throw
Word family
thruster, thrusting

Thrust as a verb

  1. Push forcefully.

    “He thrust his chin forward.”

    A kind of: push · Kinds: jab, stab, shoulder, boost, force, ram, punch, dig

  2. Press or force.

    “Stuff money into an envelope.” “She thrust the letter into his hand.”

    Synonyms: stuff, shove, squeeze · A kind of: push

  3. Make a thrusting forward movement.

    Synonyms: lunge, hurl, hurtle · A kind of: move · Kinds: dart, riposte

  4. Impose urgently, importunately, or inexorably.

    “She forced her diet fads on him.”

    Synonyms: force · A kind of: compel · Kinds: stick

  5. Penetrate or cut through with a sharp instrument.

    Synonyms: pierce · A kind of: penetrate · Kinds: stick, stick, peg, center punch, transfix, horn, gore, lance

  6. Force (molten rock) into pre-existing rock.

  7. Push upward.

    “The front of the trains that had collided head-on thrust up into the air.”

    Synonyms: push up · A kind of: stick out

  8. Place or put with great energy.

    “She threw the blanket around the child.” “Thrust the money in the hands of the beggar.”

    Synonyms: throw · A kind of: put · Kinds: pop

Thrust as a noun

  1. The force used in pushing.

    “The push of the water on the walls of the tank.” “The thrust of the jet engines.”

    Synonyms: push · A kind of: force

  2. A strong blow with a knife or other sharp pointed instrument.

    Synonyms: stab, knife thrust · A kind of: blow · Kinds: lunge, remise

  3. The act of applying force to propel something.

    “After reaching the desired velocity the drive is cut off.”

    Synonyms: drive, driving force · A kind of: propulsion · Kinds: firewall, impulse

  4. Verbal criticism.

    “He enlivened his editorials with barbed thrusts at politicians.”

    A kind of: criticism

  5. A sharp hand gesture (resembling a blow).

    “He warned me with a jab with his finger.” “He made a thrusting motion with his fist.”

    Synonyms: jab, jabbing, poke, poking, thrusting · A kind of: gesture

Phrases and compounds with "thrust"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • thrust out (verb) Push to thrust outward.
  • knife thrust (noun) A strong blow with a knife or other sharp pointed instrument.
  • thrust ahead (verb) Push one's way.
  • thrust fault (noun) A geological fault in which the upper side appears to have been pushed upward by compression.
  • thrust stage (noun) A theater stage that extends out into the audience's part of a theater and has seats on three sides.
  • cut-and-thrust (noun) Fighting with knives.
  • thrust bearing (noun) A bearing designed to take thrusts parallel to the axis of revolution.
  • straight thrust (noun) (fencing) an attacking thrust made with one foot forward and the back leg straight and with the sword arm outstretched forward.
