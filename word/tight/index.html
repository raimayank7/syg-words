Tight: meaning, synonyms and examples

Tight is used as an adjective (13 senses) and adverb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Closely constrained or constricted or constricting.

In Hindi
कसा हुआ, तंग
Synonyms
taut, compressed, mean, mingy, miserly, close, nasty, rigorous, stringent, fast
Opposites
loose, leaky
Word family
tightness

Tight as an adjective

  1. Closely constrained or constricted or constricting.

    “Tight skirts.” “He hated tight starched collars.” “Fingers closed in a tight fist.”

    Opposite: loose · Similar: choky, clenched, close, skintight, tight-fitting, viselike

  2. Pulled or drawn tight.

    “Taut sails.” “A tight drumhead.” “A tight rope.”

    Synonyms: taut · Similar: tense

  3. Set so close together as to be invulnerable to penetration.

    “In tight formation.” “A tight blockade.”

    Similar: invulnerable

  4. Pressed tightly together.

    “With lips compressed.”

    Synonyms: compressed · Similar: closed

  5. (used of persons or behavior) characterized by or indicative of lack of generosity.

    “A mean person.” “He left a miserly tip.”

    Synonyms: mean, mingy, miserly · Similar: stingy

  6. Affected by scarcity and expensive to borrow.

    “Tight money.” “A tight market.”

    Similar: scarce

  7. Of such close construction as to be impermeable.

    “A tight roof.” “Warm in our tight little house.”

    Opposite: leaky · Similar: airtight, dripless, hermetic, leakproof, rainproof, snug

  8. Of textiles.

    “A close weave.” “Smooth percale with a very tight weave.”

    Synonyms: close · Similar: fine

  9. Securely or solidly fixed in place; rigid.

    “The bolts are tight.”

    Similar: secure

  10. (of a contest or contestants) evenly matched.

    “A close contest.” “A close election.” “A tight game.”

    Synonyms: close · Similar: equal

  11. Exasperatingly difficult to handle or circumvent.

    “A nasty problem.” “A good man to have on your side in a tight situation.”

    Synonyms: nasty · Similar: difficult

  12. Demanding strict attention to rules and procedures.

    “Rigorous discipline.” “Tight security.” “Stringent safety measures.”

    Synonyms: rigorous, stringent · Similar: demanding

  13. Packed closely together.

    “They stood in a tight little group.” “Hair in tight curls.” “The pub was packed tight.”

    Similar: compact

Tight as an adverb

  1. Firmly or closely.

    “Held fast to the rope.” “Her foot was stuck fast.” “Held tight.”

    Synonyms: fast

  2. In an attentive manner.

    “He remained close on his guard.”

    Synonyms: close, closely

Phrases and compounds with "tight"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air-tight (adjective) Having no weak points.
  • gas-tight (adjective) Not allowing air or gas to pass in or out.
  • sit tight (verb) Maintain the same position; wait it out.
  • hold tight (verb) Hold firmly, usually with one's hands.
  • skin-tight (adjective) So tight as to cling to the skin.
  • tight-knit (adjective) Closely and firmly integrated.
  • light-tight (adjective) Not penetrable by light.
  • tight money (noun) The economic condition in which credit is difficult to secure and interest rates are high.
  • tight-laced (adjective) Exaggeratedly proper.
  • hug-me-tight (noun) A woman's fitted jacket.
  • tight fitting (adjective) Fitting snugly.
  • tight-fitting (adjective) Fitting snugly.
