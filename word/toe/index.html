Toe: meaning, synonyms and examples

Toe is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: One of the digits of the foot.

In Hindi
पैर की उँगली
Synonyms
toenail

Toe as a noun

  1. One of the digits of the foot.

    A kind of: digit, extremity · Kinds: big toe, hammertoe, little toe

  2. The part of footwear that provides a covering for the toes.

    A kind of: covering

  3. Forepart of a hoof.

    A kind of: body part

  4. (golf) the part of a clubhead farthest from the shaft.

    A kind of: part

Toe as a verb

  1. Walk so that the toes assume an indicated position or direction.

    “She toes inwards.”

    A kind of: walk

  2. Drive obliquely.

    “Toe a nail.”

    Synonyms: toenail · A kind of: force

  3. Hit (a golf ball) with the toe of the club.

    A kind of: hit

  4. Drive (a golf ball) with the toe of the club.

    A kind of: hit

  5. Touch with the toe.

    A kind of: touch
