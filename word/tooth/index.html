Tooth: meaning, synonyms and examples

Tooth is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Hard bonelike structures in the jaws of vertebrates; used for biting and chewing or for attack and defense.

Tooth as a noun

  1. Hard bonelike structures in the jaws of vertebrates; used for biting and chewing or for attack and defense.

    A kind of: bone · Kinds: fang, tusk, conodont, chopper, carnassial tooth, front tooth, back tooth, malposed tooth

  2. Something resembling the tooth of an animal.

    A kind of: projection · Kinds: cog

  3. Toothlike structure in invertebrates found in the mouth or alimentary canal or on a shell.

    A kind of: structure · Kinds: denticle

  4. A means of enforcement.

    “The treaty had no teeth in it.”

    A kind of: means

  5. One of a number of uniform projections on a gear.

    A kind of: projection

Phrases and compounds with "tooth"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • eye tooth (noun) One of the four pointed conical teeth (two in each jaw) located between the incisors and the premolars.
  • baby tooth (noun) One of the first temporary teeth of a young mammal (one of 20 in children).
  • back tooth (noun) A tooth situated at the back of the mouth.
  • fine-tooth (adjective) Having fine teeth set close together.
  • milk tooth (noun) One of the first temporary teeth of a young mammal (one of 20 in children).
  • tooth root (noun) The part of a tooth that is embedded in the jaw and serves as support.
  • adult tooth (noun) Any of the 32 teeth that replace the deciduous teeth of early childhood and (with luck) can last until old age.
  • front tooth (noun) A tooth situated at the front of the mouth.
  • sweet tooth (noun) A strong appetite for sweet food.
  • tooth decay (noun) Soft decayed area in a tooth; progressive decay can lead to the death of a tooth.
  • tooth fairy (noun) A fairy that is said to leave money at night under a child's pillow to compensate for a baby tooth falling out.
  • tooth shell (noun) Any of various seashore mollusks having a tapering tubular shell open at each end and a foot pointed like a spade for burrowing.
  • canine tooth (noun) One of the four pointed conical teeth (two in each jaw) located between the incisors and the premolars.
  • tooth doctor (noun) A person qualified to practice dentistry.
  • tooth enamel (noun) Hard white substance covering the crown of a tooth.
  • tooth fungus (noun) A fungus of the family Hydnaceae.
