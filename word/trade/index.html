Trade: meaning, synonyms and examples

Trade is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: The commercial exchange (buying and selling on domestic or international markets) of goods and services.

In Hindi
व्यापार
Synonyms
craft, patronage, deal, business deal, trade wind, barter, swap, swop, merchandise, trade in
Word family
trader, trading

Trade as a noun

  1. The commercial exchange (buying and selling on domestic or international markets) of goods and services.

    “Venice was an important center of trade with the East.” “They are accused of conspiring to constrain trade.”

    A kind of: commerce · Kinds: fair trade, fair trade, free trade

  2. The skilled practice of a practical occupation.

    “He learned his trade as an apprentice.”

    Synonyms: craft · A kind of: occupation · Kinds: airplane mechanics, auto mechanics, basketry, carpentry, drafting, dressmaking, electrical work, interior decoration

  3. The business given to a commercial establishment by its customers.

    “Even before noon there was a considerable patronage.”

    Synonyms: patronage · A kind of: business · Kinds: custom

  4. A particular instance of buying or selling.

    “It was a package deal.” “I had no further trade with him.” “He's a master of the business deal.”

    Synonyms: deal, business deal · A kind of: transaction · Kinds: arms deal, penny ante

  5. People who perform a particular kind of skilled work.

    “He represented the craft of brewers.” “As they say in the trade.”

    Synonyms: craft · A kind of: class

  6. Steady winds blowing from east to west above and below the equator.

    “They rode the trade winds going west.”

    Synonyms: trade wind · A kind of: prevailing wind

  7. An equal exchange.

    “We had no money so we had to live by barter.”

    Synonyms: barter, swap, swop · A kind of: exchange · Kinds: horse trade

Trade as a verb

  1. Engage in the trade of.

    “He is merchandising telephone sets.”

    Synonyms: merchandise · Kinds: traffic, arbitrage, traffic, market, import, export

  2. Turn in as payment or part payment for a purchase.

    “Trade in an old car for a new one.”

    Synonyms: trade in · A kind of: exchange · Kinds: barter away

  3. Be traded at a certain price or under certain conditions.

    “The stock traded around $20 a share.”

    A kind of: sell · Kinds: close

  4. Exchange or give (something) in exchange for.

    Synonyms: swap, swop, switch · A kind of: exchange

  5. Do business; offer for sale as for one's livelihood.

    “She deals in gold.” “The brothers sell shoes.”

    Synonyms: deal, sell · A kind of: transact · Kinds: deal, black marketeer, pyramid, market, peddle

Phrases and compounds with "trade"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • trade in (verb) Turn in as payment or part payment for a purchase.
  • trade-in (noun) An item of property that is given in part payment for a new one.
  • rag trade (noun) Makers and sellers of fashionable clothing.
  • trade gap (noun) The difference in value over a period of time of a country's imports and exports of merchandise.
  • trade rat (noun) Any of several bushy-tailed rodents of the genus Neotoma of western North America; hoards food and other objects.
  • trade-off (noun) An exchange that occurs as a compromise.
  • fair trade (noun) Trade that satisfies certain criteria on the supply chain of the goods involved, usually including fair payment for producers; often with other social and environmental considerations.
  • free trade (noun) International trade free of government interference.
  • trade bill (noun) A statute that would regulate foreign trade.
  • trade book (noun) A book intended for general readership.
  • trade good (noun) Articles of commerce.
  • trade name (noun) A name given to a product or service.
  • trade wind (noun) Steady winds blowing from east to west above and below the equator.
  • trade-last (noun) A compliment that I heard about you that I offer to trade for a compliment you have heard about me.
  • horse trade (noun) The swapping of horses (accompanied by much bargaining).
  • horse-trade (verb) Negotiate with much give and take.
