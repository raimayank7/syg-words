Train: meaning, synonyms and examples

Train is used as a verb (11 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Create by training and teaching.

In Hindi
रेलगाड़ी; प्रशिक्षण देना
Synonyms
develop, prepare, educate, discipline, check, condition, groom, school, cultivate, civilize
Word family
trainee, trainer, training

Train as a verb

  1. Create by training and teaching.

    “The old master is training world-class violinists.” “We develop the leaders for the future.”

    Synonyms: develop, prepare, educate · A kind of: teach · Kinds: retrain, drill, housebreak, toilet-train

  2. Undergo training or instruction in preparation for a particular role, function, or profession.

    “She is training to be a teacher.” “He trained as a legal aid.”

    Synonyms: prepare · A kind of: learn · Kinds: apprentice, retrain, drill

  3. Develop (children's) behavior by instruction and practice; especially to teach self-control.

    “Parents must discipline their children.” “Is this dog trained?”

    Synonyms: discipline, check, condition · A kind of: develop · Kinds: mortify

  4. Educate for a future role or function.

    “He is grooming his son to become his successor.” “The prince was prepared to become King one day.” “They trained him to be a warrior.”

    Synonyms: prepare, groom · A kind of: educate · Kinds: qualify

  5. Teach or refine to be discriminative in taste or judgment.

    “Cultivate your musical taste.” “Train your tastebuds.” “She is well schooled in poetry.”

    Synonyms: educate, school, cultivate, civilize, civilise · A kind of: polish · Kinds: sophisticate

  6. Point or cause to go (blows, weapons, or objects such as photographic equipment) towards.

    “Please don't aim at your little brother!” “He trained his gun on the burglar.” “Don't train your camera on the women.”

    Synonyms: aim, take, take aim, direct · A kind of: position · Kinds: target, draw a bead on, hold, turn, swing, charge, level, sight

  7. Teach and supervise (someone); act as a trainer or coach (to), as in sports.

    “He is training our Olympic team.” “She is coaching the crew.”

    Synonyms: coach · A kind of: teach

  8. Exercise in order to prepare for an event or competition.

    “She is training for the Olympics.”

    A kind of: exercise

  9. Cause to grow in a certain way by tying and pruning it.

    “Train the vine.”

    A kind of: control · Kinds: trellis

  10. Travel by rail or train.

    “They railed from Rome to Venice.” “She trained to Hamburg.”

    Synonyms: rail · A kind of: ride

  11. Drag loosely along a surface; allow to sweep the ground.

    “The toddler was trailing his pants.” “She trained her long scarf behind her.”

    Synonyms: trail · A kind of: drag

Train as a noun

  1. Public transport provided by a line of railway cars coupled together and drawn by a locomotive.

    “Express trains don't stop at Princeton Junction.”

    Synonyms: railroad train · A kind of: public transport · Kinds: boat train, car train, freight train, hospital train, mail train, passenger train, streamliner, subway train

  2. A sequentially ordered set of things or events or ideas in which each successive member is related to the preceding.

    “A string of islands.” “Train of mourners.” “A train of thought.”

    Synonyms: string · A kind of: series

  3. A procession (of wagons or mules or camels) traveling together in single file.

    “We were part of a caravan of almost a thousand camels.” “They joined the wagon train for safety.”

    Synonyms: caravan, wagon train · A kind of: procession

  4. A series of consequences wrought by an event.

    “It led to a train of disasters.”

    A kind of: consequence

  5. Piece of cloth forming the long back section of a gown that is drawn along the floor.

    “The bride's train was carried by her two young nephews.”

    A kind of: piece of cloth

  6. Wheelwork consisting of a connected set of rotating gears by which force is transmitted or motion or torque is changed.

    “The fool got his tie caught in the geartrain.”

    Synonyms: gearing, gear, geartrain, power train · A kind of: wheelwork · Kinds: epicyclic train, reduction gear

Phrases and compounds with "train"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • car train (noun) A train that transports passengers and their automobiles.
  • train oil (noun) A white to brown oil obtained from whale blubber; formerly used as an illuminant.
  • train set (noun) A toy consisting of small models of railroad trains and the track for them to run on.
  • boat train (noun) A train taking passengers to or from a port.
  • mail train (noun) A train that carries mail.
  • train fare (noun) The fare charged for traveling by train.
  • wave train (noun) A succession of waves spaced at regular intervals.
  • gravy train (noun) Income obtained with a minimum of effort.
  • house-train (verb) Train (a pet) to live cleanly in a house.
  • liner train (noun) A long-distance express freight train between industrial centers and seaports with facilities for rapid loading and unloading of goods.
  • power train (noun) Wheelwork consisting of a connected set of rotating gears by which force is transmitted or motion or torque is changed.
  • train depot (noun) Terminal where trains load or unload passengers or goods.
  • wagon train (noun) A procession (of wagons or mules or camels) traveling together in single file.
  • bullet train (noun) A high-speed passenger train.
  • subway train (noun) A train that runs in a subway system.
  • toilet-train (verb) Train (a small child) to use the toilet.
