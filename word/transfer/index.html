Transfer: meaning, synonyms and examples

Transfer is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Transfer somebody to a different position or location of work.

In Hindi
तबादला; स्थानांतरित करना
Synonyms
reassign, transplant, shift, change, transmit, transport, channel, channelize, channelise, remove
Word family
transferrer, transferral, transferer, transferor, transference

Transfer as a verb

  1. Transfer somebody to a different position or location of work.

    Synonyms: reassign · A kind of: delegate · Kinds: second, exchange

  2. Move from one place to another.

    “Transfer the data.” “Transmit the news.” “Transfer the patient to another hospital.”

    A kind of: move · Kinds: mail, send, convey, load, offset, import, export, offload

  3. Lift and reset in another soil or situation.

    “Transplant the young rice plants.”

    Synonyms: transplant · A kind of: displace

  4. Move around.

    “Transfer the packet from his trouser pockets to a pocket in his jacket.”

    Synonyms: shift · A kind of: move · Kinds: transpose, shunt, carry, shuffle, transship, bunker, carry over, remove

  5. Cause to change ownership.

    “I transferred my stock holdings to my children.”

    Kinds: give, demise, alien, negociate, convey, desacralize, assign, pass

  6. Change from one vehicle or transportation line to another.

    “She changed in Chicago on her way to the East coast.”

    Synonyms: change · A kind of: travel

  7. Send from one person or place to another.

    “Transmit a message.”

    Synonyms: transmit, transport, channel, channelize, channelise · A kind of: move · Kinds: bring, project, propagate, translate, turn, send

  8. Shift the position or location of, as for business, legal, educational, or military purposes.

    “He removed his children to the countryside.” “Remove the troops to the forest surrounding the city.” “Remove a case to another court.”

    Synonyms: remove · A kind of: transfer

  9. Transfer from one place or period to another.

    “The ancient Greek story was transplanted into Modern America.”

    Synonyms: transpose, transplant · A kind of: shift

Transfer as a noun

  1. The act of moving something from one location to another.

    Synonyms: transportation, transport, transferral, conveyance · A kind of: movement · Kinds: transshipment, airlift, connection, delivery, drive, carry, lighterage, resettlement

  2. Someone who transfers or is transferred from one position to another.

    “The best student was a transfer from LSU.”

    Synonyms: transferee · A kind of: person

  3. The act of transfering something from one form to another.

    “The transfer of the music from record to tape suppressed much of the background noise.”

    Synonyms: transference · A kind of: change of state

  4. A ticket that allows a passenger to change conveyances.

    A kind of: ticket

  5. Application of a skill learned in one situation to a different but similar situation.

    Synonyms: transfer of training, carry-over · A kind of: learning · Kinds: generalization

  6. Transferring ownership.

    Synonyms: transference · A kind of: transaction · Kinds: alienation, conveyance, quitclaim, lend-lease, secularization

Phrases and compounds with "transfer"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • transfer rna (noun) RNA molecules present in the cell (in at least 20 varieties, each variety capable of combining with a specific amino acid) that attach the correct amino acid to the protein chain that is being synthesized at the ribosome of the cell (according to directions coded in the mRNA).
  • transfer tax (noun) Any tax levied on the passing of title to property.
  • legal transfer (noun) The voluntary transfer of something (title or possession) from one party to another.
  • transfer agent (noun) An agency (usually a bank) that is appointed by a corporation to keep records of its stock and bond owners and to resolve problems about certificates.
  • transfer paper (noun) A paper that is coated with a preparation for transferring a design to another surface.
  • transfer payment (noun) A public expenditure (as for unemployment compensation or veteran's benefits) that is not for goods and services.
  • transfer of training (noun) Application of a skill learned in one situation to a different but similar situation.
  • file transfer protocol (noun) Protocol that allows users to copy files between their local system and any system they can reach on the network.
  • asynchronous transfer mode (noun) A means of digital communications that is capable of very high speeds; suitable for transmission of images or voice or video as well as data.
  • hypertext transfer protocol (noun) A protocol (utilizing TCP) to transfer hypertext requests and information between servers and browsers.
  • somatic cell nuclear transfer (noun) Moving a cell nucleus and its genetic material from one cell to another.
  • anonymous file transfer protocol (noun) A common way to make software available; users are allowed to log in as `guest' without a password and copy whatever has been made available.
