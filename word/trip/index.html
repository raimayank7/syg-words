Trip: meaning, synonyms and examples

Trip is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A journey for some purpose (usually including the return).

In Hindi
यात्रा, सैर; ठोकर खाना
Synonyms
slip, head trip, tripper, trip-up, stumble, misstep, trip up, travel, jaunt, actuate
Word family
tripper

Trip as a noun

  1. A journey for some purpose (usually including the return).

    “He took a trip to the shopping center.”

    A kind of: journey · Kinds: flight, junket, round trip, run, run, trek, errand, service call

  2. An accidental misstep threatening (or causing) a fall.

    “He blamed his slip on the ice.” “The jolt caused many slips and a few spills.”

    Synonyms: slip · A kind of: mishap · Kinds: spill

  3. An exciting or stimulating experience.

    Synonyms: head trip · A kind of: experience

  4. A catch mechanism that acts as a switch.

    “The pressure activates the tripper and releases the water.”

    Synonyms: tripper · A kind of: catch

  5. A light or nimble tread.

    “He heard the trip of women's feet overhead.”

    A kind of: step

  6. An unintentional but embarrassing blunder.

    “He recited the whole poem without a single trip.” “He arranged his robes to avoid a trip-up later.” “Confusion caused his unfortunate misstep.”

    Synonyms: trip-up, stumble, misstep · A kind of: blunder

Trip as a verb

  1. Miss a step and fall or nearly fall.

    “She stumbled over the tree root.”

    Synonyms: stumble · A kind of: move · Kinds: founder

  2. Cause to stumble.

    “The questions on the test tripped him up.”

    Synonyms: trip up

  3. Make a trip for pleasure.

    Synonyms: travel, jaunt · Kinds: junketeer, travel to, travel, ply, commute, peregrinate

  4. Put in motion or move to act.

    “Trigger a reaction.” “Actuate the circuits.”

    Synonyms: actuate, trigger, activate, set off, spark off, spark, trigger off, touch off · A kind of: initiate

  5. Get high, stoned, or drugged.

    “He trips every weekend.”

    Synonyms: trip out, turn on, get off

Phrases and compounds with "trip"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • trip up (verb) Detect a blunder or misstep.
  • trip-up (noun) An unintentional but embarrassing blunder.
  • ego trip (noun) An act undertaken to increase your own power and influence or to draw attention to your own importance.
  • trip out (verb) Get high, stoned, or drugged.
  • head trip (noun) An exciting or stimulating experience.
  • trip line (noun) A light rope used in lumbering to free a dog hook from a log at a distance.
  • trip wire (noun) A small military force that serves as a first line of defense; if they become engaged in hostilities it will trigger the intervention of stronger military forces.
  • field trip (noun) A group excursion (to a museum or the woods or some historic place) for firsthand examination.
  • guilt trip (noun) Remorse caused by feeling responsible for some offense.
  • ocean trip (noun) An act of traveling by water.
  • power trip (noun) (slang) a self-aggrandizing action undertaken simply for the pleasure of exercising control over other people.
  • round trip (noun) A trip to some place and back again.
  • pleasure trip (noun) A journey taken for pleasure.
  • scouting trip (noun) An expedition undertaken to gain information.
  • round-trip ticket (noun) A ticket to a place and back (usually over the same route).
  • round-trip light time (noun) The elapsed time it takes for a signal to travel from Earth to a spacecraft (or other body) and back to the starting point.
