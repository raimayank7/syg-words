Type: meaning, synonyms and examples

Type is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A subdivision of a particular kind of thing.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites
antitype
Word family
typify, typic, typist, typing

Type as a noun

  1. A subdivision of a particular kind of thing.

    “What type of sculpture do you prefer?”

    Opposite: antitype · A kind of: kind · Kinds: breed, nature, version

  2. A person of a specified kind (usually with many eccentricities).

    “A real character.” “A strange character.” “A friendly eccentric.”

    Synonyms: character, eccentric, case · A kind of: adult

  3. (biology) the taxonomic group whose characteristics are used to define the next higher taxon.

    A kind of: taxonomic group

  4. Printed characters.

    “Small type is hard to read.”

    A kind of: character · Kinds: type family, font

  5. All of the tokens of the same symbol.

    “The word `element' contains five different types of character.”

    A kind of: symbol

  6. A small metal block bearing a raised character on one end; produces a printed character when inked and pressed on paper.

    “He dropped a case of type, so they made him pick them up.”

    A kind of: block · Kinds: kern, quad

Type as a verb

  1. Write by means of a keyboard with types.

    “Type the acceptance letter, please.”

    Synonyms: typewrite · A kind of: write · Kinds: shift, backspace, double-space, triple-space, touch-type

  2. Identify as belonging to a certain type.

    “Such people can practically be typed.”

    Synonyms: typecast · A kind of: identify

Phrases and compounds with "type"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • type a (noun) The blood group whose red cells carry the A antigen.
  • type b (noun) The blood group whose red cells carry the B antigen.
  • type o (noun) The blood group whose red cells carry neither the A nor B antigens.
  • type ab (noun) The blood group whose red cells carry both the A and B antigens.
  • body type (noun) A category of physique.
  • type slug (noun) A strip of type metal used for spacing.
  • blood type (noun) Human blood cells (usually just the red blood cells) that have the same antigens.
  • roman type (noun) A typeface used in ancient Roman inscriptions.
  • touch-type (verb) Type without looking at the keyboard.
  • type genus (noun) (biology) genus from which the name of a family or subfamily is formed; it is not necessarily the most representative genus but often the largest or best known or earliest described.
  • type metal (noun) An alloy of tin and lead and antimony used to make printing type.
  • pyknic type (noun) Round, fat, and heavy.
  • type family (noun) A complete set of type suitable for printing text.
  • type species (noun) (biology) the species that best exemplifies the essential characteristics of the genus to which it belongs.
  • asthenic type (noun) Slender, weak, and lightweight.
  • athletic type (noun) Muscular and big-boned.
