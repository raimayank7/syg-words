Use: meaning, synonyms and examples

Use is used as a verb (6 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Put into service; make work or employ for a particular purpose or for its inherent or natural purpose.

In Hindi
उपयोग करना, इस्तेमाल
Synonyms
utilize, utilise, apply, employ, habituate, expend, practice, usage, utilization, utilisation
Word family
useable, usable, user, usage, using, usance

Use as a verb

  1. Put into service; make work or employ for a particular purpose or for its inherent or natural purpose.

    “Use your head!” “We only use Spanish at home.” “I can't use this tool.”

    Synonyms: utilize, utilise, apply, employ · Kinds: give, play, play, pull out all the stops, put, ply, address, waste

  2. Take or consume (regularly or habitually).

    Synonyms: habituate · A kind of: consume · Kinds: board

  3. Use up, consume fully.

    “The legislature expended its time on school questions.”

    Synonyms: expend · Kinds: pervert, abuse, spare, take, waste

  4. Seek or achieve an end by using to one's advantage.

    “She uses her influential friends to get jobs.” “The president's wife used her good connections.”

    A kind of: exploit · Kinds: trespass

  5. Avail oneself to.

    “Apply a principle.” “Practice a religion.” “Use care when going down the stairs.”

    Synonyms: practice, apply · Kinds: follow

  6. Habitually do something (use only in the past tense).

    “She used to call her mother every week but now she calls only occasionally.” “I used to get sick when I ate in that dining hall.” “They used to vacation in the Bahamas.”

    A kind of: act

Use as a noun

  1. The act of using.

    “Skilled in the utilization of computers.”

    Synonyms: usage, utilization, utilisation, employment, exercise · A kind of: activity · Kinds: practice, play, misuse, exploitation, recycling, application

  2. What something is used for.

    “The function of an auger is to bore holes.” “Ballet is beautiful but what use is it?”

    Synonyms: function, purpose, role · A kind of: utility · Kinds: raison d'etre

  3. A particular service.

    “He put his knowledge to good use.” “Patrons have their uses.”

    A kind of: utility

  4. (economics) the utilization of economic goods to satisfy needs or in manufacturing.

    “The consumption of energy has increased steadily.”

    Synonyms: consumption, economic consumption, usance, use of goods and services · A kind of: demand · Kinds: conspicuous consumption

  5. (psychology) an automatic pattern of behavior in reaction to a specific situation; may be inherited or acquired through frequent repetition.

    “Owls have nocturnal habits.” “She had a habit twirling the ends of her hair.” “Long use had hardened him to it.”

    Synonyms: habit · A kind of: custom · Kinds: ritual, second nature, cleanliness

  6. Exerting shrewd or devious influence especially for one's own advantage.

    “His manipulation of his friends was scandalous.”

    Synonyms: manipulation · A kind of: influence · Kinds: mind game

  7. (law) the exercise of the legal right to enjoy the benefits of owning property.

    “We were given the use of his boat.”

    Synonyms: enjoyment · A kind of: legal right · Kinds: fair use, fruition

Phrases and compounds with "use"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in use (adjective) (of facilities such as telephones or lavatories) unavailable for use by anyone else or indicating unavailability; (`engaged' is a British term for a busy telephone line).
  • use up (verb) Use up (resources or materials).
  • ill-use (verb) Treat badly.
  • fair use (noun) The conditions under which you can use material that is copyrighted by someone else without paying royalties.
  • out of use (adjective) Closed to traffic.
  • use immunity (noun) An exemption that displaces the privilege against self-incrimination; neither compelled testimony or any fruits of it can be used against the witness who therefore can no longer fear self-incrimination.
  • context of use (noun) Discourse that surrounds a language unit and helps to determine its interpretation.
  • use of goods and services (noun) (economics) the utilization of economic goods to satisfy needs or in manufacturing.
