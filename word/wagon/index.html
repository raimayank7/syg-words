Wagon: meaning, synonyms and examples

Wagon is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any of various kinds of wheeled vehicles drawn by an animal or a tractor.

In Hindi
डिब्बा, गाड़ी
Synonyms
waggon, police van, police wagon, paddy wagon, patrol wagon, coaster wagon, beach wagon, station wagon, estate car, beach waggon

Wagon as a noun

  1. Any of various kinds of wheeled vehicles drawn by an animal or a tractor.

    Synonyms: waggon · A kind of: wheeled vehicle · Kinds: bandwagon, cart, chuck wagon, covered wagon, ice wagon, lorry, milk wagon, tramcar

  2. Van used by police to transport prisoners.

    Synonyms: police van, police wagon, paddy wagon, patrol wagon · A kind of: van

  3. A group of seven bright stars in the constellation Ursa Major.

  4. A child's four-wheeled toy cart sometimes used for coasting.

    Synonyms: coaster wagon · A kind of: wheeled vehicle

  5. A car that has a long body and rear door with space behind rear seat.

    Synonyms: beach wagon, station wagon, estate car, beach waggon, station waggon, waggon · A kind of: car · Kinds: shooting brake
