Warm: meaning, synonyms and examples

Warm is used as an adjective (10 senses), verb (2 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Having or producing a comfortable and agreeable degree of heat or imparting or maintaining heat.

In Hindi
गरम, गुनगुना
Synonyms
affectionate, fond, lovesome, tender, strong, quick, ardent, warm up, warmly
Opposites
cool
Word family
warmness, warming, warmer

Warm as an adjective

  1. Having or producing a comfortable and agreeable degree of heat or imparting or maintaining heat.

    “A warm body.” “A warm room.” “A warm climate.”

    Opposite: cool · Similar: lukewarm, warmed, warming

  2. Psychologically warm; friendly and responsive.

    “A warm greeting.” “A warm personality.” “Warm support.”

    Opposite: cool · Similar: cordial, hearty

  3. (color) inducing the impression of warmth; used especially of reds and oranges and yellows.

    “Warm reds and yellows and orange.”

    Opposite: cool · Similar: hot

  4. Having or displaying warmth or affection.

    “Affectionate children.” “A fond embrace.” “Fond of his nephew.”

    Synonyms: affectionate, fond, lovesome, tender · Similar: loving

  5. Freshly made or left.

    “A warm trail.” “The scent is warm.”

    Synonyms: strong · Similar: fresh

  6. Easily aroused or excited.

    “A quick temper.” “A warm temper.”

    Synonyms: quick · Similar: excitable

  7. Characterized by strong enthusiasm.

    “Ardent revolutionaries.” “Warm support.”

    Synonyms: ardent · Similar: enthusiastic

  8. Characterized by liveliness or excitement or disagreement.

    “A warm debate.”

    Similar: lively

  9. Uncomfortable because of possible danger or trouble.

    “Made things warm for the bookies.”

    Similar: uncomfortable

  10. Of a seeker; near to the object sought.

    “You're getting warm.” “Hot on the trail.”

    Similar: near

Warm as a verb

  1. Get warm or warmer.

    “The soup warmed slowly on the stove.”

    Synonyms: warm up · A kind of: change

  2. Make warm or warmer.

    “The blanket will warm you.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: chafe

Warm as an adverb

  1. In a warm manner.

    “Warmly dressed.” “Warm-clad skiers.”

    Synonyms: warmly

Phrases and compounds with "warm"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • warm to (verb) Become excited about.
  • warm up (verb) Run until the normal working temperature is reached.
  • warm-up (noun) Exercising in preparation for strenuous activity.
  • warm front (noun) The front of an advancing mass of warmer air.
  • warm-toned (adjective) Used of music.
  • warm-blooded (adjective) Having warm blood (in animals whose body temperature is internally regulated).
  • warm the bench (verb) Be out of the game.
