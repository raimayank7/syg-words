Wash: meaning, synonyms and examples

Wash is used as a verb (13 senses) and noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Clean with some chemical process.

In Hindi
धोना
Synonyms
rinse, lave, launder, wash out, wash off, wash away, moisten, dampen, lap, washing
Word family
washable, washer, washing

Wash as a verb

  1. Clean with some chemical process.

    Synonyms: rinse · A kind of: serve · Kinds: elute

  2. Cleanse (one's body) with soap and water.

    Synonyms: lave · A kind of: cleanse · Kinds: sponge down, scrub, gargle, shampoo, hush

  3. Cleanse with a cleaning agent, such as soap, and water.

    “Wash the towels, please!”

    Synonyms: launder · A kind of: clean · Kinds: water-wash, wash out, pressure-wash, suds, rinse, cradle, stonewash, handwash

  4. Move by or as if by water.

    “The swollen river washed away the footbridge.”

    A kind of: move

  5. Be capable of being washed.

    “Does this material wash?”

    A kind of: be

  6. Admit to testing or proof.

    “This silly excuse won't wash in traffic court.”

    A kind of: stand

  7. Separate dirt or gravel from (precious minerals).

    A kind of: separate · Kinds: pan

  8. Apply a thin coating of paint, metal, etc., to.

    A kind of: cover · Kinds: whitewash

  9. Remove by the application of water or other liquid and soap or some other cleaning agent.

    “He washed the dirt from his coat.” “The nurse washed away the blood.” “Can you wash away the spots on the windows?”

    Synonyms: wash out, wash off, wash away · A kind of: remove

  10. Form by erosion.

    “The river washed a ravine into the mountainside.”

    A kind of: erode

  11. Make moist.

    “The dew moistened the meadows.”

    Synonyms: moisten, dampen · A kind of: wet · Kinds: humidify, baste

  12. Wash or flow against.

    “The waves laved the shore.”

    Synonyms: lave, lap · A kind of: flow

  13. To cleanse (itself or another animal) by licking.

    “The cat washes several times a day.”

    A kind of: cleanse

Wash as a noun

  1. A thin coat of water-base paint.

    A kind of: water-base paint · Kinds: blackwash, calcimine, color wash, whitewash

  2. The work of cleansing (usually with soap and water).

    Synonyms: washing, lavation · A kind of: work · Kinds: washup, ablution, dishwashing, washing-up, window-washing, rinse, soak, laundering

  3. The dry bed of an intermittent stream (as at the bottom of a canyon).

    Synonyms: dry wash · A kind of: streambed

  4. The erosive process of washing away soil or gravel by water (as from a roadway).

    “From the house they watched the washout of their newly seeded lawn by the water.”

    Synonyms: washout · A kind of: soil erosion · Kinds: rain-wash

  5. The flow of air that is driven backwards by an aircraft propeller.

    Synonyms: slipstream, airstream, race, backwash · A kind of: flow

  6. A watercolor made by applying a series of monochrome washes one over the other.

    Synonyms: wash drawing · A kind of: watercolor

  7. Garments or white goods that can be cleaned by laundering.

    Synonyms: laundry, washing, washables · A kind of: garment, white goods

  8. Any enterprise in which losses and gains cancel out.

    “At the end of the year the accounting department showed that it was a wash.”

    A kind of: business activity

Phrases and compounds with "wash"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • wash up (verb) Wash one's face and hands.
  • dry wash (noun) The dry bed of an intermittent stream (as at the bottom of a canyon).
  • wash off (verb) Remove by the application of water or other liquid and soap or some other cleaning agent.
  • wash out (verb) Prevent or interrupt due to rain.
  • acid-wash (verb) Wash with acid so as to achieve a bleached look.
  • flat wash (noun) Ironing that can be done mechanically.
  • hand-wash (verb) Wash or launder by hand instead of with a machine.
  • rain-wash (noun) The washing away of soil or other loose material by rain.
  • wash away (verb) Eliminate.
  • wash down (verb) Eat food accompanied by lots of liquid; also use metaphorically.
  • wash room (noun) A toilet that is available to the public.
  • wish-wash (noun) Any thin watery drink.
  • color wash (noun) A wash of whitewash or other water-base paint tinted with a colored pigment.
  • stone-wash (verb) Wash with stones to achieve a worn appearance.
  • water-wash (verb) Wash with water.
  • colour wash (noun) A wash of whitewash or other water-base paint tinted with a colored pigment.
