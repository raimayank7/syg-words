Wear: meaning, synonyms and examples

Wear is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be dressed in.

In Hindi
पहनना; घिसना
Synonyms
have on, bear, wear off, wear out, wear down, wear thin, hold out, endure, break, bust
Word family
wearable, wearer, wearing

Wear as a verb

  1. Be dressed in.

    “She was wearing yellow that day.”

    Synonyms: have on

  2. Have on one's person.

    “He wore a red ribbon.” “Bear a scar.”

    Synonyms: bear · A kind of: have

  3. Have in one's aspect; wear an expression of one's attitude or personality.

    “He always wears a smile.”

    A kind of: have

  4. Deteriorate through use or stress.

    “The constant friction wore out the cloth.”

    Synonyms: wear off, wear out, wear down, wear thin · A kind of: deteriorate · Kinds: ablate, scuff

  5. Have or show an appearance of.

    “Wear one's hair in a certain way.”

    A kind of: have

  6. Last and be usable.

    “This dress wore well for almost ten years.”

    Synonyms: hold out, endure · A kind of: last

  7. Go to pieces.

    “The lawn mower finally broke.” “The gears wore out.” “The old chair finally fell apart completely.”

    Synonyms: break, wear out, bust, fall apart · A kind of: decay · Kinds: fray

  8. Exhaust or get tired through overuse or great strain or stress.

    “We wore ourselves out on this hike.”

    Synonyms: tire, wear upon, tire out, weary, jade, wear out, outwear, wear down · A kind of: indispose · Kinds: exhaust, overtire

  9. Put clothing on one's body.

    “What should I wear today?” “He put on his best suit for the wedding.” “The princess donned a long blue dress.”

    Synonyms: put on, get into, don, assume · A kind of: dress · Kinds: hat, try on, scarf, slip on

Wear as a noun

  1. Impairment resulting from long use.

    “The tires showed uneven wear.”

    A kind of: deterioration

  2. A covering designed to be worn on a person's body.

    Synonyms: clothing, article of clothing, vesture, wearable, habiliment · A kind of: covering, consumer goods · Kinds: accessory, apparel, array, attire, beachwear, black, blue, change

  3. The act of having on your person as a covering or adornment.

    “She bought it for everyday wear.”

    Synonyms: wearing · A kind of: act

Phrases and compounds with "wear"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • wear off (verb) Deteriorate through use or stress.
  • wear out (verb) Exhaust or get tired through overuse or great strain or stress.
  • hand wear (noun) Clothing for the hands.
  • wear away (verb) Cut away in small pieces.
  • wear down (verb) Exhaust or get tired through overuse or great strain or stress.
  • wear ship (verb) Turn away from the wind.
  • wear thin (verb) Deteriorate through use or stress.
  • wear upon (verb) Exhaust or get tired through overuse or great strain or stress.
  • wear round (verb) Turn into the wind.
  • leisure wear (noun) Informal clothing designed to be worn when you are relaxing.
  • athletic wear (noun) Attire worn for sport or for casual wear.
  • ready-to-wear (noun) Ready-made clothing.
  • wash-and-wear (noun) A fabric treated to be easily washable and to require no ironing.
  • wear and tear (noun) Decrease in value of an asset due to obsolescence or use.
  • wear the trousers (verb) Exercise authority or be in charge.
