Whip: meaning, synonyms and examples

Whip is used as a verb (6 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Beat severely with a whip or rod.

In Hindi
चाबुक; फेंटना
Synonyms
flog, welt, lather, lash, slash, strap, trounce, worst, pip, mop up
Word family
whipper, whipping

Whip as a verb

  1. Beat severely with a whip or rod.

    “The teacher often flogged the students.” “The children were severely trounced.”

    Synonyms: flog, welt, lather, lash, slash, strap, trounce · A kind of: beat · Kinds: flagellate, leather, horsewhip, switch, cowhide, cat, birch

  2. Defeat thoroughly.

    “He mopped up the floor with his opponents.”

    Synonyms: worst, pip, mop up, rack up · A kind of: beat

  3. Thrash about flexibly in the manner of a whiplash.

    “The tall grass whipped in the wind.”

    A kind of: convulse

  4. Strike as if by whipping.

    “The curtain whipped her face.”

    Synonyms: lash · A kind of: strike · Kinds: urticate

  5. Whip with or as if with a wire whisk.

    “Whisk the eggs.”

    Synonyms: whisk · A kind of: beat

  6. Subject to harsh criticism.

    “The Senator blistered the administration in his speech on Friday.” “The professor scaled the students.” “Your invectives scorched the community.”

    Synonyms: blister, scald · A kind of: attack

Whip as a noun

  1. An instrument with a handle and a flexible lash that is used for whipping.

    A kind of: instrument · Kinds: cat-o'-nine-tails, cowhide, horsewhip, knout, quirt, riding crop, scourge, strap

  2. A legislator appointed by the party to enforce discipline.

    Synonyms: party whip · A kind of: legislator

  3. A dessert made of sugar and stiffly beaten egg whites or cream and usually flavored with fruit.

    A kind of: dessert · Kinds: prune whip

  4. (golf) the flexibility of the shaft of a golf club.

    A kind of: flexibility

  5. A quick blow delivered with a whip or whiplike object.

    “The whip raised a red welt.”

    Synonyms: lash, whiplash · A kind of: blow
