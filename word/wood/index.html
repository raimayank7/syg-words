Wood: meaning, synonyms and examples

Wood is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: The hard fibrous lignified substance under the bark of trees.

Wood as a noun

  1. The hard fibrous lignified substance under the bark of trees.

    A kind of: plant material · Kinds: bentwood, pine, larch, fir, cedar, spruce, hemlock, cypress

  2. The trees and other plants in a large densely wooded area.

    Synonyms: forest, woods · A kind of: vegetation · Kinds: bosk, grove, jungle, rain forest, old growth, second growth

  3. United States film actress (1938-1981).

  4. English conductor (1869-1944).

  5. United States painter noted for works based on life in the Midwest (1892-1942).

  6. Any wind instrument other than the brass instruments.

    Synonyms: woodwind, woodwind instrument · A kind of: wind instrument · Kinds: beating-reed instrument, flute

  7. A golf club with a long shaft used to hit long shots; originally made with a wooden head.

    “Metal woods are now standard.”

    A kind of: golf club · Kinds: brassie, driver, metal wood, spoon

Phrases and compounds with "wood"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bow wood (noun) Small shrubby deciduous yellowwood tree of south central United States having spines, glossy dark green leaves and an inedible fruit that resembles an orange; its hard orange-colored wood used for bows by Native Americans; frequently planted as boundary hedge.
  • elk-wood (noun) Small deciduous tree of eastern North America having creamy white flowers and large leaves in formations like umbrellas at the ends of branches.
  • raw wood (noun) Wood that is not finished or painted.
  • saw wood (verb) Breathe noisily during one's sleep.
  • tar-wood (noun) New Zealand shrub.
  • wood ant (noun) Reddish-brown European ant typically living in anthills in woodlands.
  • wood hen (noun) Flightless New Zealand rail of thievish disposition having short wings each with a spur used in fighting.
  • wood rat (noun) Any of various small short-tailed rodents of the northern hemisphere having soft fur grey above and white below with furred tails and large ears; some are hosts for Ixodes pacificus and Ixodes scapularis (Lyme disease ticks).
  • wood tar (noun) Any tar obtained by the destructive distillation of wood.
  • wood-rat (noun) Any of various small short-tailed rodents of the northern hemisphere having soft fur grey above and white below with furred tails and large ears; some are hosts for Ixodes pacificus and Ixodes scapularis (Lyme disease ticks).
  • ruby wood (noun) Hard durable wood of red sandalwood trees (Pterocarpus santalinus); prized for cabinetwork.
  • silk wood (noun) A fast-growing tropical American evergreen having white flowers and white fleshy edible fruit; bark yields a silky fiber used in cordage and wood is valuable for staves.
  • wood coal (noun) Intermediate between peat and bituminous coal.
  • wood duck (noun) Showy North American duck that nests in hollow trees.
  • wood fern (noun) Any of various ferns of the genus Dryopteris.
  • wood file (noun) A coarse file with sharp pointed projections.
