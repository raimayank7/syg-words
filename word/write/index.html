Write: meaning, synonyms and examples

Write is used as a verb (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Produce a literary work.

In Hindi
लिखना
Synonyms
compose, pen, indite, publish, drop a line, save, spell
Word family
writer, writing

Write as a verb

  1. Produce a literary work.

    “She composed a poem.” “He wrote four novels.”

    Synonyms: compose, pen, indite · A kind of: create verbally · Kinds: draw, lyric, write on, profile, paragraph, paragraph, write off, dash off

  2. Communicate or express by writing.

    “Please write to me every week.”

    A kind of: communicate · Kinds: write in, style, apostrophize, rewrite, sign, type, handwrite, jot down

  3. Have (one's written work) issued for publication.

    “How many books did Georges Simenon write?” “She published 25 books during her long career.”

    Synonyms: publish · A kind of: create verbally

  4. Communicate (with) in writing.

    “Write her soon, please!”

    Synonyms: drop a line · A kind of: correspond

  5. Communicate by letter.

    “He wrote that he would be coming soon.”

    A kind of: communicate

  6. Write music.

    “Beethoven composed nine symphonies.”

    Synonyms: compose · A kind of: make · Kinds: counterpoint, set to music, arrange, score, melodize, harmonize, instrument

  7. Mark or trace on a surface.

    “The artist wrote Chinese characters on a big piece of white paper.” “Russian is written with the Cyrillic alphabet.”

    A kind of: trace · Kinds: dot, stenograph, calligraph, cross, superscribe, superscribe, capitalize, letter

  8. Record data on a computer.

    “Boot-up instructions are written on the hard disk.”

    Synonyms: save · A kind of: record · Kinds: overwrite

  9. Write or name the letters that comprise the conventionally accepted form of (a word or part of a word).

    “He spelled the word wrong in this letter.”

    Synonyms: spell · Kinds: spell out, hyphenate

  10. Create code, write a computer program.

    “She writes code faster than anybody else.”

    A kind of: create by mental act

Phrases and compounds with "write"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • write in (verb) Cast a vote by inserting a name that does not appear on the ballot.
  • write of (verb) Write about a particular topic.
  • write on (verb) Write about a particular topic.
  • write up (verb) Bring to public notice by writing, with praise or condemnation.
  • write-in (noun) A candidate for public office whose name does not appear on the ballot and so must be written on the ballot by the voters.
  • write off (verb) Concede the loss or worthlessness of something or somebody.
  • write out (verb) Put into writing; write in complete form.
  • write-off (noun) (accounting) reduction in the book value of an asset.
  • write copy (verb) Write for commercial publications.
  • write down (verb) Put down in writing; of texts, musical compositions, etc.
  • write-down (noun) (accounting) reduction in the book value of an asset.
  • write about (verb) Write about a particular topic.
  • tax write-off (noun) A reduction in the gross amount on which a tax is calculated; reduces taxes by the percentage fixed for the taxpayer's income bracket.
  • write in code (verb) Convert ordinary language into code.
  • write-in candidate (noun) A candidate for public office whose name does not appear on the ballot and so must be written on the ballot by the voters.
  • compact disc write-once (noun) A compact disc on which you can write only once and thereafter is read-only memory.
