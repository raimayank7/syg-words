Wrong: meaning, synonyms and examples

Wrong is used as an adjective (9 senses), noun (2 senses), verb (1 sense) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Not correct; not in conformity with fact or truth.

Wrong as an adjective

  1. Not correct; not in conformity with fact or truth.

    “An incorrect calculation.” “The report in the paper is wrong.” “Your information is wrong.”

    Synonyms: incorrect · Opposite: right · Similar: erroneous, fallacious, false

  2. Contrary to conscience or morality or law.

    “It is wrong for the rich to take advantage of the poor.” “Cheating is wrong.” “It is wrong to lie.”

    Opposite: right · Similar: condemnable, base, misguided

  3. Not appropriate for a purpose or occasion.

    “Said all the wrong things.”

    Synonyms: improper · Similar: inappropriate

  4. Not functioning properly.

    “Something is amiss.” “Has gone completely haywire.” “Something is wrong with the engine.”

    Synonyms: amiss, awry, haywire · Similar: malfunctioning

  5. Based on or acting or judging in error.

    “It is wrong to think that way.”

    Opposite: right · Similar: wrongheaded

  6. Not in accord with established usage or procedure.

    “The wrong medicine.” “The wrong way to shuck clams.” “It is incorrect for a policeman to accept gifts.”

    Synonyms: incorrect · Similar: improper

  7. Used of the side of cloth or clothing intended to face inward.

    “Socks worn wrong side out.”

    Similar: inside

  8. Badly timed.

    “An ill-timed intervention.” “You think my intrusion unseasonable.” “An untimely remark.”

    Synonyms: ill-timed, unseasonable, untimely · Similar: inopportune

  9. Characterized by errors; not agreeing with a model or not following established rules.

    “He submitted a faulty report.” “An incorrect transcription.”

    Synonyms: faulty, incorrect · Similar: inaccurate

Wrong as a noun

  1. That which is contrary to the principles of justice or law.

    “He feels that you are in the wrong.”

    Synonyms: wrongfulness · Opposite: right · A kind of: injustice

  2. Any harm or injury resulting from a violation of a legal right.

    Synonyms: legal injury, damage · A kind of: injury

Wrong as a verb

  1. Treat unjustly; do wrong to.

    Opposite: right · A kind of: treat · Kinds: aggrieve, sandbag, victimize

Wrong as an adverb

  1. In an inaccurate manner.

    “He decided to reveal the details only after other sources had reported them incorrectly.” “She guessed wrong.”

    Synonyms: incorrectly, wrongly

Phrases and compounds with "wrong"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go wrong (verb) Be unsuccessful.
  • wrong 'un (noun) A cricket ball bowled as if to break one way that actually breaks in the opposite way.
  • civil wrong (noun) (law) any wrongdoing for which an action for damages may be brought.
  • wrong-side-out (adjective) With the inside surface on the outside.
  • wrong-site surgery (noun) A surgical operation performed on the wrong part of the body.
  • sense of right and wrong (noun) Motivation deriving logically from ethical or moral principles that govern a person's thoughts and actions.
