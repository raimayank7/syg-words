Yard: meaning, synonyms and examples

Yard is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A unit of length equal to 3 feet; defined as 91.44 centimeters; originally taken to be the average length of a stride.

In Hindi
आँगन; गज़
Synonyms
pace, grounds, curtilage, thousand, one thousand, chiliad, grand, thou, cubic yard, railway yard

Yard as a noun

  1. A unit of length equal to 3 feet; defined as 91.44 centimeters; originally taken to be the average length of a stride.

    Synonyms: pace · A kind of: linear unit

  2. The enclosed land around a house or other building.

    “It was a small house with almost no yard.”

    Synonyms: grounds, curtilage · A kind of: field · Kinds: backyard, dooryard, front yard, garden, playground, side yard

  3. A tract of land enclosed for particular activities (sometimes paved and usually associated with buildings).

    “They opened a repair yard on the edge of town.”

    A kind of: tract · Kinds: junkyard, schoolyard, churchyard, tiltyard

  4. The cardinal number that is the product of 10 and 100.

    Synonyms: thousand, one thousand, chiliad, grand, thou · A kind of: large integer · Kinds: millenary

  5. A unit of volume (as for sand or gravel).

    Synonyms: cubic yard · A kind of: volume unit

  6. A tract of land where logs are accumulated.

    A kind of: tract

  7. An area having a network of railway tracks and sidings for storage and maintenance of cars and engines.

    Synonyms: railway yard, railyard · A kind of: tract · Kinds: marshalling yard

  8. A long horizontal spar tapered at the end and used to support and spread a square sail or lateen.

    A kind of: spar · Kinds: main yard

  9. An enclosure for animals (as chicken or livestock).

    A kind of: enclosure · Kinds: barnyard, chicken yard, farmyard, stockyard

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • main yard (noun) Yard for a square mainsail.
  • navy yard (noun) A military shipyard.
  • rope yard (noun) Workplace consisting of a long narrow path or shed where rope is made.
  • side yard (noun) The grounds at either side of a house.
  • yard bird (noun) A military recruit who is assigned menial tasks.
  • yard line (noun) In football; line parallel to the goal lines indicating position on the field.
  • yard sale (noun) An outdoor sale of used personal or household items held on the seller's premises.
  • cubic yard (noun) A unit of volume (as for sand or gravel).
  • front yard (noun) The yard in front of a house; between the house and the street.
  • waste-yard (noun) A piece of land where waste materials are dumped.
  • yard goods (noun) Merchandise in the form of fabrics sold by the yard.
  • yard grass (noun) Coarse annual grass having fingerlike spikes of flowers; native to Old World tropics; a naturalized weed elsewhere.
  • square yard (noun) A unit of area equal to one yard by one yard square.
  • yard donkey (noun) A winch (or system of winches) powered by an engine and used to haul logs from a stump to a landing or to a skid road.
  • yard marker (noun) (football) a marker indicating the yard line.
