Yield: meaning, synonyms and examples

Yield is used as a verb (13 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be the cause or source of.

In Hindi
उपज; झुक जाना
Synonyms
give, afford, give way, render, return, generate, concede, cede, grant, relent
Opposites
stand
Word family
yielding, yielder

Yield as a verb

  1. Be the cause or source of.

    “He gave me a lot of trouble.” “Our meeting afforded much interesting information.”

    Synonyms: give, afford · A kind of: supply · Kinds: open, leave

  2. End resistance, as under pressure or force.

    “The door yielded to repeated blows with a battering ram.”

    Synonyms: give way · A kind of: change

  3. Give or supply.

    “The cow brings in 5 liters of milk.” “This year's crop yielded 1,000 bushels of corn.” “The estate renders some revenue for the family.”

    Synonyms: render, return, give, generate · A kind of: produce

  4. Give over; surrender or relinquish to the physical control of another.

    Synonyms: concede, cede, grant · A kind of: give

  5. Give in, as to influence or pressure.

    Synonyms: relent, soften · Opposite: stand · Kinds: truckle

  6. Move in order to make room for someone for something.

    “The park gave way to a supermarket.” “`Move over,' he told the crowd.”

    Synonyms: move over, give way, give, ease up · A kind of: move

  7. Cause to happen or be responsible for.

    “His two singles gave the team the victory.”

    Synonyms: give · A kind of: make

  8. Be willing to concede.

    “I grant you this much.”

    Synonyms: concede, grant · A kind of: agree · Kinds: forgive

  9. Be fatally overwhelmed.

    Synonyms: succumb · A kind of: die

  10. Bring in.

    “Interest-bearing accounts.” “How much does this savings certificate pay annually?”

    Synonyms: pay, bear · A kind of: gain · Kinds: net, pay off

  11. Be flexible under stress of physical force.

    “This material doesn't give.”

    Synonyms: give · A kind of: stretch

  12. Cease opposition; stop fighting.

    Kinds: surrender, fall

  13. Consent reluctantly.

    Synonyms: give in, succumb, knuckle under, buckle under · A kind of: accept · Kinds: submit

Yield as a noun

  1. Production of a certain amount.

    Synonyms: output · A kind of: production · Kinds: crop

  2. The income or profit arising from such transactions as the sale of land or other property.

    “The average return was about 5%.”

    Synonyms: return, issue, take, takings, proceeds, payoff · A kind of: income · Kinds: economic rent, payback

  3. An amount of a product.

    Synonyms: fruit · A kind of: product

  4. The quantity of something (as a commodity) that is created (usually within a given period of time).

    “Production was up in the second quarter.”

    Synonyms: output, production · A kind of: indefinite quantity · Kinds: picking

Phrases and compounds with "yield"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • yield up (verb) Surrender, as a result of pressure or force.
  • high-yield (adjective) Yielding a large amount of agricultural or industrial production.
  • high-yield bond (noun) A (speculative) bond with a credit rating of BB or lower; issued for leveraged buyouts and other takeovers by companies with questionable credit.
