About the word list

Every search on this site runs against one word list. This page says where it comes from, what it contains, and why a word you expected may be missing.

The source: SCOWL

The list is built from SCOWL, "Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists", a collection compiled and maintained by Kevin Atkinson and published at wordlist.aspell.net. SCOWL is the basis of several well-known open-source spelling checkers. We used the release dated 7 December 2020. Its licence permits anyone to use, copy, modify and distribute the lists provided the copyright notice is kept, and the full notice is published with this site: SCOWL copyright and permission notice. SCOWL draws on several earlier lists, each credited in that notice.

It is a general English word list. It is not the official word list of any board game, app or tournament, and we make no claim that a word found here is allowed in any particular game. Follow the dictionary named in your own game's rules.

What we took from it

  • The "words" lists for English as a whole and for American, British, Canadian and Australian spellings, so both colour and color, organise and organize are present.
  • SCOWL sizes 10 to 70. SCOWL grades words by how widely known they are; size 10 holds the few thousand commonest words, and 70 is described by its maker as the size of a large dictionary.
  • Only words written entirely in the small letters a to z, from 2 to 15 letters long.

The result is 114,118 words.

What is left out

  • Names of people, places and brands, and anything else that needs a capital letter.
  • Abbreviations and contractions, and words with apostrophes or hyphens (so "don't" and "well-known" are absent).
  • The rarest words: SCOWL's sizes 80 and 95, which contain a great many obscure and doubtful entries.
  • Offensive words. We removed a set of slurs and vulgar words because the site is for a general audience, including children. A word checker that says "not found" for such a word is doing so on purpose.
  • A few two-letter entries that are only plurals of letter names or abbreviations of units.

Mistakes and odd entries

No word list is perfect. A list built for spelling checkers aims to accept anything that might be correctly spelt, so it includes some short technical forms and variant spellings that will look strange. It may also lack a new word. We have not edited the list by hand beyond the removals described above. If you find an entry that should not be here, or a common word that is missing, please tell us.

Meanings

SCOWL is a list of spellings without definitions, so our tools cannot tell you what a word means, with one exception: we wrote short meanings for all the two-letter words ourselves. For any other word, look it up in a dictionary you trust.

Privacy

The list is downloaded to your browser when you first search, and every search is worked out on your own device. The letters you type are not sent to us.
