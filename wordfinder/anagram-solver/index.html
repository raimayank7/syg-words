Anagram solver

Type a word or a jumble. The solver lists every word that uses all the letters, each exactly once.

What an anagram is

An anagram of a word uses exactly the same letters, each the same number of times, in a different order. LISTEN, SILENT, ENLIST, INLETS and TINSEL are all anagrams of one another. A word finder would also offer TILE or NEST from those letters; the anagram solver does not, because they leave letters unused.

How to use the solver

  1. Type the word or the jumbled letters. Up to 15 letters.
  2. Tap Solve.
  3. You get every word in our list that uses all the letters. If you typed a real word, that word itself is left out, so that what you see are its anagrams.

A ? can stand for one unknown letter. If nothing is found, the page offers a link to the word finder, which shows the shorter words your letters can make.

Where anagrams turn up

  • Newspaper jumbles and "unscramble the word" exercises in school books. Each jumble normally has one intended answer; the solver may show others that also fit.
  • Cryptic crosswords, where a clue hides an anagram behind a hint word such as "mixed", "broken" or "upset". Enter the letters the clue points to.
  • Quiz rounds. Anagrams of film titles, cities or classmates' names make an easy round for a family quiz. Our solver finds single dictionary words only, so use it to check single-word answers.
  • Word play. Writers have long enjoyed anagrams that comment on the original: "dormitory" and "dirty room" is the best-known example. Phrases of several words are beyond this tool.

Why some jumbles have many answers

Common letters combine in many ways. In our list the letters A, E, L, S, T make nine words (least, slate, stale, steal, tales and more), and A, E, P, R, S make nine as well. A setter who wants one answer avoids such sets. See the largest anagram families in the list.

Solving without a tool

Most anagrams of up to seven letters can be solved by hand with a method: take out a common ending, join letters that go together, write the letters in a circle. All ten methods are on how to solve anagrams.

Limits

The solver finds single words of 2 to 15 letters from a general English word list. It does not find names, phrases of two or more words, or words with hyphens. About the word list.
