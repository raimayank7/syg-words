Words ending in -able

The ending -able makes an adjective meaning "can be done" (wash, washable; read, readable). Its twin, -ible, is used in an older, fixed group of words (visible, possible) and is no longer added to new ones.

Spelling: drop a silent E (love, lovable), but keep it after a soft C or G (notice, noticeable; change, changeable); change Y to I (rely, reliable).

Our word list has 1,194 words that end in -able. The shortest are arable, enable, liable, stable, unable, usable, viable, doable. Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include A, B, L, E, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
