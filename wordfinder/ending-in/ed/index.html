Words ending in -ed

The ending -ed makes the past tense and past participle of regular verbs (walk, walked) and many adjectives (a tired child). Irregular verbs go their own way (go, went), so not every verb has an -ed form.

Spelling: add only D after a silent E (bake, baked); double the last consonant after a single short vowel (plan, planned); change a final Y after a consonant to I (carry, carried).

Our word list has 9,233 words that end in -ed. The shortest are aced, aged, aped, awed, axed, bled, bred, cued. Below are the 4,072 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. To find the ones you can make from your own letters, use the word finder with ed in the "Ends with" filter.

Not every word that ends in these letters carries the suffix. Some simply happen to end that way. The list is by spelling, not by meaning.

Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include E, D, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
