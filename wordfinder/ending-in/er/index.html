Words ending in -er

The ending -er has two jobs. Added to a verb it names the doer (teach, teacher; bake, baker). Added to a short adjective it makes the comparative (tall, taller). Many plain words also happen to end in these two letters (water, paper, under).

Spelling: add only R after E (wide, wider); double the consonant after a single short vowel (big, bigger; run, runner); change Y to I (happy, happier).

Our word list has 5,720 words that end in -er. The shortest are beer, deer, doer, ever, jeer, leer, over, peer. Below are the 1,687 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. To find the ones you can make from your own letters, use the word finder with er in the "Ends with" filter.

Not every word that ends in these letters carries the suffix. Some simply happen to end that way. The list is by spelling, not by meaning.

Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include E, R, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
