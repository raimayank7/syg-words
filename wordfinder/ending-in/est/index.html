Words ending in -est

The ending -est makes the superlative of short adjectives and adverbs: the highest degree (tall, tallest; fast, fastest). Longer adjectives use "most" instead (most beautiful). A few plain words also end this way: forest, honest, request.

Spelling: the same rules as -er. Add ST after E (wide, widest), double after a short vowel (big, biggest), change Y to I (happy, happiest).

Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include E, S, T, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
