Words ending in -ful

The ending -ful makes an adjective meaning "full of" (hope, hopeful; care, careful), and a noun for an amount (a spoonful, a handful). Note the single L: only the separate word "full" has two.

Spelling: change Y to I (beauty, beautiful; plenty, plentiful). The adverb adds -ly, which brings back a double L: carefully, hopefully.

Our word list has 230 words that end in -ful. The shortest are awful, artful, cupful, fitful, gutful, joyful, lawful, rueful. Below are the 86 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include F, U, L, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
