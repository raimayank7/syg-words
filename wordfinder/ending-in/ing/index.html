Words ending in -ing

The ending -ing makes the form of a verb that shows continuing action (I am reading) and the noun for the activity itself (reading is fun). It can be added to almost every verb, which is why it is one of the largest families in the list.

Spelling: drop a silent E before adding it (make, making); double the last consonant after a single short vowel (run, running; stop, stopping); keep Y (carry, carrying); change IE to Y (lie, lying).

Our word list has 8,637 words that end in -ing. The shortest are acing, aging, aping, awing, axing, being, bring, cling. Below are the 3,426 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. To find the ones you can make from your own letters, use the word finder with ing in the "Ends with" filter.

Not every word that ends in these letters carries the suffix. Some simply happen to end that way. The list is by spelling, not by meaning.

Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include I, N, G, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
