Words ending in -less

The ending -less makes an adjective meaning "without" (hope, hopeless; home, homeless). It is the opposite of -ful, and many words have both forms: careful and careless, useful and useless.

Spelling: the base word is normally unchanged, but a final Y after a consonant becomes I (penny, penniless; mercy, merciless).

Our word list has 332 words that end in -less. The shortest are unless, aimless, endless, godless, hapless, lawless, useless, witless. Below are the 67 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include L, E, S, S, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
