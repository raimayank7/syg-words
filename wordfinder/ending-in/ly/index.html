Words ending in -ly

The ending -ly turns an adjective into an adverb that tells how something is done (quick, quickly). Added to some nouns it makes an adjective instead (friend, friendly; day, daily).

Spelling: change Y to I (happy, happily); words ending in LE drop the E (gentle, gently); words ending in IC add -ally (basic, basically); words ending in L keep it, giving a double L (final, finally).

Our word list has 4,735 words that end in -ly. The shortest are ably, ally, duly, holy, idly, lily, oily, only. Below are the 572 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. To find the ones you can make from your own letters, use the word finder with ly in the "Ends with" filter.

Not every word that ends in these letters carries the suffix. Some simply happen to end that way. The list is by spelling, not by meaning.

Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include L, Y, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
