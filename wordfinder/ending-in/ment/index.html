Words ending in -ment

The ending -ment makes a noun from a verb: the action or its result (move, movement; pay, payment; agree, agreement). A few plain words end the same way without the suffix: cement, moment, comment.

Spelling: the verb usually stays whole (excite, excitement). Two well-known exceptions drop an E: argue, argument and judge, judgment (judgement is also used in British English).

Our word list has 519 words that end in -ment. The shortest are cement, foment, lament, moment, dement, loment, ailment, augment. Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include M, E, N, T, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
