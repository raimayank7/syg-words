Words ending in -ness

The ending -ness turns an adjective into a noun for the state or quality (kind, kindness; dark, darkness). It can be added to nearly any adjective, so the family is large and its words are easy to guess.

Spelling: change a final Y to I (happy, happiness). Adjectives ending in N keep it, giving a double N (thin, thinness; open, openness).

Our word list has 2,227 words that end in -ness. The shortest are badness, dryness, fitness, harness, illness, lioness, madness, sadness. Below are the 97 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include N, E, S, S, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
