Words ending in -ous

The ending -ous makes an adjective meaning "full of" or "having the quality of" (danger, dangerous; fame, famous). The longer forms -ious and -eous belong to the same family (curious, courageous).

Spelling: drop a silent E (fame, famous) but keep it after a soft G (courage, courageous); words ending in -our drop the U before -ous (humour, humorous; glamour, glamorous).

Our word list has 1,177 words that end in -ous. The shortest are pious, thous, bayous, famous, joyous, mucous, odious, porous. Below are the 140 everyday ones of up to nine letters, grouped by length; the list would be too long to show in full. Not every word that ends in these letters carries the suffix. Some simply happen to end that way. The list is by spelling, not by meaning.

Using the ending in word puzzles

When your letters include O, U, S, set those letters aside and see what the rest can spell, then join the two. It is the quickest way to reach a long word. Remember the spelling changes above: the base word may lose an E, double a letter or change Y to I when the ending is added.
