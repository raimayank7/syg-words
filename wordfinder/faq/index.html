Word Finder questions and answers

Short answers about how the tools work and what the word list contains. The longer story of the list is on about the word list.

What does the word finder do?

You type the letters you have, and it lists every word in our list that can be spelt with some or all of them, longest first. Each letter is used only as many times as you typed it.

What is the ? for?

A question mark stands for a blank that can be any letter. Type up to two or three of them along with your letters. More blanks mean many more results and a slower search.

What is the difference between the word finder and the anagram solver?

The word finder gives words of every length that fit inside your letters. The anagram solver gives only words that use all of the letters exactly once, which is what an anagram is.

How do I find a word when I know some of its letters?

Use the pattern finder. Type the letters you know in their places and a ? for each unknown letter, for example ?a?er for a five-letter word with A second and ER at the end. It suits crosswords and fill-in puzzles.

Is this the official word list of a board game?

No. It is a general English word list built from SCOWL, an open list made for spelling checkers. Different games use different dictionaries, and some words here will not be allowed in your game while some allowed words may be missing here.

Why is a word I know not in the list?

The list leaves out names, abbreviations, words with capitals, hyphens or apostrophes, very rare words and offensive words. A new or regional word may also be missing.

Why does the list contain words I have never heard of?

A large part of the list is marked rare: technical, dialect and old words that large dictionaries carry. They are shown in italics. Tick "Everyday words only" to hide them.

Does it include British and American spellings?

Yes, both. You will find colour and color, centre and center, organise and organize.

Can it tell me what a word means?

Only for the two-letter words, for which we wrote short meanings. The source list has spellings but no definitions.

Is using a word finder cheating?

In a game against another person, looking up answers during play is unfair unless everyone has agreed to it. Used after a game or for practice, it is one of the best ways to learn new words.

Are my letters sent anywhere?

No. The word list is downloaded once to your browser and the search is done on your own device.

How many letters can I enter?

Up to 15, which is the longest word in our list. With 9 or more letters the tool also loads the long-word part of the list, which takes a moment the first time.
