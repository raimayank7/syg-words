Choosing a good first guess in a five-letter puzzle

In guessing games you learn most from a first word that tests common letters, each once. In the five-letter words of our list the most frequent letters are E, S, A, R, O, L, I, T, N, D. A first guess made of five different letters from the top of that order, with two or three vowels, gives the most information. Follow with a second word that uses five new common letters.

Common patterns

  • Endings: -ed, -er, -es, -ly, -al, -le (table, apple), -ght (light, night), -tch (watch, catch).
  • Beginnings: st-, sh-, ch-, cr-, tr-, br-, gr-, sp-, bl-, cl-.
  • Double letters are commoner than players expect: hello, sunny, apple, floor, sheep. If a puzzle resists, test a double.
