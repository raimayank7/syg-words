Shapes of four-letter words

  • Silent E: a great many are consonant, vowel, consonant plus E, where the E makes the vowel long: cake, time, home, tune. Removing the E often leaves another word (hope, hop; kite, kit).
  • Vowel pairs: boat, rain, seed, moon, pier, soup.
  • Blends at the start or end: stop, clap, drum; fast, lamp, milk.
  • Double letters: ball, bell, hill, miss, buzz, jazz.

Good ones to know

With rare letters: jazz, quiz, zinc, zero, zone, jinx, onyx, oxen, exit, taxi, quay, quit. Heavy with vowels: area, aide, idea, oboe, euro, aura. Without A, E, I, O or U: hymn, myth, lynx, gyms.
