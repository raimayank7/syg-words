Word finder: words from your letters

Type up to 15 letters. Use ? for a blank. You get every word they can make, from 114,118 English words.

More filters

How to use the word finder

  1. Type the letters you have, in any order. Capitals and spaces do not matter.
  2. Add a ? for each blank or wild letter.
  3. Tap Find words. The longest words are listed first.
  4. To narrow the list, open More filters: words that start with, end with or contain certain letters, or words of one length only.

A letter is used only as often as you typed it: from the letters of LEMON you get MELON, but not LLAMA. Words in bold are everyday words; plain words are less common; words in italics are rare or technical. Tick "Everyday words only" when you want words everybody knows.

What people use it for

  • Word puzzles. "How many words can you make from these letters?" puzzles in newspapers and school worksheets. Type the letters and check your own list against ours.
  • Practice for word games. After a game, enter a set of letters that beat you and see what was possible. Please do not use it during a game against someone unless all the players agree.
  • Teaching. Give a class the letters of a long word such as "celebration" and ask for as many words as they can find in five minutes; then show the full list.
  • Party rounds. A quick game for a family gathering: write a long word on a board, and teams race to list shorter words made from it.
  • Names and gifts. See which words hide in the letters of a name, for a card or a quiz.

About the words

The finder searches 114,118 English words of 2 to 15 letters, taken from SCOWL, an open word list made for spelling checkers. It includes British and American spellings. It is a general word list, not the official list of any game or tournament. What is in the list and what is left out.
