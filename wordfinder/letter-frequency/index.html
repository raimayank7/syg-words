How to read these figures

This is a count over a word list, in which every word counts once, whether it is "the" or "syzygy". It answers the question a puzzle solver asks: if I pick a word from the dictionary, which letters is it likely to contain? It is not the same as the frequency of letters in ordinary writing, where very common short words are repeated again and again. In running text T, H and O rank higher than they do here, because of words like the, that, to and of; in a word list S, I and R rise, because of plurals, -ing forms and words with -er.

What stands out

  • E is first by a clear margin, with about one letter in nine.
  • The five vowels together make up 38% of all letters.
  • The four rarest letters, Z, X, Q, J, together account for only 1.1% of letters.
  • The most common last letters are S (34,581 words), E (13,700 words), D (11,235 words), Y (10,170 words), G (9,021 words), R (7,310 words). The lead of S comes from plurals and verb forms.

Commonest letter pairs

Counting every pair of neighbouring letters, the dozen most frequent are: IN (21,136), ER (20,532), ES (19,285), TI (14,573), TE (13,352), AT (13,118), RE (12,494), ON (12,368), NG (12,011), ED (11,443), ST (10,954), IS (10,796). Knowing the common pairs helps with anagrams: join them first and solve what is left.

Commonest double letters

The letters most often doubled are: SS (5,799), LL (5,371), OO (2,600), EE (2,449), TT (1,979), RR (1,639), PP (1,343), MM (1,178). Doubles of other letters are rare, and a few letters, such as H, J, Q, V, W, X and Y, are almost never doubled.

Using the table

  • Guessing games: open with words made of the top letters, each used once, to learn the most from every guess. In hangman, guessing E, then the other vowels and S, T, R, N is the classic order.
  • Simple codes: in a puzzle where each letter is replaced by another, the commonest symbol in a long message is probably E. Short messages do not always follow the averages.
  • Word games with tiles: the rare letters at the bottom of the table are the ones to learn short words for. See J, Q, X and Z.

The counts were made by program from our word list when this site was built. A different dictionary would give slightly different figures, but much the same order.
