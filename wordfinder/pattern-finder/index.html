Word Finder

Pattern finder: fill in the missing letters

Type the letters you know and a ? for each letter you do not. Good for crosswords and guessing games.

How to use the pattern finder

  1. Type the word with a ? in place of every letter you do not know. A dot, a dash, a star or a space works too. Example: ?a?er.
  2. The answer has exactly as many letters as your pattern.
  3. Optional: under "Letters it must contain", type letters you know are somewhere in the word but not where. Under "Letters it cannot contain", type letters you have ruled out.
  4. Tap Find.

For crosswords

When crossing answers have given you some letters, the pattern narrows the field at once. ??O?O?ILE has only a few fits. Read the clue again with the candidates in front of you and the right one is usually plain. If the clue is cryptic and you suspect an anagram, use the anagram solver on the letters instead.

For five-letter guessing games

In games where each guess tells you which letters are right, which are in the word but misplaced, and which are absent, all three kinds of clue fit this tool: put the letters whose place you know into the pattern, the misplaced ones under "must contain" and the absent ones under "cannot contain". Tick "Everyday words only", since such games nearly always choose common words as answers. We have our own daily game of this kind at Word Puzzle. If you use the finder on a daily puzzle, you take away your own fun; it is better kept for learning afterwards why a word was possible.

For hangman, fill-in puzzles and spelling

  • Hangman: enter the revealed letters and blanks, with the wrong guesses under "cannot contain".
  • Fill-in and codeword puzzles: pattern plus length is all you need.
  • Spelling: if you remember how a word starts and ends but not the middle, type what you remember: rh???m finds rhythm.
  • Rhymes and word families: ??ight lists the five-letter words ending in -ight.

Tips for better patterns

  • Count the squares carefully. One ? too many or too few gives a different list.
  • If you get hundreds of words, add a "must contain" or "cannot contain" letter, or tick "Everyday words only".
  • If you get none, check each known letter: one wrong crossing answer in a crossword is the usual cause.
  • Plurals and verb forms are separate words in the list, so ?????s will find them.

Limits

Patterns can be 2 to 15 letters long. The tool searches single words in a general English list; it knows no names, phrases or hyphenated words. About the word list.
