Words that start with A

Our word list has 7,263 words that start with A, which makes A the fourth most common first letter out of 26 (6.4% of all words). Of these, 2,290 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is N, followed by L and C. The commonest length is 9 letters (1,208 words), and the longest A word in the list is accomplishments, with 15 letters.

Many A words begin with the prefixes ab- (away), ad- (towards), anti- (against) and auto- (self), and with the plain prefix a- as in asleep and ashore. When you hold an A and a few common consonants, try these openings first.
