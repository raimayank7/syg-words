Words that start with B

Our word list has 6,416 words that start with B, which makes B the sixth most common first letter out of 26 (5.6% of all words). Of these, 2,294 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by E and R. The commonest length is 8 letters (1,140 words), and the longest B word in the list is bloodthirstiest, with 15 letters.

B words lean on the prefixes be- (befriend, belittle), bi- (two) and bio- (life). B likes to stand before L and R, so look for BL and BR starts such as blend and bring.
