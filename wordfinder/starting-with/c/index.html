Words that start with C

Our word list has 11,260 words that start with C, which makes C the second most common first letter out of 26 (9.9% of all words). Of these, 3,949 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is O, followed by A and H. The commonest length is 8 letters (1,753 words), and the longest C word in the list is characteristics, with 15 letters.

C is the second most common first letter in our list, helped by the big prefix families con-, com-, co- and counter-. CH, CL and CR are its favourite pairs. Remember that C can sound like K or like S.
