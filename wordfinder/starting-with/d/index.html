Words that start with D

Our word list has 7,049 words that start with D, which makes D the fifth most common first letter out of 26 (6.2% of all words). Of these, 2,632 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is E, followed by I and O. The commonest length is 9 letters (1,088 words), and the longest D word in the list is differentiating, with 15 letters.

A large share of D words begin with de- (undo or remove, as in defrost) or dis- (not, apart). If your letters include D, E or D, I, S, test those prefixes in front of the word you can already see.
