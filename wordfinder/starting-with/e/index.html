Words that start with E

Our word list has 4,849 words that start with E, which makes E the tenth most common first letter out of 26 (4.2% of all words). Of these, 1,709 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is X, followed by N and M. The commonest length is 9 letters (823 words), and the longest E word in the list is electromagnetic, with 15 letters.

E words are rich in prefixes: ex- (out), en- and em- (put into), extra- and electro-. Since E is the commonest letter in English, words that start with it often contain a second E too.
