Words that start with F

Our word list has 4,703 words that start with F, which makes F the 11th most common first letter out of 26 (4.1% of all words). Of these, 1,932 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is O, followed by L and A. The commonest length is 8 letters (861 words), and the longest F word in the list is fundamentalists, with 15 letters.

F starts fewer words than you might expect. Fore- (before, in front) is its main prefix, as in forecast. FL and FR are the usual blends, and the ending -ful belongs to F as well.
