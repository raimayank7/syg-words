Words that start with G

Our word list has 3,793 words that start with G, which makes G the 14th most common first letter out of 26 (3.3% of all words). Of these, 1,351 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is R, followed by A and E. The commonest length is 7 letters (651 words), and the longest G word in the list is generalisations, with 15 letters.

G pairs with L and R at the start of words (glad, green) and with H in ghost. The scientific prefix geo- (earth) gives a family of its own. A hard G comes before A, O and U; before E and I it is often soft.
