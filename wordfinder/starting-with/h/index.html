Words that start with H

Our word list has 4,244 words that start with H, which makes H the 13th most common first letter out of 26 (3.7% of all words). Of these, 1,410 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by O and E. The commonest length is 9 letters (621 words), and the longest H word in the list is heterosexuality, with 15 letters.

H words often begin with hyper- (over), hypo- (under), hydro- (water), hetero- and homo-. H almost never follows itself and is always followed by a vowel or Y at the start of a word.
