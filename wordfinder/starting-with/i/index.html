Words that start with I

Our word list has 4,508 words that start with I, which makes I the 12th most common first letter out of 26 (4.0% of all words). Of these, 1,759 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is N, followed by M and R. The commonest length is 10 letters (711 words), and the longest I word in the list is implementations, with 15 letters.

I has more prefixes than any other vowel: in-, im-, il- and ir- (not, or into), inter- (between) and intra- (within). Many long I words are simply a prefix fixed on a word you know.
