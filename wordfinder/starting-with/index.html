Which letters start the most words?

The first letters of English words are far from evenly shared. In our list of 114,118 words, S starts the most (12,772 words), then C and P. Together those three begin 29% of all the words. At the other end, X starts only 64. The order is quite different from the order of letters by overall use, where E comes first: E is everywhere inside words but begins fewer than one word in twenty.

The reason is prefixes. S has sub-, super- and semi- and blends with almost every consonant; C has con-, com- and counter-; P has pre-, pro- and per-; R has re-; U has un-. A letter with a productive prefix collects thousands of words that are simply that prefix fixed on something else. Our guide to prefixes and suffixes explains them.

What this means for word games and puzzles

  • When guessing an unknown word, S, C, P, A and D are the likeliest first letters.
  • A dictionary is thickest at S and C and thinnest at X, Y and Z, which is why thumb-indexed dictionaries group the last letters together.
  • If you are making a quiz or an alphabet game ("name an animal for each letter"), expect trouble at Q, X and Z, and allow words that merely contain those letters.
