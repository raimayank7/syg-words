Words that start with J

Our word list has 927 words that start with J, which makes J the 22nd most common first letter out of 26 (0.8% of all words). Of these, 384 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by U and O. The commonest length is 7 letters (196 words), and the longest J word in the list is jurisprudential, with 15 letters.

J is one of the rarest letters. It nearly always starts its word and is nearly always followed by a vowel. Short J words such as jab, jam, jet, jig, job, jog, joy and jug are worth knowing by heart.
