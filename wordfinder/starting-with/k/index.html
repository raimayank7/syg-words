Words that start with K

Our word list has 971 words that start with K, which makes K the 21st most common first letter out of 26 (0.9% of all words). Of these, 263 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is I, followed by E and N. The commonest length is 7 letters (160 words), and the longest K word in the list is kindergarteners, with 15 letters.

K starts few words. Watch for the silent K before N (knee, knife, knot, know). Kilo- (a thousand) is its one common prefix.
