Words that start with L

Our word list has 3,459 words that start with L, which makes L the 15th most common first letter out of 26 (3.0% of all words). Of these, 1,235 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by I and O. The commonest length is 8 letters (574 words), and the longest L word in the list is lackadaisically, with 15 letters.

L words have no big prefix family, so they are mostly short, old words: land, late, life, long, love. L doubles easily in the middle and at the end of words, so a second L in your letters is not wasted.
