Words that start with M

Our word list has 6,039 words that start with M, which makes M the eighth most common first letter out of 26 (5.3% of all words). Of these, 1,996 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by I and O. The commonest length is 9 letters (930 words), and the longest M word in the list is maintainability, with 15 letters.

M is strong in prefixes: mis- (wrongly), micro- (small), mega- and multi- (many), mid-, mono- (one). If you see M with I and S, try mis- before a verb.
