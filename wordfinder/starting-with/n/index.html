Words that start with N

Our word list has 2,421 words that start with N, which makes N the 19th most common first letter out of 26 (2.1% of all words). Of these, 703 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is O, followed by E and A. The commonest length is 9 letters (338 words), and the longest N word in the list is nearsightedness, with 15 letters.

Non- (not) accounts for a large part of the N list, since it can be fixed on to almost any noun or adjective. Outside that family, N words are mostly short and common.
