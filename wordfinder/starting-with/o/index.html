Words that start with O

Our word list has 3,059 words that start with O, which makes O the 17th most common first letter out of 26 (2.7% of all words). Of these, 991 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is V, followed by U and R. The commonest length is 9 letters (483 words), and the longest O word in the list is ophthalmologist, with 15 letters.

The big O families are out- (outrun, outside), over- (too much, above) and ob-. With O, U and T or O, V, E, R among your letters, look for one of these in front of a short verb.
