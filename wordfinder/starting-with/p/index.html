Words that start with P

Our word list has 9,353 words that start with P, which makes P the third most common first letter out of 26 (8.2% of all words). Of these, 3,215 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is R, followed by A and E. The commonest length is 9 letters (1,407 words), and the longest P word in the list is palaeontologist, with 15 letters.

P is the third most common first letter here, thanks to pre- (before), pro- (forward, for), per-, para-, poly- (many) and post- (after). PH sounds like F, and PL and PR are everywhere.
