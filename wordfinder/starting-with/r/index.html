Words that start with R

Our word list has 6,389 words that start with R, which makes R the seventh most common first letter out of 26 (5.6% of all words). Of these, 2,429 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is E, followed by A and O. The commonest length is 8 letters (1,006 words), and the longest R word in the list is rationalisation, with 15 letters.

Re- (again, back) is the most productive prefix in English, and it explains why R starts so many words: almost any verb can take it. If you hold R and E, try them in front of every verb you can make from the rest.
