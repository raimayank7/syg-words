Words that start with S

Our word list has 12,772 words that start with S, which makes S the most common first letter out of 26 (11.2% of all words). Of these, 4,860 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is T, followed by U and E. The commonest length is 8 letters (2,212 words), and the longest S word in the list is specialisations, with 15 letters.

S starts more words than any other letter in our list. It blends with nearly every consonant (SC, SH, SK, SL, SM, SN, SP, ST, SW, and three-letter starts such as STR and SPR), and it owns the prefixes sub-, super-, semi- and self-.
