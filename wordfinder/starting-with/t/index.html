Words that start with T

Our word list has 5,735 words that start with T, which makes T the ninth most common first letter out of 26 (5.0% of all words). Of these, 2,081 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is R, followed by E and A. The commonest length is 8 letters (951 words), and the longest T word in the list is technologically, with 15 letters.

T begins the commonest words in the language (the, to, that, this), and its prefixes include trans- (across), tri- (three) and tele- (far). TH, TR and TW are the usual pairs.
