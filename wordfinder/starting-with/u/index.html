Words that start with U

Our word list has 3,278 words that start with U, which makes U the 16th most common first letter out of 26 (2.9% of all words). Of these, 797 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is N, followed by P and R. The commonest length is 10 letters (542 words), and the longest U word in the list is unconditionally, with 15 letters.

Two prefixes dominate the U list: un- (not, or reverse the action) and under-. Like re-, un- can be put before a great many words, so check it whenever you hold U and N.
