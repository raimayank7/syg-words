Words that start with V

Our word list has 1,854 words that start with V, which makes V the 20th most common first letter out of 26 (1.6% of all words). Of these, 595 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is I, followed by E and A. The commonest length is 8 letters (301 words), and the longest V word in the list is vulnerabilities, with 15 letters.

V is always followed by a vowel at the start of a word, and no common English word ends in a bare V. Vice- (deputy) is its main prefix.
