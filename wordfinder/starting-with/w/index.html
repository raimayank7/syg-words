Words that start with W

Our word list has 2,585 words that start with W, which makes W the 18th most common first letter out of 26 (2.3% of all words). Of these, 1,108 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by I and H. The commonest length is 7 letters (483 words), and the longest W word in the list is weatherboarding, with 15 letters.

W words are mostly old, short and common: was, we, what, when, with, word, work. WH and WR (with a silent W, as in write and wrong) are the pairs to remember.
