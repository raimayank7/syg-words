Words that start with X

Our word list has 64 words that start with X, which makes X the 26th most common first letter out of 26 (0.1% of all words). Of these, 3 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is E, followed by Y and A. The commonest length is 8 letters (11 words), and the longest X word in the list is xerographically, with 15 letters.

Very few words begin with X, and most of them come from Greek, where X sounds like Z: xylophone, xenon. In word games X is far more useful in the middle or at the end: ax, ox, box, fix, mix, wax.
