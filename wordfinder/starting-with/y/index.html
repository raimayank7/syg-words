Words that start with Y

Our word list has 316 words that start with Y, which makes Y the 24th most common first letter out of 26 (0.3% of all words). Of these, 123 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is A, followed by E and O. The commonest length is 6 letters (52 words), and the longest Y word in the list is yellowhammers, with 13 letters.

Y starts only a small number of words, mostly short ones: yes, yet, you, year, yard, yarn. Far more often Y is found at the end of a word, where it makes adjectives and adverbs.
