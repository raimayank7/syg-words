Words that start with Z

Our word list has 265 words that start with Z, which makes Z the 25th most common first letter out of 26 (0.2% of all words). Of these, 59 are everyday words. The letter that most often comes second is O, followed by I and E. The commonest length is 7 letters (50 words), and the longest Z word in the list is zoogeographical, with 15 letters.

Z is rare at the start of English words. Zoo- (animal) gives a small family. Useful short Z words: zap, zip, zoo, zest, zero, zone, zoom.
