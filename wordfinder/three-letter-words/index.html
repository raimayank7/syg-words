Three-letter words

Our word list has 792 three-letter words. After the two-letter words, these are the most useful small words to know for any word game or puzzle, and they are the first words children learn to read and spell: cat, dog, sun, run. The list below is grouped by first letter. Everyday words are in bold, less common words are plain, and rare or technical ones are in italics.

Patterns worth noticing

  • Consonant, vowel, consonant is by far the commonest shape: bat, pen, sit, hot, cup. Changing the vowel often gives another word (bat, bet, bit, but), a game teachers use for early spelling.
  • Rare letters: learn the short words with J (jab, jam, jar, jaw, jet, jig, job, jog, joy, jug, raj), X (axe, box, fax, fix, fox, mix, six, tax, wax), Z (zap, zip, zoo, fez) and Q (qua).
  • All vowels but one: words such as ago, are, awe, eye, ice, oak, one and use help when your letters are short of consonants.
  • No vowel at all: a few use Y instead: by-words such as cry, dry, fly, fry, gym, ply, pry, shy, sky, sly, spy, sty, try, why.

Using the list with children

Stick to the bold words. Read a row aloud together, cover it, and ask the child to write as many as they remember. Or choose an ending such as -at or -ig and collect its family: bat, cat, fat, hat, mat, pat, rat, sat.
