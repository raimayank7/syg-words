Word records and curiosities

Every dictionary hides oddities: the longest word with no repeated letter, the longest that can be typed on one row of the keyboard, words whose letters stand in alphabetical order. We searched our list of 114,118 words by program for the record holders. Because the list stops at 15 letters and leaves out names and the very rarest words, these are records for this list, not for the whole English language; a larger dictionary may beat some of them.

Length

The average word in the list has 8.8 letters. The commonest length is 8 letters. The list holds 1,441 words of 15 letters, the longest it carries.

No letter used twice

A word in which no letter repeats is called an isogram. The longest in our list: dermatoglyphics, ambidextrously, hydromagnetics, consumptively, copyrightable, draughtswomen. The first has 15 different letters, more than half the alphabet.

Only one vowel

The longest words that contain just one of the letters A, E, I, O, U: rhythmless, cryptonym, dystrophy, flyspecks, glyceryls. English can stack a surprising number of consonants around a single vowel.

All five vowels

The shortest words that contain A, E, I, O and U at least once each: eulogia, sequoia, aureolin, dialogue, edacious, equation, euphoria, jalousie. Words in which the five appear once each in alphabetical order are prized by word lovers; our list has: abstemious, abstemiously, arsenious, facetious, facetiously.

Letters in alphabetical order

The longest words whose letters run in dictionary order from left to right (repeats allowed): billowy, abbess, abhors, accent, accept, access, accost, adders.

A different word backwards

Some words spell another word when reversed. The longest such pairs here: desserts and stressed, deliver and reviled, animal and lamina, darter and retrad, deffer and reffed, denier and reined, denies and seined, dialer and relaid. Words that spell themselves backwards are palindromes.

One row of the keyboard

The longest words that can be typed using only the top row of letters of a standard QWERTY keyboard: perpetuity, proprietor, repertoire, typewriter, etiquette, pirouette.

Most of one letter, most doubles

The words with the most Es: beekeeper (5), eyeleteer (5), beekeepers (5), deepfreeze (5). The words with the most double letters: allottee, appellee, heelless, keenness, addressee.

Largest anagram family

The set of letters that makes the most words is on its own page: anagram families.

How these were found

Each record is the result of a simple test run over every word in the list when the site is built, so the page always matches the list. Where several words tie, we show the first few in alphabetical order. Many record holders are rare or technical words; if you want to know what one means, a large dictionary will have it. Our word list page explains what the list includes.
