Words with J: short words of 2 to 5 letters

J is one of the rarest letters in English. In our list of 114,118 words, 1,629 contain a J, and only 927 begin with it. This page lists every word of two to five letters that contains J: 200 words in all, of which 89 are everyday words (shown in bold). Short words are the ones that matter when you are holding a difficult letter, because they are the easiest to fit in.

How J behaves

J nearly always comes at the start of a word or of a syllable, and is followed by a vowel: jam, enjoy, major. English words almost never end in J; the handful that do, such as raj and hajj, came from other languages. J does not double, and it does not sit beside most consonants. So when you hold a J, look for a vowel to put after it and build from there.
