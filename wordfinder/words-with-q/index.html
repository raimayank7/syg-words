Words with Q: short words of 2 to 5 letters

Q is one of the rarest letters in English. In our list of 114,118 words, 1,745 contain a Q, and only 546 begin with it. This page lists every word of two to five letters that contains Q: 76 words in all, of which 35 are everyday words (shown in bold). Short words are the ones that matter when you are holding a difficult letter, because they are the easiest to fit in.

How Q behaves

Q is followed by U in almost every English word, and the pair sounds like "kw" (queen, quick) or sometimes "k" (unique, cheque). So a Q is really a two-letter piece, QU, that needs another vowel after it. If you have no U, see the short list of Q words without U. Words can end in -que (plaque, unique) but hardly ever in Q alone.
