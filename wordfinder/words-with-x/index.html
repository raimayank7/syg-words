Words with X: short words of 2 to 5 letters

X is one of the rarest letters in English. In our list of 114,118 words, 2,866 contain a X, and only 64 begin with it. This page lists every word of two to five letters that contains X: 217 words in all, of which 87 are everyday words (shown in bold). Short words are the ones that matter when you are holding a difficult letter, because they are the easiest to fit in.

How X behaves

X is far more common at the end and in the middle of words than at the start: box, next, taxi, exit. Very few words begin with it. It fits neatly after a vowel, which gives the famous short words ax, ex and ox, and the prefix ex- starts many longer ones. X is not doubled, and no common word has two of them.
