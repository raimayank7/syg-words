Words with Z: short words of 2 to 5 letters

Z is one of the rarest letters in English. In our list of 114,118 words, 3,959 contain a Z, and only 265 begin with it. This page lists every word of two to five letters that contains Z: 231 words in all, of which 94 are everyday words (shown in bold). Short words are the ones that matter when you are holding a difficult letter, because they are the easiest to fit in.

How Z behaves

Z likes the start of a word (zoo, zero, zip) and the end after a vowel (quiz, fez), and it doubles readily: buzz, fizz, jazz, pizza, dizzy, puzzle. In British and Indian spelling many words use S where American spelling has Z (organise and organize); our list has both forms, so a Z may be usable where you would normally write S.
