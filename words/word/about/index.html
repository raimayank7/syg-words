About: meaning, synonyms and examples

About is used as an adverb (7 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: (of quantities) imprecise but fairly close to correct.

In Hindi
के बारे में; लगभग
Synonyms
approximately, close to, just about, some, roughly, more or less, around, or so, almost, most

About as an adverb

  1. (of quantities) imprecise but fairly close to correct.

    “Lasted approximately an hour.” “In just about a minute.” “He's about 30 years old.”

    Synonyms: approximately, close to, just about, some, roughly, more or less, around, or so

  2. All around or on all sides.

    “Dirty clothes lying around (or about).” “Let's look about for help.” “There were trees growing all around.”

    Synonyms: around

  3. In the area or vicinity.

    “A few spectators standing about.” “Hanging around.” “Waited around for the next flight.”

    Synonyms: around

  4. Used of movement to or among many different places or in no particular direction.

    “Wandering about with no place to go.” “People were rushing about.” “News gets around (or about).”

    Synonyms: around

  5. In or to a reversed position or direction.

    “About face.” “Suddenly she turned around.”

    Synonyms: around

  6. In rotation or succession.

    “Turn about is fair play.”

  7. (of actions or states) slightly short of or not quite accomplished; all but.

    “The job is (just) about done.” “The baby was almost asleep when the alarm sounded.” “We're almost finished.”

    Synonyms: almost, most, nearly, near, nigh, virtually, well-nigh

About as an adjective

  1. On the move.

    “Up and about.” “The whole town was astir over the incident.”

    Synonyms: astir · Similar: active
