Absolute: meaning, synonyms and examples

Absolute is used as an adjective (5 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Perfect or complete or pure.

Absolute as an adjective

  1. Perfect or complete or pure.

    “Absolute loyalty.” “Absolute silence.” “Absolute truth.”

    Opposite: relative · Similar: direct, implicit, infinite, living

  2. Complete and without restriction or qualification; sometimes used informally as intensifiers.

    “Absolute freedom.” “An absolute dimwit.” “A downright lie.”

    Synonyms: downright, out-and-out, rank, right-down, sheer · Similar: complete

  3. Not limited by law.

    “An absolute monarch.”

    Similar: arbitrary

  4. Expressing finality with no implication of possible change.

    “An absolute guarantee to respect the nation's authority.”

    Similar: unequivocal

  5. Not capable of being violated or infringed.

    “Infrangible human rights.”

    Synonyms: infrangible, inviolable · Similar: inalienable

Absolute as a noun

  1. Something that is conceived or that exists independently and not in relation to other things; something that does not depend on anything else and is beyond human control; something that is not relative.

    “No mortal being can influence the absolute.”

    A kind of: abstraction

Phrases and compounds with "absolute"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bar absolute (noun) The absolute unit of pressure equal to one dyne per square centimeter.
  • absolute zero (noun) (cryogenics) the lowest temperature theoretically attainable (at which the kinetic energy of atoms and molecules is minimal); 0 Kelvin or -273.15 centigrade or -459.67 Fahrenheit.
  • absolute pitch (noun) The ability to identify the pitch of a tone.
  • absolute scale (noun) A temperature scale that defines absolute zero as 0 degrees; water freezes at 273.16 degrees and boils at 373.16 degrees.
  • absolute space (noun) Physical space independent of what occupies it.
  • absolute value (noun) A real number regardless of its sign.
  • absolute ceiling (noun) The maximum altitude at which an airplane can maintain horizontal flight.
  • ablative absolute (noun) A constituent in Latin grammar; a noun and its modifier can function as a sentence modifier.
  • absolute majority (noun) (elections) more than half of the votes.
  • absolute frequency (noun) The number of observations in a given statistical category.
  • absolute magnitude (noun) (astronomy) the magnitude that a star would have if it were viewed from a distance of 10 parsecs (32.62 light years) from the earth.
  • absolute threshold (noun) The lowest level of stimulation that a person can detect.
  • absolute viscosity (noun) A measure of the resistance to flow of a fluid under an applied force.
  • absolute temperature (noun) Temperature measured on the absolute scale.
