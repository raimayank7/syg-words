Account: meaning, synonyms and examples

Account is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A record or narrative description of past events.

In Hindi
खाता; विवरण
Synonyms
history, chronicle, story, report, news report, write up, business relationship, explanation, score, accounting
Word family
accountant, accounting, accountable

Account as a noun

  1. A record or narrative description of past events.

    “A history of France.” “The story of exposure to lead.”

    Synonyms: history, chronicle, story · A kind of: record · Kinds: ancient history, etymology, case history, historical document, annals, biography, recital

  2. A short account of the news.

    “The report of his speech.” “The story was on the 11 o'clock news.” “The account of his speech that was given on the evening news made the governor furious.”

    Synonyms: report, news report, story, write up · A kind of: news · Kinds: newsletter, bulletin, dispatch, urban legend, exclusive

  3. A formal contractual relationship established to provide for regular banking or brokerage or business services.

    “He asked to see the executive who handled his account.”

    Synonyms: business relationship · A kind of: relationship · Kinds: brokerage account, bank account, short account

  4. A statement that makes something comprehensible by describing the relevant structure or operation or circumstances etc.

    “The explanation was very simple.” “I expected a brief account.”

    Synonyms: explanation · A kind of: statement · Kinds: simplification, accounting, reason, justification, exposition, explication, gloss, deriving

  5. Grounds.

    “Don't do it on my account.” “The paper was rejected on account of its length.” “He tried to blame the victim but his success on that score was doubtful.”

    Synonyms: score · A kind of: reason

  6. Importance or value.

    “A person of considerable account.” “He predicted that although it is of small account now it will rapidly increase in importance.”

    A kind of: importance

  7. A statement of recent transactions and the resulting balance.

    “They send me an accounting every month.”

    Synonyms: accounting, account statement · A kind of: statement · Kinds: capital account, capital account, profit and loss, suspense account, expense account

  8. The act of informing by verbal report.

    “He heard reports that they were causing trouble.” “By all accounts they were a happy couple.”

    Synonyms: report · A kind of: informing · Kinds: summarization, indirect discourse, direct discourse, megillah, debriefing, anecdote, narration, gossip

  9. An itemized statement of money owed for goods shipped or services rendered.

    “He paid his bill and left.” “Send me an account of what I owe.”

    Synonyms: bill, invoice · A kind of: statement · Kinds: electric bill, hotel bill, medical bill, phone bill, reckoning, tax bill, check

  10. The quality of taking advantage.

    “She turned her writing skills to good account.”

    A kind of: profit

Account as a verb

  1. Be the sole or primary factor in the existence, acquisition, supply, or disposal of something.

    “Passing grades account for half of the grades given in this exam.”

    A kind of: be

  2. Keep an account of.

    Synonyms: calculate · Kinds: credit, balance, overbalance, debit, compound, charge

  3. To give an account or representation of in words.

    “Discreet Italian police described it in a manner typically continental.”

    Synonyms: report, describe · A kind of: inform

  4. Furnish a justifying analysis or explanation.

    “I can't account for the missing money.”

    Synonyms: answer for · A kind of: declare

Phrases and compounds with "account"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • no-account (adjective) Without merit.
  • account for (verb) Be the reason or explanation for.
  • account book (noun) A record in which commercial accounts are recorded.
  • bank account (noun) A fund that a customer has entrusted to a bank and from which the customer can make withdrawals.
  • cash account (noun) An account with a securities brokerage whose transactions are settled on a cash basis.
  • giro account (noun) An account at a post office that can be used in similar ways to an account at a bank.
  • open account (noun) Credit extended by a business to a customer.
  • take account (verb) Be fully aware of; realize fully.
  • short account (noun) A brokerage account of someone who sells short (sells securities he does not own).
  • trust account (noun) A savings account deposited by someone who makes themselves the trustee for a beneficiary and who controls it during their lifetime; afterward the balance is payable to the previously named beneficiary.
  • charge account (noun) Credit extended by a business to a customer.
  • credit account (noun) Credit extended by a business to a customer.
  • margin account (noun) An account with a securities brokerage in which the broker extends credit.
  • account payable (noun) A liability account showing how much is owed for goods and services purchased on credit.
  • audited account (noun) An inspection of the accounting procedures and records by a trained accountant or CPA.
  • book of account (noun) A record in which commercial accounts are recorded.
