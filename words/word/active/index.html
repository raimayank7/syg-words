Active: meaning, synonyms and examples

Active is used as an adjective (14 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Tending to become more severe or wider in scope.

In Hindi
सक्रिय
Synonyms
combat-ready, fighting, participating, alive, dynamic, active agent, active voice
Opposites
inactive, passive, quiet, stative, extinct, dormant
Word family
activeness, activity

Active as an adjective

  1. Tending to become more severe or wider in scope.

    “Active tuberculosis.”

    Opposite: inactive · Similar: progressive

  2. Engaged in or ready for military or naval operations.

    “On active duty.” “The platoon is combat-ready.” “Review the fighting forces.”

    Synonyms: combat-ready, fighting · Similar: operational

  3. Disposed to take action or effectuate change.

    “A director who takes an active interest in corporate operations.” “An active antagonism.” “He was active in drawing attention to their grievances.”

    Opposite: passive · Similar: activist, hands-on, proactive

  4. Taking part in an activity.

    “An active member of the club.” “He was politically active.” “The participating organizations.”

    Synonyms: participating · Similar: involved

  5. Characterized by energetic activity.

    “An active toddler.” “Active as a gazelle.” “An active man is a man of action.”

    Opposite: inactive · Similar: about, acrobatic, agile, hot, hyperactive, on the go

  6. Exerting influence or producing a change or effect.

    “An active ingredient.”

    Opposite: inactive · Similar: activated, counteractive, surface-active

  7. Full of activity or engaged in continuous activity.

    “An active seaport.” “An active bond market.” “An active account.”

    Opposite: inactive · Similar: brisk, bustling, busy, going, open, springy

  8. In operation.

    “Keep hope alive.” “The tradition was still alive.” “An active tradition.”

    Synonyms: alive · Similar: existent

  9. (of the sun) characterized by an increased occurrence of sunspots and flares and radio emissions.

    Opposite: quiet

  10. Expressing that the subject of the sentence has the semantic function of actor: "Hemingway favors active constructions".

    “Hemingway favors active constructions.”

    Opposite: passive

  11. (used of verbs (e.g. `to run') and participial adjectives (e.g. `running' in `running water')) expressing action rather than a state of being.

    Synonyms: dynamic · Opposite: stative

  12. (of e.g. volcanos) capable of erupting.

    Opposite: extinct · Similar: alive

  13. (of e.g. volcanos) erupting or liable to erupt.

    “Active volcanos.”

    Opposite: dormant · Similar: eruptive

  14. Engaged in full-time work.

    “Active duty.” “Though past retirement age he is still active in his profession.”

    Opposite: inactive · Similar: activated

Active as a noun

  1. Chemical agent capable of activity.

    Synonyms: active agent · A kind of: chemical agent

  2. The voice used to indicate that the grammatical subject of the verb is performing the action or causing the happening denoted by the verb.

    “`The boy threw the ball' uses the active voice.”

    Synonyms: active voice · A kind of: voice

  3. A person who is a participating member of an organization.

    “The club issues a list of members, both the actives and the retirees.”

    A kind of: person

Phrases and compounds with "active"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • be active (verb) Be in a state of action.
  • active site (noun) The part of an enzyme or antibody where the chemical reaction occurs.
  • active agent (noun) Chemical agent capable of activity.
  • active birth (noun) Childbirth during which the mother is encouraged to be an active participant.
  • active trust (noun) A trust in which the trustee must perform certain duties.
  • active voice (noun) The voice used to indicate that the grammatical subject of the verb is performing the action or causing the happening denoted by the verb.
  • active citizen (noun) A citizen who takes an active role in the community (as in crime prevention and neighborhood watch).
  • surface-active (adjective) Capable of lowering the surface tension of a liquid; used especially of detergents.
  • active immunity (noun) A form of acquired immunity in which the body produces its own antibodies against disease-causing antigens.
  • active air defense (noun) Air defense by the use aircraft or missiles or artillery or electronic countermeasures.
  • active application (noun) An application that is currently running and in the foreground.
  • active matrix screen (noun) A type of LCD screen used for some portable computers; there is a separate circuit for each pixel.
  • surface-active agent (noun) A chemical agent capable of reducing the surface tension of a liquid in which it is dissolved.
  • highly active antiretroviral therapy (noun) A combination of protease inhibitors taken with reverse transcriptase inhibitors; used in treating AIDS and HIV.
