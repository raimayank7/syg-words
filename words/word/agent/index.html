Agent: meaning, synonyms and examples

Agent is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: An active and efficient cause; capable of producing a certain effect.

In Hindi
एजेंट, प्रतिनिधि
Synonyms
factor, broker, federal agent, agentive role
Word family
agency, agential

Agent as a noun

  1. An active and efficient cause; capable of producing a certain effect.

    “Their research uncovered new disease agents.”

    A kind of: causal agent · Kinds: biological agent, eliminator, impairer, infectious agent, lethal agent, motor, mutagen, quickener

  2. A representative who acts on behalf of other persons or organizations.

    A kind of: representative · Kinds: business agent, literary agent, bailee, booker, deputy, employment agent, freight agent, government agent

  3. A substance that exerts some force or effect.

    A kind of: causal agent, substance · Kinds: antiflatulent, antifungal, disinfectant, antacid, bacteriostat, bleaching agent, chemical agent, coagulant

  4. A businessman who buys or sells for another in exchange for a commission.

    Synonyms: factor, broker · A kind of: businessperson · Kinds: auctioneer, insurance broker, investment banker, real estate broker, ship broker, stockbroker, syndic, travel agent

  5. Any agent or representative of a federal agency or bureau.

    Synonyms: federal agent · A kind of: official · Kinds: trustbuster

  6. The semantic role of the animate entity that instigates or causes the happening denoted by the verb in the clause.

    Synonyms: agentive role · A kind of: semantic role

Phrases and compounds with "agent"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fbi agent (noun) A special law-enforcement agent of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
  • law agent (noun) A solicitor in Scotland.
  • agent bank (noun) A bank named by a lending syndicate of several banks to protect their interests.
  • book agent (noun) A book salesman.
  • free agent (noun) (sports) a professional athlete who is free to sign a contract to play for any team.
  • land agent (noun) A person who is authorized to act as an agent for the sale of land.
  • mill agent (noun) The responsible official at a mill that is under absentee ownership.
  • road agent (noun) A holdup man who stops a vehicle and steals from it.
  • toll agent (noun) Someone employed to collect tolls.
  • claim agent (noun) One who investigates insurance claims or claims for damages and recommends an effective settlement.
  • house agent (noun) A person who is authorized to act as an agent for the sale of land.
  • nerve agent (noun) A toxic gas that is inhaled or absorbed through the skin and has harmful effects on the nervous and respiratory system.
  • press agent (noun) Someone employed to arrange publicity (for a firm or a public figure).
  • active agent (noun) Chemical agent capable of activity.
  • agent orange (noun) A herbicide used in the Vietnam War to defoliate forest areas.
  • bridge agent (noun) An operative who acts as a courier or go-between from a case officer to a secret agent in a hostile area.
