Air: meaning, synonyms and examples

Air is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A mixture of gases (especially oxygen) required for breathing; the stuff that the wind consists of.

In Hindi
हवा
Synonyms
aura, atmosphere, breeze, zephyr, gentle wind, tune, melody, strain, melodic line, line
Word family
aerate, aerify, aerial, airy

Air as a noun

  1. A mixture of gases (especially oxygen) required for breathing; the stuff that the wind consists of.

    “Air pollution.” “A smell of chemicals in the air.” “Open a window and let in some air.”

    A kind of: gas · Kinds: breath, hot air, liquid air

  2. The region above the ground.

    “Her hand stopped in mid air.” “He threw the ball into the air.”

    A kind of: region

  3. A distinctive but intangible quality surrounding a person or thing.

    “An air of mystery.” “The house had a neglected air.” “An atmosphere of defeat pervaded the candidate's headquarters.”

    Synonyms: aura, atmosphere · A kind of: quality · Kinds: mystique, note, vibration

  4. A slight wind (usually refreshing).

    “The breeze was cooled by the lake.” “As he waited he could feel the air on his neck.”

    Synonyms: breeze, zephyr, gentle wind · A kind of: wind · Kinds: sea breeze, breath, light air, light breeze, gentle breeze, moderate breeze, fresh breeze, strong breeze

  5. The mass of air surrounding the Earth.

    “There was great heat as the comet entered the atmosphere.” “It was exposed to the air.”

    Synonyms: atmosphere · A kind of: region · Kinds: airspace

  6. Once thought to be one of four elements composing the universe (Empedocles).

    A kind of: element

  7. A succession of notes forming a distinctive sequence.

    “She was humming an air from Beethoven.”

    Synonyms: tune, melody, strain, melodic line, line, melodic phrase · A kind of: music · Kinds: flourish, glissando, roulade, leitmotiv, theme song, signature, theme, part

  8. Medium for radio and television broadcasting.

    “The program was on the air from 9 til midnight.” “The president used the airwaves to take his message to the people.”

    Synonyms: airwave · A kind of: medium

  9. Travel via aircraft.

    “Air travel involves too much waiting in airports.” “If you've time to spare go by air.”

    Synonyms: air travel, aviation · A kind of: travel · Kinds: flight

Air as a verb

  1. Expose to fresh air.

    “Aerate your old sneakers.”

    Synonyms: air out, aerate · A kind of: expose

  2. Be broadcast.

    “This show will air Saturdays at 2 P.M.”

    Kinds: get on

  3. Broadcast over the airwaves, as in radio or television.

    “We cannot air this X-rated song.”

    Synonyms: send, broadcast, beam, transmit · A kind of: publicize · Kinds: satellite, sportscast, telecast, interrogate, rerun

  4. Make public.

    “She aired her opinions on welfare.”

    Synonyms: publicize, publicise, bare · A kind of: tell · Kinds: hype, bulletin, publish, circulate, air

  5. Expose to warm or heated air, so as to dry.

    “Air linen.”

    A kind of: dry

  6. Expose to cool or cold air so as to cool or freshen.

    “Air the old winter clothes.” “Air out the smoke-filled rooms.”

    Synonyms: vent, ventilate, air out · A kind of: refresh

Phrases and compounds with "air"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • on air (adverb) Very happily.
  • air bag (noun) A safety restraint in an automobile; the bag inflates on collision and prevents the driver or passenger from being thrown forward.
  • air gas (noun) A gas made of carbon monoxide and hydrogen and nitrogen; made by passing air over hot coke.
  • air gun (noun) A gun that propels a projectile by compressed air.
  • air out (verb) Expose to fresh air.
  • air sac (noun) A tiny sac for holding air in the lungs; formed by the terminal dilation of tiny air passageways.
  • air-dry (adjective) Not giving off moisture on exposure to the air.
  • hot air (noun) Air that has been heated and tends to rise.
  • air base (noun) A base for military aircraft.
  • air cell (noun) A tiny sac for holding air in the lungs; formed by the terminal dilation of tiny air passageways.
  • air crew (noun) The crew of an aircraft.
  • air duct (noun) A duct that provides ventilation (as in mines).
  • air flow (noun) The flow of air.
  • air hole (noun) A local region of low pressure or descending air that causes a plane to lose height suddenly.
  • air horn (noun) A pneumatic horn.
  • air hose (noun) A hose that carries air under pressure.
