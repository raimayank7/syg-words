Appeal: meaning, synonyms and examples

Appeal is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Earnest or urgent request.

In Hindi
अपील; आकर्षण
Synonyms
entreaty, prayer, appealingness, charm, solicitation, collection, ingathering, invoke, attract
Word family
appealable, appellant

Appeal as a noun

  1. Earnest or urgent request.

    “An entreaty to stop the fighting.” “An appeal for help.” “An appeal to the public to keep calm.”

    Synonyms: entreaty, prayer · A kind of: request · Kinds: adjuration, demagoguery, supplication, solicitation, suit, courtship

  2. Attractiveness that interests or pleases or stimulates.

    “His smile was part of his appeal to her.”

    Synonyms: appealingness, charm · Kinds: siren call, winsomeness

  3. (law) a legal proceeding in which the appellant resorts to a higher court for the purpose of obtaining a review of a lower court decision and a reversal of the lower court's judgment or the granting of a new trial.

    “Their appeal was denied in the superior court.”

    A kind of: proceeding

  4. Request for a sum of money.

    “An appeal to raise money for starving children.”

    Synonyms: solicitation, collection, ingathering · A kind of: request · Kinds: whip-round

Appeal as a verb

  1. Take a court case to a higher court for review.

    “He was found guilty but appealed immediately.”

    A kind of: challenge

  2. Request earnestly (something from somebody); ask for aid or protection.

    “Appeal to somebody for help.” “Invoke God in times of trouble.”

    Synonyms: invoke · A kind of: request · Kinds: plead, call on

  3. Be attractive to.

    “The idea of a vacation appeals to me.” “The beautiful garden attracted many people.”

    Synonyms: attract · Kinds: capture, beckon

  4. Challenge (a decision).

    “She appealed the verdict.”

    A kind of: challenge

  5. Cite as an authority; resort to.

    “He invoked the law that would save him.” “I appealed to the law of 1900.” “She invoked an ancient law.”

    Synonyms: invoke · A kind of: mention

Phrases and compounds with "appeal"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • appeal board (noun) A board of officials that are not judicial but are appointed to hear appeals.
  • personal appeal (noun) A personal attractiveness or interestingness that enables you to influence others.
