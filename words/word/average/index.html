Average: meaning, synonyms and examples

Average is used as an adjective (6 senses), verb (3 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Approximating the statistical norm or average or expected value.

Average as an adjective

  1. Approximating the statistical norm or average or expected value.

    “The average income in New England is below that of the nation.” “Of average height for his age.” “The mean annual rainfall.”

    Synonyms: mean · Similar: normal

  2. Lacking special distinction, rank, or status; commonly encountered.

    “Average people.” “The ordinary (or common) man in the street.”

    Synonyms: ordinary · Similar: common

  3. Lacking exceptional quality or ability.

    “A novel of average merit.” “Only a fair performance of the sonata.” “In fair health.”

    Synonyms: fair, mediocre, middling · Similar: ordinary

  4. Around the middle of a scale of evaluation.

    “An orange of average size.” “Intermediate capacity.”

    Synonyms: intermediate, medium · Similar: moderate

  5. Relating to or constituting the most frequent value in a distribution.

    “The modal age at which American novelists reach their peak is 30.”

    Synonyms: modal · Similar: normal

  6. Relating to or constituting the middle value of an ordered set of values (or the average of the middle two in a set with an even number of values).

    “The median value of 17, 20, and 36 is 20.” “The median income for the year was $15,000.”

    Synonyms: median · Similar: normal

Average as a verb

  1. Amount to or come to an average, without loss or gain.

    “The number of hours I work per work averages out to 40.”

    Synonyms: average out · A kind of: total

  2. Achieve or reach on average.

    “He averaged a C.”

    A kind of: achieve

  3. Compute the average of.

    Synonyms: average out · A kind of: calculate

Average as a noun

  1. A statistic describing the location of a distribution.

    “It set the norm for American homes.”

    Synonyms: norm · A kind of: statistic · Kinds: age norm, mode, median, mean

  2. (sports) the ratio of successful performances to opportunities.

    A kind of: ratio · Kinds: batting average, fielding average

  3. An intermediate scale value regarded as normal or usual.

    “He is about average in height.” “The snowfall this month is below average.”

    A kind of: scale value

Phrases and compounds with "average"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • on average (adverb) Typically.
  • average out (verb) Compute the average of.
  • average cost (noun) Total cost for all units bought (or produced) divided by the number of units.
  • on the average (adverb) Typically.
  • batting average (noun) (baseball) a measure of a batter's performance; the number of base hits divided by the number of official times at bat.
  • hitting average (noun) (baseball) a measure of a batter's performance; the number of base hits divided by the number of official times at bat.
  • fielding average (noun) (baseball) a measure of a fielder's performance; the number of assists and putouts divided by the number of chances.
  • earned run average (noun) (baseball) a measure of a pitcher's effectiveness; calculated as the average number of earned runs allowed by the pitcher for every nine innings pitched.
  • grade point average (noun) A measure of a student's academic achievement at a college or university; calculated by dividing the total number of grade points received by the total number attempted.
  • dow-jones industrial average (noun) An indicator of stock market prices; based on the share values of 30 blue-chip stocks listed on the New York Stock Exchange.
