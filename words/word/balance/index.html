Balance: meaning, synonyms and examples

Balance is used as a noun (12 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A state of equilibrium.

In Hindi
संतुलन; शेष राशि
Synonyms
proportion, proportionality, equilibrium, equipoise, counterbalance, remainder, residual, residue, residuum, rest
Opposites
imbalance, unbalance
Word family
balancer

Balance as a noun

  1. A state of equilibrium.

    Opposite: imbalance · A kind of: equilibrium · Kinds: tension, electrolyte balance, nitrogen balance

  2. Equality between the totals of the credit and debit sides of an account.

    A kind of: equality · Kinds: trial balance

  3. Harmonious arrangement or relation of parts or elements within a whole (as in a design).

    “In all perfectly beautiful objects there is found the opposition of one part to another and a reciprocal balance.”

    Synonyms: proportion, proportionality · A kind of: placement

  4. Equality of distribution.

    Synonyms: equilibrium, equipoise, counterbalance · A kind of: structure · Kinds: conformation, symmetry

  5. Something left after other parts have been taken away.

    “There was no remainder.” “He threw away the rest.” “He took what he wanted and I got the balance.”

    Synonyms: remainder, residual, residue, residuum, rest · A kind of: part · Kinds: leftover

  6. The difference between the totals of the credit and debit sides of an account.

    A kind of: remainder · Kinds: balance of trade, carry-over, compensating balance, invisible balance

  7. (astrology) a person who is born while the sun is in Libra.

    A kind of: person

  8. The seventh sign of the zodiac; the sun is in this sign from about September 23 to October 22.

  9. (mathematics) an attribute of a shape or relation; exact reflection of form on opposite sides of a dividing line or plane.

    Synonyms: symmetry, symmetricalness, correspondence · A kind of: spatial property · Kinds: regularity, bilaterality, radial symmetry

  10. A weight that balances another weight.

    Synonyms: counterweight, counterbalance, counterpoise, equalizer, equaliser · A kind of: weight · Kinds: sash weight, tare

  11. A wheel that regulates the rate of movement in a machine; especially a wheel oscillating against the hairspring of a timepiece to regulate its beat.

    Synonyms: balance wheel · A kind of: wheel

  12. A scale for weighing; depends on pull of gravity.

    A kind of: scale · Kinds: beam balance, electronic balance, microbalance, spring balance, steelyard

Balance as a verb

  1. Bring into balance or equilibrium.

    “She has to balance work and her domestic duties.” “Balance the two weights.”

    Synonyms: equilibrate, equilibrize, equilibrise · Opposite: unbalance · A kind of: match · Kinds: complement, compensate, cancel, offset, trim

  2. Compute credits and debits of an account.

    A kind of: account

  3. Hold or carry in equilibrium.

    Synonyms: poise · A kind of: hold · Kinds: juggle

  4. Be in equilibrium.

    “He was balancing on one foot.”

    A kind of: be

Phrases and compounds with "balance"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • balance beam (noun) A gymnastic apparatus used by women gymnasts.
  • beam balance (noun) A balance consisting of a lever with two equal arms and a pan suspended from each arm.
  • balance sheet (noun) A record of the financial situation of an institution on a particular date by listing its assets and the claims against those assets.
  • balance wheel (noun) A wheel that regulates the rate of movement in a machine; especially a wheel oscillating against the hairspring of a timepiece to regulate its beat.
  • trade balance (noun) The difference in value over a period of time of a country's imports and exports of merchandise.
  • trial balance (noun) A balance of debits and credits in double-entry bookkeeping; drawn up to test their equality.
  • mental balance (noun) The healthy psychological state of someone with good judgment.
  • spring balance (noun) A balance that measure weight by the tension on a helical spring.
  • dynamic balance (noun) (aeronautics) the state of equilibrium in which centrifugal forces due to a rotating mass (e.g., a propeller) do not produce force in the shaft and so vibration is reduced.
  • torsion balance (noun) Measuring instrument designed to measure small forces by the torsion they exert on a thin wire.
  • visible balance (noun) The difference in value over a period of time of a country's imports and exports of merchandise.
  • balance of power (noun) An equilibrium of power between nations.
  • balance of trade (noun) The difference in value over a period of time of a country's imports and exports of merchandise.
  • chemical balance (noun) A beam balance of great precision used in quantitative chemical analysis.
  • nitrogen balance (noun) The balance between the amount of nitrogen taken in (to the soil or the body) and the amount given off (lost or excreted).
  • sense of balance (noun) A sensory system located in structures of the inner ear that registers the orientation of the head.
