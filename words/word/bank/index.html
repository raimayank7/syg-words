Bank: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bank is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Sloping land (especially the slope beside a body of water).

In Hindi
बैंक; नदी का किनारा
Synonyms
depository financial institution, banking concern, banking company, cant, camber, savings bank, coin bank, money box, bank building, deposit
Word family
bankable, banking, banker

Bank as a noun

  1. Sloping land (especially the slope beside a body of water).

    “They pulled the canoe up on the bank.” “He sat on the bank of the river and watched the currents.”

    A kind of: slope · Kinds: riverbank, waterside

  2. A financial institution that accepts deposits and channels the money into lending activities.

    “He cashed a check at the bank.” “That bank holds the mortgage on my home.”

    Synonyms: depository financial institution, banking concern, banking company · A kind of: financial institution · Kinds: credit union, agent bank, commercial bank, state bank, lead bank, member bank, merchant bank, acquirer

  3. A long ridge or pile.

    “A huge bank of earth.”

    A kind of: ridge · Kinds: bluff, sandbank

  4. An arrangement of similar objects in a row or in tiers.

    “He operated a bank of switches.”

    A kind of: array

  5. A supply or stock held in reserve for future use (especially in emergencies).

    A kind of: reserve · Kinds: blood bank, eye bank, food bank, soil bank

  6. A slope in the turn of a road or track; the outside is higher than the inside in order to reduce the effects of centrifugal force.

    Synonyms: cant, camber · A kind of: slope

  7. A container (usually with a slot in the top) for keeping money at home.

    “The coin bank was empty.”

    Synonyms: savings bank, coin bank, money box · A kind of: container · Kinds: piggy bank

  8. A building in which the business of banking transacted.

    “The bank is on the corner of Nassau and Witherspoon.”

    Synonyms: bank building · A kind of: depository

  9. A flight maneuver; aircraft tips laterally about its longitudinal axis (especially in turning).

    “The plane went into a steep bank.”

    A kind of: flight maneuver · Kinds: vertical bank

Bank as a verb

  1. Tip laterally.

    “The pilot had to bank the aircraft.”

    A kind of: tip

  2. Enclose with a bank.

    “Bank roads.”

    A kind of: enclose

  3. Do business with a bank or keep an account at a bank.

    “Where do you bank in this town?”

    A kind of: transact

  4. Be in the banking business.

    A kind of: work

  5. Put into a bank account.

    “She deposits her paycheck every month.”

    Synonyms: deposit · A kind of: give · Kinds: redeposit

  6. Cover with ashes so to control the rate of burning.

    “Bank a fire.”

    A kind of: cover

  7. Have confidence or faith in.

    “We can trust in God.” “Rely on your friends.” “Bank on your good education.”

    Synonyms: trust, swear, rely · A kind of: believe · Kinds: credit, lean, count

Phrases and compounds with "bank"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bank run (noun) The concerted action of depositors who try to withdraw their money from a bank because they think it will fail.
  • eye bank (noun) A place for storing and preserving corneas that are obtained from human corpses immediately after death; used for corneal transplantation to patients with corneal defects.
  • bank bill (noun) A piece of paper money (especially one issued by a central bank).
  • bank card (noun) A credit card issued by a bank.
  • bank line (noun) The maximum credit that a customer is allowed.
  • bank loan (noun) A loan made by a bank; to be repaid with interest on or before a fixed date.
  • bank note (noun) A piece of paper money (especially one issued by a central bank).
  • bank rate (noun) The discount rate fixed by a central bank.
  • bank shot (noun) A basketball shot that bounces off of the backboard before passing through the hoop.
  • coin bank (noun) A container (usually with a slot in the top) for keeping money at home.
  • food bank (noun) A place where food is contributed and made available to those in need.
  • lead bank (noun) A bank named by a lending syndicate of several banks to protect their interests.
  • left bank (noun) The region of Paris on the southern bank of the Seine; a center of artistic and student life.
  • snow bank (noun) A mound or heap of snow.
  • soil bank (noun) Land retired from crop cultivation and planted with soil-building crops; government subsidies are paid to farmers for their retired land.
  • west bank (noun) An area between Israel and Jordan on the west bank of the Jordan river; populated largely by Palestinians.
