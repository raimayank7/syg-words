Bar: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bar is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A counter where you can obtain food or drink.

In Hindi
छड़; सलाख़
Synonyms
measure, prevention, legal profession, legal community, stripe, streak, cake, debar, exclude, barricade
Opposites
unbar

Bar as a noun

  1. A counter where you can obtain food or drink.

    “He bought a hot dog and a coke at the bar.”

    A kind of: counter · Kinds: cash bar, oyster bar, salad bar, snack bar, soda fountain, sushi bar, wet bar

  2. A rigid piece of metal or wood; usually used as a fastening or obstruction or weapon.

    “There were bars in the windows to prevent escape.”

    A kind of: implement · Kinds: belaying pin, bolt, bolt, carpenter's level, chinning bar, core, crossbar, drawbar

  3. Musical notation for a repeating pattern of musical beats.

    “The orchestra omitted the last twelve bars of the song.”

    Synonyms: measure · A kind of: musical notation

  4. An obstruction (usually metal) placed at the top of a goal.

    “It was an excellent kick but the ball hit the bar.”

    A kind of: obstruction

  5. The act of preventing.

    “There was no bar against leaving.” “Money was allocated to study the cause and prevention of influenza.”

    Synonyms: prevention · A kind of: hindrance · Kinds: averting, debarment, disqualification, interception, nonproliferation, obviation, prophylaxis, save

  6. (meteorology) a unit of pressure equal to a million dynes per square centimeter.

    “Unfortunately some writers have used bar for one dyne per square centimeter.”

    A kind of: pressure unit

  7. A submerged (or partly submerged) ridge in a river or along a shore.

    “The boat ran aground on a submerged bar in the river.”

    A kind of: ridge, barrier · Kinds: sandbar

  8. The body of individuals qualified to practice law in a particular jurisdiction.

    “He was admitted to the bar in New Jersey.”

    Synonyms: legal profession, legal community · A kind of: profession

  9. A narrow marking of a different color or texture from the background.

    “A green toad with small black stripes or bars.” “May the Stars and Stripes forever wave.”

    Synonyms: stripe, streak · A kind of: marking · Kinds: band

  10. A block of solid substance (such as soap or wax).

    “A bar of chocolate.”

    Synonyms: cake · A kind of: block · Kinds: tablet

  11. A portable .30 caliber automatic rifle operated by gas pressure and fed by cartridges from a magazine; used by United States troops in World War I and in World War II and in the Korean War.

    A kind of: automatic rifle

  12. A horizontal rod that serves as a support for gymnasts as they perform exercises.

    A kind of: support

  13. A heating element in an electric fire.

    “An electric fire with three bars.”

    A kind of: heating element

  14. (law) a railing that encloses the part of the courtroom where the judges and lawyers sit and the case is tried.

    “Spectators were not allowed past the bar.”

    A kind of: railing

Bar as a verb

  1. Prevent from entering; keep out.

    “He was barred from membership in the club.”

    Synonyms: debar, exclude · A kind of: forbid

  2. Render unsuitable for passage.

    “Block the way.” “Barricade the streets.” “Stop the busy road.”

    Synonyms: barricade, block, blockade, stop, block off, block up · A kind of: obstruct · Kinds: shut off, close

  3. Expel, as if by official decree.

    “He was banished from his own country.”

    Synonyms: banish, relegate · A kind of: expel · Kinds: spike

  4. Secure with, or as if with, bars.

    “He barred the door.”

    Opposite: unbar · A kind of: fasten

Phrases and compounds with "bar"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • t-bar (noun) A surface lift where riders hold a bar and are pulled up the hill on their skis.
  • bar bit (noun) A bit for horses that is a solid bar of metal.
  • bar fly (noun) A drinker who frequents bars.
  • nut bar (noun) Paste of nuts and sugar on a pastry base cut into bars.
  • pry bar (noun) A heavy iron lever with one end forged into a wedge.
  • wet bar (noun) A bar for mixing drinks that has a sink with running water.
  • axle bar (noun) An iron bar that serves as an axletree.
  • bar code (noun) Code consisting of a series of vertical bars of variable width that are scanned by a laser; printed on consumer product packages to identify the item for a computer that provides the price and registers inventory information.
  • bar exam (noun) An examination conducted at regular intervals to determine whether a candidate is qualified to practice law in a given jurisdiction.
  • bar girl (noun) A woman employed by a bar to act as a companion to men customers.
  • bar line (noun) A vertical line before the accented beat marking the boundary between musical bars.
  • bar mask (noun) A catcher's mask with bars.
  • bar soap (noun) Soap in the form of a bar.
  • cash bar (noun) A counter at a large party where you can purchase drinks by the glass.
  • date bar (noun) Fruit bar containing chopped dates.
  • gill bar (noun) One of the bony or cartilaginous arches on each side of the pharynx that support the gills of fishes and aquatic amphibians.
