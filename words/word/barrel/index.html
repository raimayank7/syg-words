Barrel: meaning, synonyms and examples

Barrel is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A tube through which a bullet travels when a gun is fired.

In Hindi
पीपा; नली
Synonyms
gun barrel, cask, drum, barrelful, bbl

Barrel as a noun

  1. A tube through which a bullet travels when a gun is fired.

    Synonyms: gun barrel · A kind of: tube

  2. A cylindrical container that holds liquids.

    Synonyms: cask · A kind of: vessel · Kinds: butt, hogshead, keg, pickle barrel, shook, tun

  3. A bulging cylindrical shape; hollow with flat ends.

    Synonyms: drum · A kind of: cylinder

  4. The quantity that a barrel (of any size) will hold.

    Synonyms: barrelful · A kind of: containerful

  5. Any of various units of capacity.

    Synonyms: bbl

Barrel as a verb

  1. Put in barrels.

    A kind of: put

Phrases and compounds with "barrel"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gun barrel (noun) A tube through which a bullet travels when a gun is fired.
  • barrel knot (noun) A knot used for tying fishing leaders together; the ends of the two leaders are wrapped around each other two or three times.
  • barrel roll (noun) A roll in which the plane follows a spiral course.
  • pork barrel (noun) A legislative appropriation designed to ingratiate legislators with their constituents.
  • rain barrel (noun) A barrel used as a cistern to hold rainwater.
  • barrel maker (noun) A craftsman who makes or repairs wooden barrels or tubs.
  • barrel organ (noun) A musical instrument that makes music by rotation of a cylinder studded with pegs.
  • barrel vault (noun) The simplest form of vault; a single continuous arch.
  • trash barrel (noun) A bin that holds rubbish until it is collected.
  • barrel cactus (noun) A cactus of the genus Ferocactus: unbranched barrel-shaped cactus having deep ribs with numerous spines and usually large funnel-shaped flowers followed by dry fruits.
  • barrel-shaped (adjective) Having the general shape of a barrel.
  • pickle barrel (noun) A barrel holding vinegar in which cucumbers are pickled.
  • cracker-barrel (adjective) Characteristic of country life.
  • rear of barrel (noun) Opening in the rear of the barrel of a gun where bullets can be loaded.
  • golden barrel cactus (noun) Large cactus of east central Mexico having golden to pale yellow flowers and spines.
