Bat: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bat is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Strike with, or as if with a baseball bat.

In Hindi
बल्ला; चमगादड़
Synonyms
flutter, cream, clobber, drub, thrash, lick, chiropteran, at-bat, squash racket, squash racquet
Word family
batter, batting

Bat as a verb

  1. Strike with, or as if with a baseball bat.

    “Bat the ball.”

    A kind of: hit · Kinds: switch-hit

  2. Wink briefly.

    “Bat one's eyelids.”

    Synonyms: flutter · A kind of: blink

  3. Have a turn at bat.

    “Jones bats first, followed by Martinez.”

    A kind of: hit

  4. Use a bat.

    “Who's batting?”

    A kind of: hit

  5. Beat thoroughly and conclusively in a competition or fight.

    “We licked the other team on Sunday!”

    Synonyms: cream, clobber, drub, thrash, lick · A kind of: beat

Bat as a noun

  1. Nocturnal mouselike mammal with forelimbs modified to form membranous wings and anatomical adaptations for echolocation by which they navigate.

    Synonyms: chiropteran · A kind of: placental · Kinds: fruit bat, carnivorous bat

  2. (baseball) a turn trying to get a hit.

    “He was at bat when it happened.” “He got four hits in four at-bats.”

    Synonyms: at-bat · A kind of: turn

  3. A small racket with a long handle used for playing squash.

    Synonyms: squash racket, squash racquet · A kind of: racket

  4. The club used in playing cricket.

    “A cricket bat has a narrow handle and a broad flat end for hitting.”

    Synonyms: cricket bat · A kind of: cricket equipment

  5. A club used for hitting a ball in various games.

    A kind of: club · Kinds: baseball bat, paddle

Phrases and compounds with "bat"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at-bat (noun) (baseball) a turn trying to get a hit.
  • bat boy (noun) (baseball) a boy who takes care of bats and other baseball equipment.
  • red bat (noun) North American bat of a brick or rusty red color with hairs tipped with white.
  • cave bat (noun) Drab yellowish big-eared bat that lives in caves.
  • brown bat (noun) Any of numerous medium to small insectivorous bats found worldwide in caves and trees and buildings.
  • fruit bat (noun) Large Old World bat of warm and tropical regions that feeds on fruit.
  • guano bat (noun) The common freetail bat of southern United States having short velvety fur; migrates southward for winter.
  • harpy bat (noun) Any of various fruit bats of the genus Nyctimene distinguished by nostrils drawn out into diverging tubes.
  • orange bat (noun) A common bat of northwestern Australia having orange or yellow fur.
  • pallid bat (noun) Drab yellowish big-eared bat that lives in caves.
  • cricket bat (noun) The club used in playing cricket.
  • frosted bat (noun) Common Eurasian bat with white-tipped hairs in its coat.
  • hognose bat (noun) Small-eared Mexican bat with a long slender nose.
  • jackass bat (noun) A large bat of the southwestern United States having spots and enormous ears.
  • mastiff bat (noun) A soft-furred chocolate-brown bat with folded ears and small wings; often runs along the ground.
  • spotted bat (noun) A large bat of the southwestern United States having spots and enormous ears.
