Bend: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bend is used as a verb (6 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Form a curve.

In Hindi
मोड़ना; मोड़
Synonyms
flex, deform, twist, turn, crouch, stoop, bow, deflect, turn away, crook
Opposites
straighten, unbend
Word family
bendable, bender

Bend as a verb

  1. Form a curve.

    “The stick does not bend.”

    Synonyms: flex · Opposite: straighten · A kind of: change shape · Kinds: crook, arch, incurvate, retroflex, lean, crouch, double over, fawn

  2. Change direction.

    “The road bends.”

    A kind of: turn

  3. Cause (a plastic object) to assume a crooked or angular form.

    “Bend the rod.” “Twist the dough into a braid.” “The strong man could turn an iron bar.”

    Synonyms: flex, deform, twist, turn · Opposite: unbend · A kind of: change shape · Kinds: indent, incurvate, gnarl, crank, convolve

  4. Bend one's back forward from the waist on down.

    “He crouched down.” “She bowed before the Queen.” “The young man stooped to pick up the girl's purse.”

    Synonyms: crouch, stoop, bow · A kind of: bend · Kinds: squinch, huddle

  5. Turn from a straight course, fixed direction, or line of interest.

    Synonyms: deflect, turn away · A kind of: turn

  6. Bend a joint.

    “Flex your wrists.” “Bend your knees.”

    Synonyms: flex · A kind of: move

Bend as a noun

  1. A circular segment of a curve.

    “A bend in the road.” “A crook in the path.”

    Synonyms: crook, twist, turn · A kind of: curve · Kinds: bight

  2. Movement that causes the formation of a curve.

    Synonyms: bending · A kind of: movement · Kinds: deflection

  3. Curved segment (of a road or river or railroad track etc.).

    Synonyms: curve · A kind of: section · Kinds: blind curve, elbow, hairpin bend

  4. An angular or rounded shape made by folding.

    “A fold in the napkin.” “A crease in his trousers.” “A plication on her blouse.”

    Synonyms: fold, crease, plication, flexure, crimp · A kind of: angular shape · Kinds: pleat, kink, pucker

  5. A town in central Oregon at the eastern foot of the Cascade Range.

  6. Diagonal line traversing a shield from the upper right corner to the lower left.

    Synonyms: bend dexter · A kind of: ordinary
