Blue: meaning, synonyms and examples

Blue is used as an adjective (7 senses), noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Of the color intermediate between green and violet; having a color similar to that of a clear unclouded sky.

In Hindi
नीला
Synonyms
bluish, blueish, gloomy, grim, depressed, dispirited, down, downcast, downhearted, down in the mouth
Word family
blueness, bluing

Blue as an adjective

  1. Of the color intermediate between green and violet; having a color similar to that of a clear unclouded sky.

    “October's bright blue weather.” “A blue flame.” “Blue haze of tobacco smoke.”

    Synonyms: bluish, blueish · Similar: chromatic

  2. Used to signify the Union forces in the American Civil War (who wore blue uniforms).

    “A ragged blue line.”

    Similar: northern

  3. Filled with melancholy and despondency.

    “Gloomy at the thought of what he had to face.” “Gloomy predictions.” “A gloomy silence.”

    Synonyms: gloomy, grim, depressed, dispirited, down, downcast, downhearted, down in the mouth · Similar: dejected

  4. Characterized by profanity or cursing.

    “Foul-mouthed and blasphemous.” “Blue language.” “Profane words.”

    Synonyms: blasphemous, profane

  5. Belonging to or characteristic of the nobility or aristocracy.

    “An aristocratic family.” “Aristocratic Bostonians.” “Aristocratic government.”

    Synonyms: aristocratic, aristocratical, blue-blooded, gentle, patrician · Similar: noble

  6. Morally rigorous and strict.

    “The puritan work ethic.” “She was anything but puritanical in her behavior.”

    Synonyms: puritanic, puritanical · Similar: nonindulgent

  7. Causing dejection.

    “A blue day.” “The dark days of the war.” “A week of rainy depressing weather.”

    Synonyms: dark, dingy, disconsolate, dismal, gloomy, grim, sorry, drab · Similar: depressing

Blue as a noun

  1. Blue color or pigment; resembling the color of the clear sky in the daytime.

    “He had eyes of bright blue.”

    Synonyms: blueness · A kind of: chromatic color · Kinds: azure, powder blue, steel blue, dark blue, greenish blue, purplish blue, ultramarine

  2. Blue clothing.

    “She was wearing blue.”

    A kind of: clothing

  3. Any organization or party whose uniforms or badges are blue.

    “The Union army was a vast blue.”

    A kind of: organization

  4. The sky as viewed during daylight.

    “He shot an arrow into the blue.”

    Synonyms: blue sky, blue air, wild blue yonder · A kind of: sky

  5. Used to whiten laundry or hair or give it a bluish tinge.

    Synonyms: bluing, blueing · A kind of: dye

  6. The sodium salt of amobarbital that is used as a barbiturate; used as a sedative and a hypnotic.

    Synonyms: amobarbital sodium, blue angel, blue devil · A kind of: amobarbital

  7. Any of numerous small butterflies of the family Lycaenidae.

    A kind of: lycaenid

Blue as a verb

  1. Turn blue.

    A kind of: discolor
