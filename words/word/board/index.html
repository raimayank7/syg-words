Board: meaning, synonyms and examples

Board is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A committee having supervisory powers.

In Hindi
तख़्ता, बोर्ड; सवार होना
Synonyms
plank, table, display panel, display board, dining table, control panel, instrument panel, control board, panel, circuit board
Word family
boarder

Board as a noun

  1. A committee having supervisory powers.

    “The board has seven members.”

    A kind of: committee · Kinds: appeal board, board of selectmen, governing board, directorate, advisory board, draft board, school board, zoning board

  2. A stout length of sawn timber; made in a wide variety of sizes and used for many purposes.

    Synonyms: plank · A kind of: lumber · Kinds: matchboard, skid, wale, chipboard, deal

  3. A flat piece of material designed for a special purpose.

    “He nailed boards across the windows.”

    A kind of: sheet · Kinds: aquaplane, breadboard, bulletin board, chopping board, drafting board, drainboard, floorboard, ironing board

  4. Food or meals in general.

    “She sets a fine table.” “Room and board.”

    Synonyms: table · A kind of: fare · Kinds: training table

  5. A vertical surface on which information can be displayed to public view.

    Synonyms: display panel, display board · A kind of: display · Kinds: big board

  6. A table at which meals are served.

    “He helped her clear the dining table.” “A feast was spread upon the board.”

    Synonyms: dining table · A kind of: table · Kinds: dining-room table, dinner table, high table, refectory table, triclinium

  7. Electrical device consisting of a flat insulated surface that contains switches and dials and meters for controlling other electrical devices.

    “He checked the instrument panel.” “Suddenly the board lit up like a Christmas tree.”

    Synonyms: control panel, instrument panel, control board, panel · A kind of: electrical device · Kinds: dashboard

  8. A printed circuit that can be inserted into expansion slots in a computer to increase the computer's capabilities.

    Synonyms: circuit board, circuit card, card, plug-in, add-in · A kind of: printed circuit

  9. A flat portable surface (usually rectangular) designed for board games.

    “He got out the board and set up the pieces.”

    Synonyms: gameboard · A kind of: surface · Kinds: backgammon board, checkerboard, cribbage board, dartboard, go board, monopoly board, pegboard

Board as a verb

  1. Get on board of (trains, buses, ships, aircraft, etc.).

    Synonyms: get on · A kind of: enter · Kinds: embark, entrain, catch

  2. Live and take one's meals at or in.

    “She rooms in an old boarding house.”

    Synonyms: room · A kind of: populate

  3. Lodge and take meals (at).

    A kind of: use

  4. Provide food and lodging (for).

    “The old lady is boarding three men.”

    A kind of: provide · Kinds: live in

Phrases and compounds with "board"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • board up (verb) Cover with wooden boards.
  • go board (noun) A board used for playing go.
  • on board (adverb) On a ship, train, plane or other vehicle.
  • pc board (noun) A removable circuit board for a personal computer; fits into a slot in the mother board.
  • big board (noun) A stock exchange in New York.
  • cpu board (noun) The main circuit board for a computer.
  • board foot (noun) The volume of a piece of wood 1 foot square and 1 inch thick.
  • board game (noun) A game played on a specially designed board.
  • board rule (noun) A measure used in computing board feet.
  • call-board (noun) A bulletin board backstage in a theater.
  • dart board (noun) A circular board of wood or cork used as the target in the game of darts.
  • fall-board (noun) The hinged protective covering that protects the keyboard of a piano when it is not being played.
  • wake board (noun) A buoyant board (resembling a surfboard) that is used to ride over water while being pulled behind a motorboat.
  • work-board (noun) A horizontal board that provides a supported surface for manual work.
  • bread board (noun) A wooden or plastic board on which dough is kneaded or bread is sliced.
  • chess board (noun) A checkerboard used to play chess.
