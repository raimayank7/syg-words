Body: meaning, synonyms and examples

Body is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The entire structure of an organism (an animal, plant, or human being).

Body as a noun

  1. The entire structure of an organism (an animal, plant, or human being).

    “He felt as if his whole body were on fire.”

    Synonyms: organic structure, physical structure · A kind of: natural object · Kinds: life form, human body, live body

  2. A group of persons associated by some common tie or occupation and regarded as an entity.

    “The whole body filed out of the auditorium.” “The student body.” “Administrative body.”

    A kind of: social group · Kinds: public, church, administration, corps, constituency, electoral college, school, college

  3. A natural object consisting of a dead animal or person.

    “They found the body in the lake.”

    Synonyms: dead body · A kind of: natural object · Kinds: carcase, carrion, cadaver, mummy

  4. An individual 3-dimensional object that has mass and that is distinguishable from other objects.

    “Heavenly body.”

    A kind of: natural object · Kinds: chromosome, inclusion body, mass, particle

  5. The body excluding the head and neck and limbs.

    “They moved their arms and legs and bodies.”

    Synonyms: torso, trunk · A kind of: body part

  6. A collection of particulars considered as a system.

    “A body of law.” “A body of doctrine.” “A body of precedents.”

    A kind of: system

  7. The property of holding together and retaining its shape.

    “Wool has more body than rayon.” “When the dough has enough consistency it is ready to bake.”

    Synonyms: consistency, consistence, eubstance · A kind of: property · Kinds: gaseousness, viscosity, thickness, thinness, hardness, softness, breakableness, unbreakableness

  8. The central message of a communication.

    “The body of the message was short.”

    A kind of: message

  9. The main mass of a thing.

    A kind of: mass

  10. A resonating chamber in a musical instrument (as the body of a violin).

    Synonyms: soundbox · A kind of: resonator

  11. The external structure of a vehicle.

    “The body of the car was badly rusted.”

    A kind of: structure · Kinds: bodywork, fuselage

Body as a verb

  1. Invest with or as with a body; give body to.

    Synonyms: personify · A kind of: embody
