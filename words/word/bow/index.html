Bow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Bow is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Bend one's knee or body, or lower one's head.

In Hindi
धनुष; झुकना
Synonyms
bow down, submit, defer, accede, give in, crouch, stoop, bend, bowknot, fore

Bow as a verb

  1. Bend one's knee or body, or lower one's head.

    “He bowed before the King.” “She bowed her head in shame.”

    Synonyms: bow down · A kind of: gesticulate · Kinds: congee

  2. Yield to another's wish or opinion.

    “The government bowed to the military pressure.”

    Synonyms: submit, defer, accede, give in · A kind of: yield

  3. Bend the head or the upper part of the body in a gesture of respect or greeting.

    “He bowed before the King.”

    A kind of: change posture · Kinds: take a bow, curtsy, scrape, genuflect

  4. Bend one's back forward from the waist on down.

    “He crouched down.” “She bowed before the Queen.” “The young man stooped to pick up the girl's purse.”

    Synonyms: crouch, stoop, bend · A kind of: bend · Kinds: squinch, huddle

  5. Play on a string instrument with a bow.

    A kind of: play

Bow as a noun

  1. A knot with two loops and loose ends; used to tie shoelaces.

    Synonyms: bowknot · A kind of: knot

  2. A slightly curved piece of resilient wood with taut horsehair strands; used in playing certain stringed instruments.

    A kind of: stick · Kinds: fiddlestick

  3. Front part of a vessel or aircraft.

    “He pointed the bow of the boat toward the finish line.”

    Synonyms: fore, prow, stem · A kind of: front

  4. A weapon for shooting arrows, composed of a curved piece of resilient wood with a taut cord to propel the arrow.

    A kind of: weapon · Kinds: crossbow, handbow, longbow

  5. Something curved in shape.

    Synonyms: arc · A kind of: curve · Kinds: rainbow

  6. Bending the head or body or knee as a sign of reverence or submission or shame or greeting.

    Synonyms: bowing, obeisance · A kind of: reverence, gesture · Kinds: genuflection, kowtow, scrape

  7. An appearance by actors or performers at the end of the concert or play in order to acknowledge the applause of the audience.

    Synonyms: curtain call · A kind of: thanks

  8. A decorative interlacing of ribbons.

    A kind of: decoration

  9. A stroke with a curved piece of wood with taut horsehair strands that is used in playing stringed instruments.

    A kind of: stroke · Kinds: down-bow, up-bow

Phrases and compounds with "bow"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • up-bow (noun) An upward stroke from the tip to the heel of the bow.
  • bow leg (noun) Outward curvature of the legs.
  • bow out (verb) Remove oneself from an obligation.
  • bow tie (noun) A man's tie that ties in a bow.
  • bow-tie (noun) A man's tie that ties in a bow.
  • bow-wow (noun) The bark of a dog.
  • bow down (verb) Get into a prostrate position, as in submission.
  • bow legs (noun) Outward curvature of the legs.
  • bow wood (noun) Small shrubby deciduous yellowwood tree of south central United States having spines, glossy dark green leaves and an inedible fruit that resembles an orange; its hard orange-colored wood used for bows by Native Americans; frequently planted as boundary hedge.
  • down-bow (noun) A downward stroke from the heel to the tip of the bow.
  • mouth bow (noun) A small lyre-shaped musical instrument that is placed between the teeth and played by twanging a wire tongue while changing the shape of the mouth cavity.
  • sound bow (noun) Contact (the part of a bell) against which the clapper strikes.
  • bow window (noun) A window that sticks out from the outside wall of a house.
  • take a bow (verb) Acknowledge praise or accept credit.
  • violin bow (noun) A bow used in playing the violin.
  • cupid's bow (noun) The double curve of the upper lip when considered to resemble Cupid's bow.
