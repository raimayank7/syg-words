Box: meaning, synonyms and examples

Box is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A (usually rectangular) container; may have a lid.

In Hindi
डिब्बा
Synonyms
loge, boxful, corner, boxwood, box seat, package
Opposites
unbox
Word family
boxy, boxer, boxing

Box as a noun

  1. A (usually rectangular) container; may have a lid.

    “He rummaged through a box of spare parts.”

    A kind of: container · Kinds: ballot box, bandbox, carton, casket, cereal box, chest, cigar box, crate

  2. Private area in a theater or grandstand where a small group can watch the performance.

    “The royal box was empty.”

    Synonyms: loge · A kind of: compartment · Kinds: skybox

  3. The quantity contained in a box.

    “He gave her a box of chocolates.”

    Synonyms: boxful · A kind of: containerful

  4. A predicament from which a skillful or graceful escape is impossible.

    “His lying got him into a tight corner.”

    Synonyms: corner · A kind of: predicament

  5. A rectangular drawing.

    “The flowchart contained many boxes.”

    A kind of: rectangle

  6. Evergreen shrubs or small trees.

    Synonyms: boxwood · A kind of: shrub · Kinds: common box

  7. Any one of several designated areas on a ball field where the batter or catcher or coaches are positioned.

    “The umpire warned the batter to stay in the batter's box.”

    A kind of: area · Kinds: batter's box

  8. The driver's seat on a coach.

    “An armed guard sat in the box with the driver.”

    Synonyms: box seat · A kind of: seat

  9. Separate partitioned area in a public place for a few people.

    “The sentry stayed in his box to avoid the cold.”

    A kind of: compartment · Kinds: jury box, press box, witness box

  10. A blow with the hand (usually on the ear).

    “I gave him a good box on the ear.”

    A kind of: blow

Box as a verb

  1. Put into a box.

    “Box the gift, please.”

    Synonyms: package · Opposite: unbox · A kind of: encase

  2. Hit with the fist.

    “I'll box your ears!”

    A kind of: hit

  3. Engage in a boxing match.

    A kind of: contend · Kinds: spar, prizefight, shadowbox

Phrases and compounds with "box"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • box in (verb) Enclose or confine as if in a box.
  • box up (verb) Enclose or confine as if in a box.
  • po box (noun) A numbered compartment in a post office where mail is put to be called for.
  • toe box (noun) The forward tip of the upper of a shoe or boot that provides space and protection for the toes.
  • toy box (noun) Chest for storage of toys.
  • alms box (noun) Box for collecting alms, especially one in a church.
  • boom box (noun) A portable stereo.
  • box beam (noun) A beam built up from boards; has a hollow rectangular cross section.
  • box calf (noun) Black calfskin leather tanned with chromium salts.
  • box coat (noun) A short coat that hangs loosely from the shoulders.
  • box kite (noun) A kite shaped like a box open at both ends.
  • box seat (noun) A special seat in a theater or grandstand box.
  • call box (noun) Booth for using a telephone.
  • coin box (noun) The part of a slot machine that serves as a receptacle for the coins.
  • dice box (noun) A small container (open at one end) in which dice are shaken by hand and from which they are thrown.
  • gear box (noun) The shell (metal casing) in which a train of gears is sealed.
