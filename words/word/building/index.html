Building: meaning, synonyms and examples

Building is used as a noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A structure that has a roof and walls and stands more or less permanently in one place.

In Hindi
इमारत, भवन
Synonyms
edifice, construction
Word family
build

Building as a noun

  1. A structure that has a roof and walls and stands more or less permanently in one place.

    “There was a three-story building on the corner.” “It was an imposing edifice.”

    Synonyms: edifice · A kind of: structure · Kinds: abattoir, apartment building, architecture, aviary, bathhouse, bowling alley, center, chapterhouse

  2. The act of constructing something.

    “During the construction we had to take a detour.” “His hobby was the building of boats.”

    Synonyms: construction · A kind of: creating from raw materials · Kinds: crenelation, erecting, house-raising, fabrication, dry walling, grading, road construction, shipbuilding

  3. The commercial activity involved in repairing old structures or constructing new ones.

    “Their main business is home construction.” “Workers in the building trades.”

    Synonyms: construction · A kind of: commercial enterprise · Kinds: jerry-building

  4. The occupants of a building.

    “The entire building complained about the noise.”

    A kind of: gathering

Phrases and compounds with "building"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bank building (noun) A building in which the business of banking transacted.
  • building code (noun) Set of standards established and enforced by local government for the structural safety of buildings.
  • building site (noun) A lot on which there are no permanent buildings.
  • farm building (noun) A building on a farm.
  • ship building (noun) The construction of ships.
  • soil-building (adjective) (of crops) planted to improve the quality of the soil.
  • building block (noun) A single undivided natural thing occurring in the composition of something else.
  • jerry-building (noun) Construction of inferior buildings for a quick profit.
  • roman building (noun) A building constructed by the ancient Romans.
  • telco building (noun) A building that houses telecommunications equipment.
  • building permit (noun) A document authorizing the holder to construct a building of a particular kind on a particular lot.
  • morale building (noun) Anything that serves to increase morale.
  • muscle building (noun) Exercise that builds muscles through tension.
  • office building (noun) A building containing offices where work is done.
  • building complex (noun) A whole structure (as a building) made up of interconnected or related structures.
  • building society (noun) British equivalent of United States savings and loan association.
