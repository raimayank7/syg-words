Business: meaning, synonyms and examples

Business is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A commercial or industrial enterprise and the people who constitute it.

In Hindi
व्यापार, कारोबार
Synonyms
concern, business concern, business organization, business organisation, commercial enterprise, business enterprise, occupation, job, line of work, line

Business as a noun

  1. A commercial or industrial enterprise and the people who constitute it.

    “He bought his brother's business.” “A small mom-and-pop business.” “A racially integrated business concern.”

    Synonyms: concern, business concern, business organization, business organisation · A kind of: enterprise · Kinds: agency, brokerage, carrier, chain, firm, franchise, manufacturer, partnership

  2. The activity of providing goods and services involving financial and commercial and industrial aspects.

    “Computers are now widely used in business.”

    Synonyms: commercial enterprise, business enterprise · A kind of: commerce · Kinds: tourism, fishing, butchery, storage, industry, field, employee-owned enterprise, finance

  3. The principal activity in your life that you do to earn money.

    “He's not in my line of business.”

    Synonyms: occupation, job, line of work, line · A kind of: activity · Kinds: confectionery, sport, farming, game, career, employment, appointment, position

  4. A rightful concern or responsibility.

    “It's none of your business.” “Mind your own business.”

    A kind of: concern

  5. An immediate objective.

    “Gossip was the main business of the evening.”

    A kind of: aim · Kinds: occasions

  6. The volume of commercial activity.

    “Business is good today.” “Show me where the business was today.”

    A kind of: business activity · Kinds: trade, land-office business

  7. Business concerns collectively.

    “Government and business could not agree.”

    Synonyms: business sector · A kind of: sector · Kinds: big business

  8. Customers collectively.

    “They have an upper class clientele.”

    Synonyms: clientele, patronage · A kind of: people

  9. Incidental activity performed by an actor for dramatic effect.

    “His business with the cane was hilarious.”

    Synonyms: stage business, byplay · A kind of: acting · Kinds: shtik

Phrases and compounds with "business"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • big business (noun) Commercial enterprises organized and financed on a scale large enough to influence social and political policies.
  • oil business (noun) An industry that produces and delivers oil and oil products.
  • toy business (noun) An industry that manufactures and sells toys for children.
  • business card (noun) A card on which are printed the person's name and business affiliation.
  • business deal (noun) A particular instance of buying or selling.
  • business firm (noun) The members of a business organization that owns or operates one or more establishments.
  • business life (noun) A career in industrial or commercial or professional activities.
  • business line (noun) A particular kind of product or merchandise.
  • business loan (noun) A bank loan granted for the use of a business.
  • business news (noun) News about businesses and investments.
  • business suit (noun) A suit of clothes traditionally worn by businessmen.
  • show business (noun) Those involved in providing entertainment: radio and television and films and theater.
  • business agent (noun) An agent who handles business affairs for another; especially one who deals with employers.
  • business cycle (noun) Recurring fluctuations in economic activity consisting of recession and recovery and growth and decline.
  • business index (noun) A statistical compilation that provides a context for economic or financial conditions.
  • business lunch (noun) Lunch (usually at a restaurant) where business is discussed and the cost is charged as a business expense.
