Buy: meaning, synonyms and examples

Buy is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Obtain by purchase; acquire by means of a financial transaction.

In Hindi
ख़रीदना
Synonyms
purchase, bribe, corrupt, grease one's palms, bargain, steal
Opposites
sell
Word family
buyer, buying

Buy as a verb

  1. Obtain by purchase; acquire by means of a financial transaction.

    “The family purchased a new car.” “The conglomerate acquired a new company.” “She buys for the big department store.”

    Synonyms: purchase · Opposite: sell · A kind of: get · Kinds: buy back, take, get, subscribe, take over, pick up, take out, impulse-buy

  2. Make illegal payments to in exchange for favors or influence.

    “This judge can be bought.”

    Synonyms: bribe, corrupt, grease one's palms · A kind of: pay · Kinds: sop, buy off

  3. Be worth or be capable of buying.

    “This sum will buy you a ride on the train.”

    A kind of: be

  4. Acquire by trade or sacrifice or exchange.

    “She wanted to buy his love with her dedication to him and his work.”

    A kind of: get

  5. Accept as true.

    “I can't buy this story.”

    A kind of: believe

Buy as a noun

  1. An advantageous purchase.

    “She got a bargain at the auction.” “The stock was a real buy at that price.”

    Synonyms: bargain, steal · A kind of: purchase · Kinds: song, travel bargain

Phrases and compounds with "buy"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • buy at (verb) Do one's shopping at; do business with; be a customer or client of.
  • buy in (verb) Amass so as to keep for future use or sale or for a particular occasion or use.
  • buy up (verb) Take over ownership of; of corporations and companies.
  • buy off (verb) Pay someone with influence in order to receive a favor.
  • buy out (verb) Take over ownership of; of corporations and companies.
  • buy back (verb) Buy what had previously been sold, lost, or given away.
  • buy food (verb) Purchase prepared food to be eaten at home.
  • buy into (verb) Buy stocks or shares of a company.
  • buy time (verb) Act so as to delay an event or action in order to gain an advantage.
  • impulse-buy (verb) Buy on impulse without proper reflection.
  • buy the farm (verb) Pass from physical life and lose all bodily attributes and functions necessary to sustain life.
