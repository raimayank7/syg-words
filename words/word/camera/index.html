Camera: meaning, synonyms and examples

Camera is used as a noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Equipment for taking photographs (usually consisting of a lightproof box with a lens at one end and light-sensitive film at the other).

In Hindi
कैमरा
Synonyms
photographic camera, television camera, tv camera

Camera as a noun

  1. Equipment for taking photographs (usually consisting of a lightproof box with a lens at one end and light-sensitive film at the other).

    Synonyms: photographic camera · A kind of: photographic equipment · Kinds: box camera, candid camera, digital camera, flash camera, motion-picture camera, point-and-shoot camera, portrait camera, reflex camera

  2. Television equipment consisting of a lens system that focuses an image on a photosensitive mosaic that is scanned by an electron beam.

    Synonyms: television camera, tv camera · A kind of: television equipment · Kinds: camcorder

Phrases and compounds with "camera"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in camera (adverb) Kept private or confined to those intimately concerned.
  • on camera (adverb) Within range of a movie or television camera.
  • tv camera (noun) Television equipment consisting of a lens system that focuses an image on a photosensitive mosaic that is scanned by an electron beam.
  • box camera (noun) A simple camera shaped like a rectangular box.
  • camera care (noun) Keeping a camera in good working order.
  • camera lens (noun) A lens that focuses the image in a camera.
  • cine-camera (noun) A camera that takes a sequence of photographs that can give the illusion of motion when viewed in rapid succession.
  • camera angle (noun) The point of view of a camera.
  • flash camera (noun) A camera with a photoflash attachment.
  • movie camera (noun) A camera that takes a sequence of photographs that can give the illusion of motion when viewed in rapid succession.
  • sound camera (noun) A movie camera that records sounds in synchrony with the visual images.
  • camera lucida (noun) An optical device consisting of an attachment that enables an observer to view simultaneously the image and a drawing surface for sketching it.
  • camera tripod (noun) A tripod used to support a camera.
  • candid camera (noun) A miniature camera with a fast lens.
  • reflex camera (noun) Camera that allows the photographer to view and focus the exact scene being photographed.
  • camera obscura (noun) A darkened enclosure in which images of outside objects are projected through a small aperture or lens onto a facing surface.
