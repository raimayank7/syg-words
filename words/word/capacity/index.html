Capacity: meaning, synonyms and examples

Capacity is used as a noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Capability to perform or produce.

In Hindi
क्षमता
Synonyms
capability, content, capacitance, electrical capacity, mental ability
Opposites
incapacity
Word family
capacitate, capacious

Capacity as a noun

  1. Capability to perform or produce.

    “Among his gifts is his capacity for true altruism.” “Limited runway capacity.” “A great capacity for growth.”

    Opposite: incapacity · A kind of: capability

  2. The susceptibility of something to a particular treatment.

    “The capability of a metal to be fused.”

    Synonyms: capability · A kind of: susceptibility · Kinds: resistance, activity

  3. The amount that can be contained.

    “The gas tank has a capacity of 12 gallons.”

    Synonyms: content · A kind of: volume · Kinds: vital capacity

  4. The maximum production possible.

    “The plant is working at 80 per cent capacity.”

    A kind of: production

  5. A specified function.

    “He was employed in the capacity of director.” “He should be retained in his present capacity at a higher salary.”

    A kind of: function

  6. (computer science) the amount of information (in bytes) that can be stored on a disk drive.

    “The capacity of a hard disk drive is usually expressed in megabytes.”

    A kind of: indefinite quantity · Kinds: formatted capacity, unformatted capacity

  7. An electrical phenomenon whereby an electric charge is stored.

    Synonyms: capacitance, electrical capacity · A kind of: electrical phenomenon

  8. The power to learn or retain knowledge; in law, the ability to understand the facts and significance of your behavior.

    Synonyms: mental ability · Opposite: incapacity · A kind of: ability · Kinds: prescience

Phrases and compounds with "capacity"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • capacity unit (noun) A unit of measurement of volume or capacity.
  • field capacity (noun) The maximum amount of water that a particular soil can hold.
  • vital capacity (noun) The maximum amount of air that can be exhaled after a maximum inhalation (usually tested with a spirometer); used to determine the condition of lung tissue.
  • mental capacity (noun) Mental ability.
  • capacity measure (noun) A unit of measurement of volume or capacity.
  • channel capacity (noun) The maximum data rate that can be attained over a given channel.
  • seating capacity (noun) The number of people that can be seated in a vehicle or auditorium or stadium etc.
  • formatted capacity (noun) (computer science) the usable capacity of a disk drive; the amount of space that is left after the sector headings and boundary definitions and timing information have been added by formatting the disk.
  • electrical capacity (noun) An electrical phenomenon whereby an electric charge is stored.
  • metric capacity unit (noun) A capacity unit defined in metric terms.
  • unformatted capacity (noun) (computer science) the total number of bytes on a disk including the space that will be required to format it.
  • british capacity unit (noun) A unit of measure for capacity officially adopted in the British Imperial System; British units are both dry and wet.
  • imperial capacity unit (noun) A unit of measure for capacity officially adopted in the British Imperial System; British units are both dry and wet.
