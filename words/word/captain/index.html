Captain: meaning, synonyms and examples

Captain is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: An officer holding a rank below a major but above a lieutenant.

Captain as a noun

  1. An officer holding a rank below a major but above a lieutenant.

    A kind of: commissioned military officer

  2. The naval officer in command of a military ship.

    Synonyms: skipper · A kind of: commissioned naval officer · Kinds: flag captain

  3. A policeman in charge of a precinct.

    Synonyms: police captain, police chief · A kind of: policeman

  4. An officer who is licensed to command a merchant ship.

    Synonyms: master, sea captain, skipper · A kind of: officer

  5. The leader of a group of people.

    “A captain of industry.”

    Synonyms: chieftain · A kind of: leader

  6. The pilot in charge of an airship.

    Synonyms: senior pilot · A kind of: pilot · Kinds: group captain

  7. A dining-room attendant who is in charge of the waiters and the seating of customers.

    Synonyms: headwaiter, maitre d'hotel, maitre d' · A kind of: dining-room attendant

Captain as a verb

  1. Be the captain of a sports team.

    A kind of: head

Phrases and compounds with "captain"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • captain bob (noun) United States explorer who accompanied Peary's expedition to the North Pole and who led many other Arctic trips (1875-1946).
  • sea captain (noun) An officer who is licensed to command a merchant ship.
  • bell captain (noun) The supervisor of bellboys in a hotel.
  • captain cook (noun) English navigator who claimed the east coast of Australia for Britain and discovered several Pacific islands (1728-1779).
  • captain kidd (noun) Scottish sea captain who was hired to protect British shipping in the Indian Ocean and then was accused of piracy and hanged (1645-1701).
  • flag captain (noun) The captain of a flagship.
  • captain bligh (noun) British admiral; was captain of the H.M.S. Bounty in 1789 when part of the crew mutinied and set him afloat in an open boat; a few weeks later he arrived safely in Timor 4,000 miles away (1754-1817).
  • captain hicks (noun) The cardinal number that is the sum of five and one.
  • group captain (noun) A commissioned officer (especially one in the Royal Air Force) equivalent in rank to a colonel in the army.
  • police captain (noun) A policeman in charge of a precinct.
  • captain james cook (noun) English navigator who claimed the east coast of Australia for Britain and discovered several Pacific islands (1728-1779).
  • captain john smith (noun) English explorer who helped found the colony at Jamestown, Virginia; was said to have been saved by Pocahontas (1580-1631).
  • captain horatio hornblower (noun) A fictional English admiral during the Napoleonic Wars in novels written by C. S. Forester.
