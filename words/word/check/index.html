Check: meaning, synonyms and examples

Check is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (13 senses). Its most common meaning is: Examine so as to determine accuracy, quality, or condition.

In Hindi
जाँचना; चेक
Synonyms
check up on, look into, check out, suss out, check over, go over, check into, see, insure, see to it
Word family
checker

Check as a verb

  1. Examine so as to determine accuracy, quality, or condition.

    “Check the brakes.” “Check out the engine.”

    Synonyms: check up on, look into, check out, suss out, check over, go over, check into · A kind of: analyze · Kinds: watch

  2. Make an examination or investigation.

    “Check into the rumor.” “Check the time of the class.”

    A kind of: examine · Kinds: screen, countercheck

  3. Be careful or certain to do something; make certain of something.

    “He verified that the valves were closed.” “See that the curtains are closed.” “Control the quality of the product.”

    Synonyms: see, insure, see to it, ensure, control, ascertain, assure · A kind of: verify · Kinds: proofread, control, double-check, cross-check, cinch, spot-check, cover

  4. Lessen the intensity of; temper; hold in restraint; hold or keep within limits.

    “Hold your tongue.” “Hold your temper.” “Control your anger.”

    Synonyms: control, hold in, hold, contain, curb, moderate · A kind of: restrain · Kinds: restrict, suppress, damp, mortify, deny, restrict, train, catch

  5. Stop for a moment, as if out of uncertainty or caution.

    “She checked for an instant and missed a step.”

    A kind of: stop

  6. Put a check mark on or near or next to.

    “Please check each name on the list.” “Tick off the items.” “Mark off the units.”

    Synonyms: check off, mark, mark off, tick off, tick · A kind of: verify · Kinds: receipt

  7. Slow the growth or development of.

    “The brain damage will retard the child's language development.”

    Synonyms: retard, delay · A kind of: change · Kinds: dampen, stay

  8. Be verified or confirmed; pass inspection.

    “These stories don't check!”

    Synonyms: check out

  9. Be compatible, similar or consistent; coincide in their characteristics.

    “The two stories don't agree in many details.” “The handwriting checks with the signature on the check.” “The suspect's fingerprints don't match those on the gun.”

    Synonyms: match, fit, correspond, jibe, gibe, tally, agree · A kind of: equal · Kinds: consist, look, answer, coincide, align, correlate, parallel, twin

  10. Block or impede (a player from the opposing team) in ice hockey.

    A kind of: obstruct

  11. Develop (children's) behavior by instruction and practice; especially to teach self-control.

    “Parents must discipline their children.” “Is this dog trained?”

    Synonyms: discipline, train, condition · A kind of: develop · Kinds: mortify

  12. Consign for shipment on a vehicle.

    “Check your luggage before boarding.”

    A kind of: consign

  13. Hand over something to somebody as for temporary safekeeping.

    “Check your coat at the door.”

    A kind of: consign

  14. Abandon the intended prey, turn, and pursue an inferior prey.

    A kind of: stop

Check as a noun

  1. A written order directing a bank to pay money.

    “He paid all his bills by check.”

    Synonyms: bank check, cheque · A kind of: draft · Kinds: bad check, kite, kite, counter check, giro, paycheck, certified check, personal check

  2. An appraisal of the state of affairs.

    “They made an assay of the contents.” “A check on its dependability under stress.”

    Synonyms: assay · A kind of: appraisal · Kinds: countercheck, paternity test, stress test

  3. The bill in a restaurant.

    “He asked the waiter for the check.”

    Synonyms: chit, tab · A kind of: bill

  4. The state of inactivity following an interruption.

    “The negotiations were in arrest.” “Held them in check.” “During the halt he got some lunch.”

    Synonyms: arrest, halt, hitch, stay, stop, stoppage · A kind of: inaction · Kinds: countercheck, logjam

  5. Additional proof that something that was believed (some fact or hypothesis or theory) is correct.

    “Fossils provided further confirmation of the evolutionary theory.”

    Synonyms: confirmation, verification, substantiation · A kind of: proof · Kinds: bed check, crosscheck, parity check, checksum

  6. The act of inspecting or verifying.

    “They made a check of their equipment.” “The pilot ran through the check-out procedure.”

    Synonyms: checkout, check-out procedure · A kind of: inspection · Kinds: spot check

  7. A mark indicating that something has been noted or completed etc.

    “As he called the role he put a check mark by each student's name.”

    Synonyms: check mark, tick · A kind of: mark

  8. Something immaterial that interferes with or delays action or progress.

    Synonyms: hindrance, hinderance, deterrent, impediment, balk, baulk, handicap · A kind of: difficulty · Kinds: albatross, bind, diriment impediment, drag, obstacle, straitjacket

  9. A mark left after a small piece has been chopped or broken off of something.

    Synonyms: chip · A kind of: blemish

  10. A textile pattern of squares or crossed lines (resembling a checkerboard).

    “She wore a skirt with checks.”

    A kind of: weave · Kinds: houndstooth check

  11. The act of restraining power or action or limiting excess.

    “His common sense is a bridle to his quick temper.”

    Synonyms: bridle, curb · A kind of: restraint

  12. Obstructing an opponent in ice hockey.

    A kind of: obstruction · Kinds: crosscheck, poke check

  13. (chess) a direct attack on an opponent's king.

    A kind of: chess move · Kinds: discovered check

Phrases and compounds with "check"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • check in (verb) Announce one's arrival, e.g. at hotels or airports.
  • check-in (noun) The act of reporting your presence (as at an airport or a hotel).
  • bad check (noun) A check that is dishonored on presentation because of insufficient funds.
  • bed check (noun) A check that everyone is in bed by the time they should be.
  • check bit (noun) (computer science) a bit that is used in an error detection procedure in which a 0 or 1 is added to each group of bits so that it will have either an odd number of 1's or an even number of 1's; e.g., if the parity is odd then any group of bits that arrives with an even number of 1's must contain an error.
  • check off (verb) Put a check mark on or near or next to.
  • check out (verb) Examine so as to determine accuracy, quality, or condition.
  • bank check (noun) A written order directing a bank to pay money.
  • check girl (noun) A female checker.
  • check into (verb) Examine so as to determine accuracy, quality, or condition.
  • check mark (noun) A mark indicating that something has been noted or completed etc.
  • check over (verb) Examine so as to determine accuracy, quality, or condition.
  • check stub (noun) The part of a check that is retained as a record.
  • poke check (noun) Knocking the puck away by jabbing at it with the hockey stick.
  • rain check (noun) A promise that an unaccepted offer will be renewed in the future.
  • spot check (noun) A check on work performance or product quality made at random times without warning.
