Chief: meaning, synonyms and examples

Chief is used as an adjective (1 sense) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Most important element.

In Hindi
मुखिया; मुख्य
Synonyms
main, primary, principal, master, head, top dog, foreman, gaffer, honcho, boss

Chief as an adjective

  1. Most important element.

    “The chief aim of living.” “The main doors were of solid glass.” “The principal rivers of America.”

    Synonyms: main, primary, principal, master · Similar: important

Chief as a noun

  1. A person who is in charge.

    “The head of the whole operation.”

    Synonyms: head, top dog · A kind of: leader · Kinds: administrator, administrator, capo, department head, don, general, general manager, grand dragon

  2. A person who exercises control over workers.

    “If you want to leave early you have to ask the foreman.”

    Synonyms: foreman, gaffer, honcho, boss · A kind of: supervisor · Kinds: baas, ganger, straw boss

  3. The head of a tribe or clan.

    Synonyms: headman, tribal chief, chieftain · A kind of: leader · Kinds: pendragon

Phrases and compounds with "chief"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in-chief (adjective) Indicating the head of a staff.
  • arab chief (noun) The leader of an Arab village or family.
  • fire chief (noun) The head of a fire department.
  • chief joseph (noun) Leader of the Nez Perce in their retreat from United States troops (1840-1904).
  • indian chief (noun) The leader of a group of Native Americans.
  • police chief (noun) A policeman in charge of a precinct.
  • tribal chief (noun) The head of a tribe or clan.
  • chief justice (noun) The judge who presides over a supreme court.
  • chief of staff (noun) The senior officer of a service of the armed forces.
  • chief of state (noun) The chief public representative of a country who may also be the head of government.
  • chief assistant (noun) The most helpful assistant.
  • chief constable (noun) The head of the police force in a county (or similar area).
  • chief executive (noun) The person who holds the office of head of state of the United States government.
  • chief secretary (noun) A member of the British Cabinet.
  • editor in chief (noun) A person responsible for the editorial aspects of publication; the person who determines the final content of a text (especially of a newspaper or magazine).
  • little chief hare (noun) North American pika.
