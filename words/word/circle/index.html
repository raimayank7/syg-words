Circle: meaning, synonyms and examples

Circle is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Ellipse in which the two axes are of equal length; a plane curve generated by one point moving at a constant distance from a fixed point.

In Hindi
वृत्त, घेरा
Synonyms
set, band, lot, lap, circuit, traffic circle, rotary, roundabout, rophy, rope
Word family
circular, circulate

Circle as a noun

  1. Ellipse in which the two axes are of equal length; a plane curve generated by one point moving at a constant distance from a fixed point.

    “He calculated the circumference of the circle.”

    A kind of: ellipse · Kinds: circle of curvature, circlet, equator, epicycle

  2. An unofficial association of people or groups.

    “The smart set goes there.” “They were an angry lot.”

    Synonyms: set, band, lot · A kind of: social group · Kinds: car pool, clique, cohort, conspiracy, horsy set, jet set, party

  3. Something approximating the shape of a circle.

    “The chairs were arranged in a circle.”

    A kind of: shape

  4. Movement once around a course.

    “He drove an extra lap just for insurance.”

    Synonyms: lap, circuit · A kind of: locomotion · Kinds: pace lap, victory lap

  5. A road junction at which traffic streams circularly around a central island.

    “The accident blocked all traffic at the rotary.”

    Synonyms: traffic circle, rotary, roundabout · A kind of: junction

  6. Street names for flunitrazepan.

    Synonyms: rophy, rope, roofy, roach

  7. A curved section or tier of seats in a hall or theater or opera house; usually the first tier above the orchestra.

    “They had excellent seats in the dress circle.”

    Synonyms: dress circle · A kind of: seating

  8. Any circular or rotating mechanism.

    “The machine punched out metal circles.”

    Synonyms: round · A kind of: rotating mechanism · Kinds: disk

Circle as a verb

  1. Travel around something.

    “Circle the globe.”

    A kind of: travel · Kinds: circumambulate, circumnavigate, revolve around

  2. Move in circles.

    Synonyms: circulate · A kind of: travel · Kinds: orb, troll, loop, loop

  3. Form a circle around.

    “Encircle the errors.”

    Synonyms: encircle · A kind of: shape

Phrases and compounds with "circle"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • dip circle (noun) A measuring instrument for measuring the angle of magnetic dip (as from an airplane).
  • back circle (noun) A feat in which an acrobat arches the back from a prone position and bends the knees until the toes touch the head.
  • hour circle (noun) A great circle on the celestial sphere that passes through both celestial poles.
  • circle round (verb) Move around in a circular motion.
  • color circle (noun) A chart in which complementary colors (or their names) are arranged on opposite sides of a circle.
  • dress circle (noun) A curved section or tier of seats in a hall or theater or opera house; usually the first tier above the orchestra.
  • fairy circle (noun) A ring of fungi marking the periphery of the perennial underground growth of the mycelium.
  • grand circle (noun) A gymnastic exercise performed on the horizontal bar by swinging around it with the body fully extended.
  • great circle (noun) A circular line on the surface of a sphere formed by intersecting it with a plane passing through the center.
  • inner circle (noun) An exclusive circle of people with a common purpose.
  • polar circle (noun) A line of latitude at the north or south poles.
  • arctic circle (noun) A line of latitude near but to the south of the north pole; it marks the northernmost point at which the sun is visible on the northern winter solstice and the southernmost point at which the midnight sun can be seen on the northern summer solstice.
  • circle around (verb) Move around in a circular motion.
  • family circle (noun) Rearmost or uppermost area in the balcony containing the least expensive seats.
  • parquet circle (noun) Seating at the rear of the main floor (beneath the balconies).
  • quarter-circle (noun) A quarter of the circumference of a circle.
