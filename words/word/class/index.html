Class: meaning, synonyms and examples

Class is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A collection of things sharing a common attribute.

In Hindi
कक्षा; वर्ग
Synonyms
category, family, form, grade, course, stratum, social class, socio-economic class, course of study, course of instruction
Word family
classify, classy

Class as a noun

  1. A collection of things sharing a common attribute.

    “There are two classes of detergents.”

    Synonyms: category, family · A kind of: collection · Kinds: grammatical category, substitution class, brass family, violin family, woodwind family, stamp, declension, conjugation

  2. A body of students who are taught together.

    “Early morning classes are always sleepy.”

    Synonyms: form, grade, course · A kind of: gathering · Kinds: master class, section

  3. People having the same social, economic, or educational status.

    “The working class.” “An emerging professional class.”

    Synonyms: stratum, social class, socio-economic class · A kind of: people · Kinds: world, age class, agriculture, brotherhood, estate of the realm, labor, lower class, middle class

  4. Education imparted in a series of lessons or meetings.

    “He took a course in basket weaving.” “Flirting is not unknown in college classes.”

    Synonyms: course, course of study, course of instruction · A kind of: education · Kinds: adult education, art class, correspondence course, course of lectures, directed study, elective course, extension course, home study

  5. A league ranked by quality.

    “He played baseball in class D for two years.” “Princeton is in the NCAA Division 1-AA.”

    Synonyms: division · A kind of: league

  6. A body of students who graduate together.

    “The class of '97.” “She was in my year at Hoehandle High.”

    Synonyms: year · A kind of: gathering · Kinds: graduating class, senior class, junior class, sophomore class, freshman class

  7. (biology) a taxonomic group containing one or more orders.

    A kind of: taxonomic group · Kinds: amphibia

  8. Elegance in dress or behavior.

    “She has a lot of class.”

    A kind of: elegance

Class as a verb

  1. Arrange or order by classes or categories.

    “How would you classify these pottery shards--are they prehistoric?”

    Synonyms: classify, sort, assort, sort out, separate · A kind of: categorize · Kinds: unitize, catalogue, isolate, refer, reclassify, size, dichotomize, pigeonhole

Phrases and compounds with "class"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • age class (noun) People in the same age range.
  • art class (noun) A class in which you learn to draw or paint.
  • class act (noun) Someone who shows impressive and stylish excellence.
  • class war (noun) Conflict between social or economic classes (especially between the capitalist and proletariat classes).
  • low-class (adjective) Occupying the lowest socioeconomic position in a society.
  • class aves (noun) (ornithology) the class of birds.
  • class list (noun) A list issued by examiners that categorizes students according to the class of honours they achieved in their degree examinations.
  • class nuda (noun) Ctenophores lacking tentacles; comprises one genus: beroe.
  • form class (noun) One of the traditional categories of words intended to reflect their functions in a grammatical context.
  • high-class (adjective) Pretentiously elegant.
  • life class (noun) An art class using a live human model.
  • shop class (noun) A course of instruction in a trade (as carpentry or electricity).
  • word class (noun) One of the traditional categories of words intended to reflect their functions in a grammatical context.
  • cabin class (noun) A class of accommodations on a ship or train or plane that are less expensive than first class accommodations.
  • class musci (noun) True mosses: bryophytes having leafy rather than thalloid gametophytes: comprises orders Andreaeales; Bryales; Dicranales; Eubryales; Sphagnales.
  • first class (noun) The highest rank in a classification.
