Coach: meaning, synonyms and examples

Coach is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: (sports) someone in charge of training an athlete or a team.

In Hindi
कोच; डिब्बा
Synonyms
manager, handler, private instructor, tutor, passenger car, carriage, four-in-hand, coach-and-four, bus, autobus
Word family
coaching

Coach as a noun

  1. (sports) someone in charge of training an athlete or a team.

    Synonyms: manager, handler · A kind of: trainer · Kinds: baseball coach, basketball coach, conditioner, football coach, hockey coach, tennis coach

  2. A person who gives private instruction (as in singing, acting, etc.).

    Synonyms: private instructor, tutor · A kind of: teacher · Kinds: crammer

  3. A railcar where passengers ride.

    Synonyms: passenger car, carriage · A kind of: car · Kinds: dining car, nonsmoker, parlor car, sleeping car, smoker

  4. A carriage pulled by four horses with one driver.

    Synonyms: four-in-hand, coach-and-four · A kind of: carriage · Kinds: stagecoach

  5. A vehicle carrying many passengers; used for public transport.

    “He always rode the bus to work.”

    Synonyms: bus, autobus, charabanc, double-decker, jitney, motorbus, motorcoach, omnibus · A kind of: public transport · Kinds: minibus, school bus, trolleybus

Coach as a verb

  1. Teach and supervise (someone); act as a trainer or coach (to), as in sports.

    “He is training our Olympic team.” “She is coaching the crew.”

    Synonyms: train · A kind of: teach

  2. Drive a coach.

    A kind of: drive
