Collapse: meaning, synonyms and examples

Collapse is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Break down, literally or metaphorically.

In Hindi
ढह जाना
Synonyms
fall in, cave in, give, give way, break, founder, break down, crumble, crumple, tumble
Word family
collapsible

Collapse as a verb

  1. Break down, literally or metaphorically.

    “The wall collapsed.” “The business collapsed.” “The dam broke.”

    Synonyms: fall in, cave in, give, give way, break, founder · A kind of: change · Kinds: implode, buckle, flop, break, slump

  2. Collapse due to fatigue, an illness, or a sudden attack.

    Synonyms: break down · A kind of: suffer · Kinds: drop like flies, fall over

  3. Fold or close up.

    “Fold up your umbrella.” “Collapse the music stand.”

    A kind of: fold · Kinds: deflate, concertina

  4. Fall apart.

    “The building crumbled after the explosion.” “Negotiations broke down.”

    Synonyms: crumble, crumple, tumble, break down · A kind of: change integrity

  5. Cause to burst.

    “The ice broke the pipe.”

    Synonyms: burst · Kinds: pop

  6. Suffer a nervous breakdown.

    Synonyms: crack up, crack, crock up, break up · A kind of: suffer

  7. Lose significance, effectiveness, or value.

    “The school system is collapsing.” “The stock market collapsed.”

    A kind of: weaken

Collapse as a noun

  1. An abrupt failure of function or complete physical exhaustion.

    “The commander's prostration demoralized his men.”

    Synonyms: prostration · A kind of: illness · Kinds: breakdown, shock, heatstroke, algidity

  2. A natural event caused by something suddenly falling down or caving in.

    “The roof is in danger of collapse.” “The collapse of the old star under its own gravity.”

    A kind of: happening · Kinds: cave in, debacle, implosion

  3. The act of throwing yourself down.

    “He landed on the bed with a great flop.”

    Synonyms: flop · A kind of: descent

  4. A sudden large decline of business or the prices of stocks (especially one that causes additional failures).

    Synonyms: crash · A kind of: happening

Phrases and compounds with "collapse"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gravitational collapse (noun) The implosion of a star resulting from its own gravity; the result is a smaller and denser celestial object.
