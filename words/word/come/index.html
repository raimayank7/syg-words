Come: meaning, synonyms and examples

Come is used as a verb (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move toward, travel toward something or somebody or approach something or somebody.

In Hindi
आना
Synonyms
come up, arrive, get, follow, issue forth, hail, fall, total, number, add up
Opposites
go
Word family
coming, comer

Come as a verb

  1. Move toward, travel toward something or somebody or approach something or somebody.

    “He came singing down the road.” “Come with me to the Casbah.” “Come down here!”

    Synonyms: come up · Opposite: go · A kind of: travel · Kinds: emanate, address, approach, approach

  2. Reach a destination; arrive by movement or progress.

    “She arrived home at 7 o'clock.” “She didn't get to Chicago until after midnight.”

    Synonyms: arrive, get · Kinds: land, drive in, land, land, roll up, come, reach, flood in

  3. Come to pass; arrive, as in due course.

    “The first success came three days later.” “It came as a shock.” “Dawn comes early in June.”

    A kind of: happen · Kinds: fall

  4. Reach or enter a state, relation, condition, use, or position.

    “The water came to a boil.” “We came to understand the true meaning of life.” “Their anger came to a boil.”

    A kind of: change

  5. To be the product or result.

    “Melons come from a vine.” “Understanding comes from experience.”

    Synonyms: follow · A kind of: originate

  6. Be found or available.

    “These shoes come in three colors; The furniture comes unassembled.”

    A kind of: exist

  7. Come forth.

    “A scream came from the woman's mouth.” “His breath came hard.”

    Synonyms: issue forth

  8. Be a native of.

    “She hails from Kalamazoo.”

    Synonyms: hail · A kind of: be

  9. Extend or reach.

    “The water came up to my waist.” “The sleeves come to your knuckles.”

    A kind of: run

  10. Exist or occur in a certain point in a series.

    “Next came the student from France.”

    A kind of: be

  11. Cover a certain distance.

    “She came a long way.”

    A kind of: travel

  12. Come under, be classified or included.

    “Fall into a category.” “This comes under a new heading.”

    Synonyms: fall · A kind of: be

  13. Happen as a result.

    “Nothing good will come of this.”

    A kind of: result

  14. Add up in number or quantity.

    “The bills amounted to $2,000.” “The bill came to $2,000.”

    Synonyms: total, number, add up, amount · A kind of: be · Kinds: work out, outnumber, average, make

Phrases and compounds with "come"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • come by (verb) Visit informally and spontaneously.
  • come in (verb) To come or go into.
  • come on (verb) Appear or become visible; make a showing.
  • come to (verb) Cause to experience suddenly.
  • come up (verb) Bring forth, usually something desirable.
  • come-on (noun) Anything that serves as an enticement.
  • come off (verb) Come to be detached.
  • come out (verb) Appear or become visible; make a showing.
  • come away (verb) Come to be detached.
  • come back (verb) Be restored.
  • come down (verb) Move downward and lower, but not necessarily all the way.
  • come home (verb) Become clear or enter one's consciousness or emotions.
  • come into (verb) Obtain, especially accidentally.
  • come near (verb) Almost do or experience something.
  • come over (verb) Communicate the intended meaning or impression.
  • come upon (verb) Find unexpectedly.
