Commercial: meaning, synonyms and examples

Commercial is used as an adjective (3 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Connected with or engaged in or sponsored by or used in commerce or commercial enterprises.

In Hindi

Commercial as an adjective

  1. Connected with or engaged in or sponsored by or used in commerce or commercial enterprises.

    “Commercial trucker.” “Commercial TV.” “Commercial diamonds.”

    Opposite: noncommercial · Similar: commercialized, mercantile, mercantile, technical

  2. Of or relating to commercialism.

    “A commercial attache.” “Commercial paper.” “Commercial law.”

  3. Of the kind or quality used in commerce; average or inferior.

    “Commercial grade of beef.” “Commercial oxalic acid.”

    Synonyms: commercial-grade · Similar: inferior

Commercial as a noun

  1. A commercially sponsored ad on radio or television.

    Synonyms: commercial message · A kind of: ad · Kinds: infomercial

Phrases and compounds with "commercial"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • commercial art (noun) Art used for commercial purposes (as in advertising).
  • commercial law (noun) The body of rules applied to commercial transactions; derived from the practices of traders rather than from jurisprudence.
  • commercial bank (noun) A financial institution that accepts demand deposits and makes loans and provides other services for the public.
  • commercial loan (noun) A bank loan granted for the use of a business.
  • commercial paper (noun) An unsecured and unregistered short-term obligation issued by an institutional borrower to investors who have temporarily idle cash.
  • commercial-grade (adjective) Of the kind or quality used in commerce; average or inferior.
  • commercial agency (noun) An organization that provides businesses with credit ratings of other firms.
  • commercial artist (noun) An illustrator who is supported by advertising.
  • commercial credit (noun) Credit granted by a bank to a business concern for commercial purposes.
  • commercial treaty (noun) A treaty governing commerce between two or more nations.
  • commercial bribery (noun) Bribery of a purchasing agent in order to induce the agent to enter into a transaction.
  • commercial message (noun) A commercially sponsored ad on radio or television.
  • commercial activity (noun) Activity undertaken as part of a commercial enterprise.
  • commercial document (noun) A document of or relating to commerce.
  • commercial traveler (noun) A salesman who travels to call on customers.
  • commercial traveller (noun) A salesman who travels to call on customers.
