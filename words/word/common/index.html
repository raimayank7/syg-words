Common: meaning, synonyms and examples

Common is used as an adjective (9 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Belonging to or participated in by a community as a whole; public.

In Hindi
आम, साझा
Synonyms
mutual, usual, vernacular, plebeian, unwashed, coarse, rough-cut, uncouth, park, commons
Opposites
individual, uncommon
Word family
commonness, commonality

Common as an adjective

  1. Belonging to or participated in by a community as a whole; public.

    “For the common good.” “Common lands are set aside for use by all members of a community.”

    Opposite: individual · Similar: communal, public

  2. Having no special distinction or quality; widely known or commonly encountered; average or ordinary or usual.

    “The common man.” “A common sailor.” “The common cold.”

    Opposite: uncommon · Similar: average, democratic, demotic, frequent, general, grassroots

  3. Common to or shared by two or more parties.

    “A common friend.” “The mutual interests of management and labor.”

    Synonyms: mutual · Similar: shared

  4. Commonly encountered.

    “A common (or familiar) complaint.” “The usual greeting.”

    Synonyms: usual · Similar: familiar

  5. Being or characteristic of or appropriate to everyday language.

    “Common parlance.” “A vernacular term.” “Vernacular speakers.”

    Synonyms: vernacular · Similar: informal

  6. Of or associated with the great masses of people.

    “The common people in those days suffered greatly.” “Behavior that branded him as common.” “His square plebeian nose.”

    Synonyms: plebeian, unwashed · Similar: lowborn

  7. Of low or inferior quality or value.

    “Of what coarse metal ye are molded.” “Produced...the common cloths used by the poorer population.”

    Synonyms: coarse · Similar: inferior

  8. Lacking refinement or cultivation or taste.

    “He had coarse manners but a first-rate mind.” “Behavior that branded him as common.” “An untutored and uncouth human being.”

    Synonyms: coarse, rough-cut, uncouth · Similar: unrefined

  9. To be expected; standard.

    “Common decency.”

    Similar: ordinary

Common as a noun

  1. A piece of open land for recreational use in an urban area.

    “They went for a walk in the park.”

    Synonyms: park, commons, green · A kind of: tract · Kinds: amusement park, village green
