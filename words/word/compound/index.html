Compound: meaning, synonyms and examples

Compound is used as a verb (5 senses), noun (3 senses) and adjective (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make more intense, stronger, or more marked.

In Hindi
यौगिक; अहाता
Synonyms
intensify, heighten, deepen, combine, chemical compound, colonial
Opposites
simple
Word family
compounding

Compound as a verb

  1. Make more intense, stronger, or more marked.

    “The efforts were intensified.” “Her rudeness intensified his dislike for her.” “Pot smokers claim it heightens their awareness.”

    Synonyms: intensify, heighten, deepen · A kind of: increase · Kinds: heat up, fan, enhance, amplify, sharpen, sharpen

  2. Put or add together.

    “Combine resources.”

    Synonyms: combine · A kind of: add · Kinds: totalize, recombine, mix, synthesize

  3. Calculate principal and interest.

    A kind of: account

  4. Create by mixing or combining.

    A kind of: assemble

  5. Combine so as to form a whole; mix.

    “Compound the ingredients.”

    Synonyms: combine · A kind of: mix · Kinds: integrate, heterodyne, sulfurette, carburet

Compound as a noun

  1. A whole formed by a union of two or more elements or parts.

    A kind of: whole

  2. (chemistry) a substance formed by chemical union of two or more elements or ingredients in definite proportion by weight.

    Synonyms: chemical compound · A kind of: chemical · Kinds: vanillin, acceptor, adduct, antiknock, acid, arsenide, hydrogen cyanide, anionic compound

  3. An enclosure of residences and other building (especially in the Orient).

    A kind of: enclosure

Compound as an adjective

  1. Composed of more than one part.

    “Compound leaves are composed of several lobes;.”

    Opposite: simple · Similar: bilobate, binate, bipartite, bipinnate, bipinnatifid, cleft

  2. Consisting of two or more substances or ingredients or elements or parts.

    “Soap is a compound substance.” “Housetop is a compound word.” “A blackberry is a compound fruit.”

    Similar: complex

  3. Composed of many distinct individuals united to form a whole or colony.

    “Coral is a colonial organism.”

    Synonyms: colonial · Similar: complex

Phrases and compounds with "compound"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • compound eye (noun) In insects and some crustaceans: composed of many light-sensitive elements each forming a portion of an image.
  • compound leaf (noun) A leaf composed of a number of leaflets on a common stalk.
  • compound lens (noun) A lens system consisting of two or more lenses on the same axis.
  • compound lever (noun) A pair of levers hinged at the fulcrum.
  • binary compound (noun) Chemical compound composed of only two elements.
  • compound number (noun) A quantity expressed in two different units.
  • compound pistil (noun) Consists of two or more fused carpels.
  • anionic compound (noun) A compound characterized by an active anion.
  • chelate compound (noun) A heterocyclic compound having a metal ion attached by coordinate bonds to at least two nonmetal ions.
  • compound protein (noun) A protein complex combining amino acids with other substances.
  • organic compound (noun) Any compound of carbon and another element or a radical.
  • aromatic compound (noun) A hydrocarbon containing one or more benzene rings that are characteristic of the benzene series of compounds.
  • chemical compound (noun) (chemistry) a substance formed by chemical union of two or more elements or ingredients in definite proportion by weight.
  • compound fraction (noun) A fraction with fractions in the numerator or denominator.
  • compound fracture (noun) Bone fracture associated with lacerated soft tissue or an open wound.
  • compound interest (noun) Interest calculated on both the principal and the accrued interest.
