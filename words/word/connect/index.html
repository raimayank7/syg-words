Connect: meaning, synonyms and examples

Connect is used as a verb (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: Connect, fasten, or put together two or more pieces.

In Hindi
जोड़ना
Synonyms
link, tie, link up, associate, tie in, relate, colligate, join, unite, get in touch
Opposites
disconnect
Word family
connective, connector, connecter, connection, connexion

Connect as a verb

  1. Connect, fasten, or put together two or more pieces.

    “Can you connect the two loudspeakers?” “Tie the ropes together.” “Link arms.”

    Synonyms: link, tie, link up · Opposite: disconnect · Kinds: join, ground, join, attach, daisy-chain, tie, interconnect, tee

  2. Make a logical or causal connection.

    “I cannot connect these two pieces of evidence in my mind.” “Colligate these facts.” “I cannot relate these events at all.”

    Synonyms: associate, tie in, relate, link, colligate, link up · A kind of: think · Kinds: remember, interrelate, correlate, identify, free-associate, think of

  3. Be or become joined or united or linked.

    “The two streets connect to become a highway.” “Our paths joined.” “The travelers linked up again at the airport.”

    Synonyms: link, link up, join, unite · Kinds: syndicate, articulate, complect

  4. Join by means of communication equipment.

    “The telephone company finally put in lines to connect the towns in this area.”

    A kind of: join

  5. Land on or hit solidly.

    “The brick connected on her head, knocking her out.”

    A kind of: hit

  6. Join for the purpose of communication.

    “Operator, could you connect me to the Raffles in Singapore?”

    A kind of: join

  7. Be scheduled so as to provide continuing service, as in transportation.

    “The local train does not connect with the Amtrak train.” “The planes don't connect and you will have to wait for four hours.”

    A kind of: be

  8. Establish a rapport or relationship.

    “The President of this university really connects with the faculty.”

    A kind of: relate

  9. Establish communication with someone.

    “Did you finally connect with your long-lost cousin?”

    Synonyms: get in touch, touch base · A kind of: interact

  10. Plug into an outlet.

    “Please plug in the toaster!” “Connect the TV so we can watch the football game tonight.”

    Synonyms: plug in, plug into · A kind of: insert

  11. Hit or play a ball successfully.

    “The batter connected for a home run.”

    A kind of: hit

Word family
