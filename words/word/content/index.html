Content: meaning, synonyms and examples

Content is used as a noun (7 senses), verb (2 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Everything that is included in a collection and that is held or included in something.

In Hindi
सामग्री; संतुष्ट
Synonyms
message, subject matter, substance, capacity, cognitive content, mental object, contentedness, subject, depicted object, contented
Opposites
discontent
Word family
contain, contentment

Content as a noun

  1. Everything that is included in a collection and that is held or included in something.

    “He emptied the contents of his pockets.” “The two groups were similar in content.”

    A kind of: collection

  2. What a communication that is about something is about.

    Synonyms: message, subject matter, substance · A kind of: communication · Kinds: body, corker, reminder, request, memorial, latent content, subject, digression

  3. The proportion of a substance that is contained in a mixture or alloy etc.

    A kind of: proportion

  4. The amount that can be contained.

    “The gas tank has a capacity of 12 gallons.”

    Synonyms: capacity · A kind of: volume · Kinds: vital capacity

  5. The sum or range of what has been perceived, discovered, or learned.

    Synonyms: cognitive content, mental object · A kind of: cognition · Kinds: tradition, object, food, noumenon, universe, topic, issue, idea

  6. The state of being contented with your situation in life.

    “He relaxed in sleepy contentedness.” “They could read to their heart's content.”

    Synonyms: contentedness · A kind of: acceptance

  7. Something (a person or object or scene) selected by an artist or photographer for graphic representation.

    “A moving picture of a train is more dramatic than a still picture of the same subject.”

    Synonyms: subject, depicted object · A kind of: thing

Content as a verb

  1. Satisfy in a limited way.

    A kind of: limit

  2. Make content.

    “I am contented.”

    Opposite: discontent · A kind of: satisfy

Content as an adjective

  1. Satisfied or showing satisfaction with things as they are.

    “A contented smile.”

    Synonyms: contented · Similar: complacent, satisfied, smug

Phrases and compounds with "content"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • content word (noun) A word to which an independent meaning can be assigned.
  • heat content (noun) (thermodynamics) a thermodynamic quantity equal to the internal energy of a system plus the product of its volume and pressure.
  • latent content (noun) (psychoanalysis) hidden meaning of a fantasy or dream.
  • cognitive content (noun) The sum or range of what has been perceived, discovered, or learned.
  • cubic content unit (noun) A unit of measurement of volume or capacity.
