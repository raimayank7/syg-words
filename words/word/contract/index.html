Contract: meaning, synonyms and examples

Contract is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A binding agreement between two or more persons that is enforceable by law.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites

Word family

Contract as a noun

  1. A binding agreement between two or more persons that is enforceable by law.

    A kind of: written agreement · Kinds: adhesion contract, aleatory contract, bilateral contract, charter, conditional contract, cost-plus contract, lease, marriage contract

  2. (contract bridge) the highest bid becomes the contract setting the number of tricks that the bidder must make.

    Synonyms: declaration · A kind of: bid

  3. A variety of bridge in which the bidder receives points toward game only for the number of tricks he bid.

    Synonyms: contract bridge · A kind of: bridge · Kinds: no-trump

Contract as a verb

  1. Enter into a contractual arrangement.

    Synonyms: undertake · A kind of: promise · Kinds: stipulate, sign, stipulate, rent, rent

  2. Engage by written agreement.

    “They signed two new pitchers for the next season.”

    Synonyms: sign, sign on, sign up · A kind of: hire · Kinds: contract out

  3. Squeeze or press together.

    “She compressed her lips.” “The spasm contracted the muscle.”

    Synonyms: compress, constrict, squeeze, compact, press · A kind of: tighten · Kinds: choke, prim, astringe, strangulate, convulse, convulse, bear down, choke

  4. Be stricken by an illness, fall victim to an illness.

    “He got AIDS.” “She came down with pneumonia.” “She took a chill.”

    Synonyms: take, get · A kind of: sicken · Kinds: catch

  5. Become smaller or draw together.

    “The fabric shrank.” “The balloon shrank.”

    Synonyms: shrink · Opposite: expand · A kind of: decrease · Kinds: flex

  6. Make smaller.

    “The heat contracted the woollen garment.”

    A kind of: shrink · Kinds: purse

  7. Compress or concentrate.

    “Congress condensed the three-year plan into a six-month plan.”

    Synonyms: condense, concentrate · A kind of: change

  8. Make or become more narrow or restricted.

    “The selection was narrowed.” “The road narrowed.”

    Synonyms: narrow · A kind of: change · Kinds: bottleneck, taper off

  9. Reduce in scope while retaining essential elements.

    “The manuscript must be shortened.”

    Synonyms: abridge, foreshorten, abbreviate, shorten, cut, reduce · Opposite: expand · A kind of: decrease · Kinds: bowdlerize, digest

Phrases and compounds with "contract"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • contract in (verb) Consent in writing to pay money to a trade union for political use.
  • contract law (noun) That branch of jurisprudence that studies the rights and obligations of parties entering into contracts.
  • contract out (verb) Assign a job to someone outside one's own business.
  • oral contract (noun) An agreement that is not in writing and is not signed by the parties but is a real existing contract that lacks only the formal requirement of a memorandum to render it enforceable in litigation.
  • contract offer (noun) Offer by an employer to contract to pay an employee at a given rate.
  • labor contract (noun) Contract between labor and management governing wages and benefits and working conditions.
  • quasi contract (noun) A contract created by law for reasons of justice without any expression of assent.
  • contract bridge (noun) A variety of bridge in which the bidder receives points toward game only for the number of tricks he bid.
  • output contract (noun) A contract in which you promise to deliver your entire output to the other party who promises to accept it.
  • social contract (noun) An implicit agreement among people that results in the organization of society; individual surrenders liberty in return for protection.
  • futures contract (noun) An agreement to buy or sell a specific amount of a commodity or financial instrument at a particular price on a stipulated future date; the contract can be sold before the settlement date.
  • service contract (noun) A contract for maintenance services.
  • special contract (noun) A contract that is signed and has the (wax) seal of the signer attached.
  • adhesion contract (noun) A contract that heavily restricts one party while leaving the other free (as some standard form printed contracts); implies inequality in bargaining power.
  • aleatory contract (noun) A contract whose performance by one party depends on the occurrence of an uncertain contingent event (but if it is contingent on the outcome of a wager it is not enforceable).
  • marriage contract (noun) A prenuptial agreement or contract.
