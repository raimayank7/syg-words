Control: meaning, synonyms and examples

Control is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Power to direct or determine.

In Hindi
नियंत्रण
Synonyms
control condition, dominance, ascendance, ascendence, ascendancy, ascendency, restraint, command, mastery, controller
Word family
controller

Control as a noun

  1. Power to direct or determine.

    “Under control.”

    A kind of: power · Kinds: authority, corporatism, hold, iron fist, rein

  2. A relation of constraint of one entity (thing or person or group) by another.

    “Measures for the control of disease.” “They instituted controls over drinking on campus.”

    A kind of: relation

  3. (physiology) regulation or maintenance of a function or action or reflex etc.

    “The timing and control of his movements were unimpaired.” “He had lost control of his sphincters.”

    A kind of: bodily process · Kinds: motor control

  4. A standard against which other conditions can be compared in a scientific experiment.

    “The control condition was inappropriate for the conclusions he wished to draw.”

    Synonyms: control condition · A kind of: criterion

  5. The activity of managing or exerting control over something.

    “The control of the mob by the police was admirable.”

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: repression, crowd control, damage control, federalization, flight control, flood control, imperialism, regulation

  6. The state that exists when one person or group has power over another.

    “Her apparent dominance of her husband was really her attempt to make him pay attention to her.”

    Synonyms: dominance, ascendance, ascendence, ascendancy, ascendency · A kind of: condition · Kinds: ascendant, domination, predominance, dominion, regulation, absolutism, monopoly

  7. Discipline in personal and social activities.

    “He was a model of polite restraint.” “She never lost control of herself.”

    Synonyms: restraint · A kind of: discipline · Kinds: self-restraint, temperance, inhibition, continence

  8. Great skillfulness and knowledge of some subject or activity.

    “A good command of French.”

    Synonyms: command, mastery · A kind of: skillfulness

  9. A mechanism that controls the operation of a machine.

    “The speed controller on his turntable was not working properly.” “I turned the controls over to her.”

    Synonyms: controller · A kind of: mechanism · Kinds: cruise control, dial, disk controller, governor, handwheel, joystick, regulator, switch

  10. A spiritual agency that is assumed to assist the medium during a seance.

    A kind of: spirit

  11. The economic policy of controlling or limiting or curbing prices or wages etc.

    “They wanted to repeal all the legislation that imposed economic controls.”

    A kind of: economic policy · Kinds: price control, ceiling, floor

Control as a verb

  1. Exercise authoritative control or power over.

    “Control the budget.” “Command the military forces.”

    Synonyms: command · Kinds: preoccupy, steer, steer, hold one's own, manage, internationalize, hold, hold sway

  2. Lessen the intensity of; temper; hold in restraint; hold or keep within limits.

    “Hold your tongue.” “Hold your temper.” “Control your anger.”

    Synonyms: hold in, hold, contain, check, curb, moderate · A kind of: restrain · Kinds: restrict, suppress, damp, mortify, deny, restrict, train, catch

  3. Handle and cause to function.

    “Control the lever.”

    Synonyms: operate · A kind of: manipulate · Kinds: synchronize, dial, turn, submarine, treadle, relay, gate, pedal

  4. Control (others or oneself) or influence skillfully, usually to one's advantage.

    “She manipulates her boss.” “She is a very controlling mother and doesn't let her children grow up.” “The teacher knew how to keep the class in line.”

    Synonyms: manipulate, keep in line · A kind of: interact · Kinds: tease, handle, ingratiate

  5. Check or regulate (a scientific experiment) by conducting a parallel experiment or comparing with another standard.

    “Are you controlling for the temperature?”

    Synonyms: verify · A kind of: test

  6. Verify by using a duplicate register for comparison.

    “Control an account.”

    A kind of: see

  7. Be careful or certain to do something; make certain of something.

    “He verified that the valves were closed.” “See that the curtains are closed.” “Control the quality of the product.”

    Synonyms: see, check, insure, see to it, ensure, ascertain, assure · A kind of: verify · Kinds: proofread, control, double-check, cross-check, cinch, spot-check, cover

  8. Have a firm understanding or knowledge of; be on top of.

    “Do you control these data?”

    Synonyms: master · A kind of: know · Kinds: cinch

Phrases and compounds with "control"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • control key (noun) (computer science) the key on a computer keyboard that is used (in combination with some other key) to type control characters.
  • control rod (noun) A steel or aluminum rod that can be moved up or down to control the rate of the nuclear reaction.
  • gun control (noun) Efforts to regulate or control sales of guns.
  • job control (noun) A program that is called to prepare each job to be run.
  • arms control (noun) A limitation on the size and armament of the armed forces of a country.
  • control grid (noun) An electrode placed between the cathode and anode of a vacuum tube to control the flow of electrons through the tube.
  • control room (noun) A room housing control equipment (as in a recording studio).
  • fire control (noun) Preparation for the delivery of shellfire on a target.
  • riot control (noun) The measures taken to control a riot.
  • self-control (noun) The act of denying yourself; controlling your impulses.
  • take control (verb) Assume control.
  • birth control (noun) Limiting the number of children born.
  • control board (noun) Electrical device consisting of a flat insulated surface that contains switches and dials and meters for controlling other electrical devices.
  • control freak (noun) Someone with a compulsive desire to exert control over situations and people.
  • control panel (noun) Electrical device consisting of a flat insulated surface that contains switches and dials and meters for controlling other electrical devices.
  • control stick (noun) A lever used by a pilot to control the ailerons and elevators of an airplane.
