Core: meaning, synonyms and examples

Core is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A small group of indispensable persons or things.

In Hindi
केंद्र, मूल भाग; गूदा
Synonyms
nucleus, core group, kernel, substance, center, centre, essence, gist, heart, heart and soul

Core as a noun

  1. A small group of indispensable persons or things.

    “Five periodicals make up the core of their publishing program.”

    Synonyms: nucleus, core group · A kind of: set · Kinds: cadre

  2. The center of an object.

    “The ball has a titanium core.”

    A kind of: center · Kinds: corncob

  3. The central part of the Earth.

    A kind of: center

  4. The choicest or most essential or most vital part of some idea or experience.

    “The gist of the prosecutor's argument.” “The heart and soul of the Republican Party.” “The nub of the story.”

    Synonyms: kernel, substance, center, centre, essence, gist, heart, heart and soul · A kind of: content · Kinds: bare bones, hypostasis, quiddity, quintessence, stuff

  5. A cylindrical sample of soil or rock obtained with a hollow drill.

    A kind of: sample

  6. An organization founded by James Leonard Farmer in 1942 to work for racial equality.

    A kind of: nongovernmental organization

  7. The central meaning or theme of a speech or literary work.

    Synonyms: effect, essence, burden, gist · A kind of: meaning

  8. (computer science) a tiny ferrite toroid formerly used in a random access memory to store one bit of data; now superseded by semiconductor memories.

    “Each core has three wires passing through it, providing the means to select and detect the contents of each bit.”

    Synonyms: magnetic core · A kind of: torus

  9. The chamber of a nuclear reactor containing the fissile material where the reaction takes place.

    A kind of: chamber

  10. A bar of magnetic material (as soft iron) that passes through a coil and serves to increase the inductance of the coil.

    A kind of: bar

Core as a verb

  1. Remove the core or center from.

    “Core an apple.”

    A kind of: hollow

Phrases and compounds with "core"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • core bit (noun) A hollow drilling bit that is the cutting part of a core drill; allows core samples to be taken.
  • core out (verb) Remove the interior of.
  • core dump (noun) (computer science) dump of the contents of the chief registers in the CPU; usually used for debugging.
  • hard core (noun) The most dedicated and intensely loyal nucleus of a group or movement.
  • hard-core (adjective) Stubbornly resistant to change or improvement.
  • core drill (noun) A drill that removes a cylindrical core from the drill hole.
  • core group (noun) A small group of indispensable persons or things.
  • core memory (noun) (computer science) a computer memory consisting of an array of magnetic cores; now superseded by semiconductor memories.
  • magnetic core (noun) (computer science) a tiny ferrite toroid formerly used in a random access memory to store one bit of data; now superseded by semiconductor memories.
  • magnetic core memory (noun) (computer science) a computer memory consisting of an array of magnetic cores; now superseded by semiconductor memories.
