Crash: meaning, synonyms and examples

Crash is used as a verb (13 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Fall or come down violently.

In Hindi
टक्कर; धड़ाम से गिरना
Synonyms
ram, break up, break apart, barge in, gate-crash, dash, go down, doss, doss down, clang
Word family
crasher

Crash as a verb

  1. Fall or come down violently.

    “The branch crashed down on my car.” “The plane crashed in the sea.”

    A kind of: descend

  2. Move with, or as if with, a crashing noise.

    “The car crashed through the glass door.”

    A kind of: move

  3. Undergo damage or destruction on impact.

    “The plane crashed into the ocean.” “The car crashed into the lamp post.”

    Synonyms: ram · A kind of: collide

  4. Move violently as through a barrier.

    A kind of: pass

  5. Break violently or noisily; smash.

    Synonyms: break up, break apart · A kind of: disintegrate

  6. Occupy, usually uninvited.

    “My son's friends crashed our house last weekend.”

    A kind of: occupy

  7. Make a sudden loud sound.

    “The waves crashed on the shore and kept us awake all night.”

    A kind of: sound

  8. Enter uninvited; informal.

    “Let's crash the party!”

    Synonyms: barge in, gate-crash · A kind of: intrude

  9. Cause to crash.

    “Mother crashed the motorbike into the lamppost.”

    A kind of: collide · Kinds: wrap, prang, ditch

  10. Hurl or thrust violently.

    “He dashed the plate against the wall.” “Waves were dashing against the rock.”

    Synonyms: dash · A kind of: hurl

  11. Undergo a sudden and severe downturn.

    “The economy crashed.” “Will the stock market crash again?”

    A kind of: change

  12. Stop operating.

    “My computer crashed last night.” “The system goes down at least once a week.”

    Synonyms: go down · A kind of: fail

  13. Sleep in a convenient place.

    “You can crash here, though it's not very comfortable.”

    Synonyms: doss, doss down · A kind of: bed down

Crash as a noun

  1. A loud resonant repeating noise.

    “He could hear the clang of distant bells.”

    Synonyms: clang, clangor, clangour, clangoring, clank, clash · A kind of: noise

  2. A serious accident (usually involving one or more vehicles).

    “They are still investigating the crash of the TWA plane.”

    Synonyms: wreck · A kind of: accident · Kinds: prang

  3. A sudden large decline of business or the prices of stocks (especially one that causes additional failures).

    Synonyms: collapse · A kind of: happening

  4. The act of colliding with something.

    “His crash through the window.” “The fullback's smash into the defensive line.”

    Synonyms: smash · A kind of: hit · Kinds: impingement

  5. (computer science) an event that causes a computer system to become inoperative.

    “The crash occurred during a thunderstorm and the system has been down ever since.”

    A kind of: mishap · Kinds: head crash

Phrases and compounds with "crash"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • crash dive (noun) A rapid descent by a submarine.
  • crash land (verb) Make an emergency landing.
  • crash-dive (verb) Descend steeply and rapidly.
  • gate-crash (verb) Enter uninvited; informal.
  • head crash (noun) (computer science) a crash of a read/write head in a hard disk drive (usually caused by contact of the head with the surface of the magnetic disk).
  • crash course (noun) A rapid and intense course of training or research (usually undertaken in an emergency).
  • crash helmet (noun) A padded helmet worn by people riding bicycles or motorcycles; protects the head in case of accidents.
  • crash barrier (noun) A strong protective barrier that is erected around a racetrack or in the middle of a dual-lane highway in order to reduce the likelihood of severe accidents.
  • crash landing (noun) An emergency landing under circumstances where a normal landing is impossible (usually damaging the aircraft).
  • crash program (noun) A rapid and intense course of training or research (usually undertaken in an emergency).
  • crash programme (noun) A rapid and intense course of training or research (usually undertaken in an emergency).
