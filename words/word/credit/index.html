Credit: meaning, synonyms and examples

Credit is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Approval.

In Hindi
श्रेय; उधार
Synonyms
recognition, credit entry, deferred payment, course credit, citation, cite, acknowledgment, reference, mention, quotation
Opposites
debit, cash
Word family
creditor

Credit as a noun

  1. Approval.

    “Give her recognition for trying.” “He was given credit for his work.” “Give her credit for trying.”

    Synonyms: recognition · A kind of: approval · Kinds: memorial, ovation, salute

  2. Money available for a client to borrow.

    A kind of: assets · Kinds: cheap money, export credit, import credit, credit line, commercial credit, letter of credit

  3. An accounting entry acknowledging income or capital items.

    Synonyms: credit entry · Opposite: debit · A kind of: entry

  4. Used in the phrase `to your credit' in order to indicate an achievement deserving praise.

    “She already had several performances to her credit.”

    A kind of: accomplishment

  5. Arrangement for deferred payment for goods and services.

    Synonyms: deferred payment · Opposite: cash · A kind of: payment

  6. Recognition by a college or university that a course of studies has been successfully completed; typically measured in semester hours.

    Synonyms: course credit · A kind of: attainment · Kinds: semester hour

  7. A short note recognizing a source of information or of a quoted passage.

    “The student's essay failed to list several important citations.” “The acknowledgments are usually printed at the front of a book.” “The article includes mention of similar clinical cases.”

    Synonyms: citation, cite, acknowledgment, reference, mention, quotation · A kind of: note · Kinds: photo credit, cross-reference

  8. An entry on a list of persons who contributed to a film or written work.

    “The credits were given at the end of the film.”

    A kind of: title

  9. An estimate, based on previous dealings, of a person's or an organization's ability to fulfill their financial commitments.

    Synonyms: credit rating · A kind of: estimate

Credit as a verb

  1. Give someone credit for something.

    “We credited her for saving our jobs.”

    A kind of: impute

  2. Ascribe an achievement to.

    “She was not properly credited in the program.”

    Synonyms: accredit · A kind of: impute

  3. Accounting: enter as credit.

    “We credit your account with $100.”

    Opposite: debit · A kind of: account · Kinds: finance

  4. Have trust in; trust in the truth or veracity of.

    A kind of: trust · Kinds: believe

Phrases and compounds with "credit"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • tax credit (noun) A direct reduction in tax liability (not dependent on the taxpayer's tax bracket).
  • credit card (noun) A card (usually plastic) that assures a seller that the person using it has a satisfactory credit rating and that the issuer will see to it that the seller receives payment for the merchandise delivered.
  • credit hour (noun) A unit of academic credit; one hour a week for an academic semester.
  • credit line (noun) The maximum credit that a customer is allowed.
  • credit side (noun) Account of payments received; usually the right side of a financial statement.
  • credit entry (noun) An accounting entry acknowledging income or capital items.
  • credit order (noun) An order that is received without payment; requires billing at a later date.
  • credit union (noun) A cooperative depository financial institution whose members can obtain loans from their combined savings.
  • photo credit (noun) A note acknowledging the source of a published photograph.
  • course credit (noun) Recognition by a college or university that a course of studies has been successfully completed; typically measured in semester hours.
  • credit bureau (noun) A private firm that maintains consumer credit data files and provides credit information to authorized users for a fee.
  • credit crunch (noun) A state in which there is a short supply of cash to lend to businesses and consumers and interest rates are high.
  • credit rating (noun) An estimate, based on previous dealings, of a person's or an organization's ability to fulfill their financial commitments.
  • credit system (noun) A system for allowing people to purchase things on credit.
  • export credit (noun) A credit opened by an importer with a bank in an exporter's country to finance an export operation.
  • import credit (noun) Credit opened by an importer at a bank in his own country upon which an exporter may draw.
