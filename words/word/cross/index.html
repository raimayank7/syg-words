Cross: meaning, synonyms and examples

Cross is used as a verb (8 senses), adjective (2 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Travel across or pass over.

In Hindi
पार करना; क्रॉस
Synonyms
traverse, track, cover, pass over, get over, get across, cut through, cut across, intersect, thwart
Opposites
uncross
Word family
crossing, crossness

Cross as a verb

  1. Travel across or pass over.

    “The caravan covered almost 100 miles each day.”

    Synonyms: traverse, track, cover, pass over, get over, get across, cut through, cut across · A kind of: pass · Kinds: tramp, stride, walk, crisscross, ford, bridge, jaywalk, drive

  2. Meet at a point.

    Synonyms: intersect · A kind of: meet · Kinds: cross

  3. Hinder or prevent (the efforts, plans, or desires) of.

    “What ultimately frustrated every challenger was Ruth's amazing September surge.” “Foil your opponent.”

    Synonyms: thwart, queer, spoil, scotch, foil, frustrate, baffle, bilk · A kind of: prevent · Kinds: disappoint, dash, short-circuit, ruin

  4. Fold so as to resemble a cross.

    “She crossed her legs.”

    Opposite: uncross · A kind of: fold

  5. To cover or extend over an area or time period.

    “Rivers traverse the valley floor.” “The parking lot spans 3 acres.” “The novel spans three centuries.”

    Synonyms: traverse, span, sweep · A kind of: cover

  6. Meet and pass.

    “The trains crossed.”

    A kind of: intersect · Kinds: decussate

  7. Trace a line through or across.

    “Cross your `t'.”

    A kind of: write

  8. Breed animals or plants using parents of different races and varieties.

    “Cross a horse and a donkey.” “Mendel tried crossbreeding.” “These species do not interbreed.”

    Synonyms: crossbreed, hybridize, hybridise, interbreed · A kind of: breed · Kinds: backcross

Cross as an adjective

  1. Extending or lying across; in a crosswise direction; at right angles to the long axis.

    “Cross members should be all steel.” “From the transverse hall the stairway ascends gracefully.” “Transversal vibrations.”

    Synonyms: transverse, transversal, thwartwise · Similar: crosswise

  2. Annoyed and irritable.

    Synonyms: crabbed, crabby, fussy, grouchy, grumpy, bad-tempered, ill-tempered · Similar: ill-natured

Cross as a noun

  1. A wooden structure consisting of an upright post with a transverse piece.

    A kind of: structure

  2. A marking that consists of lines that cross each other.

    Synonyms: crisscross, mark · A kind of: marking

  3. Any affliction that causes great suffering.

    “That is his cross to bear.” “He bears his afflictions like a crown of thorns.”

    Synonyms: crown of thorns · A kind of: affliction

  4. (genetics) an organism that is the offspring of genetically dissimilar parents or stock; especially offspring produced by breeding plants or animals of different varieties or breeds or species.

    “A mule is a cross between a horse and a donkey.”

    Synonyms: hybrid, crossbreed · A kind of: organism · Kinds: dihybrid, monohybrid

  5. (genetics) the act of mixing different species or varieties of animals or plants and thus to produce hybrids.

    Synonyms: hybridization, hybridisation, crossbreeding, crossing, interbreeding, hybridizing · A kind of: coupling · Kinds: dihybrid cross, monohybrid cross, testcross
