Cry: meaning, synonyms and examples

Cry is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Utter a sudden loud cry.

In Hindi
रोना; चिल्लाना
Synonyms
shout, shout out, call, yell, scream, holler, hollo, squall, weep, exclaim
Opposites
laugh
Word family
crier, crying

Cry as a verb

  1. Utter a sudden loud cry.

    “She cried with pain when the doctor inserted the needle.” “I yelled to her from the window but she couldn't hear me.”

    Synonyms: shout, shout out, call, yell, scream, holler, hollo, squall · A kind of: utter · Kinds: hollo, hurrah, halloo, whoop, shriek, howl, squawk

  2. Shed tears because of sadness, rage, or pain.

    “She cried bitterly when she heard the news of his death.” “The girl in the wheelchair wept with frustration when she could not get up the stairs.”

    Synonyms: weep · Opposite: laugh · A kind of: express emotion · Kinds: wail, bawl, tear, sob, snivel

  3. Utter aloud; often with surprise, horror, or joy.

    “`I won!' he exclaimed.” “`Help!' she cried.” “`I'm here,' the mother shouted when she saw her child looking lost.”

    Synonyms: exclaim, cry out, outcry, call out, shout · A kind of: express · Kinds: gee, ooh

  4. Proclaim or announce in public.

    “Before we had newspapers, a town crier would cry the news.” “He cried his merchandise in the market square.”

    Synonyms: blazon out · A kind of: announce

  5. Demand immediate action.

    “This situation is crying for attention.”

    A kind of: want

  6. Utter a characteristic sound.

    “The cat was crying.”

    A kind of: utter

  7. Bring into a particular state by crying.

    “The little boy cried himself to sleep.”

    A kind of: change

Cry as a noun

  1. A loud utterance; often in protest or opposition.

    “The speaker was interrupted by loud cries from the rear of the audience.”

    Synonyms: outcry, call, yell, shout, vociferation · A kind of: utterance · Kinds: bellow, catcall, clamor, halloo, hoot, hosanna, noise, scream

  2. A loud utterance of emotion (especially when inarticulate).

    “A cry of rage.” “A yell of pain.”

    Synonyms: yell · A kind of: utterance · Kinds: complaint

  3. A slogan used to rally support for a cause.

    “A cry to arms.” “Our watchword will be `democracy'.”

    Synonyms: war cry, rallying cry, battle cry, watchword · A kind of: motto

  4. A fit of weeping.

    “Had a good cry.”

    A kind of: effusion

  5. The characteristic utterance of an animal.

    “Animal cries filled the night.”

    A kind of: sound · Kinds: baa, bark, bay, bleat, bray, cackle, caterwaul, caw

Phrases and compounds with "cry"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • cry for (verb) Need badly or desperately.
  • cry out (verb) Utter aloud; often with surprise, horror, or joy.
  • far cry (noun) Distance estimated in terms of the audibility of a cry.
  • war cry (noun) A slogan used to rally support for a cause.
  • battle cry (noun) A yell intended to rally a group of soldiers in battle.
  • cry out for (verb) Need badly or desperately.
  • hue and cry (noun) Loud and persistent outcry from many people.
  • rallying cry (noun) A slogan used to rally support for a cause.
  • cry-baby tree (noun) Small South American spiny tree with dark crimson and scarlet flowers solitary or clustered.
