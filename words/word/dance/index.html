Dance: meaning, synonyms and examples

Dance is used as a verb (3 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move in a graceful and rhythmical way.

In Hindi
नाचना; नृत्य
Synonyms
trip the light fantastic, trip the light fantastic toe, dancing, terpsichore, saltation
Word family
dancer, dancing

Dance as a verb

  1. Move in a graceful and rhythmical way.

    “The young girl danced into the room.”

    A kind of: move · Kinds: glissade, chasse, capriole

  2. Move in a pattern; usually to musical accompaniment; do or perform a dance.

    “My husband and I like to dance at home to the radio.”

    Synonyms: trip the light fantastic, trip the light fantastic toe · A kind of: move · Kinds: jive, hoof, clog, tap dance, belly dance, heel, shimmy, bop

  3. Skip, leap, or move up and down or sideways.

    “Dancing flames.” “The children danced with joy.”

    A kind of: move

Dance as a noun

  1. An artistic form of nonverbal communication.

    A kind of: art · Kinds: extension, choreography

  2. A party of people assembled for dancing.

    A kind of: party · Kinds: ball

  3. Taking a series of rhythmical steps (and movements) in time to music.

    Synonyms: dancing, terpsichore, saltation · A kind of: diversion, performing arts · Kinds: adagio, break dancing, courante, nautch, pavane, phrase, saraband, skank

  4. A party for social dancing.

    A kind of: party · Kinds: ball, barn dance, hop, rave

Phrases and compounds with "dance"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fan dance (noun) A solo dance in which large fans are manipulated to suggest or reveal nakedness.
  • sun dance (noun) A ceremonial dance performed by Amerindians at the summer solstice.
  • tap dance (noun) A dance step tapped out audibly with the feet.
  • toe dance (noun) A dance performed on tiptoe.
  • war dance (noun) A ceremonial dance performed before a battle or after a victory.
  • barn dance (noun) A dance party featuring country dancing.
  • clog dance (noun) A dance performed while wearing shoes with wooden soles; has heavy stamping steps.
  • corn dance (noun) A rain dance of Amerindians.
  • dance band (noun) A group of musicians playing popular music for dancing.
  • dance hall (noun) Large room used mainly for dancing.
  • dance step (noun) A sequence of foot movements that make up a particular dance.
  • folk dance (noun) A style of dancing that originated among ordinary people (not in the royal courts).
  • rain dance (noun) A ritual dance intended to bring rain.
  • ring dance (noun) A folk dance; dancers form a circle.
  • slam dance (noun) A form of dancing in which dancers slam into one another; normally performed to punk rock.
  • belly dance (noun) A Middle Eastern dance in which the dancer makes sensuous movements of the hips and abdomen.
