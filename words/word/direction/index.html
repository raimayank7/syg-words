Direction: meaning, synonyms and examples

Direction is used as a noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: A line leading to a place or point.

In Hindi
दिशा; निर्देश
Synonyms
way, guidance, counsel, counseling, counselling, management, instruction, steering, commission, charge

Direction as a noun

  1. A line leading to a place or point.

    “He looked the other direction.” “Didn't know the way home.”

    Synonyms: way · A kind of: path · Kinds: bearing, course, east-west direction, north-south direction, tendency

  2. The spatial relation between something and the course along which it points or moves.

    “He checked the direction and velocity of the wind.”

    A kind of: position · Kinds: frontage, orientation, opposition, windward, leeward, seaward, compass point, north

  3. A general course along which something has a tendency to develop.

    “I couldn't follow the direction of his thoughts.” “His ideals determined the direction of his career.” “They proposed a new direction for the firm.”

    A kind of: inclination · Kinds: tenor

  4. Something that provides direction or advice as to a decision or course of action.

    Synonyms: guidance, counsel, counseling, counselling · A kind of: message · Kinds: road map, career counseling, cynosure, genetic counseling, marriage counseling, tip

  5. The act of managing something.

    “He was given overall management of the program.” “Is the direction of the economy a function of government?”

    Synonyms: management · A kind of: social control · Kinds: conducting, database management, finance, homemaking, misconduct, mismanagement, treatment, supervision

  6. A message describing how something is to be done.

    “He gave directions faster than she could follow them.”

    Synonyms: instruction · A kind of: message · Kinds: rule, rule, misdirection, address, markup, prescription, recipe, rule

  7. The act of setting and holding a course.

    “A new council was installed under the direction of the king.”

    Synonyms: steering, guidance · A kind of: control · Kinds: driving, aim, navigation, celestial guidance, inertial guidance, command guidance, terrestrial guidance

  8. A formal statement of a command or injunction to do something.

    “The judge's charge to the jury.”

    Synonyms: commission, charge · A kind of: command · Kinds: misdirection

  9. The concentration of attention or energy on something.

    “The focus of activity shifted to molecular biology.” “He had no direction in his life.”

    Synonyms: focus, focusing, focussing, focal point, centering · A kind of: concentration · Kinds: particularism

Phrases and compounds with "direction"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • self-direction (noun) Personal independence.
  • stage direction (noun) An instruction written as part of the script of a play.
  • direction finder (noun) Radio; determines the direction of incoming radio waves.
  • sense of direction (noun) An awareness of your orientation in space.
  • change of direction (noun) The act of changing the direction in which something is oriented.
  • east-west direction (noun) In a direction parallel with lines of latitude.
  • north-south direction (noun) In a direction parallel with lines of longitude.
