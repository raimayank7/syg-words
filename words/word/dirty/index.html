Dirty: meaning, synonyms and examples

Dirty is used as an adjective (11 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Soiled or likely to soil with dirt or grime.

In Hindi
गंदा
Synonyms
soiled, unclean, filthy, lousy, contaminating, pestiferous, dingy, muddied, muddy, foul
Opposites
clean
Word family
dirt, dirtiness, dirtying

Dirty as an adjective

  1. Soiled or likely to soil with dirt or grime.

    “Dirty unswept sidewalks.” “A child in dirty overalls.” “Dirty slums.”

    Synonyms: soiled, unclean · Opposite: clean · Similar: bedraggled, befouled, begrimed, black, buggy, cobwebby

  2. Vile; despicable.

    “A dirty (or lousy) trick.” “A filthy traitor.”

    Synonyms: filthy, lousy

  3. Spreading pollution or contamination; especially radioactive contamination.

    “The air near the foundry was always dirty.”

    Synonyms: contaminating · Opposite: clean

  4. Contaminated with infecting organisms.

    “Dirty wounds.” “Obliged to go into infected rooms.”

    Synonyms: pestiferous · Similar: septic

  5. (of color) discolored by impurities; not bright and clear.

    “Dirty.” “A dirty (or dingy) white.” “The muddied grey of the sea.”

    Synonyms: dingy, muddied, muddy · Similar: impure

  6. (of a manuscript) defaced with changes.

    “Foul (or dirty) copy.”

    Synonyms: foul, marked-up · Similar: illegible

  7. Obtained illegally or by improper means.

    “Dirty money.” “Ill-gotten gains.”

    Synonyms: ill-gotten · Similar: illegal

  8. Expressing or revealing hostility or dislike.

    “Dirty looks.”

    Similar: hostile

  9. Violating accepted standards or rules.

    “A dirty fighter.” “Used foul means to gain power.” “A nasty unsporting serve.”

    Synonyms: cheating, foul, unsporting, unsportsmanlike · Similar: unfair

  10. Unethical or dishonest.

    “Dirty police officers.” “A sordid political campaign.”

    Synonyms: sordid · Similar: corrupt

  11. Unpleasantly stormy.

    “There's dirty weather in the offing.”

    Similar: stormy

Dirty as a verb

  1. Make soiled, filthy, or dirty.

    “Don't soil your clothes when you play outside!”

    Synonyms: soil, begrime, grime, colly, bemire · Opposite: clean · A kind of: change · Kinds: foul, pollute, smear, slime, muddy, splash, mire, crock

Phrases and compounds with "dirty"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • dirty dog (noun) A person who is deemed to be despicable or contemptible.
  • dirty joke (noun) An indelicate joke.
  • dirty pool (noun) Conduct that is unfair or unethical or unsportsmanlike.
  • dirty linen (noun) Personal matters that could be embarrassing if made public.
  • dirty money (noun) Goods or money obtained illegally.
  • dirty story (noun) An indelicate joke.
  • dirty trick (noun) An unkind or aggressive trick.
  • dirty-faced (adjective) Having a dirty face.
  • dirty tricks (noun) Underhand commercial or political behavior designed to discredit an opponent.
  • dirty-minded (adjective) Having lewd thoughts.
  • dirty laundry (noun) Personal matters that could be embarrassing if made public.
  • dirty old man (noun) A middle-aged man with lecherous inclinations.
