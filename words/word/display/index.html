Display: meaning, synonyms and examples

Display is used as a verb (2 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: To show, make visible or apparent.

In Hindi
प्रदर्शन; दिखाना
Synonyms
expose, exhibit, show, showing, presentation, video display

Display as a verb

  1. To show, make visible or apparent.

    “The Metropolitan Museum is exhibiting Goya's works this month.” “Why don't you show your nice legs and wear shorter skirts?” “National leaders will have to display the highest skills of statesmanship.”

    Synonyms: expose, exhibit · A kind of: show · Kinds: open, produce, hold up, bench, flaunt, brandish, model, model

  2. Attract attention by displaying some body part or posing; of animals.

    A kind of: woo

Display as a noun

  1. Something intended to communicate a particular impression.

    “Made a display of strength.” “A show of impatience.” “A good show of looking interested.”

    Synonyms: show · A kind of: demonstration · Kinds: big stick, gaudery

  2. Something shown to the public.

    “The museum had many exhibits of oriental art.”

    Synonyms: exhibit, showing · A kind of: show · Kinds: parade, light show

  3. A visual representation of something.

    Synonyms: presentation · A kind of: representation · Kinds: float, spectacle

  4. Behavior that makes your feelings public.

    “A display of emotion.”

    A kind of: disclosure · Kinds: histrionics, production, sackcloth and ashes

  5. Exhibiting openly in public view.

    “A display of courage.”

    A kind of: communication · Kinds: acting out, array, screening, sight, ostentation, flaunt, presentation

  6. An electronic device that represents information in visual form.

    Synonyms: video display · A kind of: electronic device · Kinds: digital display, display panel, flat panel display, monitor, screen, visual display unit, window

Phrases and compounds with "display"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • display case (noun) A glass container used to store and display items in a shop or museum or home.
  • display board (noun) A vertical surface on which information can be displayed to public view.
  • display panel (noun) A vertical surface on which information can be displayed to public view.
  • video display (noun) An electronic device that represents information in visual form.
  • display window (noun) A window of a store facing onto the street; used to display merchandise for sale in the store.
  • digital display (noun) A display that gives the information in the form of characters (numbers or letters).
  • display adapter (noun) (computer science) an electronic device that converts information in memory to video output to a display.
  • display adaptor (noun) (computer science) an electronic device that converts information in memory to video output to a display.
  • computer display (noun) A screen used to display the output of a computer to the user.
  • dual scan display (noun) A type of passive matrix display in which the top and bottom half of the screen are refreshed simultaneously.
  • flat panel display (noun) A type of video display that is thin and flat; commonly used in laptop computers.
  • visual display unit (noun) (British) British term for video display.
  • alphanumeric display (noun) A display that gives the information in the form of characters (numbers or letters).
  • liquid crystal display (noun) A digital display that uses liquid crystal cells that change reflectivity in an applied electric field; used for portable computer displays and watches etc.
  • passive matrix display (noun) A type of LCD display used for some portable computers; parallel wires run both vertically and horizontally and pixels are turned on when the wires intersecting at that pixel are both energized.
