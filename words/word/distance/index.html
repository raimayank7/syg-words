Distance: meaning, synonyms and examples

Distance is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The property created by the space between two objects or points.

In Hindi
दूरी
Synonyms
length, aloofness, space, outdistance, outstrip
Word family
distant

Distance as a noun

  1. The property created by the space between two objects or points.

    A kind of: spacing · Kinds: way, piece, mean distance, farness, nearness, wavelength, focal distance, hyperfocal distance

  2. A distant region.

    “I could see it in the distance.”

    A kind of: region

  3. Size of the gap between two places.

    “The distance from New York to Chicago.” “He determined the length of the shortest line segment joining the two points.”

    Synonyms: length · A kind of: size · Kinds: leg, arm's length, gauge, light time, skip distance, wingspan, wingspread, altitude

  4. Indifference by personal withdrawal.

    “Emotional distance.”

    Synonyms: aloofness · A kind of: indifference

  5. The interval between two times.

    “The distance from birth to death.” “It all happened in the space of 10 minutes.”

    Synonyms: space · A kind of: time interval

  6. A remote point in time.

    “If that happens it will be at some distance in the future.” “At a distance of ten years he had forgotten many of the details.”

    A kind of: point

Distance as a verb

  1. Keep at a distance.

    “We have to distance ourselves from these events in order to continue living.”

    A kind of: keep

  2. Go far ahead of.

    “He outdistanced the other runners.”

    Synonyms: outdistance, outstrip · A kind of: leave behind

Phrases and compounds with "distance"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • long distance (noun) A telephone call made outside the local calling area.
  • long-distance (adjective) Of or relating to or being a long-distance telephone call.
  • mean distance (noun) The arithmetic mean of the maximum and minimum distances of a celestial body (satellite or secondary star) from its primary.
  • skip distance (noun) The shortest distance that permits radio signals (of a given frequency) to travel from the transmitter to the receiver by reflection from the ionosphere.
  • focal distance (noun) The distance from a lens to its focus.
  • distance vision (noun) Vision for objects that a 20 feet or more from the viewer.
  • middle distance (noun) The part of a scene between the foreground and the background.
  • angular distance (noun) The angular separation between two objects as perceived by an observer.
  • long-distance call (noun) A telephone call made outside the local calling area.
  • hyperfocal distance (noun) The distance in front of a lens that is focused at infinity beyond which all objects are well defined and clear.
  • keep one's distance (verb) Stay clear of, avoid.
  • long-distance runner (noun) Someone who participates in long-distance races (especially in marathons).
