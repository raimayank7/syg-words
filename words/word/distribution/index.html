Distribution: meaning, synonyms and examples

Distribution is used as a noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: (statistics) an arrangement of values of a variable showing their observed or theoretical frequency of occurrence.

Distribution as a noun

  1. (statistics) an arrangement of values of a variable showing their observed or theoretical frequency of occurrence.

    Synonyms: statistical distribution · A kind of: arrangement · Kinds: equidistribution, frequency distribution, normal distribution, sample distribution, binomial distribution

  2. The spatial or geographic property of being scattered about over a range, area, or volume.

    “Worldwide in distribution.” “The distribution of nerve fibers.” “In complementary distribution.”

    Synonyms: dispersion · Opposite: concentration · A kind of: spacing · Kinds: complementary distribution, diaspora, dissemination, innervation, scatter

  3. The act of distributing or spreading or apportioning.

    A kind of: act · Kinds: assignment, redistribution, dispensation, allotment, deal, revenue sharing, sharing

  4. The commercial activity of transporting and selling goods from a producer to a consumer.

    A kind of: commerce

Phrases and compounds with "distribution"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • distribution law (noun) (chemistry) the total energy in an assembly of molecules is not distributed equally but is distributed around an average value according to a statistical distribution.
  • distribution cost (noun) Any cost incurred by a producer or wholesaler or retailer or distributor (as for advertising and shipping etc).
  • distribution list (noun) List of names to whom a communication should be sent.
  • normal distribution (noun) A theoretical distribution with finite mean and variance.
  • sample distribution (noun) Items selected at random from a population and used to test hypotheses about the population.
  • distribution channel (noun) A way of selling a company's product either directly or via distributors.
  • poisson distribution (noun) A theoretical distribution that is a good approximation to the binomial distribution when the probability is small and the number of trials is large.
  • binomial distribution (noun) A theoretical distribution of the number of successes in a finite set of independent trials with a constant probability of success.
  • gaussian distribution (noun) A theoretical distribution with finite mean and variance.
  • bernoulli distribution (noun) A theoretical distribution of the number of successes in a finite set of independent trials with a constant probability of success.
  • distribution agreement (noun) A contract governing the marketing of an item of merchandise.
  • frequency distribution (noun) A distribution of observed frequencies of occurrence of the values of a variable.
  • statistical distribution (noun) (statistics) an arrangement of values of a variable showing their observed or theoretical frequency of occurrence.
  • boltzmann distribution law (noun) (physics) a law expressing the distribution of energy among the molecules of a gas in thermal equilibrium.
  • complementary distribution (noun) (linguistics) a distribution of related speech sounds or forms in such a way that they only appear in different contexts.
  • distribution free statistic (noun) A statistic computed without knowledge of the form or the parameters of the distribution from which observations are drawn.
