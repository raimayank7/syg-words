Division: meaning, synonyms and examples

Division is used as a noun (12 senses). Its most common meaning is: An army unit large enough to sustain combat.

In Hindi
विभाजन; भाग; मंडल
Synonyms
part, section, variance, class, air division, naval division, partition, partitioning, segmentation, sectionalization
Word family
divisional, divide

Division as a noun

  1. An army unit large enough to sustain combat.

    “Two infantry divisions were held in reserve.”

    A kind of: army unit

  2. One of the portions into which something is regarded as divided and which together constitute a whole.

    “The written part of the exam.” “The finance section of the company.” “The BBC's engineering division.”

    Synonyms: part, section · A kind of: concept · Kinds: frame, beginning, middle, end, high point, component, chukker, inning

  3. The act or process of dividing.

    A kind of: separation · Kinds: parcellation, cleavage, bisection, quartering, schism, cut, atomization, branching

  4. An administrative unit in government or business.

    A kind of: administrative unit · Kinds: department, branch

  5. Discord that splits a group.

    Synonyms: variance · A kind of: discord

  6. A league ranked by quality.

    “He played baseball in class D for two years.” “Princeton is in the NCAA Division 1-AA.”

    Synonyms: class · A kind of: league

  7. (biology) a group of organisms forming a subdivision of a larger category.

    A kind of: biological group · Kinds: form division

  8. (botany) taxonomic unit of plants corresponding to a phylum.

    A kind of: phylum

  9. A unit of the United States Air Force usually comprising two or more wings.

    Synonyms: air division · A kind of: air unit

  10. A group of ships of similar type.

    Synonyms: naval division · A kind of: naval unit

  11. An arithmetic operation that is the inverse of multiplication; the quotient of two numbers is computed.

    A kind of: arithmetic operation · Kinds: long division, short division

  12. The act of dividing or partitioning; separation by the creation of a boundary that divides or keeps apart.

    Synonyms: partition, partitioning, segmentation, sectionalization, sectionalisation · A kind of: separation · Kinds: subdivision, septation, zoning

Phrases and compounds with "division"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air division (noun) A unit of the United States Air Force usually comprising two or more wings.
  • cell division (noun) The process in reproduction and growth by which a cell divides to form daughter cells.
  • form division (noun) An artificial taxonomic category for organisms of which the true relationships are obscure.
  • long division (noun) The operation of division in which the sequence of steps are indicated in detail.
  • word division (noun) Division of a word especially at the end of a line on a page.
  • cross-division (noun) Classification according to more than one attribute at the same time.
  • naval division (noun) A group of ships of similar type.
  • sales division (noun) The division of a business that is responsible for selling products or services.
  • short division (noun) The operation of division in which the sequence of steps is performed without writing them out.
  • benthic division (noun) A region including the bottom of the sea and the littoral zones.
  • cellular division (noun) The process in reproduction and growth by which a cell divides to form daughter cells.
  • division eumycota (noun) True fungi; eukaryotic heterotrophic walled organisms; distinguished from Myxomycota (funguslike slime molds): comprises subdivisions Mastigomycotina; Zygomycotina; Ascomycotina; Basidiomycotina; Deuteromycotina (imperfect fungi).
  • division lichenes (noun) Comprising the lichens which grow symbiotically with algae; sometimes treated as an independent group more or less coordinate with algae and fungi.
  • division protista (noun) Eukaryotic one-celled living organisms distinct from multicellular plants and animals: protozoa, slime molds, and eukaryotic algae.
  • division bryophyta (noun) A division of nonflowering plants characterized by rhizoids rather than true roots and having little or no organized vascular tissue and showing alternation of generations between gamete-bearing forms and spore-bearing forms; comprises true mosses (Bryopsida) and liverworts (Hepaticopsida) and hornworts (Anthoceropsida).
  • division anthophyta (noun) Comprising flowering plants that produce seeds enclosed in an ovary; in some systems considered a class (Angiospermae) and in others a division (Magnoliophyta or Anthophyta).
