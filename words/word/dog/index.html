Dog: meaning, synonyms and examples

Dog is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A member of the genus Canis (probably descended from the common wolf) that has been domesticated by man since prehistoric times; occurs in many breeds.

Synonyms
domestic dog, frump, cad, bounder, blackguard, hound, heel, frank, frankfurter, hotdog

Dog as a noun

  1. A member of the genus Canis (probably descended from the common wolf) that has been domesticated by man since prehistoric times; occurs in many breeds.

    “The dog barked all night.”

    Synonyms: domestic dog · A kind of: canine, domestic animal · Kinds: puppy, pooch, cur, lapdog, toy dog, hunting dog, working dog, dalmatian

  2. A dull unattractive unpleasant girl or woman.

    “She got a reputation as a frump.” “She's a real dog.”

    Synonyms: frump · A kind of: unpleasant woman

  3. Informal term for a man.

    “You lucky dog.”

    A kind of: chap

  4. Someone who is morally reprehensible.

    “You dirty dog.”

    Synonyms: cad, bounder, blackguard, hound, heel · A kind of: villain · Kinds: perisher

  5. A smooth-textured sausage of minced beef or pork usually smoked; often served on a bread roll.

    Synonyms: frank, frankfurter, hotdog, hot dog, wiener, wienerwurst, weenie · A kind of: sausage

  6. A hinged catch that fits into a notch of a ratchet to move a wheel forward or prevent it from moving backward.

    Synonyms: pawl, detent, click · A kind of: catch

  7. Metal supports for logs in a fireplace.

    “The andirons were too hot to touch.”

    Synonyms: andiron, firedog, dog-iron · A kind of: support

Dog as a verb

  1. Go after with the intent to catch.

    “The policeman chased the mugger down the alley.” “The dog chased the rabbit.”

    Synonyms: chase, chase after, trail, tail, tag, give chase, go after, track · A kind of: pursue · Kinds: tree, quest, hound, run down
