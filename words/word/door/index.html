Door: meaning, synonyms and examples

Door is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A swinging or sliding barrier that will close the entrance to a room or building or vehicle.

In Hindi
दरवाज़ा
Synonyms
doorway, room access, threshold

Door as a noun

  1. A swinging or sliding barrier that will close the entrance to a room or building or vehicle.

    “He knocked on the door.” “He slammed the door as he left.”

    A kind of: movable barrier · Kinds: car door, cargo door, double door, fire door, interior door, revolving door, screen door, sliding door

  2. The entrance (the space in a wall) through which you enter or leave a room or building; the space that a door can close.

    “He stuck his head in the doorway.”

    Synonyms: doorway, room access, threshold · A kind of: entrance · Kinds: exterior door

  3. Anything providing a means of access (or escape).

    “We closed the door to Haitian immigrants.” “Education is the door to success.”

    A kind of: entree · Kinds: open door

  4. A structure where people live or work (usually ordered along a street or road).

    “The office next door.” “They live two doors up the street from us.”

    A kind of: structure

  5. A room that is entered via a door.

    “His office is the third door down the hall on the left.”

    A kind of: room

Phrases and compounds with "door"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • car door (noun) The door of a car.
  • back door (noun) A secret or underhand means of access (to a place or a position).
  • barn door (noun) The large sliding door of a barn.
  • fire door (noun) A fire-resistant door that can be closed to stop the spread of a fire.
  • half door (noun) An exterior door divided in two horizontally; either half can be closed or open independently.
  • next door (adverb) At or in or to the adjacent residence.
  • open door (noun) The policy of granting equal trade opportunities to all countries.
  • side door (noun) An exterior door at one side of a building.
  • trap door (noun) A hinged or sliding door in a floor or ceiling.
  • cargo door (noun) Door used to load or unload cargo.
  • door guard (noun) Someone who guards an entrance.
  • door latch (noun) Spring-loaded doorlock that can only be opened from the outside with a key.
  • door prize (noun) Tickets are passed out at the entrance to a dance or party or other social function and a prize is awarded to the holder of the winning ticket.
  • dutch door (noun) An exterior door divided in two horizontally; either half can be closed or open independently.
  • front door (noun) Exterior door (at the entrance) at the front of a building.
  • stage door (noun) An entrance to the backstage area of theater; used by performers and other theater personnel.
