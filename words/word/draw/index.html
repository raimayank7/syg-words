Draw: meaning, synonyms and examples

Draw is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Cause to move by pulling.

In Hindi
चित्र बनाना; खींचना
Synonyms
pull, force, reap, trace, line, describe, delineate, make, pull out, get out
Word family
drawing, drawer, drawee

Draw as a verb

  1. Cause to move by pulling.

    “Draw a wagon.” “Pull a sled.”

    Synonyms: pull, force · A kind of: move · Kinds: twitch, pull back, adduct, abduct, stretch, pluck, tug, drag

  2. Get or derive.

    “He drew great benefits from his membership in the association.”

    Synonyms: reap · A kind of: derive

  3. Make a mark or lines on a surface.

    “Draw a line.” “Trace the outline of a figure in the sand.”

    Synonyms: trace, line, describe, delineate · A kind of: mark · Kinds: construct, inscribe, circumscribe, circumscribe, write

  4. Make, formulate, or derive in the mind.

    “I draw a line here.” “Draw a conclusion.” “Draw parallels.”

    Synonyms: make · A kind of: create by mental act

  5. Bring, take, or pull out of a container or from under a cover.

    “Draw a weapon.” “Pull out a gun.” “The mugger pulled a knife on his victim.”

    Synonyms: pull, pull out, get out, take out · A kind of: remove · Kinds: unsheathe

  6. Represent by making a drawing of, as with a pencil, chalk, etc. on a surface.

    “She drew an elephant.” “Draw me a horse.”

    A kind of: represent · Kinds: pencil, delineate, rule, chalk, project, crayon, check, charcoal

  7. Take liquid out of a container or well.

    “She drew water from the barrel.”

    Synonyms: take out · A kind of: remove · Kinds: milk, pump, siphon, sluice, suck, rack, deglycerolize

  8. Give a description of.

    “He drew an elaborate plan of attack.”

    Synonyms: describe, depict · A kind of: set forth · Kinds: represent, delineate, sketch

  9. Select or take in from a given group or region.

    “The participants in the experiment were drawn from a representative population.”

    A kind of: choose

  10. Elicit responses, such as objections, criticism, applause, etc.

    “The President's comments drew sharp criticism from the Republicans.” “The comedian drew a lot of laughter.”

    A kind of: arouse

  11. Suck in or take (air).

    “Draw a deep breath.” “Draw on a cigarette.”

    Synonyms: puff, drag · A kind of: inhale

  12. Move or go steadily or gradually.

    “The ship drew near the shore.”

    A kind of: travel

  13. Remove (a commodity) from (a supply source).

    “She drew $2,000 from the account.” “The doctors drew medical supplies from the hospital's emergency bank.”

    Synonyms: withdraw, take out, draw off · A kind of: remove · Kinds: cheque, dip, divert, overdraw, tap, divest

  14. Choose at random.

    “Draw a card.” “Cast lots.”

    Synonyms: cast · A kind of: move

Draw as a noun

  1. A gully that is shallower than a ravine.

    A kind of: gully

  2. An entertainer who attracts large audiences.

    “He was the biggest drawing card they had.”

    Synonyms: drawing card, attraction, attractor, attracter · A kind of: entertainer

  3. The finish of a contest in which the score is tied and the winner is undecided.

    “The game ended in a draw.” “Their record was 3 wins, 6 losses and a tie.”

    Synonyms: standoff, tie · A kind of: finish · Kinds: dead heat, stalemate

  4. Anything (straws or pebbles etc.) taken or chosen at random.

    “The luck of the draw.” “They drew lots for it.”

    Synonyms: lot · A kind of: object

  5. A playing card or cards dealt or taken from the pack.

    “He got a pair of kings in the draw.”

    A kind of: playing card

  6. A golf shot that curves to the left for a right-handed golfer.

    “He took lessons to cure his hooking.”

    Synonyms: hook, hooking · A kind of: golf stroke

  7. (American football) the quarterback moves back as if to pass and then hands the ball to the fullback who is running toward the line of scrimmage.

    Synonyms: draw play · A kind of: run

  8. Poker in which a player can discard cards and receive substitutes from the dealer.

    “He played only draw and stud.”

    Synonyms: draw poker

  9. The act of drawing or hauling something.

    “The haul up the hill went very slowly.”

    Synonyms: haul, haulage · A kind of: pull · Kinds: tow

Phrases and compounds with "draw"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • draw in (verb) Pull inward or towards a center.
  • draw up (verb) Form or arrange in order or formation, as of a body of soldiers.
  • draw off (verb) Remove by drawing or pulling.
  • draw out (verb) Cause to speak, "Can you draw her out--she is always so quiet".
  • draw away (verb) Move ahead of (one's competitors) in a race.
  • draw back (verb) Pull back or move away or backward.
  • draw near (verb) Move towards.
  • draw play (noun) (American football) the quarterback moves back as if to pass and then hands the ball to the fullback who is running toward the line of scrimmage.
  • draw rein (verb) Control and direct with or as if by reins.
  • draw close (verb) Move or arrange oneself in a comfortable and cozy position.
  • draw poker (noun) Poker in which a player can discard cards and receive substitutes from the dealer.
  • draw a line (verb) Reasonably object (to) or set a limit (on).
  • draw a blank (verb) Be unable to remember.
  • draw the line (verb) Reasonably object (to) or set a limit (on).
  • draw together (verb) Bring together in a common cause or emotion.
  • draw a bead on (verb) Aim with a gun.
