Dust: meaning, synonyms and examples

Dust is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Fine powdery material such as dry earth or pollen that can be blown about in the air.

In Hindi
धूल
Synonyms
debris, junk, rubble, detritus, scatter, sprinkle, dot, disperse
Word family
dusty, duster

Dust as a noun

  1. Fine powdery material such as dry earth or pollen that can be blown about in the air.

    “The furniture was covered with dust.”

    A kind of: particulate · Kinds: chalk dust, fallout

  2. The remains of something that has been destroyed or broken up.

    Synonyms: debris, junk, rubble, detritus · A kind of: rubbish · Kinds: slack

  3. Free microscopic particles of solid material.

    “Astronomers say that the empty space between planets actually contains measurable amounts of dust.”

    A kind of: material · Kinds: interplanetary dust

Dust as a verb

  1. Remove the dust from.

    “Dust the cabinets.”

    A kind of: clean

  2. Rub the dust over a surface so as to blur the outlines of a shape.

    “The artist dusted the charcoal drawing down to a faint image.”

    A kind of: smear

  3. Cover with a light dusting of a substance.

    “Dust the bread with flour.”

    A kind of: cover

  4. Distribute loosely.

    “He scattered gun powder under the wagon.”

    Synonyms: scatter, sprinkle, dot, disperse · A kind of: discharge · Kinds: spray, spray, spatter, sprinkle, bespangle, aerosolize
