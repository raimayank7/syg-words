Edge: meaning, synonyms and examples

Edge is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: The boundary of a surface.

In Hindi
किनारा, धार
Synonyms
border, boundary, bound, sharpness, inch, adjoin, abut, march, butt, butt against
Word family
edgy, edger, edging

Edge as a noun

  1. The boundary of a surface.

    Synonyms: border · A kind of: boundary · Kinds: brink, limb

  2. A line determining the limits of an area.

    Synonyms: boundary, bound · A kind of: line · Kinds: rim, margin, periphery, brink, upper bound, lower bound, thalweg

  3. A sharp side formed by the intersection of two surfaces of an object.

    “He rounded the edges of the box.”

    A kind of: side · Kinds: bevel, bezel, brim, curb, deckle edge, featheredge, groin, knife edge

  4. The attribute of urgency in tone of voice.

    “His voice had an edge to it.”

    Synonyms: sharpness · A kind of: urgency

  5. A slight competitive advantage.

    “He had an edge on the competition.”

    A kind of: superiority

  6. The outside limit of an object or area or surface; a place farthest away from the center of something.

    “The edge of the leaf is wavy.” “She sat on the edge of the bed.” “The water's edge.”

    A kind of: limit · Kinds: border, hem, luff, molding, selvage, shoulder, wayside

Edge as a verb

  1. Advance slowly, as if by inches.

    “He edged towards the car.”

    Synonyms: inch · A kind of: advance

  2. Provide with a border or edge.

    “Edge the tablecloth with embroidery.”

    Synonyms: border · A kind of: supply

  3. Lie adjacent to another or share a boundary.

    “Canada adjoins the U.S.” “England marches with Scotland.”

    Synonyms: border, adjoin, abut, march, butt, butt against, butt on · A kind of: touch · Kinds: neighbor

  4. Provide with an edge.

    “Edge a blade.”

    A kind of: sharpen

Phrases and compounds with "edge"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • edge in (verb) Push one's way into (a space).
  • edge up (verb) Push one's way into (a space).
  • edge tool (noun) Any cutting tool with a sharp cutting edge (as a chisel or knife or plane or gouge).
  • fore edge (noun) The part of a book that faces inward when the book is shelved; the part opposite the spine.
  • knife edge (noun) The sharp cutting side of the blade of a knife.
  • knife-edge (noun) A narrow boundary.
  • razor edge (noun) An edge that is as sharp as the cutting side of a razor.
  • deckle edge (noun) Rough edge left by a deckle on handmade paper or produced artificially on machine-made paper.
  • cutting edge (noun) The position of greatest importance or advancement; the leading position in any movement or field.
  • cutting-edge (adjective) In accord with the most fashionable ideas or style.
  • leading edge (noun) Forward edge of an airfoil.
  • trailing edge (noun) The rear edge of an airfoil.
