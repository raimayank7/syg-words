Egg: meaning, synonyms and examples

Egg is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Animal reproductive body consisting of an ovum or embryo together with nutritive and protective envelopes; especially the thin-shelled reproductive body laid by e.g. female birds.

In Hindi
अंडा
Synonyms
eggs, testis, testicle, orchis, ball, ballock, bollock, nut

Egg as a noun

  1. Animal reproductive body consisting of an ovum or embryo together with nutritive and protective envelopes; especially the thin-shelled reproductive body laid by e.g. female birds.

    A kind of: ovum · Kinds: nit, spawn, roe, silkworm seed, roe

  2. Oval reproductive body of a fowl (especially a hen) used as food.

    Synonyms: eggs · A kind of: foodstuff

  3. One of the two male reproductive glands that produce spermatozoa and secrete androgens.

    “She kicked him in the balls and got away.”

    Synonyms: testis, testicle, orchis, ball, ballock, bollock, nut · A kind of: male reproductive gland · Kinds: cobblers, undescended testis

Egg as a verb

  1. Throw eggs at.

    A kind of: pelt

  2. Coat with beaten egg.

    “Egg a schnitzel.”

    A kind of: coat
