End: meaning, synonyms and examples

End is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Either extremity of something that has length.

In Hindi
अंत; समाप्त करना
Synonyms
terminal, ending, last, final stage, goal, destruction, death, conclusion, close, closing
Opposites
beginning, middle, begin
Word family
ending

End as a noun

  1. Either extremity of something that has length.

    “The end of the pier.” “She knotted the end of the thread.” “They rode to the end of the line.”

    Synonyms: terminal · A kind of: extremity · Kinds: bitter end, bitthead, heel, point, pole, railhead, terminus, yardarm

  2. The point in time at which something ends.

    “The end of the year.” “The ending of warranty period.”

    Synonyms: ending · Opposite: beginning, middle · A kind of: point · Kinds: death, death, period, year-end, stopping point, cease, fag end, last gasp

  3. The concluding parts of an event or occurrence.

    “The end was exciting.” “I had to miss the last of the movie.”

    Synonyms: last, final stage · A kind of: ending · Kinds: endgame, endgame, homestretch, passing

  4. The state of affairs that a plan is intended to achieve and that (when achieved) terminates behavior intended to achieve it.

    “The ends justify the means.”

    Synonyms: goal · A kind of: content · Kinds: aim, bourn, end-all, destination, no-goal, purpose, intention

  5. A final part or section.

    “We have given it at the end of the section since it involves the calculus.” “Start at the beginning and go on until you come to the end.”

    Opposite: beginning, middle · A kind of: part

  6. A final state.

    “He came to a bad end.” “The so-called glorious experiment came to an inglorious end.”

    Synonyms: destruction, death · A kind of: state

  7. The surface at either extremity of a three-dimensional object.

    “One end of the box was marked `This side up'.”

    A kind of: surface

  8. (football) the person who plays at one end of the line of scrimmage.

    “The end managed to hold onto the pass.”

    A kind of: lineman

  9. A boundary marking the extremities of something.

    “The end of town.”

    A kind of: boundary

  10. One of two places from which people are communicating to each other.

    “The phone rang at the other end.” “Both ends wrote at the same time.”

    A kind of: topographic point

  11. The part you are expected to play.

    “He held up his end.”

    A kind of: contribution

  12. The last section of a communication.

    “In conclusion I want to say...”

    Synonyms: conclusion, close, closing, ending · A kind of: section · Kinds: anticlimax, epilogue, epilogue, peroration, finale

  13. A piece of cloth that is left over after the rest has been used or sold.

    Synonyms: remainder, remnant, oddment · A kind of: piece of cloth · Kinds: fag end

  14. (American football) a position on the line of scrimmage.

    “No one wanted to play end.”

    A kind of: lineman

End as a verb

  1. Have an end, in a temporal, spatial, or quantitative sense; either spatial or metaphorical.

    “The bronchioles terminate in a capillary bed.” “Your rights stop where you infringe upon the rights of other.” “My property ends by the bushes.”

    Synonyms: stop, finish, terminate, cease · Opposite: begin · Kinds: pass away, lapse, cut out, go out, adjourn, vanish, culminate, run out

  2. Bring to an end or halt.

    “She ended their friendship when she found out that he had once been convicted of a crime.” “The attack on Poland terminated the relatively peaceful period after WW I.”

    Synonyms: terminate · Opposite: begin, begin · A kind of: change · Kinds: close out, finish, culminate, lift, ax, stamp out, dissolve, dissolve

  3. Be the end of; be the last or concluding part of.

    “This sad scene ended the movie.”

    Synonyms: terminate · A kind of: be · Kinds: close

  4. Put an end to.

    “The terrible news ended our hopes that he had survived.”

    A kind of: destroy

Phrases and compounds with "end"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • end on (adverb) With the end forward or toward the observer.
  • end up (verb) Finally be or do something.
  • no end (adverb) On and on for a long time.
  • end man (noun) A man at one end of line of performers in a minstrel show; carries on humorous dialogue with the interlocutor.
  • end run (noun) (American football) an attempt to advance the ball by running around the end of the line.
  • end-all (noun) The ultimate goal.
  • fag end (noun) The time of the last part of something.
  • tag end (noun) A small piece of cloth or paper.
  • back end (noun) The side of an object that is opposite its front.
  • butt end (noun) Thick end of the handle.
  • dead end (noun) A passage with access only at one end.
  • dead-end (adjective) Lacking opportunities for development or advancement.
  • end game (noun) The final stages of an extended process of negotiation.
  • end user (noun) The ultimate user for which something is intended.
  • hind end (noun) The fleshy part of the human body that you sit on.
  • rear end (noun) The fleshy part of the human body that you sit on.
