Entry: meaning, synonyms and examples

Entry is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: An item inserted in a written record.

In Hindi
प्रवेश; प्रविष्टि
Synonyms
introduction, debut, first appearance, launching, unveiling, accounting entry, ledger entry, submission, entrance, entranceway
Word family
enter

Entry as a noun

  1. An item inserted in a written record.

    A kind of: written record · Kinds: lexical entry, nolle prosequi, notebook entry

  2. The act of beginning something new.

    “They looked forward to the debut of their new product line.”

    Synonyms: introduction, debut, first appearance, launching, unveiling · A kind of: beginning · Kinds: induction, product introduction, ushering in, naturalization

  3. A written record of a commercial transaction.

    Synonyms: accounting entry, ledger entry · A kind of: accounting · Kinds: adjusting entry, credit, debit

  4. Something (manuscripts or architectural plans and models or estimates or works of art of all genres etc.) submitted for the judgment of others (as in a competition).

    “Several of his submissions were rejected by publishers.” “What was the date of submission of your proposal?”

    Synonyms: submission · A kind of: message · Kinds: filing

  5. Something that provides access (to get in or get out).

    “They waited at the entrance to the garden.” “Beggars waited just outside the entryway to the cathedral.”

    Synonyms: entrance, entranceway, entryway, entree · A kind of: access · Kinds: arch, doorway, gateway, hatchway, pithead, portal, porte-cochere, service door

  6. The act of entering.

    “She made a grand entrance.”

    Synonyms: entrance, entering, ingress, incoming · A kind of: arrival · Kinds: incursion, intrusion, irruption, entree, registration, penetration, admission

Phrases and compounds with "entry"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • entry word (noun) The form of a word that heads a lexical entry and is alphabetized in a dictionary.
  • debit entry (noun) An accounting entry acknowledging sums that are owing.
  • credit entry (noun) An accounting entry acknowledging income or capital items.
  • double entry (noun) Bookkeeper debits the transaction to one account and credits it to another.
  • ledger entry (noun) A written record of a commercial transaction.
  • single entry (noun) A simple bookkeeping system; transactions are entered in only one account.
  • bill of entry (noun) A list of goods received at a customhouse for export or import.
  • lexical entry (noun) The entry in a dictionary of information about a word.
  • port of entry (noun) A port in the United States where customs officials are stationed to oversee the entry and exit of people and merchandise.
  • notebook entry (noun) An entry in a notebook.
  • point of entry (noun) A port in the United States where customs officials are stationed to oversee the entry and exit of people and merchandise.
  • right of entry (noun) The legal right to take possession of real estate in a peaceable manner.
  • adjusting entry (noun) An accounting entry made at the end of an accounting period to allocate items between accounting periods.
  • main entry word (noun) The form of a word that heads a lexical entry and is alphabetized in a dictionary.
  • accounting entry (noun) A written record of a commercial transaction.
  • dictionary entry (noun) The entry in a dictionary of information about a word.
