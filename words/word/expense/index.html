Expense: meaning, synonyms and examples

Expense is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Amounts paid for goods and services that may be currently tax deductible (as opposed to capital expenditures).

In Hindi
ख़र्च
Synonyms
disbursal, disbursement, write off, write down

Expense as a noun

  1. Amounts paid for goods and services that may be currently tax deductible (as opposed to capital expenditures).

    Synonyms: disbursal, disbursement · A kind of: cost · Kinds: business expense, interest expense, lobbying expense, medical expense, non-cash expense, moving expense, operating expense, personal expense

  2. A detriment or sacrifice.

    “At the expense of.”

    A kind of: detriment

  3. Money spent to perform work and usually reimbursed by an employer.

    “He kept a careful record of his expenses at the meeting.”

    A kind of: outgo · Kinds: incidental expense, travel expense

Expense as a verb

  1. Reduce the estimated value of something.

    “For tax purposes you can write off the laser printer.”

    Synonyms: write off, write down · A kind of: depreciate

Phrases and compounds with "expense"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • minor expense (noun) (frequently plural) an expense not budgeted or not specified.
  • trade expense (noun) Ordinary and necessary expenses incurred in a taxpayer's business or trade.
  • at any expense (adverb) Regardless of the cost involved.
  • expense record (noun) A written record of money spent.
  • moving expense (noun) The cost of moving your residence from one location to another.
  • travel expense (noun) (frequently plural) expenses incurred by an employee in the performance of the job and usually reimbursed by the employer.
  • expense account (noun) An account to which salespersons or executives can charge travel and entertainment expenses.
  • medical expense (noun) Amount spent for diagnosis or treatment or prevention of medical problems.
  • business expense (noun) Ordinary and necessary expenses incurred in a taxpayer's business or trade.
  • interest expense (noun) Interest paid on loans.
  • lobbying expense (noun) Expenses incurred in promoting or evaluating legislation.
  • non-cash expense (noun) An expense (such as depreciation) that is not paid for in cash.
  • personal expense (noun) The cost of personal or family living.
  • operating expense (noun) The expense of maintaining property (e.g., paying property taxes and utilities and insurance); it does not include depreciation or the cost of financing or income taxes.
  • incidental expense (noun) (frequently plural) an expense not budgeted or not specified.
  • promotional expense (noun) The cost of promoting a product.
