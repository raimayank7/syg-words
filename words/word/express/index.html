Express: meaning, synonyms and examples

Express is used as a verb (7 senses), adjective (2 senses), adverb (1 sense) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Give expression to.

In Hindi
व्यक्त करना; तेज़ (गाड़ी)
Synonyms
show, evince, verbalize, verbalise, utter, give tongue to, carry, convey, state, press out
Opposites
local
Word family
expressible, expressive, expression, expressage

Express as a verb

  1. Give expression to.

    “She showed her disappointment.”

    Synonyms: show, evince · A kind of: convey · Kinds: sneer, imply, burst out, suggest, imply, give, exude, vent

  2. Articulate; either verbally or with a cry, shout, or noise.

    “She expressed her anger.” “He uttered a curse.”

    Synonyms: verbalize, verbalise, utter, give tongue to · Kinds: wish, exclaim, clamor, vociferate, marvel, voice, raise, breathe

  3. Serve as a means for expressing something.

    “The painting of Mary carries motherly love.” “His voice carried a lot of anger.”

    Synonyms: carry, convey · A kind of: communicate · Kinds: quantify

  4. Indicate through a symbol, formula, etc.

    “Can you express this distance in kilometers?”

    Synonyms: state · A kind of: denote · Kinds: vote, vote

  5. Manifest the effects of (a gene or genetic trait).

    “Many of the laboratory animals express the trait.”

    A kind of: realize

  6. Obtain from a substance, as by mechanical action.

    “Italians express coffee rather than filter it.”

    Synonyms: press out, extract · A kind of: get · Kinds: ream

  7. Send by rapid transport or special messenger service.

    “She expressed the letter to Florida.”

    A kind of: mail

Express as an adjective

  1. Not tacit or implied.

    “Her express wish.”

    Similar: explicit

  2. Without unnecessary stops.

    “An express train.” “An express shipment.”

    Similar: fast

Express as an adverb

  1. By express.

    “Please send the letter express.”

Express as a noun

  1. Mail that is distributed by a rapid and efficient system.

    Synonyms: express mail · A kind of: mail · Kinds: pony express

  2. Public transport consisting of a fast train or bus that makes only a few scheduled stops.

    “He caught the express to New York.”

    Synonyms: limited · Opposite: local · A kind of: public transport

  3. Rapid transport of goods.

    Synonyms: expressage · A kind of: transportation

Phrases and compounds with "express"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • express joy (verb) Produce laughter.
  • express mail (noun) Mail that is distributed by a rapid and efficient system.
  • express-mail (verb) Send by express mail or courier.
  • pony express (noun) Express mail carried by relays of riders on horseback; especially between Missouri and California around 1860.
  • express mirth (verb) Produce laughter.
  • express trust (noun) A trust created by the free and deliberate act of the parties involved (usually on the basis of written documentation).
  • express emotion (verb) Give verbal or other expression to one's feelings.
  • express feelings (verb) Give verbal or other expression to one's feelings.
  • express luxury liner (noun) A liner equipped for sumptuous living.
