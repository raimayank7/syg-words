Extend: meaning, synonyms and examples

Extend is used as a verb (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: Extend in scope or range or area.

In Hindi
बढ़ाना, फैलाना
Synonyms
widen, broaden, run, go, pass, lead, cover, continue, offer, exsert
Word family
extensive, extension, extent, extensible, extendible, extensor

Extend as a verb

  1. Extend in scope or range or area.

    “The law was extended to all citizens.” “Widen the range of applications.” “Broaden your horizon.”

    Synonyms: widen, broaden · A kind of: increase · Kinds: broaden, territorialize, globalize, stretch

  2. Stretch out over a distance, space, time, or scope; run or extend between two points or beyond a certain point.

    “Service runs all the way to Cranbury.” “His knowledge doesn't go very far.” “My memory extends back to my fourth year of life.”

    Synonyms: run, go, pass, lead · A kind of: be · Kinds: come, radiate, go far

  3. Span an interval of distance, space or time.

    “The war extended over five years.” “The period covered the turn of the century.” “My land extends over the hills on the horizon.”

    Synonyms: cover, continue · A kind of: be · Kinds: cross, sweep, overlap, spread, ridge, dot

  4. Make available; provide.

    “Extend a loan.” “The bank offers a good deal on new mortgages.”

    Synonyms: offer · A kind of: supply

  5. Thrust or extend out.

    “He held out his hand.” “Point a finger.” “Extend a hand.”

    Synonyms: exsert, stretch out, put out, hold out, stretch forth · A kind of: gesticulate · Kinds: hyperextend

  6. Reach outward in space.

    “The awning extends several feet over the sidewalk.”

    Synonyms: poke out, reach out · A kind of: be

  7. Offer verbally.

    “Extend my greetings.” “He offered his sympathy.”

    Synonyms: offer · A kind of: give

  8. Extend one's limbs or muscles, or the entire body.

    “Stretch your legs!” “Extend your right arm above your head.”

    Synonyms: stretch · A kind of: tense · Kinds: spread-eagle, crane

  9. Expand the influence of.

    “The King extended his rule to the Eastern part of the continent.”

    Synonyms: expand · A kind of: change

  10. Lengthen in time; cause to be or last longer.

    “We prolonged our stay.” “She extended her visit by another day.” “The meeting was drawn out until midnight.”

    Synonyms: prolong, protract, draw out · A kind of: lengthen · Kinds: extend, temporize, spin

  11. Extend or stretch out to a greater or the full length.

    “Unfold the newspaper.” “Stretch out that piece of cloth.” “Extend the TV antenna.”

    Synonyms: unfold, stretch, stretch out · A kind of: change shape

  12. Cause to move at full gallop.

    “Did you gallop the horse just now?”

    Synonyms: gallop · A kind of: ride

  13. Open or straighten out; unbend.

    “Can we extend the legs of this dining table?”

    A kind of: straighten

  14. Use to the utmost; exert vigorously or to full capacity.

    “He really extended himself when he climbed Kilimanjaro.” “Don't strain your mind too much.”

    Synonyms: strain · A kind of: use · Kinds: overstrain, tax

Phrases and compounds with "extend"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • extend to (verb) To extend as far as.
  • extend oneself (verb) Strain to the utmost.

Word family

Words built from the same root as "extend": extensive, extension, extent, extensible, extendible, extensor. Learning a family together is quicker than learning each word alone.
