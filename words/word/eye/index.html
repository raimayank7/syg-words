Eye: meaning, synonyms and examples

Eye is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The organ of sight.

In Hindi
आँख
Synonyms
oculus, optic, center, centre, middle, heart, eyeball
Word family
eyelet

Eye as a noun

  1. The organ of sight.

    Synonyms: oculus, optic · A kind of: sense organ · Kinds: peeper, oculus dexter, oculus sinister, simple eye, compound eye

  2. Good discernment (either visually or as if visually).

    “She has an eye for fresh talent.” “He has an artist's eye.”

    A kind of: sagacity

  3. Attention to what is seen.

    “He tried to catch her eye.”

    A kind of: attention

  4. An area that is approximately central within some larger region.

    “It is in the center of town.” “They ran forward into the heart of the struggle.” “They were in the eye of the storm.”

    Synonyms: center, centre, middle, heart · A kind of: area · Kinds: center stage, city center, storm center, financial center, hub, inner city, medical center, midfield

  5. A small hole or loop (as in a needle).

    “The thread wouldn't go through the eye.”

    A kind of: hole

Eye as a verb

  1. Look at.

    Synonyms: eyeball · A kind of: look

Phrases and compounds with "eye"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • eye cup (noun) A small vessel with a rim curved to fit the orbit of the eye; use to apply medicated or cleansing solution to the eyeball.
  • red-eye (verb) Travel on an overnight flight.
  • evil eye (noun) A look that is believed to have the power of inflicting harm.
  • eye bank (noun) A place for storing and preserving corneas that are obtained from human corpses immediately after death; used for corneal transplantation to patients with corneal defects.
  • eye mask (noun) A mask covering the upper part of the face but with holes for the eyes.
  • eye-drop (noun) A drop from an eye dropper.
  • black eye (noun) A swollen bruise caused by a blow to the eye.
  • cat's eye (noun) Any of various gems (as chrysoberyl or chalcedony) that reflect light when cut in a rounded shape.
  • cross-eye (noun) Strabismus in which one or both eyes turn inward toward the nose.
  • eye blink (noun) A reflex that closes and opens the eyes rapidly.
  • eye candy (noun) Visual images that are pleasing to see but are intellectually undemanding.
  • eye chart (noun) A chart that is read from a fixed distance; used as a test of vision.
  • eye of ra (noun) A lion-headed Egyptian goddess; typifies life-destroying power of the sun.
  • eye rhyme (noun) An imperfect rhyme (e.g., `love' and `move').
  • eye tooth (noun) One of the four pointed conical teeth (two in each jaw) located between the incisors and the premolars.
  • glass eye (noun) Prosthesis consisting of an artificial eye made of glass.
