Father: meaning, synonyms and examples

Father is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A male parent (also used as a term of address to your father).

Opposites

Father as a noun

  1. A male parent (also used as a term of address to your father).

    “His father was born in Atlanta.”

    Synonyms: male parent, begetter · Opposite: mother · A kind of: parent · Kinds: dad, father-in-law, old man, pater

  2. The founder of a family.

    “Keep the faith of our forefathers.”

    Synonyms: forefather, sire · A kind of: ancestor · Kinds: patriarch

  3. `Father' is a term of address for priests in some churches (especially the Roman Catholic Church or the Orthodox Catholic Church); `Padre' is frequently used in the military.

    A kind of: title

  4. A person who holds an important or distinguished position in some organization.

    “The tennis fathers ruled in her favor.” “The city fathers endorsed the proposal.”

    A kind of: leader

  5. God when considered as the first person in the Trinity.

    “Hear our prayers, Heavenly Father.”

  6. A person who founds or establishes some institution.

    “George Washington is the father of his country.”

    Synonyms: founder, beginner, founding father · A kind of: originator · Kinds: cofounder, colonizer, foundress

  7. The head of an organized crime family.

    Synonyms: don · A kind of: head

Father as a verb

  1. Make children.

    “Abraham begot Isaac.” “Men often father children but don't recognize them.”

    Synonyms: beget, get, engender, mother, sire, generate, bring forth · A kind of: make

Phrases and compounds with "father"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • father-god (noun) God when considered as the first person in the Trinity.
  • city father (noun) An important municipal official.
  • holy father (noun) The head of the Roman Catholic Church.
  • father brown (noun) A Catholic priest who was the hero of detective stories by G. K. Chesterton.
  • father figure (noun) A man who takes over all the functions of the real father.
  • father-figure (noun) A man (often a powerful or influential man) who arouses emotions usually felt for your real father and with whom you identify psychologically.
  • father-in-law (noun) The father of your spouse.
  • foster father (noun) A man who is a foster parent.
  • foster-father (noun) A man who is a foster parent.
  • pilgrim father (noun) One of the colonists from England who sailed to America on the Mayflower and founded the colony of Plymouth in New England in 1620.
  • father of radio (noun) United States electrical engineer who in 1907 patented the first triode vacuum tube, which made it possible to detect and amplify radio waves (1873-1961).
  • founding father (noun) A member of the Constitutional Convention that drafted the United States Constitution in 1787.
  • father christmas (noun) The legendary patron saint of children; an imaginary being who is thought to bring presents to children at Christmas.
  • father surrogate (noun) A man who takes over all the functions of the real father.
  • father of the submarine (noun) American inventor who in 1775 designed a man-propelled submarine that was ineffectual but subsequently earned him recognition as a submarine pioneer (1742-1824).
