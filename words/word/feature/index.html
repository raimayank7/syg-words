Feature: meaning, synonyms and examples

Feature is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A prominent attribute or aspect of something.

In Hindi
विशेषता
Synonyms
characteristic, lineament, feature film, feature article, feature of speech, have, sport, boast

Feature as a noun

  1. A prominent attribute or aspect of something.

    “The map showed roads and other features.” “Generosity is one of his best characteristics.”

    Synonyms: characteristic · A kind of: property · Kinds: invariant, aspect, attraction, badge, centerpiece, contour, excellence, external

  2. The characteristic parts of a person's face: eyes and nose and mouth and chin.

    “An expression of pleasure crossed his features.” “His lineaments were very regular.”

    Synonyms: lineament · A kind of: body part · Kinds: chin, brow, temple, cheek, jowl, jaw

  3. The principal (full-length) film in a program at a movie theater.

    “The feature tonight is `Casablanca'.”

    Synonyms: feature film · A kind of: movie

  4. A special or prominent article in a newspaper or magazine.

    “They ran a feature on retirement planning.”

    Synonyms: feature article · A kind of: article

  5. (linguistics) a distinctive characteristic of a linguistic unit that serves to distinguish it from other units of the same kind.

    Synonyms: feature of speech · A kind of: property

  6. An article of merchandise that is displayed or advertised more than other articles.

    A kind of: merchandise · Kinds: drawing card

Feature as a verb

  1. Have as a feature.

    “This restaurant features the most famous chefs in France.”

    Synonyms: have · Kinds: wear, wear, carry, carry, bear, give off, unite, star

  2. Wear or display in an ostentatious or proud manner.

    “She was sporting a new hat.”

    Synonyms: sport, boast · A kind of: have

Phrases and compounds with "feature"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • feature film (noun) The principal (full-length) film in a program at a movie theater.
  • double feature (noun) Two games instead of one (especially in baseball when the same two teams play two games on the same day).
  • safety feature (noun) Feature of an artifact that is added to insure a user's safety.
  • feature article (noun) A special or prominent article in a newspaper or magazine.
  • feature of speech (noun) (linguistics) a distinctive characteristic of a linguistic unit that serves to distinguish it from other units of the same kind.
  • attractive feature (noun) A characteristic that provides pleasure and attracts.
  • distinctive feature (noun) An odd or unusual characteristic.
  • psychological feature (noun) A feature of the mental life of a living organism.
