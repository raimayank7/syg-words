Feel: meaning, synonyms and examples

Feel is used as a verb (12 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Undergo an emotional sensation or be in a particular state of mind.

In Hindi
महसूस करना
Synonyms
experience, find, sense, finger, palpate, spirit, tone, feeling, flavor, flavour
Word family
feelings, feeling, feeler

Feel as a verb

  1. Undergo an emotional sensation or be in a particular state of mind.

    “She felt resentful.” “He felt regret.”

    Synonyms: experience · Kinds: incline, recapture, pride, smolder, harbor, cool off, anger, chafe

  2. Come to believe on the basis of emotion, intuitions, or indefinite grounds.

    “I feel that he doesn't like me.” “I find him to be obnoxious.” “I found the movie rather entertaining.”

    Synonyms: find · A kind of: reason

  3. Perceive by a physical sensation, e.g., coming from the skin or muscles.

    “He felt the wind.” “She felt an object brushing her arm.” “He felt his flesh crawl.”

    Synonyms: sense · A kind of: perceive

  4. Be conscious of a physical, mental, or emotional state.

    “My cold is gone--I feel fine today.” “She felt tired after the long hike.” “She felt sad after her loss.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: feel like a million, suffocate

  5. Have a feeling or perception about oneself in reaction to someone's behavior or attitude.

    “She felt small and insignificant.” “I made the students feel different about themselves.”

    A kind of: think

  6. Undergo passive experience of:"We felt the effects of inflation".

    “We felt the effects of inflation.” “Her fingers felt their way through the string quartet.” “She felt his contempt of her.”

    A kind of: experience

  7. Be felt or perceived in a certain way.

    “The ground feels shaky.” “The sheets feel soft.”

    A kind of: look · Kinds: crawl

  8. Grope or feel in search of something.

    “He felt for his wallet.”

    A kind of: search · Kinds: grope for

  9. Examine by touch.

    “Feel this soft cloth!” “The customer fingered the sweater.”

    Synonyms: finger

  10. Examine (a body part) by palpation.

    “The nurse palpated the patient's stomach.” “The runner felt her pulse.”

    Synonyms: palpate · A kind of: touch

  11. Find by testing or cautious exploration.

    “He felt his way around the dark room.”

    A kind of: find

  12. Produce a certain impression.

    “It feels nice to be home again.”

    A kind of: look

Feel as a noun

  1. An intuitive awareness.

    “He has a feel for animals.” “It's easy when you get the feel of it.”

    A kind of: awareness

  2. The general atmosphere of a place or situation and the effect that it has on people.

    “The feel of the city excited him.” “A clergyman improved the tone of the meeting.” “It had the smell of treason.”

    Synonyms: spirit, tone, feeling, flavor, flavour, look, smell · A kind of: atmosphere

  3. A property perceived by touch.

    Synonyms: tactile property · A kind of: property · Kinds: touch, texture

Phrases and compounds with "feel"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • feel for (verb) Share the suffering of.
  • feel out (verb) Try to learn someone's opinions and intentions.
  • feel like (verb) Have an inclination for something or some activity.
  • feel like a million (verb) Be in excellent health and spirits.
  • feel like a million dollars (verb) Be in excellent health and spirits.
