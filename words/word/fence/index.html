Fence: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fence is used as a noun (2 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A barrier that serves to enclose an area.

In Hindi
बाड़
Synonyms
fencing, fence in, wall, palisade, surround, argue, contend, debate
Word family
fencing, fencer

Fence as a noun

  1. A barrier that serves to enclose an area.

    Synonyms: fencing · A kind of: barrier · Kinds: backstop, chainlink fence, hedge, picket fence, rail fence, stone wall, wall, weir

  2. A dealer in stolen property.

    A kind of: trader

Fence as a verb

  1. Enclose with a fence.

    “We fenced in our yard.”

    Synonyms: fence in · A kind of: enclose

  2. Receive stolen goods.

    A kind of: receive

  3. Fight with fencing swords.

    A kind of: contend · Kinds: parry

  4. Surround with a wall in order to fortify.

    Synonyms: wall, palisade, fence in, surround · A kind of: protect · Kinds: stockade, circumvallate

  5. Have an argument about something.

    Synonyms: argue, contend, debate · A kind of: converse · Kinds: stickle, spar, quibble, quarrel, oppose

Phrases and compounds with "fence"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fence in (verb) Enclose with a fence.
  • fence line (noun) A boundary line created by a fence.
  • fence rail (noun) A rail that is split from a log.
  • rail fence (noun) A fence (usually made of split logs laid across each other at an angle).
  • sunk fence (noun) A ditch with one side being a retaining wall; used to divide lands without defacing the landscape.
  • worm fence (noun) Rail fence consisting of a zigzag of interlocking rails.
  • snake fence (noun) Rail fence consisting of a zigzag of interlocking rails.
  • fence lizard (noun) Spiny lizard often seen basking on fences in the United States and northern Mexico.
  • fence-sitter (noun) A person who won't take sides in a controversy.
  • on the fence (adjective) Characterized by indecision.
  • picket fence (noun) A fence made of upright pickets.
  • fence mending (noun) Social action to improve poor relations (especially in politics).
  • virginia fence (noun) Rail fence consisting of a zigzag of interlocking rails.
  • chainlink fence (noun) A fence of steel wires woven into a diamond pattern.
  • snake-rail fence (noun) Rail fence consisting of a zigzag of interlocking rails.
  • eastern fence lizard (noun) Small active lizard of United States and north to British Columbia.
