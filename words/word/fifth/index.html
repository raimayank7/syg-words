Fifth: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fifth is used as an adjective (1 sense) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Coming next after the fourth and just before the sixth in position.

In Hindi
पाँचवाँ
Synonyms
one-fifth, fifth part, twenty percent

Fifth as an adjective

  1. Coming next after the fourth and just before the sixth in position.

    Similar: ordinal

Fifth as a noun

  1. Position five in a countable series of things.

    “He was fifth out of several hundred runners.”

    A kind of: rank

  2. One part in five equal parts.

    Synonyms: one-fifth, fifth part, twenty percent · A kind of: common fraction

  3. The musical interval between one note and another five notes away from it.

    A kind of: interval
