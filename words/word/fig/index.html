Fig: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fig is used as a noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A diagram or picture illustrating textual material.

In Hindi
अंजीर; आकृति (figure का छोटा रूप)
Synonyms
figure, common fig, common fig tree

Fig as a noun

  1. A diagram or picture illustrating textual material.

    “The area covered can be seen from Figure 2.”

    Synonyms: figure · A kind of: illustration

  2. Mediterranean tree widely cultivated for its edible fruit.

    Synonyms: common fig, common fig tree · A kind of: fig tree · Kinds: caprifig

  3. Fleshy sweet pear-shaped yellowish or purple multiple fruit eaten fresh or preserved or dried.

    A kind of: edible fruit
