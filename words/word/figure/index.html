Figure: meaning, synonyms and examples

Figure is used as a noun (13 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A diagram or picture illustrating textual material.

In Hindi
आकृति; अंक, आँकड़ा
Synonyms
fig, human body, physical body, material body, soma, build, physique, anatomy, shape, bod
Opposites
ground
Word family
figuration, figurer, figuring

Figure as a noun

  1. A diagram or picture illustrating textual material.

    “The area covered can be seen from Figure 2.”

    Synonyms: fig · A kind of: illustration

  2. Alternative names for the body of a human being.

    “Leonardo studied the human body.” “He has a strong physique.” “The spirit is willing but the flesh is weak.”

    Synonyms: human body, physical body, material body, soma, build, physique, anatomy, shape · A kind of: body · Kinds: person, juvenile body, adult body, male body, female body

  3. One of the elements that collectively form a system of numeration.

    “0 and 1 are digits.”

    Synonyms: digit · A kind of: integer · Kinds: binary digit, octal digit, decimal digit, duodecimal digit, hexadecimal digit, significant digit, zero, one

  4. A model of a bodily form (especially of a person).

    “He made a figure of Santa Claus.”

    A kind of: model · Kinds: dummy, figurehead, figurine, puppet, snowman

  5. A well-known or notable person.

    “They studied all the great names in the history of France.” “She is an important figure in modern music.”

    Synonyms: name, public figure · A kind of: important person

  6. A combination of points and lines and planes that form a visible palpable shape.

    A kind of: shape · Kinds: equilateral, pencil, plane figure, solid figure, subfigure, parallel

  7. An amount of money expressed numerically.

    “A figure of $17 was suggested.”

    A kind of: sum

  8. The impression produced by a person.

    “He cut a fine figure.” “A heroic figure.”

    A kind of: impression

  9. The property possessed by a sum or total or indefinite quantity of units or individuals.

    “He had a number of chores to do.” “The number of parameters is small.” “The figure was about a thousand.”

    Synonyms: number · A kind of: amount · Kinds: numerousness, preponderance, innumerableness, majority, minority, fewness, roundness

  10. Language used in a figurative or nonliteral sense.

    Synonyms: trope, figure of speech, image · A kind of: rhetorical device · Kinds: conceit, irony, hyperbole, kenning, metaphor, metonymy, oxymoron, personification

  11. A unitary percept having structure and coherence that is the object of attention and that stands out against a ground.

    Opposite: ground · A kind of: percept

  12. A decorative or artistic work.

    “The coach had a design on the doors.”

    Synonyms: design, pattern · A kind of: decoration · Kinds: argyle, bear claw, damascene, decal, device, emblem, herringbone, linocut

  13. A predetermined set of movements in dancing or skating.

    “She made the best score on compulsory figures.”

    A kind of: maneuver · Kinds: figure eight, spread eagle

Figure as a verb

  1. Judge to be probable.

    Synonyms: calculate, estimate, reckon, count on, forecast · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: allow

  2. Be or play a part of or in.

    “Elections figure prominently in every government program.” “How do the elections figure in the current pattern of internal politics?”

    Synonyms: enter · A kind of: be

  3. Imagine; conceive of; see in one's mind.

    “I can't see him on horseback!” “I can see what will happen.” “I can see a risk in this strategy.”

    Synonyms: visualize, visualise, envision, project, fancy, see, picture, image · A kind of: imagine

  4. Make a mathematical calculation or computation.

    Synonyms: calculate, cipher, cypher, compute, work out, reckon · A kind of: reason · Kinds: quantize, extract, process, prorate, miscalculate, recalculate, average, factor

  5. Understand.

    “He didn't figure her.”

    A kind of: grok

Phrases and compounds with "figure"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • figure out (verb) Find the solution to (a problem or question) or understand the meaning of.
  • lay figure (noun) Dummy in the form of an artist's jointed model of the human body.
  • wax figure (noun) An effigy (usually of a famous person) made of wax.
  • figure loom (noun) A loom for weaving figured fabrics.
  • figure eight (noun) A knot having the shape of the numeral 8; tied in a rope that has been passed through a hole or pulley and that prevents the rope from coming loose.
  • figure skate (noun) An ice skate worn for figure skating; has a slightly curved blade and a row of jagged points at the front of the blade.
  • plane figure (noun) A two-dimensional shape.
  • solid figure (noun) A three-dimensional shape.
  • stick figure (noun) Drawing of a human or animal that represents the head by a circle and the rest of the body by straight lines.
  • three-figure (adjective) (of numbers) written with three figures.
  • father figure (noun) A man who takes over all the functions of the real father.
  • father-figure (noun) A man (often a powerful or influential man) who arouses emotions usually felt for your real father and with whom you identify psychologically.
  • mother figure (noun) A woman who evokes the feelings usually reserved for a mother.
  • public figure (noun) A well-known or notable person.
  • figure skating (noun) Ice skating where the skates trace outlines of selected figures.
  • figure of eight (noun) A knot having the shape of the numeral 8; tied in a rope that has been passed through a hole or pulley and that prevents the rope from coming loose.
