Fire: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fire is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: The event of something burning (often destructive).

In Hindi
आग; नौकरी से निकालना
Synonyms
firing, flame, flaming, ardor, ardour, fervor, fervour, fervency, fervidness, attack
Opposites
hire
Word family
fiery, firing

Fire as a noun

  1. The event of something burning (often destructive).

    “They lost everything in the fire.”

    A kind of: happening · Kinds: backfire, bonfire, brush fire, campfire, conflagration, forest fire, grassfire, smoulder

  2. The act of firing weapons or artillery at an enemy.

    “Hold your fire until you can see the whites of their eyes.” “They retreated in the face of withering enemy fire.”

    Synonyms: firing · Kinds: antiaircraft fire, barrage, broadside, fusillade, call fire, cover, concentrated fire, counterfire

  3. The process of combustion of inflammable materials producing heat and light and (often) smoke.

    “Fire was one of our ancestors' first discoveries.”

    Synonyms: flame, flaming · A kind of: combustion · Kinds: blaze, flare, ignition

  4. A fireplace in which a relatively small fire is burning.

    “They sat by the fire and talked.”

    A kind of: fireplace · Kinds: cookfire

  5. Once thought to be one of four elements composing the universe (Empedocles).

    A kind of: element

  6. Feelings of great warmth and intensity.

    “He spoke with great ardor.”

    Synonyms: ardor, ardour, fervor, fervour, fervency, fervidness · A kind of: passion · Kinds: zeal

  7. Fuel that is burning and is used as a means for cooking.

    “Put the kettle on the fire.” “Barbecue over an open fire.”

    A kind of: fuel

  8. A severe trial.

    “He went through fire and damnation.”

    A kind of: trial

  9. Intense adverse criticism.

    “Clinton directed his fire at the Republican Party.” “The government has come under attack.” “Don't give me any flak.”

    Synonyms: attack, flak, flack, blast · A kind of: criticism

Fire as a verb

  1. Start firing a weapon.

    Synonyms: open fire · A kind of: blast

  2. Cause to go off.

    “Fire a gun.” “Fire a bullet.”

    Synonyms: discharge · Kinds: pop, loose off, blast, fusillade

  3. Bake in a kiln so as to harden.

    “Fire pottery.”

    A kind of: bake

  4. Terminate the employment of; discharge from an office or position.

    “The boss fired his secretary today.” “The company terminated 25% of its workers.”

    Synonyms: displace, give notice, can, dismiss, give the axe, send away, sack, force out · Opposite: hire · A kind of: remove · Kinds: retire, pension off, clean out, furlough, squeeze out, dismiss

  5. Go off or discharge.

    “The gun fired.”

    Synonyms: discharge, go off

  6. Drive out or away by or as if by fire.

    “The soldiers were fired.” “Surrender fires the cold skepticism.”

    A kind of: chase away

  7. Call forth (emotions, feelings, and responses).

    “Arouse pity.” “Raise a smile.” “Evoke sympathy.”

    Synonyms: arouse, elicit, enkindle, kindle, evoke, raise, provoke · A kind of: make · Kinds: strike a chord, invite, draw, rekindle, infatuate, prick, inflame, stimulate

  8. Destroy by fire.

    “They burned the house and his diaries.”

    Synonyms: burn, burn down · A kind of: destroy · Kinds: backfire, cremate, torch, scorch

  9. Provide with fuel.

    “Oil fires the furnace.”

    Synonyms: fuel · A kind of: supply

Phrases and compounds with "fire"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fire up (verb) Arouse or excite feelings and passions.
  • on fire (adjective) Lighted up by or as by fire or flame.
  • fire ant (noun) Omnivorous ant of tropical and subtropical America that can inflict a painful sting.
  • fire pit (noun) A pit whose floor is incandescent lava.
  • red fire (noun) Combustible material (usually salts of lithium or strontium) that burns bright red; used in flares and fireworks.
  • call fire (noun) Fire delivered on a specific target in response to a request from the supported unit.
  • fire bell (noun) A bell rung to give a fire alarm.
  • fire bush (noun) Evergreen South American shrub having showy trumpet-shaped orange flowers; grown as an ornamental or houseplant.
  • fire code (noun) Set of standards established and enforced by government for fire prevention and safety in case of fire as in fire escapes etc.
  • fire door (noun) A fire-resistant door that can be closed to stop the spread of a fire.
  • fire hook (noun) Fire iron consisting of a metal rod with a handle; used to stir a fire.
  • fire hose (noun) A large hose that carries water from a fire hydrant to the site of the fire.
  • fire iron (noun) Metal fireside implements.
  • fire opal (noun) An opal with flaming orange and yellow and red colors.
  • fire pink (noun) Perennial herb of eastern North America, having red flowers with narrow notched petals.
  • fire sale (noun) A sale of assets at very low prices typically when the seller faces bankruptcy.
