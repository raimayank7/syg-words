Fit: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fit is used as a verb (9 senses), adjective (3 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be agreeable or acceptable to.

In Hindi
ठीक बैठना; स्वस्थ
Synonyms
suit, accommodate, go, meet, conform to, match, correspond, check, jibe, gibe
Opposites
unfit
Word family
fitter, fitting, fitness

Fit as a verb

  1. Be agreeable or acceptable to.

    “This suits my needs.”

    Synonyms: suit, accommodate · A kind of: meet

  2. Be the right size or shape; fit correctly or as desired.

    “This piece won't fit into the puzzle.”

    Synonyms: go · A kind of: fit · Kinds: tessellate, joint

  3. Satisfy a condition or restriction.

    “Does this paper meet the requirements for the degree?”

    Synonyms: meet, conform to · A kind of: match · Kinds: coordinate, fit the bill, behoove, satisfy, suit

  4. Make fit.

    “Fit a dress.” “He fitted other pieces of paper to his cut-out.”

    A kind of: shape · Kinds: dovetail

  5. Insert or adjust several objects or people.

    “Can you fit the toy into the box?” “This man can't fit himself into our work environment.”

    A kind of: adapt

  6. Be compatible, similar or consistent; coincide in their characteristics.

    “The two stories don't agree in many details.” “The handwriting checks with the signature on the check.” “The suspect's fingerprints don't match those on the gun.”

    Synonyms: match, correspond, check, jibe, gibe, tally, agree · A kind of: equal · Kinds: consist, look, answer, coincide, align, correlate, parallel, twin

  7. Conform to some shape or size.

    “How does this shirt fit?”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: fit

  8. Provide with (something) usually for a specific purpose.

    “The expedition was equipped with proper clothing, food, and other necessities.”

    Synonyms: equip, fit out, outfit · A kind of: supply · Kinds: rig, mechanize, fuse, collar, spur, fin, motorize, motorize

  9. Make correspond or harmonize.

    “Match my sweater.”

    Synonyms: match · A kind of: adjust · Kinds: balance

Fit as an adjective

  1. Meeting adequate standards for a purpose.

    “A fit subject for discussion.” “It is fit and proper that you be there.” “Water fit to drink.”

    Opposite: unfit · Similar: acceptable, suitable, worthy

  2. (usually followed by `to' or `for') on the point of or strongly disposed.

    “In no fit state to continue.” “Fit to drop.” “Laughing fit to burst.”

    Synonyms: primed, set · Similar: ready

  3. Physically and mentally sound or healthy.

    “Felt relaxed and fit after their holiday.” “Keeps fit with diet and exercise.”

    Opposite: unfit · Similar: able, conditioned

Fit as a noun

  1. A display of bad temper.

    “He had a fit.” “She threw a tantrum.” “He made a scene.”

    Synonyms: tantrum, scene, conniption · A kind of: bad temper

  2. A sudden uncontrollable attack.

    “A paroxysm of giggling.” “A fit of coughing.” “Convulsions of laughter.”

    Synonyms: paroxysm, convulsion · A kind of: attack

  3. The manner in which something fits.

    “I admired the fit of her coat.”

    A kind of: manner · Kinds: hang

  4. A sudden flurry of activity (often for no obvious reason).

    “A burst of applause.” “A fit of housecleaning.”

    Synonyms: burst · A kind of: activity · Kinds: fits and starts

Phrases and compounds with "fit"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fit in (verb) Go together.
  • fit out (verb) Provide with (something) usually for a specific purpose.
  • have a fit (verb) Get very angry and fly into a rage.
  • throw a fit (verb) Get very angry and fly into a rage.
  • fit the bill (verb) Be what is needed or be good enough for what is required.
