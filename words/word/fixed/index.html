Fixed: meaning, synonyms and examples

Fixed is used as an adjective (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: (of a number) having a fixed and unchanging value.

In Hindi
तय, स्थिर
Synonyms
set, rigid, frozen
Opposites
unfixed
Word family
fixedness, fixity

Fixed as an adjective

  1. (of a number) having a fixed and unchanging value.

    Similar: determinate

  2. Fixed and unmoving.

    “With eyes set in a fixed glassy stare.” “His bearded face already has a set hollow look.” “A face rigid with pain.”

    Synonyms: set, rigid · Similar: nonmoving

  3. Securely placed or fastened or set.

    “A fixed piece of wood.” “A fixed resistor.”

    Opposite: unfixed · Similar: fast, geostationary, geosynchronous, leaded, stationary, taped

  4. Incapable of being changed or moved or undone; e.g. "frozen prices".

    “Frozen prices.” “Living on fixed incomes.”

    Synonyms: frozen · Similar: unchangeable

Phrases and compounds with "fixed"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fixed oil (noun) Nonvolatile animal or plant oil.
  • fixed cost (noun) A periodic charge that does not vary with business volume (as insurance or rent or mortgage payments etc.).
  • fixed disk (noun) A rigid magnetic disk mounted permanently in a drive unit.
  • fixed star (noun) Any star in the Ptolemaic theory of planetary motion.
  • well-fixed (adjective) In fortunate circumstances financially; moderately rich.
  • fixed costs (noun) A periodic charge that does not vary with business volume (as insurance or rent or mortgage payments etc.).
  • fixed charge (noun) A periodic charge that does not vary with business volume (as insurance or rent or mortgage payments etc.).
  • fixed storage (noun) (computer science) memory whose contents can be accessed and read but cannot be changed.
  • fixed phagocyte (noun) A phagocyte that does not circulate in the blood but is fixed in the liver or spleen or bone marrow etc.
  • fixed intonation (noun) The intonation of keyboard instruments where the pitch of each note is fixed and cannot be varied by the performer.
  • fixed-point part (noun) The positive fractional part of the representation of a logarithm; in the expression log 643 = 2.808 the mantissa is .808.
  • fixed-width font (noun) A typeface is which each character is given the same width (as by a typewriter).
  • fixed-point number (noun) A number represented in fixed-point notation.
  • fixed-point notation (noun) A radix numeration system in which the location of the decimal point is fixed by convention.
  • fixed-cycle operation (noun) An operation that is completed in a specified number of regularly timed execution cycles.
  • fixed investment trust (noun) An investment trust that can buy only those securities listed when the trust was organized.
