Flag: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flag is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Emblem usually consisting of a rectangular piece of cloth of distinctive design.

In Hindi
झंडा
Synonyms
masthead, iris, fleur-de-lis, sword lily, signal flag, pin, flagstone, sag, droop, swag

Flag as a noun

  1. Emblem usually consisting of a rectangular piece of cloth of distinctive design.

    A kind of: emblem · Kinds: banner, battle flag, black flag, colors, fanion, jack, pennant, standard

  2. A listing printed in all issues of a newspaper or magazine (usually on the editorial page) that gives the name of the publication and the names of the editorial staff, etc.

    Synonyms: masthead · A kind of: list

  3. Plants with sword-shaped leaves and erect stalks bearing bright-colored flowers composed of three petals and three drooping sepals.

    Synonyms: iris, fleur-de-lis, sword lily · A kind of: iridaceous plant · Kinds: bearded iris, beardless iris, bulbous iris, dwarf iris, stinking iris, yellow iris, dwarf iris, blue flag

  4. A rectangular piece of fabric used as a signalling device.

    Synonyms: signal flag · A kind of: visual signal · Kinds: pennant, code flag, red flag

  5. Flagpole used to mark the position of the hole on a golf green.

    Synonyms: pin · A kind of: golf equipment

  6. Stratified stone that splits into pieces suitable as paving stones.

    Synonyms: flagstone · A kind of: paving stone

  7. A conspicuously marked or shaped tail.

    A kind of: tail

Flag as a verb

  1. Communicate or signal with a flag.

    A kind of: sign

  2. Provide with a flag.

    “Flag this file so that I can recognize it immediately.”

    A kind of: mark

  3. Droop, sink, or settle from or as if from pressure or loss of tautness.

    Synonyms: sag, droop, swag · A kind of: sink · Kinds: slump, bag

  4. Decorate with flags.

    “The building was flagged for the holiday.”

    A kind of: decorate

  5. Become less intense.

    Synonyms: ease up, ease off, slacken off · A kind of: decrease

  • flag rank (noun) The rank of a flag officer.
  • flag smut (noun) Smut affecting leaves and stems of cereals and other grasses.
  • flag stop (noun) A small railway station between the principal stations or a station where the train stops only on a signal.
  • soft flag (noun) Reed maces of America, Europe, North Africa, Asia.
  • black flag (noun) A flag usually bearing a white skull and crossbones on a black background; indicates a pirate ship.
  • flag-waver (noun) An extreme bellicose nationalist.

  • battle flag (noun) A flag that leads troops into battle.
  • flag waving (noun) An appeal intended to arouse patriotic emotions.
