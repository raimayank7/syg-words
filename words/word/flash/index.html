Flash: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flash is used as a verb (8 senses), noun (10 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Gleam or glow intermittently.

In Hindi
चमक; कौंधना
Synonyms
blink, wink, twinkle, winkle, flaunt, show off, ostentate, swank, dart, dash
Word family
flasher, flashing, flashy

Flash as a verb

  1. Gleam or glow intermittently.

    “The lights were flashing.”

    Synonyms: blink, wink, twinkle, winkle · A kind of: radiate · Kinds: flicker

  2. Appear briefly.

    “The headlines flashed on the screen.”

    A kind of: appear

  3. Display proudly; act ostentatiously or pretentiously.

    “He showed off his new sports car.”

    Synonyms: flaunt, show off, ostentate, swank · A kind of: expose · Kinds: flex, splurge

  4. Make known or cause to appear with great speed.

    “The latest intelligence is flashed to all command posts.”

    A kind of: convey

  5. Run or move very quickly or hastily.

    “She dashed into the yard.”

    Synonyms: dart, dash, scoot, scud, shoot · A kind of: rush · Kinds: plunge

  6. Expose or show briefly.

    “He flashed a $100 bill.”

    A kind of: show

  7. Protect by covering with a thin sheet of metal.

    “Flash the roof.”

    A kind of: cover

  8. Emit a brief burst of light.

    “A shooting star flashed and was gone.”

    A kind of: appear

Flash as a noun

  1. A sudden intense burst of radiant energy.

    A kind of: happening · Kinds: flicker, gleam, glitter, heat flash, lightning, streak

  2. A momentary brightness.

    A kind of: brightness

  3. A short vivid experience.

    “A flash of emotion swept over him.” “The flashings of pain were a warning.”

    Synonyms: flashing · A kind of: experience

  4. A sudden brilliant understanding.

    “He had a flash of intuition.”

    A kind of: insight

  5. A very short time (as the time it takes the eye to blink or the heart to beat).

    “If I had the chance I'd do it in a flash.”

    Synonyms: blink of an eye, heartbeat, instant, jiffy, split second, trice, twinkling, wink · A kind of: moment

  6. A gaudy outward display.

    Synonyms: ostentation, fanfare · A kind of: display · Kinds: bravado, exhibitionism, ritz, splurge, pedantry

  7. A burst of light used to communicate or illuminate.

    Synonyms: flare · A kind of: visual signal · Kinds: star shell

  8. A short news announcement concerning some on-going news story.

    Synonyms: news bulletin, newsflash, newsbreak · A kind of: bulletin

  9. A bright patch of color used for decoration or identification.

    “Red flashes adorned the airplane.” “A flash sewn on his sleeve indicated the unit he belonged to.”

    A kind of: patch

  10. A lamp for providing momentary light to take a photograph.

    Synonyms: photoflash, flash lamp, flashgun, flashbulb, flash bulb · A kind of: lamp, photographic equipment

Flash as an adjective

  1. Tastelessly showy.

    “A flash car.” “A flashy ring.” “Garish colors.”

    Synonyms: brassy, cheap, flashy, garish, gaudy, gimcrack, loud, meretricious · Similar: tasteless

Phrases and compounds with "flash"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • hot flash (noun) Sudden brief sensation of heat (associated with menopause and some mental disorders).
  • flash back (verb) Return in time.
  • flash bulb (noun) A lamp for providing momentary light to take a photograph.
  • flash card (noun) A card with words or numbers or pictures that is flashed to a class by the teacher.
  • flash lamp (noun) A lamp for providing momentary light to take a photograph.
  • heat flash (noun) A flash of intense heat (as released by an atomic explosion).
  • in a flash (adverb) Without any delay.
  • flash flood (noun) A sudden local flood of great volume and short duration.
  • flash point (noun) Point at which something is ready to blow up.
  • flash camera (noun) A camera with a photoflash attachment.
  • flash memory (noun) Nonvolatile storage that can be electrically erased and programmed anew.
  • flash-freeze (verb) Freeze rapidly so as to preserve the natural juices and flavors.
  • flash-frozen (adjective) (used of foods) preserved by freezing sufficiently rapidly to retain flavor and nutritional value.
  • flash welding (noun) Butt welding by creating an electric arc between the two pieces which melts and joins them; used for joining segments of metal pipe.
  • flash-forward (noun) A transition (in literary or theatrical works or films) to a later event or scene that interrupts the normal chronological development of the story.
  • shoulder flash (noun) Something worn on the shoulder of a military uniform as an emblem of a division etc.
