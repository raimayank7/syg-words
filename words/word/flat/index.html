Flat: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flat is used as an adjective (14 senses), noun (7 senses) and adverb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Having a surface without slope, tilt in which no part is higher or lower than another.

In Hindi
समतल, चपटा; फ़्लैट
Synonyms
level, plane, categoric, categorical, unconditional, prostrate, compressed, bland, flavorless, flavourless
Opposites
contrasty, natural, sharp
Word family
flatness, flatlet

Flat as an adjective

  1. Having a surface without slope, tilt in which no part is higher or lower than another.

    “A flat desk.” “Acres of level farmland.” “A plane surface.”

    Synonyms: level, plane · Similar: even

  2. Having a relatively broad surface in relation to depth or thickness.

    “Flat computer monitors.”

    Similar: planar

  3. Not modified or restricted by reservations.

    “A categorical denial.” “A flat refusal.”

    Synonyms: categoric, categorical, unconditional · Similar: unqualified

  4. Stretched out and lying at full length along the ground.

    “Found himself lying flat on the floor.”

    Synonyms: prostrate · Similar: unerect

  5. Lacking contrast or shading between tones.

    Opposite: contrasty

  6. (of a musical note) lowered in pitch by one chromatic semitone.

    “B flat.”

    Opposite: natural, sharp

  7. Flattened laterally along the whole length (e.g., certain leafstalks or flatfishes).

    Synonyms: compressed · Similar: thin

  8. Lacking taste or flavor or tang.

    “A bland diet.” “Insipid hospital food.” “Flavorless supermarket tomatoes.”

    Synonyms: bland, flavorless, flavourless, insipid, savorless, savourless, vapid · Similar: tasteless

  9. Lacking stimulating characteristics; uninteresting.

    “A bland little drama.” “A flat joke.”

    Synonyms: bland · Similar: unstimulating

  10. Having lost effervescence.

    “A flat cola.”

    Similar: noneffervescent

  11. Sounded or spoken in a tone unvarying in pitch.

    “The owl's faint monotonous hooting.”

    Synonyms: monotone, monotonic, monotonous · Similar: unmodulated

  12. Horizontally level.

    “A flat roof.”

    Similar: horizontal

  13. Lacking the expected range or depth; not designed to give an illusion or depth.

    “A film with two-dimensional characters.” “A flat two-dimensional painting.”

    Synonyms: two-dimensional · Similar: multidimensional

  14. Not reflecting light; not glossy.

    “Flat wall paint.” “A photograph with a matte finish.”

    Synonyms: mat, matt, matte, matted · Similar: dull

Flat as a noun

  1. A level tract of land.

    “The salt flats of Utah.”

    A kind of: plain · Kinds: alluvial flat, salt flat

  2. A shallow box in which seedlings are started.

    A kind of: box

  3. A musical notation indicating one half step lower than the note named.

    A kind of: musical notation · Kinds: double flat

  4. Freight car without permanent sides or roof.

    Synonyms: flatcar, flatbed · A kind of: freight car

  5. A deflated pneumatic tire.

    Synonyms: flat tire · A kind of: pneumatic tire

  6. Scenery consisting of a wooden frame covered with painted canvas; part of a stage setting.

    A kind of: scenery · Kinds: coulisse, tormenter

  7. A suite of rooms usually on one floor of an apartment house.

    Synonyms: apartment · A kind of: housing · Kinds: bedsitting room, cold-water flat, duplex apartment, efficiency apartment, flatlet, maisonette, penthouse, railroad flat

Flat as an adverb

  1. With flat sails.

    “Sail flat against the wind.”

  2. In a forthright manner; candidly or frankly.

    “He didn't answer directly.” “Told me straight out.” “Came out flat for less work and more pay.”

    Synonyms: directly, straight

  • flat-hat (verb) Fly very close to the ground.
  • flat-top (adjective) Having a flat or flattened upper surface.
  • mud flat (noun) A tract of low muddy land near an estuary; covered at high tide and exposed at low tide.
  • fall flat (verb) Fail utterly; collapse.
  • flat arch (noun) An arch with mutually supporting voussoirs that has a straight horizontal extrados and intrados.
  • flat bone (noun) Part of the sirloin next to the wedge bone.
  • flat coat (noun) The first or preliminary coat of paint or size applied to a surface.
  • flat file (noun) A file with two flat surfaces.
  • flat knot (noun) A square knot used in a reef line.
  • flat tire (noun) A deflated pneumatic tire.
  • flat wash (noun) Ironing that can be done mechanically.
  • salt flat (noun) A flat expanse of salt left by the evaporation of a body of salt water.
  • wing flat (noun) A flat situated in the wings.
