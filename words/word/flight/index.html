Flight: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flight is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A formation of aircraft in flight.

In Hindi
उड़ान
Synonyms
flying, flight of stairs, flight of steps, escape, trajectory, fledge
Word family
flee

Flight as a noun

  1. A formation of aircraft in flight.

    A kind of: formation

  2. An instance of traveling by air.

    “Flying was still an exciting adventure for him.”

    Synonyms: flying · A kind of: air travel · Kinds: acrobatics, blind flying, ballooning, flyover, glide, maiden flight, overflight, pass

  3. A stairway (set of steps) between one floor or landing and the next.

    Synonyms: flight of stairs, flight of steps · A kind of: stairway

  4. The act of escaping physically.

    “He made his escape from the mental hospital.” “The canary escaped from its cage.” “His flight was an indication of his guilt.”

    Synonyms: escape · A kind of: running away · Kinds: evasion, break, getaway, exodus, skedaddle

  5. An air force unit smaller than a squadron.

    A kind of: air unit

  6. Passing above and beyond ordinary bounds.

    “A flight of fancy.” “Flights of rhetoric.” “Flights of imagination.”

    A kind of: creativity

  7. The path followed by an object moving through space.

    Synonyms: trajectory · A kind of: mechanical phenomenon · Kinds: ballistics, gravity-assist

  8. A flock of flying birds.

    A kind of: flock

  9. A scheduled trip by plane between designated airports.

    “I took the noon flight to Chicago.”

    A kind of: trip · Kinds: connecting flight, direct flight, domestic flight, international flight, nonstop flight, redeye

Flight as a verb

  1. Shoot a bird in flight.

  2. Fly in a flock.

    “Flighting wild geese.”

    A kind of: fly

  3. Decorate with feathers.

    “Fledge an arrow.”

    Synonyms: fledge · A kind of: decorate

Phrases and compounds with "flight"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in flight (adverb) Flying through the air.
  • top-flight (adjective) Excellent; best possible.
  • flight deck (noun) The upper deck of an aircraft carrier; used as a runway.
  • flight line (noun) Place where airplanes are parked and the maintenance hangars (but not the runways or taxiways).
  • flight path (noun) The path of a rocket or projectile or aircraft through the air.
  • take flight (verb) Run away quickly.
  • flight strip (noun) An airfield without normal airport facilities.
  • direct flight (noun) A flight with one or more intermediate stops but no change of aircraft.
  • maiden flight (noun) The first flight of its kind.
  • redeye flight (noun) A night flight from which the passengers emerge with eyes red from lack of sleep.
  • flight control (noun) Control from ground stations of airplanes in flight by means of messages transmitted to the pilot electronically.
  • flight feather (noun) Any of the larger wing or tail feathers of a bird.
  • flight surgeon (noun) A medical officer specializing in aviation medicine.
  • line of flight (noun) The path along which a freely moving object travels through the air.
  • nonstop flight (noun) A flight made without intermediate stops between source and destination.
  • terrain flight (noun) Flight at very low altitudes.
