Flow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flow is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: The motion characteristic of fluids (liquids or gases).

In Hindi
बहना; प्रवाह
Synonyms
flowing, flow rate, rate of flow, stream, current, menstruation, menses, menstruum, catamenia, period
Word family
flowing, flowage

Flow as a noun

  1. The motion characteristic of fluids (liquids or gases).

    Synonyms: flowing · A kind of: change of location · Kinds: fountain, ebb, backflow, airflow, current, freshet, overflow, dripping

  2. The amount of fluid that flows in a given time.

    Synonyms: flow rate, rate of flow · A kind of: rate · Kinds: cardiac output

  3. The act of flowing or streaming; continuous progression.

    Synonyms: stream · A kind of: motion · Kinds: spill, flood

  4. Any uninterrupted stream or discharge.

    A kind of: natural process · Kinds: slipstream, turbulent flow, streamline flow, filling, flowage, inflow, outflow

  5. Something that resembles a flowing stream in moving continuously.

    “A stream of people emptied from the terminal.” “The museum had planned carefully for the flow of visitors.”

    Synonyms: stream · A kind of: motion

  6. Dominant course (suggestive of running water) of successive events or ideas.

    “Two streams of development run through American history.” “Stream of consciousness.” “The flow of thought.”

    Synonyms: stream, current · A kind of: course

  7. The monthly discharge of blood from the uterus of nonpregnant women from puberty to menopause.

    “The women were sickly and subject to excessive menstruation.” “A woman does not take the gout unless her menses be stopped.” “The semen begins to appear in males and to be emitted at the same time of life that the catamenia begin to flow in females.”

    Synonyms: menstruation, menses, menstruum, catamenia, period · A kind of: discharge · Kinds: menorrhagia, oligomenorrhea

Flow as a verb

  1. Move or progress freely as if in a stream.

    “The crowd flowed out of the stadium.”

    Synonyms: flux · A kind of: move · Kinds: ripple, transpire

  2. Move along, of liquids.

    “Water flowed into the cave.” “The Missouri feeds into the Mississippi.”

    Synonyms: run, feed, course · A kind of: move · Kinds: flush, jet, tide, circulate, eddy, waste, run down, pour

  3. Cause to flow.

    “The artist flowed the washes on the paper.”

  4. Be abundantly present.

    “The champagne flowed at the wedding.”

    A kind of: exist

  5. Fall or flow in a certain way.

    “This dress hangs well.” “Her long black hair flowed down her back.”

    Synonyms: hang, fall

  6. Cover or swamp with water.

    A kind of: flood · Kinds: lave

  7. Undergo menstruation.

    “She started menstruating at the age of 11.”

    Synonyms: menstruate · A kind of: shed blood
