Flower: meaning, synonyms and examples

Flower is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A plant cultivated for its blooms or blossoms.

In Hindi
फूल
Synonyms
bloom, blossom, prime, peak, heyday, efflorescence, flush
Word family
flowery, flowering

Flower as a noun

  1. A plant cultivated for its blooms or blossoms.

    A kind of: angiosperm · Kinds: bloomer, peony, lesser celandine, pheasant's-eye, anemone, rue anemone, columbine, rocket larkspur

  2. Reproductive organ of angiosperm plants especially one having showy or colorful parts.

    Synonyms: bloom, blossom · A kind of: reproductive structure · Kinds: floret, apetalous flower, inflorescence, ray flower, bud, chrysanthemum

  3. The period of greatest prosperity or productivity.

    Synonyms: prime, peak, heyday, bloom, blossom, efflorescence, flush · A kind of: time period · Kinds: golden age

Flower as a verb

  1. Produce or yield flowers.

    “The cherry tree bloomed.”

    Synonyms: bloom, blossom · A kind of: develop · Kinds: effloresce

Phrases and compounds with "flower"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • flower bed (noun) A bed in which flowers are growing.
  • flower bud (noun) A bud from which only a flower or flowers develop.
  • new flower (noun) The capital of Ethiopia and the country's largest city; located in central Ethiopia.
  • ray flower (noun) Small flower with a flat strap-shaped corolla usually occupying the peripheral rings of a composite flower.
  • flower girl (noun) A woman who sells flowers in the street.
  • flower head (noun) A shortened compact cluster of flowers so arranged that the whole gives the effect of a single flower as in clover or members of the family Compositae.
  • gand flower (noun) Small European perennial with numerous branches having racemes of blue, pink or white flowers; formerly reputed to promote human lactation.
  • lyre-flower (noun) Garden plant having deep-pink drooping heart-shaped flowers.
  • mist-flower (noun) Rhizomatous plant of central and southeastern United States and West Indies having large showy heads of clear blue flowers; sometimes placed in genus Eupatorium.
  • shad-flower (noun) Annual weed of Europe and North America having a rosette of basal leaves and tiny flowers followed by oblong seed capsules.
  • swan-flower (noun) Any of several orchids of the genus Cycnoches having slender arching columns of flowers suggesting the neck of a swan.
  • tail-flower (noun) Any of various tropical American plants cultivated for their showy foliage and flowers.
  • wild flower (noun) Wild or uncultivated flowering plant.
  • blood flower (noun) Tropical herb having orange-red flowers followed by pods suggesting a swallow with outspread wings; a weed throughout the tropics.
  • crepe flower (noun) Ornamental shrub from eastern India commonly planted in the southern United States.
  • flame flower (noun) A plant of the genus Kniphofia having long grasslike leaves and tall scapes of red or yellow drooping flowers.
