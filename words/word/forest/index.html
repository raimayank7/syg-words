Forest: meaning, synonyms and examples

Forest is used as a noun (2 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The trees and other plants in a large densely wooded area.

In Hindi
जंगल, वन
Synonyms
wood, woods, woodland, timberland, timber, afforest
Word family
forestry

Forest as a noun

  1. The trees and other plants in a large densely wooded area.

    Synonyms: wood, woods · A kind of: vegetation · Kinds: bosk, grove, jungle, rain forest, old growth, second growth

  2. Land that is covered with trees and shrubs.

    Synonyms: woodland, timberland, timber · A kind of: land, biome · Kinds: greenwood, riparian forest, silva, tree farm

Forest as a verb

  1. Establish a forest on previously unforested land.

    “Afforest the mountains.”

    Synonyms: afforest · A kind of: plant · Kinds: re-afforest, reforest
