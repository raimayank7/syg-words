Forward: meaning, synonyms and examples

Forward is used as an adverb (5 senses), adjective (4 senses), verb (1 sense) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: At or to or toward the front.

In Hindi
आगे
Synonyms
forwards, frontward, frontwards, forrad, forrard, forth, onward, ahead, onwards, forrader
Opposites
backward, reverse
Word family
forwardness, forwarding

Forward as an adverb

  1. At or to or toward the front.

    “He faced forward.” “Step forward.” “She practiced sewing backward as well as frontward on her new sewing machine.”

    Synonyms: forwards, frontward, frontwards, forrad, forrard · Opposite: backward

  2. Forward in time or order or degree.

    “From that time forth.” “From the sixth century onward.”

    Synonyms: forth, onward

  3. Toward the future; forward in time.

    “I like to look ahead in imagination to what the future may bring.” “I look forward to seeing you.”

    Synonyms: ahead · Opposite: backward

  4. In a forward direction.

    “Go ahead.” “The train moved ahead slowly.” “The boat lurched ahead.”

    Synonyms: ahead, onward, onwards, forwards, forrader

  5. Near or toward the bow of a ship or cockpit of a plane.

    “The captain went fore (or forward) to check the instruments.”

    Synonyms: fore

Forward as an adjective

  1. At or near or directed toward the front.

    “The forward section of the aircraft.” “A forward plunge down the stairs.” “Forward motion.”

    Opposite: backward · Similar: guardant, headfirst

  2. Used of temperament or behavior; lacking restraint or modesty.

    “A forward child badly in need of discipline.”

    Opposite: backward · Similar: overfamiliar, fresh, assumptive

  3. Of the transmission gear causing forward movement in a motor vehicle.

    “In a forward gear.”

    Opposite: reverse

  4. Moving forward.

    Synonyms: advancing, forward-moving · Similar: progressive

Forward as a verb

  1. Send or ship onward from an intermediate post or station in transit.

    “Forward my mail.”

    Synonyms: send on · A kind of: transport

Forward as a noun

  1. The person who plays the position of forward in certain games, such as basketball, soccer, or hockey.

    A kind of: basketball player

  2. A position on a basketball, soccer, or hockey team.

    A kind of: position

Phrases and compounds with "forward"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go forward (verb) Move ahead; travel onward in time or space.
  • put forward (verb) Put before.
  • come forward (verb) Make oneself visible; take action.
  • forward pass (noun) A pass to a receiver downfield from the passer.
  • look forward (verb) Expect or hope for.
  • push forward (verb) Push one's way.
  • step forward (verb) Make oneself visible; take action.
  • bring forward (verb) Cause to move forward.
  • carry forward (verb) Transfer from one time period to the next.
  • carry-forward (noun) The accumulated and undivided profits of a corporation after provision has been made for dividends and reserves.
  • flash-forward (noun) A transition (in literary or theatrical works or films) to a later event or scene that interrupts the normal chronological development of the story.
  • hunch forward (verb) Round one's back by bending forward and drawing the shoulders forward.
  • forward market (noun) A commodity exchange where futures contracts are traded.
  • forward motion (noun) The act of moving forward (as toward a goal).
  • forward passer (noun) (football) a ball carrier who tries to gain ground by throwing a forward pass.
  • forward-moving (adjective) Moving forward.
