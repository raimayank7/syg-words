Free: meaning, synonyms and examples

Free is used as an adjective (9 senses), verb (11 senses), noun (1 sense) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Able to act at will; not hampered; not under compulsion or restraint.

In Hindi
आज़ाद; मुफ़्त
Synonyms
complimentary, costless, gratis, gratuitous, detached, spare, barren, destitute, devoid, innocent
Opposites
unfree, bound, confine, obstruct
Word family
freeing

Free as an adjective

  1. Able to act at will; not hampered; not under compulsion or restraint.

    “Free enterprise.” “A free port.” “A free country.”

    Opposite: unfree · Similar: at large, autonomous, available, aweigh, clear, emancipated

  2. Unconstrained or not chemically bound in a molecule or not fixed and capable of relatively unrestricted motion.

    “Free expansion.” “Free oxygen.” “A free electron.”

    Opposite: bound · Similar: liberated, unbound

  3. Costing nothing.

    “Complimentary tickets.” “Free admission.”

    Synonyms: complimentary, costless, gratis, gratuitous · Similar: unpaid

  4. Not occupied or in use.

    “A free locker.” “A free lane.”

    Similar: unoccupied

  5. Not fixed in position.

    “The detached shutter fell on him.” “He pulled his arm free and ran.”

    Synonyms: detached · Similar: unfixed

  6. Not held in servitude.

    “After the Civil War he was a free man.”

    Opposite: unfree · Similar: freeborn

  7. Not taken up by scheduled activities.

    “A free hour between classes.” “Spare time on my hands.”

    Synonyms: spare · Similar: unoccupied

  8. Completely wanting or lacking.

    “Writing barren of insight.” “Young recruits destitute of experience.” “Innocent of literary merit.”

    Synonyms: barren, destitute, devoid, innocent · Similar: nonexistent

  9. Not literal.

    “A loose interpretation of what she had been told.” “A free translation of the poem.”

    Synonyms: loose, liberal · Similar: inexact

Free as a verb

  1. Grant freedom to; free from confinement.

    Synonyms: liberate, release, unloose, unloosen, loose · Opposite: confine · Kinds: unspell, unchain, bail, run, bail out, parole

  2. Relieve from.

    “Rid the house of pests.”

    Synonyms: rid, disembarrass · Kinds: cleanse, relieve, smooth, clear, disinfest, disembody

  3. Remove or force out from a position.

    “The dentist dislodged the piece of food that had been stuck under my gums.” “He finally could free the legs of the earthquake victim who was buried in the rubble.”

    Synonyms: dislodge · A kind of: remove

  4. Grant relief or an exemption from a rule or requirement to.

    “She exempted me from the exam.”

    Synonyms: exempt, relieve · Kinds: derestrict, deregulate, dispense, forgive, spare

  5. Make (information) available for publication.

    “Release the list with the names of the prisoners.”

    Synonyms: release · A kind of: issue · Kinds: declassify

  6. Free from obligations or duties.

    Synonyms: discharge · Kinds: divest, take over, cut, clear, cashier, liberate, disinvolve

  7. Free or remove obstruction from.

    “Free a path across the cluttered floor.”

    Synonyms: disengage · Opposite: obstruct · Kinds: dig out, unclog, unstuff, extricate

  8. Let off the hook.

    “I absolve you from this responsibility.”

    Synonyms: absolve, justify · A kind of: forgive · Kinds: excuse, wash one's hands

  9. Part with a possession or right.

    “I am relinquishing my bedroom to the long-term house guest.” “Resign a claim to the throne.”

    Synonyms: release, relinquish, resign, give up · A kind of: pass · Kinds: derequisition, sacrifice

  10. Release (gas or energy) as a result of a chemical reaction or physical decomposition.

    Synonyms: release, liberate · A kind of: generate

  11. Make (assets) available.

    “Release the holdings in the dictator's bank account.”

    Synonyms: unblock, unfreeze, release · A kind of: issue

Free as a noun

  1. People who are free.

    “The home of the free and the brave.”

    Synonyms: free people · A kind of: people

Free as an adverb

  1. Without restraint.

    “Cows in India are running loose.”

    Synonyms: loose

Phrases and compounds with "free"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fat-free (adjective) Without fat or fat solids.
  • for free (adverb) Without payment.
  • ice-free (adjective) Free of ice and open to travel.
  • set free (verb) Grant freedom to.
  • tax-free (adjective) (of goods or funds) not taxed.
  • cell-free (adjective) Lacking cells.
  • duty-free (adjective) Exempt from duty.
  • free fall (noun) The ideal falling motion of something subject only to a gravitational field.
  • free form (noun) A morpheme that can occur alone.
  • free hand (noun) Freedom to do as you see fit.
  • free kick (noun) (soccer) a place kick that is allowed for a foul or infringement by the other team.
  • free list (noun) A list of commodities that are not subject to tariffs.
  • free port (noun) An area adjoining a port where goods that are intended for reshipment can be received and stored without payment of duties.
  • free rein (noun) The removal of constraints.
  • free time (noun) Time available for hobbies and other activities that you enjoy.
  • free will (noun) The power of making free choices unconstrained by external agencies.
