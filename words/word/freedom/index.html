Freedom: meaning, synonyms and examples

Freedom is used as a noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The condition of being free; the power to act or speak or think without externally imposed restraints.

In Hindi
आज़ादी, स्वतंत्रता
Synonyms
exemption

Freedom as a noun

  1. The condition of being free; the power to act or speak or think without externally imposed restraints.

    A kind of: state · Kinds: academic freedom, enfranchisement, free hand, free rein, freedom of the seas, independence, liberty, civil liberty

  2. Immunity from an obligation or duty.

    Synonyms: exemption · A kind of: unsusceptibility · Kinds: amnesty, diplomatic immunity, indemnity, impunity, grandfather clause

Phrases and compounds with "freedom"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • freedom rider (noun) One of an interracial group of civil rights activists who rode buses through parts of the South in order to protest racial segregation.
  • freedom fighter (noun) A person who takes part in an armed rebellion against the constituted authority (especially in the hope of improving conditions).
  • academic freedom (noun) The freedom of teachers and students to express their ideas in school without religious or political or institutional restrictions.
  • degree of freedom (noun) (statistics) an unrestricted variable in a frequency distribution.
  • freedom of speech (noun) A civil right guaranteed by the First Amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom of thought (noun) The right to hold unpopular ideas.
  • freedom of assembly (noun) The right to peaceably assemble and to petition the government for redress of grievances; guaranteed by the First Amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom of religion (noun) A civil right guaranteed by the First Amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom of the seas (noun) The right of merchant ships to travel freely in international waters.
  • freedom of the press (noun) A right guaranteed by the First Amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom to bear arms (noun) A right guaranteed by the 2nd amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom from double jeopardy (noun) A civil right guaranteed by the Fifth Amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom from search and seizure (noun) A right guaranteed by the 4th amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom from self-incrimination (noun) The civil right (guaranteed by the Fifth Amendment to the United States Constitution) to refuse to answer questions or otherwise give testimony against yourself.
  • freedom from involuntary servitude (noun) A civil right guaranteed by the 13th amendment to the US Constitution.
  • freedom from cruel and unusual punishment (noun) A right guaranteed by the 8th amendment to the US Constitution.
