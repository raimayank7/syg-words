Glass: meaning, synonyms and examples

Glass is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A brittle transparent solid with irregular atomic structure.

In Hindi

Glass as a noun

  1. A brittle transparent solid with irregular atomic structure.

    A kind of: solid · Kinds: natural glass, opal glass, optical glass, crown glass, soft glass, ground glass, ground glass, lead glass

  2. A container for holding liquids while drinking.

    Synonyms: drinking glass · A kind of: container · Kinds: bumper, goblet, highball glass, liqueur glass, parfait glass, rummer, snifter, tumbler

  3. The quantity a glass will hold.

    Synonyms: glassful · A kind of: containerful

  4. A small refracting telescope.

    Synonyms: field glass, spyglass · A kind of: refracting telescope

  5. An amphetamine derivative (trade name Methedrine) used in the form of a crystalline hydrochloride; used as a stimulant to the nervous system and as an appetite suppressant.

    Synonyms: methamphetamine, methamphetamine hydrochloride, meth, deoxyephedrine, chalk, chicken feed, crank, ice · A kind of: amphetamine

  6. A mirror; usually a ladies' dressing mirror.

    Synonyms: looking glass · A kind of: mirror

  7. Glassware collectively.

    “She collected old glass.”

    A kind of: glassware

Glass as a verb

  1. Furnish with glass.

    “Glass the windows.”

    Synonyms: glaze · A kind of: supply · Kinds: double-glaze

  2. Scan (game in the forest) with binoculars.

    A kind of: scan

  3. Enclose with glass.

    “Glass in a porch.”

    Synonyms: glass in · A kind of: enclose

  4. Put in a glass container.

    A kind of: insert

  5. Become glassy or take on a glass-like appearance.

    “Her eyes glaze over when she is bored.”

    Synonyms: glaze, glass over, glaze over · A kind of: change

Phrases and compounds with "glass"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • glass in (verb) Enclose with glass.
  • cut glass (noun) Glass decorated by cutting or grinding facets.
  • glass eye (noun) Prosthesis consisting of an artificial eye made of glass.
  • bell glass (noun) A bell-shaped glass cover used to protect and display delicate objects or to cover scientific apparatus or to contain gases.
  • glass over (verb) Become glassy or take on a glass-like appearance.
  • glass wool (noun) Glass fibers spun and massed into bundles resembling wool.
  • glass-like (adjective) Resembling glass.
  • hand glass (noun) A mirror intended to be held in the hand.
  • lead glass (noun) Glass containing lead oxide; has a high refractive index.
  • milk glass (noun) A milky white translucent or opaque glass.
  • opal glass (noun) A milky white translucent or opaque glass.
  • pier glass (noun) A large mirror between two windows.
  • soft glass (noun) Glass having a relatively low softening point.
  • wire glass (noun) A glass that contains a layer of wire netting in it.
  • cover glass (noun) A small and very thin piece of glass used to cover the specimen on a microscope slide.
  • crown glass (noun) A glass blown into a globe which is later flattened and spun to form a disk.
