Glory: meaning, synonyms and examples

Glory is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A state of high honor.

In Hindi
गौरव, महिमा
Synonyms
glorification, resplendence, resplendency, aura, aureole, halo, nimbus, gloriole
Word family
glorious, glorify

Glory as a noun

  1. A state of high honor.

    “He valued glory above life itself.”

    Synonyms: glorification · A kind of: honor

  2. Brilliant radiant beauty.

    “The glory of the sunrise.”

    Synonyms: resplendence, resplendency · A kind of: beauty

  3. An indication of radiant light drawn around the head of a saint.

    Synonyms: aura, aureole, halo, nimbus, gloriole · A kind of: light

Glory as a verb

  1. Rejoice proudly.

    A kind of: exuberate

Phrases and compounds with "glory"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • glory pea (noun) Any of various shrubs or vines of the genus Clianthus having compound leaves and pea-like red flowers in drooping racemes.
  • old glory (noun) The national flag of the United States of America.
  • glory fern (noun) Named for a country house in Barbados where it was discovered.
  • glory hole (noun) A small locker at the stern of a boat or between decks of a ship.
  • glory lily (noun) Any plant of the genus Gloriosa of tropical Africa and Asia; a perennial herb climbing by means of tendrils at leaf tips having showy yellow to red or purple flowers; all parts are poisonous.
  • star-glory (noun) Tropical American annual climber having red (sometimes white) flowers and finely dissected leaves; naturalized in United States and elsewhere.
  • morning glory (noun) Any of various twining vines having funnel-shaped flowers that close late in the day.
  • red morning-glory (noun) Annual herb having scarlet flowers; the eastern United States.
  • wild morning-glory (noun) Common Eurasian and American wild climber with pink flowers; sometimes placed in genus Convolvulus.
  • beach morning glory (noun) A prostrate perennial of coastal sand dunes Florida to Texas.
  • common morning glory (noun) Annual or perennial climbing herb of Central America having sky-blue flowers; most commonly cultivated morning glory.
  • morning-glory family (noun) Morning glory; bindweed; sweet potato; plants having trumpet-shaped flowers and a climbing or twining habit.
  • japanese morning glory (noun) Annual Old World tropical climbing herb distinguished by wide color range and frilled or double flowers.
  • imperial japanese morning glory (noun) Hybrid from Ipomoea nil.
