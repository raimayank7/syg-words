Glow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Glow is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Emit a steady even light without flames.

In Hindi
चमक, दमक
Synonyms
beam, radiate, shine, burn, freshness, luminescence, incandescence, radiance, glowing, gleam

Glow as a verb

  1. Emit a steady even light without flames.

    “The fireflies were glowing and flying about in the garden.”

    A kind of: radiate · Kinds: fluoresce, flush

  2. Have a complexion with a strong bright color, such as red or pink.

    “Her face glowed when she came out of the sauna.”

    Synonyms: beam, radiate, shine · A kind of: look

  3. Shine intensely, as if with heat.

    “The coals were glowing in the dark.” “The candles were burning.”

    Synonyms: burn · A kind of: shine · Kinds: gutter

  4. Be exuberant or high-spirited.

    “Make the people's hearts glow.”

    A kind of: feel

  5. Experience a feeling of well-being or happiness, as from good health or an intense emotion.

    “She was beaming with joy.” “Her face radiated with happiness.”

    Synonyms: beam, radiate, shine · A kind of: feel

Glow as a noun

  1. An alert and refreshed state.

    Synonyms: freshness · A kind of: good health

  2. Light from nonthermal sources.

    Synonyms: luminescence · A kind of: luminosity

  3. The phenomenon of light emission by a body as its temperature is raised.

    Synonyms: incandescence · A kind of: light

  4. A feeling of considerable warmth.

    “The glow of new love.” “A glow of regret.”

    A kind of: feeling

  5. A steady even light without flames.

    A kind of: light · Kinds: sky glow

  6. The amount of electromagnetic radiation leaving or arriving at a point on a surface.

    Synonyms: radiance, glowing · A kind of: light · Kinds: aureole

  7. An appearance of reflected light.

    Synonyms: gleam, gleaming, lambency · A kind of: radiance

Phrases and compounds with "glow"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • sky glow (noun) Illumination of the night sky in urban areas.
  • glow lamp (noun) A gas-discharge tube with a hot cathode; used in stroboscopes.
  • glow tube (noun) A gas-discharge tube consisting of a cold cathode and a diode in a tube filled with gas; the color of the glow depends on the particular gas.
  • golden glow (noun) Very tall branching herb with showy much-doubled yellow flower heads.
  • electric glow (noun) An electrical discharge accompanied by ionization of surrounding atmosphere.
