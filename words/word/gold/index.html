Gold: meaning, synonyms and examples

Gold is used as a noun (5 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Coins made of gold.

In Hindi
सोना
Synonyms
amber, golden, gilded, aureate, gilt

Gold as a noun

  1. Coins made of gold.

    A kind of: precious metal

  2. A deep yellow color.

    “An amber light illuminated the room.” “He admired the gold of her hair.”

    Synonyms: amber · A kind of: yellow

  3. A soft yellow malleable ductile (trivalent and univalent) metallic element; occurs mainly as nuggets in rocks and alluvial deposits; does not react with most chemicals but is attacked by chlorine and aqua regia.

    A kind of: noble metal · Kinds: gold dust, green gold, guinea gold

  4. Great wealth.

    “Whilst that for which all virtue now is sold, and almost every vice--almighty gold.”

    A kind of: wealth

  5. Something likened to the metal in brightness or preciousness or superiority etc.

    “The child was as good as gold.” “She has a heart of gold.”

    A kind of: invaluableness

Gold as an adjective

  1. Made from or covered with gold.

    “Gold coins.” “The gold dome of the Capitol.” “The golden calf.”

    Synonyms: golden, gilded · Similar: metallic

  2. Having the deep slightly brownish color of gold.

    “Long aureate (or golden) hair.” “A gold carpet.”

    Synonyms: aureate, gilded, gilt, golden · Similar: chromatic

Phrases and compounds with "gold"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • old gold (noun) A dark yellow.
  • gold dust (noun) The particles and flakes (and sometimes small nuggets) of gold obtained in placer mining.
  • gold fern (noun) Fern of West Indies and South America having fronds with bright golden-yellow undersides.
  • gold foil (noun) Foil made of gold.
  • gold leaf (noun) A very thin form of gold foil.
  • gold mine (noun) A good source of something that is desired.
  • gold rush (noun) A sudden happening that brings good fortune (as a sudden opportunity to make money).
  • pure gold (noun) 100 per cent gold.
  • gold braid (noun) Trimming used to decorate clothes or curtains.
  • gold coast (noun) A republic in West Africa on the Gulf of Guinea.
  • gold fever (noun) Greed and the contagious excitement of a gold rush.
  • gold medal (noun) A trophy made of gold (or having the appearance of gold) that is usually awarded for winning first place in a competition.
  • gold miner (noun) A miner who digs or pans for gold in a gold field.
  • gold plate (noun) Tableware that is plated with gold.
  • gold-plate (verb) Plate with gold.
  • green gold (noun) A gold alloy (at least 14 karat gold with silver or silver and cadmium) that has a green appearance.
