Grade: meaning, synonyms and examples

Grade is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A body of students who are taught together.

In Hindi
कक्षा; श्रेणी, ग्रेड
Synonyms
class, form, course, level, tier, grad, gradation, mark, score, ground level
Word family
gradual, gradation, grader, grading

Grade as a noun

  1. A body of students who are taught together.

    “Early morning classes are always sleepy.”

    Synonyms: class, form, course · A kind of: gathering · Kinds: master class, section

  2. A relative position or degree of value in a graded group.

    “Lumber of the highest grade.”

    Synonyms: level, tier · A kind of: rank · Kinds: biosafety level, college level

  3. The gradient of a slope or road or other surface.

    “The road had a steep grade.”

    A kind of: gradient · Kinds: upgrade, downgrade

  4. One-hundredth of a right angle.

    Synonyms: grad · A kind of: angular unit

  5. A degree of ablaut.

    Synonyms: gradation · A kind of: ablaut

  6. A number or letter indicating quality (especially of a student's performance).

    “She made good marks in algebra.” “Grade A milk.” “What was your score on your homework?”

    Synonyms: mark, score · A kind of: evaluation · Kinds: grade point, percentile, decile, quartile

  7. The height of the ground on which something stands.

    “The base of the tower was below grade.”

    Synonyms: ground level · A kind of: elevation

  8. A position on a scale of intensity or amount or quality.

    “A moderate grade of intelligence.” “A high level of care is required.” “It is all a matter of degree.”

    Synonyms: degree, level · A kind of: property · Kinds: quality, intensity, grind, depth, highness, high, low, lowness

  9. A variety of cattle produced by crossbreeding with a superior breed.

Grade as a verb

  1. Assign a rank or rating to.

    “How would you rank these students?” “The restaurant is rated highly in the food guide.”

    Synonyms: rate, rank, range, order, place · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: superordinate, shortlist, seed, reorder, subordinate, prioritize, sequence, downgrade

  2. Level to the right gradient.

    A kind of: flush · Kinds: aggrade

  3. Assign a grade or rank to, according to one's evaluation.

    “Grade tests.” “Score the SAT essays.” “Mark homework.”

    Synonyms: score, mark · A kind of: measure

  4. Determine the grade of or assign a grade to.

    A kind of: classify

Phrases and compounds with "grade"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • low-grade (adjective) Of inferior quality.
  • top-grade (adjective) Surpassing in quality.
  • high-grade (adjective) Surpassing in quality.
  • grade point (noun) A numerical value assigned to a letter grade received in a course taken at a college or university multiplied by the number of credit hours awarded for the course.
  • grade school (noun) A school for young children; usually the first 6 or 8 grades.
  • junior-grade (adjective) Inferior in rank or status.
  • utility-grade (adjective) Used of beef; usable but inferior.
  • weapons-grade (adjective) Of a quality adequate for use in weapons (especially in weapons of mass destruction).
  • grade crossing (noun) Intersection of a railway and a road on the same level; barriers close road when trains pass.
  • grade insignia (noun) V-shaped sleeve badge indicating military rank and service.
  • technical grade (adjective) Containing small amounts of other chemicals, hence slightly impure.
  • technical-grade (adjective) Containing small amounts of other chemicals, hence slightly impure.
  • commercial-grade (adjective) Of the kind or quality used in commerce; average or inferior.
  • grade separation (noun) A crossing that uses an underpass or overpass.
  • grade-appropriate (adjective) The quality of ability and work that is appropriate for students in a specified grade.
  • grade-constructed (adjective) Constructed at ground level.
