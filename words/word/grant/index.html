Grant: meaning, synonyms and examples

Grant is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Let have.

In Hindi
अनुदान; मंज़ूर करना
Synonyms
allow, award, concede, yield, accord, allot, give, cede, deed over, subsidization
Word family
granter, grantee, grantor

Grant as a verb

  1. Let have.

    “Grant permission.” “Mandela was allowed few visitors in prison.”

    Synonyms: allow · A kind of: give · Kinds: vouchsafe, allowance

  2. Give as judged due or on the basis of merit.

    “The referee awarded a free kick to the team.” “The jury awarded a million dollars to the plaintiff.” “Funds are granted to qualified researchers.”

    Synonyms: award · A kind of: give · Kinds: pension

  3. Be willing to concede.

    “I grant you this much.”

    Synonyms: concede, yield · A kind of: agree · Kinds: forgive

  4. Allow to have.

    “Grant a privilege.”

    Synonyms: accord, allot · A kind of: give · Kinds: give, enfranchise

  5. Bestow, especially officially.

    “Grant a degree.” “Give a divorce.” “This bill grants us new rights.”

    Synonyms: give

  6. Give over; surrender or relinquish to the physical control of another.

    Synonyms: concede, yield, cede · A kind of: give

  7. Transfer by deed.

    “Grant land.”

    Synonyms: deed over · A kind of: give

Grant as a noun

  1. Any monetary aid.

    A kind of: aid · Kinds: subsidy, grant-in-aid

  2. The act of providing a subsidy.

    Synonyms: subsidization, subsidisation · A kind of: allotment · Kinds: award, block grant, grant-in-aid

  3. (law) a transfer of property by deed of conveyance.

    Synonyms: assignment · A kind of: transferred property · Kinds: appanage, land grant

  4. Scottish painter; cousin of Lytton Strachey and member of the Bloomsbury Group (1885-1978).

  5. United States actor (born in England) who was the elegant leading man in many films (1904-1986).

  6. 18th President of the United States; commander of the Union armies in the American Civil War (1822-1885).

  7. A contract granting the right to operate a subsidiary business.

    Synonyms: concession · A kind of: contract · Kinds: franchise

  8. A right or privilege that has been granted.

    A kind of: right
