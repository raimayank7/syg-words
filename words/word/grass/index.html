Grass: meaning, synonyms and examples

Grass is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Narrow-leaved green herbage: grown as lawns; used as pasture for grazing animals; cut and dried as hay.

Grass as a noun

  1. Narrow-leaved green herbage: grown as lawns; used as pasture for grazing animals; cut and dried as hay.

    A kind of: gramineous plant · Kinds: beach grass, bunchgrass, midgrass, shortgrass, sword grass, tallgrass, lemongrass, goat grass

  2. German writer of novels and poetry and plays (born 1927).

  3. A police informer who implicates many people.

    Synonyms: supergrass · A kind of: informer

  4. Bulky food like grass or hay for browsing or grazing horses or cattle.

    Synonyms: eatage, forage, pasture, pasturage · A kind of: fodder

Grass as a verb

  1. Shoot down, of birds.

    A kind of: shoot

  2. Cover with grass.

    “The owners decided to grass their property.”

    A kind of: cover

  3. Spread out clothes on the grass to let it dry and bleach.

    A kind of: unfold

  4. Cover with grass.

    Synonyms: grass over · A kind of: cover

  5. Feed with grass.

    A kind of: crop

  6. Give away information about somebody.

    “He told on his classmate who had cheated on the exam.”

    Synonyms: denounce, tell on, betray, give away, rat, shit, shop, snitch · A kind of: inform · Kinds: sell out

Phrases and compounds with "grass"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bay grass (noun) Any of various grasses of the genus Eragrostis; specially useful for forage and for the prevention of erosion.
  • bur grass (noun) A grass of the genus Cenchrus.
  • dog grass (noun) European grass spreading rapidly by creeping rhizomes; naturalized in North America as a weed.
  • grass pea (noun) European annual grown for forage; seeds used for food in India and for stock elsewhere.
  • nut grass (noun) A widely distributed perennial sedge having small edible nutlike tubers.
  • pin grass (noun) European weed naturalized in southwestern United States and Mexico having reddish decumbent stems with small fernlike leaves and small deep reddish-lavender flowers followed by slender fruits that stick straight up; often grown for forage.
  • rye grass (noun) Any of several annual or perennial Eurasian grasses.
  • ague grass (noun) Colicroot having a scurfy or granuliferous perianth and white flowers; southeastern United States.
  • barn grass (noun) A coarse annual panic grass; a cosmopolitan weed; occasionally used for hay or grazing.
  • bear grass (noun) Yucca of southern United States having a clump of basal grasslike leaves and a central stalk with a terminal raceme of small whitish flowers.
  • bent grass (noun) Grass for pastures and lawns especially bowling and putting greens.
  • bent-grass (noun) Grass for pastures and lawns especially bowling and putting greens.
  • blue grass (noun) Any of various grasses of the genus Poa.
  • cord grass (noun) Any of several perennial grasses of the genus Spartina; some important as coastal soil binders.
  • crab grass (noun) Grasses with creeping stems that root freely; a pest in lawns.
  • deer grass (noun) Any of several plants of the genus Rhexia usually having pink-purple to magenta flowers; eastern North America.
