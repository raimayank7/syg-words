Grow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Grow is used as a verb (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Pass into a condition gradually, take on a specific property or attribute; become.

In Hindi
बढ़ना, उगाना
Synonyms
turn, mature, maturate, originate, arise, rise, develop, uprise, spring up, raise
Word family
growth, growing, grower

Grow as a verb

  1. Pass into a condition gradually, take on a specific property or attribute; become.

    “The weather turned nasty.” “She grew angry.”

    Synonyms: turn · A kind of: change · Kinds: bald, turn

  2. Become larger, greater, or bigger; expand or gain.

    “The problem grew too large for me.” “Her business grew fast.”

    A kind of: increase · Kinds: hypertrophy, rise, enlarge, augment, vegetate, mushroom, overgrow, stretch

  3. Increase in size by natural process.

    “Corn doesn't grow here.” “In these forests, mushrooms grow under the trees.” “Her hair doesn't grow much anymore.”

    A kind of: develop · Kinds: cut, ankylose, undergrow, exfoliate, vegetate, vegetate, twin, shoot

  4. Cause to grow or develop.

    “He grows vegetables in his backyard.”

    A kind of: develop · Kinds: vegetate, culture, rotate, swell, germinate, root

  5. Develop and reach maturity; undergo maturation.

    “He matured fast.” “The child grew fast.”

    Synonyms: mature, maturate · A kind of: develop · Kinds: ripen, find oneself, grow up

  6. Come into existence; take on form or shape.

    “A new religious movement originated in that country.” “A love that sprang up from friendship.” “The idea for the book grew out of a short story.”

    Synonyms: originate, arise, rise, develop, uprise, spring up · A kind of: become · Kinds: resurge, come forth, come, well up, head

  7. Cultivate by growing, often involving improvements by means of agricultural techniques.

    “The Bordeaux region produces great red wines.” “They produce good ham in Parma.” “We grow wheat here.”

    Synonyms: raise, farm, produce · A kind of: cultivate · Kinds: carry, overproduce, keep

  8. Come to have or undergo a change of (physical features and attributes).

    “He grew a beard.” “The patient developed abdominal pains.” “I got funny spots all over my body.”

    Synonyms: develop, produce, get, acquire · A kind of: change · Kinds: fledge, regrow, spring, sprout, stool, leaf, pod, teethe

  9. Grow emotionally or mature.

    “The child developed beautifully in her new kindergarten.” “When he spent a summer at camp, the boy grew noticeably and no longer showed some of his old adolescent behavior.”

    Synonyms: develop · A kind of: change · Kinds: outgrow, make

  10. Become attached by or as if by the process of growth.

    “The tree trunks had grown together.”

    A kind of: change

Phrases and compounds with "grow"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • grow up (verb) Become an adult.
  • grow over (verb) Grow beyond or across.
  • make grow (verb) Cause to grow and differentiate in ways conforming to its natural development.
