Guard: meaning, synonyms and examples

Guard is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A person who keeps watch over something or someone.

In Hindi
पहरेदार; रक्षा करना
Synonyms
safety, safety device, precaution, safeguard, guard duty, sentry duty, sentry go, ward, defend, hold

Guard as a noun

  1. A person who keeps watch over something or someone.

    A kind of: defender · Kinds: bouncer, doorkeeper, halberdier, screener, watchman

  2. The person who plays that position on a football team.

    “The left guard was injured on the play.”

    A kind of: lineman

  3. A device designed to prevent injury or accidents.

    Synonyms: safety, safety device · A kind of: device · Kinds: fender, safety catch

  4. A posture of defence in boxing or fencing.

    “Keep your guard up.”

    A kind of: position

  5. The person who plays the position of guard on a basketball team.

    A kind of: basketball player

  6. A military unit serving to protect some place or person.

    A kind of: military unit · Kinds: bodyguard, home guard, territorial

  7. A precautionary measure warding off impending danger or damage or injury etc.

    “He put an ice pack on the injury as a precaution.” “An insurance policy is a good safeguard.” “We let our guard down.”

    Synonyms: precaution, safeguard · A kind of: measure · Kinds: backstop, security

  8. The duty of serving as a sentry.

    “He was on guard that night.”

    Synonyms: guard duty, sentry duty, sentry go · A kind of: assignment

  9. (American football) a position on the line of scrimmage.

    “Guards must be good blockers.”

    A kind of: lineman

  10. A position on a basketball team.

    A kind of: position

Guard as a verb

  1. To keep watch over.

    “There would be men guarding the horses.”

    A kind of: watch · Kinds: patrol, stand guard, baby-sit, baby-sit

  2. Watch over or shield from danger or harm; protect.

    “Guard my possessions while I'm away.”

    Synonyms: ward · A kind of: protect · Kinds: shepherd

  3. Protect against a challenge or attack.

    “Hold that position behind the trees!” “Hold the bridge against the enemy's attacks.”

    Synonyms: defend, hold · A kind of: protect

  4. Take precautions in order to avoid some unwanted consequence.

    “Guard against becoming too friendly with the staff.” “Guard against infection.”

    A kind of: act

Phrases and compounds with "guard"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • on guard (adjective) Vigilant.
  • arm guard (noun) A pad worn by football players and hockey goalkeepers.
  • guard dog (noun) A dog trained to guard property.
  • off guard (adjective) Not prepared or vigilant.
  • off-guard (adjective) Not prepared or vigilant.
  • old guard (noun) A faction that is unwilling to accept new ideas.
  • red guard (noun) A radical political movement by Chinese youths who espoused Maoist principles.
  • bank guard (noun) A security guard at a bank.
  • body guard (verb) Accompany and protect from physical harm.
  • door guard (noun) Someone who guards an entrance.
  • face guard (noun) Face mask consisting of a strong wire mesh on the front of football helmets.
  • guard boat (noun) A boat that is on guard duty (as in a harbor) around a fleet of warships.
  • guard duty (noun) The duty of serving as a sentry.
  • guard hair (noun) Coarse hairs that form the outer fur and protect the underfur of certain mammals.
  • guard ship (noun) A warship (at anchor or under way) required to maintain a higher degree of readiness than others in its squadron.
  • home guard (noun) A volunteer unit formed to defend the homeland while the regular army is fighting elsewhere.
