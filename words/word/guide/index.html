Guide: meaning, synonyms and examples

Guide is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Direct the course; determine the direction of travelling.

In Hindi
मार्गदर्शक; राह दिखाना
Synonyms
steer, maneuver, manoeuver, manoeuvre, direct, point, head, channelize, channelise, lead
Word family
guidance

Guide as a verb

  1. Direct the course; determine the direction of travelling.

    Synonyms: steer, maneuver, manoeuver, manoeuvre, direct, point, head, channelize · A kind of: control · Kinds: dock, sheer, pull over, helm, crab, navigate, stand out, starboard

  2. Take somebody somewhere.

    “We lead him to our chief.” “Can you take me to the main entrance?” “He conducted us to the palace.”

    Synonyms: lead, take, direct, conduct · Kinds: beacon, hand, mislead, usher

  3. Be a guiding or motivating force or drive.

    “The teacher steered the gifted students towards the more challenging courses.”

    Synonyms: steer · A kind of: direct

  4. Use as a guide.

    “They had the lights to guide on.”

    Synonyms: guide on · A kind of: orient

  5. Pass over, across, or through.

    “He ran his eyes over her body.” “She ran her fingers along the carved figurine.” “He drew her hair through his fingers.”

    Synonyms: run, draw, pass · Kinds: rub, thread, thread

Guide as a noun

  1. Someone employed to conduct others.

    Synonyms: usher · A kind of: escort · Kinds: usherette

  2. Someone who shows the way by leading or advising.

    A kind of: leader · Kinds: cicerone, tour guide

  3. Something that offers basic information or instruction.

    Synonyms: guidebook · A kind of: handbook · Kinds: field guide, roadbook, travel guidebook

  4. A model or standard for making comparisons.

    Synonyms: template, templet · A kind of: model · Kinds: rhythm

  5. Someone who can find paths through unexplored territory.

    Synonyms: scout, pathfinder · A kind of: expert · Kinds: hunting guide, trailblazer

  6. A structure or marking that serves to direct the motion or positioning of something.

    A kind of: structure

Phrases and compounds with "guide"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • guide on (verb) Use as a guide.
  • guide dog (noun) A dog trained to guide the blind.
  • guide rope (noun) A rope used to guide the movement of the load of a crane.
  • guide word (noun) A word printed at the top of the page of a dictionary or other reference book to indicate the first or last item on that page.
  • tour guide (noun) A guide who leads others on a tour.
  • wave guide (noun) A hollow metal conductor that provides a path to guide microwaves; used in radar.
  • field guide (noun) A guidebook describing natural objects of some type that might be encountered in the field.
  • honey guide (noun) Small bird of tropical Africa and Asia; feeds on beeswax and honey and larvae.
  • guide fossil (noun) A fossil known to have lived in a particular geologic age that can be used to date the rock layer in which it is found.
  • hunting guide (noun) Guide to people hunting in unfamiliar territory.
