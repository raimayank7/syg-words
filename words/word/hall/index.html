Hall: meaning, synonyms and examples

Hall is used as a noun (13 senses). Its most common meaning is: An interior passage or corridor onto which rooms open.

In Hindi
हॉल, बड़ा कमरा
Synonyms
hallway, anteroom, antechamber, entrance hall, foyer, lobby, vestibule, dormitory, dorm, residence hall

Hall as a noun

  1. An interior passage or corridor onto which rooms open.

    “The elevators were at the end of the hall.”

    Synonyms: hallway · A kind of: corridor · Kinds: concourse

  2. A large entrance or reception room or area.

    Synonyms: anteroom, antechamber, entrance hall, foyer, lobby, vestibule · A kind of: room · Kinds: narthex

  3. A large room for gatherings or entertainment.

    “Lecture hall.” “Pool hall.”

    A kind of: room · Kinds: concert hall, exhibition hall, floor, great hall

  4. A college or university building containing living quarters for students.

    Synonyms: dormitory, dorm, residence hall, student residence · A kind of: living quarters, building · Kinds: hall of residence

  5. The large room of a manor or castle.

    Synonyms: manor hall · A kind of: room

  6. English writer whose novel about a lesbian relationship was banned in Britain for many years (1883-1943).

  7. United States child psychologist whose theories of child psychology strongly influenced educational psychology (1844-1924).

  8. United States chemist who developed an economical method of producing aluminum from bauxite (1863-1914).

  9. United States explorer who led three expeditions to the Arctic (1821-1871).

  10. United States astronomer who discovered Phobos and Deimos (the two satellites of Mars) (1829-1907).

  11. A large and imposing house.

    Synonyms: mansion, mansion house, manse, residence · A kind of: house · Kinds: manor, palace, stately home

  12. A large building used by a college or university for teaching or research.

    “Halls of learning.”

    A kind of: building

  13. A large building for meetings or entertainment.

    A kind of: building · Kinds: city hall, guildhall, lyceum

Phrases and compounds with "hall"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • city hall (noun) A building that houses administrative offices of a municipal government.
  • hall pass (noun) Written permission from a teacher for a student to be out the classroom and in the halls of the school.
  • mess hall (noun) A (large) military dining room where service personnel eat or relax.
  • town hall (noun) A government building that houses administrative offices of a town government.
  • asaph hall (noun) United States astronomer who discovered Phobos and Deimos (the two satellites of Mars) (1829-1907).
  • dance hall (noun) Large room used mainly for dancing.
  • great hall (noun) The principal hall in a castle or mansion; can be used for dining or entertainment.
  • manor hall (noun) The large room of a manor or castle.
  • music hall (noun) A theater in which vaudeville is staged.
  • study hall (noun) A period of time during the school day that is set aside for study.
  • dining-hall (noun) A large room at a college or university; used especially for dining.
  • hall porter (noun) Someone who guards an entrance.
  • hiring hall (noun) A union-operated placement office where jobs are allotted to applicants according to seniority or rotation.
  • concert hall (noun) A hall where concerts are given.
  • hall of fame (noun) A building containing trophies honoring famous people.
  • tammany hall (noun) A political organization within the Democratic Party in New York City (late 1800's and early 1900's) seeking political control by corruption and bossism.
