Hang: meaning, synonyms and examples

Hang is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be suspended or hanging.

In Hindi
लटकाना, टाँगना
Synonyms
hang up, fall, flow, attend, advert, pay heed, give ear, cling, bent, knack
Word family
hanging, hanger

Hang as a verb

  1. Be suspended or hanging.

    “The flag hung on the wall.”

    Kinds: overhang, dangle

  2. Cause to be hanging or suspended.

    “Hang that picture on the wall.”

    Synonyms: hang up · A kind of: fasten · Kinds: suspend, sling

  3. Let drop or droop.

    “Hang one's head in shame.”

    A kind of: drop

  4. Fall or flow in a certain way.

    “This dress hangs well.” “Her long black hair flowed down her back.”

    Synonyms: fall, flow

  5. Be menacing, burdensome, or oppressive.

    “This worry hangs on my mind.” “The cloud of suspicion hangs over her.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: brood

  6. Give heed (to).

    “The children in the audience attended the recital quietly.” “She hung on his every word.” “They attended to everything he said.”

    Synonyms: attend, advert, pay heed, give ear · A kind of: listen · Kinds: fixate

  7. Be suspended or poised.

    “Heavy fog hung over the valley.”

  8. Hold on tightly or tenaciously.

    “Hang on to your father's hands.” “The child clung to his mother's apron.”

    Synonyms: cling · A kind of: grasp

  9. Be exhibited.

    “Picasso hangs in this new wing of the museum.”

  10. Prevent from reaching a verdict, of a jury.

    A kind of: obstruct

  11. Decorate or furnish with something suspended.

    “Hang wallpaper.”

    A kind of: decorate

  12. Be placed in position as by a hinge.

    “This cabinet door doesn't hang right!”

  13. Place in position as by a hinge so as to allow free movement in one direction.

    “Hang a door.”

    A kind of: fasten

  14. Suspend (meat) in order to get a gamey taste.

    “Hang the venison for a few days.”

    A kind of: decompose

Hang as a noun

  1. A special way of doing something.

    “He had a bent for it.” “He had a special knack for getting into trouble.” “He couldn't get the hang of it.”

    Synonyms: bent, knack · A kind of: endowment

  2. The way a garment hangs.

    “He adjusted the hang of his coat.”

    A kind of: fit

  3. A gymnastic exercise performed on the rings or horizontal bar or parallel bars when the gymnast's weight is supported by the arms.

    A kind of: gymnastic exercise · Kinds: bent hang, inverted hang, lever hang, reverse hang, straight hang

Phrases and compounds with "hang"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • hang in (verb) Be persistent, refuse to stop.
  • hang on (verb) Fix to; attach.
  • hang up (verb) Put a telephone receiver back in its cradle.
  • hang-up (noun) An emotional preoccupation.
  • hang out (verb) Spend time in a certain location or with certain people.
  • bent hang (noun) A hang performed with the elbows bent.
  • hang back (verb) To lag or linger behind.
  • hang glide (verb) Fly by means of a hang glider.
  • lever hang (noun) A hang performed on the rings with the body stationary in a horizontal position.
  • care a hang (verb) Show no concern or interest; always used in the negative.
  • give a hang (verb) Show no concern or interest; always used in the negative.
  • hang around (verb) Be about.
  • hang glider (noun) A rider of a hang glider.
  • get the hang (verb) Be or become completely proficient or skilled in.
  • hang gliding (noun) Gliding in a hang glider.
  • reverse hang (noun) A hang with the arms extended in back.
