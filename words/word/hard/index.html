Hard: meaning, synonyms and examples

Hard is used as an adjective (10 senses) and adverb (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Not easy; requiring great physical or mental effort to accomplish or comprehend or endure.

In Hindi
कठिन; कड़ा
Synonyms

Opposites
soft
Word family

Hard as an adjective

  1. Not easy; requiring great physical or mental effort to accomplish or comprehend or endure.

    “A difficult task.” “Nesting places on the cliffs are difficult of access.” “Difficult times.”

    Synonyms: difficult · Similar: ambitious, arduous, awkward, baffling, catchy, delicate

  2. Dispassionate.

    “Took a hard look.” “A hard bargainer.”

    Opposite: soft · Similar: calculating, case-hardened, steely

  3. Resisting weight or pressure.

    Opposite: soft · Similar: adamantine, al dente, corneous, firm, granitic, hardened

  4. Very strong or vigorous.

    “Strong winds.” “A hard left to the chin.” “A knockout punch.”

    Synonyms: knockout, severe · Similar: strong

  5. Characterized by effort to the point of exhaustion; especially physical effort.

    “Worked their arduous way up the mining valley.” “A grueling campaign.” “Hard labor.”

    Synonyms: arduous, backbreaking, grueling, gruelling, heavy, laborious, operose, punishing · Similar: effortful

  6. Produced without vibration of the vocal cords.

    “Unvoiced consonants such as `p' and `k' and `s'.”

    Synonyms: unvoiced, voiceless, surd · Similar: whispered

  7. (of light) transmitted directly from a pointed light source.

    Synonyms: concentrated · Opposite: soft

  8. (of speech sounds); produced with the back of the tongue raised toward or touching the velum.

    “Russian distinguished between hard consonants and palatalized or soft consonants.”

    Opposite: soft · Similar: velar

  9. Unfortunate or hard to bear.

    “Had hard luck.” “A tough break.”

    Synonyms: tough · Similar: bad

  10. Dried out.

    “Hard dry rolls left over from the day before.”

    Similar: stale

Hard as an adverb

  1. With effort or force or vigor.

    “The team played hard.” “Worked hard all day.” “Pressed hard on the lever.”

  2. With firmness.

    “Held hard to the railing.”

    Synonyms: firmly

  3. Earnestly or intently.

    “Thought hard about it.” “Stared hard at the accused.”

  4. Causing great damage or hardship.

    “Industries hit hard by the depression.” “She was severely affected by the bank's failure.”

    Synonyms: severely

  5. Slowly and with difficulty.

    “Prejudices die hard.”

  6. Indulging excessively.

    “He drank heavily.”

    Synonyms: heavily, intemperately

  7. Into a solid condition.

    “Concrete that sets hard within a few hours.”

  8. Very near or close in space or time.

    “It stands hard by the railroad tracks.” “They were hard on his heels.” “A strike followed hard upon the plant's opening.”

  9. With pain or distress or bitterness.

    “He took the rejection very hard.”

  10. To the full extent possible; all the way.

    “Hard alee.” “The ship went hard astern.” “Swung the wheel hard left.”

Phrases and compounds with "hard"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • hard up (adjective) Not having enough money to pay for necessities.
  • ni-hard (noun) Cast iron to which nickel has been added to make it resist abrasion.
  • die hard (verb) Continue to exist.
  • die-hard (adjective) Tradition-bound and obstinately opinionated.
  • hard hat (noun) A worker skilled in building offices or dwellings etc.
  • hard put (adjective) Facing or experiencing financial trouble or difficulty.
  • hard roe (noun) Fish eggs or egg-filled ovary; having a grainy texture.
  • hard cash (noun) Money in the form of bills or coins.
  • hard clam (noun) An edible American clam; the heavy shells were used as money by some American Indians.
  • hard coal (noun) A hard natural coal that burns slowly and gives intense heat.
  • hard copy (noun) (computer science) matter that is held in a computer and is typed or printed on paper.
  • hard core (noun) The most dedicated and intensely loyal nucleus of a group or movement.
  • hard disc (noun) A rigid magnetic disk mounted permanently in a drive unit.
  • hard disk (noun) A rigid magnetic disk mounted permanently in a drive unit.
  • hard fern (noun) Any of several ferns of the genus Blechnum.
  • hard lead (noun) A lead alloy that contains about 5% antimony.
