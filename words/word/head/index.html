Head: meaning, synonyms and examples

Head is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: The upper part of the human body or the front part of the body in animals; contains the face and brains.

In Hindi
सिर; मुखिया
Synonyms
caput, mind, brain, psyche, nous, chief, top dog, fountainhead, headspring, head word
Opposites
rear, foot, tail
Word family
headship, heady, heading, header

Head as a noun

  1. The upper part of the human body or the front part of the body in animals; contains the face and brains.

    “He stuck his head out the window.”

    Synonyms: caput · A kind of: external body part · Kinds: human head

  2. A single domestic animal.

    “200 head of cattle.”

    A kind of: domestic animal

  3. That which is responsible for one's thoughts and feelings; the seat of the faculty of reason.

    “His mind wandered.” “I couldn't get his words out of my head.”

    Synonyms: mind, brain, psyche, nous · A kind of: cognition · Kinds: noddle, tabula rasa, ego, unconscious mind, subconscious mind

  4. A person who is in charge.

    “The head of the whole operation.”

    Synonyms: chief, top dog · A kind of: leader · Kinds: administrator, administrator, capo, department head, don, general, general manager, grand dragon

  5. The front of a military formation or procession.

    “The head of the column advanced boldly.” “They were at the head of the attack.”

    Opposite: rear · A kind of: formation

  6. The pressure exerted by a fluid.

    “A head of steam.”

    A kind of: pressure

  7. The top of something.

    “The head of the stairs.” “The head of the page.” “The head of the list.”

    Opposite: foot · A kind of: top

  8. The source of water from which a stream arises.

    “They tracked him back toward the head of the stream.”

    Synonyms: fountainhead, headspring · A kind of: beginning

  9. (grammar) the word in a grammatical constituent that plays the same grammatical role as the whole constituent.

    Synonyms: head word · A kind of: word

  10. The tip of an abscess (where the pus accumulates).

    A kind of: point

  11. The length or height based on the size of a human or animal head.

    “He is two heads taller than his little sister.” “His horse won by a head.”

    A kind of: linear unit

  12. A dense cluster of flowers or foliage.

    “A head of cauliflower.” “A head of lettuce.”

    Synonyms: capitulum · A kind of: plant organ

  13. The educator who has executive authority for a school.

    “She sent unruly pupils to see the principal.”

    Synonyms: principal, school principal, head teacher · A kind of: educator · Kinds: chancellor, headmaster, headmistress

  14. An individual person.

    “Tickets are $5 per head.”

    A kind of: individual

Head as a verb

  1. To go or travel towards.

    “Where is she heading.” “We were headed for the mountains.”

    A kind of: proceed · Kinds: take

  2. Be in charge of.

    “Who is heading this project?”

    Synonyms: lead · A kind of: direct · Kinds: chair, captain, spearhead, take hold

  3. Travel in front of; go in advance of others.

    “The procession was headed by John.”

    Synonyms: lead · A kind of: precede · Kinds: draw away

  4. Be the first or leading member of (a group) and excel.

    “This student heads the class.”

    Synonyms: head up · A kind of: be

  5. Direct the course; determine the direction of travelling.

    Synonyms: steer, maneuver, manoeuver, manoeuvre, direct, point, guide, channelize · A kind of: control · Kinds: dock, sheer, pull over, helm, crab, navigate, stand out, starboard

  6. Take its rise.

    “These rivers head from a mountain range in the Himalayas.”

    A kind of: originate

  7. Be in the front of or on top of.

    “The list was headed by the name of the president.”

    A kind of: lie · Kinds: surmount, crown

  8. Form a head or come or grow to a head.

    “The wheat headed early this year.”

    A kind of: form

  9. Remove the head of.

    “Head the fish.”

    A kind of: remove
