Heavy: meaning, synonyms and examples

Heavy is used as an adjective (14 senses), noun (2 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Of comparatively great physical weight or density.

In Hindi
भारी
Synonyms
fleshy, overweight, clayey, cloggy, lowering, sullen, threatening, dense, impenetrable, big
Opposites
light
Word family
heaviness

Heavy as an adjective

  1. Of comparatively great physical weight or density.

    “A heavy load.” “Lead is a heavy metal.” “Heavy mahogany furniture.”

    Opposite: light · Similar: dense, doughy, heavier-than-air, hefty, massive, non-buoyant

  2. Unusually great in degree or quantity or number.

    “Heavy taxes.” “A heavy fine.” “Heavy casualties.”

    Opposite: light · Similar: harsh

  3. Of the military or industry; using (or being) the heaviest and most powerful armaments or weapons or equipment.

    “Heavy artillery.” “Heavy infantry.” “A heavy cruiser.”

    Opposite: light

  4. Marked by great psychological weight; weighted down especially with sadness or troubles or weariness.

    “A heavy heart.” “A heavy schedule.” “Heavy news.”

    Opposite: light · Similar: burdensome, distressing, leaden, oppressive, weighty

  5. Usually describes a large person who is fat but has a large frame to carry it.

    Synonyms: fleshy, overweight · Similar: fat

  6. (used of soil) compact and fine-grained.

    “The clayey soil was heavy and easily saturated.”

    Synonyms: clayey, cloggy · Similar: compact

  7. Darkened by clouds.

    “A heavy sky.”

    Synonyms: lowering, sullen, threatening · Similar: cloudy

  8. Of great intensity or power or force.

    “A heavy blow.” “The fighting was heavy.” “Heavy seas.”

    Opposite: light · Similar: big

  9. (physics, chemistry) being or containing an isotope with greater than average atomic mass or weight.

    “Heavy hydrogen.” “Heavy water.”

    Opposite: light

  10. (of an actor or role) being or playing the villain.

    “Iago is the heavy role in `Othello'.”

    Similar: wicked

  11. Permitting little if any light to pass through because of denseness of matter.

    “Dense smoke.” “Heavy fog.” “Impenetrable gloom.”

    Synonyms: dense, impenetrable · Similar: thick

  12. Of relatively large extent and density.

    “A heavy line.”

    Similar: thick

  13. Made of fabric having considerable thickness.

    “A heavy coat.”

    Similar: thick

  14. Prodigious.

    “Big spender.” “Big eater.” “Heavy investor.”

    Synonyms: big · Similar: intemperate

Heavy as a noun

  1. An actor who plays villainous roles.

    A kind of: actor

  2. A serious (or tragic) role in a play.

    A kind of: character

Heavy as an adverb

  1. Slowly as if burdened by much weight.

    “Time hung heavy on their hands.”

    Synonyms: heavily

Phrases and compounds with "heavy"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • top-heavy (adjective) Unstable by being overloaded at the top.
  • heavy spar (noun) A white or colorless mineral (BaSO4); the main source of barium.
  • heavy-duty (adjective) Designed for heavy work.
  • heavy cream (noun) Contains more than 36% butterfat.
  • heavy metal (noun) A metal of relatively high density (specific gravity greater than about 5) or of high relative atomic weight (especially one that is poisonous like mercury or lead).
  • heavy swell (noun) A broad and deep undulation of the ocean.
  • heavy water (noun) Water containing a substantial proportion of deuterium atoms, used in nuclear reactors.
  • heavy-armed (adjective) Having massive arms.
  • heavy-laden (adjective) Burdened by cares.
  • heavy hitter (noun) An influential person who works hard to promote the causes they are interested in.
  • heavy weapon (noun) Large but transportable armament.
  • heavy-coated (adjective) Wearing a heavy coat.
  • heavy-footed (adjective) (of movement) lacking ease or lightness.
  • heavy-handed (adjective) Lacking physical movement skills, especially with the hands.
  • heavy-limbed (adjective) Having heavy limbs.
  • heavy lifting (noun) Difficult work.
