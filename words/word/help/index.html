Help: meaning, synonyms and examples

Help is used as a verb (8 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Give help or assistance; be of service.

In Hindi
मदद करना; सहायता
Synonyms
assist, aid, facilitate, help oneself, serve, avail, assistance, assistant, helper, supporter
Word family
helper, helping

Help as a verb

  1. Give help or assistance; be of service.

    “Everyone helped out during the earthquake.” “Can you help me carry this table?” “She never helps around the house.”

    Synonyms: assist, aid · A kind of: support · Kinds: facilitate, serve, benefact, help out, subserve, succor, expedite, avail

  2. Improve the condition of.

    “These pills will help the patient.”

    Synonyms: aid · A kind of: better · Kinds: bring around, benefit

  3. Be of use.

    “This will help to prevent accidents.”

    Synonyms: facilitate · A kind of: serve

  4. Abstain from doing; always used with a negative.

    “I can't help myself--I have to smoke.” “She could not help watching the sad spectacle.”

    Synonyms: help oneself · A kind of: refrain

  5. Help to some food; help with food or drink.

    “I served him three times, and after that he helped himself.”

    Synonyms: serve · A kind of: provide

  6. Contribute to the furtherance of.

    “This money will help the development of literacy in developing countries.”

    A kind of: promote

  7. Take or use.

    “She helped herself to some of the office supplies.”

    Synonyms: avail · A kind of: exploit

  8. Improve; change for the better.

    “New slipcovers will help the old living room furniture.”

    A kind of: better

Help as a noun

  1. The activity of contributing to the fulfillment of a need or furtherance of an effort or purpose.

    “He gave me an assist with the housework.” “Could not walk without assistance.” “Rescue party went to their aid.”

    Synonyms: aid, assist, assistance · A kind of: activity · Kinds: self-help, facilitation, hand, recourse, thanks, relief, lift, service

  2. A person who contributes to the fulfillment of a need or furtherance of an effort or purpose.

    “My invaluable assistant.” “They hired additional help to finish the work.”

    Synonyms: assistant, helper, supporter · A kind of: worker · Kinds: coadjutor, accomplice, aide, attendant, bat boy, dental assistant, deputy, dresser

  3. A resource.

    “Visual aids in teaching.”

    Synonyms: aid, assistance · A kind of: resource

  4. A means of serving.

    “Of no avail.” “There's no help for it.”

    Synonyms: avail, service · A kind of: helpfulness

Phrases and compounds with "help"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • help out (verb) Be of help, as in a particular situation of need.
  • help desk (noun) A service that provides information and assistance to the users of a computer network.
  • home help (noun) A person hired to help in another's home (especially one employed by a local authority to help the infirm with domestic work).
  • self-help (noun) The act of helping or improving yourself without relying on anyone else.
  • hired help (noun) Employee hired for domestic or farm work (often used in the singular to refer to several employees collectively).
  • help oneself (verb) Abstain from doing; always used with a negative.
  • kitchen help (noun) Help hired to work in the kitchen.
  • domestic help (noun) A servant who is paid to perform menial tasks around the household.
  • unable to help (adverb) In a helpless manner.
