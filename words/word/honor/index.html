Honor: meaning, synonyms and examples

Honor is used as a verb (3 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Bestow honor or rewards upon.

In Hindi
सम्मान, इज़्ज़त
Synonyms
honour, reward, respect, abide by, observe, award, accolade, laurels, purity, pureness
Opposites
dishonor
Word family
honoree, honorable, honoring, honorary

Honor as a verb

  1. Bestow honor or rewards upon.

    “Today we honor our soldiers.” “The scout was rewarded for courageous action.”

    Synonyms: honour, reward · Opposite: dishonor · A kind of: recognize · Kinds: toast, ennoble, decorate

  2. Show respect towards.

    “Honor your parents!”

    Synonyms: respect, honour, abide by, observe · A kind of: accept · Kinds: lionize, tolerate

  3. Accept as pay.

    “We honor checks and drafts.”

    Synonyms: honour · Opposite: dishonor · A kind of: accept

Honor as a noun

  1. A tangible symbol signifying approval or distinction.

    “An award for bravery.”

    Synonyms: award, accolade, honour, laurels · A kind of: symbol · Kinds: trophy, aliyah, academic degree, pennant, cachet, citation, mention, letter

  2. The state of being honored.

    Synonyms: honour, laurels · Opposite: dishonor · A kind of: standing · Kinds: glory, fame, esteem, repute

  3. The quality of being honorable and having a good name.

    “A man of honor.”

    Synonyms: honour · Opposite: dishonor · A kind of: righteousness

  4. A woman's virtue or chastity.

    Synonyms: honour, purity, pureness

Phrases and compounds with "honor"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • honor guard (noun) An escort for a distinguished guest or for the casket at a military funeral.
  • honor system (noun) A system of conduct in which participants are trusted not to take unfair advantage of others.
  • maid of honor (noun) An unmarried woman who attends the bride at a wedding.
  • word of honor (noun) A promise.
  • field of honor (noun) The scene of a duel.
  • guard of honor (noun) An escort for a distinguished guest or for the casket at a military funeral.
  • guest of honor (noun) The person in whose honor a gathering is held.
  • medal of honor (noun) The highest U.S. military decoration awarded for bravery and valor in action `above and beyond the call of duty'.
  • point of honor (noun) A concern that seriously reflects on your honor.
  • matron of honor (noun) A married woman serving as the attendant to the bride at a wedding.
  • congressional medal of honor (noun) The highest U.S. military decoration awarded for bravery and valor in action `above and beyond the call of duty'.
