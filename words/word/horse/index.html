Horse: meaning, synonyms and examples

Horse is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Solid-hoofed herbivorous quadruped domesticated since prehistoric times.

In Hindi
घोड़ा
Synonyms
gymnastic horse, cavalry, horse cavalry, sawhorse, sawbuck, buck, knight

Horse as a noun

  1. Solid-hoofed herbivorous quadruped domesticated since prehistoric times.

    A kind of: equine · Kinds: roan, stablemate, gee-gee, eohippus, mesohippus, protohippus, male horse, mare

  2. A padded gymnastic apparatus on legs.

    Synonyms: gymnastic horse · A kind of: gymnastic apparatus · Kinds: pommel horse, vaulting horse

  3. Troops trained to fight on horseback.

    “500 horse led the attack.”

    Synonyms: cavalry, horse cavalry · A kind of: military personnel

  4. A framework for holding wood that is being sawed.

    Synonyms: sawhorse, sawbuck, buck · A kind of: framework · Kinds: trestle

  5. A chessman shaped to resemble the head of a horse; can move two squares horizontally and one vertically (or vice versa).

    Synonyms: knight · A kind of: chessman

Horse as a verb

  1. Provide with a horse or horses.

    A kind of: provide · Kinds: remount

  • cart horse (noun) Draft horse kept for pulling carts.
  • dark horse (noun) A political candidate who is not well known but could win unexpectedly.
  • dawn horse (noun) Earliest horse; extinct primitive dog-sized four-toed Eocene animal.
  • dray horse (noun) Horse adapted for drawing heavy loads.
  • farm horse (noun) A quiet plodding workhorse.
  • gift horse (noun) A gift (usually of inferior quality) that should be accepted uncritically.
  • high horse (noun) An attitude of arrogant superiority.
  • horse balm (noun) Erect perennial strong-scented with serrate pointed leaves and a loose panicle of yellowish flowers; the eastern United States.
