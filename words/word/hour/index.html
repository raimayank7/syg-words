Hour: meaning, synonyms and examples

Hour is used as a noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A period of time equal to 1/24th of a day.

In Hindi
घंटा
Synonyms
hr, time of day, minute
Word family
hourly, horary

Hour as a noun

  1. A period of time equal to 1/24th of a day.

    “The job will take more than an hour.”

    Synonyms: hr · A kind of: time unit · Kinds: sidereal hour, man hour

  2. Clock time.

    “The hour is getting late.”

    Synonyms: time of day · A kind of: clock time · Kinds: noon, mealtime, late-night hour, midnight, small hours, bedtime, closing time, dawn

  3. A special and memorable period.

    “It was their finest hour.”

    A kind of: time period

  4. Distance measured by the time taken to cover it.

    “We live an hour from the airport.” “Its just 10 minutes away.”

    Synonyms: minute · A kind of: distance

Phrases and compounds with "hour"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • man hour (noun) A time unit used in industry for measuring work.
  • half-hour (noun) A half of an hour.
  • hour hand (noun) The shorter hand of a clock that points to the hours.
  • rush hour (noun) The times at the beginning and end of the working day when many people are traveling to or from work.
  • watt-hour (noun) A unit of energy equal to the power of one watt operating for one hour.
  • zero hour (noun) The time set for the start of an action or operation.
  • hour angle (noun) The angular distance along the celestial equator from the observer's meridian to the hour circle of a given celestial body.
  • light hour (noun) The distance light travels in a vacuum in one hour; approximately one billion kilometers.
  • ampere-hour (noun) A unit of charge equal to 3600 coulombs.
  • credit hour (noun) A unit of academic credit; one hour a week for an academic semester.
  • hour circle (noun) A great circle on the celestial sphere that passes through both celestial poles.
  • person hour (noun) A time unit used in industry for measuring work.
  • quarter-hour (noun) A quarter of an hour.
  • eleventh hour (noun) The latest possible moment.
  • kilowatt hour (noun) A unit of energy equal to the work done by a power of 1000 watts operating for one hour.
  • semester hour (noun) A unit of academic credit; one hour a week for an academic semester.
