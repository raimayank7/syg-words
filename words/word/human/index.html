Human: meaning, synonyms and examples

Human is used as an adjective (3 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Characteristic of humanity.

In Hindi
मानव, इंसान
Synonyms
homo, man, human being
Opposites
nonhuman
Word family
humanness, humanity

Human as an adjective

  1. Characteristic of humanity.

    “Human nature.”

  2. Relating to a person.

    “The experiment was conducted on 6 monkeys and 2 human subjects.”

  3. Having human form or attributes as opposed to those of animals or divine beings.

    “Human beings.” “The human body.” “Human kindness.”

    Opposite: nonhuman · Similar: anthropoid, anthropomorphic, earthborn, fallible, hominal, hominian

Human as a noun

  1. Any living or extinct member of the family Hominidae characterized by superior intelligence, articulate speech, and erect carriage.

    Synonyms: homo, man, human being · A kind of: hominid · Kinds: world

Phrases and compounds with "human"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • human body (noun) Alternative names for the body of a human being.
  • human face (noun) The front of the human head from the forehead to the chin and ear to ear.
  • human foot (noun) The part of the leg of a human being below the ankle joint.
  • human head (noun) The head of a human being.
  • human knee (noun) Hinge joint in the human leg connecting the tibia and fibula with the femur and protected in front by the patella.
  • human race (noun) All of the living human inhabitants of the earth.
  • human being (noun) Any living or extinct member of the family Hominidae characterized by superior intelligence, articulate speech, and erect carriage.
  • human death (noun) A death resulting from an accident or a disaster.
  • human elbow (noun) Hinge joint between the forearm and upper arm and the corresponding joint in the forelimb of a quadruped.
  • human right (noun) (law) any basic right or freedom to which all human beings are entitled and in whose exercise a government may not interfere (including rights to life and liberty as well as freedom of thought and expression and equality before the law).
  • human waste (noun) The body wastes of human beings.
  • human-sized (adjective) Having the approximate size of a human being.
  • human action (noun) Something that people do or cause to happen.
  • human beings (noun) All of the living human inhabitants of the earth.
  • human botfly (noun) Large tropical American fly; parasitic on humans and other mammals.
  • human dynamo (noun) A highly energetic and indefatigable person.
