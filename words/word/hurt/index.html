Hurt: meaning, synonyms and examples

Hurt is used as a verb (7 senses), noun (5 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be the source of pain.

In Hindi
चोट पहुँचाना; दुखना
Synonyms
ache, smart, pain, anguish, injure, wound, bruise, offend, spite, suffer
Word family
hurting

Hurt as a verb

  1. Be the source of pain.

    Synonyms: ache, smart · A kind of: cause to be perceived · Kinds: bite, burn, itch, hunger, thirst, act up, throb, shoot

  2. Give trouble or pain to.

    “This exercise will hurt your back.”

    A kind of: indispose · Kinds: injure, trouble, disagree with

  3. Cause emotional anguish or make miserable.

    “It pains me to see my children not being taught well in school.”

    Synonyms: pain, anguish · A kind of: upset · Kinds: break someone's heart, agonize, try, torment

  4. Cause damage or affect negatively.

    “Our business was hurt by the new competition.”

    Synonyms: injure · A kind of: damage

  5. Hurt the feelings of.

    “She hurt me when she did not include me among her guests.” “This remark really bruised my ego.”

    Synonyms: wound, injure, bruise, offend, spite · A kind of: arouse · Kinds: diss, lacerate, sting, humiliate

  6. Feel physical pain.

    “Were you hurting after the accident?”

    Synonyms: ache, suffer · A kind of: perceive · Kinds: catch, twinge, prick

  7. Feel pain or be in pain.

    Synonyms: suffer · A kind of: be · Kinds: have, gag, ail, starve

Hurt as a noun

  1. Any physical damage to the body caused by violence or accident or fracture etc.

    Synonyms: injury, harm, trauma · A kind of: ill health · Kinds: brain damage, birth trauma, bleeding, blunt trauma, bruise, bump, burn, dislocation

  2. Psychological suffering.

    “The death of his wife caused him great distress.”

    Synonyms: distress, suffering · A kind of: pain · Kinds: anguish, self-torture, tsoris, wound

  3. Feelings of mental or physical pain.

    Synonyms: suffering · A kind of: pain · Kinds: agony, throes, discomfort

  4. A damage or loss.

    Synonyms: detriment · A kind of: damage · Kinds: expense

  5. The act of damaging something or someone.

    Synonyms: damage, harm, scathe · A kind of: change of integrity · Kinds: impairment, defacement, wound, burn, defloration

Hurt as an adjective

  1. Suffering from physical injury especially that suffered in battle.

    “Nursing his wounded arm.” “Ambulances...for the hurt men and women.”

    Synonyms: wounded · Similar: injured

  2. Damaged inanimate objects or their value.

    Synonyms: weakened · Similar: damaged
