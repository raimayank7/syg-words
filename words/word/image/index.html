Image: meaning, synonyms and examples

Image is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An iconic mental representation.

In Hindi
छवि, चित्र
Synonyms
mental image, persona, picture, icon, ikon, prototype, paradigm, epitome, trope, figure of speech
Word family
imaging, imagery

Image as a noun

  1. An iconic mental representation.

    “Her imagination forced images upon her too awful to contemplate.”

    Synonyms: mental image · A kind of: representation · Kinds: imagination image, memory image, visual image, mental picture, auditory image

  2. (Jungian psychology) a personal facade that one presents to the world.

    “A public image is as fragile as Humpty Dumpty.”

    Synonyms: persona · A kind of: appearance

  3. A visual representation (of an object or scene or person or abstraction) produced on a surface.

    “They showed us the pictures of their wedding.” “A movie is a series of images projected so rapidly that the eye integrates them.”

    Synonyms: picture, icon, ikon · A kind of: representation · Kinds: bitmap, chiaroscuro, collage, foil, graphic, iconography, inset, likeness

  4. A standard or typical example.

    “He is the prototype of good breeding.” “He provided America with an image of the good father.”

    Synonyms: prototype, paradigm, epitome · A kind of: model · Kinds: concentrate, imago

  5. Language used in a figurative or nonliteral sense.

    Synonyms: trope, figure of speech, figure · A kind of: rhetorical device · Kinds: conceit, irony, hyperbole, kenning, metaphor, metonymy, oxymoron, personification

  6. Someone who closely resembles a famous person (especially an actor).

    “He could be Gingrich's double.” “She's the very image of her mother.”

    Synonyms: double, look-alike · A kind of: person · Kinds: ringer

  7. (mathematics) the set of values of the dependent variable for which a function is defined.

    “The image of f(x) = x^2 is the set of all non-negative real numbers if the domain of the function is the set of all real numbers.”

    Synonyms: range, range of a function · A kind of: set

  8. The general impression that something (a person or organization or product) presents to the public.

    “Although her popular image was contrived it served to inspire music and pageantry.” “The company tried to project an altruistic image.”

    A kind of: impression

  9. A representation of a person (especially in the form of sculpture).

    “The coin bears an effigy of Lincoln.” “The emperor's tomb had his image carved in stone.”

    Synonyms: effigy, simulacrum · A kind of: representation · Kinds: idol, scarecrow, waxwork

Image as a verb

  1. Render visible, as by means of MRI.

    A kind of: visualize

  2. Imagine; conceive of; see in one's mind.

    “I can't see him on horseback!” “I can see what will happen.” “I can see a risk in this strategy.”

    Synonyms: visualize, visualise, envision, project, fancy, see, figure, picture · A kind of: imagine

Phrases and compounds with "image"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • graven image (noun) A material effigy that is worshipped.
  • memory image (noun) A mental image of something previously experienced.
  • mental image (noun) An iconic mental representation.
  • mirror image (noun) A likeness in which left and right are reversed.
  • visual image (noun) A mental image that is similar to a visual perception.
  • image breaker (noun) A destroyer of images used in religious worship.
  • image scanner (noun) An electronic device that generates a digital representation of an image for data input to a computer.
  • thought-image (noun) A mental image produced by the imagination.
  • virtual image (noun) A reflected optical image (as seen in a plane mirror).
  • auditory image (noun) A mental image that is similar to an auditory perception.
  • image orthicon (noun) A now obsolete picture pickup tube in a television camera; electrons emitted from a photoemissive surface in proportion to the intensity of the incident light are focused onto the target causing secondary emission of electrons.
  • spitting image (noun) A perfect likeness or counterpart.
  • electronic image (noun) An image represented as a two dimensional array of brightness values for pixels.
  • image compression (noun) The compression of graphics for storage or transmission.
  • imagination image (noun) A mental image produced by the imagination.
  • mirror-image relation (noun) The relation of opposition between crystals or molecules that are reflections of one another.
