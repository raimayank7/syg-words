Inside: meaning, synonyms and examples

Inside is used as an adverb (4 senses), noun (2 senses) and adjective (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Within a building.

In Hindi
अंदर
Synonyms
indoors, within, inwardly, at heart, at bottom, deep down, in spite of appearance, interior, inner, privileged
Opposites
outside

Inside as an adverb

  1. Within a building.

    “In winter we play inside.”

    Synonyms: indoors · Opposite: outside

  2. On the inside.

    “Inside, the car is a mess.”

    Synonyms: within · Opposite: outside

  3. With respect to private feelings.

    “Inwardly, she was raging.”

    Synonyms: inwardly

  4. In reality.

    “She is very kind at heart.”

    Synonyms: at heart, at bottom, deep down, in spite of appearance

Inside as a noun

  1. The region that is inside of something.

    Synonyms: interior · Opposite: outside · A kind of: region · Kinds: midland, midst, penetralia

  2. The inner or enclosed surface of something.

    Synonyms: interior · Opposite: outside · A kind of: surface · Kinds: belly

Inside as an adjective

  1. Relating to or being on the side closer to the center or within a defined space.

    “He reached into his inside jacket pocket.” “Inside out.” “An inside pitch is between home plate and the batter.”

    Opposite: outside · Similar: wrong

  2. Being or applying to the inside of a building.

    “An inside wall.”

    Similar: interior

  3. Confined to an exclusive group.

    “Privy to inner knowledge.” “Inside information.” “Privileged information.”

    Synonyms: inner, privileged · Similar: exclusive

  4. Away from the outer edge.

    “An inner lahne.” “The inside lane.”

    Similar: inner

Phrases and compounds with "inside"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • inside job (noun) Some transgression committed with the assistance of someone trusted by the victim.
  • inside out (adverb) With the inside facing outward.
  • inside-out (adjective) With the inside surface on the outside.
  • inside loop (noun) A loop consisting of a climb followed by inverted flight followed by a dive that returns to horizontal flight.
  • inside track (noun) A favorable position in a competition.
  • inside clinch (noun) A clinch with the end of the line inside the loop.
  • inside caliper (noun) Caliper for measuring inside dimensions (the size of a cavity or hole); points on its legs curve outward.
  • inside passage (noun) A naturally protected waterway from Seattle to Skagway in southeastern Alaska.
  • inside information (noun) True confidential information.
