Jacket: meaning, synonyms and examples

Jacket is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A short coat.

In Hindi
जैकेट
Synonyms
crown, crownwork, jacket crown, cap

Jacket as a noun

  1. A short coat.

    A kind of: coat · Kinds: banyan, bed jacket, bolero, bush jacket, dolman, donkey jacket, doublet, dressing sack

  2. An outer wrapping or casing.

    “Phonograph records were sold in cardboard jackets.”

    A kind of: wrapping · Kinds: record jacket, book jacket

  3. (dentistry) dental appliance consisting of an artificial crown for a broken or decayed tooth.

    “Tomorrow my dentist will fit me for a crown.”

    Synonyms: crown, crownwork, jacket crown, cap · A kind of: dental appliance

  4. The outer skin of a potato.

    A kind of: peel

  5. The tough metal shell casing for certain kinds of ammunition.

    A kind of: shell

Jacket as a verb

  1. Provide with a thermally non-conducting cover.

    “The tubing needs to be jacketed.”

    A kind of: cover

  2. Put a jacket on.

    “The men were jacketed.”

    A kind of: dress

Phrases and compounds with "jacket"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air jacket (noun) An inflatable life jacket.
  • bed jacket (noun) A lightweight jacket worn over bedclothes (as when sitting in bed).
  • mao jacket (noun) A light weight jacket with a high collar; worn by Mao Zedong and the Chinese people during his regime.
  • ski jacket (noun) A parka to be worn while skiing.
  • book jacket (noun) A paper jacket for a book; a jacket on which promotional information is usually printed.
  • bush jacket (noun) A loose fitting jacket; resembles a shirt with four patch pockets and a belt.
  • cork jacket (noun) Life preserver consisting of a sleeveless jacket of buoyant or inflatable design.
  • dust jacket (noun) A paper jacket for a book; a jacket on which promotional information is usually printed.
  • eton jacket (noun) A jacket hanging to the waist and cut square at the bottom.
  • life jacket (noun) Life preserver consisting of a sleeveless jacket of buoyant or inflatable design.
  • mess jacket (noun) Waist-length jacket tapering to a point at the back; worn by officers in the mess for formal dinners.
  • jacket crown (noun) (dentistry) dental appliance consisting of an artificial crown for a broken or decayed tooth.
  • shell jacket (noun) Waist-length jacket tapering to a point at the back; worn by officers in the mess for formal dinners.
  • water jacket (noun) A container filled with water that surrounds a machine to cool it; especially that surrounding the cylinder block of an engine.
  • dinner jacket (noun) Semiformal evening dress for men.
  • dolman jacket (noun) A hussar's jacket worn over the shoulders.
