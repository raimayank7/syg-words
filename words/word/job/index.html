Job: meaning, synonyms and examples

Job is used as a noun (12 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: The principal activity in your life that you do to earn money.

In Hindi
नौकरी, काम
Synonyms
occupation, business, line of work, line, task, chore, problem, caper, subcontract, farm out

Job as a noun

  1. The principal activity in your life that you do to earn money.

    “He's not in my line of business.”

    Synonyms: occupation, business, line of work, line · A kind of: activity · Kinds: confectionery, sport, farming, game, career, employment, appointment, position

  2. A specific piece of work required to be done as a duty or for a specific fee.

    “Estimates of the city's loss on that job ranged as high as a million dollars.” “The job of repairing the engine took several hours.” “The endless task of classifying the samples.”

    Synonyms: task, chore · A kind of: duty · Kinds: ball-buster, stint, scut work

  3. A workplace; as in the expression "on the job".

    “On the job.”

    A kind of: workplace

  4. An object worked on; a result produced by working.

    “He held the job in his left hand and worked on it with his right.”

    A kind of: product

  5. The responsibility to do something.

    “It is their job to print the truth.”

    A kind of: duty

  6. The performance of a piece of work.

    “She did an outstanding job as Ophelia.” “He gave it up as a bad job.”

    A kind of: work

  7. A damaging piece of work.

    “Dry rot did the job of destroying the barn.” “The barber did a real job on my hair.”

    A kind of: work

  8. A state of difficulty that needs to be resolved.

    “She and her husband are having problems.” “It is always a job to contact him.” “Urban problems such as traffic congestion and smog.”

    Synonyms: problem · A kind of: difficulty · Kinds: race problem, balance-of-payments problem

  9. Any long-suffering person who withstands affliction without despairing.

    A kind of: unfortunate

  10. (computer science) a program application that may consist of several steps but is a single logical unit.

    A kind of: application

  11. A book in the Old Testament containing Job's pleas to God about his afflictions and God's reply.

  12. A crime (especially a robbery).

    “The gang pulled off a bank job in St. Louis.”

    Synonyms: caper · A kind of: robbery

Job as a verb

  1. Profit privately from public office and official business.

    A kind of: cheat

  2. Arranged for contracted work to be done by others.

    Synonyms: subcontract, farm out · A kind of: hire

  3. Work occasionally.

    “As a student I jobbed during the semester breaks.”

    A kind of: work

  4. Invest at a risk.

    “I bought this house not because I want to live in it but to sell it later at a good price, so I am speculating.”

    Synonyms: speculate · A kind of: invest · Kinds: bull

Phrases and compounds with "job"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • job lot (noun) A miscellaneous collection of things sold together.
  • odd-job (adjective) Not regular or skilled.
  • hand job (noun) Slang for masturbation.
  • nose job (noun) Cosmetic surgery to improve the appearance of your nose.
  • snow job (noun) A long and elaborate misrepresentation.
  • inside job (noun) Some transgression committed with the assistance of someone trusted by the victim.
  • job action (noun) A temporary action by workers to protest management decision or to make demands.
  • on the job (adjective) Actively engaged in paid work.
  • on-the-job (adjective) Relating to or associated with a job or employment.
  • book of job (noun) A book in the Old Testament containing Job's pleas to God about his afflictions and God's reply.
  • do a job on (verb) Destroy completely or make ugly or useless.
  • hatchet job (noun) A false accusation of an offense or a malicious misrepresentation of someone's words or actions.
  • job control (noun) A program that is called to prepare each job to be run.
  • odd-job man (noun) A man skilled in various odd jobs and other small tasks.
  • coaching job (noun) The job of a professional coach.
  • job candidate (noun) An applicant who is being considered for a job.
