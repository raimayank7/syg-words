Keep: meaning, synonyms and examples

Keep is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Keep in a certain state, position, or activity; e.g., "keep clean".

In Hindi
रखना, बनाए रखना
Synonyms
maintain, hold, continue, go on, proceed, go along, hold on, prevent, observe, retain
Opposites
lose, break
Word family
keeping, keeper

Keep as a verb

  1. Keep in a certain state, position, or activity; e.g., "keep clean".

    “Keep clean.” “Hold in place.” “She always held herself as a lady.”

    Synonyms: maintain, hold · Kinds: pressurize, continue, hold over, conserve, preserve, distance, housekeep

  2. Continue a certain state, condition, or activity.

    “Keep on working!” “We continued to work into the night.” “Keep smiling.”

    Synonyms: continue, go on, proceed, go along · A kind of: act · Kinds: hold, run on, ride

  3. Retain possession of.

    “Can I keep my old stuffed animals?” “She kept her maiden name after she married.”

    Synonyms: hold on · Opposite: lose · A kind of: have · Kinds: carry over, deny, withhold, save, store, store, retain, harbor

  4. Stop (someone or something) from doing something or being in a certain state.

    “We must prevent the cancer from spreading.” “His snoring kept me from falling asleep.” “Keep the child from eating the marbles.”

    Synonyms: prevent · Kinds: defend, exclude, hold, keep away, blank, impede, rain out

  5. Conform one's action or practice to.

    “Keep appointments.” “She never keeps her promises.” “We kept to the original conditions of the contract.”

    Synonyms: observe · Opposite: break · Kinds: make good

  6. Stick to correctly or closely.

    “The pianist kept time with the metronome.” “Keep count.” “I cannot keep track of all my employees.”

    Synonyms: observe, maintain

  7. Look after; be the keeper of; have charge of.

    “He keeps the shop when I am gone.”

    A kind of: have · Kinds: conserve

  8. Maintain by writing regular records.

    “Keep a diary.” “Maintain a record.” “Keep notes.”

    Synonyms: maintain · A kind of: record

  9. Supply with room and board.

    “He is keeping three women in the guest cottage.” “Keep boarders.”

    A kind of: lodge

  10. Allow to remain in a place or position or maintain a property or feature.

    “We cannot continue several servants any longer.” “She retains a lawyer.” “The family's fortune waned and they could not keep their household staff.”

    Synonyms: retain, continue, keep on · A kind of: prolong

  11. Supply with necessities and support.

    “She alone sustained her family.” “The money will sustain our good cause.” “There's little to earn and many to keep.”

    Synonyms: sustain, maintain · A kind of: have · Kinds: patronage, reseed, carry

  12. Fail to spoil or rot.

    “These potatoes keep for a long time.”

    Synonyms: stay fresh · A kind of: stay

  13. Behave as expected during of holidays or rites.

    “Keep the commandments.” “Celebrate Christmas.” “Observe Yom Kippur.”

    Synonyms: observe, celebrate · Kinds: commemorate, mourn, solemnize

  14. Keep under control; keep in check.

    “Suppress a smile.” “Keep your temper.” “Keep your cool.”

    Synonyms: restrain, keep back, hold back · A kind of: inhibit · Kinds: hold, confine, inhibit, control

Keep as a noun

  1. The financial means whereby one lives.

    “Each child was expected to pay for their keep.” “He applied to the state for support.” “He could no longer earn his own livelihood.”

    Synonyms: support, livelihood, living, bread and butter, sustenance · A kind of: resource · Kinds: comforts, maintenance, meal ticket, subsistence

  2. The main tower within the walls of a medieval castle or fortress.

    Synonyms: donjon, dungeon · A kind of: stronghold

  3. A cell in a jail or prison.

    Synonyms: hold · A kind of: cell

Phrases and compounds with "keep"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • keep in (verb) Cause to stay indoors.
  • keep on (verb) Allow to remain in a place or position or maintain a property or feature.
  • keep up (verb) Maintain a required pace or level.
  • keep mum (verb) Refuse to talk or stop talking; fall silent.
  • keep off (verb) Refrain from certain foods or beverages.
  • keep out (verb) Prevent from entering; shut out.
  • keep away (verb) Prevent from coming close.
  • keep back (verb) Keep under control; keep in check.
  • keep down (verb) Place a limit on the number of.
  • keep note (verb) Maintain in the forefront of one's awareness.
  • keep open (verb) Retain rights to.
  • keep pace (verb) Maintain the same pace.
  • keep step (verb) Maintain the same pace.
  • keep apart (verb) Set apart from others.
  • keep going (verb) Continue uninterrupted.
  • keep guard (verb) Watch over so as to protect.
