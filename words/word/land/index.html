Land: meaning, synonyms and examples

Land is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: The land on which real estate is located.

In Hindi
ज़मीन, भूमि; उतरना
Synonyms
ground, soil, domain, demesne, dry land, earth, solid ground, terra firma, country, state
Word family
lander, landing

Land as a noun

  1. The land on which real estate is located.

    “He built the house on land leased from the city.”

    A kind of: real property · Kinds: farmstead, homestead, no man's land

  2. Material in the top layer of the surface of the earth in which plants can grow (especially with reference to its quality or use).

    “The land had never been plowed.” “Good agricultural soil.”

    Synonyms: ground, soil · A kind of: object · Kinds: badlands, bottomland, coastland, cultivated land, overburden, permafrost, polder, rangeland

  3. Territory over which rule or control is exercised.

    “His domain extended into Europe.” “He made it the law of the land.”

    Synonyms: domain, demesne · A kind of: region · Kinds: archduchy, barony, duchy, earldom, emirate, empire, fiefdom, grand duchy

  4. The solid part of the earth's surface.

    “The plane turned away from the sea and moved back over land.” “The earth shook for several minutes.” “He dropped the logs on the ground.”

    Synonyms: dry land, earth, ground, solid ground, terra firma · A kind of: object · Kinds: archipelago, beachfront, cape, coastal plain, floor, foreland, forest, island

  5. The territory occupied by a nation.

    “He returned to the land of his birth.” “He visited several European countries.”

    Synonyms: country, state · A kind of: administrative district · Kinds: banana republic, fatherland, buffer state, kingdom, tax haven, sultanate

  6. A domain in which something is dominant.

    “The untroubled kingdom of reason.” “A land of make-believe.” “The rise of the realm of cotton in the south.”

    Synonyms: kingdom, realm · A kind of: sphere · Kinds: lotusland

  7. Extensive landed property (especially in the country) retained by the owner for his own use.

    “The family owned a large estate on Long Island.”

    Synonyms: estate, landed estate, acres, demesne · A kind of: real property · Kinds: freehold, glebe, leasehold, smallholding, homestead, fief, barony, countryseat

  8. The people who live in a nation or country.

    “A statement that sums up the nation's mood.” “The news was announced to the nation.” “The whole country worshipped him.”

    Synonyms: nation, country · A kind of: people

  9. A politically organized body of people under a single government.

    “The state has elected a new president.” “African nations.” “Students who had come to the nation's capitol.”

    Synonyms: state, nation, country, commonwealth, res publica, body politic · A kind of: political unit · Kinds: commonwealth country, developing country, foreign country, rogue state, suzerain, sea power, world power, city state

  10. United States inventor who incorporated Polaroid film into lenses and invented the one step photographic process (1909-1991).

  11. Agriculture considered as an occupation or way of life.

    “Farming is a strenuous life.” “There's no work on the land any more.”

    Synonyms: farming · A kind of: occupation

Land as a verb

  1. Reach or come to rest.

    “The bird landed on the highest branch.” “The plane landed in Istanbul.”

    Synonyms: set down · A kind of: arrive · Kinds: alight, force-land, beach, port, disembark, touch down, undershoot, belly-land

  2. Cause to come to the ground.

    “The pilot managed to land the airplane safely.”

    Synonyms: put down, bring down · A kind of: arrive

  3. Bring into a different state.

    “This may land you in jail.”

    Synonyms: bring · A kind of: change

  4. Bring ashore.

    A kind of: bring · Kinds: ground, strand

  5. Deliver (a blow).

    “He landed several blows on his opponent's head.”

    A kind of: deliver

  6. Arrive on shore.

    “The ship landed in Pearl Harbor.”

    Synonyms: set ashore, shore · A kind of: arrive

  7. Shoot at and force to come down.

    “The enemy landed several of our aircraft.”

    Synonyms: down, shoot down

Phrases and compounds with "land"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • land up (verb) Block with earth, as after a landslide.
  • din land (noun) United States inventor who incorporated Polaroid film into lenses and invented the one step photographic process (1909-1991).
  • dry land (noun) The solid part of the earth's surface.
  • land tax (noun) A capital tax on property imposed by municipalities; based on the estimated value of the property.
  • land area (noun) An area of ground used for some particular purpose (such as building or farming).
  • land line (noun) A telephone line that travels over terrestrial circuits.
  • land mass (noun) A large continuous extent of land.
  • land mile (noun) A unit of length equal to 1,760 yards or 5,280 feet; exactly 1609.344 meters.
  • land mine (noun) An explosive mine hidden underground; explodes when stepped on or driven over.
  • land rail (noun) Common Eurasian rail that frequents grain fields.
  • land site (noun) The piece of land on which something is located (or is to be located).
  • belly-land (verb) Land on the underside without the landing gear.
  • coats land (noun) A region of western Antarctica along the southeastern shore of the Weddell Sea.
  • crash land (verb) Make an emergency landing.
  • crown land (noun) Land that belongs to the Crown.
  • force-land (verb) Make a forced landing.
