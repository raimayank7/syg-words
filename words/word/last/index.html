Last: meaning, synonyms and examples

Last is used as an adjective (9 senses), verb (2 senses), noun (8 senses) and adverb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Immediately past.

In Hindi
आख़िरी, पिछला; टिकना
Synonyms
concluding, final, terminal, net, utmost, last-place, lowest, endure, survive, live
Opposites
first

Last as an adjective

  1. Immediately past.

    “Last Thursday.” “The last chapter we read.”

    Similar: past

  2. Coming after all others in time or space or degree or being the only one remaining.

    “The last time I saw Paris.” “The last day of the month.” “Had the last word.”

    Opposite: first · Similar: senior, sunset, ultimate

  3. Occurring at or forming an end or termination.

    “His concluding words came as a surprise.” “The final chapter.” “The last days of the dinosaurs.”

    Synonyms: concluding, final, terminal · Similar: closing

  4. Most unlikely or unsuitable.

    “The last person we would have suspected.” “The last man they would have chosen for the job.”

    Similar: unlikely

  5. Occurring at the time of death.

    “His last words.” “The last rites.”

    Similar: dying

  6. Conclusive in a process or progression.

    “The final answer.” “A last resort.” “The net result.”

    Synonyms: final, net · Similar: ultimate

  7. Highest in extent or degree.

    “To the last measure of human endurance.” “Whether they were accomplices in the last degree or a lesser one was...to be determined individually.”

    Synonyms: utmost · Similar: high

  8. Not to be altered or undone.

    “The judge's decision is final.” “The arbiter will have the last say.”

    Synonyms: final · Similar: unalterable

  9. Lowest in rank or importance.

    “Last prize.” “In last place.”

    Synonyms: last-place, lowest · Similar: worst

Last as a verb

  1. Persist for a specified period of time.

    “The bad weather lasted for three days.”

    Synonyms: endure · A kind of: measure · Kinds: run, wear, drag on

  2. Continue to live through hardship or adversity.

    “We went without water and food for 3 days.” “These superstitions survive in the backwaters of America.” “The race car driver lived through several very serious accidents.”

    Synonyms: survive, live, live on, go, endure, hold up, hold out · Kinds: stand up, perennate, live out

Last as a noun

  1. The temporal end; the concluding time.

    “The stopping point of each round was signaled by a bell.” “The market was up at the finish.” “They were playing better at the close of the season.”

    Synonyms: stopping point, finale, finis, finish, conclusion, close · A kind of: end

  2. The last or lowest in an ordering or series.

    “He was the last to leave.” “He finished an inglorious last.”

    A kind of: rank

  3. A person's dying act; the final thing a person can do.

    “He breathed his last.”

    A kind of: activity

  4. The time at which life ends; continuing until dead.

    “She stayed until his death.” “A struggle to the last.”

    Synonyms: death · A kind of: end

  5. A unit of weight equal to 4,000 pounds.

    A kind of: weight unit

  6. A unit of capacity for grain equal to 80 bushels.

    A kind of: volume unit

  7. The concluding parts of an event or occurrence.

    “The end was exciting.” “I had to miss the last of the movie.”

    Synonyms: end, final stage · A kind of: ending · Kinds: endgame, endgame, homestretch, passing

  8. Holding device shaped like a human foot that is used to fashion or repair shoes.

    Synonyms: shoemaker's last, cobbler's last · A kind of: holding device

Last as an adverb

  1. Most_recently.

    “I saw him last in London.”

  2. The item at the end.

    “Last, I'll discuss family values.”

    Synonyms: lastly, in conclusion, finally

Phrases and compounds with "last"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at last (adverb) As the end result of a succession or process.
  • last day (noun) (New Testament) day at the end of time following Armageddon when God will decree the fates of all individual humans according to the good and evil of their earthly lives.
  • last out (verb) Hang on during a trial of endurance.
  • last gasp (noun) The point of death or exhaustion or completion.
  • last half (noun) The second of two halves of play.
  • last mile (noun) The last walk of a condemned person to the execution place.
  • last name (noun) The name used to identify the members of a family (as distinguished from each member's given name).
  • last word (noun) The final statement in a verbal argument.
  • every last (adjective) (used as intensive) every.
  • last laugh (noun) Ultimate success achieved after a near failure (inspired by the saying `he laughs best who laughs last').
  • last rites (noun) A Catholic sacrament; a priest anoints a dying person with oil and prays for salvation.
  • last straw (noun) The final irritation that stretches your patience beyond the limit.
  • last-ditch (adjective) Of something done as a final recourse (especially to prevent a crisis or disaster).
  • last-place (adjective) Lowest in rank or importance.
  • trade-last (noun) A compliment that I heard about you that I offer to trade for a compliment you have heard about me.
  • last hurrah (noun) A final performance or effort (especially before retirement).
