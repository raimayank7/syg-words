Lean: meaning, synonyms and examples

Lean is used as a verb (5 senses), adjective (4 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: To incline or bend from a vertical position.

In Hindi
झुकना; दुबला
Synonyms
tilt, tip, slant, angle, tend, be given, incline, run, list, thin
Opposites
rich
Word family
leaner, leanness

Lean as a verb

  1. To incline or bend from a vertical position.

    “She leaned over the banister.”

    Synonyms: tilt, tip, slant, angle · A kind of: bend · Kinds: slope, weather, list, lean back

  2. Cause to lean or incline.

    “He leaned his rifle against the wall.”

    A kind of: put

  3. Have a tendency or disposition to do or be something; be inclined.

    “She tends to be nervous before her lectures.” “These dresses run small.” “He inclined to corpulence.”

    Synonyms: tend, be given, incline, run · A kind of: be · Kinds: take kindly to, suffer, gravitate

  4. Rely on for support.

    “We can lean on this man.”

    A kind of: trust

  5. Cause to lean to the side.

    “Erosion listed the old tree.”

    Synonyms: list · A kind of: move

Lean as an adjective

  1. Lacking excess flesh.

    “You can't be too rich or too thin.” “Yon Cassius has a lean and hungry look.”

    Synonyms: thin · Similar: anorexic, bony, deep-eyed, gangling, lank, rawboned

  2. Lacking in mineral content or combustible material.

    “Lean ore.” “Lean fuel.”

    Opposite: rich

  3. Containing little excess.

    “A lean budget.” “A skimpy allowance.”

    Synonyms: skimpy · Similar: insufficient

  4. Not profitable or prosperous.

    “A lean year.”

    Similar: unprofitable

Lean as a noun

  1. The property possessed by a line or surface that departs from the vertical.

    “The tower had a pronounced tilt.” “The ship developed a list to starboard.” “He walked with a heavy inclination to the right.”

    Synonyms: tilt, list, inclination, leaning · A kind of: position

Phrases and compounds with "lean"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • lean on (verb) Rest on for support.
  • lean-to (noun) Rough shelter whose roof has only one slope.
  • lean back (verb) Move the upper body backwards and down.
  • lean against (verb) Rest on for support.
  • lean-to tent (noun) Tent that is attached to the side of a building.
