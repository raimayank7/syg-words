Letter: meaning, synonyms and examples

Letter is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A written message addressed to a person or organization.

In Hindi
पत्र, चिट्ठी; अक्षर
Synonyms
missive, letter of the alphabet, alphabetic character, varsity letter
Word family
let, letterer

Letter as a noun

  1. A written message addressed to a person or organization.

    “Mailed an indignant letter to the editor.”

    Synonyms: missive · A kind of: text, document · Kinds: business letter, covering letter, crank letter, encyclical, fan letter, personal letter, form letter, open letter

  2. The conventional characters of the alphabet used to represent speech.

    “His grandmother taught him his letters.”

    Synonyms: letter of the alphabet, alphabetic character · A kind of: character · Kinds: ascender, descender, digraph, initial, alpha, beta, gamma, delta

  3. Owner who lets another person use something (housing usually) for hire.

    A kind of: owner

  4. A strictly literal interpretation (as distinct from the intention).

    “He followed instructions to the letter.” “He obeyed the letter of the law.”

    A kind of: literal interpretation

  5. An award earned by participation in a school sport.

    “He won letters in three sports.”

    Synonyms: varsity letter · A kind of: award

Letter as a verb

  1. Win an athletic letter.

    A kind of: earn

  2. Set down or print with letters.

    A kind of: print

  3. Mark letters on or mark with letters.

    A kind of: write

Phrases and compounds with "letter"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • air letter (noun) A letter sent by air mail.
  • fan letter (noun) A letter that is a piece of fan mail.
  • letter box (noun) A numbered compartment in a post office where mail is put to be called for.
  • red-letter (adjective) Memorable for being a special occasion.
  • dead letter (noun) The state of something that has outlived its relevance.
  • form letter (noun) A letter that is printed in multiple copies and mailed to a list of recipients.
  • letter bond (noun) A bond that has not been registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission and cannot be sold to the general public.
  • letter case (noun) Case for carrying letters.
  • love letter (noun) A personal letter to a loved one expressing affection.
  • open letter (noun) A letter of protest; addressed to one person but intended for the general public.
  • black letter (noun) A heavy typeface in use from 15th to 18th centuries.
  • block letter (noun) A plain hand-drawn letter.
  • chain letter (noun) A letter that is sent successively to several people.
  • cover letter (noun) A letter sent along with other documents to provide additional information.
  • crank letter (noun) A hostile (usually anonymous) letter.
  • letter paper (noun) Writing paper for use in writing correspondence.
