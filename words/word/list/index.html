List: meaning, synonyms and examples

List is used as a noun (2 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A database containing an ordered array of items (names or topics).

In Hindi
सूची
Synonyms
listing, tilt, inclination, lean, leaning, name, heel, number
Word family
lister, listing

List as a noun

  1. A database containing an ordered array of items (names or topics).

    Synonyms: listing · A kind of: database · Kinds: agenda, bibliography, bill, blacklist, calendar, calorie chart, canon, catalog

  2. The property possessed by a line or surface that departs from the vertical.

    “The tower had a pronounced tilt.” “The ship developed a list to starboard.” “He walked with a heavy inclination to the right.”

    Synonyms: tilt, inclination, lean, leaning · A kind of: position

List as a verb

  1. Give or make a list of; name individually; give the names of.

    “List the states west of the Mississippi.”

    Synonyms: name · A kind of: enumerate

  2. Include in a list.

    “Am I listed in your register?”

    A kind of: register · Kinds: inventory, empanel, index, blacklist, post

  3. Cause to lean to the side.

    “Erosion listed the old tree.”

    Synonyms: lean · A kind of: move

  4. Tilt to one side.

    “The balloon heeled over.” “The wind made the vessel heel.” “The ship listed to starboard.”

    Synonyms: heel · A kind of: lean

  5. Enumerate.

    “We must number the names of the great mathematicians.”

    Synonyms: number · A kind of: name · Kinds: itemize

Phrases and compounds with "list"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • a-list (noun) A list of names of specially favored people.
  • free list (noun) A list of commodities that are not subject to tariffs.
  • play list (noun) A list of musical selections for performance or for broadcast by radio.
  • sick list (noun) A list of those who are ill (e.g. on a warship or in a regiment etc).
  • wish list (noun) A list of events that you wish would occur.
  • civil list (noun) A sum of money voted by British Parliament each year for the expenses of the British royal family.
  • class list (noun) A list issued by examiners that categorizes students according to the class of honours they achieved in their degree examinations.
  • list price (noun) The selling price of something as stated in a catalogue or price list; often subject to discounts.
  • price list (noun) A listing of prices for different goods or services.
  • short list (noun) A list of applicants winnowed from a longer list who have been deemed suitable and from which the successful person will be chosen.
  • stock list (noun) A detailed list of all the items in stock.
  • list system (noun) Based on the principle of proportional representation; voters choose between party lists, the number elected from each list being determined by the percentage cast for each list out of the total vote.
  • grocery list (noun) A list of heterogenous items that someone wants.
  • honours list (noun) A list issued by examiners that categorizes students according to the class of honours they achieved in their degree examinations.
  • mailing list (noun) A list of names and addresses to which advertising material is mailed.
  • waiting list (noun) A roster of those waiting to obtain something.
