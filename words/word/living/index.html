Living: meaning, synonyms and examples

Living is used as a noun (4 senses) and adjective (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: The experience of being alive; the course of human events and activities.

In Hindi
जीविका; जीवित
Synonyms
life, animation, aliveness, support, keep, livelihood, bread and butter, sustenance, surviving
Opposites
dead
Word family
live

Living as a noun

  1. The experience of being alive; the course of human events and activities.

    “He could no longer cope with the complexities of life.”

    Synonyms: life · A kind of: experience

  2. People who are still living.

    “Save your pity for the living.”

    Opposite: dead · A kind of: people

  3. The condition of living or the state of being alive.

    “While there's life there's hope.” “Life depends on many chemical and physical processes.”

    Synonyms: animation, life, aliveness · A kind of: being · Kinds: eternal life, skin, survival

  4. The financial means whereby one lives.

    “Each child was expected to pay for their keep.” “He applied to the state for support.” “He could no longer earn his own livelihood.”

    Synonyms: support, keep, livelihood, bread and butter, sustenance · A kind of: resource · Kinds: comforts, maintenance, meal ticket, subsistence

Living as an adjective

  1. Pertaining to living persons.

    “Within living memory.”

  2. True to life; lifelike.

    “The living image of her mother.”

    Similar: realistic

  3. (informal) absolute.

    “She is a living doll.” “Scared the living daylights out of them.”

    Similar: absolute

  4. Still in existence.

    “The Wollemi pine found in Australia is a surviving specimen of a conifer thought to have been long extinct and therefore known as a living fossil.” “The only surviving frontier blockhouse in Pennsylvania.”

    Synonyms: surviving · Similar: extant

  5. Still in active use.

    “A living language.”

    Similar: extant

  6. (used of minerals or stone) in its natural state and place; not mined or quarried.

    “Carved into the living stone.”

    Similar: live

Phrases and compounds with "living"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • non-living (adjective) Not endowed with life.
  • free living (noun) A lifestyle given to easy indulgence of the appetites.
  • free-living (adjective) Not parasitic on another organism.
  • living dead (noun) A dead body that has been brought back to life by a supernatural force.
  • living rock (noun) Usually unbranched usually spineless cactus covered with warty tubercles and having magenta flowers and white or green fruit; resembles the related mescal; northeastern Mexico and southwestern United States.
  • living room (noun) A room in a private house or establishment where people can sit and talk and relax.
  • living wage (noun) A wage sufficient for a worker and family to subsist comfortably.
  • living will (noun) A document written by someone still legally capable requesting that he should be allowed to die if subsequently severely disabled or suffering terminal illness.
  • living-room (noun) A room in a private house or establishment where people can sit and talk and relax.
  • tree-living (adjective) Inhabiting or frequenting trees.
  • clean-living (adjective) Morally pure.
  • living death (noun) A state of constant misery.
  • living space (noun) Space sought for occupation by a nation whose population is expanding.
  • living stone (noun) Any plant of the genus Lithops native to Africa having solitary yellow or white flowers and thick leaves that resemble stones.
  • living thing (noun) A living (or once living) entity.
  • living trust (noun) A trust created and operating during the grantor's lifetime.
