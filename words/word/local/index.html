Local: meaning, synonyms and examples

Local is used as an adjective (3 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Relating to or applicable to or concerned with the administration of a city or town or district rather than a larger area.

Local as an adjective

  1. Relating to or applicable to or concerned with the administration of a city or town or district rather than a larger area.

    “Local taxes.” “Local authorities.”

    Opposite: national

  2. Of or belonging to or characteristic of a particular locality or neighborhood.

    “Local customs.” “Local schools.” “The local citizens.”

  3. Affecting only a restricted part or area of the body.

    “Local anesthesia.”

    Opposite: general · Similar: localized, topical

Local as a noun

  1. Public transport consisting of a bus or train that stops at all stations or stops.

    “The local seemed to take forever to get to New York.”

    Opposite: express · A kind of: public transport

  2. Anesthetic that numbs a particular area of the body.

    Synonyms: local anesthetic, local anaesthetic, topical anesthetic, topical anaesthetic · Kinds: anesthyl, antipruritic, benzocaine, butacaine, ethyl chloride, procaine, tetracaine
