Long: meaning, synonyms and examples

Long is used as an adjective (9 senses), adverb (2 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Primarily temporal sense; being or indicating a relatively great or greater than average duration or passage of time or a duration as specified.

Long as an adjective

  1. Primarily temporal sense; being or indicating a relatively great or greater than average duration or passage of time or a duration as specified.

    “A long life.” “A long boring speech.” “A long time.”

    Opposite: short · Similar: agelong, bimestrial, chronic, daylong, drawn-out, durable

  2. Primarily spatial sense; of relatively great or greater than average spatial extension or extension as specified.

    “A long road.” “A long distance.” “Contained many long words.”

    Opposite: short · Similar: elongate, elongated, extendible, far, lank, long-handled

  3. Of relatively great height.

    “A race of long gaunt men.” “Looked out the long French windows.”

    Similar: tall

  4. Good at remembering.

    “A retentive mind.” “Tenacious memory.”

    Synonyms: retentive, recollective, tenacious

  5. Holding securities or commodities in expectation of a rise in prices.

    “Is long on coffee.” “A long position in gold.”

    Opposite: short

  6. (of speech sounds or syllables) of relatively long duration.

    “The English vowel sounds in `bate', `beat', `bite', `boat', `boot' are long.”

    Opposite: short

  7. Involving substantial risk.

    “Long odds.”

    Similar: unsound

  8. Planning prudently for the future.

    “Large goals that required farsighted policies.” “Took a long view of the geopolitical issues.”

    Synonyms: farseeing, farsighted, foresighted, foresightful, prospicient, longsighted · Similar: provident

  9. Having or being more than normal or necessary:"long on brains".

    “Long on brains.” “In long supply.”

    Similar: abundant

Long as an adverb

  1. For an extended time or at a distant time.

    “A promotion long overdue.” “Something long hoped for.” “His name has long been forgotten.”

  2. For an extended distance.

Long as a verb

  1. Desire strongly or persistently.

    Synonyms: hanker, yearn · A kind of: desire · Kinds: ache
