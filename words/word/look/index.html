Look: meaning, synonyms and examples

Look is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Perceive with attention; direct one's gaze towards.

In Hindi
देखना; रूप
Synonyms
appear, seem, search, front, face, attend, take care, see, expect, await
Word family
looker

Look as a verb

  1. Perceive with attention; direct one's gaze towards.

    “She looked over the expanse of land.” “Look at your child!” “Look--a deer in the backyard!”

    Kinds: stare, take a look, look back, look away, look around, gaze, regard, ogle

  2. Give a certain impression or have a certain outward aspect.

    “She seems to be sleeping.” “This appears to be a very difficult problem.” “This project looks fishy.”

    Synonyms: appear, seem · A kind of: be · Kinds: make, cut, feel, pass off, sound, come across, glow, glitter

  3. Have a certain outward or facial expression.

    “How does she look?” “The child looks unhappy.” “She looked pale after the surgery.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: squint

  4. Search or seek.

    “We looked all day and finally found the child in the forest.” “Look elsewhere for the perfect gift!”

    Synonyms: search · A kind of: examine · Kinds: hunt, prospect, intrude

  5. Be oriented in a certain direction, often with respect to another reference point; be opposite to.

    “The house looks north.” “My backyard look onto the pond.” “The building faces the park.”

    Synonyms: front, face · A kind of: lie · Kinds: confront

  6. Take charge of or deal with.

    “Could you see about lunch?” “I must attend to this matter.” “She took care of this business.”

    Synonyms: attend, take care, see · A kind of: care · Kinds: minister, tend

  7. Convey by one's expression.

    “She looked her devotion to me.”

    A kind of: convey

  8. Look forward to the probable occurrence of.

    “We were expecting a visit from our relatives.” “She is looking to a promotion.” “He is waiting to be drafted.”

    Synonyms: expect, await, wait · Kinds: expect, look forward, look to, hang on, anticipate

  9. Accord in appearance with.

    “You don't look your age!”

    A kind of: match

  10. Have faith or confidence in.

    “You can count on me to help you any time.” “Look to your friends for support.” “Depend on your family in times of crisis.”

    Synonyms: count, depend, calculate, reckon · A kind of: trust

Look as a noun

  1. The feelings expressed on a person's face.

    “A sad expression.” “A look of triumph.” “An angry face.”

    Synonyms: expression, aspect, facial expression, face · A kind of: countenance · Kinds: leer, sparkle

  2. The act of directing the eyes toward something and perceiving it visually.

    “He went out to have a look.” “His look was fixed on her eyes.” “He gave it a good looking at.”

    Synonyms: looking, looking at · A kind of: sensing · Kinds: glance, scrutiny, peek, squint, stare, evil eye, sightseeing, observation

  3. Physical appearance.

    “I don't like the looks of this place.”

    A kind of: appearance

  4. The general atmosphere of a place or situation and the effect that it has on people.

    “The feel of the city excited him.” “A clergyman improved the tone of the meeting.” “It had the smell of treason.”

    Synonyms: spirit, tone, feel, feeling, flavor, flavour, smell · A kind of: atmosphere

Phrases and compounds with "look"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • look at (verb) Take into consideration for exemplifying purposes.
  • look on (verb) Observe with attention.
  • look to (verb) Turn one's interests or expectations towards.
  • look up (verb) Seek information from.
  • look for (verb) Try to locate or discover, or try to establish the existence of.
  • look out (verb) Be vigilant, be on the lookout or be careful.
  • look away (verb) Avert one's gaze.
  • look back (verb) Look towards one's back.
  • look into (verb) Investigate scientifically.
  • look like (verb) Bear a physical resemblance to.
  • look upon (verb) Look on as or consider.
  • look-over (noun) A swift cursory examination or inspection.
  • side-look (noun) A glance sideways.
  • get a look (verb) See something for a brief time.
  • look after (verb) Keep under careful scrutiny.
  • look sharp (verb) Act or move at high speed.
