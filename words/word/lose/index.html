Lose: meaning, synonyms and examples

Lose is used as a verb (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: Fail to keep or to maintain; cease to have, either physically or in an abstract sense.

In Hindi
खोना, हारना
Synonyms
misplace, mislay, turn a loss, fall back, drop off, fall behind, recede, miss, suffer
Opposites
keep, win, find, profit, break even
Word family
loser

Lose as a verb

  1. Fail to keep or to maintain; cease to have, either physically or in an abstract sense.

    “She lost her purse when she left it unattended on her seat.”

    Opposite: keep · Kinds: white-out, sleep off

  2. Fail to win.

    “We lost the battle but we won the war.”

    Opposite: win · Kinds: go down, drop, take the count, drop one's serve

  3. Suffer the loss of a person through death or removal.

    “She lost her husband in the war.” “The couple that wanted to adopt the child lost her when the biological parents claimed her.”

    A kind of: suffer

  4. Place (something) where one cannot find it again.

    “I misplaced my eyeglasses.”

    Synonyms: misplace, mislay · A kind of: put

  5. Miss from one's possessions; lose sight of.

    “I've lost my glasses again!”

    Opposite: find · Kinds: forget

  6. Allow to go out of sight.

    “The detective lost the man he was shadowing after he had to stop at a red light.”

  7. Fail to make money in a business; make a loss or fail to profit.

    “I lost thousands of dollars on that bad investment!” “The company turned a loss after the first year.”

    Synonyms: turn a loss · Opposite: profit, break even

  8. Fail to get or obtain.

    “I lost the opportunity to spend a year abroad.”

    Opposite: win

  9. Retreat.

    Synonyms: fall back, drop off, fall behind, recede · A kind of: regress

  10. Fail to perceive or to catch with the senses or the mind.

    “I missed that remark.” “She missed his point.” “We lost part of what he said.”

    Synonyms: miss · Kinds: overlook

  11. Be set at a disadvantage.

    “This author really suffers in translation.”

    Synonyms: suffer · A kind of: worsen

Phrases and compounds with "lose"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • lose it (verb) Lose control of one's emotions.
  • lose track (verb) Fail to keep informed or aware.
  • lose weight (verb) Take off weight.
  • lose sight of (verb) Be no longer able to see.
  • lose one's temper (verb) Get very angry and fly into a rage.
