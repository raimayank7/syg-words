Loss: meaning, synonyms and examples

Loss is used as a noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Something that is lost.

In Hindi
हानि, नुक़सान
Synonyms
deprivation, red ink, red, personnel casualty, passing, departure, exit, expiration, going, release
Opposites
gain
Word family
lossy

Loss as a noun

  1. Something that is lost.

    “The car was a total loss.” “Loss of livestock left the rancher bankrupt.”

    A kind of: transferred property · Kinds: forfeit, financial loss, sacrifice, wastage

  2. Gradual decline in amount or activity.

    “Weight loss.” “A serious loss of business.”

    A kind of: decline · Kinds: epilation, reducing

  3. The act of losing someone or something.

    “Everyone expected him to win so his loss was a shock.”

    A kind of: failure · Kinds: default, capitulation

  4. The disadvantage that results from losing something.

    “His loss of credibility led to his resignation.” “Losing him is no great deprivation.”

    Synonyms: deprivation · A kind of: disadvantage

  5. The experience of losing a loved one.

    “He sympathized on the loss of their grandfather.”

    A kind of: experience

  6. The amount by which the cost of a business exceeds its revenue.

    “The company operated at a loss last year.” “The company operated in the red last year.”

    Synonyms: red ink, red · Opposite: gain · A kind of: sum · Kinds: paper loss, squeeze

  7. Military personnel lost by death or capture.

    Synonyms: personnel casualty · A kind of: casualty · Kinds: wound, sacrifice

  8. Euphemistic expressions for death.

    “Thousands mourned his passing.”

    Synonyms: passing, departure, exit, expiration, going, release · A kind of: death

Phrases and compounds with "loss"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at a loss (adjective) Filled with bewilderment.
  • loss ratio (noun) The ratio of the annual claims paid by an insurance company to the premiums received.
  • paper loss (noun) An unrealized loss on an investment calculated by subtracting the current market price from the investor's cost.
  • loss leader (noun) A featured article of merchandise sold at a loss in order to draw customers.
  • memory loss (noun) Partial or total loss of memory.
  • turn a loss (verb) Fail to make money in a business; make a loss or fail to profit.
  • capital loss (noun) The amount by which the purchase price of an asset exceeds the selling price; the loss is realized when the asset is sold.
  • hearing loss (noun) Partial or complete loss of hearing.
  • financial loss (noun) Loss of money or decrease in financial value.
  • profit and loss (noun) An account compiled at the end of an accounting period to show gross and net profit or loss.
  • stop-loss order (noun) An order to a broker to sell (buy) when the price of a security falls (rises) to a designated level.
  • loss of consciousness (noun) The occurrence of a loss of the ability to perceive and respond.
  • conductive hearing loss (noun) Hearing loss due to problems with the bones of the middle ear.
  • profit and loss account (noun) An account compiled at the end of an accounting period to show gross and net profit or loss.
  • profit-and-loss statement (noun) A financial statement that gives operating results for a specific period.
  • sensorineural hearing loss (noun) Hearing loss due to failure of the auditory nerve.
