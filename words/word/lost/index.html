Lost: meaning, synonyms and examples

Lost is used as an adjective (9 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: No longer in your possession or control; unable to be found or recovered.

In Hindi
खोया हुआ
Synonyms
confused, disoriented, missed, bemused, deep in thought, preoccupied, baffled, befuddled, bewildered, confounded
Opposites
found, saved, won

Lost as an adjective

  1. No longer in your possession or control; unable to be found or recovered.

    “A lost child.” “Lost friends.” “His lost book.”

    Opposite: found · Similar: mislaid, gone, missing, squandered, stray, straying

  2. Having lost your bearings; confused as to time or place or personal identity.

    “I frequently find myself disoriented when I come up out of the subway.” “The anesthetic left her completely disoriented.”

    Synonyms: confused, disoriented · Similar: unoriented

  3. Spiritually or physically doomed or destroyed.

    “Lost souls.” “A lost generation.” “A lost ship.”

    Opposite: saved · Similar: destroyed

  4. Not gained or won.

    “A lost battle.” “A lost prize.”

    Opposite: won · Similar: confiscate

  5. Incapable of being recovered or regained.

    “His lost honor.”

    Similar: unrecoverable

  6. Not caught with the senses or the mind.

    “Words lost in the din.”

    Synonyms: missed · Similar: incomprehensible

  7. Deeply absorbed in thought.

    “As distant and bemused as a professor listening to the prattling of his freshman class.” “Lost in thought.” “A preoccupied frown.”

    Synonyms: bemused, deep in thought, preoccupied · Similar: thoughtful

  8. Perplexed by many conflicting situations or statements; filled with bewilderment.

    “Obviously bemused by his questions.” “Bewildered and confused.” “A cloudy and confounded philosopher.”

    Synonyms: baffled, befuddled, bemused, bewildered, confounded, confused, mazed, mixed-up · Similar: perplexed

  9. Unable to function; without help.

    Synonyms: helpless · Similar: hopeless

Lost as a noun

  1. People who are destined to die soon.

    “The agony of the doomed was in his voice.”

    Synonyms: doomed · A kind of: people

Phrases and compounds with "lost"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • lost cause (noun) A defeated cause or a cause for which defeat is inevitable.
  • lost tribes (noun) The ten Tribes of Israel that were deported into captivity in Assyria around 720 BC (leaving only the tribes of Judah and Benjamin).
  • lost-and-found (noun) Repository in a public building where lost articles can be kept until their owners reclaim them.
  • won-lost record (noun) (sports) a record of win versus losses.
