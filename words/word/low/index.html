Low: meaning, synonyms and examples

Low is used as an adjective (10 senses), adverb (1 sense), noun (4 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Less than normal in degree or intensity or amount.

In Hindi
नीचा, कम
Synonyms
low-toned, low-pitched, abject, low-down, miserable, scummy, scurvy, humble, lowly, modest
Opposites
high
Word family
lowness

Low as an adjective

  1. Less than normal in degree or intensity or amount.

    “Low prices.” “The reservoir is low.”

    Opposite: high · Similar: debased, depressed, low-level, reduced

  2. Literal meanings; being at or having a relatively small elevation or upward extension.

    “Low ceilings.” “Low clouds.” “Low hills.”

    Opposite: high · Similar: deep, low-growing, low-level, low-lying, lowset, nether

  3. Very low in volume.

    “A low murmur.” “The low-toned murmur of the surf.”

    Synonyms: low-toned · Similar: soft

  4. Unrefined in character.

    “Low comedy.”

    Similar: unrefined

  5. Used of sounds and voices; low in pitch or frequency.

    Synonyms: low-pitched · Opposite: high · Similar: alto, baritone, bass, contrabass, throaty

  6. Of the most contemptible kind.

    “Abject cowardice.” “A low stunt to pull.” “A low-down sneak.”

    Synonyms: abject, low-down, miserable, scummy, scurvy · Similar: contemptible

  7. Low or inferior in station or quality.

    “A humble cottage.” “A lowly parish priest.” “A modest man of the people.”

    Synonyms: humble, lowly, modest, small · Similar: inferior

  8. No longer sufficient.

    “Supplies are low.” “Our funds are depleted.”

    Synonyms: depleted · Similar: insufficient

  9. Subdued or brought low in condition or status.

    “Brought low.” “A broken man.” “His broken spirit.”

    Synonyms: broken, crushed, humbled, humiliated · Similar: humble

  10. Filled with melancholy and despondency.

    “Gloomy at the thought of what he had to face.” “Gloomy predictions.” “A gloomy silence.”

    Synonyms: gloomy, grim, blue, depressed, dispirited, down, downcast, downhearted · Similar: dejected

Low as an adverb

  1. In a low position; near the ground.

    “The branches hung low.”

Low as a noun

  1. An air mass of lower pressure; often brings precipitation.

    “A low moved in over night bringing sleet and snow.”

    Synonyms: depression · A kind of: air mass

  2. British political cartoonist (born in New Zealand) who created the character Colonel Blimp (1891-1963).

  3. A low level or position or degree.

    “The stock market fell to a new low.”

    Opposite: high · A kind of: degree

  4. The lowest forward gear ratio in the gear box of a motor vehicle; used to start a car moving.

    Synonyms: first gear, first, low gear · A kind of: gear

Low as a verb

  1. Make a low noise, characteristic of bovines.

    Synonyms: moo · A kind of: utter
