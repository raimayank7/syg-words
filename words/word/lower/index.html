Lower: meaning, synonyms and examples

Lower is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Move something or somebody to a lower position.

In Hindi
निचला; नीचे करना
Synonyms
take down, let down, get down, bring down, lour, turn down, depress, frown, glower, lower berth
Opposites
raise
Word family
lowering

Lower as a verb

  1. Move something or somebody to a lower position.

    “Take down the vase from the shelf.”

    Synonyms: take down, let down, get down, bring down · Opposite: raise · A kind of: move · Kinds: reef, depress, dip, incline

  2. Set lower.

    “Lower a rating.” “Lower expectations.”

    Synonyms: lour · A kind of: devalue · Kinds: derate, subordinate

  3. Make lower or quieter.

    “Turn down the volume of a radio.”

    Synonyms: turn down, lour · A kind of: decrease

  4. Cause to drop or sink.

    “The lack of rain had depressed the water level in the reservoir.”

    Synonyms: depress · A kind of: change

  5. Look angry or sullen, wrinkle one's forehead, as if to signal disapproval.

    Synonyms: frown, glower, lour · A kind of: grimace · Kinds: scowl

Lower as a noun

  1. The lower of two berths.

    Synonyms: lower berth · A kind of: berth
