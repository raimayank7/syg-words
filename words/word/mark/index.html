Mark: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mark is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: Attach a tag or label to.

In Hindi
निशान; अंक
Synonyms
tag, label, distinguish, differentiate, commemorate, stigmatize, stigmatise, brand, denounce, notice
Word family
marking, marker

Mark as a verb

  1. Attach a tag or label to.

    “Label these bottles.”

    Synonyms: tag, label · A kind of: attach · Kinds: brand, point, point, point, calibrate, code, badge

  2. Designate as if by a mark.

    “This sign marks the border.”

    A kind of: bespeak · Kinds: blaze, dimension, signpost, signalize, buoy

  3. Be a distinctive feature, attribute, or trait; sometimes in a very positive sense.

    “His modesty distinguishes him from his peers.”

    Synonyms: distinguish, differentiate · A kind of: qualify · Kinds: characterize

  4. Mark by some ceremony or observation.

    “The citizens mark the anniversary of the revolution with a march and a parade.”

    Synonyms: commemorate · A kind of: observe

  5. Make or leave a mark on.

    “The scouts marked the trail.” “Ash marked the believers' foreheads.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: dot, stigmatize, raddle, striate, ink, dimple, spot, crisscross

  6. To accuse or condemn or openly or formally or brand as disgraceful.

    “He denounced the government action.” “She was stigmatized by society because she had a child out of wedlock.”

    Synonyms: stigmatize, stigmatise, brand, denounce · A kind of: label

  7. Notice or perceive.

    “She noted that someone was following her.” “Mark my words.”

    Synonyms: notice, note · Kinds: take notice

  8. Mark with a scar.

    “The skin disease scarred his face permanently.”

    Synonyms: scar, pock, pit · A kind of: deface · Kinds: pockmark, cicatrize

  9. Make small marks into the surface of.

    “Score the clay before firing it.”

    Synonyms: score, nock · A kind of: notch · Kinds: scarify, scotch, scribe, line

  10. Establish as the highest level or best performance.

    “Set a record.”

    Synonyms: set · A kind of: lay down

  11. Make underscoring marks.

    Synonyms: score · A kind of: record · Kinds: cancel

  12. Remove from a list.

    “Cross the name of the dead person off the list.”

    Synonyms: cross off, cross out, strike out, strike off · A kind of: take away

  13. Put a check mark on or near or next to.

    “Please check each name on the list.” “Tick off the items.” “Mark off the units.”

    Synonyms: check, check off, mark off, tick off, tick · A kind of: verify · Kinds: receipt

  14. Assign a grade or rank to, according to one's evaluation.

    “Grade tests.” “Score the SAT essays.” “Mark homework.”

    Synonyms: grade, score · A kind of: measure

Mark as a noun

  1. A number or letter indicating quality (especially of a student's performance).

    “She made good marks in algebra.” “Grade A milk.” “What was your score on your homework?”

    Synonyms: grade, score · A kind of: evaluation · Kinds: grade point, percentile, decile, quartile

  2. A distinguishing symbol.

    “The owner's mark was on all the sheep.”

    Synonyms: marker, marking · A kind of: symbol · Kinds: earmark, brand, trademark, authentication, postmark, watermark, broad arrow, milestone

  3. A reference point to shoot at.

    “His arrow hit the mark.”

    Synonyms: target · A kind of: reference point · Kinds: clout, drogue

  4. A visible indication made on a surface.

    “Some previous reader had covered the pages with dozens of marks.” “Paw prints were everywhere.”

    Synonyms: print · A kind of: indication · Kinds: fingerprint, footprint, trace, mintmark, stroke, hoofprint, line, line

  5. The impression created by doing something unusual or extraordinary that people notice and remember.

    “It was in London that he made his mark.” “He left an indelible mark on the American theater.”

    A kind of: impression

  6. A symbol of disgrace or infamy.

    “And the Lord set a mark upon Cain.”

    Synonyms: stigma, brand, stain · A kind of: symbol · Kinds: demerit, bar sinister, cloven hoof

  7. Formerly the basic unit of money in Germany.

  8. Apostle and companion of Saint Peter; assumed to be the author of the second Gospel.

  9. A person who is gullible and easy to take advantage of.

    Synonyms: chump, fool, gull, patsy, fall guy, sucker, soft touch, mug · A kind of: victim

  10. A written or printed symbol (as for punctuation).

    “His answer was just a punctuation mark.”

    A kind of: written symbol · Kinds: arrow, broad arrow, call mark, caret, check mark, diacritical mark, ditto mark, dollar mark

  11. A perceptible indication of something not immediately apparent (as a visible clue that something has happened).

    “He showed signs of strain.” “They welcomed the signs of spring.”

    Synonyms: sign · A kind of: clue · Kinds: token

  12. The shortest of the four Gospels in the New Testament.

  13. An indication of damage.

    Synonyms: scratch, scrape, scar · A kind of: blemish

  14. A marking that consists of lines that cross each other.

    Synonyms: crisscross, cross · A kind of: marking

Phrases and compounds with "mark"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • mark up (verb) Increase the price of.
  • mark off (verb) Set boundaries to and delimit.
  • mark out (verb) Set boundaries to and delimit.
  • burn mark (noun) A place or area that has been burned (especially on a person's body).
  • call mark (noun) A mark consisting of characters written on a book; used to indicate shelf location.
  • date-mark (verb) Mark with a date and place.
  • easy mark (noun) A defenseless victim.
  • hash mark (noun) An insignia worn to indicate years of service.
  • hoof mark (noun) A visible impression on a surface made by the hoof of an animal.
  • hoof-mark (noun) A visible impression on a surface made by the hoof of an animal.
  • mark down (verb) Reduce the price of.
  • assay-mark (noun) A mark on an article of trade to indicate its origin and authenticity.
  • bench mark (noun) A surveyor's mark on a permanent object of predetermined position and elevation used as a reference point.
  • check mark (noun) A mark indicating that something has been noted or completed etc.
  • ditto mark (noun) A mark used to indicate the word above it should be repeated.
  • mark clark (noun) United States general who was Allied commander in Africa and Italy in World War II and was commander of the United Nations forces in Korea (1896-1984).
