Match: meaning, synonyms and examples

Match is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be compatible, similar or consistent; coincide in their characteristics.

In Hindi
मैच; माचिस; मेल खाना
Synonyms
fit, correspond, check, jibe, gibe, tally, agree, mate, couple, pair
Word family
matcher

Match as a verb

  1. Be compatible, similar or consistent; coincide in their characteristics.

    “The two stories don't agree in many details.” “The handwriting checks with the signature on the check.” “The suspect's fingerprints don't match those on the gun.”

    Synonyms: fit, correspond, check, jibe, gibe, tally, agree · A kind of: equal · Kinds: consist, look, answer, coincide, align, correlate, parallel, twin

  2. Provide funds complementary to.

    “The company matched the employees' contributions.”

    A kind of: supply

  3. Bring two objects, ideas, or people together.

    “This fact is coupled to the other one.” “Matchmaker, can you match my daughter with a nice young man?” “The student was paired with a partner for collaboration on the project.”

    Synonyms: mate, couple, pair, twin · A kind of: join · Kinds: mismate, mismatch

  4. Be equal to in quality or ability.

    “Nothing can rival cotton for durability.” “Your performance doesn't even touch that of your colleagues.” “Her persistence and ambition only matches that of her parents.”

    Synonyms: equal, touch, rival · A kind of: compete

  5. Make correspond or harmonize.

    “Match my sweater.”

    Synonyms: fit · A kind of: adjust · Kinds: balance

  6. Satisfy or fulfill.

    “Meet a need.” “This job doesn't match my dreams.”

    Synonyms: meet, cope with · A kind of: cope

  7. Give or join in marriage.

    A kind of: join

  8. Set into opposition or rivalry.

    “Let them match their best athletes against ours.” “Pit a chess player against the Russian champion.” “He plays his two children off against each other.”

    Synonyms: pit, oppose, play off · A kind of: confront

  9. Be equal or harmonize.

    “The two pieces match.”

    A kind of: adjust

  10. Make equal, uniform, corresponding, or matching.

    “Let's equalize the duties among all employees in this office.” “The company matched the discount policy of its competitors.”

    Synonyms: equal, equalize, equalise, equate · A kind of: change · Kinds: homologize, homogenize, tie

Match as a noun

  1. Lighter consisting of a thin piece of wood or cardboard tipped with combustible chemical; ignites with friction.

    “He always carries matches to light his pipe.” “As long you've a lucifer to light your fag.”

    Synonyms: lucifer, friction match · A kind of: lighter · Kinds: fusee, kitchen match, safety match, slow match

  2. A formal contest in which two or more persons or teams compete.

    A kind of: contest · Kinds: boxing match, chess match, cockfight, cricket match, diving, field event, final, quarterfinal

  3. A burning piece of wood or cardboard.

    “If you drop a match in there the whole place will explode.”

    A kind of: lighter

  4. An exact duplicate.

    “When a match is found an entry is made in the notebook.”

    Synonyms: mate · A kind of: duplicate

  5. The score needed to win a match.

    A kind of: score

  6. A person regarded as a good matrimonial prospect.

    Synonyms: catch · A kind of: adult

  7. A person who is of equal standing with another in a group.

    Synonyms: peer, equal, compeer · A kind of: person · Kinds: associate, contemporary, gangsta, stand-in, successor, townsman

  8. A pair of people who live together.

    “A married couple from Chicago.”

    Synonyms: couple, mates · A kind of: family · Kinds: power couple

  9. Something that resembles or harmonizes with.

    “That tie makes a good match with your jacket.”

    A kind of: counterpart

Phrases and compounds with "match"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • match-up (noun) The pairing of people or things as for comparison or competition.
  • love match (noun) A marriage for love's sake; not an arranged marriage.
  • match game (noun) An international championship match.
  • match play (noun) Golf scoring by holes won.
  • slow match (noun) Match or fuse made to burn slowly and evenly.
  • test match (noun) An international championship match.
  • chess match (noun) A match between chess players.
  • match plane (noun) A plane having cutters designed to make the tongues and grooves on the edges of matchboards.
  • match point (noun) (tennis) the final point needed to win a match (especially in tennis).
  • boxing match (noun) A match between boxers; usually held in a boxing ring.
  • safety match (noun) A paper match that strikes only on a specially prepared surface.
  • tennis match (noun) A match between tennis players.
  • cricket match (noun) A match between two cricket teams.
  • kitchen match (noun) A wooden friction match that will light on any granular surface; useful to light wood or gas stoves.
  • friction match (noun) Lighter consisting of a thin piece of wood or cardboard tipped with combustible chemical; ignites with friction.
  • sparring match (noun) A practice or exhibition boxing match.
