Mate: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mate is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The officer below the master on a commercial ship.

In Hindi
साथी, जोड़ीदार
Synonyms
first mate, teammate, spouse, partner, married person, better half, match, fellow, checkmate, couple

Mate as a noun

  1. The officer below the master on a commercial ship.

    Synonyms: first mate · A kind of: officer

  2. A fellow member of a team.

    “It was his first start against his former teammates.”

    Synonyms: teammate · A kind of: associate

  3. A person's partner in marriage.

    Synonyms: spouse, partner, married person, better half · A kind of: relative · Kinds: bigamist, consort, helpmate, husband, monogamist, newlywed, polygamist, wife

  4. An exact duplicate.

    “When a match is found an entry is made in the notebook.”

    Synonyms: match · A kind of: duplicate

  5. One of a pair.

    “He lost the mate to his shoe.” “One eye was blue but its fellow was brown.”

    Synonyms: fellow · A kind of: singleton

  6. South American holly; leaves used in making a drink like tea.

    A kind of: holly

  7. South American tea-like drink made from leaves of a South American holly called mate.

    A kind of: beverage

  8. A chess move constituting an inescapable and indefensible attack on the opponent's king.

    Synonyms: checkmate · A kind of: chess move

Mate as a verb

  1. Bring two objects, ideas, or people together.

    “This fact is coupled to the other one.” “Matchmaker, can you match my daughter with a nice young man?” “The student was paired with a partner for collaboration on the project.”

    Synonyms: match, couple, pair, twin · A kind of: join · Kinds: mismate, mismatch

  2. Place an opponent's king under an attack from which it cannot escape and thus ending the game.

    “Kasparov checkmated his opponent after only a few moves.”

    Synonyms: checkmate · A kind of: beat

Phrases and compounds with "mate"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • soul mate (noun) Someone for whom you have a deep affinity.
  • first mate (noun) The officer below the master on a commercial ship.
  • running mate (noun) A nominee for the lesser of two closely related political offices.
  • sparring mate (noun) A boxer who spars with another boxer who is training for an important fight.
