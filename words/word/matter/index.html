Matter: meaning, synonyms and examples

Matter is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A vaguely specified concern.

In Hindi
मामला; पदार्थ; मायने रखना
Synonyms
affair, thing, topic, subject, issue, count, weigh

Matter as a noun

  1. A vaguely specified concern.

    “Several matters to attend to.” “It is none of your affair.” “Things are going well.”

    Synonyms: affair, thing · A kind of: concern · Kinds: least

  2. Some situation or event that is thought about.

    “He kept drifting off the topic.” “He had been thinking about the subject for several years.” “It is a matter for the police.”

    Synonyms: topic, subject, issue · A kind of: content · Kinds: area, blind spot, remit, res judicata

  3. That which has mass and occupies space.

    “Physicists study both the nature of matter and the forces which govern it.”

    A kind of: physical entity · Kinds: substance, substance, sediment, ylem, dark matter, antimatter, glop, fluid

  4. A problem.

    “Is anything the matter?”

    A kind of: trouble

  5. (used with negation) having consequence.

    “They were friends and it was no matter who won the games.”

    A kind of: consequence

  6. Written works (especially in books or magazines).

    “He always took some reading matter with him on the plane.”

    A kind of: writing · Kinds: dictation, text, text, typescript, front matter, back matter, soft copy, hard copy

Matter as a verb

  1. Have weight; have import, carry weight.

    “It does not matter much.”

    Synonyms: count, weigh · A kind of: be · Kinds: weigh

Phrases and compounds with "matter"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • matter to (verb) Be of importance or consequence.
  • no matter (adverb) In spite of everything; without regard to drawbacks.
  • end matter (noun) Written matter following the main text of a book.
  • back matter (noun) Written matter following the main text of a book.
  • dark matter (noun) (cosmology) a hypothetical form of matter that is believed to make up 90 percent of the universe; it is invisible (does not absorb or emit light) and does not collide with atomic particles but exerts gravitational force.
  • gray matter (noun) Greyish nervous tissue containing cell bodies as well as fibers; forms the cerebral cortex consisting of unmyelinated neurons.
  • grey matter (noun) Greyish nervous tissue containing cell bodies as well as fibers; forms the cerebral cortex consisting of unmyelinated neurons.
  • fecal matter (noun) Solid excretory product evacuated from the bowels.
  • front matter (noun) Written matter preceding the main text of a book.
  • waste matter (noun) Any materials unused and rejected as worthless or unwanted.
  • white matter (noun) Whitish nervous tissue of the CNS consisting of neurons and their myelin sheaths.
  • faecal matter (noun) Solid excretory product evacuated from the bowels.
  • matter of law (noun) A disputed legal contention that is generally left for a judge to decide.
  • matter of fact (noun) A disputed factual contention that is generally left for a jury to decide.
  • matter-of-fact (adjective) Not fanciful or imaginative.
  • subject matter (noun) What a communication that is about something is about.
