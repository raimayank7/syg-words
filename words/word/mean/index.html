Mean: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mean is used as a verb (7 senses), adjective (8 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Mean or intend to express or convey.

In Hindi
मतलब होना; कंजूस, नीच
Synonyms
intend, entail, imply, signify, stand for, think, think of, have in mind, average, hateful
Word family
meaning, meanness

Mean as a verb

  1. Mean or intend to express or convey.

    “You never understand what I mean!” “What do his words intend?”

    Synonyms: intend · A kind of: convey · Kinds: typify, signify, spell, aim, drive

  2. Have as a logical consequence.

    “The water shortage means that we have to stop taking long showers.”

    Synonyms: entail, imply · A kind of: necessitate

  3. Denote or connote.

    “`maison' means `house' in French.” “An example sentence would show what this word means.”

    Synonyms: intend, signify, stand for · Kinds: denote

  4. Have in mind as a purpose.

    “I mean no harm.” “I only meant to help you.” “She didn't think to harm me.”

    Synonyms: intend, think · Kinds: design, plan, aim

  5. Have a specified degree of importance.

    “My ex-husband means nothing to me.” “Happiness means everything.”

  6. Intend to refer to.

    “I'm thinking of good food when I talk about France.” “Yes, I meant you when I complained about people who gossip!”

    Synonyms: think of, have in mind · A kind of: associate · Kinds: mention

  7. Destine or designate for a certain purpose.

    “These flowers were meant for you.”

    A kind of: intend

Mean as an adjective

  1. Approximating the statistical norm or average or expected value.

    “The average income in New England is below that of the nation.” “Of average height for his age.” “The mean annual rainfall.”

    Synonyms: average · Similar: normal

  2. Characterized by malice.

    “A hateful thing to do.” “In a mean mood.”

    Synonyms: hateful

  3. Having or showing an ignoble lack of honor or morality.

    “That liberal obedience without which your army would be a base rabble.” “Taking a mean advantage.” “Chok'd with ambition of the meaner sort.”

    Synonyms: base, meanspirited · Similar: ignoble

  4. Excellent.

    “Famous for a mean backhand.”

    Similar: skilled

  5. Marked by poverty befitting a beggar.

    “A beggarly existence in the slums.” “A mean hut.”

    Synonyms: beggarly · Similar: poor

  6. (used of persons or behavior) characterized by or indicative of lack of generosity.

    “A mean person.” “He left a miserly tip.”

    Synonyms: mingy, miserly, tight · Similar: stingy

  7. (used of sums of money) so small in amount as to deserve contempt.

    Synonyms: beggarly · Similar: stingy

  8. Of no value or worth.

    “I was caught in the bastardly traffic.”

    Synonyms: bastardly · Similar: contemptible

Mean as a noun

  1. An average of n numbers computed by adding some function of the numbers and dividing by some function of n.

    Synonyms: mean value · A kind of: average · Kinds: arithmetic mean, geometric mean, harmonic mean

Phrases and compounds with "mean"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • mean sun (noun) A theoretical sun that moves along the celestial equator at a constant speed and completes its annual course in the same amount of time the real sun takes at variable speeds.
  • mean time (noun) (astronomy) time based on the motion of the mean sun (an imaginary sun moving uniformly along the celestial equator).
  • mean value (noun) An average of n numbers computed by adding some function of the numbers and dividing by some function of n.
  • golden mean (noun) The proportional relation between two divisions of line or two dimension of a plane figure such that short : long :: long : (short + long).
  • harmonic mean (noun) The mean of n numbers expressed as the reciprocal of the arithmetic mean of the reciprocals of the numbers.
  • mean distance (noun) The arithmetic mean of the maximum and minimum distances of a celestial body (satellite or secondary star) from its primary.
  • geometric mean (noun) The mean of n numbers expressed as the n-th root of their product.
  • mean deviation (noun) The arithmetic mean of the absolute values of deviations from the mean of a distribution.
  • mean solar day (noun) Time for Earth to make a complete rotation on its axis.
  • arithmetic mean (noun) The sum of the values of a random variable divided by the number of values.
  • mean solar time (noun) (astronomy) time based on the motion of the mean sun (an imaginary sun moving uniformly along the celestial equator).
  • greenwich mean time (noun) The local time at the 0 meridian passing through Greenwich, England; it is the same everywhere.
  • regression toward the mean (noun) The relation between selected values of x and observed values of y (from which the most probable value of y can be predicted for any value of x).
  • mean deviation from the mean (noun) The arithmetic mean of the absolute values of deviations from the mean of a distribution.
