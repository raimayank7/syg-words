Measure: meaning, synonyms and examples

Measure is used as a verb (4 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Determine the measurements of something or somebody, take measurements of.

In Hindi
नापना; माप; उपाय
Synonyms
mensurate, measure out, quantify, evaluate, valuate, assess, appraise, value, step, quantity
Word family
measurable, measurement, measurer, measuring

Measure as a verb

  1. Determine the measurements of something or somebody, take measurements of.

    “Measure the length of the wall.”

    Synonyms: mensurate, measure out · A kind of: decide · Kinds: shoot, triangulate, caliper, calibrate

  2. Express as a number or measure or quantity.

    “Can you quantify your results?”

    Synonyms: quantify · A kind of: carry · Kinds: gauge, scale, meter, pace, clock, fathom, titrate, plumb

  3. Have certain dimensions.

    “This table surfaces measures 20inches by 36 inches.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: stand, weigh, last, scale

  4. Evaluate or estimate the nature, quality, ability, extent, or significance of.

    “I will have the family jewels appraised by a professional.” “Access all the factors when taking a risk.”

    Synonyms: evaluate, valuate, assess, appraise, value · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: grade, rate, standardize, reassess, censor, praise

Measure as a noun

  1. Any maneuver made as part of progress toward a goal.

    “The situation called for strong measures.” “The police took steps to reduce crime.”

    Synonyms: step · A kind of: maneuver · Kinds: countermeasure, shark repellent, precaution

  2. How much there is or how many there are of something that you can quantify.

    Synonyms: quantity, amount · A kind of: abstraction · Kinds: probability, quantum, value, fundamental quantity, definite quantity, indefinite quantity, relative quantity, system of measurement

  3. A statute in draft before it becomes law.

    “They held a public hearing on the bill.”

    Synonyms: bill · A kind of: legal document · Kinds: appropriation bill, bill of attainder, bottle bill, farm bill, trade bill

  4. The act or process of assigning numbers to phenomena according to a rule.

    “The measurements were carefully done.” “His mental measurings proved remarkably accurate.”

    Synonyms: measurement, measuring, mensuration · A kind of: activity · Kinds: seismography, quantitative analysis, actinometry, algometry, anemography, anemometry, angulation, anthropometry

  5. A basis for comparison; a reference point against which other things can be evaluated.

    “The schools comply with federal standards.” “They set the measure for all subsequent work.”

    Synonyms: standard, criterion, touchstone · A kind of: system of measurement · Kinds: benchmark, earned run average, grade point average, procrustean standard, yardstick, medium of exchange, scale, gauge

  6. (prosody) the accent in a metrical foot of verse.

    Synonyms: meter, metre, beat, cadence · A kind of: poetic rhythm · Kinds: catalexis, scansion, common measure, metrical foot

  7. Musical notation for a repeating pattern of musical beats.

    “The orchestra omitted the last twelve bars of the song.”

    Synonyms: bar · A kind of: musical notation

  8. Measuring instrument having a sequence of marks at regular intervals; used as a reference in making measurements.

    Synonyms: measuring stick, measuring rod · A kind of: measuring instrument · Kinds: board rule, rule, size stick

  9. A container of some standard capacity that is used to obtain fixed amounts of a substance.

    A kind of: container · Kinds: measuring cup

Phrases and compounds with "measure"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • measure up (verb) Prove capable or fit; meet requirements.
  • dry measure (noun) A unit of capacity for dry commodities (as fruit or grain).
  • measure out (verb) Determine the measurements of something or somebody, take measurements of.
  • long measure (noun) A measure of length.
  • tape measure (noun) Measuring instrument consisting of a narrow strip (cloth or metal) marked in inches or centimeters and used for measuring lengths.
  • yard measure (noun) A ruler or tape that is three feet long.
  • board measure (noun) A system of units for measuring lumber based on the board foot.
  • cubic measure (noun) A unit of measurement of volume or capacity.
  • beyond measure (adverb) In excess or without limit.
  • common measure (noun) A time signature indicating four beats to the bar.
  • linear measure (noun) A unit of measurement of length.
  • liquid measure (noun) A unit of capacity for liquids (for measuring the volumes of liquids or their containers).
  • square measure (noun) A system of units used to measure areas.
  • capacity measure (noun) A unit of measurement of volume or capacity.
  • circular measure (noun) Measurement of angles in radians.
  • for good measure (adverb) In addition (as to close a deal).
