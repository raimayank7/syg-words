Meeting: meaning, synonyms and examples

Meeting is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A formally arranged gathering.

In Hindi
बैठक, सभा
Synonyms
group meeting, get together, encounter, coming together, merging, confluence
Word family
meet

Meeting as a noun

  1. A formally arranged gathering.

    “Next year the meeting will be in Chicago.” “The meeting elected a chairperson.”

    Synonyms: group meeting · A kind of: gathering · Kinds: board meeting, camp meeting, caucus, conclave, conference, congress, convention, council

  2. A small informal social gathering.

    “There was an informal meeting in my living room.”

    Synonyms: get together · A kind of: social gathering · Kinds: conventicle, date, visit, luncheon meeting, power breakfast, seance

  3. A casual or unexpected convergence.

    “He still remembers their meeting in Paris.” “There was a brief encounter in the hallway.”

    Synonyms: encounter · A kind of: convergence · Kinds: conjunction

  4. The social act of assembling for some common purpose.

    “His meeting with the salesmen was the high point of his day.”

    Synonyms: coming together · A kind of: assembly · Kinds: rendezvous, session, socialization, visit, visit, visit

  5. The act of joining together as one.

    “The merging of the two groups occurred quickly.” “There was no meeting of minds.”

    Synonyms: merging, coming together · A kind of: convergence · Kinds: concourse

  6. A place where things merge or flow together (especially rivers).

    “Pittsburgh is located at the confluence of the Allegheny and Monongahela rivers.”

    Synonyms: confluence · A kind of: geographic point

Phrases and compounds with "meeting"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • camp meeting (noun) Religious (usually evangelistic) meeting held in a large tent or outdoors and lasting several days.
  • mass meeting (noun) A large gathering of people intended to arouse enthusiasm.
  • race meeting (noun) A regular occasion on which a number of horse races are held on the same track.
  • town meeting (noun) Government of a town by an assembly of the qualified voters.
  • board meeting (noun) A meeting for administrative purposes.
  • go-to-meeting (adjective) Used of clothing.
  • group meeting (noun) A formally arranged gathering.
  • lunch meeting (noun) A meeting for lunch; usually to conduct business while eating.
  • meeting house (noun) Columbine of eastern North America having long-spurred red flowers.
  • meeting place (noun) A public facility to meet for open discussion.
  • prayer meeting (noun) A service at which people sing hymns and pray together.
  • sports meeting (noun) A meeting at which a number of athletic contests are held.
  • summit meeting (noun) A meeting of heads of governments.
  • revival meeting (noun) An evangelistic meeting intended to reawaken interest in religion.
  • luncheon meeting (noun) A meeting for lunch; usually to conduct business while eating.
  • meeting of minds (noun) A state of cooperation.
