Milk: meaning, synonyms and examples

Milk is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A white nutritious liquid secreted by mammals and used as food by human beings.

Milk as a noun

  1. A white nutritious liquid secreted by mammals and used as food by human beings.

    A kind of: dairy product, beverage · Kinds: sour milk, pasteurized milk, cows' milk, yak's milk, goats' milk, acidophilus milk, raw milk, scalded milk

  2. Produced by mammary glands of female mammals for feeding their young.

    A kind of: liquid body substance, nutriment · Kinds: mother's milk, colostrum

  3. A river that rises in the Rockies in northwestern Montana and flows eastward to become a tributary of the Missouri River.

  4. Any of several nutritive milklike liquids.

    A kind of: foodstuff · Kinds: coconut milk, coconut milk, soya milk, formula

Milk as a verb

  1. Take milk from female mammals.

    “Cows need to be milked every morning.”

    A kind of: draw · Kinds: strip

  2. Exploit as much as possible.

    “I am milking this for all it's worth.”

    A kind of: exploit

  3. Add milk to.

    “Milk the tea.”

    A kind of: add

Phrases and compounds with "milk"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • dry milk (noun) Dehydrated milk.
  • ice milk (noun) Similar to ice cream but made of milk.
  • milk bar (noun) Snack bar that sells milk drinks and light refreshments (such as ice cream).
  • milk can (noun) Large can for transporting milk.
  • milk cow (noun) Cattle that are reared for their milk.
  • milk leg (noun) Painful thrombosis of the femoral vein in the leg following childbirth.
  • milk pox (noun) A mild form of smallpox caused by a less virulent form of the virus.
  • raw milk (noun) Unpasteurized milk.
  • milk whey (noun) The serum or watery part of milk that is separated from the curd in making cheese.
  • milk-sick (adjective) Affected with or related to milk sickness.
  • skim milk (noun) Milk from which the cream has been skimmed.
  • sour milk (noun) Milk that has turned sour.
  • soya milk (noun) A milk substitute containing soybean flour and water; used in some infant formulas and in making tofu.
  • cows' milk (noun) Milk obtained from dairy cows.
  • dried milk (noun) Dehydrated milk.
  • milk adder (noun) Nonvenomous tan and brown king snake with an arrow-shaped occipital spot; southeastern ones have red stripes like coral snakes.
