Mind: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mind is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: That which is responsible for one's thoughts and feelings; the seat of the faculty of reason.

In Hindi
मन, दिमाग़; बुरा मानना
Synonyms
head, brain, psyche, nous, judgment, judgement, thinker, creative thinker, idea, intellect
Opposites
forget

Mind as a noun

  1. That which is responsible for one's thoughts and feelings; the seat of the faculty of reason.

    “His mind wandered.” “I couldn't get his words out of my head.”

    Synonyms: head, brain, psyche, nous · A kind of: cognition · Kinds: noddle, tabula rasa, ego, unconscious mind, subconscious mind

  2. Recall or remembrance.

    “It came to mind.”

    A kind of: recall

  3. An opinion formed by judging something.

    “He was reluctant to make his judgment known.” “She changed her mind.”

    Synonyms: judgment, judgement · A kind of: opinion · Kinds: decision

  4. An important intellectual.

    “The great minds of the 17th century.”

    Synonyms: thinker, creative thinker · A kind of: intellectual

  5. Attention.

    “Don't pay him any mind.”

    A kind of: notice

  6. Your intention; what you intend to do.

    “He had in mind to see his old teacher.” “The idea of the game is to capture all the pieces.”

    Synonyms: idea · A kind of: purpose

  7. Knowledge and intellectual ability.

    “He reads to improve his mind.” “He has a keen intellect.”

    Synonyms: intellect · A kind of: intelligence

Mind as a verb

  1. Be offended or bothered by; take offense with, be bothered by.

    “I don't mind your behavior.”

    A kind of: object · Kinds: bristle at

  2. Be concerned with or about something or somebody.

    A kind of: think about · Kinds: worry

  3. Be in charge of or deal with.

    “She takes care of all the necessary arrangements.”

    Synonyms: take care · A kind of: manage · Kinds: tend

  4. Pay close attention to; give heed to.

    “Heed the advice of the old men.”

    Synonyms: heed, listen · A kind of: obey

  5. Be on one's guard; be cautious or wary about; be alert to.

    “Beware of telephone salesmen.”

    Synonyms: beware · A kind of: watch

  6. Keep in mind.

    Synonyms: bear in mind · Opposite: forget · A kind of: remember · Kinds: attend to

Phrases and compounds with "mind"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • mind-set (noun) A habitual or characteristic mental attitude that determines how you will interpret and respond to situations.
  • mind game (noun) Any game designed to exercise the intellect.
  • mind reader (noun) Someone with the power of communicating thoughts directly.
  • bear in mind (verb) Keep in mind.
  • come to mind (verb) Be remembered.
  • have in mind (verb) Intend to refer to.
  • mind-bending (adjective) Intensely affecting the mind especially in producing hallucinations.
  • mind-blowing (adjective) Intensely affecting the mind especially in producing hallucinations.
  • frame of mind (noun) A temporary psychological state.
  • mind-altering (adjective) Producing mood changes or distorted perception.
  • mind-boggling (adjective) Intellectually or emotionally overwhelming.
  • of sound mind (adjective) Of sound mind, memory, and understanding; in law, competent to go to trial.
  • peace of mind (noun) The absence of mental stress or anxiety.
  • state of mind (noun) A temporary psychological state.
  • change of mind (noun) A decision to reverse an earlier decision.
  • spring to mind (verb) Be remembered.
