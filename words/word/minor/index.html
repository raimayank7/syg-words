Minor: meaning, synonyms and examples

Minor is used as an adjective (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Of lesser importance or stature or rank.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites
major
Word family

Minor as an adjective

  1. Of lesser importance or stature or rank.

    “A minor poet.” “Had a minor part in the play.” “A minor official.”

    Opposite: major · Similar: insignificant, secondary

  2. Lesser in scope or effect.

    “Had minor differences.” “A minor disturbance.”

    Opposite: major

  3. Inferior in number or size or amount.

    “A minor share of the profits.” “Ursa Minor.”

    Opposite: major

  4. Of a scale or mode.

    “The minor keys.” “In B flat minor.”

    Opposite: major

  5. Not of legal age.

    “Minor children.”

    Synonyms: nonaged, underage · Opposite: major

  6. Of lesser seriousness or danger.

    “Suffered only minor injuries.” “Some minor flooding.” “A minor tropical disturbance.”

    Opposite: major

  7. Of your secondary field of academic concentration or specialization.

    Opposite: major

  8. Of the younger of two boys with the same family name.

    “Jones minor.”

    Similar: junior

  9. Warranting only temporal punishment.

    “Venial sin.”

    Synonyms: venial · Similar: pardonable

  10. Limited in size or scope.

    “A small business.” “A newspaper with a modest circulation.” “Small-scale plans.”

    Synonyms: modest, small, small-scale, pocket-size, pocket-sized · Similar: limited

Phrases and compounds with "minor"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • minor key (noun) A key based on the minor scale.
  • asia minor (noun) A peninsula in southwestern Asia that forms the Asian part of Turkey.
  • minor axis (noun) The shorter or shortest axis of an ellipse or ellipsoid.
  • minor mode (noun) A key based on the minor scale.
  • minor role (noun) A small role.
  • minor suit (noun) ( bridge) a suit of inferior scoring value, either diamonds or clubs.
  • minor term (noun) The term in a syllogism that is the subject of the conclusion.
  • ursa minor (noun) A constellation outside the zodiac that rotates around the North Star.
  • canis minor (noun) A constellation to the east of Orion; contains Procyon.
  • lemna minor (noun) Of temperate regions except eastern Asia and Australia.
  • minor scale (noun) A diatonic scale with notes separated by whole tones except for the 2nd and 3rd and 5th and 6th.
  • teres minor (noun) Teres muscle that adducts the arm and rotates it laterally.
  • vinca minor (noun) Widely cultivated as a groundcover for its dark green shiny leaves and usually blue-violet flowers.
  • minor league (noun) A league of teams that do not belong to a major league (especially baseball).
  • minor planet (noun) Any of numerous small celestial bodies that move around the sun.
  • pyrola minor (noun) The common wintergreen having many-flowered racemes of pink-tinged white flowers; Europe and North America.
