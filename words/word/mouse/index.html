Mouse: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mouse is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any of numerous small rodents typically resembling diminutive rats having pointed snouts and small ears on elongated bodies with slender usually hairless tails.

In Hindi
चूहा; माउस
Synonyms
shiner, black eye, computer mouse, sneak, creep, pussyfoot
Word family
mousy, mousey

Mouse as a noun

  1. Any of numerous small rodents typically resembling diminutive rats having pointed snouts and small ears on elongated bodies with slender usually hairless tails.

    A kind of: rodent · Kinds: house mouse, harvest mouse, field mouse, wood mouse

  2. A swollen bruise caused by a blow to the eye.

    Synonyms: shiner, black eye · A kind of: bruise

  3. Person who is quiet or timid.

    A kind of: person

  4. A hand-operated electronic device that controls the coordinates of a cursor on your computer screen as you move it around on a pad; on the bottom of the device is a ball that rolls on the surface of the pad.

    “A mouse takes much more room than a trackball.”

    Synonyms: computer mouse · A kind of: electronic device

Mouse as a verb

  1. To go stealthily or furtively.

    “..stead of sneaking around spying on the neighbor's house.”

    Synonyms: sneak, creep, pussyfoot · A kind of: walk

  2. Manipulate the mouse of a computer.

    A kind of: manipulate

Phrases and compounds with "mouse"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • mouse ear (noun) Small perennial herb having bright blue or white flowers.
  • mouse mat (noun) A small portable pad that provides traction for the ball of a computer mouse.
  • sea mouse (noun) Any of several large worms having a broad flattened body with a mat of coarse hairs covering the back.
  • deer mouse (noun) Brownish New World mouse; most widely distributed member of the genus.
  • mouse deer (noun) Very small hornless deer-like ruminant of tropical Asia and west Africa.
  • mouse hare (noun) Small short-eared burrowing mammal of rocky uplands of Asia and western North America.
  • mouse nest (noun) Where mice bear and raise their young.
  • pine mouse (noun) Short-tailed glossy-furred burrowing vole of the eastern United States.
  • wood mouse (noun) Any of various New World woodland mice.
  • field mouse (noun) Any of various small mouselike rodents of the family Cricetidae (especially of genus Microtus) having a stout short-tailed body and inconspicuous ears and inhabiting fields or meadows.
  • hazel mouse (noun) A variety of dormouse.
  • house mouse (noun) Brownish-grey Old World mouse now a common household pest worldwide.
  • mouse click (noun) Depression of a button on a computer mouse.
  • mouse-eared (adjective) Having ears like a mouse's.
  • mouse-sized (adjective) Having the approximate size of a mouse.
  • pygmy mouse (noun) Very small dark greyish brown mouse resembling a house mouse; of Texas and Mexico.
