Mouth: meaning, synonyms and examples

Mouth is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: The opening through which food is taken in and vocalizations emerge.

In Hindi
मुँह
Synonyms
oral cavity, oral fissure, rima oris, mouthpiece, sass, sassing, backtalk, back talk, lip, talk

Mouth as a noun

  1. The opening through which food is taken in and vocalizations emerge.

    “He stuffed his mouth with candy.”

    Synonyms: oral cavity, oral fissure, rima oris · A kind of: rima · Kinds: trap

  2. The externally visible part of the oral cavity on the face and the system of organs surrounding the opening.

    “She wiped lipstick from her mouth.”

    A kind of: orifice · Kinds: cytostome, beak, beak

  3. An opening that resembles a mouth (as of a cave or a gorge).

    “He rode into the mouth of the canyon.” “They built a fire at the mouth of the cave.”

    A kind of: opening

  4. The point where a stream issues into a larger body of water.

    “New York is at the mouth of the Hudson.”

    A kind of: geological formation

  5. A person conceived as a consumer of food.

    “He has four mouths to feed.”

    A kind of: eater

  6. A spokesperson (as a lawyer).

    Synonyms: mouthpiece · A kind of: spokesperson

  7. An impudent or insolent rejoinder.

    “Don't give me any of your sass.”

    Synonyms: sass, sassing, backtalk, back talk, lip · A kind of: rejoinder

  8. The opening of a jar or bottle.

    “The jar had a wide mouth.”

    A kind of: opening

Mouth as a verb

  1. Express in speech.

    “She talks a lot of nonsense.” “This depressed patient does not verbalize.”

    Synonyms: talk, speak, utter, verbalize, verbalise · A kind of: communicate · Kinds: read, vocalize, troll, begin, lip off, shout, whisper, peep

  2. Articulate silently; form words with the lips only.

    “She mouthed a swear word.”

    A kind of: feign · Kinds: lip-synch

  3. Touch with the mouth.

    A kind of: touch
