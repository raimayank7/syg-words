Music: meaning, synonyms and examples

Music is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: An artistic form of auditory communication incorporating instrumental or vocal tones in a structured and continuous manner.

In Hindi
संगीत
Synonyms
euphony, medicine
Word family
musician, musical

Music as a noun

  1. An artistic form of auditory communication incorporating instrumental or vocal tones in a structured and continuous manner.

    A kind of: auditory communication · Kinds: section, pizzicato, monophony, polyphony, polytonality, popularism, harmony, tune

  2. Any agreeable (pleasing and harmonious) sounds.

    “He fell asleep to the music of the wind chimes.”

    Synonyms: euphony · A kind of: sound · Kinds: music of the spheres

  3. Musical activity (singing or whistling etc.).

    “His music was his central interest.”

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: bell ringing, instrumental music, intonation, percussion, vocal music, whistling

  4. (music) the sounds produced by singers or musical instruments (or reproductions of such sounds).

    A kind of: sound · Kinds: piano music

  5. Punishment for one's actions.

    “You have to face the music.” “Take your medicine.”

    Synonyms: medicine · A kind of: punishment

Phrases and compounds with "music"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • music box (noun) Produces music by means of pins on a revolving cylinder that strike the tuned teeth of a comb-like metal plate.
  • pop music (noun) Music of general appeal to teenagers; a bland watered-down version of rock'n'roll with more rhythm and harmony and an emphasis on romantic love.
  • rap music (noun) Genre of African-American music of the 1980s and 1990s in which rhyming lyrics are chanted to a musical accompaniment; several forms of rap have emerged.
  • chin music (noun) Idle or foolish and irrelevant talk.
  • folk music (noun) The traditional and typically anonymous music that is an expression of the life of people in a community.
  • music hall (noun) A theater in which vaudeville is staged.
  • music rack (noun) A light stand for holding sheets of printed music.
  • part music (noun) Vocal music for several voices in independent parts (usually performed without accompaniment).
  • rock music (noun) A genre of popular music originating in the 1950s; a blend of black rhythm-and-blues with white country-and-western.
  • black music (noun) Music created by African-American musicians; early forms were songs that had a melodic line and a strong rhythmic beat with repeated choruses.
  • dance music (noun) A genre of popular music composed for ballroom dancing.
  • disco music (noun) Popular dance music (especially in the late 1970s); melodic with a regular bass beat; intended mainly for dancing at discotheques.
  • music genre (noun) An expressive style of music.
  • music lover (noun) Someone who attends concerts.
  • music paper (noun) Paper with lines appropriate for writing music.
  • music stand (noun) A light stand for holding sheets of printed music.
