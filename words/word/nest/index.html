Nest: meaning, synonyms and examples

Nest is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A structure in which animals lay eggs or give birth to their young.

In Hindi
घोंसला
Synonyms
cuddle, snuggle, nestle, nuzzle, draw close
Word family
nester

Nest as a noun

  1. A structure in which animals lay eggs or give birth to their young.

    A kind of: natural object · Kinds: beehive, bird's nest, drey, mouse nest, nidus

  2. A kind of gun emplacement.

    “A machine-gun nest.” “A nest of snipers.”

    A kind of: gun emplacement

  3. A cosy or secluded retreat.

    A kind of: retreat

  4. Furniture pieces made to fit close together.

    A kind of: furniture

Nest as a verb

  1. Inhabit a nest, usually after building.

    “Birds are nesting outside my window every Spring.”

    A kind of: populate

  2. Fit together or fit inside.

    “Nested bowls.”

    A kind of: implant

  3. Move or arrange oneself in a comfortable and cozy position.

    “We cuddled against each other to keep warm.” “The children snuggled into their sleeping bags.”

    Synonyms: cuddle, snuggle, nestle, nuzzle, draw close · A kind of: cling to

  4. Gather nests.

    A kind of: gather
