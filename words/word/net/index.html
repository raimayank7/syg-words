Net: meaning, synonyms and examples

Net is used as an adjective (2 senses), noun (6 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Remaining after all deductions.

In Hindi
जाल; शुद्ध (आय)
Synonyms
nett, final, last, internet, cyberspace, net income, net profit, lucre, profit, profits
Opposites
gross
Word family
netting

Net as an adjective

  1. Remaining after all deductions.

    “Net profit.”

    Synonyms: nett · Opposite: gross · Similar: clear, take-home

  2. Conclusive in a process or progression.

    “The final answer.” “A last resort.” “The net result.”

    Synonyms: final, last · Similar: ultimate

Net as a noun

  1. A computer network consisting of a worldwide network of computer networks that use the TCP/IP network protocols to facilitate data transmission and exchange.

    Synonyms: internet, cyberspace · A kind of: computer network

  2. A trap made of netting to catch fish or birds or insects.

    A kind of: trap · Kinds: brail, fishnet

  3. The excess of revenues over outlays in a given period of time (including depreciation and other non-cash expenses).

    Synonyms: net income, net profit, lucre, profit, profits, earnings · A kind of: income · Kinds: earning per share, windfall profit, fast buck, filthy lucre, gross profit, markup, accumulation, dividend

  4. A goal lined with netting (as in soccer or hockey).

    A kind of: goal · Kinds: cage

  5. Game equipment consisting of a strip of netting dividing the playing area in tennis or badminton.

    A kind of: game equipment · Kinds: volleyball net

  6. An open fabric of string or rope or wire woven together at regular intervals.

    Synonyms: network, mesh, meshing, meshwork · A kind of: fabric · Kinds: chicken wire, gauze, hairnet, reseau, safety net, save-all, snood, spark arrester

Net as a verb

  1. Make as a net profit.

    “The company cleared $1 million.”

    Synonyms: sack, sack up, clear · A kind of: profit

  2. Yield as a net profit.

    “This sale netted me $1 million.”

    Synonyms: clear · A kind of: yield

  3. Construct or form a web, as if by weaving.

    Synonyms: web · A kind of: weave

  4. Catch with a net.

    “Net a fish.”

    Synonyms: nett · A kind of: catch
