Night: meaning, synonyms and examples

Night is used as a noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: The time after sunset and before sunrise while it is dark outside.

Night as a noun

  1. The time after sunset and before sunrise while it is dark outside.

    Synonyms: nighttime, dark · Opposite: day · A kind of: time period · Kinds: weeknight, wedding night

  2. A period of ignorance or backwardness or gloom.

    A kind of: time period

  3. The period spent sleeping.

    “I had a restless night.”

    A kind of: time period

  4. The dark part of the diurnal cycle considered a time unit.

    “Three nights later he collapsed.”

    A kind of: time unit

  5. Darkness.

    “It vanished into the night.”

    A kind of: dark

  6. A shortening of nightfall.

    “They worked from morning to night.”

    A kind of: twilight

  7. The time between sunset and midnight.

    “He watched television every night.”

    A kind of: time period

  8. Roman goddess of night; daughter of Erebus; counterpart of Greek Nyx.

Phrases and compounds with "night"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • all-night (adjective) Lasting, open, or operating through the whole night.
  • night owl (noun) A person who likes to be active late at night.
  • good night (noun) A conventional expression of farewell.
  • night bell (noun) A doorbell to be used at night.
  • night bird (noun) Any bird associated with night: owl; nightingale; nighthawk; etc.
  • night club (noun) A spot that is open late at night and that provides entertainment (as singers or dancers) as well as dancing and food and drink.
  • night game (noun) A game played under artificial illumination at night.
  • night life (noun) The entertainment available to people seeking nighttime diversion.
  • night soil (noun) Human excreta used as fertilizer.
  • night-line (noun) A fishing line with baited hooks left in the water to catch fish over night.
  • night-robe (noun) Lingerie consisting of a loose dress designed to be worn in bed by women.
  • night-stop (noun) A break in a journey for the night.
  • this night (adverb) During the night of the present day.
  • every night (adverb) At the end of each day.
  • guest night (noun) An evening when members of a club or college can bring their friends as guests.
  • night court (noun) A criminal court (in large cities) that sits at night.
