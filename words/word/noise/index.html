Noise: meaning, synonyms and examples

Noise is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Sound of any kind (especially unintelligible or dissonant sound).

Noise as a noun

  1. Sound of any kind (especially unintelligible or dissonant sound).

    “He enjoyed the street noises.” “They heard indistinct noises of people talking.” “During the firework display that ended the gala the noise reached 98 decibels.”

    A kind of: sound · Kinds: bang, banging, bark, blare, boom, chatter, chatter, chug

  2. The auditory experience of sound that lacks musical quality; sound that is a disagreeable auditory experience.

    “Modern music is just noise to me.”

    Synonyms: dissonance, racket · A kind of: sound

  3. Electrical or acoustic activity that can disturb communication.

    Synonyms: interference, disturbance · A kind of: trouble · Kinds: clutter, background, crosstalk, fadeout, jitter, static, white noise

  4. A loud outcry of protest or complaint.

    “The announcement of the election recount caused a lot of noise.” “Whatever it was he didn't like it and he was going to let them know by making as loud a noise as he could.”

    A kind of: cry

  5. Incomprehensibility resulting from irrelevant information or meaningless facts or remarks.

    “All the noise in his speech concealed the fact that he didn't have anything to say.”

    A kind of: incomprehensibility

  6. The quality of lacking any predictable order or plan.

    Synonyms: randomness, haphazardness, stochasticity · A kind of: irregularity · Kinds: ergodicity

Noise as a verb

  1. Emit a noise.

    Synonyms: make noise, resound · A kind of: sound · Kinds: sizzle, roar, sough, honk, hum, crunch, whine, racket

Phrases and compounds with "noise"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • make noise (verb) Emit a noise.
  • noise level (noun) The amplitude level of the undesired background noise.
  • radio noise (noun) Static at radio wavelengths.
  • white noise (noun) A noise produced by a stimulus containing all of the audible frequencies of vibration.
  • ground noise (noun) Extraneous noise contaminating sound measurements that cannot be separated from the desired signal.
  • surface noise (noun) Noise produced by the friction of the stylus of a record player moving over the rotating record.
  • noise pollution (noun) Annoying and potentially harmful environmental noise.
  • signal-to-noise (noun) The ratio of signal intensity to noise intensity.
  • background noise (noun) Extraneous noise contaminating sound measurements that cannot be separated from the desired signal.
  • noise conditions (noun) The condition of being noisy (as in a communication channel).
  • signal-to-noise ratio (noun) The ratio of signal intensity to noise intensity.
