Nose: meaning, synonyms and examples

Nose is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: The organ of smell and entrance to the respiratory tract; the prominent part of the face of man or other mammals.

In Hindi
नाक
Synonyms
olfactory organ, nozzle, intrude, horn in, pry, poke, scent, wind, nuzzle

Nose as a noun

  1. The organ of smell and entrance to the respiratory tract; the prominent part of the face of man or other mammals.

    “He has a cold in the nose.”

    Synonyms: olfactory organ · A kind of: chemoreceptor · Kinds: snout, snout, beak, conk, hawk nose, proboscis, pug nose

  2. A front that resembles a human nose (especially the front of an aircraft).

    “The nose of the rocket heated up on reentry.”

    A kind of: front

  3. The front or forward projection of a tool or weapon.

    “He ducked under the nose of the gun.”

    A kind of: front

  4. A small distance.

    “My horse lost the race by a nose.”

    A kind of: small indefinite quantity

  5. A symbol of inquisitiveness.

    “Keep your nose out of it.”

    A kind of: symbol

  6. The sense of smell (especially in animals).

    “The hound has a good nose.”

    A kind of: smell

  7. A natural skill.

    “He has a nose for good deals.”

    A kind of: skill

  8. A projecting spout from which a fluid is discharged.

    Synonyms: nozzle · A kind of: spout · Kinds: showerhead

Nose as a verb

  1. Search or inquire in a meddlesome way.

    “This guy is always nosing around the office.”

    Synonyms: intrude, horn in, pry, poke · A kind of: search

  2. Advance the forward part of with caution.

    “She nosed the car into the left lane.”

    A kind of: advance

  3. Catch the scent of; get wind of.

    Synonyms: scent, wind · A kind of: smell

  4. Push or move with the nose.

    A kind of: push

  5. Rub noses.

    Synonyms: nuzzle · A kind of: caress

  6. Defeat by a narrow margin.

    A kind of: get the better of
