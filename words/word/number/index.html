Number: meaning, synonyms and examples

Number is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: The property possessed by a sum or total or indefinite quantity of units or individuals.

In Hindi
संख्या, नंबर
Synonyms
figure, act, routine, turn, bit, phone number, telephone number, numeral, issue, identification number
Word family
numerical, numbering

Number as a noun

  1. The property possessed by a sum or total or indefinite quantity of units or individuals.

    “He had a number of chores to do.” “The number of parameters is small.” “The figure was about a thousand.”

    Synonyms: figure · A kind of: amount · Kinds: numerousness, preponderance, innumerableness, majority, minority, fewness, roundness

  2. A concept of quantity involving zero and units.

    “Every number has a unique position in the sequence.”

    A kind of: definite quantity · Kinds: arity, coordinate, pagination, decimal, constant, oxidation number, cardinality, count

  3. A short theatrical performance that is part of a longer program.

    “He did his act three times every evening.” “She had a catchy little routine.” “It was one of the best numbers he ever did.”

    Synonyms: act, routine, turn, bit · A kind of: performance · Kinds: show-stopper

  4. The number is used in calling a particular telephone.

    “He has an unlisted number.”

    Synonyms: phone number, telephone number · A kind of: signal

  5. A symbol used to represent a number.

    “He learned to write the numerals before he went to school.”

    Synonyms: numeral · A kind of: symbol · Kinds: antilogarithm

  6. One of a series published periodically.

    “She found an old issue of the magazine in her dentist's waiting room.”

    Synonyms: issue · A kind of: periodical · Kinds: edition

  7. A select company of people.

    “I hope to become one of their number before I die.”

    A kind of: company

  8. A numeral or string of numerals that is used for identification.

    “She refused to give them her Social Security number.”

    Synonyms: identification number · A kind of: positive identification · Kinds: bank identification number, license number, personal identification number

  9. A clothing measurement.

    “A number 13 shoe.”

    A kind of: size

  10. The grammatical category for the forms of nouns and pronouns and verbs that are used depending on the number of entities involved (singular or dual or plural).

    “In English the subject and the verb must agree in number.”

    A kind of: grammatical category

  11. An item of merchandise offered for sale.

    “She preferred the black nylon number.” “This sweater is an all-wool number.”

    A kind of: merchandise

Number as a verb

  1. Add up in number or quantity.

    “The bills amounted to $2,000.” “The bill came to $2,000.”

    Synonyms: total, add up, come, amount · A kind of: be · Kinds: work out, outnumber, average, make

  2. Give numbers to.

    “You should number the pages of the thesis.”

    A kind of: designate · Kinds: foliate

  3. Enumerate.

    “We must number the names of the great mathematicians.”

    Synonyms: list · A kind of: name · Kinds: itemize

  4. Put into a group.

    “The academy counts several Nobel Prize winners among its members.”

    Synonyms: count · A kind of: classify

  5. Determine the number or amount of.

    “Can you count the books on your shelf?” “Count your change.”

    Synonyms: count, enumerate, numerate · A kind of: determine · Kinds: recount, miscount, census, total

  6. Place a limit on the number of.

    Synonyms: keep down · A kind of: limit

Phrases and compounds with "number"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • f number (noun) The ratio of the focal length to the diameter of a (camera) lens system.
  • box number (noun) The number of a letter box at the post office where mail is collected.
  • number one (noun) A reference to yourself or myself etc.; `take care of number one' means to put your own interests first.
  • pin number (noun) A number you choose and use to gain access to various accounts.
  • back-number (noun) Someone who is no longer popular.
  • call number (noun) A mark consisting of characters written on a book; used to indicate shelf location.
  • mach number (noun) The ratio of the speed of a moving body to the speed of sound.
  • mass number (noun) The sum of the number of neutrons and protons in an atomic nucleus.
  • page number (noun) The system of numbering pages.
  • real number (noun) Any rational or irrational number.
  • stop number (noun) The ratio of the focal length to the diameter of a (camera) lens system.
  • wave number (noun) The reciprocal of the wavelength of a wave.
  • index number (noun) A number or ratio (a value on a scale of measurement) derived from a series of observed facts; can reveal relative changes as a function of time.
  • large number (noun) A large indefinite number.
  • magic number (noun) The atomic number of an extra stable strongly bound atomic nucleus: 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82 or 126.
  • phone number (noun) The number is used in calling a particular telephone.
