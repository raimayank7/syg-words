Office: meaning, synonyms and examples

Office is used as a noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Place of business where professional or clerical duties are performed.

In Hindi
दफ़्तर, कार्यालय; पद
Synonyms
business office, agency, federal agency, government agency, bureau, authority, function, part, role, power
Word family
official, officiate

Office as a noun

  1. Place of business where professional or clerical duties are performed.

    “He rented an office in the new building.”

    Synonyms: business office · A kind of: place of business · Kinds: box office, countinghouse, government office, headquarters, life office, loan office, newsroom, shipping office

  2. An administrative unit of government.

    “The Central Intelligence Agency.” “The Census Bureau.” “Office of Management and Budget.”

    Synonyms: agency, federal agency, government agency, bureau, authority · A kind of: administrative unit · Kinds: executive agency, independent agency, law enforcement agency, organ, local authority

  3. The actions and activities assigned to or required or expected of a person or group.

    “The function of a teacher.” “The government must do its part.” “Play its role.”

    Synonyms: function, part, role · A kind of: duty · Kinds: capacity, hat, portfolio, stead, second fiddle

  4. (of a government or government official) holding an office means being in power.

    “Being in office already gives a candidate a great advantage.” “During his first year in office.” “During his first year in power.”

    Synonyms: power · A kind of: state · Kinds: executive clemency, war power

  5. Professional or clerical workers in an office.

    “The whole office was late the morning of the blizzard.”

    Synonyms: office staff · A kind of: staff

  6. A religious rite or service prescribed by ecclesiastical authorities.

    “The offices of the mass.”

    A kind of: rite

  7. A job in an organization.

    “He occupied a post in the treasury.”

    Synonyms: position, post, berth, spot, billet, place, situation · A kind of: occupation · Kinds: academicianship, accountantship, admiralty, ambassadorship, apostleship, apprenticeship, associateship, attorneyship

Phrases and compounds with "office"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • box office (noun) Total admission receipts for an entertainment.
  • office boy (noun) A young man who is employed to do odd jobs in a business office.
  • home office (noun) The government department in charge of domestic affairs.
  • land office (noun) A government office where business relating to public lands is transacted.
  • life office (noun) Life assurance office.
  • loan office (noun) A shop where loans are made with personal property as security.
  • main office (noun) (usually plural) the office that serves as the administrative center of an enterprise.
  • oval office (noun) The office of the President of the United States in the White House.
  • post office (noun) A local branch where postal services are available".
  • take office (verb) Assume an office, duty, or title.
  • leave office (verb) Give up or retire from a position.
  • office block (noun) A building containing offices where work is done.
  • office staff (noun) Professional or clerical workers in an office.
  • staff office (noun) The department responsible for hiring and training and placing employees and for setting policies for personnel management.
  • divine office (noun) Canonical prayers recited daily by priests (e.g. the breviary of the Roman Catholic Church).
  • little office (noun) A Roman Catholic office honoring the Virgin Mary; similar to but shorter than the Divine Office.
