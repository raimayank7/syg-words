Open: meaning, synonyms and examples

Open is used as a verb (11 senses), adjective (14 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Cause to open or to become open.

In Hindi
खोलना; खुला
Synonyms
open up, unfold, spread, spread out, afford, give, unfastened, exposed, opened, assailable
Opposites
close, shut, closed
Word family
opener, opening, openness

Open as a verb

  1. Cause to open or to become open.

    “Mary opened the car door.”

    Synonyms: open up · Opposite: close · Kinds: unbar, break open, click open, reopen, unlock, unbolt, unseal, uncork

  2. Start to operate or function or cause to start operating or functioning.

    “Open a business.”

    Synonyms: open up · Opposite: close · Kinds: establish

  3. Become open.

    “The door opened.”

    Synonyms: open up · Opposite: close · A kind of: change state · Kinds: unfasten, fly open

  4. Begin or set in action, of meetings, speeches, recitals, etc.

    “He opened the meeting with a long speech.”

    Opposite: close · A kind of: start · Kinds: inaugurate, call to order

  5. Spread out or open from a closed or folded state.

    “Open the map.” “Spread your arms.”

    Synonyms: unfold, spread, spread out · A kind of: undo · Kinds: divaricate, exfoliate, grass, butterfly, uncross, splay

  6. Make available.

    “This opens up new possibilities.”

    Synonyms: open up · A kind of: yield

  7. Become available.

    “An opportunity opened up.”

    Synonyms: open up · A kind of: arise

  8. Have an opening or passage or outlet.

    “The bedrooms open into the hall.”

  9. Make the opening move.

    “Kasparov opened with a standard opening.”

    A kind of: move

  10. Afford access to.

    “The door opens to the patio.” “The French doors give onto a terrace.”

    Synonyms: afford, give

  11. Display the contents of a file or start an application as on a computer.

    Opposite: close · A kind of: expose

Open as an adjective

  1. Affording unobstructed entrance and exit; not shut or closed.

    “An open door.” “They left the door open.”

    Synonyms: unfastened · Opposite: shut · Similar: ajar, wide-open

  2. Affording free passage or access.

    “Open drains.” “The road is open to traffic.” “Open ranks.”

    Opposite: closed · Similar: opened, unstoppered, yawning

  3. With no protection or shield.

    “The exposed northeast frontier.” “Open to the weather.” “An open wound.”

    Synonyms: exposed · Similar: unprotected

  4. Open to or in view of all.

    “An open protest.” “An open letter to the editor.”

    Similar: public

  5. Used of mouth or eyes.

    “Keep your eyes open.” “His mouth slightly opened.”

    Synonyms: opened · Opposite: closed · Similar: agape, agaze, wide-eyed, yawning

  6. Not having been filled.

    “The job is still open.”

    Similar: available

  7. Accessible to all.

    “Open season.” “An open economy.”

    Similar: unrestricted

  8. Not defended or capable of being defended.

    “An open city.” “Open to attack.”

    Synonyms: assailable, undefendable, undefended · Similar: vulnerable

  9. (of textures) full of small openings or gaps.

    “An open texture.” “A loose weave.”

    Synonyms: loose · Similar: coarse

  10. Having no protecting cover or enclosure.

    “An open boat.” “An open fire.” “Open sports cars.”

    Similar: unenclosed

  11. (set theory) of an interval that contains neither of its endpoints.

    Opposite: closed

  12. Not brought to a conclusion; subject to further thought.

    “An open question.” “Our position on this bill is still undecided.” “Our lawsuit is still undetermined.”

    Synonyms: undecided, undetermined, unresolved · Similar: unsettled

  13. Not sealed or having been unsealed.

    “The letter was already open.” “The opened package lay on the table.”

    Synonyms: opened · Similar: unsealed

  14. Without undue constriction as from e.g. tenseness or inhibition.

    “The clarity and resonance of an open tone.” “Her natural and open response.”

    Similar: unconstricted

Open as a noun

  1. A clear or unobstructed space or expanse of land or water.

    “Finally broke out of the forest into the open.”

    Synonyms: clear · A kind of: area

  2. Where the air is unconfined.

    “He wanted to get outdoors a little.” “The concert was held in the open air.” “Camping in the open.”

    Synonyms: outdoors, out-of-doors, open air · A kind of: outside

  3. A tournament in which both professionals and amateurs may play.

    A kind of: tournament

  4. Information that has become public.

    “All the reports were out in the open.” “The facts had been brought to the surface.”

    Synonyms: surface · A kind of: public knowledge

Phrases and compounds with "open"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • open up (verb) Cause to open or to become open.
  • fly open (verb) Come open suddenly.
  • open air (noun) Where the air is unconfined.
  • open-air (adjective) In the open air.
  • drop open (verb) Open involuntarily.
  • fall open (verb) Open involuntarily.
  • hold open (verb) Retain rights to.
  • keep open (verb) Retain rights to.
  • open door (noun) The policy of granting equal trade opportunities to all countries.
  • open fire (verb) Start firing a weapon.
  • open shop (noun) A company whose workers are hired without regard to their membership in a labor union.
  • open-eyed (adjective) Carefully observant or attentive; on the lookout for possible danger.
  • open-plan (adjective) (of rooms or buildings) having large rooms with few dividing partitions.
  • wide-open (adjective) Open wide.
  • break open (verb) Open with force.
  • click open (verb) Open with a clicking sound.
