Opening: meaning, synonyms and examples

Opening is used as a noun (13 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: An open or empty space in or between things.

In Hindi
शुरुआत; छेद; ख़ाली पद
Synonyms
gap, opening night, curtain raising, possibility, possible action, orifice, porta, hatchway, scuttle, chess opening
Opposites
closing
Word family
open

Opening as a noun

  1. An open or empty space in or between things.

    “There was a small opening between the trees.” “The explosion made a gap in the wall.”

    Synonyms: gap · A kind of: space · Kinds: pocket, diastema, foramen, breach, chasm, crack, hole, mouth

  2. A ceremony accompanying the start of some enterprise.

    A kind of: ceremony

  3. Becoming open or being made open.

    “The opening of his arms was the sign I was waiting for.”

    A kind of: change of integrity · Kinds: cut

  4. The first performance (as of a theatrical production).

    “The opening received good critical reviews.”

    Synonyms: opening night, curtain raising · A kind of: start

  5. The act of opening something.

    “The ray of light revealed his cautious opening of the door.”

    Opposite: closing · A kind of: motion

  6. Opportunity especially for employment or promotion.

    “There is an opening in the sales department.”

    A kind of: opportunity

  7. The initial part of the introduction.

    “The opening established the basic theme.”

    A kind of: introduction · Kinds: teaser, salutation

  8. A possible alternative.

    “Bankruptcy is always a possibility.”

    Synonyms: possibility, possible action · A kind of: option · Kinds: possible

  9. An aperture or hole that opens into a bodily cavity.

    “The orifice into the aorta from the lower left chamber of the heart.”

    Synonyms: orifice, porta · A kind of: passage · Kinds: blastopore, aortic orifice, stoma, porta hepatis, spiracle, mouth, os, cervix

  10. A vacant or unobstructed space that is man-made.

    “They left a small opening for the cat at the bottom of the door.”

    A kind of: artifact · Kinds: aperture, bell, breech, exit, fly, gap, gun muzzle, hole

  11. An entrance equipped with a hatch; especially a passageway between decks of a ship.

    Synonyms: hatchway, scuttle · A kind of: entrance · Kinds: escape hatch

  12. A recognized sequence of moves at the beginning of a game of chess.

    “He memorized all the important chess openings.”

    Synonyms: chess opening · A kind of: succession

  13. The first of a series of actions.

    Synonyms: first step, initiative, opening move · A kind of: beginning · Kinds: curtain raiser, first base, peace initiative

Opening as an adjective

  1. First or beginning.

    “The memorable opening bars of Beethoven's Fifth.” “The play's opening scene.”

    Opposite: closing · Similar: beginning, inaugural, introductory, starting

Phrases and compounds with "opening"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • neck opening (noun) An opening in a garment for the neck of the wearer; a part of the garment near the wearer's neck.
  • opening line (noun) The first line of a piece of writing (as a newspaper story).
  • opening move (noun) The first of a series of actions.
  • chess opening (noun) A recognized sequence of moves at the beginning of a game of chess.
  • opening night (noun) The first performance (as of a theatrical production).
