Opinion: meaning, synonyms and examples

Opinion is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A personal belief or judgment that is not founded on proof or certainty.

In Hindi
राय, मत
Synonyms
sentiment, persuasion, view, thought, public opinion, popular opinion, vox populi, legal opinion, judgment, judgement
Word family
opine

Opinion as a noun

  1. A personal belief or judgment that is not founded on proof or certainty.

    “My opinion differs from yours.” “I am not of your persuasion.” “What are your thoughts on Haiti?”

    Synonyms: sentiment, persuasion, view, thought · A kind of: belief · Kinds: idea, judgment, eyes, preconception, pole, politics

  2. A message expressing a belief about something; the expression of a belief that is held with confidence but not substantiated by positive knowledge or proof.

    “His opinions appeared frequently on the editorial page.”

    Synonyms: view · A kind of: message · Kinds: adverse opinion, guess, side

  3. A belief or sentiment shared by most people; the voice of the people.

    “He asked for a poll of public opinion.”

    Synonyms: public opinion, popular opinion, vox populi · A kind of: belief

  4. The legal document stating the reasons for a judicial decision.

    “Opinions are usually written by a single judge.”

    Synonyms: legal opinion, judgment, judgement · A kind of: legal document · Kinds: concurring opinion, dissenting opinion, majority opinion, obiter dictum

  5. The reason for a court's judgment (as opposed to the decision itself).

    Synonyms: ruling · A kind of: judgment

  6. A vague idea in which some confidence is placed.

    “His impression of her was favorable.” “What are your feelings about the crisis?” “It strengthened my belief in his sincerity.”

    Synonyms: impression, feeling, belief, notion · A kind of: idea · Kinds: presence, effect, first blush, intuition

Phrases and compounds with "opinion"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • opinion poll (noun) An inquiry into public opinion conducted by interviewing a random sample of people.
  • legal opinion (noun) The legal document stating the reasons for a judicial decision.
  • public opinion (noun) A belief or sentiment shared by most people; the voice of the people.
  • adverse opinion (noun) An opinion concerning financial statements (usually based on an audit by a CPA) that the statements as a whole do not present results fairly or are not in conformity with the generally accepted accounting practices of the United States.
  • popular opinion (noun) A belief or sentiment shared by most people; the voice of the people.
  • majority opinion (noun) The opinion joined by a majority of the court (generally known simply as `the opinion').
  • concurring opinion (noun) An opinion that agrees with the court's disposition of the case but is written to express a particular judge's reasoning.
  • dissenting opinion (noun) An opinion that disagrees with the court's disposition of the case.
  • public opinion poll (noun) An inquiry into public opinion conducted by interviewing a random sample of people.
  • preconceived opinion (noun) An opinion formed beforehand without adequate evidence.
  • difference of opinion (noun) A disagreement or argument about something important.
