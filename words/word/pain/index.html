Pain: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pain is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A symptom of some physical hurt or disorder.

In Hindi
दर्द
Synonyms
hurting, painfulness, pain sensation, painful sensation, pain in the neck, nuisance, annoyance, bother, botheration, infliction
Opposites
pleasure

Pain as a noun

  1. A symptom of some physical hurt or disorder.

    “The patient developed severe pain and distension.”

    Synonyms: hurting · A kind of: symptom · Kinds: ache, agony, arthralgia, burn, causalgia, colic, chest pain, chiralgia

  2. Emotional distress; a fundamental feeling that people try to avoid.

    “The pain of loneliness.”

    Synonyms: painfulness · Opposite: pleasure · A kind of: feeling · Kinds: growing pains, unpleasantness, mental anguish, suffering, distress

  3. A somatic sensation of acute discomfort.

    “As the intensity increased the sensation changed from tickle to pain.”

    Synonyms: pain sensation, painful sensation · A kind of: somesthesia · Kinds: mittelschmerz, phantom limb pain, twinge

  4. A bothersome annoying person.

    “That kid is a terrible pain.”

    Synonyms: pain in the neck, nuisance · A kind of: unpleasant person

  5. Something or someone that causes trouble; a source of unhappiness.

    “Washing dishes was a nuisance before we got a dish washer.” “A bit of a bother.” “He's not a friend, he's an infliction.”

    Synonyms: annoyance, bother, botheration, infliction, pain in the neck, pain in the ass · A kind of: negative stimulus · Kinds: irritant, plague

Pain as a verb

  1. Cause bodily suffering to and make sick or indisposed.

    Synonyms: trouble, ail · A kind of: hurt · Kinds: erupt

  2. Cause emotional anguish or make miserable.

    “It pains me to see my children not being taught well in school.”

    Synonyms: anguish, hurt · A kind of: upset · Kinds: break someone's heart, agonize, try, torment

Phrases and compounds with "pain"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • pain pill (noun) A medicine used to relieve pain.
  • pain unit (noun) A unit measuring the intensity of pain.
  • pain-free (adjective) Not accompanied by pain sensations.
  • chest pain (noun) Pain in the chest.
  • labor pain (noun) Pain and discomfort associated with contractions of the uterus during labor.
  • lumbar pain (noun) Backache affecting the lumbar region or lower back; can be caused by muscle strain or arthritis or vascular insufficiency or a ruptured intervertebral disc.
  • referred pain (noun) Pain that is felt at a place in the body different from the injured or diseased part where the pain would be expected.
  • pain sensation (noun) A somatic sensation of acute discomfort.
  • pain threshold (noun) The lowest intensity of stimulation at which pain is experienced.
  • pain in the ass (noun) Something or someone that causes trouble; a source of unhappiness.
  • pain in the neck (noun) A bothersome annoying person.
  • phantom limb pain (noun) Pain felt by an amputee that seems to be located in the missing limb.
  • pleasure-pain principle (noun) (psychoanalysis) the governing principle of the id; the principle that an infant seeks gratification and fails to distinguish fantasy from reality.
