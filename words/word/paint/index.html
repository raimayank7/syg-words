Paint: meaning, synonyms and examples

Paint is used as a verb (4 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Make a painting.

In Hindi
रंगना; रंग
Synonyms
pigment, key, rouge, blusher
Word family
painter, painting

Paint as a verb

  1. Make a painting.

    “He painted all day in the garden.” “He painted a painting of the garden.”

    A kind of: create · Kinds: finger-paint, stipple, bodypaint, fresco, distemper, charge, shade, stipple

  2. Apply paint to; coat with paint.

    “We painted the rooms yellow.”

    A kind of: coat · Kinds: grain, repaint, airbrush, prime

  3. Make a painting of.

    “He painted his mistress many times.”

    A kind of: represent · Kinds: repaint

  4. Apply a liquid to; e.g., paint the gutters with linseed oil.

    A kind of: cover

Paint as a noun

  1. A substance used as a coating to protect or decorate a surface (especially a mixture of pigment suspended in a liquid); dries to form a hard coating.

    “Artists use `paint' and `pigment' interchangeably.”

    Synonyms: pigment · A kind of: coating, coloring material · Kinds: acrylic, antifouling paint, coat of paint, distemper, enamel, encaustic, finger paint, house paint

  2. (basketball) a space (including the foul line) in front of the basket at each end of a basketball court; usually painted a different color from the rest of the court.

    “He hit a jump shot from the top of the key.” “He dominates play in the paint.”

    Synonyms: key · A kind of: space

  3. Makeup consisting of a pink or red powder applied to the cheeks.

    Synonyms: rouge, blusher · A kind of: makeup

Phrases and compounds with "paint"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • oil paint (noun) Paint in which a drying oil is the vehicle.
  • war paint (noun) Adornment consisting of paint applied to the face and body of certain Amerindians before a battle.
  • paint leaf (noun) Showy poinsettia found from the southern United States to Peru.
  • house paint (noun) Paint used to cover the exterior woodwork of a house.
  • latex paint (noun) A water-base paint that has a latex binder.
  • spray paint (noun) Paint applied with a spray gun.
  • casein paint (noun) A water-base paint made with a protein precipitated from milk.
  • finger paint (noun) Paint that has the consistency of jelly.
  • finger-paint (verb) Apply colors with one's fingers.
  • indian paint (noun) Perennial North American plant with greyish hairy foliage yielding a red or yellow pigment.
  • paint roller (noun) A roller that has an absorbent surface used for spreading paint.
  • poster paint (noun) Pigment mixed with water-soluble glutinous materials such as size and egg yolk.
  • acrylic paint (noun) Used especially by artists.
  • coat of paint (noun) A layer of paint covering something else.
  • paint the lily (verb) Adorn unnecessarily (something that is already beautiful).
  • paint a picture (verb) Call to mind.
