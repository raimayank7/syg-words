Pair: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pair is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A set of two similar things considered as a unit.

In Hindi
जोड़ा
Synonyms
brace, couple, twosome, twain, span, yoke, couplet, distich, duo, duet
Word family
pairing

Pair as a noun

  1. A set of two similar things considered as a unit.

    Synonyms: brace · A kind of: set

  2. Two items of the same kind.

    Synonyms: couple, twosome, twain, brace, span, yoke, couplet, distich · A kind of: two · Kinds: doubleton

  3. Two people considered as a unit.

    A kind of: gathering · Kinds: couple, yoke

  4. A poker hand with 2 cards of the same value.

    A kind of: poker hand

Pair as a verb

  1. Form a pair or pairs.

    “The two old friends paired off.”

    Synonyms: pair off, partner off, couple · A kind of: unite

  2. Bring two objects, ideas, or people together.

    “This fact is coupled to the other one.” “Matchmaker, can you match my daughter with a nice young man?” “The student was paired with a partner for collaboration on the project.”

    Synonyms: match, mate, couple, twin · A kind of: join · Kinds: mismate, mismatch

  3. Occur in pairs.

    Synonyms: geminate · A kind of: occur

  4. Arrange in pairs.

    “Pair these numbers.”

    Synonyms: geminate · A kind of: arrange
