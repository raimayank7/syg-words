Pattern: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pattern is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A perceptual structure.

In Hindi
नमूना, ढाँचा
Synonyms
form, shape, practice, design, figure, convention, normal, rule, formula, blueprint

Pattern as a noun

  1. A perceptual structure.

    “The composition presents problems for students of musical form.” “A visual pattern must include not only objects but the spaces between them.”

    Synonyms: form, shape · A kind of: structure · Kinds: fractal, gestalt, grid, kaleidoscope, mosaic, strand

  2. A customary way of operation or behavior.

    “It is their practice to give annual raises.” “They changed their dietary pattern.”

    Synonyms: practice · A kind of: activity · Kinds: biologism, cooperation, featherbedding, formalism, one-upmanship, pluralism, symbolism, modernism

  3. A decorative or artistic work.

    “The coach had a design on the doors.”

    Synonyms: design, figure · A kind of: decoration · Kinds: argyle, bear claw, damascene, decal, device, emblem, herringbone, linocut

  4. Something regarded as a normative example.

    “The convention of not naming the main character.” “Violence is the rule not the exception.” “His formula for impressing visitors.”

    Synonyms: convention, normal, rule, formula · A kind of: practice · Kinds: mores, code of conduct, universal

  5. A model considered worthy of imitation.

    “The American constitution has provided a pattern for many republics.”

    A kind of: exemplar

  6. Something intended as a guide for making something else.

    “A blueprint for a house.” “A pattern for a skirt.”

    Synonyms: blueprint, design · A kind of: plan

  7. The path that is prescribed for an airplane that is preparing to land at an airport.

    “The traffic patterns around O'Hare are very crowded.” “They stayed in the pattern until the fog lifted.”

    Synonyms: traffic pattern, approach pattern · A kind of: path

  8. Graphical representation (in polar or Cartesian coordinates) of the spatial distribution of radiation from an antenna as a function of angle.

    Synonyms: radiation pattern, radiation diagram · A kind of: graph

Pattern as a verb

  1. Plan or create according to a model or models.

    Synonyms: model · A kind of: imitate · Kinds: scale, sovietize

  2. Form a pattern.

    “These sentences pattern like the ones we studied before.”

    A kind of: match

Phrases and compounds with "pattern"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • key pattern (noun) An ornamental pattern consisting of repeated vertical and horizontal lines (often in relief).
  • tonal pattern (noun) The perception of pleasant arrangements of musical notes.
  • speech pattern (noun) Distinctive manner of oral expression.
  • willow-pattern (noun) Chinaware decorated with a blue Chinese design on a white background depicting a willow tree and often a river.
  • holding pattern (noun) A state of inaction with no progress and no change.
  • traffic pattern (noun) The path that is prescribed for an airplane that is preparing to land at an airport.
  • approach pattern (noun) The path that is prescribed for an airplane that is preparing to land at an airport.
  • rhythmic pattern (noun) (prosody) a system of versification.
  • radiation pattern (noun) Graphical representation (in polar or Cartesian coordinates) of the spatial distribution of radiation from an antenna as a function of angle.
  • hereditary pattern (noun) (genetics) attributes acquired via biological heredity from the parents.
  • intonation pattern (noun) Intonations characteristic of questions and requests and statements.
  • herringbone pattern (noun) A pattern of columns of short parallel lines with all the lines in one column sloping one way and lines in adjacent columns sloping the other way; it is used in weaving, masonry, parquetry, embroidery.
  • male pattern baldness (noun) Loss of hair on the crown of the head.
