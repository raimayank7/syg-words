Pay: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pay is used as a verb (11 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Give money, usually in exchange for goods or services.

In Hindi
भुगतान करना; वेतन
Synonyms
give, pay up, ante up, yield, bear, pay off, make up, compensate, devote, wage
Word family
payment, payee, payer

Pay as a verb

  1. Give money, usually in exchange for goods or services.

    “I paid four dollars for this sandwich.” “Pay the waitress, please.”

    A kind of: give · Kinds: subsidize, finance, put up, compensate, overpay, underpay, prepay, compensate

  2. Convey, as of a compliment, regards, attention, etc.; bestow.

    “Don't pay him any mind.” “Give the orders.” “Give him my best regards.”

    Synonyms: give · A kind of: communicate · Kinds: offer

  3. Cancel or discharge a debt.

    “Pay up, please!”

    Synonyms: pay up, ante up · A kind of: pay · Kinds: liquidate

  4. Bring in.

    “Interest-bearing accounts.” “How much does this savings certificate pay annually?”

    Synonyms: yield, bear · A kind of: gain · Kinds: net, pay off

  5. Do or give something to somebody in return.

    “Does she pay you for the work you are doing?”

    Synonyms: pay off, make up, compensate · A kind of: settle

  6. Dedicate.

    “Give thought to.” “Give priority to.” “Pay attention to.”

    Synonyms: give, devote · A kind of: think

  7. Be worth it.

    “It pays to go through the trouble.”

    A kind of: be

  8. Render.

    “Pay a visit.” “Pay a call.”

    A kind of: make

  9. Bear (a cost or penalty), in recompense for some action.

    “You'll pay for this!” “She had to pay the penalty for speaking out rashly.” “You'll pay for this opinion later.”

    A kind of: digest · Kinds: take one's lumps

  10. Make a compensation for.

    “A favor that cannot be paid back.”

    A kind of: requite

  11. Discharge or settle.

    “Pay a debt.” “Pay an obligation.”

    A kind of: settle · Kinds: pay up

Pay as a noun

  1. Something that remunerates.

    “Wages were paid by check.” “He wasted his pay on drink.” “They saved a quarter of all their earnings.”

    Synonyms: wage, earnings, remuneration, salary · A kind of: regular payment · Kinds: combat pay, double time, found, half-pay, living wage, merit pay, minimum wage, pay envelope

Phrases and compounds with "pay"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • pay up (verb) Cancel or discharge a debt.
  • pay cut (noun) The act of reducing a salary.
  • pay for (verb) Have as a guest.
  • pay off (verb) Yield a profit or result.
  • pay out (verb) Expend, as from a fund.
  • half-pay (noun) Reduced wage paid to someone who is not working full time.
  • pay back (verb) Act or give recompense in recognition of someone's behavior or actions.
  • pay cash (verb) Pay (for something) with cash.
  • pay dirt (noun) Ore that yields a substantial profit to the miner.
  • pay heed (verb) Give heed (to).
  • pay rate (noun) Amount of money received per unit time.
  • sick pay (noun) Wages paid to an employee who is on sick leave.
  • merit pay (noun) Extra pay awarded to an employee on the basis of merit (especially to school teachers).
  • pay claim (noun) The wage demanded from management for workers by their union representatives.
  • pay-phone (noun) A coin-operated telephone.
  • combat pay (noun) Extra pay for soldiers engaged in active combat.
