Phone: meaning, synonyms and examples

Phone is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Electronic equipment that converts sound into electrical signals that can be transmitted over distances and then converts received signals back into sounds.

In Hindi
फ़ोन
Synonyms
telephone, telephone set, speech sound, sound, earphone, earpiece, headphone, call, call up, ring
Word family
phonic, phonetic, phonate, phoner

Phone as a noun

  1. Electronic equipment that converts sound into electrical signals that can be transmitted over distances and then converts received signals back into sounds.

    “I talked to him on the telephone.”

    Synonyms: telephone, telephone set · A kind of: electronic equipment · Kinds: desk phone, dial telephone, extension, handset, pay-phone, radiotelephone, speakerphone

  2. (phonetics) an individual sound unit of speech without concern as to whether or not it is a phoneme of some language.

    Synonyms: speech sound, sound · A kind of: language unit · Kinds: phoneme, vowel, semivowel, consonant, orinasal phone, sonant

  3. Electro-acoustic transducer for converting electric signals into sounds; it is held over or inserted into the ear.

    “It was not the typing but the earphones that she disliked.”

    Synonyms: earphone, earpiece, headphone · A kind of: electro-acoustic transducer · Kinds: earplug, telephone receiver

Phone as a verb

  1. Get or try to get into communication (with someone) by telephone.

    “I tried to call you all night.” “Take two aspirin and call me in the morning.”

    Synonyms: call, telephone, call up, ring · A kind of: telecommunicate · Kinds: cell phone, call in

Phrases and compounds with "phone"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • phone-in (noun) A program in which the audience participates by telephone.
  • pay-phone (noun) A coin-operated telephone.
  • cell phone (verb) Call up by using a cellular phone.
  • desk phone (noun) A telephone set that sits on a desk or table.
  • dial phone (noun) A telephone with a dial for registering the number to be called.
  • phone bill (noun) Statement of charges for telephone service.
  • phone book (noun) A directory containing an alphabetical list of telephone subscribers and their telephone numbers.
  • phone call (noun) A telephone connection.
  • phone card (noun) A card that is used instead of cash to make telephone calls.
  • phone cord (noun) The telephone wire that connects to the handset.
  • phone jack (noun) A jack for plugging in a telephone.
  • phone line (noun) A telephone connection.
  • phone plug (noun) A plug for connecting a telephone.
  • phone booth (noun) Booth for using a telephone.
  • mobile phone (noun) A hand-held mobile radiotelephone for use in an area divided into small sections, each with its own short-range transmitter/receiver.
  • phone number (noun) The number is used in calling a particular telephone.
