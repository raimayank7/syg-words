Pick: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pick is used as a verb (12 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Select carefully from a group.

In Hindi
चुनना; तोड़ना
Synonyms
pluck, cull, blame, find fault, clean, foot, plunk, break up, peck, beak
Word family
picker, picking, pickings

Pick as a verb

  1. Select carefully from a group.

    “She finally picked her successor.” “He picked his way carefully.”

    A kind of: choose · Kinds: hand-pick

  2. Look for and gather.

    “Pick mushrooms.” “Pick flowers.”

    Synonyms: pluck, cull · A kind of: gather · Kinds: mushroom, berry

  3. Harass with constant criticism.

    “Don't always pick on your little brother.”

    Synonyms: blame, find fault · A kind of: knock

  4. Provoke.

    “Pick a fight or a quarrel.”

    A kind of: provoke

  5. Remove in small bits.

    “Pick meat from a bone.”

    A kind of: remove

  6. Remove unwanted substances from, such as feathers or pits.

    “Clean the turkey.”

    Synonyms: clean · A kind of: remove

  7. Pilfer or rob.

    “Pick pockets.”

    A kind of: rob

  8. Pay for something.

    “Pick up the tab.” “Pick up the burden of high-interest mortgages.” “Foot the bill.”

    Synonyms: foot · A kind of: pay

  9. Pull lightly but sharply with a plucking motion.

    “He plucked the strings of his mandolin.”

    Synonyms: pluck, plunk · A kind of: pull · Kinds: twang

  10. Attack with or as if with a pickaxe of ice or rocky ground, for example.

    “Pick open the ice.”

    Synonyms: break up · A kind of: pierce

  11. Hit lightly with a picking motion.

    Synonyms: peck, beak · A kind of: strike

  12. Eat intermittently; take small bites of.

    “He pieced at the sandwich all morning.” “She never eats a full meal--she just nibbles.”

    Synonyms: nibble, piece · A kind of: eat

Pick as a noun

  1. The person or thing chosen or selected.

    “He was my pick for mayor.”

    Synonyms: choice, selection · A kind of: decision making · Kinds: pleasure, favorite, way

  2. The quantity of a crop that is harvested.

    “He sent the first picking of berries to the market.” “It was the biggest peach pick in years.”

    Synonyms: picking · A kind of: output

  3. The best people or things in a group.

    Synonyms: cream · A kind of: elite

  4. The yarn woven across the warp yarn in weaving.

    Synonyms: woof, weft, filling · A kind of: thread

  5. A small thin device (of metal or plastic or ivory) used to pluck a stringed instrument.

    Synonyms: plectrum, plectron · A kind of: device · Kinds: guitar pick

  6. A thin sharp implement used for removing unwanted material.

    “He used a pick to clean the dirt out of the cracks.”

    A kind of: hand tool · Kinds: icepick, toothpick

  7. A heavy iron tool with a wooden handle and a curved head that is pointed on both ends.

    “They used picks and sledges to break the rocks.”

    Synonyms: pickax, pickaxe · A kind of: edge tool · Kinds: mattock

  8. A basketball maneuver; obstructing an opponent with one's body.

    “He was called for setting an illegal pick.”

    A kind of: basketball play

  9. The act of choosing or selecting.

    “Your choice of colors was unfortunate.” “You can take your pick.”

    Synonyms: choice, selection, option · A kind of: action · Kinds: casting, coloration, sampling, decision, volition, election, vote

Phrases and compounds with "pick"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • pick at (verb) Pluck or pull at with the fingers.
  • pick up (verb) Take and lift upward.
  • ice pick (noun) Pick consisting of a steel rod with a sharp point; used for breaking up blocks of ice.
  • pick off (verb) Shoot one by one.
  • pick out (verb) Pick out, select, or choose from a number of alternatives.
  • pick-off (noun) A baseball play in which a base runner is caught off base and tagged out.
  • hand-pick (verb) Pick personally and very carefully.
  • pick over (verb) Separate or remove.
  • pick apart (verb) Find fault with; express criticism of; point out real or perceived flaws.
  • pick-me-up (noun) Anything with restorative powers.
  • guitar pick (noun) A plectrum used to pluck a guitar.
  • niemann-pick disease (noun) A disorder of lipid metabolism that is inherited as an autosomal recessive trait.
  • pick up the gauntlet (verb) Be dared to do something and attempt it.
