Piece: meaning, synonyms and examples

Piece is used as a noun (13 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A separate part of a whole.

In Hindi
टुकड़ा
Synonyms
part, musical composition, opus, composition, piece of music, bit, firearm, small-arm, slice, objet d'art

Piece as a noun

  1. A separate part of a whole.

    “An important piece of the evidence.”

    A kind of: part · Kinds: beam, piece of cloth, piece of leather, scrap, shard, snip, spindle

  2. An item that is an instance of some type.

    “He designed a new piece of equipment.” “She bought a lovely piece of china.”

    A kind of: item

  3. A portion of a natural object.

    “They analyzed the river into three parts.” “He needed a piece of granite.”

    Synonyms: part · A kind of: thing · Kinds: body part, corpus, acicula, base, corner, craton, cutting, fragment

  4. A musical work that has been created.

    “The composition is written in four movements.”

    Synonyms: musical composition, opus, composition, piece of music · A kind of: music · Kinds: morceau, sheet music, musical arrangement, realization, intermezzo, allegro, allegretto, andante

  5. An instance of some kind.

    “It was a nice piece of work.” “He had a bit of good luck.”

    Synonyms: bit · A kind of: case

  6. An artistic or literary composition.

    “He wrote an interesting piece on Iran.” “The children acted out a comic piece to amuse the guests.”

    A kind of: creation · Kinds: article, morceau, afterpiece, fragment, hiatus

  7. A portable gun.

    “He wore his firearm in a shoulder holster.”

    Synonyms: firearm, small-arm · A kind of: gun · Kinds: autoloader, muzzle loader, pistol, repeating firearm, rifle, riot gun, shotgun, smoothbore

  8. A serving that has been cut from a larger portion.

    “A piece of pie.” “A slice of bread.”

    Synonyms: slice · A kind of: helping · Kinds: cutlet, fillet

  9. A distance.

    “It is down the road a piece.”

    A kind of: distance

  10. A work of art of some artistic value.

    “This store sells only objets d'art.” “It is not known who created this piece.”

    Synonyms: objet d'art, art object · A kind of: work of art · Kinds: virtu

  11. A period of indeterminate length (usually short) marked by some action or condition.

    “He was here for a little while.” “I need to rest for a piece.” “A spell of good weather.”

    Synonyms: while, spell, patch · A kind of: time · Kinds: cold spell, hot spell, snap

  12. A share of something.

    “A slice of the company's revenue.”

    Synonyms: slice · A kind of: share

  13. Game equipment consisting of an object used in playing certain board games.

    “He taught me to set up the men on the chess board.” “He sacrificed a piece to get a strategic advantage.”

    Synonyms: man · A kind of: game equipment · Kinds: black, checker, chessman, tile, white

Piece as a verb

  1. To join or unite the pieces of.

    “Patch the skirt.”

    Synonyms: patch · A kind of: join · Kinds: vamp

  2. Create by putting components or members together.

    “She pieced a quilt.” “He tacked together some verses.” “They set up a committee.”

    Synonyms: assemble, put together, set up, tack, tack together · A kind of: join, make · Kinds: confect, jumble, reassemble, configure, compound, rig up

  3. Join during spinning.

    “Piece the broken pieces of thread, slivers, and rovings.”

    A kind of: splice

  4. Eat intermittently; take small bites of.

    “He pieced at the sandwich all morning.” “She never eats a full meal--she just nibbles.”

    Synonyms: nibble, pick · A kind of: eat

  5. Repair by adding pieces.

    “She pieced the china cup.”

    Synonyms: patch · A kind of: repair

Phrases and compounds with "piece"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • fur-piece (noun) A neckpiece made of fur.
  • one-piece (adjective) (of clothing) consisting of or fashioned in a single whole piece.
  • piece-dye (verb) Dye after weaving.
  • set piece (noun) A piece of scenery intended to stand alone as part of the stage setting.
  • two-piece (noun) A business suit consisting of a matching jacket and skirt or trousers.
  • knee piece (noun) Armor plate that protects the knee.
  • chess piece (noun) Any of 16 white and 16 black pieces used in playing the game of chess.
  • piece goods (noun) Merchandise in the form of fabrics sold by the yard.
  • think piece (noun) An article in a newspaper or magazine or journal that represents opinions and ideas and discussion rather than bare facts.
  • three-piece (adjective) (of clothing) made in or consisting of three parts or pieces.
  • by the piece (adverb) One piece at a time.
  • period piece (noun) Any work of art whose special value lies in its evocation of a historical period.
  • fowling piece (noun) A light shotgun used for fowling.
  • masking piece (noun) Scenery used to block the audience's view of parts of the stage that should not be seen.
  • piece of cake (noun) Any undertaking that is easy to do.
  • piece of land (noun) An extended area of land.
