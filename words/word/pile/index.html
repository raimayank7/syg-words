Pile: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pile is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A collection of objects laid on top of each other.

In Hindi
ढेर
Synonyms
heap, mound, agglomerate, cumulation, cumulus, batch, deal, flock, good deal, great deal
Word family
pilary, pilous

Pile as a noun

  1. A collection of objects laid on top of each other.

    Synonyms: heap, mound, agglomerate, cumulation, cumulus · A kind of: collection · Kinds: compost heap, dunghill, scrapheap, shock, slagheap, stack, pyre, woodpile

  2. (often followed by `of') a large number or amount or extent.

    “A batch of letters.” “A deal of trouble.” “A lot of money.”

    Synonyms: batch, deal, flock, good deal, great deal, hatful, heap, lot · A kind of: large indefinite quantity · Kinds: flood, haymow

  3. A large sum of money (especially as pay or profit).

    “She made a bundle selling real estate.” “They sank megabucks into their new house.”

    Synonyms: bundle, big bucks, megabucks, big money · A kind of: money

  4. Fine soft dense hair (as the fine short hair of cattle or deer or the wool of sheep or the undercoat of certain dogs).

    Synonyms: down · A kind of: hair · Kinds: lanugo

  5. Battery consisting of voltaic cells arranged in series; the earliest electric battery devised by Volta.

    Synonyms: voltaic pile, galvanic pile · A kind of: battery

  6. A column of wood or steel or concrete that is driven into the ground to provide support for a structure.

    Synonyms: spile, piling, stilt · A kind of: column · Kinds: sheet pile

  7. The yarn (as in a rug or velvet or corduroy) that stands up from the weave.

    “For uniform color and texture tailors cut velvet with the pile running the same direction.”

    Synonyms: nap · A kind of: thread

  8. A nuclear reactor that uses controlled nuclear fission to generate energy.

    Synonyms: atomic pile, atomic reactor, chain reactor · A kind of: nuclear reactor

Pile as a verb

  1. Arrange in stacks.

    “Heap firewood around the fireplace.” “Stack your books up on the shelves.”

    Synonyms: stack, heap · A kind of: arrange · Kinds: rick, cord

  2. Press tightly together or cram.

    “The crowd packed the auditorium.”

    Synonyms: throng, mob, pack, jam · A kind of: crowd

  3. Place or lay as if in a pile.

    “The teacher piled work on the students until the parents protested.”

    A kind of: put

Phrases and compounds with "pile"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • pile up (verb) Collect or gather.
  • junk pile (noun) An accumulation of refuse and discarded matter.
  • sheet pile (noun) A pile in a row of piles driven side by side to retain earth or prevent seepage.
  • trash pile (noun) An accumulation of refuse and discarded matter.
  • atomic pile (noun) A nuclear reactor that uses controlled nuclear fission to generate energy.
  • pile driver (noun) A machine that drives piling into the ground.
  • sheath pile (noun) A pile in a row of piles driven side by side to retain earth or prevent seepage.
  • compost pile (noun) A heap of manure and vegetation and other organic residues that are decaying to become compost.
  • voltaic pile (noun) Battery consisting of voltaic cells arranged in series; the earliest electric battery devised by Volta.
  • galvanic pile (noun) Battery consisting of voltaic cells arranged in series; the earliest electric battery devised by Volta.
  • pile dwelling (noun) Dwelling built on piles in or near a lake; specifically in prehistoric villages.
