Pitch: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pitch is used as a verb (12 senses) and noun (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Throw or toss with a light motion.

In Hindi
पिच; स्वर की ऊँचाई
Synonyms
flip, toss, sky, lurch, shift, peddle, monger, huckster, hawk, vend
Word family
pitching, pitcher, pitchy

Pitch as a verb

  1. Throw or toss with a light motion.

    “Flip me the beachball.” “Toss me newspaper.”

    Synonyms: flip, toss, sky · A kind of: fling · Kinds: submarine, lag, throw back

  2. Move abruptly.

    “The ship suddenly lurched to the left.”

    Synonyms: lurch, shift · A kind of: move

  3. Fall or plunge forward.

    “She pitched over the railing of the balcony.”

    A kind of: descend

  4. Set to a certain pitch.

    “He pitched his voice very low.”

    A kind of: set

  5. Sell or offer for sale from place to place.

    Synonyms: peddle, monger, huckster, hawk, vend · A kind of: deal

  6. Be at an angle.

    “The terrain sloped down.”

    Synonyms: slope, incline · A kind of: lean · Kinds: ascend, stoop, fall, climb, dip

  7. Heel over.

    “The tower is tilting.” “The ceiling is slanting.”

    Synonyms: cant, cant over, tilt, slant · A kind of: move · Kinds: cock

  8. Erect and fasten.

    “Pitch a tent.”

    Synonyms: set up · A kind of: rear · Kinds: camp

  9. Throw or hurl from the mound to the batter, as in baseball.

    “The pitcher delivered the ball.”

    Synonyms: deliver · A kind of: throw

  10. Hit (a golf ball) in a high arc with a backspin.

    A kind of: hit

  11. Lead (a card) and establish the trump suit.

    A kind of: play

  12. Set the level or character of.

    “She pitched her speech to the teenagers in the audience.”

    Synonyms: gear · A kind of: adapt · Kinds: popularize

Pitch as a noun

  1. The property of sound that varies with variation in the frequency of vibration.

    A kind of: sound property · Kinds: concert pitch, high pitch, soprano, tenor, key, low pitch, alto, alto

  2. (baseball) the act of throwing a baseball by a pitcher to a batter.

    Synonyms: delivery · A kind of: throw · Kinds: balk, ball, beanball, change-up, curve, duster, fastball, knuckleball

  3. A vendor's position (especially on the sidewalk).

    “He was employed to see that his paper's news pitches were not trespassed upon by rival vendors.”

    A kind of: position

  4. Promotion by means of an argument and demonstration.

    Synonyms: sales talk, sales pitch · A kind of: promotion

  5. Degree of deviation from a horizontal plane.

    “The roof had a steep pitch.”

    Synonyms: rake, slant · A kind of: gradient · Kinds: loft

  6. Any of various dark heavy viscid substances obtained as a residue.

    Synonyms: tar · A kind of: bitumen · Kinds: coal tar

  7. A high approach shot in golf.

    Synonyms: pitch shot · A kind of: approach

  8. An all-fours game in which the first card led is a trump.

    Synonyms: auction pitch · A kind of: all fours

  9. Abrupt up-and-down motion (as caused by a ship or other conveyance).

    “The pitching and tossing was quite exciting.”

    Synonyms: lurch, pitching · A kind of: motion · Kinds: rock

  10. The action or manner of throwing something.

    “His pitch fell short and his hat landed on the floor.”

    A kind of: throw

Phrases and compounds with "pitch"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • pitch in (verb) Eat heartily.
  • low pitch (noun) A pitch that is perceived as below other pitches.
  • high pitch (noun) A pitch that is perceived as above other pitches.
  • pitch into (verb) Hit violently, as in an attack.
  • pitch pine (noun) Large three-needled pine of southeastern United States having very long needles and gnarled twisted limbs; bark is red-brown deeply ridged; an important timber tree.
  • pitch pipe (noun) A small pipe sounding a tone of standard frequency; used to establish the starting pitch for unaccompanied singing.
  • pitch shot (noun) A high approach shot in golf.
  • pitch-dark (adjective) Extremely dark.
  • wild pitch (noun) An errant pitch that the catcher cannot be expected to catch and that allows a base runner to advance a base.
  • fever pitch (noun) A state of extreme excitement.
  • pitch apple (noun) A common tropical American clusia having solitary white or rose flowers.
  • pitch black (noun) A very dark black.
  • pitch-black (adjective) Extremely dark.
  • sales pitch (noun) Promotion by means of an argument and demonstration.
  • pitch accent (noun) Emphasis that results from pitch rather than loudness.
  • auction pitch (noun) An all-fours game in which the first card led is a trump.
