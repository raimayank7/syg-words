Plan: meaning, synonyms and examples

Plan is used as a verb (4 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Have the will and intention to carry out some action.

In Hindi
योजना
Synonyms
be after, project, contrive, design, program, programme, architectural plan
Word family
planner, planning

Plan as a verb

  1. Have the will and intention to carry out some action.

    “He plans to be in graduate school next year.” “The rebels had planned turmoil and confusion.”

    Synonyms: be after · A kind of: intend · Kinds: think out, draw a bead on, hope

  2. Make plans for something.

    “He is planning a trip with his family.”

    A kind of: think · Kinds: schedule, chart, project, frame, plot, scheme, intend, mastermind

  3. Make or work out a plan for; devise.

    “Design a new sales strategy.” “Plan an attack.”

    Synonyms: project, contrive, design · A kind of: create by mental act · Kinds: plot, concert, map

  4. Make a design of; plan out in systematic, often graphic form.

    “Design a better mousetrap.” “Plan the new wing of the museum.”

    Synonyms: design · A kind of: create by mental act · Kinds: engineer, redesign, blueprint

Plan as a noun

  1. A series of steps to be carried out or goals to be accomplished.

    “They drew up a six-step plan.” “They discussed plans for a new bond issue.”

    Synonyms: program, programme · A kind of: idea · Kinds: audit program, outline, master plan, blueprint, plan of action, regimen, project, agenda

  2. An arrangement scheme.

    “The awkward design of the keyboard made operation difficult.” “It was an excellent design for living.” “A plan for seating guests.”

    Synonyms: design · A kind of: arrangement · Kinds: configuration, layout, trap, hotel plan

  3. Scale drawing of a structure.

    “The plans for City Hall were on file.”

    Synonyms: architectural plan · A kind of: drawing · Kinds: elevation, floor plan

Phrases and compounds with "plan"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • game plan (noun) (figurative) a carefully thought out strategy for achieving an objective in war or politics or business or personal affairs.
  • meal plan (noun) A plan and a room rate for providing a room and meals to guests at a hotel.
  • open-plan (adjective) (of rooms or buildings) having large rooms with few dividing partitions.
  • time plan (noun) A system for paying for goods by installments.
  • floor plan (noun) Scale drawing of a horizontal section through a building at a given level; contrasts with elevation.
  • hotel plan (noun) A plan and a room rate for providing a room and meals to guests at a hotel.
  • keogh plan (noun) A tax-deferred pension plan for employees of unincorporated businesses or for self-employed persons.
  • battle plan (noun) Plan for conducting a battle.
  • ground plan (noun) A floor plan for the ground level of a building.
  • master plan (noun) A long-term outline of a project or government function.
  • secret plan (noun) A secret scheme to do something (especially something underhand or illegal).
  • travel plan (noun) A proposed route of travel.
  • bermuda plan (noun) A hotel plan that provides a full breakfast daily.
  • pension plan (noun) A plan for setting aside money to be spent after retirement.
  • american plan (noun) A hotel plan that includes three meals daily.
  • european plan (noun) A hotel plan that provides a continental breakfast daily.
