Plane: meaning, synonyms and examples

Plane is used as a noun (5 senses), verb (3 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: An aircraft that has a fixed wing and is powered by propellers or jets.

In Hindi
हवाई जहाज़; समतल
Synonyms
airplane, aeroplane, sheet, planer, planing machine, carpenter's plane, woodworking plane, shave, skim, flat
Word family
planar, planer, planation, planeness

Plane as a noun

  1. An aircraft that has a fixed wing and is powered by propellers or jets.

    “The flight was delayed due to trouble with the airplane.”

    Synonyms: airplane, aeroplane · A kind of: heavier-than-air craft · Kinds: airliner, amphibian, biplane, delta wing, fighter, hangar queen, jet, monoplane

  2. (mathematics) an unbounded two-dimensional shape.

    “We will refer to the plane of the graph as the X-Y plane.” “Any line joining two points on a plane lies wholly on that plane.”

    Synonyms: sheet · A kind of: shape · Kinds: facet plane, midplane, orbital plane, picture plane, tangent plane

  3. A level of existence or development.

    “He lived on a worldly plane.”

    A kind of: degree

  4. A power tool for smoothing or shaping wood.

    Synonyms: planer, planing machine · A kind of: power tool

  5. A carpenter's hand tool with an adjustable blade for smoothing or shaping wood.

    “The cabinetmaker used a plane for the finish work.”

    Synonyms: carpenter's plane, woodworking plane · A kind of: edge tool, hand tool · Kinds: beading plane, block plane, bullnose, chamfer plane, circular plane, combination plane, dovetail plane, fore plane

Plane as a verb

  1. Cut or remove with or as if with a plane.

    “The machine shaved off fine layers from the piece of wood.”

    Synonyms: shave · A kind of: cut

  2. Travel on the surface of water.

    Synonyms: skim · A kind of: glide · Kinds: aquaplane, aquaplane

  3. Make even or smooth, with or as with a carpenter's plane.

    “Plane the top of the door.”

    A kind of: smooth

Plane as an adjective

  1. Having a surface without slope, tilt in which no part is higher or lower than another.

    “A flat desk.” “Acres of level farmland.” “A plane surface.”

    Synonyms: flat, level · Similar: even

Phrases and compounds with "plane"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • jet plane (noun) An airplane powered by one or more jet engines.
  • ski-plane (noun) An airplane equipped with skis so it can land on a snowfield.
  • dado plane (noun) A plane for making a dado groove.
  • fore plane (noun) A carpenter's plane intermediate between a jack plane and a jointer plane.
  • jack plane (noun) A carpenter's plane for rough finishing.
  • long plane (noun) A long carpenter's plane used to shape the edges of boards so they will fit together.
  • plane seat (noun) A seat on a commercial airliner.
  • plane tree (noun) Any of several trees of the genus Platanus having thin pale bark that scales off in small plates and lobed leaves and ball-shaped heads of fruits.
  • block plane (noun) A small plane used on end grains of wood.
  • facet plane (noun) The plane of a facet of an object (as of a cube).
  • match plane (noun) A plane having cutters designed to make the tongues and grooves on the edges of matchboards.
  • plane angle (noun) An angle formed by two straight lines (in the same plane).
  • plane table (noun) Surveying instrument consisting of a drawing board and a ruler that are mounted on a tripod; used to sight and map topographical details.
  • scrub plane (noun) A narrow woodworking plane used to cut away excess stock.
  • london plane (noun) Very large fast-growing tree much planted as a street tree.
  • plane figure (noun) A two-dimensional shape.
