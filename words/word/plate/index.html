Plate: meaning, synonyms and examples

Plate is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: (baseball) base consisting of a rubber slab where the batter stands; it must be touched by a base runner in order to score.

In Hindi
थाली, प्लेट
Synonyms
home plate, home base, home, plateful, crustal plate, photographic plate, collection plate, scale, shell, denture
Word family
plater, plating

Plate as a noun

  1. (baseball) base consisting of a rubber slab where the batter stands; it must be touched by a base runner in order to score.

    “He ruled that the runner failed to touch home.”

    Synonyms: home plate, home base, home · A kind of: base

  2. A sheet of metal or wood or glass or plastic.

    A kind of: sheet · Kinds: baffle, cylinder head, damper, disk, engraving, escutcheon, fin keel, fishplate

  3. A full-page illustration (usually on slick paper).

    A kind of: illustration · Kinds: fashion plate

  4. Dish on which food is served or from which food is eaten.

    A kind of: flatware · Kinds: dessert plate, dinner plate, paper plate, salad plate, soup plate, steel plate

  5. The quantity contained in a plate.

    Synonyms: plateful · A kind of: containerful

  6. A rigid layer of the Earth's crust that is believed to drift slowly.

    Synonyms: crustal plate · A kind of: crust

  7. The thin under portion of the forequarter.

    A kind of: cut of beef

  8. A main course served on a plate.

    “A vegetable plate.” “The blue plate special.”

    A kind of: entree

  9. Any flat platelike body structure or part.

    A kind of: structure · Kinds: operculum, comb, frill, lamina

  10. The positively charged electrode in a vacuum tube.

    A kind of: anode

  11. A flat sheet of metal or glass on which a photographic image can be recorded.

    Synonyms: photographic plate · A kind of: sheet · Kinds: quarter plate

  12. Structural member consisting of a horizontal beam that provides bearing and anchorage.

    A kind of: structural member · Kinds: wall plate

  13. A shallow receptacle for collection in church.

    Synonyms: collection plate · A kind of: receptacle

  14. A metal sheathing of uniform thickness (such as the shield attached to an artillery piece to protect the gunners).

    Synonyms: scale, shell · A kind of: shield · Kinds: armor plate, horseshoe, shell plating

Plate as a verb

  1. Coat with a layer of metal.

    “Plate spoons with silver.”

    A kind of: coat · Kinds: tin, tin, silver, nickel, electroplate, chrome, goldplate, silverplate

Phrases and compounds with "plate"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • l-plate (noun) A square plate bearing the letter L that is attached to both ends of a car to indicate that the driver is a learner.
  • dry plate (noun) A former photographic method that used a glass plate coated with a light-sensitive gelatinous emulsion.
  • end-plate (noun) The flattened end of a motor neuron that transmits neural impulses to a muscle.
  • hot plate (noun) A portable electric appliance for heating or cooking or keeping food warm.
  • tin plate (noun) A thin sheet of metal (iron or steel) coated with tin to prevent rusting; used especially for cans, pots, and tins.
  • bite plate (noun) A removable dental appliance that is worn in the palate for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.
  • comb-plate (noun) A locomotor organ consisting of a row of strong cilia whose bases are fused.
  • gold plate (noun) Tableware that is plated with gold.
  • gold-plate (verb) Plate with gold.
  • home plate (noun) (baseball) base consisting of a rubber slab where the batter stands; it must be touched by a base runner in order to score.
  • plate iron (noun) A plate of iron.
  • plate rack (noun) A rack for holding plates to dry after they have been washed.
  • plate rail (noun) Rail or narrow shelf fixed to a wall to display plates.
  • soup plate (noun) A deep plate with a wide rim.
  • wall plate (noun) Plate (a timber along the top of a wall) to support the ends of joists, etc., and distribute the load.
  • armor plate (noun) Specially hardened steel plate used to protect fortifications or vehicles from enemy fire.
