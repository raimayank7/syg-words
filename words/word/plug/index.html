Plug: meaning, synonyms and examples

Plug is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Blockage consisting of an object designed to fill a hole tightly.

In Hindi
प्लग; डाट
Synonyms
stopper, stopple, chew, chaw, cud, quid, wad, ballyhoo, hoopla, hype
Word family
plugger

Plug as a noun

  1. Blockage consisting of an object designed to fill a hole tightly.

    Synonyms: stopper, stopple · A kind of: blockage · Kinds: bung, drainplug, earplug, fipple, tampion, tampon, tap

  2. A wad of something chewable as tobacco.

    Synonyms: chew, chaw, cud, quid, wad · A kind of: morsel

  3. Blatant or sensational promotion.

    Synonyms: ballyhoo, hoopla, hype · A kind of: promotion

  4. Electrical device that fits into the cylinder head of an internal-combustion engine and ignites the gas by means of an electric spark.

    Synonyms: spark plug, sparking plug · A kind of: electrical device

  5. An electrical device with two or three pins that is inserted in a socket to make an electrical connection.

    Synonyms: male plug · A kind of: electrical device · Kinds: telephone plug

  6. An upright hydrant for drawing water to use in fighting a fire.

    Synonyms: fireplug, fire hydrant · A kind of: hydrant

  7. An old or over-worked horse.

    Synonyms: hack, jade, nag · A kind of: horse

Plug as a verb

  1. Fill or close tightly with or as if with a plug.

    “Plug the hole.” “Stop up the leak.”

    Synonyms: stop up, secure · A kind of: close · Kinds: stopper, tampon, chink, cork

  2. Persist in working hard.

    “Students must plug away at this problem.”

    Synonyms: plug away · A kind of: persevere

  3. Deliver a quick blow to.

    “He punched me in the stomach.”

    Synonyms: punch · A kind of: hit

  4. Make a plug for; praise the qualities or in order to sell or promote.

    A kind of: advertise

  5. Insert a plug into.

    “Plug the wall.”

    A kind of: insert

  6. Insert as a plug.

    A kind of: insert
