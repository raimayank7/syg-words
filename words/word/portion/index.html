Portion: meaning, synonyms and examples

Portion is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Something determined in relation to something that includes it.

In Hindi
हिस्सा
Synonyms
part, component part, component, constituent, parcel, share, percentage, fortune, destiny, fate

Portion as a noun

  1. Something determined in relation to something that includes it.

    “He wanted to feel a part of something bigger than himself.” “I read a portion of the manuscript.” “The smaller component is hard to reach.”

    Synonyms: part, component part, component, constituent · A kind of: relation · Kinds: substance, language unit, item, butt, basis, detail, unit, member

  2. Something less than the whole of a human artifact.

    “The rear part of the house.” “Glue the two parts together.”

    Synonyms: part · A kind of: object · Kinds: appendage, bit, bottleneck, bulb, butt, component, cutout, foible

  3. The allotment of some amount by dividing something.

    “Death gets more than its share of attention from theologians.”

    Synonyms: parcel, share · A kind of: allotment

  4. Assets belonging to or due to or contributed by an individual person or group.

    “He wanted his share in cash.”

    Synonyms: share, part, percentage · A kind of: assets · Kinds: tranche, dispensation, dole, way, ration, allowance, slice, split

  5. Your overall circumstances or condition in life (including everything that happens to you).

    “Whatever my fortune may be.” “Deserved a better fate.” “Has a happy lot.”

    Synonyms: fortune, destiny, fate, luck, lot, circumstances · A kind of: condition · Kinds: good fortune, providence, misfortune, failure

  6. Money or property brought by a woman to her husband at marriage.

    Synonyms: dowry, dowery, dower · A kind of: gift

  7. An individual quantity of food or drink taken as part of a meal.

    “The helpings were all small.” “His portion was larger than hers.” “There's enough for two servings each.”

    Synonyms: helping, serving · A kind of: small indefinite quantity · Kinds: drumstick, second joint, wing, medallion, oyster, parson's nose, piece, drink

Portion as a verb

  1. Give out.

    “We were assigned new uniforms.”

    Synonyms: assign, allot · A kind of: distribute · Kinds: allow, allocate
