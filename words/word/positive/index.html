Positive: meaning, synonyms and examples

Positive is used as an adjective (11 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Characterized by or displaying affirmation or acceptance or certainty etc.

In Hindi
सकारात्मक
Synonyms
convinced, confident, plus, confirming, prescribed, incontrovertible, irrefutable, positivist, positivistic, electropositive
Opposites
negative, neutral
Word family
positivity, positiveness

Positive as an adjective

  1. Characterized by or displaying affirmation or acceptance or certainty etc.

    “A positive attitude.” “The reviews were all positive.” “A positive benefit.”

    Opposite: negative, neutral · Similar: affirmative, constructive

  2. Persuaded of; very sure.

    “Were convinced that it would be to their advantage to join.” “I am positive he is lying.” “Was confident he would win.”

    Synonyms: convinced, confident · Similar: certain

  3. Involving advantage or good.

    “A plus (or positive) factor.”

    Synonyms: plus · Similar: advantageous

  4. Indicating existence or presence of a suspected condition or pathogen.

    Synonyms: confirming · Opposite: negative

  5. Formally laid down or imposed.

    “Positive laws.”

    Synonyms: prescribed · Similar: formal

  6. Impossible to deny or disprove.

    “Incontrovertible proof of the defendant's innocence.” “Proof positive.” “An irrefutable argument.”

    Synonyms: incontrovertible, irrefutable · Similar: undeniable

  7. Of or relating to positivism.

    “Positivist thinkers.” “Positivist doctrine.” “Positive philosophy.”

    Synonyms: positivist, positivistic

  8. Reckoned, situated or tending in the direction which naturally or arbitrarily is taken to indicate increase or progress or onward motion.

    “Positive increase in graduating students.”

    Opposite: negative

  9. Greater than zero.

    “Positive numbers.”

    Similar: plus

  10. Having a positive charge.

    “Protons are positive.”

    Synonyms: electropositive, positively charged · Similar: charged

  11. Marked by excessive confidence.

    “An arrogant and cocksure materialist.” “So overconfident and impudent as to speak to the queen.” “The less he knows the more positive he gets.”

    Synonyms: cocksure, overconfident · Similar: confident

Positive as a noun

  1. The primary form of an adjective or adverb; denotes a quality without qualification, comparison, or relation to increase or diminution.

    Synonyms: positive degree · A kind of: adjective, adverb

  2. A film showing a photographic image whose tones correspond to those of the original subject.

    A kind of: film

Phrases and compounds with "positive"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rh positive (noun) The blood group (approximately 85% of people) whose red cells have the Rh factor (Rh antigen).
  • rh-positive (adjective) Of persons (or their blood) having the Rh factor present in their red blood cells.
  • gram-positive (adjective) (of bacteria) being or relating to a bacterium that retains the violet stain used in Gram's method.
  • positive muon (noun) The antiparticle of a muon; decays to positron and neutrino and antineutrino.
  • positive pole (noun) The terminal of a battery that is connected to the positive plate.
  • positive fraud (noun) Actual deceit; concealing something or making a false representation with an evil intent to cause injury to another.
  • positive charge (noun) Having a deficiency of electrons; having a higher electric potential.
  • positive degree (noun) The primary form of an adjective or adverb; denotes a quality without qualification, comparison, or relation to increase or diminution.
  • positive feedback (noun) Feedback in phase with (augmenting) the input.
  • positive stimulus (noun) A stimulus with desirable consequences.
  • positive chemotaxis (noun) Movement toward a chemical stimulus.
  • positive reinforcer (noun) A reinforcing stimulus that serves to increase the likelihood of the response that produces it.
  • positive correlation (noun) A correlation in which large values of one variable are associated with large values of the other and small with small; the correlation coefficient is between 0 and +1.
  • positive magnetic pole (noun) The pole of a magnet that points toward the north when the magnet is suspended freely.
  • rh-positive blood type (noun) The blood group (approximately 85% of people) whose red cells have the Rh factor (Rh antigen).
  • positive identification (noun) Evidence proving that you are who you say you are; evidence establishing that you are among the group of people already known to the system; recognition by the system leads to acceptance.
