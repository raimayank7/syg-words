Pot: meaning, synonyms and examples

Pot is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Metal or earthenware cooking vessel that is usually round and deep; often has a handle and lid.

In Hindi
बरतन, गमला
Synonyms
potful, flowerpot, batch, deal, flock, good deal, great deal, hatful, heap, lot

Pot as a noun

  1. Metal or earthenware cooking vessel that is usually round and deep; often has a handle and lid.

    A kind of: cooking utensil, vessel · Kinds: caldron, coffeepot, dixie, kettle, marmite, pottle, saucepot, stockpot

  2. The quantity contained in a pot.

    Synonyms: potful · A kind of: containerful

  3. A container in which plants are cultivated.

    Synonyms: flowerpot · A kind of: container · Kinds: planter

  4. (often followed by `of') a large number or amount or extent.

    “A batch of letters.” “A deal of trouble.” “A lot of money.”

    Synonyms: batch, deal, flock, good deal, great deal, hatful, heap, lot · A kind of: large indefinite quantity · Kinds: flood, haymow

  5. The cumulative amount involved in a game (such as poker).

    Synonyms: jackpot, kitty

  6. Slang for a paunch.

    Synonyms: potbelly, bay window, corporation, tummy · A kind of: belly

  7. A resistor with three terminals, the third being an adjustable center terminal; used to adjust voltages in radios and TV sets.

    Synonyms: potentiometer · A kind of: potential divider

Pot as a verb

  1. Plant in a pot.

    “He potted the palm.”

    A kind of: implant · Kinds: repot

Phrases and compounds with "pot"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • hot pot (noun) A stew of meat and potatoes cooked in a tightly covered pot.
  • dash-pot (noun) A mechanical damper; the vibrating part is attached to a piston that moves in a chamber filled with liquid.
  • drip pot (noun) A coffeepot for making drip coffee.
  • crock pot (noun) An electric cooker that maintains a relatively low temperature.
  • go to pot (verb) Become ruined.
  • pot metal (noun) Cast iron used for making cooking wares.
  • pot plant (noun) A plant suitable for growing in a flowerpot (especially indoors).
  • pot roast (noun) Cut of beef suitable for simmering in liquid in a closed pot.
  • pepper pot (noun) A soup made with vegetables and tripe and seasoned with peppercorns; often contains dumplings.
  • pot cheese (noun) Mild white cheese made from curds of soured skim milk.
  • pot likker (noun) The liquid in which vegetables or meat have be cooked.
  • pot-au-feu (noun) Traditional French stew of vegetables and beef.
  • lobster pot (noun) Trap for catching lobsters.
  • melting pot (noun) An environment in which many ideas and races are socially assimilated.
  • pot-trained (adjective) (of children) trained to use the toilet.
  • pot marigold (noun) The common European annual marigold.
