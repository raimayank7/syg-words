Practice: meaning, synonyms and examples

Practice is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A customary way of operation or behavior.

In Hindi
अभ्यास
Synonyms
pattern, exercise, drill, practice session, recitation, praxis, practise, do, rehearse, apply
Word family
practical

Practice as a noun

  1. A customary way of operation or behavior.

    “It is their practice to give annual raises.” “They changed their dietary pattern.”

    Synonyms: pattern · A kind of: activity · Kinds: biologism, cooperation, featherbedding, formalism, one-upmanship, pluralism, symbolism, modernism

  2. Systematic training by multiple repetitions.

    “Practice makes perfect.”

    Synonyms: exercise, drill, practice session, recitation · A kind of: training · Kinds: fire drill, manual of arms, military drill, rehearsal, rehearsal, review, scrimmage, shadowboxing

  3. Translating an idea into action.

    “A hard theory to put into practice.” “Differences between theory and praxis of communism.”

    Synonyms: praxis · A kind of: implementation

  4. The exercise of a profession.

    “The practice of the law.” “I took over his practice when he retired.”

    A kind of: use · Kinds: consultancy, cosmetology, dental practice, law practice, medical practice, optometry, private practice, witching

  5. Knowledge of how something is usually done.

    “It is not the local practice to wear shorts to dinner.”

    A kind of: cognition · Kinds: custom, convention, heritage

Practice as a verb

  1. Carry out or practice; as of jobs and professions.

    “Practice law.”

    Synonyms: practise, exercise, do · Kinds: shamanize

  2. Learn by repetition.

    “We drilled French verbs every day.” “Pianists practice scales.”

    Synonyms: drill, exercise, practise · A kind of: learn

  3. Engage in a rehearsal (of).

    Synonyms: rehearse, practise · A kind of: perform · Kinds: walk through, scrimmage

  4. Avail oneself to.

    “Apply a principle.” “Practice a religion.” “Use care when going down the stairs.”

    Synonyms: apply, use · Kinds: follow

  5. Engage in or perform.

    “Commit a random act of kindness.”

    Synonyms: commit · A kind of: prosecute

Phrases and compounds with "practice"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in practice (adverb) In practical applications.
  • law practice (noun) The practice of law.
  • practice game (noun) A game whose outcome is not recorded in the season's standing.
  • group practice (noun) (medicine) the practice of medicine by a group of physicians who share their premises and other resources.
  • practice range (noun) A place for practicing golf shots.
  • skull practice (noun) Teaching strategy to an athletic team.
  • dental practice (noun) The practice of dentistry.
  • out of practice (adjective) Impaired in skill by neglect.
  • practice of law (noun) The learned profession that is mastered by graduate study in a law school and that is responsible for the judicial system.
  • target practice (noun) Practice in shooting at targets.
  • medical practice (noun) The practice of medicine.
  • practice session (noun) Systematic training by multiple repetitions.
  • practice teacher (noun) A college student who is teaching under the supervision of a certified teacher in order to qualify for a degree in education.
  • private practice (noun) The practice of a profession independently and not as an employee.
  • practice bundling (verb) Sleep fully clothed in the same bed with one's betrothed.
  • practice of medicine (noun) The learned profession that is mastered by graduate training in a medical school and that is devoted to preventing or alleviating or curing diseases and injuries.
