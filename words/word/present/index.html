Present: meaning, synonyms and examples

Present is used as a verb (13 senses), adjective (2 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Give an exhibition of to an interested audience.

In Hindi
उपहार; वर्तमान; उपस्थित
Synonyms
show, demo, exhibit, demonstrate, represent, lay out, stage, submit, pose, award
Opposites
future, past, absent
Word family
presentment, presentation, presenter, presentness, presence

Present as a verb

  1. Give an exhibition of to an interested audience.

    “She shows her dogs frequently.” “We will demo the new software in Washington.”

    Synonyms: show, demo, exhibit, demonstrate · A kind of: show · Kinds: bring home

  2. Bring forward and present to the mind.

    “We presented the arguments to him.” “We cannot represent this knowledge to our formal reason.”

    Synonyms: represent, lay out · A kind of: state · Kinds: argue, argue, spin

  3. Perform (a play), especially on a stage.

    “We are going to stage `Othello'.”

    Synonyms: stage, represent · A kind of: re-create · Kinds: set

  4. Hand over formally.

    Synonyms: submit · A kind of: give · Kinds: bring in

  5. Introduce.

    “This poses an interesting question.”

    Synonyms: pose · A kind of: constitute

  6. Give, especially as an honor or reward.

    “Bestow honors and prizes at graduation.”

    Synonyms: award · A kind of: allocate · Kinds: certificate, confer

  7. Give as a present; make a gift of.

    “What will you give her for her birthday?”

    Synonyms: give, gift · Kinds: endow, distribute, give away, raffle, tip, bequeath, surrender, grant

  8. Deliver (a speech, oration, or idea).

    “The commencement speaker presented a forceful speech that impressed the students.”

    Synonyms: deliver · A kind of: talk · Kinds: speechify

  9. Cause to come to know personally.

    “Permit me to acquaint you with my son.” “Introduce the new neighbors to the community.”

    Synonyms: introduce, acquaint · A kind of: inform · Kinds: reintroduce, present, introduce

  10. Represent abstractly, for example in a painting, drawing, or sculpture.

    “The father is portrayed as a good-looking man in this painting.”

    Synonyms: portray · A kind of: represent · Kinds: commend

  11. Present somebody with something, usually to accuse or criticize.

    “We confronted him with the evidence.” “He was faced with all the evidence and could no longer deny his actions.” “An enormous dilemma faces us.”

    Synonyms: confront, face

  12. Formally present a debutante, a representative of a country, etc.

    A kind of: introduce

  13. Recognize with a gesture prescribed by a military regulation; assume a prescribed position.

    “When the officers show up, the soldiers have to salute.”

    Synonyms: salute · A kind of: greet

Present as an adjective

  1. Temporal sense; intermediate between past and future; now existing or happening or in consideration.

    “The present leader.” “Articles for present use.” “The present topic.”

    Opposite: future, past · Similar: existing, immediate, instant, latter-day

  2. Being or existing in a specified place.

    “Present at the wedding.” “Present at the creation.”

    Opposite: absent · Similar: attendant, ever-present, existing, here, naturally occurring, omnipresent

Present as a noun

  1. The period of time that is happening now; any continuous stretch of time including the moment of speech.

    “That is enough for the present.” “He lives in the present with no thought of tomorrow.”

    Synonyms: nowadays · A kind of: time · Kinds: now, here and now, date, time being, today, tonight

  2. Something presented as a gift.

    “His tie was a present from his wife.”

    A kind of: gift · Kinds: birthday present, wedding present

  3. A verb tense that expresses actions or states at the time of speaking.

    Synonyms: present tense · A kind of: tense · Kinds: historical present

Phrases and compounds with "present"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • at present (adverb) At the present moment.
  • present-day (adjective) Belonging to the present time.
  • ever-present (adjective) Being always present.
  • present tense (noun) A verb tense that expresses actions or states at the time of speaking.
  • present times (noun) The circumstances and ideas of the present age.
  • present moment (noun) At this time.
  • present perfect (noun) A perfective tense used to express action completed in the present.
  • wedding present (noun) A present given to someone getting married.
  • birthday present (noun) A present given in celebration of a person's birthday.
  • christmas present (noun) A present given at Christmas time.
  • historical present (noun) The use of the present tense to describe past actions or states.
  • present participle (noun) A participle expressing present action; in English is formed by adding -ing.
  • present progressive (noun) A tense used to express action that is on-going at the time of utterance.
  • present perfect tense (noun) A perfective tense used to express action completed in the present.
  • clear and present danger (noun) A standard for judging when freedom of speech can be abridged.
  • present progressive tense (noun) A tense used to express action that is on-going at the time of utterance.
