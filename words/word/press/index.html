Press: meaning, synonyms and examples

Press is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Exert pressure or force to or upon.

In Hindi
दबाना; प्रेस
Synonyms
urge, urge on, exhort, weigh, compress, constrict, squeeze, compact, contract, crusade
Word family
pressure, pressing

Press as a verb

  1. Exert pressure or force to or upon.

    “He pressed down on the boards.” “Press your thumb on this spot.”

    A kind of: touch · Kinds: squeeze, squash, crunch, drag down, knuckle, push

  2. Force or impel in an indicated direction.

    “I urged him to finish his studies.”

    Synonyms: urge, urge on, exhort · A kind of: rede · Kinds: rush, push, preach

  3. To be oppressive or burdensome.

    “Weigh heavily on the mind.” “Something pressed on his mind.”

    Synonyms: weigh · A kind of: count

  4. Place between two surfaces and apply weight or pressure.

    “Pressed flowers.”

    A kind of: flatten · Kinds: iron, calender

  5. Squeeze or press together.

    “She compressed her lips.” “The spasm contracted the muscle.”

    Synonyms: compress, constrict, squeeze, compact, contract · A kind of: tighten · Kinds: choke, prim, astringe, strangulate, convulse, convulse, bear down, choke

  6. Crowd closely.

    “The crowds pressed along the street.”

    A kind of: mass

  7. Create by pressing.

    “Press little holes into the soft clay.”

    A kind of: make

  8. Be urgent.

    “This is a pressing problem.”

    A kind of: be

  9. Exert oneself continuously, vigorously, or obtrusively to gain an end or engage in a crusade for a certain cause or person; be an advocate for.

    “The liberal party pushed for reforms.” “She is crusading for women's rights.” “The Dean is pushing for his favorite candidate.”

    Synonyms: crusade, fight, campaign, push, agitate · A kind of: advertise

  10. Press from a plastic.

    “Press a record.”

    Synonyms: press out · A kind of: cast

  11. Make strenuous pushing movements during birth to expel the baby.

    “`Now push hard,' said the doctor to the woman.”

    Synonyms: push · A kind of: push

  12. Press and smooth with a heated iron.

    “Press your shirts.” “She stood there ironing.”

    Synonyms: iron, iron out · A kind of: press · Kinds: mangle

  13. Lift weights.

    “This guy can press 300 pounds.”

    Synonyms: weight-lift, weightlift · A kind of: exercise

  14. Ask for or request earnestly.

    “The prophet bid all people to become good persons.”

    Synonyms: bid, beseech, entreat, adjure, conjure · A kind of: plead

Press as a noun

  1. The state of demanding notice or attention.

    “The insistence of their hunger.” “The press of business matters.”

    Synonyms: imperativeness, insistence, insistency, pressure · A kind of: urgency

  2. The print media responsible for gathering and publishing news in the form of newspapers or magazines.

    Synonyms: public press · A kind of: print media · Kinds: free press, newspaper, magazine

  3. A machine used for printing.

    Synonyms: printing press · A kind of: machine · Kinds: flatbed press, rotary press, standing press

  4. A dense crowd of people.

    Synonyms: crush, jam · A kind of: crowd · Kinds: traffic jam

  5. A tall piece of furniture that provides storage space for clothes; has a door and rails or hooks for hanging clothes.

    Synonyms: wardrobe, closet · A kind of: furniture · Kinds: armoire, clothes closet, coat closet

  6. Clamp to prevent wooden rackets from warping when not in use.

    A kind of: clamp

  7. Any machine that exerts pressure to form or shape or cut materials or extract liquids or compress solids.

    Synonyms: mechanical press · A kind of: machine · Kinds: cheese press, ciderpress, garlic press, hydraulic press, punch press, winepress

  8. A weightlift in which the barbell is lifted to shoulder height and then smoothly lifted overhead.

    Synonyms: military press · A kind of: weightlift

  9. The act of pressing; the exertion of pressure.

    “He gave the button a press.” “He used pressure to stop the bleeding.” “At the pressing of a button.”

    Synonyms: pressure, pressing · A kind of: push · Kinds: impression, compression

Phrases and compounds with "press"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • press on (verb) Continue moving forward.
  • press-up (noun) An arm exercise performed lying face to the floor and pushing the body up and down with the arms.
  • press box (noun) Box reserved for reporters (as at a sports event).
  • press out (verb) Extinguish by crushing.
  • press run (noun) The period that presses run to produce an issue of a newspaper.
  • drop press (noun) Device for making large forgings.
  • free press (noun) A press not restricted or controlled by government censorship regarding politics or ideology.
  • press down (verb) Press down.
  • press gang (noun) A detachment empowered to force civilians to serve in the army or navy.
  • press home (verb) Make clear by special emphasis and try to convince somebody of something.
  • press lord (noun) A powerful newspaper proprietor.
  • press stud (noun) A fastener used on clothing; fastens with a snapping sound.
  • stop press (noun) Late news that is inserted into the newspaper at the last minute.
  • bench press (noun) A weightlift in which you lie on your back on a bench and press weights upward.
  • drill press (noun) A machine tool with a separate, upright stand; an electric drill is pressed into the work automatically or with a hand lever.
  • press agent (noun) Someone employed to arrange publicity (for a firm or a public figure).
