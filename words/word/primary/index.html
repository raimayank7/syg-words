Primary: meaning, synonyms and examples

Primary is used as an adjective (5 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: Of first rank or importance or value; direct and immediate rather than secondary.

In Hindi
प्राथमिक, मुख्य
Synonyms
chief, main, principal, master, elementary, elemental, basal, primary election, primary feather, primary quill
Opposites
secondary

Primary as an adjective

  1. Of first rank or importance or value; direct and immediate rather than secondary.

    “Primary goals.” “A primary effect.” “Primary sources.”

    Opposite: secondary · Similar: capital, direct, firsthand, first-string, original, particular

  2. Not derived from or reducible to something else; basic.

    “A primary instinct.”

    Similar: underived

  3. Most important element.

    “The chief aim of living.” “The main doors were of solid glass.” “The principal rivers of America.”

    Synonyms: chief, main, principal, master · Similar: important

  4. Of or being the essential or basic part.

    “An elementary need for love and nurturing.”

    Synonyms: elementary, elemental · Similar: basic

  5. Of primary importance.

    Synonyms: basal · Similar: essential

Primary as a noun

  1. A preliminary election where delegates or nominees are chosen.

    Synonyms: primary election · A kind of: election · Kinds: direct primary

  2. One of the main flight feathers projecting along the outer edge of a bird's wing.

    Synonyms: primary feather, primary quill · A kind of: flight feather

  3. (astronomy) a celestial body (especially a star) relative to other objects in orbit around it.

    A kind of: celestial body

  4. Coil forming the part of an electrical circuit such that changing current in it induces a current in a neighboring circuit.

    “Current through the primary coil induces current in the secondary coil.”

    Synonyms: primary coil, primary winding · A kind of: coil

Phrases and compounds with "primary"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • open primary (noun) A primary in which any registered voter can vote (but must vote for candidates of only one party).
  • primary care (noun) The medical care received on first contact with the medical system (before being referred elsewhere).
  • primary cell (noun) An electric cell that generates an electromotive force by an irreversible conversion of chemical to electrical energy; cannot be recharged.
  • primary coil (noun) Coil forming the part of an electrical circuit such that changing current in it induces a current in a neighboring circuit.
  • primary color (noun) Any of three colors from which all others can be obtained by mixing.
  • primary quill (noun) One of the main flight feathers projecting along the outer edge of a bird's wing.
  • primary tooth (noun) One of the first temporary teeth of a young mammal (one of 20 in children).
  • closed primary (noun) A primary in which only registered members of a particular political party can vote.
  • direct primary (noun) A primary where voters directly select the candidates who will run for office.
  • primary colour (noun) Any of three colors from which all others can be obtained by mixing.
  • primary school (noun) A school for young children; usually the first 6 or 8 grades.
  • primary feather (noun) One of the main flight feathers projecting along the outer edge of a bird's wing.
  • primary winding (noun) Coil forming the part of an electrical circuit such that changing current in it induces a current in a neighboring circuit.
  • primary election (noun) A preliminary election where delegates or nominees are chosen.
  • primary syphilis (noun) The first stage; characterized by a chancre at the site of infection.
  • primary dentition (noun) Dentition of deciduous teeth.
