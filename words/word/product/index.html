Product: meaning, synonyms and examples

Product is used as a noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Commodities offered for sale.

In Hindi
उत्पाद; गुणनफल
Synonyms
merchandise, ware, production, mathematical product, intersection
Word family
produce

Product as a noun

  1. Commodities offered for sale.

    “Good business depends on having good merchandise.” “That store offers a variety of products.”

    Synonyms: merchandise, ware · A kind of: commodity · Kinds: cargo, contraband, feature, generic, ironmongery, irregular, line, number

  2. An artifact that has been created by someone or some process.

    “They improve their product every year.” “They export most of their agricultural production.”

    Synonyms: production · A kind of: creation · Kinds: book, book, by-product, deliverable, end product, inspiration, job, magazine

  3. A quantity obtained by multiplication.

    “The product of 2 and 3 is 6.”

    Synonyms: mathematical product · A kind of: quantity · Kinds: factorial, multiple

  4. A chemical substance formed as a result of a chemical reaction.

    “A product of lime and nitric acid.”

    A kind of: chemical · Kinds: hydrolysate, filtrate

  5. A consequence of someone's efforts or of a particular set of circumstances.

    “Skill is the product of hours of practice.” “His reaction was the product of hunger and fatigue.”

    A kind of: consequence

  6. The set of elements common to two or more sets.

    “The set of red hats is the intersection of the set of hats and the set of red things.”

    Synonyms: intersection · A kind of: set

Phrases and compounds with "product"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • by-product (noun) A secondary and sometimes unexpected consequence.
  • dot product (noun) A real number (a scalar) that is the product of two vectors.
  • end product (noun) Final product; the things produced.
  • food product (noun) A substance that can be used or prepared for use as food.
  • product line (noun) A particular kind of product or merchandise.
  • cross product (noun) A vector that is the product of two other vectors.
  • dairy product (noun) Milk and butter and cheese.
  • inner product (noun) A real number (a scalar) that is the product of two vectors.
  • plant product (noun) A product made from plant material.
  • waste product (noun) Any materials unused and rejected as worthless or unwanted.
  • animal product (noun) A product made from animal material.
  • scalar product (noun) A real number (a scalar) that is the product of two vectors.
  • vector product (noun) A vector that is the product of two other vectors.
  • product research (noun) Marketing research that yields information about desired characteristics of the product or service.
  • software product (noun) Merchandise consisting of a computer program that is offered for sale.
  • cartesian product (noun) The set of elements common to two or more sets.
