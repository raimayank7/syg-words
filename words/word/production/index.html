Production: meaning, synonyms and examples

Production is used as a noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: The act or process of producing something.

In Hindi
उत्पादन
Synonyms
product, output, yield
Word family
produce

Production as a noun

  1. The act or process of producing something.

    “Shakespeare's production of poetry was enormous.” “The production of white blood cells.”

    A kind of: act · Kinds: rainmaking, devising, foliation, canalization, growing, steel production

  2. A presentation for the stage or screen or radio or television.

    “Have you seen the new production of Hamlet?”

    A kind of: presentation · Kinds: theatrical production

  3. An artifact that has been created by someone or some process.

    “They improve their product every year.” “They export most of their agricultural production.”

    Synonyms: product · A kind of: creation · Kinds: book, book, by-product, deliverable, end product, inspiration, job, magazine

  4. (law) the act of exhibiting in a court of law.

    “The appellate court demanded the production of all documents.”

    A kind of: exhibition

  5. The quantity of something (as a commodity) that is created (usually within a given period of time).

    “Production was up in the second quarter.”

    Synonyms: output, yield · A kind of: indefinite quantity · Kinds: picking

  6. A display that is exaggerated or unduly complicated.

    “She tends to make a big production out of nothing.”

    A kind of: display

  7. (economics) manufacturing or mining or growing something (usually in large quantities) for sale.

    “He introduced more efficient methods of production.”

    A kind of: industry · Kinds: mass production, overproduction, underproduction, output, capacity, breeding, cultivation, cultivation

  8. The creation of value or wealth by producing goods and services.

    A kind of: creation

Phrases and compounds with "production"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • oil production (noun) The act of drilling a hole in the earth in the hope of producing petroleum.
  • mass production (noun) The production of large quantities of a standardized article (often using assembly line techniques).
  • pair production (noun) The transformation of a gamma-ray photon into an electron and a positron when the photon passes close to an atomic nucleus.
  • production cost (noun) Combined costs of raw material and labor incurred in producing goods.
  • production line (noun) Mechanical system in a factory whereby an article is conveyed through sites at which successive operations are performed on it.
  • production order (noun) An order that initiates the manufacturing process.
  • steel production (noun) Making steel from pig iron.
  • speech production (noun) The utterance of intelligible speech.
  • artistic production (noun) The creation of beautiful or significant things.
  • dramatic production (noun) The act of performing a drama.
  • theatrical production (noun) The production of a drama on the stage.
