Property: meaning, synonyms and examples

Property is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Something owned; any tangible or intangible possession that is owned by someone.

In Hindi
संपत्ति; गुण
Synonyms
belongings, holding, place, attribute, dimension, prop

Property as a noun

  1. Something owned; any tangible or intangible possession that is owned by someone.

    “That hat is my property.” “He is a man of property.”

    Synonyms: belongings, holding · A kind of: possession · Kinds: tangible possession, worldly possessions, ratables, hereditament, intellectual property, community property, personal property, things

  2. A basic or essential attribute shared by all members of a class.

    “A study of the physical properties of atomic particles.”

    A kind of: attribute · Kinds: actinism, isotropy, anisotropy, characteristic, connectivity, duality, heredity, age

  3. Any area set aside for a particular purpose.

    “Who owns this place?” “The president was concerned about the property across from the White House.”

    Synonyms: place · A kind of: geographical area · Kinds: boatyard, sanctuary, center, colony, hatchery

  4. A construct whereby objects or individuals can be distinguished.

    “Self-confidence is not an endearing property.”

    Synonyms: attribute, dimension · A kind of: concept · Kinds: quality, feature, feature of speech

  5. Any movable articles or objects used on the set of a play or movie.

    “Before every scene he ran down his checklist of props.”

    Synonyms: prop · A kind of: object · Kinds: custard pie
