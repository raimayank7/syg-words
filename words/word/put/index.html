Put: meaning, synonyms and examples

Put is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Put into a certain place or abstract location.

In Hindi
रखना
Synonyms
set, place, pose, position, lay, frame, redact, cast, couch, assign

Put as a verb

  1. Put into a certain place or abstract location.

    “Put your things here.” “Set the tray down.” “Set the dogs on the scent of the missing children.”

    Synonyms: set, place, pose, position, lay · A kind of: move · Kinds: insert, docket, cock, postpose, prepose, step, replace, stratify

  2. Cause to be in a certain state; cause to be in a certain relation.

    “That song put me in awful good humor.” “Put your ideas in writing.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: put to sleep

  3. Formulate in a particular style or language.

    “I wouldn't put it that way.” “She cast her request in very polite language.”

    Synonyms: frame, redact, cast, couch · A kind of: give voice

  4. Attribute or give.

    “She put too much emphasis on her the last statement.” “He put all his efforts into this job.” “The teacher put an interesting twist to the interpretation of the story.”

    Synonyms: assign · A kind of: use · Kinds: repose

  5. Make an investment.

    “Put money into bonds.”

    Synonyms: invest, commit, place · A kind of: spend · Kinds: fund, roll over, shelter, tie up, speculate, buy into

  6. Estimate.

    “We put the time of arrival at 8 P.M.”

    Synonyms: place, set · A kind of: estimate

  7. Cause (someone) to undergo something.

    “He put her to the torture.”

    A kind of: subject

  8. Adapt.

    “Put these words to music.”

    A kind of: arrange

  9. Arrange thoughts, ideas, temporal events.

    “Arrange my schedule.” “Set up one's life.” “I put these memories with those of bygone times.”

    Synonyms: arrange, set up, order · A kind of: organize · Kinds: synchronize, phrase

Put as a noun

  1. The option to sell a given stock (or stock index or commodity future) at a given price before a given date.

    Synonyms: put option · A kind of: option

Phrases and compounds with "put"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • put in (verb) Introduce.
  • put on (verb) Put clothing on one's body.
  • put up (verb) Place so as to be noticed.
  • put-on (noun) A composition that imitates or misrepresents somebody's style, usually in a humorous way.
  • put-up (adjective) Planned secretly.
  • put off (verb) Hold back to a later time.
  • put out (verb) To cause inconvenience or discomfort to.
  • put-put (noun) A small gasoline engine (as on motor boat).
  • hard put (adjective) Facing or experiencing financial trouble or difficulty.
  • put away (verb) Place in a place where something cannot be removed or someone cannot escape.
  • put back (verb) Put something back where it belongs.
  • put down (verb) Cause to sit or seat or be in a settled position or place.
  • put over (verb) Communicate successfully.
  • put-down (noun) A crushing remark.
  • put-upon (adjective) Of persons; taken advantage of.
  • shot put (noun) An athletic competition in which a heavy metal ball is hurled as far as possible.
