Quality: meaning, synonyms and examples

Quality is used as a noun (5 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An essential and distinguishing attribute of something or someone.

In Hindi
गुणवत्ता, गुण
Synonyms
caliber, calibre, character, lineament, timbre, timber, tone, choice, prime, prize
Word family
qualify

Quality as a noun

  1. An essential and distinguishing attribute of something or someone.

    “The quality of mercy is not strained.”

    A kind of: attribute · Kinds: appearance, attraction, clearness, opacity, divisibility, ease, difficulty, combustibility

  2. A degree or grade of excellence or worth.

    “The quality of students has risen.” “An executive of low caliber.”

    Synonyms: caliber, calibre · A kind of: degree · Kinds: superiority, inferiority

  3. A characteristic property that defines the apparent individual nature of something.

    “Each town has a quality all its own.” “The radical character of our demands.”

    Synonyms: character, lineament · A kind of: property · Kinds: texture

  4. (music) the distinctive property of a complex sound (a voice or noise or musical sound).

    “The timbre of her soprano was rich and lovely.” “The muffled tones of the broken bell summoned them to meet.”

    Synonyms: timbre, timber, tone · A kind of: sound property · Kinds: harmonic, resonance, color, nasality, plangency, shrillness, register

  5. High social status.

    “A man of quality.”

    A kind of: social station

Quality as an adjective

  1. Of superior grade.

    “Choice wines.” “Prime beef.” “Prize carnations.”

    Synonyms: choice, prime, prize, select · Similar: superior

  2. Of high social status.

    “People of quality.” “A quality family.”

    Similar: upper-class

Phrases and compounds with "quality"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • low quality (noun) An inferior quality.
  • top-quality (adjective) Surpassing in quality.
  • high quality (noun) The quality of being superior.
  • quality control (noun) Maintenance of standards of quality of manufactured goods.
  • quality of life (noun) Your personal satisfaction (or dissatisfaction) with the cultural or intellectual conditions under which you live (as distinct from material comfort).
  • maternal quality (noun) The quality of having or showing the tenderness and warmth and affection of or befitting a mother.
  • parental quality (noun) A quality appropriate to a parent.
  • paternal quality (noun) The benignity and protectiveness of or befitting a father.
  • decline in quality (noun) Process of changing to an inferior state.
