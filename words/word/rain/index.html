Rain: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rain is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Water falling in drops from vapor condensed in the atmosphere.

In Hindi
बारिश
Synonyms
rainfall, rainwater, pelting, rain down
Word family
rainy

Rain as a noun

  1. Water falling in drops from vapor condensed in the atmosphere.

    Synonyms: rainfall · A kind of: precipitation · Kinds: monsoon, rainstorm, downpour, drizzle, shower

  2. Drops of fresh water that fall as precipitation from clouds.

    Synonyms: rainwater · A kind of: fresh water

  3. Anything happening rapidly or in quick successive.

    “A rain of bullets.” “A pelting of insults.”

    Synonyms: pelting · A kind of: sequence

Rain as a verb

  1. Precipitate as rain.

    “If it rains much more, we can expect some flooding.”

    Synonyms: rain down · A kind of: precipitate · Kinds: drizzle, shower, sprinkle, pour

Phrases and compounds with "rain"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rain out (verb) Prevent or interrupt due to rain.
  • acid rain (noun) Rain containing acids that form in the atmosphere when industrial gas emissions (especially sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides) combine with water.
  • rain date (noun) An alternative date set for some outdoor event in case it rains on the appointed date.
  • rain down (verb) Precipitate as rain.
  • rain gage (noun) Gauge consisting of an instrument to measure the quantity of precipitation.
  • rain tree (noun) Large ornamental tropical American tree with bipinnate leaves and globose clusters of flowers with crimson stamens and seed pods that are eaten by cattle.
  • rain-wash (noun) The washing away of soil or other loose material by rain.
  • rain check (noun) A promise that an unaccepted offer will be renewed in the future.
  • rain cloud (noun) A dark grey cloud bearing rain.
  • rain dance (noun) A ritual dance intended to bring rain.
  • rain gauge (noun) Gauge consisting of an instrument to measure the quantity of precipitation.
  • rain stick (noun) A percussion instrument that is made from a dried cactus branch that is hollowed out and filled with small pebbles and capped at both ends; makes the sound of falling rain when tilted; origin was in Chile where tribesmen used it in ceremonies to bring rain.
  • rain-giver (noun) An epithet for Jupiter.
  • golden rain (noun) An ornamental shrub or tree of the genus Laburnum; often cultivated for Easter decorations.
  • rain barrel (noun) A barrel used as a cistern to hold rainwater.
  • rain forest (noun) A forest with heavy annual rainfall.
