Rank: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rank is used as a noun (5 senses), verb (3 senses) and adjective (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A row or line of people (especially soldiers or police) standing abreast of one another.

In Hindi
पद, दर्जा
Synonyms
rank and file, social station, social status, social rank, membership, rate, range, order, grade, place
Word family
ranker, ranking, rankness

Rank as a noun

  1. A row or line of people (especially soldiers or police) standing abreast of one another.

    “The entrance was guarded by ranks of policemen.”

    A kind of: line

  2. Relative status.

    “His salary was determined by his rank and seniority.”

    A kind of: status · Kinds: first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth

  3. The ordinary members of an organization (such as the enlisted soldiers of an army).

    “The strike was supported by the union rank and file.” “He rose from the ranks to become a colonel.”

    Synonyms: rank and file · A kind of: force

  4. Position in a social hierarchy.

    “The British are more aware of social status than Americans are.”

    Synonyms: social station, social status, social rank · A kind of: status · Kinds: place, quality

  5. The body of members of an organization or group.

    “They polled their membership.” “They found dissension in their own ranks.” “He joined the ranks of the unemployed.”

    Synonyms: membership · A kind of: body

Rank as a verb

  1. Take or have a position relative to others.

    “This painting ranks among the best in the Western World.”

    A kind of: be · Kinds: place, come

  2. Assign a rank or rating to.

    “How would you rank these students?” “The restaurant is rated highly in the food guide.”

    Synonyms: rate, range, order, grade, place · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: superordinate, shortlist, seed, reorder, subordinate, prioritize, sequence, downgrade

  3. Take precedence or surpass others in rank.

    Synonyms: outrank · A kind of: excel

Rank as an adjective

  1. Very fertile; producing profuse growth.

    “Rank earth.”

    Similar: fertile

  2. Conspicuously and outrageously bad or reprehensible.

    “A crying shame.” “An egregious lie.” “Flagrant violation of human rights.”

    Synonyms: crying, egregious, flagrant, glaring, gross · Similar: conspicuous

  3. Complete and without restriction or qualification; sometimes used informally as intensifiers.

    “Absolute freedom.” “An absolute dimwit.” “A downright lie.”

    Synonyms: absolute, downright, out-and-out, right-down, sheer · Similar: complete

  4. Growing profusely.

    “Rank jungle vegetation.”

    Similar: abundant
