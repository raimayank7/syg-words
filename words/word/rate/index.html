Rate: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rate is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A magnitude or frequency relative to a time unit.

In Hindi
दर
Synonyms
charge per unit, pace, rank, range, order, grade, place, value
Word family
rating

Rate as a noun

  1. A magnitude or frequency relative to a time unit.

    “They traveled at a rate of 55 miles per hour.” “The rate of change was faster than expected.”

    A kind of: magnitude relation · Kinds: acceleration, deceleration, attrition rate, birthrate, bits per second, crime rate, data rate, deathrate

  2. Amount of a charge or payment relative to some basis.

    “A 10-minute phone call at that rate would cost $5.”

    Synonyms: charge per unit · A kind of: charge · Kinds: pay rate, payment rate, tax rate, interest rate, freight, rate of depreciation, rate of exchange, excursion rate

  3. The relative speed of progress or change.

    “He lived at a fast pace.” “He works at a great rate.” “The pace of events accelerated.”

    Synonyms: pace · A kind of: temporal property · Kinds: speed, beat, celerity, slowness, sluggishness

  4. A quantity or amount or measure considered as a proportion of another quantity or amount or measure.

    “The literacy rate.” “The retention rate.” “The dropout rate.”

    A kind of: proportion

Rate as a verb

  1. Assign a rank or rating to.

    “How would you rank these students?” “The restaurant is rated highly in the food guide.”

    Synonyms: rank, range, order, grade, place · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: superordinate, shortlist, seed, reorder, subordinate, prioritize, sequence, downgrade

  2. Be worthy of or have a certain rating.

    “This bond rates highly.”

    A kind of: be

  3. Estimate the value of.

    “How would you rate his chances to become President?” “Gold was rated highly among the Romans.”

    Synonyms: value · A kind of: measure · Kinds: revalue

Phrases and compounds with "rate"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • cut rate (noun) A price below the standard price.
  • cut-rate (adjective) Costing less than standard price.
  • pay rate (noun) Amount of money received per unit time.
  • sed rate (noun) The rate at which red blood cells settle out in a tube of blood under standardized conditions; a high rate usually indicates the presence of inflammation.
  • tax rate (noun) Rate used to calculate tax liability.
  • bank rate (noun) The discount rate fixed by a central bank.
  • base rate (noun) The interest rate set by the Bank of England for lending to other banks.
  • baud rate (noun) (computer science) a data transmission rate (bits/second) for modems.
  • data rate (noun) The rate at which circuits or other devices operate when handling digital information.
  • dose rate (noun) The quantity of radiation absorbed per unit time.
  • flow rate (noun) The amount of fluid that flows in a given time.
  • room rate (noun) The rate charged daily for a hotel room.
  • birth rate (noun) The ratio of live births in an area to the population of that area; expressed per 1000 population per year.
  • crime rate (noun) The ratio of crimes in an area to the population of that area; expressed per 1000 population per year.
  • death rate (noun) The ratio of deaths in an area to the population of that area; expressed per 1000 per year.
  • first-rate (adjective) Of the highest quality.
