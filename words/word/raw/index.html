Raw: meaning, synonyms and examples

Raw is used as an adjective (13 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: (used especially of commodities) being unprocessed or manufactured using only simple or minimal processes.

In Hindi
कच्चा
Synonyms

Opposites
cooked
Word family
rawness

Raw as an adjective

  1. (used especially of commodities) being unprocessed or manufactured using only simple or minimal processes.

    “Natural yogurt.” “Natural produce.” “Raw wool.”

    Synonyms: natural, rude · Similar: unprocessed

  2. Having the surface exposed and painful.

    “A raw wound.”

    Similar: injured

  3. Not treated with heat to prepare it for eating.

    Opposite: cooked · Similar: half-baked, rare, uncooked, untoasted

  4. Not processed or refined.

    “Raw sewage.”

    Similar: untreated

  5. Devoid of elaboration or diminution or concealment; bare and pure.

    “Raw fury.”

    Similar: overt

  6. Brutally unfair or harsh.

    “Received raw treatment from his friends.” “A raw deal.”

    Similar: unfair

  7. Not processed or subjected to analysis.

    “Raw data.” “The raw cost of production.” “Only the crude vital statistics.”

    Synonyms: crude · Similar: unanalyzed

  8. Untempered and unrefined.

    “Raw talent.” “Raw beauty.”

    Similar: unpolished

  9. Hurting.

    “The tender spot on his jaw.”

    Synonyms: sensitive, sore, tender · Similar: painful

  10. Unpleasantly cold and damp.

    “Bleak winds of the North Atlantic.”

    Synonyms: bleak, cutting · Similar: cold

  11. Used of wood and furniture.

    “Raw wood.”

    Synonyms: unsanded · Similar: unfinished

  12. Lacking training or experience.

    “The new men were eager to fight.” “Raw recruits.”

    Synonyms: new · Similar: inexperienced

  13. (used informally) completely unclothed.

    Synonyms: bare-assed, bare-ass, in the altogether, in the buff, in the raw, peeled · Similar: unclothed

Raw as a noun

  1. Informal terms for nakedness.

    “In the raw.” “In the altogether.” “In his birthday suit.”

    Synonyms: altogether, birthday suit · A kind of: nakedness

Phrases and compounds with "raw"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • raw data (noun) Unanalyzed data; data not yet subjected to analysis.
  • raw deal (noun) Unfair treatment.
  • raw meat (noun) Uncooked meat.
  • raw milk (noun) Unpasteurized milk.
  • raw wood (noun) Wood that is not finished or painted.
  • raw wool (noun) Wool in its natural condition; not refined or processed.
  • raw umber (noun) Greenish brown pigment.
  • raw wound (noun) A wound that exposes subcutaneous tissue.
  • in the raw (adjective) (used informally) completely unclothed.
  • raw beauty (noun) Beauty that is stark and powerfully impressive.
  • raw sienna (noun) A medium to dark tan color.
  • raw talent (noun) Powerfully impressive talent.
  • raw throat (noun) Inflammation of the fauces and pharynx.
  • raw recruit (noun) An inexperienced and untrained recruit.
  • raw weather (noun) Unpleasantly cold and damp weather.
  • raw material (noun) Material suitable for manufacture or use or finishing.
