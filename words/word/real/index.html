Real: meaning, synonyms and examples

Real is used as an adjective (9 senses), adverb (1 sense) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Being or occurring in fact or actuality; having verified existence; not illusory.

In Hindi
असली, वास्तविक
Synonyms
existent, tangible, actual, genuine, literal, substantial, material, veridical, very, really
Opposites
unreal, nominal
Word family
realness, reality

Real as an adjective

  1. Being or occurring in fact or actuality; having verified existence; not illusory.

    “Real objects.” “Real people; not ghosts.” “A film based on real life.”

    Synonyms: existent · Opposite: unreal · Similar: actual, actual, objective, historical

  2. No less than what is stated; worthy of the name.

    “The real reason.” “Real war.” “A real friend.”

    Opposite: unreal · Similar: proper, true

  3. Not to be taken lightly.

    “Statistics demonstrate that poverty and unemployment are very real problems.” “To the man sleeping regularly in doorways homelessness is real.”

    Similar: serious

  4. Capable of being treated as fact.

    “Tangible evidence.” “His brief time as Prime Minister brought few real benefits to the poor.”

    Synonyms: tangible · Similar: concrete

  5. Being or reflecting the essential or genuine character of something.

    “Her actual motive.” “A literal solitude like a desert.” “A genuine dilemma.”

    Synonyms: actual, genuine, literal · Similar: true

  6. Of, relating to, or representing an amount that is corrected for inflation.

    “Real prices.” “Real income.” “Real wages.”

    Opposite: nominal

  7. Having substance or capable of being treated as fact; not imaginary.

    “The substantial world.” “A mere dream, neither substantial nor practical.” “Most ponderous and substantial things.”

    Synonyms: substantial, material

  8. (of property) fixed or immovable.

    “Real property consists of land and buildings.”

    Similar: tangible

  9. Coinciding with reality.

    “Perceptual error...has a surprising resemblance to veridical perception.”

    Synonyms: veridical · Similar: realistic

Real as an adverb

  1. Used as intensifiers; `real' is sometimes used informally for `really'; `rattling' is informal.

    “She was very gifted.” “He played very well.” “A really enjoyable evening.”

    Synonyms: very, really, rattling

Real as a noun

  1. Any rational or irrational number.

    Synonyms: real number · A kind of: complex number · Kinds: scalar product, rational number, irrational number

  2. The basic unit of money in Brazil; equal to 100 centavos.

  3. An old small silver Spanish coin.

    A kind of: coin
