Rear: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rear is used as a noun (5 senses), verb (5 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The back of a military formation or procession.

In Hindi

Opposites

Rear as a noun

  1. The back of a military formation or procession.

    “Infantrymen were in the rear.”

    Opposite: head · A kind of: formation

  2. The side of an object that is opposite its front.

    “His room was toward the rear of the hotel.”

    Synonyms: backside, back end · Opposite: front · A kind of: side · Kinds: nape, rearward

  3. The part of something that is furthest from the normal viewer.

    “He stood at the back of the stage.” “It was hidden in the rear of the store.”

    Synonyms: back · A kind of: position

  4. The fleshy part of the human body that you sit on.

    “He deserves a good kick in the butt.” “Are you going to sit on your fanny and do nothing?”

    Synonyms: nates, arse, butt, backside, bum, buns, can, fundament · A kind of: body part

  5. The side that goes last or is not normally seen.

    “He wrote the date on the back of the photograph.”

    Synonyms: back · Opposite: front · A kind of: side · Kinds: stern, tail

Rear as a verb

  1. Stand up on the hind legs, of quadrupeds.

    Synonyms: rise up · A kind of: straighten · Kinds: rear back

  2. Bring up.

    “Raise a family.” “Bring up children.”

    Synonyms: raise, bring up, nurture, parent · Kinds: fledge, cradle, foster

  3. Rise up.

    “The building rose before them.”

    Synonyms: rise, lift · A kind of: look · Kinds: loom

  4. Cause to rise up.

    Synonyms: erect · A kind of: raise · Kinds: pitch, prick up

  5. Construct, build, or erect.

    “Raise a barn.”

    Synonyms: raise, erect, set up, put up · A kind of: construct

Rear as an adjective

  1. Located in or toward the back or rear.

    “The chair's rear legs.” “The rear door of the plane.” “On the rearward side.”

    Synonyms: rearward · Similar: back
