Recall: meaning, synonyms and examples

Recall is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Recall knowledge from memory; have a recollection.

In Hindi
याद करना; वापस बुलाना
Synonyms
remember, retrieve, call back, call up, recollect, think, hark back, return, come back, echo
Opposites
issue

Recall as a verb

  1. Recall knowledge from memory; have a recollection.

    “I can't remember saying any such thing.” “I can't think what her last name was.” “Can you remember her phone number?”

    Synonyms: remember, retrieve, call back, call up, recollect, think · Kinds: know, recognize, review

  2. Go back to something earlier.

    “This harks back to a previous remark of his.”

    Synonyms: hark back, return, come back · A kind of: denote · Kinds: recur

  3. Call to mind.

    “His words echoed John F. Kennedy.”

    Synonyms: echo · A kind of: resemble

  4. Summon to return.

    “The ambassador was recalled to his country.” “The company called back many of the workers it had laid off during the recession.”

    Synonyms: call back · A kind of: call

  5. Cause one's (or someone else's) thoughts or attention to return from a reverie or digression.

    “She was recalled by a loud laugh.”

    A kind of: concentrate

  6. Make unavailable; bar from sale or distribution.

    “The company recalled the product when it was found to be faulty.”

    Opposite: issue · A kind of: cancel · Kinds: retire

  7. Cause to be returned.

    “Recall the defective auto tires.” “The manufacturer tried to call back the spoilt yoghurt.”

    Synonyms: call in, call back, withdraw · A kind of: take · Kinds: decommission

Recall as a noun

  1. A request by the manufacturer of a defective product to return the product (as for replacement or repair).

    Synonyms: callback · A kind of: request

  2. A call to return.

    “The recall of our ambassador.”

    A kind of: call

  3. A bugle call that signals troops to return.

    A kind of: bugle call

  4. The process of remembering (especially the process of recovering information by mental effort).

    “He has total recall of the episode.”

    Synonyms: recollection, reminiscence · A kind of: memory · Kinds: mind, reconstruction, reproduction, regurgitation

  5. The act of removing an official by petition.

    A kind of: abrogation

Phrases and compounds with "recall"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • recall dose (noun) An additional dose that makes sure the first dose was effective.
