Record: meaning, synonyms and examples

Record is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Anything (such as a document or a phonograph record or a photograph) providing permanent evidence of or information about past events.

Synonyms
phonograph record, phonograph recording, disk, disc, platter, track record, record book, book, criminal record, enter
Opposites
erase
Word family
recorder, recording

Record as a noun

  1. Anything (such as a document or a phonograph record or a photograph) providing permanent evidence of or information about past events.

    “The film provided a valuable record of stage techniques.”

    A kind of: evidence · Kinds: written record, memorabilia, stub, file, history, working papers

  2. Sound recording consisting of a disk with a continuous groove; used to reproduce music by rotating while a phonograph needle tracks in the groove.

    Synonyms: phonograph record, phonograph recording, disk, disc, platter · A kind of: sound recording · Kinds: seventy-eight

  3. The number of wins versus losses and ties a team has had.

    “At 9-0 they have the best record in their league.”

    A kind of: number

  4. The sum of recognized accomplishments.

    “The lawyer has a good record.” “The track record shows that he will be a good president.”

    Synonyms: track record · A kind of: accomplishment

  5. A compilation of the known facts regarding something or someone.

    “Al Smith used to say, `Let's look at the record'.” “His name is in all the record books.”

    Synonyms: record book, book · A kind of: fact · Kinds: logbook, won-lost record, card

  6. An extreme attainment; the best (or worst) performance ever attested (as in a sport).

    “He tied the Olympic record.” “Coffee production last year broke all previous records.” “Chicago set the homicide record.”

    A kind of: attainment · Kinds: track record, world record

  7. A document that can serve as legal evidence of a transaction.

    “They could find no record of the purchase.”

    A kind of: document · Kinds: balance sheet, expense record, ledger, payslip, register, bankbook, checkbook

  8. A list of crimes for which an accused person has been previously convicted.

    “He ruled that the criminal record of the defendant could not be disclosed to the court.”

    Synonyms: criminal record · A kind of: list

Record as a verb

  1. Make a record of; set down in permanent form.

    Synonyms: enter, put down · A kind of: save · Kinds: tally, clock in, record, accession, post, ring up, manifest, inscribe

  2. Register electronically.

    “They recorded her singing.”

    Synonyms: tape · Opposite: erase · A kind of: record · Kinds: write, tape record, prerecord, cut, cut

  3. Indicate a certain reading; of gauges and instruments.

    “The thermometer showed thirteen degrees below zero.” “The gauge read `empty'.”

    Synonyms: read, register, show · A kind of: indicate · Kinds: say, show, strike

  4. Be aware of.

    “Did you register any change when I pressed the button?”

    Synonyms: register

  5. Be or provide a memorial to a person or an event.

    “This sculpture commemorates the victims of the concentration camps.” “We memorialized the Dead.”

    Synonyms: commemorate, memorialize, memorialise, immortalize, immortalise · A kind of: remind · Kinds: monumentalize

Phrases and compounds with "record"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • record hop (noun) An informal dance where popular music is played.
  • record book (noun) A compilation of the known facts regarding something or someone.
  • tape record (verb) Record with a tape recorder.
  • record album (noun) One or more recordings issued together; originally released on 12-inch phonograph records (usually with attractive record covers) and later on cassette audiotape and compact disc.
  • record cover (noun) A sleeve for storing a phonograph record.
  • track record (noun) The fastest time ever recorded for a specific distance at a particular racetrack.
  • world record (noun) The best record in the whole world.
  • record jacket (noun) The jacket for a phonograph record.
  • record player (noun) Machine in which rotating records cause a stylus to vibrate and the vibrations are amplified acoustically or electronically.
  • record sleeve (noun) A sleeve for storing a phonograph record.
  • record-holder (noun) Someone who breaks a record.
  • record-keeper (noun) Someone responsible for keeping records.
  • expense record (noun) A written record of money spent.
  • medical record (noun) The case history of a medical patient as recalled by the patient.
  • off the record (adverb) Not for quotation.
  • record changer (noun) An automatic mechanical device on a record player that causes new records to be played without manual intervention.
