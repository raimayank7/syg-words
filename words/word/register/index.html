Register: meaning, synonyms and examples

Register is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Record in writing; enter into a book of names or events or transactions.

In Hindi
रजिस्टर; दर्ज करना
Synonyms
file, record, read, show, cross-file, registry, cash register
Word family
registration, registrant, registry

Register as a verb

  1. Record in writing; enter into a book of names or events or transactions.

    A kind of: record · Kinds: file, patent, enroll, list, book

  2. Record in a public office or in a court of law.

    “File for divorce.” “File a complaint.”

    Synonyms: file · A kind of: register · Kinds: trademark

  3. Enroll to vote.

    “Register for an election.”

    A kind of: enroll

  4. Be aware of.

    “Did you register any change when I pressed the button?”

    Synonyms: record

  5. Indicate a certain reading; of gauges and instruments.

    “The thermometer showed thirteen degrees below zero.” “The gauge read `empty'.”

    Synonyms: read, show, record · A kind of: indicate · Kinds: say, show, strike

  6. Have one's name listed as a candidate for several parties.

    Synonyms: cross-file · A kind of: campaign · Kinds: slate

  7. Show in one's face.

    “Her surprise did not register.”

    A kind of: show

  8. Manipulate the registers of an organ.

    A kind of: play

  9. Send by registered mail.

    “I'd like to register this letter.”

    A kind of: mail

  10. Enter into someone's consciousness.

    “Did this event register in your parents' minds?”

    A kind of: affect

Register as a noun

  1. An official written record of names or events or transactions.

    Synonyms: registry · A kind of: written record · Kinds: cadaster, check register, studbook, rent-roll

  2. (music) the timbre that is characteristic of a certain range and manner of production of the human voice or of different pipe organ stops or of different musical instruments.

    A kind of: timbre · Kinds: head register, chest register

  3. A book in which names and transactions are listed.

    A kind of: record · Kinds: accounting, inventory, payroll

  4. (computer science) memory device that is the part of computer memory that has a specific address and that is used to hold information of a specific kind.

    A kind of: memory device · Kinds: accumulator, counter, index register, shift register

  5. An air passage (usually in the floor or a wall of a room) for admitting or excluding heated air from the room.

    A kind of: air passage

  6. A regulator (as a sliding plate) for regulating the flow of air into a furnace or other heating device.

    A kind of: regulator

  7. A cashbox with an adding machine to register transactions; used in shops to add up the bill.

    Synonyms: cash register · A kind of: cashbox

Phrases and compounds with "register"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • cash register (noun) A cashbox with an adding machine to register transactions; used in shops to add up the bill.
  • head register (noun) The higher ranges of the voice in speaking or singing; the vibrations of sung notes are felt in the head.
  • check register (noun) A register of checks issued (usually in numeric order).
  • chest register (noun) The lower ranges of the voice in speaking or singing.
  • index register (noun) (computer science) a register used to determine the address of an operand.
  • shift register (noun) (computer science) register in which all bits can be shifted one or more positions to the left or to the right.
  • register language (noun) A tone language that uses different voice registers.
  • accumulator register (noun) (computer science) a register that has a built-in adder that adds an input number to the contents of the register.
