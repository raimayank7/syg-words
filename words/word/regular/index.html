Regular: meaning, synonyms and examples

Regular is used as an adjective (13 senses) and noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: In accordance with fixed order or procedure or principle.

In Hindi
नियमित
Synonyms

Opposites
irregular
Word family
regularity

Regular as an adjective

  1. In accordance with fixed order or procedure or principle.

    “His regular calls on his customers.” “Regular meals.” “Regular duties.”

    Opposite: irregular · Similar: first-string, lawful, official, standard, timed, uniform

  2. Often used as intensifiers.

    “A regular morass of details.” “A regular nincompoop.” “He's a veritable swine.”

    Synonyms: veritable · Similar: typical

  3. Conforming to a standard or pattern.

    “Following the regular procedure of the legislature.” “A regular electrical outlet.”

    Similar: standard

  4. Regularly scheduled for fixed times.

    “At a regular meeting of the PTA.” “Regular bus departures.”

    Similar: scheduled

  5. In accord with regular practice or procedure.

    “Took his regular morning walk.” “Her regular bedtime.”

    Similar: usual

  6. Occurring at fixed intervals.

    “A regular beat.” “The even rhythm of his breathing.”

    Synonyms: even · Similar: steady

  7. Relating to a person who does something regularly.

    “A regular customer.” “A steady drinker.”

    Synonyms: steady · Similar: frequent

  8. (used of the military) belonging to or engaged in by legitimate army forces.

    “The regular army.”

    Opposite: irregular · Similar: standing

  9. (of solids) having clear dimensions that can be measured; volume can be determined with a suitable geometric formula.

    Opposite: irregular

  10. Not constipated.

    Synonyms: unconstipated · Similar: diarrheal, lax

  11. Symmetrically arranged.

    “Even features.” “Regular features.” “A regular polygon.”

    Synonyms: even · Similar: symmetrical

  12. Not deviating from what is normal.

    “Her regular bedtime.”

    Similar: normal

  13. Officially full-time.

    “Regular students.”

    Similar: full-time

Regular as a noun

  1. A regular patron.

    “An habitue of the racetrack.” “A bum who is a Central Park fixture.”

    Synonyms: habitue, fixture · A kind of: patron

  2. A soldier in the regular army.

    A kind of: soldier

  3. A dependable follower (especially in party politics).

    “He is one of the party regulars.”

    A kind of: follower

  4. A garment size for persons of average height and weight.

    A kind of: size

  • regular polygon (noun) A polygon with all sides and all angles equal.

  • regular hexahedron (noun) A hexahedron with six equal squares as faces.

  • regular recurrence (noun) Recurring at regular intervals.
  • regular icosahedron (noun) An icosahedron with twenty equilateral triangles as faces.
  • regular tetrahedron (noun) A tetrahedron with four equilateral triangular faces.
  • regular convex solid (noun) Any one of five solids whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose polyhedral angles are all congruent.
  • regular dodecahedron (noun) A dodecahedron with twelve regular pentagons as faces.
