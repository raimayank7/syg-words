Release: meaning, synonyms and examples

Release is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (12 senses). Its most common meaning is: Release, as from one's grip.

In Hindi
छोड़ना, जारी करना
Synonyms
let go of, let go, relinquish, free, liberate, unloose, unloosen, loose, turn, publish

Release as a verb

  1. Release, as from one's grip.

    “Let go of the door handle, please!” “Relinquish your grip on the rope--you won't fall.”

    Synonyms: let go of, let go, relinquish · Kinds: discharge, unclasp, pop, toggle, unhand, bring out, unleash, unleash

  2. Grant freedom to; free from confinement.

    Synonyms: free, liberate, unloose, unloosen, loose · Kinds: unspell, unchain, bail, run, bail out, parole

  3. Let (something) fall or spill from a container.

    “Turn the flour onto a plate.”

    Synonyms: turn · A kind of: transmit · Kinds: deflate, throw

  4. Prepare and issue for public distribution or sale.

    “Publish a magazine or newspaper.”

    Synonyms: publish, bring out, put out, issue · A kind of: publicize · Kinds: edit

  5. Eliminate (a substance).

    “Combustion products are exhausted in the engine.” “The plant releases a gas.”

    Synonyms: exhaust, discharge, expel, eject · Kinds: expectorate, blow, ejaculate, ovulate, excrete, shed blood, spew, fester

  6. Generate and separate from cells or bodily fluids.

    “Secrete digestive juices.” “Release a hormone into the blood stream.”

    Synonyms: secrete · A kind of: exude · Kinds: water

  7. Make (information) available for publication.

    “Release the list with the names of the prisoners.”

    Synonyms: free · A kind of: issue · Kinds: declassify

  8. Part with a possession or right.

    “I am relinquishing my bedroom to the long-term house guest.” “Resign a claim to the throne.”

    Synonyms: relinquish, resign, free, give up · A kind of: pass · Kinds: derequisition, sacrifice

  9. Release (gas or energy) as a result of a chemical reaction or physical decomposition.

    Synonyms: free, liberate · A kind of: generate

  10. Make (assets) available.

    “Release the holdings in the dictator's bank account.”

    Synonyms: unblock, unfreeze, free · A kind of: issue

Release as a noun

  1. Merchandise issued for sale or public showing (especially a record or film).

    “A new release from the London Symphony Orchestra.”

    A kind of: merchandise

  2. The act of liberating someone or something.

    Synonyms: liberation, freeing · A kind of: accomplishment · Kinds: jail delivery, deregulation, relief, unsnarling, emancipation, clearing, parole, probation

  3. A process that liberates or discharges something.

    “There was a sudden release of oxygen.” “The release of iodine from the thyroid gland.”

    A kind of: natural process · Kinds: emission

  4. An announcement distributed to members of the press in order to supplement or replace an oral presentation.

    Synonyms: handout, press release · A kind of: announcement

  5. The termination of someone's employment (leaving them free to depart).

    Synonyms: dismissal, dismission, discharge, firing, liberation, sack, sacking · A kind of: termination · Kinds: superannuation, conge, removal, deactivation, honorable discharge

  6. Euphemistic expressions for death.

    “Thousands mourned his passing.”

    Synonyms: passing, loss, departure, exit, expiration, going · A kind of: death

  7. A legal document evidencing the discharge of a debt or obligation.

    Synonyms: acquittance · A kind of: legal document

  8. A device that when pressed will release part of a mechanism.

    Synonyms: button · A kind of: device

  9. Activity that frees or expresses creative energy or emotion.

    “She had no other outlet for her feelings.” “He gave vent to his anger.”

    Synonyms: outlet, vent · A kind of: activity

  10. The act of allowing a fluid to escape.

    Synonyms: spill, spillage · A kind of: flow

  11. A formal written statement of relinquishment.

    Synonyms: waiver, discharge · A kind of: relinquishment · Kinds: exemption

  12. (music) the act or manner of terminating a musical phrase or tone.

    Synonyms: tone ending · A kind of: termination · Kinds: plosion
