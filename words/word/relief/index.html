Relief: meaning, synonyms and examples

Relief is used as a noun (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: The feeling that comes when something burdensome is removed or reduced.

In Hindi
राहत
Synonyms
alleviation, assuagement, ease, stand-in, substitute, reliever, backup, backup man, fill-in, succor

Relief as a noun

  1. The feeling that comes when something burdensome is removed or reduced.

    “As he heard the news he was suddenly flooded with relief.”

    Synonyms: alleviation, assuagement · A kind of: comfort

  2. The condition of being comfortable or relieved (especially after being relieved of distress).

    “He enjoyed his relief from responsibility.” “Getting it off his conscience gave him some ease.”

    Synonyms: ease · A kind of: comfort · Kinds: reprieve

  3. (law) redress awarded by a court.

    “Was the relief supposed to be protection from future harm or compensation for past injury?”

    A kind of: damages

  4. Someone who takes the place of another (as when things get dangerous or difficult).

    “The star had a stand-in for dangerous scenes.” “We need extra employees for summer fill-ins.”

    Synonyms: stand-in, substitute, reliever, backup, backup man, fill-in · A kind of: peer · Kinds: locum tenens, double, surrogate

  5. Assistance in time of difficulty.

    “The contributions provided some relief for the victims.”

    Synonyms: succor, succour, ministration · A kind of: aid · Kinds: consolation, mercy

  6. A pause for relaxation.

    “People actually accomplish more when they take time for short rests.”

    Synonyms: respite, rest, rest period · A kind of: pause · Kinds: breath

  7. A change for the better.

    Synonyms: easing, moderation · A kind of: change · Kinds: disembarrassment, breath of fresh air

  8. Aid for the aged or indigent or handicapped.

    “He has been on relief for many years.”

    A kind of: social welfare

  9. The act of reducing something unpleasant (as pain or annoyance).

    “He asked the nurse for relief from the constant pain.”

    Synonyms: easing, easement, alleviation · A kind of: decrease · Kinds: spasmolysis, detente, palliation, liberalization, decompression

  10. Sculpture consisting of shapes carved on a surface so as to stand out from the surrounding background.

    Synonyms: relievo, rilievo, embossment, sculptural relief · A kind of: sculpture · Kinds: alto relievo, bas relief, mezzo-relievo

  11. The act of freeing a city or town that has been besieged.

    “He asked for troops for the relief of Atlanta.”

    A kind of: liberation

Phrases and compounds with "relief"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bas relief (noun) A sculptural relief in which forms extend only slightly from the background; no figures are undercut.
  • low relief (noun) A sculptural relief in which forms extend only slightly from the background; no figures are undercut.
  • relief map (noun) A map having contour lines through points of equal elevation.
  • half-relief (noun) A sculptural relief between low relief and high relief.
  • high relief (noun) A sculptural relief in which forms extend out from the background to at least half their depth.
  • relief valve (noun) A valve in a container in which pressure can build up (as a steam boiler); it opens automatically when the pressure reaches a dangerous level.
  • relief pitcher (noun) A pitcher who does not start the game.
  • relief printing (noun) Printing from a plate with raised characters.
  • sculptural relief (noun) Sculpture consisting of shapes carved on a surface so as to stand out from the surrounding background.
