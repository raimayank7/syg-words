Representation: meaning, synonyms and examples

Representation is used as a noun (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: A presentation to the mind in the form of an idea or image.

In Hindi
प्रतिनिधित्व; चित्रण
Synonyms
mental representation, internal representation, delegacy, agency, theatrical performance, theatrical, histrionics
Word family
represent

Representation as a noun

  1. A presentation to the mind in the form of an idea or image.

    Synonyms: mental representation, internal representation · A kind of: content · Kinds: overlap, instantiation, antitype, stereotype, schema, image, interpretation, phantasmagoria

  2. A creation that is a visual or tangible rendering of someone or something.

    A kind of: creation · Kinds: adumbration, audiogram, copy, cosmography, creche, cutaway, display, document

  3. The act of representing; standing in for someone or some group and speaking with authority in their behalf.

    A kind of: cooperation · Kinds: proportional representation

  4. The state of serving as an official and authorized delegate or agent.

    Synonyms: delegacy, agency · A kind of: state · Kinds: free agency, legal representation, virus

  5. A body of legislators that serve in behalf of some constituency.

    “A Congressional vacancy occurred in the representation from California.”

    A kind of: body

  6. A factual statement made by one party in order to induce another party to enter into a contract.

    “The sales contract contains several representations by the vendor.”

    A kind of: statement

  7. A performance of a play.

    Synonyms: theatrical performance, theatrical, histrionics · A kind of: performance · Kinds: matinee

  8. A statement of facts and reasons made in appealing or protesting.

    “Certain representations were made concerning police brutality.”

    A kind of: statement

  9. The right of being represented by delegates who have a voice in some legislative body.

    A kind of: right

  10. An activity that stands as an equivalent of something or results in an equivalent.

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: model, dramatization, pageant, figuration, schematization, pictorial representation, typification

Phrases and compounds with "representation"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • legal representation (noun) Personal representation that has legal status.
  • mental representation (noun) A presentation to the mind in the form of an idea or image.
  • concrete representation (noun) A representation of an abstract idea in concrete terms.
  • internal representation (noun) A presentation to the mind in the form of an idea or image.
  • symbolic representation (noun) Something visible that by association or convention represents something else that is invisible.
  • pictorial representation (noun) Visual representation as by photography or painting.
  • proportional representation (noun) Representation of all parties in proportion to their popular vote.
  • number representation system (noun) Any notation for the representation of numbers.
  • positional representation system (noun) A numeration system in which a real number is represented by an ordered set of characters where the value of a character depends on its position.
  • fixed-point representation system (noun) A radix numeration system in which the location of the decimal point is fixed by convention.
  • floating-point representation system (noun) A radix numeration system in which the location of the decimal point is indicated by an exponent of the radix; in the floating-point representation system, 0.0012 is represented as 0.12-2 where -2 is the exponent.
