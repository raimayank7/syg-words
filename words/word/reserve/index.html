Reserve: meaning, synonyms and examples

Reserve is used as a verb (4 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Hold back or set aside, especially for future use or contingency.

In Hindi
सुरक्षित रखना; आरक्षित भंडार
Synonyms
allow, appropriate, earmark, set aside, hold, book, modesty, backlog, stockpile, substitute
Word family
reservation, reservist

Reserve as a verb

  1. Hold back or set aside, especially for future use or contingency.

    “They held back their applause in anticipation.”

    A kind of: withhold · Kinds: devote

  2. Give or assign a resource to a particular person or cause.

    “I will earmark this money for your research.” “She sets aside time for meditation every day.”

    Synonyms: allow, appropriate, earmark, set aside · A kind of: assign

  3. Obtain or arrange (for oneself) in advance.

    “We managed to reserve a table at Maxim's.”

    A kind of: request · Kinds: book up

  4. Arrange for and reserve (something for someone else) in advance.

    “Reserve me a seat on a flight.” “The agent booked tickets to the show for the whole family.” “Please hold a table at Maxim's.”

    Synonyms: hold, book · A kind of: request · Kinds: keep open

Reserve as a noun

  1. Formality and propriety of manner.

    Synonyms: modesty · A kind of: propriety · Kinds: demureness

  2. Something kept back or saved for future use or a special purpose.

    Synonyms: backlog, stockpile · A kind of: accumulation · Kinds: bank, fuel level, reserve account

  3. An athlete who plays only when a starter on the team is replaced.

    Synonyms: substitute, second-stringer · A kind of: athlete · Kinds: bench warmer, pinch hitter

  4. (medicine) potential capacity to respond in order to maintain vital functions.

    A kind of: indefinite quantity · Kinds: pulmonary reserve

  5. A district that is reserved for particular purpose.

    Synonyms: reservation · A kind of: administrative district · Kinds: preserve

  6. Armed forces that are not on active duty but can be called in an emergency.

    Synonyms: military reserve · A kind of: military

  7. The trait of being uncommunicative; not volunteering anything more than necessary.

    Synonyms: reticence, taciturnity · A kind of: uncommunicativeness

Phrases and compounds with "reserve"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • home reserve (noun) United States military reserves recruited by the states and equipped by the federal government; subject to call by either.
  • reserve bank (noun) One of 12 regional banks that monitor and act as depositories for banks in their region.
  • reserve fund (noun) Funds taken out of earnings to provide for anticipated future payments.
  • hidden reserve (noun) Reserves that do not show up on the balance sheet (as by understating values).
  • reserve assets (noun) Capital held back from investment in order to meet probable or possible demands.
  • reserve clause (noun) A clause that used to be part of the contract with a professional athlete extending the contract for a year beyond its expiration.
  • federal reserve (noun) The central bank of the United States; incorporates 12 Federal Reserve branch banks and all national banks and state-chartered commercial banks and some trust companies.
  • reserve account (noun) Funds taken out of earnings to provide for anticipated future payments.
  • military reserve (noun) Armed forces that are not on active duty but can be called in an emergency.
  • pulmonary reserve (noun) The additional volume of air that the lungs can inhale and exhale when breathing to the limit of capacity in times of stress.
  • valuation reserve (noun) A reserve fund created by a charge against profits in order to provide for changes in the value of a company's assets.
  • cookie jar reserve (noun) A hidden reserve that can be used to adjust quarterly earning reports.
  • territorial reserve (noun) A territorial military unit.
  • federal reserve bank (noun) One of 12 regional banks that monitor and act as depositories for banks in their region.
  • federal reserve note (noun) A piece of paper money (especially one issued by a central bank).
  • federal reserve board (noun) The seven-member board governing the Federal Reserve System.
