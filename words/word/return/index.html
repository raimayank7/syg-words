Return: meaning, synonyms and examples

Return is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (13 senses). Its most common meaning is: Go or come back to place, condition, or activity where one has been before.

In Hindi
लौटना, लौटाना
Synonyms
render, revert, retrovert, regress, turn back, hark back, come back, recall, take back, bring back

Return as a verb

  1. Go or come back to place, condition, or activity where one has been before.

    “Return to your native land.” “The professor returned to his teaching position after serving as Dean.”

    A kind of: travel · Kinds: resurrect, revisit, trace, backtrack, cut back, home, go home, return

  2. Give back.

    “Render money.”

    Synonyms: render · A kind of: give · Kinds: feed back

  3. Go back to a previous state.

    “We reverted to the old rules.”

    Synonyms: revert, retrovert, regress, turn back · A kind of: change by reversal · Kinds: relapse, resile, recover

  4. Go back to something earlier.

    “This harks back to a previous remark of his.”

    Synonyms: hark back, come back, recall · A kind of: denote · Kinds: recur

  5. Bring back to the point of departure.

    Synonyms: take back, bring back · A kind of: bring

  6. Return in kind.

    “Return a compliment.” “Return her love.”

    A kind of: reciprocate · Kinds: redound

  7. Make a return.

    “Return a kickback.”

    A kind of: transport

  8. Answer back.

    Synonyms: retort, come back, repay, riposte, rejoin · A kind of: answer

  9. Be restored.

    “Her old vigor returned.”

    Synonyms: come back · A kind of: reappear

  10. Pay back.

    “Please refund me my money.”

    Synonyms: refund, repay, give back · A kind of: pay · Kinds: reimburse, restore

  11. Pass down.

    “Render a verdict.” “Deliver a judgment.”

    Synonyms: render, deliver · A kind of: communicate

  12. Elect again.

    Synonyms: reelect · A kind of: elect

  13. Be inherited by.

    “The estate fell to my sister.” “The land returned to the family.” “The estate devolved to an heir that everybody had assumed to be dead.”

    Synonyms: fall, pass, devolve · A kind of: change hands · Kinds: fall

  14. Return to a previous position; in mathematics.

    “The point returned to the interior of the figure.”

    A kind of: return

Return as a noun

  1. Document giving the tax collector information about the taxpayer's tax liability.

    “His gross income was enough that he had to file a tax return.”

    Synonyms: tax return, income tax return · A kind of: legal document · Kinds: amended return, declaration of estimated tax, false return, information return, joint return

  2. A coming to or returning home.

    “On his return from Australia we gave him a welcoming party.”

    Synonyms: homecoming · A kind of: arrival · Kinds: repatriation

  3. The occurrence of a change in direction back in the opposite direction.

    Synonyms: coming back · A kind of: turning

  4. Getting something back again.

    “Upon the restitution of the book to its rightful owner the child was given a tongue lashing.”

    Synonyms: restitution, restoration, regaining · A kind of: acquisition · Kinds: clawback

  5. The act of going back to a prior location.

    “They set out on their return to the base camp.”

    A kind of: motion · Kinds: reentry, remand

  6. The income or profit arising from such transactions as the sale of land or other property.

    “The average return was about 5%.”

    Synonyms: issue, take, takings, proceeds, yield, payoff · A kind of: income · Kinds: economic rent, payback

  7. Happening again (especially at regular intervals).

    “The return of spring.”

    Synonyms: recurrence · A kind of: repeat · Kinds: atavism

  8. A quick reply to a question or remark (especially a witty or critical one).

    “It brought a sharp rejoinder from the teacher.”

    Synonyms: rejoinder, retort, riposte, replication, comeback, counter · A kind of: reply · Kinds: sass

  9. The key on electric typewriters or computer keyboards that causes a carriage return and a line feed.

    Synonyms: return key · A kind of: key

  10. A reciprocal group action.

    “In return we gave them as good as we got.”

    Synonyms: paying back, getting even · A kind of: group action · Kinds: requital, retaliation, reciprocation, tit for tat

  11. A tennis stroke that sends the ball back to the other player.

    “He won the point on a cross-court return.”

    A kind of: tennis stroke · Kinds: backhand, chop, drive, drop shot, forehand, get, ground stroke, half volley

  12. (American football) the act of running back the ball after a kickoff or punt or interception or fumble.

    A kind of: run

  13. The act of someone appearing again.

    “His reappearance as Hamlet has been long awaited.”

    Synonyms: reappearance · A kind of: appearance · Kinds: comeback

Phrases and compounds with "return"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in return (adverb) (often followed by `for') in exchange or in reciprocation.
  • day return (noun) A return ticket (at reduced fare) for traveling both ways in the same day.
  • return key (noun) The key on electric typewriters or computer keyboards that causes a carriage return and a line feed.
  • tax return (noun) Document giving the tax collector information about the taxpayer's tax liability.
  • false return (noun) An incorrect income tax return.
  • joint return (noun) A return filed by a husband and wife.
  • return ticket (noun) A ticket to a place and back (usually over the same route).
  • amended return (noun) A tax return that corrects the information in an earlier return.
  • rate of return (noun) The amount returned per unit of time expressed as a percentage of the cost.
  • return address (noun) The address of the sender of a letter or parcel indicating where it should be returned if it cannot be delivered.
  • carriage return (noun) The operation that prepares for the next character to be printed or displayed as the first character on a line.
  • income tax return (noun) Document giving the tax collector information about the taxpayer's tax liability.
  • exponential return (noun) A decrease that follows an exponential function.
  • information return (noun) A return that provides information to the tax collector but does not compute the tax liability.
  • point of no return (noun) A line that when crossed permits of no return and typically results in irrevocable commitment.
  • estimated tax return (noun) Return required of a taxpayer whose tax withheld from income does not meet the tax liability for the year.
