Rich: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rich is used as an adjective (12 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Possessing material wealth.

In Hindi
अमीर, धनी
Synonyms
fat, fertile, productive, deep, full-bodied, racy, robust, ample, copious, plenteous
Opposites
poor, lean
Word family
richness

Rich as an adjective

  1. Possessing material wealth.

    “Her father is extremely rich.” “Many fond hopes are pinned on rich uncles.”

    Opposite: poor · Similar: affluent, comfortable

  2. Having an abundant supply of desirable qualities or substances (especially natural resources).

    “Blessed with a land rich in minerals.” “Rich in ideas.” “Rich with cultural interest.”

    Opposite: poor

  3. Of great worth or quality.

    “A rich collection of antiques.”

    Similar: valuable

  4. Marked by great fruitfulness.

    “Fertile farmland.” “A fat land.” “A productive vineyard.”

    Synonyms: fat, fertile, productive · Similar: fruitful

  5. Strong; intense.

    “Deep purple.” “A rich red.”

    Synonyms: deep · Similar: colorful

  6. Very productive.

    “Rich seams of coal.”

    Similar: productive

  7. High in mineral content; having a high proportion of fuel to air.

    “A rich vein of copper.” “A rich gas mixture.”

    Opposite: lean

  8. Suggestive of or characterized by great expense.

    “A rich display.”

    Opposite: poor · Similar: deluxe, lavish

  9. Containing plenty of fat, or eggs, or sugar.

    “Rich desserts.” “They kept gorging on rich foods.”

    Similar: unwholesome

  10. Marked by richness and fullness of flavor.

    “A rich ruby port.” “Full-bodied wines.” “A robust claret.”

    Synonyms: full-bodied, racy, robust · Similar: tasty

  11. Pleasantly full and mellow.

    “A rich tenor voice.”

    Similar: full

  12. Affording an abundant supply.

    “Had ample food for the party.” “Copious provisions.” “Food is plentiful.”

    Synonyms: ample, copious, plenteous, plentiful · Similar: abundant

Rich as a noun

  1. People who have possessions and wealth (considered as a group).

    “Only the very rich benefit from this legislation.”

    Synonyms: rich people · Opposite: poor · A kind of: people

Phrases and compounds with "rich"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rich man (noun) A man who is wealthy.
  • nectar-rich (adjective) Of plants that are rich in nectar.
  • rich people (noun) People who have possessions and wealth (considered as a group).
  • rich person (noun) A person who possesses great material wealth.
  • oil-rich seed (noun) Any of several seeds that yield oil.
