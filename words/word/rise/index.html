Rise: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rise is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move upward.

In Hindi
उठना, बढ़ना
Synonyms
lift, arise, move up, go up, come up, uprise, climb, get up, stand up, rear
Opposites
fall, set
Word family
rising, riser

Rise as a verb

  1. Move upward.

    “The fog lifted.” “The smoke arose from the forest fire.” “The mist uprose from the meadows.”

    Synonyms: lift, arise, move up, go up, come up, uprise · Opposite: fall · A kind of: travel · Kinds: scend, climb, soar, go up, rocket, bubble, uplift, chandelle

  2. Increase in value or to a higher point.

    “Prices climbed steeply.” “The value of our house rose sharply last year.”

    Synonyms: go up, climb · A kind of: grow · Kinds: soar, bull

  3. Rise to one's feet.

    “The audience got up and applauded.”

    Synonyms: arise, uprise, get up, stand up · A kind of: change posture · Kinds: take the floor

  4. Rise up.

    “The building rose before them.”

    Synonyms: lift, rear · A kind of: look · Kinds: loom

  5. Come to the surface.

    Synonyms: surface, come up, rise up · A kind of: ascend · Kinds: emerge, resurface, bubble up, well

  6. Come into existence; take on form or shape.

    “A new religious movement originated in that country.” “A love that sprang up from friendship.” “The idea for the book grew out of a short story.”

    Synonyms: originate, arise, develop, uprise, spring up, grow · A kind of: become · Kinds: resurge, come forth, come, well up, head

  7. Move to a better position in life or to a better job.

    Synonyms: ascend, move up · A kind of: change

  8. Go up or advance.

    “Sales were climbing after prices were lowered.”

    Synonyms: wax, mount, climb · A kind of: increase · Kinds: jump, advance

  9. Become more extreme.

    “The tension heightened.”

    Synonyms: heighten · A kind of: increase

  10. Get up and out of bed.

    “I get up at 7 A.M. every day.” “They rose early.” “He uprose at night.”

    Synonyms: get up, turn out, arise, uprise

  11. Rise in rank or status.

    “Her new novel jumped high on the bestseller list.”

    Synonyms: jump, climb up · A kind of: change

  12. Become heartened or elated.

    “Her spirits rose when she heard the good news.”

  13. Exert oneself to meet a challenge.

    “Rise to a challenge.” “Rise to the occasion.”

    A kind of: undertake

  14. Take part in a rebellion; renounce a former allegiance.

    Synonyms: rebel, arise, rise up · A kind of: protest · Kinds: revolt, mutiny

Rise as a noun

  1. A growth in strength or number or importance.

    Opposite: fall · A kind of: emergence · Kinds: crime wave, wave, spike

  2. The act of changing location in an upward direction.

    Synonyms: ascent, ascension, ascending · A kind of: motion · Kinds: levitation, heave, climb, soar

  3. An upward slope or grade (as in a road).

    “The car couldn't make it up the rise.”

    Synonyms: ascent, acclivity, raise, climb, upgrade · A kind of: slope · Kinds: uphill

  4. A movement upward.

    “They cheered the rise of the hot-air balloon.”

    Synonyms: rising, ascent, ascension · Opposite: fall · A kind of: change of location · Kinds: climb, elevation, heave, liftoff, rapid climb, takeoff, upheaval, uplifting

  5. The amount a salary is increased.

    “He got a 3% raise.” “He got a wage hike.”

    Synonyms: raise, wage hike, hike, wage increase, salary increase · A kind of: increase

  6. The property possessed by a slope or surface that rises.

    Synonyms: upgrade, rising slope · A kind of: grade

  7. A wave that lifts the surface of the water or ground.

    Synonyms: lift · A kind of: wave

  8. (theology) the origination of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost.

    “The emanation of the Holy Spirit.” “The rising of the Holy Ghost.” “The doctrine of the procession of the Holy Spirit from the Father and the Son.”

    Synonyms: emanation, procession · A kind of: origin

  9. An increase in cost.

    “They asked for a 10% rise in rates.”

    Synonyms: boost, hike, cost increase · A kind of: increase

  10. Increase in price or value.

    “The news caused a general advance on the stock market.”

    Synonyms: advance · A kind of: increase

Phrases and compounds with "rise"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rise up (verb) Come to the surface.
  • low-rise (adjective) Used of buildings of one or only a few stories and usually no elevator; low.
  • give rise (verb) Cause to happen, occur or exist.
  • high-rise (noun) Tower consisting of a multistoried building of offices or apartments.
  • rise to power (noun) The act of attaining or gaining access to a new office or right or position (especially the throne).
