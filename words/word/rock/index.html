Rock: meaning, synonyms and examples

Rock is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A lump or mass of hard consolidated mineral matter.

In Hindi
चट्टान; झुलाना
Synonyms
stone, rock candy, rock 'n' roll, rock'n'roll, rock-and-roll, rock and roll, rock music, careen, sway, tilt
Word family
rocky, rocker

Rock as a noun

  1. A lump or mass of hard consolidated mineral matter.

    “He threw a rock at me.”

    Synonyms: stone · A kind of: natural object · Kinds: achondrite, bedrock, boulder, calculus, chondrite, clastic rock, crystal, intrusion

  2. Material consisting of the aggregate of minerals like those making up the Earth's crust.

    “That mountain is solid rock.” “Stone is abundant in New England and there are many quarries.”

    Synonyms: stone · A kind of: material · Kinds: quartzite, road metal, sedimentary rock, sial, sima, metamorphic rock, gravel, caliche

  3. United States gynecologist and devout Catholic who conducted the first clinical trials of the oral contraceptive pill (1890-1984).

  4. (figurative) someone who is strong and stable and dependable.

    “He was her rock during the crisis.” “Thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church.”

    A kind of: good person

  5. Hard bright-colored stick candy (typically flavored with peppermint).

    Synonyms: rock candy · A kind of: candy

  6. A genre of popular music originating in the 1950s; a blend of black rhythm-and-blues with white country-and-western.

    “Rock is a generic term for the range of styles that evolved out of rock'n'roll.”

    Synonyms: rock 'n' roll, rock'n'roll, rock-and-roll, rock and roll, rock music · A kind of: popular music · Kinds: heavy metal, progressive rock

  7. Pitching dangerously to one side.

    Synonyms: careen, sway, tilt · A kind of: lurch

Rock as a verb

  1. Move back and forth or sideways.

    “The ship was rocking.” “The tall building swayed.” “She rocked back and forth on her feet.”

    Synonyms: sway, shake · A kind of: move back and forth · Kinds: roll, nutate, swag, totter

  2. Cause to move back and forth.

    “Rock the cradle.” “Rock the baby.” “The wind swayed the trees gently.”

    Synonyms: sway · A kind of: move

Phrases and compounds with "rock"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rock elm (noun) Tall widely distributed elm of eastern North America.
  • rock oil (noun) A dark oil consisting mainly of hydrocarbons.
  • john rock (noun) United States gynecologist and devout Catholic who conducted the first clinical trials of the oral contraceptive pill (1890-1984).
  • rock band (noun) A band of musicians who play rock'n'roll music.
  • rock bass (noun) A kind of sea bass.
  • rock cake (noun) A small cake with a hard surface said to resemble a rock.
  • rock crab (noun) Crab of eastern coast of North America.
  • rock dove (noun) Pale grey Eurasian pigeon having black-striped wings from which most domestic species are descended.
  • rock hind (noun) Found around rocky coasts or on reefs.
  • rock pink (noun) Pink-flowered perennial of rocky regions of western United States.
  • rock rose (noun) Any of numerous varieties of helianthemums having small rose-like yellow or white or reddish flowers.
  • rock salt (noun) Naturally occurring crystalline sodium chloride.
  • rock star (noun) A famous singer of rock music.
  • rock wool (noun) A light fibrous material used as an insulator.
