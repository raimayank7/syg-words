Roll: meaning, synonyms and examples

Roll is used as a verb (14 senses) and noun (14 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move by turning over or rotating.

In Hindi
लुढ़कना, बेलना; रोल
Synonyms
turn over, wheel, undulate, roll out, wind, wrap, twine, hustle, pluck, flap
Word family
roller, rolling

Roll as a verb

  1. Move by turning over or rotating.

    “The child rolled down the hill.” “Turn over on your left side.”

    Synonyms: turn over · A kind of: turn · Kinds: rim

  2. Move along on or as if on wheels or a wheeled vehicle.

    “The President's convoy rolled past the crowds.”

    Synonyms: wheel · A kind of: travel · Kinds: bowl, troll

  3. Occur in soft rounded shapes.

    “The hills rolled past.”

    Synonyms: undulate

  4. Flatten or spread with a roller.

    “Roll out the paper.”

    Synonyms: roll out · A kind of: flatten · Kinds: cog, mill

  5. Emit, produce, or utter with a deep prolonged reverberating sound.

    “The thunder rolled.” “Rolling drums.”

    A kind of: sound

  6. Arrange or or coil around.

    “Roll your hair around your finger.” “Twine the thread around the spool.” “She wrapped her arms around the child.”

    Synonyms: wind, wrap, twine · A kind of: move · Kinds: spool, reel, ball, clue, coil

  7. Begin operating or running.

    “The cameras were rolling.” “The presses are already rolling.”

    A kind of: function

  8. Shape by rolling.

    “Roll a cigarette.”

    A kind of: shape

  9. Execute a roll, in tumbling.

    “The gymnasts rolled and jumped.”

    A kind of: tumble

  10. Sell something to or obtain something from by energetic and especially underhanded activity.

    Synonyms: hustle, pluck · A kind of: steal

  11. Move in a wavy pattern or with a rising and falling motion.

    “The curtains undulated.” “The waves rolled towards the beach.”

    Synonyms: undulate, flap, wave · A kind of: move · Kinds: luff

  12. Move about aimlessly or without any destination, often in search of food or employment.

    “The gypsies roamed the woods.” “Roving vagabonds.” “The cattle roam across the prairie.”

    Synonyms: wander, swan, stray, tramp, roam, cast, ramble, rove · A kind of: travel · Kinds: maunder, gallivant

  13. Move, rock, or sway from side to side.

    “The ship rolled on the heavy seas.”

    A kind of: rock

  14. Cause to move by turning over or in a circular manner of as if on an axis.

    “She rolled the ball.” “They rolled their eyes at his words.”

    Synonyms: revolve · A kind of: move · Kinds: transit

Roll as a noun

  1. Rotary motion of an object around its own axis.

    “Wheels in axial rotation.”

    Synonyms: axial rotation, axial motion · A kind of: rotation · Kinds: wallow

  2. A list of names.

    “His name was struck off the rolls.”

    Synonyms: roster · A kind of: list · Kinds: batting order, muster roll, church roll, rota, waiting list

  3. A long heavy sea wave as it advances towards the shore.

    Synonyms: roller, rolling wave · A kind of: wave

  4. Photographic film rolled up inside a container to protect it from light.

    A kind of: film

  5. A round shape formed by a series of concentric circles (as formed by leaves or flower petals).

    Synonyms: coil, whorl, curl, curlicue, ringlet, gyre, scroll · A kind of: round shape · Kinds: corolla, calyx, verticil

  6. A roll of currency notes (often taken as the resources of a person or business etc.).

    “He shot his roll on a bob-tailed nag.”

    Synonyms: bankroll · A kind of: funds

  7. Small rounded bread either plain or sweet.

    Synonyms: bun · A kind of: bread · Kinds: tea bread, frankfurter bun, hamburger bun, brioche, crescent roll, hard roll, soft roll, kaiser roll

  8. A deep prolonged sound (as of thunder or large bells).

    Synonyms: peal, pealing, rolling · A kind of: sound

  9. The sound of a drum (especially a snare drum) beaten rapidly and continuously.

    Synonyms: paradiddle, drum roll · A kind of: sound

  10. A document that can be rolled up (as for storage).

    Synonyms: scroll · A kind of: manuscript

  11. Anything rolled up in cylindrical form.

    A kind of: cylinder · Kinds: bolt, rouleau, rouleau

  12. The act of throwing dice.

    Synonyms: cast · A kind of: throw · Kinds: natural

  13. Walking with a swaying gait.

    A kind of: gait

  14. A flight maneuver; aircraft rotates about its longitudinal axis without changing direction or losing altitude.

    A kind of: flight maneuver · Kinds: barrel roll, snap roll

Phrases and compounds with "roll"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • roll in (verb) Pour or flow in a steady stream.
  • roll up (verb) Form into a cylinder by rolling.
  • roll-on (noun) A dispenser of a liquid cosmetic (such as a deodorant) having a revolving ball as an applicator.
  • egg roll (noun) Minced vegetables and meat wrapped in a pancake and fried.
  • roll off (verb) Recite volubly or extravagantly.
  • roll out (verb) Flatten or spread with a roller.
  • drum roll (noun) The sound of a drum (especially a snare drum) beaten rapidly and continuously.
  • hard roll (noun) Yeast-raised roll with a hard crust.
  • rent-roll (noun) A register of rents; includes the names of tenants and the amount of rent they pay.
  • roll call (noun) Calling out an official list of names.
  • roll down (verb) Gather into a huge mass and roll down a mountain, of snow.
  • roll film (noun) Photographic film wound on a spool.
  • roll over (verb) Make a rolling motion or turn.
  • snap roll (noun) A fast roll.
  • soft roll (noun) Yeast-raised roll with a soft crust.
  • onion roll (noun) Yeast-raised roll flavored with onion.
