Root: meaning, synonyms and examples

Root is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: (botany) the usually underground organ that lacks buds or leaves or nodes; absorbs water and mineral salts; usually it anchors the plant to the ground.

In Hindi
जड़; मूल
Synonyms
beginning, origin, rootage, source, root word, base, stem, theme, radical, solution
Word family
rootlet, rooting, rootage

Root as a noun

  1. (botany) the usually underground organ that lacks buds or leaves or nodes; absorbs water and mineral salts; usually it anchors the plant to the ground.

    A kind of: plant organ · Kinds: calamus, taro, ginseng, horseradish, radish, chicory, salsify, briarroot

  2. The place where something begins, where it springs into being.

    “The Italian beginning of the Renaissance.” “Jupiter was the origin of the radiation.” “Pittsburgh is the source of the Ohio River.”

    Synonyms: beginning, origin, rootage, source · A kind of: point · Kinds: derivation, spring, fountainhead, headwater, wellhead, jumping-off place, birthplace, home

  3. (linguistics) the form of a word after all affixes are removed.

    “Thematic vowels are part of the stem.”

    Synonyms: root word, base, stem, theme, radical · A kind of: form

  4. A number that, when multiplied by itself some number of times, equals a given number.

    A kind of: number · Kinds: square root, cube root

  5. The set of values that give a true statement when substituted into an equation.

    Synonyms: solution · A kind of: set

  6. Someone from whom you are descended (but usually more remote than a grandparent).

    Synonyms: ancestor, ascendant, ascendent, antecedent · A kind of: relative · Kinds: ancestress, forebear, forefather, foremother, progenitor

  7. A simple form inferred as the common basis from which related words in several languages can be derived by linguistic processes.

    Synonyms: etymon · A kind of: form

  8. The part of a tooth that is embedded in the jaw and serves as support.

    Synonyms: tooth root · A kind of: structure

Root as a verb

  1. Take root and begin to grow.

    “This plant roots quickly.”

    A kind of: grow

  2. Come into existence, originate.

    “The problem roots in her depression.”

    A kind of: become

  3. Plant by the roots.

    A kind of: plant

  4. Dig with the snout.

    “The pig was rooting for truffles.”

    Synonyms: rout, rootle · A kind of: dig

  5. Become settled or established and stable in one's residence or life style.

    “He finally settled down.”

    Synonyms: settle, take root, steady down, settle down · A kind of: stabilize · Kinds: roost

  6. Cause to take roots.

    A kind of: grow

Phrases and compounds with "root"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • root on (verb) Spur on or encourage especially by cheers and shouts.
  • root cap (noun) Thimble-shaped mass of cells covering and protecting the growing tip of a root.
  • root for (verb) Take sides with; align oneself with; show strong sympathy for.
  • root out (verb) Pull up by or as if by the roots.
  • root rot (noun) Disease characterized by root decay; caused by various fungi.
  • ague root (noun) Colicroot having a scurfy or granuliferous perianth and white flowers; southeastern United States.
  • cube root (noun) A number that when multiplied three times equals a given number.
  • prop root (noun) A root that grows from and supports the stem above the ground in plants such as mangroves.
  • root crop (noun) Crop grown for its enlarged roots: e.g. beets; potatoes; turnips.
  • root hair (noun) Thin hairlike outgrowth of an epidermal cell just behind the tip; absorbs nutrients from the soil.
  • root word (noun) (linguistics) the form of a word after all affixes are removed.
  • rose-root (noun) Eurasian mountain plant with fleshy pink-tipped leaves and a cluster of yellow flowers.
  • take root (verb) Become settled or established and stable in one's residence or life style.
  • taro root (noun) Tropical starchy tuberous root.
  • tuba root (noun) Woody vine having bright green leaves and racemes of rose-tinted white flowers; the swollen roots contain rotenone.
  • colic root (noun) Any of several perennials of the genus Aletris having grasslike leaves and bitter roots reputed to cure colic.
