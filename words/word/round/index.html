Round: meaning, synonyms and examples

Round is used as an adjective (3 senses), noun (12 senses), verb (7 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Having a circular shape.

In Hindi
गोल; दौर
Synonyms
circular, orotund, rotund, pear-shaped, unit of ammunition, one shot, cycle, rhythm, beat, round of golf
Opposites
square
Word family
roundness, rounder

Round as an adjective

  1. Having a circular shape.

    Synonyms: circular · Opposite: square · Similar: apple-shaped, ball-shaped, barrel-shaped, bulblike, capitate, coccoid

  2. (of sounds) full and rich.

    “Orotund tones.” “The rotund and reverberating phrase.” “Pear-shaped vowels.”

    Synonyms: orotund, rotund, pear-shaped · Similar: full

  3. (mathematics) expressed to the nearest integer, ten, hundred, or thousand.

    “In round numbers.”

    Similar: inexact

Round as a noun

  1. A charge of ammunition for a single shot.

    Synonyms: unit of ammunition, one shot · A kind of: ammunition

  2. An interval during which a recurring sequence of events occurs.

    “The never-ending cycle of the seasons.”

    Synonyms: cycle, rhythm · A kind of: time interval

  3. A regular route for a sentry or policeman.

    “In the old days a policeman walked a beat and knew all his people by name.”

    Synonyms: beat · A kind of: path

  4. (often plural) a series of professional calls (usually in a set order).

    “The doctor goes on his rounds first thing every morning.” “The postman's rounds.” “We enjoyed our round of the local bars.”

    A kind of: call

  5. The activity of playing 18 holes of golf.

    “A round of golf takes about 4 hours.”

    Synonyms: round of golf · A kind of: golf

  6. The usual activities in your day.

    “The doctor made his rounds.”

    Synonyms: daily round · A kind of: habitude

  7. The course along which communications spread.

    “The story is going the rounds in Washington.”

    A kind of: path

  8. A cut of beef between the rump and the lower leg.

    A kind of: cut of beef

  9. A partsong in which voices follow each other; one voice starts and others join in one after another until all are singing different parts of the song at the same time.

    “They enjoyed singing rounds.”

    Synonyms: troll · A kind of: partsong

  10. An outburst of applause.

    “There was a round of applause.”

    A kind of: applause

  11. A crosspiece between the legs of a chair.

    Synonyms: rung, stave · A kind of: crosspiece

  12. Any circular or rotating mechanism.

    “The machine punched out metal circles.”

    Synonyms: circle · A kind of: rotating mechanism · Kinds: disk

Round as a verb

  1. Wind around; move along a circular course.

    “Round the bend.”

    A kind of: travel

  2. Make round.

    “Round the edges.”

    Synonyms: round out, round off · A kind of: shape · Kinds: purse

  3. Pronounce with rounded lips.

    Synonyms: labialize, labialise · A kind of: pronounce

  4. Attack in speech or writing.

    “The editors of the left-leaning paper attacked the new House Speaker.”

    Synonyms: attack, assail, lash out, snipe, assault · A kind of: knock · Kinds: abuse, claw, vitriol, rip, whang, bombard, blister, rubbish

  5. Bring to a highly developed, finished, or refined state.

    “Polish your social manners.”

    Synonyms: polish, round off, polish up, brush up · A kind of: perfect

  6. Express as a round number.

    “Round off the amount.”

    Synonyms: round off, round down, round out · A kind of: change

  7. Become round, plump, or shapely.

    “The young woman is fleshing out.”

    Synonyms: flesh out, fill out · A kind of: gain

Round as an adverb

  1. From beginning to end; throughout.

    “It rains all year round on Skye.” “Frigid weather the year around.”

    Synonyms: around

Phrases and compounds with "round"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • round up (verb) Seek out and bring together.
  • all-round (adjective) Many-sided.
  • ask round (verb) Invite someone to one's house.
  • round off (verb) Make round.
  • round out (verb) Fill out.
  • round top (noun) A canvas tent to house the audience at a circus performance.
  • round-arm (adjective) With the arm swung round at shoulder height.
  • top round (noun) Roast cut from the round; usually suitable for roasting.
  • come round (verb) Change one's position or opinion.
  • moon-round (adjective) Resembling the moon in shape.
  • pull round (verb) Continue in existence after (an adversity, etc.).
  • round arch (noun) An arch formed in a continuous curve; characteristic of Roman architecture.
  • round bone (noun) Bones that are round in shape.
  • round clam (noun) Atlantic coast round clams with hard shells; large clams usually used for chowders or other clam dishes.
  • round down (verb) Express as a round number.
  • round file (noun) A file with a circular cross section; used to file the inside of holes.
