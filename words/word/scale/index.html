Scale: meaning, synonyms and examples

Scale is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: An ordered reference standard.

In Hindi
पैमाना; तराज़ू
Synonyms
scale of measurement, graduated table, ordered series, scale leaf, scurf, exfoliation, musical scale, weighing machine, plate, shell
Word family
scalar, scalic, scaly, scaley, scalage, scaling

Scale as a noun

  1. An ordered reference standard.

    “Judging on a scale of 1 to 10.”

    Synonyms: scale of measurement, graduated table, ordered series · A kind of: standard · Kinds: index, logarithmic scale, moment magnitude scale, temperature scale, wage scale

  2. Relative magnitude.

    “They entertained on a grand scale.”

    A kind of: magnitude relation

  3. The ratio between the size of something and a representation of it.

    “The scale of the map.” “The scale of the model.”

    A kind of: proportion

  4. A specialized leaf or bract that protects a bud or catkin.

    Synonyms: scale leaf · A kind of: leaf · Kinds: squamule

  5. A thin flake of dead epidermis shed from the surface of the skin.

    Synonyms: scurf, exfoliation · A kind of: bit · Kinds: dander, dandruff

  6. (music) a series of notes differing in pitch according to a specific scheme (usually within an octave).

    Synonyms: musical scale · A kind of: musical notation · Kinds: gamut, diatonic scale, chromatic scale, gapped scale

  7. A measuring instrument for weighing; shows amount of mass.

    Synonyms: weighing machine · A kind of: measuring instrument · Kinds: balance, weighbridge

  8. An indicator having a graduated sequence of marks.

    A kind of: indicator · Kinds: vernier scale

  9. A metal sheathing of uniform thickness (such as the shield attached to an artillery piece to protect the gunners).

    Synonyms: plate, shell · A kind of: shield · Kinds: armor plate, horseshoe, shell plating

  10. A flattened rigid plate forming part of the body covering of many animals.

    A kind of: covering · Kinds: fish scale, squama

Scale as a verb

  1. Measure by or as if by a scale.

    “This bike scales only 25 pounds.”

    A kind of: measure

  2. Pattern, make, regulate, set, measure, or estimate according to some rate or standard.

    A kind of: model

  3. Take by attacking with scaling ladders.

    “The troops scaled the walls of the fort.”

    A kind of: take

  4. Reach the highest point of.

    “We scaled the Mont Blanc.”

    Synonyms: surmount · A kind of: reach

  5. Climb up by means of a ladder.

    A kind of: climb

  6. Remove the scales from.

    “Scale fish.”

    Synonyms: descale · A kind of: remove

  7. Measure with or as if with scales.

    “Scale the gold.”

    A kind of: quantify

  8. Size or measure according to a scale.

    “This model must be scaled down.”

    A kind of: size

  • wage scale (noun) A schedule of wages paid for different jobs.
  • wind scale (noun) An international scale of wind force from 0 (calm air) to 12 (hurricane).
