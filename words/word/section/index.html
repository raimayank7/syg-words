Section: meaning, synonyms and examples

Section is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A self-contained part of a larger composition (written or musical).

In Hindi
भाग, खंड
Synonyms
subdivision, segment, part, division, plane section, discussion section, department, incision, surgical incision

Section as a noun

  1. A self-contained part of a larger composition (written or musical).

    “He always turns first to the business section.” “The history of this work is discussed in the next section.”

    Synonyms: subdivision · A kind of: writing, music · Kinds: lead, canto, above, sports section, article, book, chapter, episode

  2. A very thin slice (of tissue or mineral or other substance) for examination under a microscope.

    “Sections from the left ventricle showed diseased tissue.”

    A kind of: slice

  3. A distinct region or subdivision of a territorial or political area or community or group of people.

    “No section of the nation is more ardent than the South.”

    A kind of: area · Kinds: outskirts, vicinity

  4. One of several parts or pieces that fit with others to constitute a whole object.

    “A section of a fishing rod.” “Metal sections were used below ground.” “Finished the final segment of the road.”

    Synonyms: segment · A kind of: part · Kinds: bend, dado, leaf, length, straightaway, subsection

  5. A small team of policemen working as part of a police platoon.

    A kind of: team

  6. One of the portions into which something is regarded as divided and which together constitute a whole.

    “The written part of the exam.” “The finance section of the company.” “The BBC's engineering division.”

    Synonyms: part, division · A kind of: concept · Kinds: frame, beginning, middle, end, high point, component, chukker, inning

  7. A land unit equal to 1 square mile.

    A kind of: square mile

  8. (geometry) the area created by a plane cutting through a solid.

    Synonyms: plane section · A kind of: area · Kinds: cross section

  9. A small class of students who are part of a larger course but are taught separately.

    “A graduate student taught sections for the professor's lecture course.”

    Synonyms: discussion section · A kind of: class

  10. A division of an orchestra containing all instruments of the same class.

    A kind of: musical organization · Kinds: brass section, string section, percussion section, reed section

  11. A small army unit usually having a special function.

    A kind of: army unit

  12. A specialized division of a large organization.

    “You'll find it in the hardware department.” “She got a job in the historical section of the Treasury.”

    Synonyms: department · A kind of: division · Kinds: academic department, business department, personnel department, government department, payroll, security

  13. A segment of a citrus fruit.

    “He ate a section of the orange.”

    A kind of: segment

  14. The cutting of or into body tissues or organs (especially by a surgeon as part of an operation).

    Synonyms: incision, surgical incision · A kind of: cut · Kinds: gastromy, perineotomy, laparotomy, orchotomy, nephrotomy, thoracotomy, valvotomy, venesection

Section as a verb

  1. Divide into segments.

    “Segment an orange.” “Segment a compound word.”

    Synonyms: segment · A kind of: separate · Kinds: syllabify, quarter

Phrases and compounds with "section"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • c-section (noun) The delivery of a fetus by surgical incision through the abdominal wall and uterus (from the belief that Julius Caesar was born that way).
  • section man (noun) Someone who teaches a section of a large college course.
  • reed section (noun) The section of a band or orchestra that plays reed instruments.
  • section gang (noun) A work crew assigned to a section of a railroad.
  • section hand (noun) A laborer assigned to a section gang.
  • brass section (noun) The section of a band or orchestra that plays brass instruments.
  • conic section (noun) (geometry) a curve generated by the intersection of a plane and a circular cone.
  • cross section (noun) A section created by a plane cutting a solid perpendicular to its longest axis.
  • cross-section (adjective) Representing a plane made by cutting across something at right angles to its length.
  • plane section (noun) (geometry) the area created by a plane cutting through a solid.
  • section eight (noun) A soldier who received a Section Eight discharge as unfit for military service.
  • golden section (noun) The proportional relation between two divisions of line or two dimension of a plane figure such that short : long :: long : (short + long).
  • rhythm section (noun) The section of a band or orchestra that plays percussion instruments.
  • sports section (noun) The section of a newspaper that reports on sports.
  • string section (noun) The section of an orchestra that plays stringed instruments.
  • violin section (noun) The section of an orchestra that plays violins.
