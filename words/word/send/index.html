Send: meaning, synonyms and examples

Send is used as a verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Cause to go somewhere.

In Hindi
भेजना
Synonyms
direct, send out, mail, post, transport, ship, station, place, get off, send off
Word family
sending, sender, sendee

Send as a verb

  1. Cause to go somewhere.

    “The explosion sent the car flying in the air.” “She sent her children to camp.” “He directed all his energies into his dissertation.”

    Synonyms: direct · A kind of: move · Kinds: project, turn, turn, divert, route, refer, redirect, blow

  2. To cause or order to be taken, directed, or transmitted to another place.

    “He had sent the dispatches downtown to the proper people and had slept.”

    Synonyms: send out · A kind of: transmit · Kinds: send in, mail out, mail

  3. Cause to be directed or transmitted to another place.

    “Send me your latest results.” “I'll mail you the paper when it's written.”

    Synonyms: mail, post · A kind of: transfer · Kinds: express, airmail, register, express-mail

  4. Transport commercially.

    Synonyms: transport, ship · A kind of: move · Kinds: barge, railroad, dispatch, forward

  5. Assign to a station.

    Synonyms: station, post, place · A kind of: move · Kinds: garrison, fort, locate

  6. Transfer.

    “The spy sent the classified information off to Russia.”

    Synonyms: get off, send off · A kind of: transfer

  7. Cause to be admitted; of persons to an institution.

    “After the second episode, she had to be committed.” “He was committed to prison.”

    Synonyms: commit, institutionalize, institutionalise, charge · A kind of: transfer · Kinds: hospitalize

  8. Broadcast over the airwaves, as in radio or television.

    “We cannot air this X-rated song.”

    Synonyms: air, broadcast, beam, transmit · A kind of: publicize · Kinds: satellite, sportscast, telecast, interrogate, rerun

Phrases and compounds with "send"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • send in (verb) Mail in; cause to be delivered.
  • send on (verb) Send or ship onward from an intermediate post or station in transit.
  • send for (verb) Order, request, or command to come.
  • send off (verb) Send away towards a designated goal.
  • send out (verb) To cause or order to be taken, directed, or transmitted to another place.
  • send-off (noun) An organized expression of goodwill at the start of a trip or new venture.
  • send away (verb) Stop associating with.
  • send back (verb) Refer (a matter or legal case) to another committee or authority or court for decision.
  • send down (verb) Suspend temporarily from college or university, in England.
  • send word (verb) Inform (somebody) of something.
  • send around (verb) Forward to others.
  • send packing (verb) Stop associating with.
  • send a message (verb) Give or constitute a signal, not necessarily verbally.
