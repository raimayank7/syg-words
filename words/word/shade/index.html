Shade: meaning, synonyms and examples

Shade is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Relative darkness caused by light rays being intercepted by an opaque body.

In Hindi
छाया; रंगत
Synonyms
shadiness, shadowiness, tint, tincture, tone, nuance, nicety, subtlety, refinement, tad
Word family
shady, shading

Shade as a noun

  1. Relative darkness caused by light rays being intercepted by an opaque body.

    “It is much cooler in the shade.” “There's too much shadiness to take good photographs.”

    Synonyms: shadiness, shadowiness · A kind of: semidarkness · Kinds: shadow

  2. A quality of a given color that differs slightly from another color.

    “After several trials he mixed the shade of pink that she wanted.”

    Synonyms: tint, tincture, tone · A kind of: color · Kinds: mellowness, richness, undertone

  3. Protective covering that protects something from direct sunlight.

    “They used umbrellas as shades.” “As the sun moved he readjusted the shade.”

    A kind of: protective covering · Kinds: lampshade, parasol, sun visor

  4. A subtle difference in meaning or opinion or attitude.

    “Without understanding the finer nuances you can't enjoy the humor.” “Don't argue about shades of meaning.”

    Synonyms: nuance, nicety, subtlety, refinement · A kind of: meaning

  5. A position of relative inferiority.

    “An achievement that puts everything else in the shade.” “His brother's success left him in the shade.”

    A kind of: inferiority

  6. A slight amount or degree of difference.

    “A tad too expensive.” “Not a tad of difference.” “The new model is a shade better than the old one.”

    Synonyms: tad · A kind of: small indefinite quantity

  7. A mental representation of some haunting experience.

    “He looked like he had seen a ghost.” “It aroused specters from his past.”

    Synonyms: ghost, spook, wraith, specter, spectre · A kind of: apparition

  8. A representation of the effect of shadows in a picture or drawing (as by shading or darker pigment).

    A kind of: representation

Shade as a verb

  1. Cast a shadow over.

    Synonyms: shadow, shade off · A kind of: darken

  2. Represent the effect of shade or shadow on.

    Synonyms: fill in · A kind of: draw, paint · Kinds: crosshatch

  3. Protect from light, heat, or view.

    “Shade your eyes when you step out into the bright sunlight.”

    A kind of: screen

  4. Vary slightly.

    “Shade the meaning.”

    A kind of: change

  5. Pass from one quality such as color to another by a slight degree.

    “The butterfly wings shade to yellow.”

    A kind of: change

Phrases and compounds with "shade"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • shade off (verb) Cast a shadow over.
  • lamp shade (noun) A protective ornamental shade used to screen a light bulb from direct view.
  • shade tree (noun) A tree planted or valued chiefly for its shade from sunlight.
  • window shade (noun) An opaque window blind that can cover or uncover a window.
