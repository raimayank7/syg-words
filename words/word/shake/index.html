Shake: meaning, synonyms and examples

Shake is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move or cause to move back and forth.

In Hindi
हिलाना
Synonyms
agitate, didder, judder, rock, sway, stimulate, shake up, excite, stir, shake off
Word family
shaker, shaking, shaky

Shake as a verb

  1. Move or cause to move back and forth.

    “The chemist shook the flask vigorously.” “My hands were shaking.”

    Synonyms: agitate · A kind of: move · Kinds: fluff up, fan, tremble, tremor, palpitate, convulse, sparge, succuss

  2. Move with or as if with a tremor.

    “His hands shook.”

    Synonyms: didder · A kind of: move involuntarily

  3. Shake or vibrate rapidly and intensively.

    “The old engine was juddering.”

    Synonyms: judder · A kind of: vibrate

  4. Move back and forth or sideways.

    “The ship was rocking.” “The tall building swayed.” “She rocked back and forth on her feet.”

    Synonyms: rock, sway · A kind of: move back and forth · Kinds: roll, nutate, swag, totter

  5. Undermine or cause to waver.

    “My faith has been shaken.” “The bad news shook her hopes.”

    A kind of: weaken

  6. Stir the feelings, emotions, or peace of.

    “These stories shook the community.” “The civil war shook the country.”

    Synonyms: stimulate, shake up, excite, stir · A kind of: arouse · Kinds: fuel, frighten, thrill, tempt, elate, inspire

  7. Get rid of.

    “I couldn't shake the car that was following me.”

    Synonyms: shake off, throw off, escape from · A kind of: escape

  8. Bring to a specified condition by or as if by shaking.

    “He was shaken from his dreams.” “Shake the salt out of the salt shaker.”

    A kind of: change

  9. Shake (a body part) to communicate a greeting, feeling, or cognitive state.

    “Shake one's head.” “She shook her finger at the naughty students.” “The old enemies shook hands.”

    A kind of: gesticulate

Shake as a noun

  1. Building material used as siding or roofing.

    Synonyms: shingle · A kind of: building material

  2. Frothy drink of milk and flavoring and sometimes fruit or ice cream.

    Synonyms: milkshake, milk shake · A kind of: drink · Kinds: eggshake, frappe, malted

  3. A note that alternates rapidly with another note a semitone above it.

    Synonyms: trill · A kind of: note

  4. Grasping and shaking a person's hand (as to acknowledge an introduction or to agree on a contract).

    Synonyms: handshake, handshaking, handclasp · A kind of: acknowledgment

  5. A reflex motion caused by cold or fear or excitement.

    Synonyms: tremble, shiver · A kind of: reflex

  6. Causing to move repeatedly from side to side.

    Synonyms: wag, waggle · A kind of: agitation

Phrases and compounds with "shake"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • shake up (verb) Shock physically.
  • shake-up (noun) The imposition of a new organization; organizing differently (often involving extensive and drastic changes).
  • shake off (verb) Get rid of.
  • milk shake (noun) Frothy drink of milk and flavoring and sometimes fruit or ice cream.
  • shake hands (verb) Take someone's hands and shake them as a gesture of greeting or congratulation.
