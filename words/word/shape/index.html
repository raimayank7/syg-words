Shape: meaning, synonyms and examples

Shape is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any spatial attributes (especially as defined by outline).

In Hindi
आकार
Synonyms
form, configuration, contour, conformation, human body, physical body, material body, soma, build, figure
Word family
shapely, shaping, shaper

Shape as a noun

  1. Any spatial attributes (especially as defined by outline).

    “He could barely make out their shapes.”

    Synonyms: form, configuration, contour, conformation · A kind of: spatial property · Kinds: sharpness, dullness, topography, lobularity, concavity, convexity, angularity, narrowing

  2. The spatial arrangement of something as distinct from its substance.

    “Geometry is the mathematical science of shape.”

    Synonyms: form · A kind of: attribute · Kinds: solid, plane, natural shape, flare, figure, line, angular shape, round shape

  3. Alternative names for the body of a human being.

    “Leonardo studied the human body.” “He has a strong physique.” “The spirit is willing but the flesh is weak.”

    Synonyms: human body, physical body, material body, soma, build, figure, physique, anatomy · A kind of: body · Kinds: person, juvenile body, adult body, male body, female body

  4. A concrete representation of an otherwise nebulous concept.

    “A circle was the embodiment of his concept of life.”

    Synonyms: embodiment · A kind of: concretism

  5. The visual appearance of something or someone.

    “The delicate cast of his features.”

    Synonyms: form, cast · A kind of: appearance

  6. The state of (good) health (especially in the phrases `in condition' or `in shape' or `out of condition' or `out of shape').

    Synonyms: condition · A kind of: good health · Kinds: fitness

  7. The supreme headquarters that advises NATO on military matters and oversees all aspects of the Allied Command Europe.

    A kind of: high command

  8. A perceptual structure.

    “The composition presents problems for students of musical form.” “A visual pattern must include not only objects but the spaces between them.”

    Synonyms: form, pattern · A kind of: structure · Kinds: fractal, gestalt, grid, kaleidoscope, mosaic, strand

Shape as a verb

  1. Shape or influence; give direction to.

    “Experience often determines ability.” “Mold public opinion.”

    Synonyms: determine, mold, influence, regulate · A kind of: cause · Kinds: dispose, indispose, miscreate, carry weight, decide, reshape, time, index

  2. Make something, usually for a specific function.

    “She molded the rice balls carefully.” “Form cylinders from the dough.” “Shape a figure.”

    Synonyms: form, work, mold, mould, forge · A kind of: create from raw material · Kinds: carve, swage, chip, layer, cut out, machine, grind, stamp

  3. Give shape or form to.

    “Shape the dough.” “Form the young child's character.”

    Synonyms: form · A kind of: change · Kinds: individuate, tie, terrace, fork, tabulate, dimension, roll, draw

Phrases and compounds with "shape"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • s-shape (noun) A double curve resembling the letter S.
  • shape up (verb) Develop in a positive way.
  • shape-up (noun) A way of hiring longshoremen by the day; applicants gather around a union boss who selects those to be hired.
  • bell shape (noun) The shape of a bell.
  • cone shape (noun) A shape whose base is a circle and whose sides taper up to a point.
  • disk shape (noun) The roundness of a 2-dimensional figure.
  • leaf shape (noun) Any of the various shape that leaves of plants can assume.
  • take shape (verb) Develop into a distinctive entity.
  • wave shape (noun) The shape of a wave illustrated graphically by plotting the values of the period quantity against time.
  • round shape (noun) A shape that is curved and without sharp angles.
  • wedge shape (noun) Any shape that is triangular in cross section.
  • change shape (verb) Assume a different shape or form.
  • convex shape (noun) A shape that curves or bulges outward.
  • curved shape (noun) The trace of a point whose direction of motion changes.
  • funnel shape (noun) A conical shape with a wider and a narrower opening at the two ends.
  • angular shape (noun) A shape having one or more sharp angles.
