Share: meaning, synonyms and examples

Share is used as a verb (5 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Have in common.

In Hindi
बाँटना; हिस्सा
Synonyms
partake, partake in, divvy up, portion out, apportion, deal, portion, part, percentage, parcel
Word family
sharing, sharer

Share as a verb

  1. Have in common.

    “Our children share a love of music.” “The two countries share a long border.”

    A kind of: overlap · Kinds: partake, osculate

  2. Use jointly or in common.

    A kind of: use · Kinds: double up, pool, communalize

  3. Have, give, or receive a share of.

    “We shared the cake.”

    Synonyms: partake, partake in · A kind of: get · Kinds: cut in

  4. Give out as one's portion or share.

    Synonyms: divvy up, portion out, apportion, deal · A kind of: distribute

  5. Communicate.

    “I'd like to share this idea with you.”

    A kind of: communicate

Share as a noun

  1. Assets belonging to or due to or contributed by an individual person or group.

    “He wanted his share in cash.”

    Synonyms: portion, part, percentage · A kind of: assets · Kinds: tranche, dispensation, dole, way, ration, allowance, slice, split

  2. Any of the equal portions into which the capital stock of a corporation is divided and ownership of which is evidenced by a stock certificate.

    “He bought 100 shares of IBM at the market price.”

    A kind of: stock certificate

  3. The allotment of some amount by dividing something.

    “Death gets more than its share of attention from theologians.”

    Synonyms: parcel, portion · A kind of: allotment

  4. The part played by a person in bringing about a result.

    “I am proud of my contribution in advancing the project.” “They all did their share of the work.”

    Synonyms: contribution, part · A kind of: attempt · Kinds: end

  5. A sharp steel wedge that cuts loose the top layer of soil.

    Synonyms: plowshare, ploughshare · A kind of: wedge

Phrases and compounds with "share"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • share-out (noun) A distribution in shares.
  • earning per share (noun) The portion of a company's profit allocated to each outstanding share of common stock.
