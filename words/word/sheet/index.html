Sheet: meaning, synonyms and examples

Sheet is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Any broad thin expanse or surface.

In Hindi
चादर; पन्ना
Synonyms
piece of paper, sheet of paper, bed sheet, plane, tabloid, rag, flat solid, tack, mainsheet, weather sheet
Word family
sheeting

Sheet as a noun

  1. Any broad thin expanse or surface.

    “A sheet of ice.”

    A kind of: expanse

  2. Paper used for writing or printing.

    Synonyms: piece of paper, sheet of paper · A kind of: paper · Kinds: slip, signature, leaf, tear sheet, foolscap, style sheet, worksheet, revenue stamp

  3. Bed linen consisting of a large rectangular piece of cotton or linen cloth; used in pairs.

    Synonyms: bed sheet · A kind of: bed linen · Kinds: fitted sheet

  4. (mathematics) an unbounded two-dimensional shape.

    “We will refer to the plane of the graph as the X-Y plane.” “Any line joining two points on a plane lies wholly on that plane.”

    Synonyms: plane · A kind of: shape · Kinds: facet plane, midplane, orbital plane, picture plane, tangent plane

  5. Newspaper with half-size pages.

    Synonyms: tabloid, rag · A kind of: newspaper

  6. A flat artifact that is thin relative to its length and width.

    Synonyms: flat solid · A kind of: artifact · Kinds: blackboard, blank, board, film, laminate, membrane, panel, plate

  7. (nautical) a line (rope or chain) that regulates the angle at which a sail is set in relation to the wind.

    Synonyms: tack, mainsheet, weather sheet, shroud · A kind of: line · Kinds: futtock shroud

  8. A large piece of fabric (usually canvas fabric) by means of which wind is used to propel a sailing vessel.

    Synonyms: sail, canvas, canvass · A kind of: piece of cloth · Kinds: balloon sail, crossjack, fore-and-aft sail, foresail, headsail, mainsail, main-topsail, press of sail

Sheet as a verb

  1. Come down as if in sheets.

    “The rain was sheeting down during the monsoon.”

    A kind of: pour

  2. Cover with a sheet, as if by wrapping.

    “Sheet the body.”

    A kind of: cover

Phrases and compounds with "sheet"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bed sheet (noun) Bed linen consisting of a large rectangular piece of cotton or linen cloth; used in pairs.
  • fly sheet (noun) Flap consisting of a piece of canvas that can be drawn back to provide entrance to a tent.
  • rap sheet (noun) The daily written record of events (as arrests) in a police station.
  • sheet web (noun) An irregular spider web woven in a single plane and looking like flattened hammocks.
  • tip sheet (noun) A publication containing the latest information or tips or predictions for a particular business or stock market information or horse racing results, etc.
  • dope sheet (noun) A racing publication giving information on horses and the outcomes of horse races.
  • dust sheet (noun) A large piece of cloth used to cover furniture that is not in use for a long period.
  • flow sheet (noun) A diagram of the sequence of operations in a computer program or an accounting system.
  • lead sheet (noun) A sheet containing the words and melody for a song (and some indication of harmony) written in simple form.
  • sheet bend (noun) A hitch used for temporarily tying a rope to the middle of another rope (or to an eye).
  • sheet iron (noun) Plate iron thinner than tank iron.
  • sheet pile (noun) A pile in a row of piles driven side by side to retain earth or prevent seepage.
  • tear sheet (noun) A sheet that can be easily torn out of a publication.
  • time sheet (noun) A record of the hours worked by employees.
  • sheet glass (noun) Glass formed into large thin sheets.
  • sheet metal (noun) Sheet of metal formed into a thin plate.
