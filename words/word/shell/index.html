Shell: meaning, synonyms and examples

Shell is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: Ammunition consisting of a cylindrical metal casing containing an explosive charge and a projectile; fired from a large gun.

In Hindi
खोल, छिलका; सीप
Synonyms
carapace, cuticle, shield, eggshell, racing shell, case, casing, plate, scale, blast
Word family
shelling, sheller

Shell as a noun

  1. Ammunition consisting of a cylindrical metal casing containing an explosive charge and a projectile; fired from a large gun.

    A kind of: ammunition · Kinds: artillery shell, shotgun shell, shrapnel, tank shell, whizbang

  2. The material that forms the hard outer covering of many animals.

    A kind of: animal material · Kinds: mother-of-pearl

  3. Hard outer covering or case of certain organisms such as arthropods and turtles.

    Synonyms: carapace, cuticle, shield · A kind of: scute · Kinds: cuticula

  4. The hard usually fibrous outer layer of some fruits especially nuts.

    A kind of: hull · Kinds: nutshell

  5. The exterior covering of a bird's egg.

    Synonyms: eggshell · A kind of: covering

  6. A rigid covering that envelops an object.

    “The satellite is covered with a smooth shell of ice.”

    A kind of: covering

  7. A very light narrow racing boat.

    Synonyms: racing shell · A kind of: racing boat · Kinds: racing skiff, scull

  8. The housing or outer covering of something.

    “The clock has a walnut case.”

    Synonyms: case, casing · A kind of: housing · Kinds: boot, gearbox, jacket

  9. A metal sheathing of uniform thickness (such as the shield attached to an artillery piece to protect the gunners).

    Synonyms: plate, scale · A kind of: shield · Kinds: armor plate, horseshoe, shell plating

  10. The hard largely calcareous covering of a mollusc or a brachiopod.

    A kind of: covering · Kinds: valve, valve, scallop shell, oyster shell, phragmocone, seashell, clamshell

Shell as a verb

  1. Use explosives on.

    “The enemy has been shelling us all day.”

    Synonyms: blast · Kinds: crump

  2. Create by using explosives.

    “Blast a passage through the mountain.”

    Synonyms: blast · A kind of: make

  3. Fall out of the pod or husk.

    “The corn shelled.”

    A kind of: emerge

  4. Hit the pitches of hard and regularly.

    “He shelled the pitcher for eight runs in the first inning.”

    A kind of: hit

  5. Look for and collect shells by the seashore.

    A kind of: gather

  6. Come out better in a competition, race, or conflict.

    “Agassi beat Becker in the tennis championship.” “We beat the competition.” “Harvard defeated Yale in the last football game.”

    Synonyms: beat, beat out, crush, trounce, vanquish · A kind of: get the better of · Kinds: outpoint, walk over, eliminate, worst, whomp, get the best, spread-eagle, get the jump

  7. Remove from its shell or outer covering.

    “Shell the legumes.” “Shell mussels.”

    A kind of: remove · Kinds: pod

  8. Remove the husks from.

    “Husk corn.”

    Synonyms: husk · A kind of: remove

Phrases and compounds with "shell"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • ark shell (noun) Marine bivalve mollusk having a heavy toothed shell with a deep boat-like inner surface.
  • ear-shell (noun) Any of various large edible marine gastropods of the genus Haliotis having an ear-shaped shell with pearly interior.
  • pie shell (noun) Pastry used to hold pie fillings.
  • shell out (verb) Administer or bestow, as in small portions.
  • lamp shell (noun) Marine animal with bivalve shell having a pair of arms bearing tentacles for capturing food; found worldwide.
  • moon shell (noun) Marine gastropods having smooth rounded shells that form short spires.
  • shell bean (noun) A bean plant grown primarily for its edible seed rather than its pod.
  • shell game (noun) A swindling sleight-of-hand game; victim guesses which of three things a pellet is under.
  • shell-less (adjective) Of animals or fruits that have no shell.
  • star shell (noun) An artillery shell containing an illuminant.
  • tank shell (noun) A shell fired by the cannon on a tank.
  • tusk shell (noun) Any of various seashore mollusks having a tapering tubular shell open at each end and a foot pointed like a spade for burrowing.
  • blank shell (noun) A cartridge containing an explosive charge but no bullet.
  • patty shell (noun) Shell of puff paste.
  • shell shock (noun) A mental disorder caused by stress of active warfare.
  • sugar shell (noun) A spoon for serving sugar; often made in the shape of a seashell.
