Shop: meaning, synonyms and examples

Shop is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: A mercantile establishment for the retail sale of goods or services.

In Hindi
दुकान
Synonyms
store, workshop, shop class, patronize, patronise, shop at, buy at, frequent, sponsor, browse
Word family
shopper, shopping

Shop as a noun

  1. A mercantile establishment for the retail sale of goods or services.

    “He bought it at a shop on Cape Cod.”

    Synonyms: store · A kind of: mercantile establishment · Kinds: bakery, barbershop, bazaar, bookshop, booth, boutique, building supply store, butcher shop

  2. Small workplace where handcrafts or manufacturing are done.

    Synonyms: workshop · A kind of: workplace · Kinds: bindery, bookbindery, brickyard, chop shop, dye-works, machine shop, pottery, print shop

  3. A course of instruction in a trade (as carpentry or electricity).

    “I built a birdhouse in shop.”

    Synonyms: shop class · A kind of: course

Shop as a verb

  1. Do one's shopping.

    “She goes shopping every Friday.”

    A kind of: obtain · Kinds: market

  2. Do one's shopping at; do business with; be a customer or client of.

    Synonyms: patronize, patronise, shop at, buy at, frequent, sponsor · A kind of: support

  3. Shop around; not necessarily buying.

    “I don't need help, I'm just browsing.”

    Synonyms: browse · A kind of: search · Kinds: comparison-shop, antique, window-shop

  4. Give away information about somebody.

    “He told on his classmate who had cheated on the exam.”

    Synonyms: denounce, tell on, betray, give away, rat, grass, shit, snitch · A kind of: inform · Kinds: sell out

Phrases and compounds with "shop"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • shop at (verb) Do one's shopping at; do business with; be a customer or client of.
  • hat shop (noun) Shop selling women's hats.
  • pet shop (noun) A shop where pet animals can be purchased.
  • shop boy (noun) A young male shop assistant.
  • chop shop (noun) A place where stolen cars are disassembled for their parts.
  • food shop (noun) A shop selling ready-to-eat food products.
  • gift shop (noun) A shop that sells miscellaneous articles appropriate as gifts.
  • junk shop (noun) A shop that sells cheap secondhand goods.
  • open shop (noun) A company whose workers are hired without regard to their membership in a labor union.
  • shoe shop (noun) A shop where shoes are sold.
  • shoe-shop (noun) A shop where shoes are sold.
  • shop bell (noun) A bell attached to the door of a small shop; warns the proprietor that a customer has entered the shop.
  • shop girl (noun) A young female shop assistant.
  • shop talk (noun) Talk about your business that only others in the same business can understand.
  • talk shop (verb) Discuss matters that are related to work.
  • tuck shop (noun) A candy store in Great Britain.
