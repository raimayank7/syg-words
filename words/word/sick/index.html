Sick: meaning, synonyms and examples

Sick is used as an adjective (7 senses), noun (1 sense) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Affected by an impairment of normal physical or mental function.

In Hindi
बीमार
Synonyms
ill, nauseated, nauseous, queasy, sickish, brainsick, crazy, demented, disturbed, mad
Word family
sickness

Sick as an adjective

  1. Affected by an impairment of normal physical or mental function.

    “Ill from the monotony of his suffering.”

    Synonyms: ill · Similar: afflicted, aguish, ailing, airsick, autistic, bedfast

  2. Feeling nausea; feeling about to vomit.

    Synonyms: nauseated, nauseous, queasy, sickish · Similar: ill

  3. Affected with madness or insanity.

    “A man who had gone mad.”

    Synonyms: brainsick, crazy, demented, disturbed, mad, unbalanced, unhinged · Similar: insane

  4. Having a strong distaste from surfeit.

    “Grew more and more disgusted.” “Fed up with their complaints.” “Sick of it all.”

    Synonyms: disgusted, fed up, sick of, tired of · Similar: displeased

  5. (of light) lacking in intensity or brightness; dim or feeble.

    “The pale light of a half moon.” “A pale sun.” “The late afternoon light coming through the el tracks fell in pale oblongs on the street.”

    Synonyms: pale, pallid, wan · Similar: weak

  6. Deeply affected by a strong feeling.

    “Sat completely still, sick with envy.” “She was sick with longing.”

    Similar: moved

  7. Shockingly repellent; inspiring horror.

    “Ghastly wounds.” “The grim task of burying the victims.” “Gruesome evidence of human sacrifice.”

    Synonyms: ghastly, grim, grisly, gruesome, macabre · Similar: alarming

Sick as a noun

  1. People who are sick.

    “They devote their lives to caring for the sick.”

    A kind of: people

Sick as a verb

  1. Eject the contents of the stomach through the mouth.

    “After drinking too much, the students vomited.” “He purged continuously.” “The patient regurgitated the food we gave him last night.”

    Synonyms: vomit, vomit up, purge, cast, cat, be sick, disgorge, regorge · A kind of: excrete

Phrases and compounds with "sick"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • be sick (verb) Eject the contents of the stomach through the mouth.
  • sick of (adjective) Having a strong distaste from surfeit.
  • air sick (adjective) Experiencing motion sickness.
  • sick bag (noun) A bag provided on an airplane for passengers who are suffering from airsickness and need to vomit.
  • sick pay (noun) Wages paid to an employee who is on sick leave.
  • milk-sick (adjective) Affected with or related to milk sickness.
  • sick call (noun) The daily military formation at which individuals report to the medical officer as sick.
  • sick joke (noun) A joke in bad taste.
  • sick list (noun) A list of those who are ill (e.g. on a warship or in a regiment etc).
  • sick-abed (adjective) Confined to bed (by illness).
  • sick berth (noun) (nautical) a room for the treatment of the sick or injured (as on a ship).
  • sick leave (noun) A leave of absence from work because of illness.
  • sick parade (noun) The daily military formation at which individuals report to the medical officer as sick.
  • sick person (noun) A person suffering from an illness.
  • sick benefit (noun) Money paid (by the government) to someone who is too ill to work.
  • sick headache (noun) A headache accompanied by nausea.
