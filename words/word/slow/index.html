Slow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Slow is used as an adjective (6 senses), verb (3 senses) and adverb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Not moving quickly; taking a comparatively long time.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites
fast
Word family
slowness, slowing

Slow as an adjective

  1. Not moving quickly; taking a comparatively long time.

    “A slow walker.” “The slow lane of traffic.” “Her steps were slow.”

    Opposite: fast · Similar: bumper-to-bumper, dilatory, drawn-out, lazy, long-play, slow-moving

  2. At a slow tempo.

    “The band played a slow waltz.”

    Opposite: fast · Similar: adagio, andante, lento, lentissimo, largo, larghetto

  3. Slow to learn or understand; lacking intellectual acuity.

    “So dense he never understands anything I say to him.” “Never met anyone quite so dim.” “Although dull at classical learning, at mathematics he was uncommonly quick.”

    Synonyms: dense, dim, dull, dumb, obtuse · Similar: stupid

  4. (used of timepieces) indicating a time earlier than the correct time.

    “The clock is slow.”

    Opposite: fast

  5. So lacking in interest as to cause mental weariness.

    “A boring evening with uninteresting people.” “The deadening effect of some routine tasks.” “A dull play.”

    Synonyms: boring, deadening, dull, ho-hum, irksome, tedious, tiresome, wearisome · Similar: uninteresting

  6. (of business) not active or brisk.

    “Business is dull (or slow).” “A sluggish market.”

    Synonyms: dull, sluggish · Similar: inactive

Slow as a verb

  1. Lose velocity; move more slowly.

    “The car decelerated.”

    Synonyms: decelerate, slow down, slow up, retard · A kind of: decrease · Kinds: slow, delay

  2. Become slow or slower.

    “Production slowed.”

    Synonyms: slow down, slow up, slack, slacken · A kind of: weaken

  3. Cause to proceed more slowly.

    “The illness slowed him down.”

    Synonyms: slow down, slow up · A kind of: decelerate · Kinds: bog down, clog

Slow as an adverb

  1. Without speed (`slow' is sometimes used informally for `slowly').

    “He spoke slowly.” “Go easy here--the road is slippery.” “Glaciers move tardily.”

    Synonyms: slowly, easy, tardily

  2. Of timepieces.

    “The clock is almost an hour slow.” “My watch is running behind.”

    Synonyms: behind

Phrases and compounds with "slow"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go-slow (noun) A form of protest by workers in which they deliberately slow down in order to cause problem from their employers.
  • slow up (verb) Become slow or slower.
  • slow down (verb) Lose velocity; move more slowly.
  • slow lane (noun) The traffic lane for vehicles that are moving slowly.
  • slow loris (noun) Stocky lemur of southeastern Asia.
  • slow match (noun) Match or fuse made to burn slowly and evenly.
  • slow virus (noun) A virus that remains dormant in the body for a long time before symptoms appear.
  • slow motion (noun) A movie that apparently takes place at a slower than normal speed; achieved by taking the film at a faster rate.
  • slow-moving (adjective) Moving slowly.
  • slow-witted (adjective) Retarded in intellectual development.
  • slow time scale (noun) (simulation) the time scale used in data processing when the time-scale factor is greater than one.
  • slow-wittedness (noun) The quality of being mentally slow and limited.
