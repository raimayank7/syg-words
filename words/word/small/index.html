Small: meaning, synonyms and examples

Small is used as an adjective (10 senses), noun (2 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Limited or below average in number or quantity or magnitude or extent.

In Hindi
छोटा
Synonyms
little, minor, modest, small-scale, pocket-size, pocket-sized, humble, low, lowly, minuscule
Opposites
large, big
Word family
smallness

Small as an adjective

  1. Limited or below average in number or quantity or magnitude or extent.

    “A little dining room.” “A little house.” “A small car.”

    Synonyms: little · Opposite: large · Similar: atomic, subatomic, bantam, bitty, dinky, dwarfish

  2. Limited in size or scope.

    “A small business.” “A newspaper with a modest circulation.” “Small-scale plans.”

    Synonyms: minor, modest, small-scale, pocket-size, pocket-sized · Similar: limited

  3. (of children and animals) young, immature.

    “What a big little boy you are.” “Small children.”

    Synonyms: little · Similar: young

  4. Slight or limited; especially in degree or intensity or scope.

    “A series of death struggles with small time in between.”

    Similar: little

  5. Low or inferior in station or quality.

    “A humble cottage.” “A lowly parish priest.” “A modest man of the people.”

    Synonyms: humble, low, lowly, modest · Similar: inferior

  6. Lowercase.

    “Little a.” “Small a.” “E.e.cummings's poetry is written all in minuscule letters.”

    Synonyms: little, minuscule · Similar: lowercase

  7. (of a voice) faint.

    “A little voice.” “A still small voice.”

    Synonyms: little · Similar: soft

  8. Have fine or very small constituent particles.

    “A small misty rain.”

    Similar: fine

  9. Not large but sufficient in size or amount.

    “A modest salary.” “Modest inflation.” “Helped in my own small way.”

    Synonyms: modest · Similar: moderate

  10. Made to seem smaller or less (especially in worth).

    “Her comments made me feel small.”

    Synonyms: belittled, diminished · Similar: decreased

Small as a noun

  1. The slender part of the back.

    A kind of: body part

  2. A garment size for a small person.

    A kind of: size

Small as an adverb

  1. On a small scale.

    “Think small.”

    Opposite: big

Phrases and compounds with "small"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • small cap (noun) A corporation with a small capitalization.
  • small fry (noun) Someone who is small and insignificant.
  • small-arm (noun) A portable gun.
  • small-cap (adjective) Of stocks of companies with a market capitalization of less than one billion dollars.
  • small boat (noun) A boat that is small.
  • small cane (noun) Small cane of watery or moist areas in southern United States.
  • small ship (noun) A ship that is small.
  • small slam (noun) Winning all but one of the tricks in a hand of bridge.
  • small talk (noun) Light informal conversation for social occasions.
  • small town (noun) A community of people smaller than a town.
  • small-time (adjective) Of minor importance.
  • small civet (noun) A common civet of southeast Asia.
  • small hours (noun) The hours just after midnight.
  • small print (noun) The part of a contract that contains reservations and qualifications that are often printed in small type.
  • small stuff (noun) Any light rope used on shipboard.
  • small voice (noun) An inner voice that judges your behavior.
