Smart: meaning, synonyms and examples

Smart is used as an adjective (7 senses), verb (1 sense) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Showing mental alertness and calculation and resourcefulness.

In Hindi
होशियार, चतुर
Synonyms
chic, voguish, bright, fresh, impertinent, impudent, overbold, saucy, sassy, wise
Opposites
stupid
Word family
smartness, smarting

Smart as an adjective

  1. Showing mental alertness and calculation and resourcefulness.

    Opposite: stupid · Similar: astute, cagey, streetwise

  2. Elegant and stylish.

    “Chic elegance.” “A smart new dress.” “A suit of voguish cut.”

    Synonyms: chic, voguish · Similar: stylish

  3. Characterized by quickness and ease in learning.

    “Some children are brighter in one subject than another.” “Smart children talk earlier than the average.”

    Synonyms: bright · Similar: intelligent

  4. Improperly forward or bold.

    “Don't be fresh with me.” “Impertinent of a child to lecture a grownup.” “An impudent boy given to insulting strangers.”

    Synonyms: fresh, impertinent, impudent, overbold, saucy, sassy, wise · Similar: forward

  5. Painfully severe.

    “He gave the dog a smart blow.”

    Similar: intense

  6. Quick and brisk.

    “I gave him a smart salute.” “We walked at a smart pace.”

    Similar: fast

  7. Capable of independent and apparently intelligent action.

    “Smart weapons.”

    Similar: automatic

Smart as a verb

  1. Be the source of pain.

    Synonyms: ache, hurt · A kind of: cause to be perceived · Kinds: bite, burn, itch, hunger, thirst, act up, throb, shoot

Smart as a noun

  1. A kind of pain such as that caused by a wound or a burn or a sore.

    Synonyms: smarting, smartness · A kind of: pain

Phrases and compounds with "smart"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • smart set (noun) The fashionable elite.
  • smart card (noun) A plastic card containing a microprocessor that enables the holder to perform operations requiring data that is stored in the microprocessor; typically used to perform financial transactions.
  • right smart (adjective) (Southern or Midland) considerable.
  • smart aleck (noun) An upstart who makes conceited, sardonic, insolent comments.
  • street smart (adjective) Having the shrewd resourcefulness needed to survive in an urban environment.
  • smart as a whip (adjective) Having or marked by unusual and impressive intelligence.
