Snake: meaning, synonyms and examples

Snake is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Limbless scaly elongate reptile; some are venomous.

In Hindi
साँप
Synonyms
serpent, ophidian, snake in the grass
Word family
snaky

Snake as a noun

  1. Limbless scaly elongate reptile; some are venomous.

    Synonyms: serpent, ophidian · A kind of: diapsid · Kinds: colubrid snake, blind snake, elapid, sea snake, viper

  2. A deceitful or treacherous person.

    Synonyms: snake in the grass · A kind of: bad person

  3. A tributary of the Columbia River that rises in Wyoming and flows westward; discovered in 1805 by the Lewis and Clark Expedition.

  4. A long faint constellation in the southern hemisphere near the equator stretching between Virgo and Cancer.

  5. Something long, thin, and flexible that resembles a snake.

    A kind of: object · Kinds: closet auger, plumber's snake, trap-and-drain auger

Snake as a verb

  1. Move smoothly and sinuously, like a snake.

    A kind of: glide

  2. Form a snake-like pattern.

    “The river snakes through the valley.”

    A kind of: wind

  3. Move along a winding path.

    “The army snaked through the jungle.”

    A kind of: weave

Phrases and compounds with "snake"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • rat snake (noun) Any of various nonvenomous rodent-eating snakes of North America and Asia.
  • sea snake (noun) Any of numerous venomous aquatic viviparous snakes having a fin-like tail; of warm littoral seas; feed on fish which they immobilize with quick-acting venom.
  • snake god (noun) A god of voodoo cults of African origin worshipped especially in West Indies.
  • snake oil (noun) (medicine) any of various liquids sold as medicine (as by a travelling medicine show) but medically worthless.
  • snake pit (noun) Any place of pain and turmoil.
  • bull snake (noun) Any of several large harmless rodent-eating North American burrowing snakes.
  • bull-snake (noun) Any of several large harmless rodent-eating North American burrowing snakes.
  • corn snake (noun) Large harmless snake of southeastern United States; often on farms.
  • hoop snake (noun) Any of various harmless North American snakes that were formerly believed to take tail in mouth and roll along like a hoop.
  • king snake (noun) Any of numerous nonvenomous North American constrictors; feed on other snakes and small mammals.
  • lyre snake (noun) Mildly venomous snake with a lyre-shaped mark on the head; found in rocky areas from southwestern United States to Central America.
  • milk snake (noun) Nonvenomous tan and brown king snake with an arrow-shaped occipital spot; southeastern ones have red stripes like coral snakes.
  • pine snake (noun) Any of several bull snakes of eastern and southeastern United States found chiefly in pine woods; now threatened.
  • ring snake (noun) Harmless European snake with a bright yellow collar; common in England.
  • rock snake (noun) Very large python of tropical and southern Africa.
  • sand snake (noun) Small North American burrowing snake.
