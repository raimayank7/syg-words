Social: meaning, synonyms and examples

Social is used as an adjective (6 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Relating to human society and its members.

In Hindi
सामाजिक
Synonyms
societal, sociable, mixer
Opposites
unsocial
Word family
sociality

Social as an adjective

  1. Relating to human society and its members.

    “Social institutions.” “Societal evolution.” “Societal forces.”

    Synonyms: societal

  2. Living together or enjoying life in communities or organized groups.

    “A human being is a social animal.” “Mature social behavior.”

    Opposite: unsocial · Similar: cultural, gregarious, interpersonal, multiethnic

  3. Relating to or belonging to or characteristic of high society.

    “Made fun of her being so social and high-toned.” “A social gossip colum.” “The society page.”

  4. Composed of sociable people or formed for the purpose of sociability.

    “A purely social club.” “The church has a large social hall.” “A social director.”

    Similar: sociable

  5. Tending to move or live together in groups or colonies of the same kind.

    “Ants are social insects.”

    Similar: gregarious

  6. Marked by friendly companionship with others.

    “A social cup of coffee.”

    Similar: friendly

Social as a noun

  1. A party of people assembled to promote sociability and communal activity.

    Synonyms: sociable, mixer · A kind of: party

Phrases and compounds with "social"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • social club (noun) A formal association of people with similar interests.
  • social lion (noun) A celebrity who is lionized (much sought after).
  • social rank (noun) Position in a social hierarchy.
  • social unit (noun) An organization regarded as part of a larger social group.
  • social work (noun) Any of various services designed to aid the poor and aged and to increase the welfare of children.
  • social class (noun) People having the same social, economic, or educational status.
  • social event (noun) An event characteristic of persons forming groups.
  • social group (noun) People sharing some social relation.
  • social action (noun) A social policy of reform (especially socioeconomic reform).
  • social affair (noun) A gathering for the purpose of promoting fellowship.
  • social dancer (noun) A person who participates in a social gathering arranged for dancing (as a ball).
  • social insect (noun) An insect that lives in a colony with other insects of the same species.
  • social phobia (noun) Any phobia (other than agoraphobia) associated with situations in which you are subject to criticism by others (as fear of eating in public or public speaking etc).
  • social policy (noun) A policy of for dealing with social issues.
  • social season (noun) The season for major social events.
  • social status (noun) Position in a social hierarchy.
