Soil: meaning, synonyms and examples

Soil is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: The state of being covered with unclean things.

In Hindi
मिट्टी
Synonyms
dirt, filth, grime, stain, grease, grunge, land, ground, territory, dirty
Word family
soilure, soiling

Soil as a noun

  1. The state of being covered with unclean things.

    Synonyms: dirt, filth, grime, stain, grease, grunge · A kind of: dirtiness

  2. The part of the earth's surface consisting of humus and disintegrated rock.

    Synonyms: dirt · A kind of: earth · Kinds: marl, regosol, residual soil, topsoil, subsoil, alluvial soil, bog soil, clay

  3. Material in the top layer of the surface of the earth in which plants can grow (especially with reference to its quality or use).

    “The land had never been plowed.” “Good agricultural soil.”

    Synonyms: land, ground · A kind of: object · Kinds: badlands, bottomland, coastland, cultivated land, overburden, permafrost, polder, rangeland

  4. The geographical area under the jurisdiction of a sovereign state.

    “American troops were stationed on Japanese soil.”

    Synonyms: territory · A kind of: geographical area

Soil as a verb

  1. Make soiled, filthy, or dirty.

    “Don't soil your clothes when you play outside!”

    Synonyms: dirty, begrime, grime, colly, bemire · A kind of: change · Kinds: foul, pollute, smear, slime, muddy, splash, mire, crock

Phrases and compounds with "soil"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bog soil (noun) Poorly drained soils on top of peat and under marsh or swamp vegetation.
  • leaf soil (noun) Soil composed mainly of decaying leaves.
  • soil bank (noun) Land retired from crop cultivation and planted with soil-building crops; government subsidies are paid to farmers for their retired land.
  • soil pipe (noun) Drain that conveys liquid waste from toilets, etc.
  • gumbo soil (noun) Any of various fine-grained silty soils that become waxy and very sticky mud when saturated with water.
  • night soil (noun) Human excreta used as fertilizer.
  • regur soil (noun) A rich black loam of India.
  • desert soil (noun) A type of soil that develops in arid climates.
  • gilgai soil (noun) Soil in the melon holes of Australia.
  • podsol soil (noun) A soil that develops in temperate to cold moist climates under coniferous or heath vegetation; an organic mat over a grey leached layer.
  • podzol soil (noun) A soil that develops in temperate to cold moist climates under coniferous or heath vegetation; an organic mat over a grey leached layer.
  • tundra soil (noun) A black mucky soil with a frozen subsoil that is characteristic of Arctic and subarctic regions.
  • prairie soil (noun) A type of soil occurring under grasses in temperate climates.
  • soil erosion (noun) The washing away of soil by the flow of water.
  • soil horizon (noun) A layer in a soil profile.
  • soil profile (noun) A vertical section of soil from the ground surface to the parent rock.
