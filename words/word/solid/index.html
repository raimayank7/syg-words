Solid: meaning, synonyms and examples

Solid is used as an adjective (14 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Characterized by good substantial quality.

In Hindi
ठोस
Synonyms
hearty, satisfying, square, substantial, strong, firm, upstanding, self-colored, self-coloured, unanimous
Opposites
liquid, gaseous, hollow
Word family
solidness, solidity, solidify

Solid as an adjective

  1. Characterized by good substantial quality.

    “Solid comfort.” “A solid base hit.”

    Similar: good

  2. Of definite shape and volume; firm; neither liquid nor gaseous.

    “Ice is water in the solid state.”

    Opposite: liquid, gaseous · Similar: coagulated, concrete, congealed, dry, semisolid, solid-state

  3. Entirely of one substance with no holes inside.

    “A solid block of wood.”

    Opposite: hollow · Similar: massive

  4. Of one substance or character throughout.

    “Solid gold.” “Carved out of solid rock.”

    Similar: homogeneous

  5. Uninterrupted in space; having no gaps or breaks.

    “A solid line across the page.” “Solid sheets of water.”

    Similar: unbroken

  6. Providing abundant nourishment.

    “A hearty meal.” “Good solid food.” “Ate a substantial breakfast.”

    Synonyms: hearty, satisfying, square, substantial · Similar: wholesome

  7. Of good quality and condition; solidly built.

    “A solid foundation.” “Several substantial timber buildings.”

    Synonyms: strong, substantial · Similar: sound

  8. Not soft or yielding to pressure.

    “A firm mattress.” “The snow was firm underfoot.” “Solid ground.”

    Synonyms: firm · Similar: hard

  9. Having three dimensions.

    “A solid object.”

    Similar: cubic

  10. Impenetrable for the eye.

    “Solid blackness.”

    Similar: opaque

  11. Financially sound.

    “The bank is solid and will survive this attack.”

    Similar: sound

  12. Of a substantial character and not frivolous or superficial.

    “Work of solid scholarship.” “Based on solid facts.”

    Similar: serious

  13. Meriting respect or esteem.

    “An upstanding member of the community.”

    Synonyms: upstanding · Similar: respectable

  14. Of the same color throughout.

    “Solid color.”

    Synonyms: self-colored, self-coloured · Similar: homogeneous

Solid as a noun

  1. Matter that is solid at room temperature and pressure.

    A kind of: matter · Kinds: food, precipitate, plastic, glass, crystal, powder, dry ice, vitrification

  2. The state in which a substance has no tendency to flow under moderate stress; resists forces (such as compression) that tend to deform it; and retains a definite size and shape.

    Synonyms: solidness, solid state · A kind of: state of matter

  3. A three-dimensional shape.

    A kind of: shape · Kinds: convex shape, concave shape, cylinder, polyhedron, toroid, cast, block, ovoid

Phrases and compounds with "solid"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • flat solid (noun) A flat artifact that is thin relative to its length and width.
  • solid food (noun) Any solid substance (as opposed to liquid) that is used as a source of nourishment.
  • ideal solid (noun) Any one of five solids whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose polyhedral angles are all congruent.
  • solid angle (noun) An angle formed by three or more planes intersecting at a common point (the vertex).
  • solid state (noun) The state in which a substance has no tendency to flow under moderate stress; resists forces (such as compression) that tend to deform it; and retains a definite size and shape.
  • solid-state (adjective) Characteristic of or relating to the physical properties of solid materials especially electromagnetic or thermodynamic or structural properties of crystalline solids.
  • solid figure (noun) A three-dimensional shape.
  • solid ground (noun) The solid part of the earth's surface.
  • solid-hoofed (adjective) Having solid hooves.
  • solid-colored (adjective) Having the same color all over.
  • platonic solid (noun) Any one of five solids whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose polyhedral angles are all congruent.
  • solid geometry (noun) The geometry of 3-dimensional space.
  • solid solution (noun) A homogeneous solid that can exist over a range of component chemicals; a constituent of alloys that is formed when atoms of an element are incorporated into the crystals of a metal.
  • solid-coloured (adjective) Having the same color all over.
  • solid-state physics (noun) The branch of physics that studies the properties of materials in the solid state: electrical conduction in crystals of semiconductors and metals; superconductivity; photoconductivity.
  • regular convex solid (noun) Any one of five solids whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose polyhedral angles are all congruent.
