Spare: meaning, synonyms and examples

Spare is used as a verb (4 senses), adjective (6 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Refrain from harming.

In Hindi
अतिरिक्त; बख़्श देना
Synonyms
save, give up, part with, dispense with, trim, excess, extra, redundant, supererogatory, superfluous
Word family
sparer, spareness

Spare as a verb

  1. Refrain from harming.

    Synonyms: save · A kind of: refrain · Kinds: favor

  2. Save or relieve from an experience or action.

    “I'll spare you from having to apologize formally.”

    A kind of: exempt

  3. Give up what is not strictly needed.

    “He asked if they could spare one of their horses to speed his journey.”

    Synonyms: give up, part with, dispense with · A kind of: give

  4. Use frugally or carefully.

    A kind of: use

Spare as an adjective

  1. Thin and fit.

    “The spare figure of a marathon runner.” “A body kept trim by exercise.”

    Synonyms: trim · Similar: thin

  2. More than is needed, desired, or required.

    “Trying to lose excess weight.” “Found some extra change lying on the dresser.” “Yet another book on heraldry might be thought redundant.”

    Synonyms: excess, extra, redundant, supererogatory, superfluous, supernumerary, surplus · Similar: unnecessary

  3. Not taken up by scheduled activities.

    “A free hour between classes.” “Spare time on my hands.”

    Synonyms: free · Similar: unoccupied

  4. Kept in reserve especially for emergency use.

    “A reserve supply of food.” “A spare tire.” “Spare parts.”

    Similar: unnecessary

  5. Lacking in amplitude or quantity.

    “A bare livelihood.” “A scanty harvest.” “A spare diet.”

    Synonyms: bare, scanty · Similar: meager

  6. Lacking embellishment or ornamentation.

    “A plain hair style.” “Unembellished white walls.” “Functional architecture featuring stark unornamented concrete.”

    Synonyms: plain, bare, unembellished, unornamented · Similar: unadorned

Spare as a noun

  1. An extra component of a machine or other apparatus.

    Synonyms: spare part · A kind of: component

  2. An extra car wheel and tire for a four-wheel vehicle.

    Synonyms: fifth wheel · A kind of: car wheel

  3. A score in tenpins; knocking down all ten after rolling two balls.

    A kind of: score

Phrases and compounds with "spare"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • spare part (noun) An extra component of a machine or other apparatus.
  • spare time (noun) Time available for hobbies and other activities that you enjoy.
  • spare tire (noun) Excess fat around the waistline.
  • spare-time activity (noun) An auxiliary activity.
