Special: meaning, synonyms and examples

Special is used as an adjective (7 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Unique or specific to a person or thing or category.

In Hindi
ख़ास, विशेष
Synonyms
particular, peculiar, especial, exceptional, limited, extra
Word family
specialness, speciality

Special as an adjective

  1. Unique or specific to a person or thing or category.

    “The particular demands of the job.” “Has a particular preference for Chinese art.” “A peculiar bond of sympathy between them.”

    Synonyms: particular, peculiar · Similar: specific

  2. For a special service or occasion.

    “A special correspondent.” “A special adviser to the committee.” “Had to get special permission for the event.”

    Similar: extraordinary

  3. Surpassing what is common or usual or expected.

    “He paid especial attention to her.” “Exceptional kindness.” “A matter of particular and unusual importance.”

    Synonyms: especial, exceptional, particular · Similar: uncommon

  4. Adapted to or reserved for a particular purpose.

    “A special kind of paint.” “A special medication for arthritis.”

    Similar: specialized

  5. Having a specific function or scope.

    “A special (or specific) role in the mission.”

    Synonyms: limited · Similar: specific

  6. First and most important.

    “His special interest is music.” “She gets special (or particular) satisfaction from her volunteer work.”

    Synonyms: particular · Similar: primary

  7. Added to a regular schedule.

    “A special holiday flight.” “Put on special buses for the big game.”

    Synonyms: extra · Similar: unscheduled

Special as a noun

  1. A special offering (usually temporary and at a reduced price) that is featured in advertising.

    “They are having a special on pork chops.”

    A kind of: offer

  2. A dish or meal given prominence in e.g. a restaurant.

    A kind of: dish

  3. A television production that features a particular person or work or topic.

    “The last of a series of BBC specials on Iran is being shown tonight.”

    A kind of: television program

Phrases and compounds with "special"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • special k (noun) Street names for ketamine.
  • special act (noun) A legislative act that applies only to a particular person or particular district.
  • special jury (noun) A jury whose members are selected for special knowledge for a case involving complicated issues.
  • special agent (noun) Someone whose authority is limited to the special undertaking they have been instructed to perform.
  • special branch (noun) A government police department dealing with political security.
  • special effect (noun) An effect used to produce scenes that cannot be achieved by normal techniques (especially on film).
  • special forces (noun) A division of the United States Army that is specially trained for guerilla fighting.
  • special session (noun) A session that is held in addition to the regular sessions.
  • special verdict (noun) A verdict rendered on certain specific factual issues posed by the court without finding for one party or the other.
  • special contract (noun) A contract that is signed and has the (wax) seal of the signer attached.
  • special delivery (noun) Mail that is delivered by a special carrier (for an additional charge).
  • special interest (noun) An individual or group who are concerned with some particular part of the economy and who try to influence legislators or bureaucrats to act in their favor.
  • special olympics (noun) An athletic contest modeled after the Olympic Games but intended for mentally or physically handicapped persons.
  • special pleading (noun) An argument that ignores all unfavorable evidence.
  • special education (noun) Education of physically or mentally handicapped children whose needs cannot be met in an ordinary classroom.
  • special assessment (noun) An additional tax levied on private property for public improvements that enhance the value of the property.
