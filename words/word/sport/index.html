Sport: meaning, synonyms and examples

Sport is used as a noun (7 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An active diversion requiring physical exertion and competition.

In Hindi
खेल
Synonyms
athletics, summercater, sportsman, sportswoman, mutant, mutation, variation, fun, play, feature

Sport as a noun

  1. An active diversion requiring physical exertion and competition.

    Synonyms: athletics · A kind of: diversion · Kinds: funambulism, rock climbing, contact sport, outdoor sport, gymnastics, track and field, skiing, water sport

  2. The occupation of athletes who compete for pay.

    A kind of: occupation · Kinds: professional boxing, professional wrestling, sumo, professional golf, professional football, professional baseball, professional basketball, professional tennis

  3. (Maine colloquial) a temporary summer resident of Maine.

    Synonyms: summercater · A kind of: vacationer

  4. A person known for the way she (or he) behaves when teased or defeated or subjected to trying circumstances.

    “A good sport.” “A poor sport.”

    A kind of: person

  5. Someone who engages in sports.

    Synonyms: sportsman, sportswoman · A kind of: athlete

  6. (biology) an organism that has characteristics resulting from chromosomal alteration.

    Synonyms: mutant, mutation, variation · A kind of: organism · Kinds: freak

  7. Verbal wit or mockery (often at another's expense but not to be taken seriously).

    “He became a figure of fun.” “He said it in sport.”

    Synonyms: fun, play · A kind of: wit · Kinds: jocosity, waggery, drollery, pun

Sport as a verb

  1. Wear or display in an ostentatious or proud manner.

    “She was sporting a new hat.”

    Synonyms: feature, boast · A kind of: have

  2. Play boisterously.

    “The children frolicked in the garden.” “The gamboling lambs in the meadows.” “The toddlers romped in the playroom.”

    Synonyms: frolic, lark, rollick, skylark, disport, cavort, gambol, frisk · A kind of: play
