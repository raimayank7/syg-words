Spot: meaning, synonyms and examples

Spot is used as a noun (14 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: A point located with respect to surface features of some region.

In Hindi
धब्बा; जगह; देख लेना
Synonyms
topographic point, place, point, smudge, blot, daub, smear, smirch, slur, speckle
Word family
spotter, spotting

Spot as a noun

  1. A point located with respect to surface features of some region.

    “This is a nice place for a picnic.” “A bright spot on a planet.”

    Synonyms: topographic point, place · A kind of: point · Kinds: junction, birthplace, polling place, end, hiding place, high, hole-in-the-wall, holy place

  2. A short section or illustration (as between radio or tv programs or in a magazine) that is often used for advertising.

    A kind of: section

  3. An outstanding characteristic.

    “His acting was one of the high points of the movie.”

    Synonyms: point · A kind of: characteristic

  4. A blemish made by dirt.

    “He had a smudge on his cheek.”

    Synonyms: smudge, blot, daub, smear, smirch, slur · A kind of: blemish · Kinds: blotch, fingermark, inkblot

  5. A small contrasting part of something.

    “A bald spot.” “A leopard's spots.” “A patch of clouds.”

    Synonyms: speckle, dapple, patch, fleck, maculation · A kind of: marking · Kinds: speck, nebula, splash, worn spot, plaque, macule, parhelion, sunspot

  6. A section of an entertainment that is assigned to a specific performer or performance.

    “They changed his spot on the program.”

    A kind of: section

  7. A business establishment for entertainment.

    “Night spot.”

    A kind of: place of business · Kinds: cabaret, clip joint, hot spot, joint

  8. A job in an organization.

    “He occupied a post in the treasury.”

    Synonyms: position, post, berth, office, billet, place, situation · A kind of: occupation · Kinds: academicianship, accountantship, admiralty, ambassadorship, apostleship, apprenticeship, associateship, attorneyship

  9. A slight attack of illness.

    “He has a touch of rheumatism.”

    Synonyms: touch · A kind of: attack

  10. A small piece or quantity of something.

    “A spot of tea.” “A bit of paper.” “A bit of lint.”

    Synonyms: bit · A kind of: small indefinite quantity

  11. A mark on a die or on a playing card (shape depending on the suit).

    Synonyms: pip · A kind of: marker

  12. A lamp that produces a strong beam of light to illuminate a restricted area; used to focus attention of a stage performer.

    Synonyms: spotlight · A kind of: lamp

  13. A playing card with a specified number of pips on it to indicate its value.

    “An eight-spot.”

    A kind of: playing card · Kinds: eight-spot, five-spot, four-spot, nine-spot, seven-spot, six-spot, ten-spot

  14. An act that brings discredit to the person who does it.

    “He made a huge blot on his copybook.”

    Synonyms: blot, smear, smirch, stain · A kind of: mistake

Spot as a verb

  1. Catch sight of.

    Synonyms: descry, espy, spy · A kind of: spy

  2. Detect with the senses.

    “The fleeing convicts were picked out of the darkness by the watchful prison guards.” “I can't make out the faces in this photograph.”

    Synonyms: recognize, recognise, distinguish, discern, pick out, make out, tell apart · Kinds: resolve, discriminate

  3. Mar or impair with a flaw.

    “Her face was blemished.”

    Synonyms: blemish · A kind of: dirty

  4. Make a spot or mark onto.

    Synonyms: fleck, blob, blot · A kind of: change surface · Kinds: splotch, tarnish, speckle, spatter

  5. Become spotted.

    “This dress spots quickly.”

    A kind of: change · Kinds: freckle, fox

  6. Mark with a spot or spots so as to allow easy recognition.

    “Spot the areas that one should clearly identify.”

    A kind of: mark · Kinds: freckle, mottle

Phrases and compounds with "spot"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • hot spot (noun) A place of political unrest and potential violence.
  • one-spot (noun) A domino or die whose upward face shows one pip.
  • six-spot (noun) A playing card or domino or die whose upward face shows six pips.
  • spot jam (verb) Jam a single frequency.
  • ten-spot (noun) One of four playing cards in a deck with ten pips on the face.
  • five-spot (noun) A United States bill worth 5 dollars.
  • four-spot (noun) A playing card or domino or die whose upward face shows four pips.
  • halo spot (noun) A blight of bean plants.
  • high spot (noun) The most interesting or memorable part.
  • nine-spot (noun) One of four playing cards in a deck with nine pips on the face.
  • soft spot (noun) A place of especial vulnerability.
  • spot pass (noun) A pass to a designated spot on the field; the receiver should arrive at that spot the same time the ball does.
  • spot weld (noun) Each of the welds made by welding at a separate point.
  • spot-weld (noun) Each of the welds made by welding at a separate point.
  • weak spot (noun) A place of especial vulnerability.
  • worn spot (noun) A spot that has been worn away by abrasion or erosion.
