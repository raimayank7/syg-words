Spread: meaning, synonyms and examples

Spread is used as a verb (10 senses), noun (10 senses) and adjective (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Distribute or disperse widely.

In Hindi
फैलाना
Synonyms
distribute, propagate, overspread, unfold, spread out, open, circulate, circularize, circularise, disseminate
Opposites
gather
Word family
spreader, spreading

Spread as a verb

  1. Distribute or disperse widely.

    “The invaders spread their language all over the country.”

    Synonyms: distribute · Opposite: gather · Kinds: generalize, metastasize, discharge, strew, export, propagate, deploy, redistribute

  2. Become distributed or widespread.

    “The infection spread.” “Optimism spread among the population.”

    Synonyms: propagate · A kind of: move · Kinds: catch

  3. Spread across or over.

    “A big oil spot spread across the water.”

    Synonyms: overspread · A kind of: cover · Kinds: transgress

  4. Spread out or open from a closed or folded state.

    “Open the map.” “Spread your arms.”

    Synonyms: unfold, spread out, open · A kind of: undo · Kinds: divaricate, exfoliate, grass, butterfly, uncross, splay

  5. Cause to become widely known.

    “Spread information.” “Circulate a rumor.” “Broadcast the news.”

    Synonyms: circulate, circularize, circularise, distribute, disseminate, propagate, broadcast, diffuse · A kind of: publicize · Kinds: podcast, sow, popularize, carry

  6. Become widely known and passed on.

    “The rumor spread.” “The story went around in the office.”

    Synonyms: go around, circulate · A kind of: travel

  7. Strew or distribute over an area.

    “He spread fertilizer over the lawn.” “Scatter cards across the table.”

    Synonyms: scatter, spread out · A kind of: circulate · Kinds: manure, birdlime, circumfuse, distribute

  8. Move outward.

    “The soldiers fanned out.”

    Synonyms: diffuse, spread out, fan out · A kind of: spread · Kinds: percolate, creep, run, mantle

  9. Cover by spreading something over.

    “Spread the bread with cheese.”

    A kind of: cover · Kinds: slather

  10. Distribute over a surface in a layer.

    “Spread cheese on a piece of bread.”

Spread as a noun

  1. Process or result of distributing or extending over a wide expanse of space.

    Synonyms: spreading · A kind of: change of location · Kinds: diffusion, dispersion, invasion, irradiation, radiation

  2. A conspicuous disparity or difference as between two figures.

    “Gap between income and outgo.” “The spread between lending and borrowing costs.”

    Synonyms: gap · A kind of: disparity

  3. Farm consisting of a large tract of land along with facilities needed to raise livestock (especially cattle).

    Synonyms: ranch, cattle ranch, cattle farm · A kind of: farm

  4. A haphazard distribution in all directions.

    Synonyms: scatter · A kind of: distribution · Kinds: diffuseness

  5. A tasty mixture to be spread on bread or crackers or used in preparing other dishes.

    Synonyms: paste · A kind of: condiment · Kinds: margarine, nut butter, peanut butter, marshmallow fluff, onion butter, pimento butter, shrimp butter, lobster butter

  6. A meal that is well prepared and greatly enjoyed.

    “A banquet for the graduating seniors.” “The Thanksgiving feast.” “They put out quite a spread.”

    Synonyms: banquet, feast · A kind of: meal

  7. Two facing pages of a book or other publication.

    Synonyms: spread head, spreadhead, facing pages · A kind of: page · Kinds: center spread, centerfold

  8. The expansion of a person's girth (especially at middle age).

    “She exercised to avoid that middle-aged spread.”

    A kind of: girth

  9. Decorative cover for a bed.

    Synonyms: bedspread, bedcover, bed cover, bed covering, counterpane · A kind of: bedclothes · Kinds: coverlet, quilted bedspread

  10. Act of extending over a wider scope or expanse of space or time.

    Synonyms: spreading · A kind of: extension · Kinds: circulation, dispersion, scatter, decentralization

Spread as an adjective

  1. Distributed or spread over a considerable extent.

    “Has ties with many widely dispersed friends.”

    Synonyms: dispersed · Similar: distributed

  2. Prepared or arranged for a meal; especially having food set out.

    “A table spread with food.”

    Similar: prepared

  3. Fully extended in width.

    “Outspread wings.” “With arms spread wide.”

    Synonyms: outspread · Similar: extended

Phrases and compounds with "spread"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • spread out (verb) Move outward.
  • spread-out (adjective) Especially spread in a fan shape.
  • spread head (noun) Two facing pages of a book or other publication.
  • spread over (verb) Form a cover over.
  • spread eagle (noun) An emblem (an eagle with wings and legs spread) on the obverse of the Great Seal of the United States.
  • spread-eagle (verb) Stretch out completely.
  • center spread (noun) The spread at the center of a magazine.
  • centre spread (noun) The spread at the center of a magazine.
  • cheese spread (noun) Spread made of cheese mixed with butter or cream or cream cheese and seasonings.
