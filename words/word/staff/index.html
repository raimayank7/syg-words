Staff: meaning, synonyms and examples

Staff is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Personnel who assist their superior in carrying out an assigned task.

In Hindi
कर्मचारी; लाठी
Synonyms
faculty, stave

Staff as a noun

  1. Personnel who assist their superior in carrying out an assigned task.

    “The hospital has an excellent nursing staff.” “The general relied on his staff to make routine decisions.”

    A kind of: force · Kinds: newsroom, office, research staff, sales staff, security staff, service staff, general staff, headquarters staff

  2. A strong rod or stick with a specialized utilitarian purpose.

    “He walked with the help of a wooden staff.”

    A kind of: stick · Kinds: alpenstock, baton, crook, crutch, distaff, flagpole, pikestaff, quarterstaff

  3. The body of teachers and administrators at a school.

    “The dean addressed the letter to the entire staff of the university.”

    Synonyms: faculty · A kind of: body

  4. Building material consisting of plaster and hair; used to cover external surfaces of temporary structure (as at an exposition) or for decoration.

    A kind of: building material

  5. A rod carried as a symbol.

    A kind of: symbol · Kinds: crosier, mace, scepter, tipstaff

  6. (music) the system of five horizontal lines on which the musical notes are written.

    Synonyms: stave · A kind of: musical notation

Staff as a verb

  1. Provide with staff.

    “This position is not always staffed.”

    A kind of: provide · Kinds: man

  2. Serve on the staff of.

    “The two men staff the reception desk.”

    A kind of: serve

Phrases and compounds with "staff"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • half-staff (noun) A position some distance below the top of a mast to which a flag is lowered in mourning or to signal distress.
  • staff line (noun) Any of the 5 horizontal marks comprising a staff.
  • staff tree (noun) Any small tree or twining shrub of the genus Celastrus.
  • staff vine (noun) Twining shrub of North America having yellow capsules enclosing scarlet seeds.
  • sales staff (noun) Those in a business who are responsible for sales.
  • office staff (noun) Professional or clerical workers in an office.
  • staff member (noun) An employee who is a member of a staff of workers (especially a member of the staff that works for the President of the United States).
  • staff office (noun) The department responsible for hiring and training and placing employees and for setting policies for personnel management.
  • treble staff (noun) A clef that puts the G above middle C on the second line of a staff.
  • general staff (noun) Military officers assigned to assist a senior officer in planning military policy.
  • service staff (noun) Those in a business responsible for maintaining the physical plant.
  • staff of life (noun) Food made from dough of flour or meal and usually raised with yeast or baking powder and then baked.
  • staff officer (noun) A commissioned officer assigned to a military commander's staff.
  • chief of staff (noun) The senior officer of a service of the armed forces.
  • research staff (noun) A group of associated research workers in a university or library or laboratory.
  • security staff (noun) Those in an organization responsible for preventing spying or theft.
