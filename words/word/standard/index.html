Standard: meaning, synonyms and examples

Standard is used as a noun (6 senses) and adjective (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A basis for comparison; a reference point against which other things can be evaluated.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites
nonstandard
Word family
standardize, standardise, stand

Standard as a noun

  1. A basis for comparison; a reference point against which other things can be evaluated.

    “The schools comply with federal standards.” “They set the measure for all subsequent work.”

    Synonyms: criterion, measure, touchstone · A kind of: system of measurement · Kinds: benchmark, earned run average, grade point average, procrustean standard, yardstick, medium of exchange, scale, gauge

  2. The ideal in terms of which something can be judged.

    “They live by the standards of their community.”

    Synonyms: criterion · A kind of: ideal · Kinds: control condition, design criteria

  3. A board measure = 1980 board feet.

    A kind of: volume unit

  4. The value behind the money in a monetary system.

    Synonyms: monetary standard · A kind of: value · Kinds: gold standard, silver standard, bimetallism

  5. An upright pole or beam (especially one used as a support).

    “Distance was marked by standards every mile.” “Lamps supported on standards provided illumination.”

    A kind of: post

  6. Any distinctive flag.

    Synonyms: banner · A kind of: flag · Kinds: oriflamme

Standard as an adjective

  1. Conforming to or constituting a standard of measurement or value; or of the usual or regularized or accepted kind.

    “Windows of standard width.” “Standard sizes.” “The standard fixtures.”

    Opposite: nonstandard · Similar: modular, regular, regulation, standardized, stock

  2. Commonly used or supplied.

    “Standard procedure.” “Standard car equipment.”

    Similar: common

  3. Established or well-known or widely recognized as a model of authority or excellence.

    “A standard reference work.” “The classical argument between free trade and protectionism.”

    Opposite: nonstandard · Similar: authoritative, basic, casebook, criterial

  4. Conforming to the established language usage of educated native speakers.

    “Standard English.” “Received standard English is sometimes called the King's English.”

    Synonyms: received · Opposite: nonstandard · Similar: acceptable, classical

  5. Regularly and widely used or sold.

    “A standard size.” “A stock item.”

    Synonyms: stock · Similar: regular
