State: meaning, synonyms and examples

State is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: The territory occupied by one of the constituent administrative districts of a nation.

In Hindi
राज्य; हालत; कहना
Synonyms
province, nation, country, land, commonwealth, res publica, body politic, state of matter, say, tell
Word family
statement

State as a noun

  1. The territory occupied by one of the constituent administrative districts of a nation.

    “His state is in the deep south.”

    Synonyms: province · A kind of: administrative district · Kinds: commonwealth, eparchy

  2. The way something is with respect to its main attributes.

    “The current state of knowledge.” “His state of health.” “In a weak financial state.”

    A kind of: attribute · Kinds: feeling, skillfulness, cleavage, medium, ornamentation, condition, condition, conditionality

  3. The group of people comprising the government of a sovereign state.

    “The state has lowered its income tax.”

    A kind of: government · Kinds: welfare state

  4. A politically organized body of people under a single government.

    “The state has elected a new president.” “African nations.” “Students who had come to the nation's capitol.”

    Synonyms: nation, country, land, commonwealth, res publica, body politic · A kind of: political unit · Kinds: commonwealth country, developing country, foreign country, rogue state, suzerain, sea power, world power, city state

  5. (chemistry) the three traditional states of matter are solids (fixed shape and volume) and liquids (fixed volume and shaped by the container) and gases (filling the container).

    “The solid state of water is called ice.”

    Synonyms: state of matter · A kind of: chemical phenomenon · Kinds: phase, liquid, solid, gas, plasma

  6. A state of depression or agitation.

    “He was in such a state you just couldn't reason with him.”

    A kind of: emotional state

  7. The territory occupied by a nation.

    “He returned to the land of his birth.” “He visited several European countries.”

    Synonyms: country, land · A kind of: administrative district · Kinds: banana republic, fatherland, buffer state, kingdom, tax haven, sultanate

  8. The federal department in the United States that sets and maintains foreign policies.

    “The Department of State was created in 1789.”

    A kind of: executive department

State as a verb

  1. Express in words.

    “He said that he wanted to marry her.” “Tell me what is bothering you.” “State your opinion.”

    Synonyms: say, tell · A kind of: express · Kinds: present, misstate, answer, precede, announce, articulate, get out, declare

  2. Put before.

    “I submit to you that the accused is guilty.”

    Synonyms: submit, put forward, posit · A kind of: propose

  3. Indicate through a symbol, formula, etc.

    “Can you express this distance in kilometers?”

    Synonyms: express · A kind of: denote · Kinds: vote, vote

Phrases and compounds with "state"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gem state (noun) A state in the Rocky Mountains.
  • city state (noun) A state consisting of a sovereign city.
  • city-state (noun) A state consisting of a sovereign city.
  • free state (noun) A Mid-Atlantic state; one of the original 13 colonies.
  • state bank (noun) A bank chartered by a state rather than by the federal government.
  • state line (noun) The boundary between two states.
  • aloha state (noun) A state in the United States in the central Pacific on the Hawaiian Islands.
  • first state (noun) A Mid-Atlantic state; one of the original 13 colonies.
  • ocean state (noun) A state in New England; one of the original 13 colonies; the smallest state.
  • peach state (noun) A state in southeastern United States; one of the Confederate states during the American Civil War.
  • rogue state (noun) A state that does not respect other states in its international actions.

  • solid-state (adjective) Characteristic of or relating to the physical properties of solid materials especially electromagnetic or thermodynamic or structural properties of crystalline solids.
  • badger state (noun) A midwestern state in north central United States.
  • balkan state (noun) Any one of the countries on the Balkan Peninsula.
