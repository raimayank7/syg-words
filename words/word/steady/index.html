Steady: meaning, synonyms and examples

Steady is used as an adjective (6 senses), verb (2 senses), noun (1 sense) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Not subject to change or variation especially in behavior.

In Hindi

Synonyms

Opposites
unsteady
Word family
steadiness

Steady as an adjective

  1. Not subject to change or variation especially in behavior.

    “A steady beat.” “A steady job.” “A steady breeze.”

    Opposite: unsteady · Similar: dependable, even, firm, level, steadied, sure

  2. Not liable to fluctuate or especially to fall.

    “Stocks are still firm.”

    Synonyms: firm, unfluctuating · Similar: stable

  3. Securely in position; not shaky.

    “Held the ladder steady.”

    Similar: secure

  4. Marked by firm determination or resolution; not shakable.

    “Firm convictions.” “A firm mouth.” “Steadfast resolve.”

    Synonyms: firm, steadfast, stiff, unbendable, unfaltering, unshakable, unwavering · Similar: resolute

  5. Relating to a person who does something regularly.

    “A regular customer.” “A steady drinker.”

    Synonyms: regular · Similar: frequent

  6. Not easily excited or upset.

    “Steady nerves.”

    Similar: unexcitable

Steady as a verb

  1. Make steady.

    “Steady yourself.”

    Synonyms: calm, becalm · A kind of: stabilize

  2. Support or hold steady and make steadfast, with or as if with a brace.

    “Brace your elbows while working on the potter's wheel.”

    Synonyms: brace, stabilize, stabilise · A kind of: strengthen · Kinds: ballast, guy

Steady as a noun

  1. A person loved by another person.

    Synonyms: sweetheart, sweetie, truelove · A kind of: lover · Kinds: ladylove, sugar daddy, valentine

Steady as an adverb

  1. In a steady manner.

    “He could still walk steadily.”

    Synonyms: steadily

Phrases and compounds with "steady"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • go steady (verb) Date regularly; have a steady relationship with.
  • rock-steady (adjective) Consistent in performance or behavior.
  • steady down (verb) Become settled or established and stable in one's residence or life style.
  • steady-going (adjective) Consistent in performance or behavior.
  • steady state theory (noun) (cosmology) the theory that the universe maintains a constant average density with matter created to fill the void left by galaxies that are receding from each other.
