Stir: meaning, synonyms and examples

Stir is used as a verb (8 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Move an implement through.

In Hindi
चलाना, हिलाना
Synonyms
shift, budge, agitate, stimulate, excite, shake, shake up, touch, raise, conjure
Word family
stirrer, stirring

Stir as a verb

  1. Move an implement through.

    “Stir the soup.” “Stir my drink.” “Stir the soil.”

    A kind of: move · Kinds: churn, paddle

  2. Move very slightly.

    “He shifted in his seat.”

    Synonyms: shift, budge, agitate · A kind of: move

  3. Stir feelings in.

    “Stimulate my appetite.” “Excite the audience.” “Stir emotions.”

    Synonyms: stimulate, excite · A kind of: sensitize · Kinds: horripilate, work, thrill, whet, disgust

  4. Stir the feelings, emotions, or peace of.

    “These stories shook the community.” “The civil war shook the country.”

    Synonyms: stimulate, shake, shake up, excite · A kind of: arouse · Kinds: fuel, frighten, thrill, tempt, elate, inspire

  5. Affect emotionally.

    “A stirring movie.” “I was touched by your kind letter of sympathy.”

    Synonyms: touch · A kind of: affect · Kinds: get

  6. Summon into action or bring into existence, often as if by magic.

    “Raise the specter of unemployment.” “He conjured wild birds in the air.” “Call down the spirits from the mountain.”

    Synonyms: raise, conjure, conjure up, invoke, evoke, call down, arouse, bring up · A kind of: make · Kinds: curse, bless

  7. To begin moving, "As the thunder started the sleeping children began to stir".

    “As the thunder started the sleeping children began to stir.”

    Synonyms: arouse · A kind of: move

  8. Mix or add by stirring.

    “Stir nuts into the dough.”

    A kind of: work

Stir as a noun

  1. A prominent or sensational but short-lived news event.

    “He made a great splash and then disappeared.”

    Synonyms: splash · A kind of: disturbance

  2. Emotional agitation and excitement.

    A kind of: agitation · Kinds: electricity, sensation

  3. A rapid active commotion.

    Synonyms: bustle, hustle, flurry, ado, fuss · A kind of: commotion

Phrases and compounds with "stir"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • stir up (verb) Try to stir up public opinion.
  • stir fry (verb) Fry very quickly over high heat.

Word family
