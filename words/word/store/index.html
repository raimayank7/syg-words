Store: meaning, synonyms and examples

Store is used as a noun (4 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A mercantile establishment for the retail sale of goods or services.

In Hindi
दुकान; जमा रखना
Synonyms
shop, stock, fund, memory, computer memory, storage, computer storage, memory board, storehouse, depot
Word family
storage

Store as a noun

  1. A mercantile establishment for the retail sale of goods or services.

    “He bought it at a shop on Cape Cod.”

    Synonyms: shop · A kind of: mercantile establishment · Kinds: bakery, barbershop, bazaar, bookshop, booth, boutique, building supply store, butcher shop

  2. A supply of something available for future use.

    “He brought back a large store of Cuban cigars.”

    Synonyms: stock, fund · A kind of: accumulation · Kinds: infrastructure, hoard, provision, issue, seed stock

  3. An electronic memory device.

    “A memory and the CPU form the central part of a computer to which peripherals are attached.”

    Synonyms: memory, computer memory, storage, computer storage, memory board · A kind of: memory device, hardware · Kinds: non-volatile storage, read-only memory, real storage, scratchpad, virtual memory, volatile storage

  4. A depository for goods.

    “Storehouses were built close to the docks.”

    Synonyms: storehouse, depot, entrepot, storage · A kind of: depository · Kinds: dump, granary, magazine, railhead, treasure house, warehouse

Store as a verb

  1. Keep or lay aside for future use.

    “Store grain for the winter.” “The bear stores fat for the period of hibernation when he doesn't eat.”

    Synonyms: hive away, lay in, put in, salt away, stack away, stash away · A kind of: keep · Kinds: bin, computerize, victual, roll up, hive

  2. Find a place for and put away for storage.

    “Where should we stow the vegetables?” “I couldn't store all the books in the attic so I sold some.”

    A kind of: keep · Kinds: garner, keep, mothball, reposit, wharf, tank, loft, warehouse

Phrases and compounds with "store"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in store (adjective) In readiness; awaiting.
  • mens store (noun) A store where men's clothes are sold.
  • shoe store (noun) A shop where shoes are sold.
  • candy store (noun) A confectioner's shop.
  • chain store (noun) One of a chain of retail stores under the same management and selling the same merchandise.
  • buffer store (noun) (computer science) a part of RAM used for temporary storage of data that is waiting to be sent to a device; used to compensate for differences in the rate of flow of data between components of a computer system.
  • flower store (noun) A shop where flowers and ornamental plants are sold.
  • powder store (noun) A storehouse (as a compartment on a warship) where weapons and ammunition are stored.
  • retail store (noun) A place of business for retailing goods.
  • store cheese (noun) Informal names for American cheddar.
  • store-bought (adjective) Purchased; not homemade.
  • country store (noun) A retail store serving a sparsely populated region; usually stocked with a wide variety of merchandise.
  • general store (noun) A retail store serving a sparsely populated region; usually stocked with a wide variety of merchandise.
  • grocery store (noun) A marketplace where groceries are sold.
  • jewelry store (noun) A firm that sells and buys jewelry.
  • clothing store (noun) A store where men's clothes are sold.
