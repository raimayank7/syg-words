Storm: meaning, synonyms and examples

Storm is used as a noun (3 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: A violent weather condition with winds 64-72 knots (11 on the Beaufort scale) and precipitation and thunder and lightning.

In Hindi
तूफ़ान, आँधी
Synonyms
violent storm, tempest, ramp, rage, force, surprise
Word family
stormy

Storm as a noun

  1. A violent weather condition with winds 64-72 knots (11 on the Beaufort scale) and precipitation and thunder and lightning.

    Synonyms: violent storm · A kind of: atmospheric phenomenon · Kinds: northeaster, hailstorm, ice storm, rainstorm, blizzard, thunderstorm, windstorm

  2. A violent commotion or disturbance.

    “The storms that had characterized their relationship had died away.” “It was only a tempest in a teapot.”

    Synonyms: tempest · A kind of: disturbance

  3. A direct and violent assault on a stronghold.

    A kind of: assault

Storm as a verb

  1. Behave violently, as if in state of a great anger.

    Synonyms: ramp, rage · A kind of: act

  2. Take by force.

    “Storm the fort.”

    Synonyms: force · A kind of: penetrate

  3. Rain, hail, or snow hard and be very windy, often with thunder or lightning.

    “If it storms, we'll need shelter.”

  4. Blow hard.

    “It was storming all night.”

    A kind of: blow

  5. Attack by storm; attack suddenly.

    Synonyms: surprise · A kind of: attack

Phrases and compounds with "storm"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • ice storm (noun) A storm with freezing rain that leaves everything glazed with ice.
  • dust storm (noun) A windstorm that lifts up clouds of dust or sand.
  • line storm (noun) A violent rainstorm near the time of an equinox.
  • storm cone (noun) A canvas cone hoisted to warn of high winds.
  • storm door (noun) An extra outer door for protection against severe weather or winter.
  • storm lamp (noun) An oil lamp with a glass chimney and perforated metal lid to protect the flame from high winds; candlestick with a glass chimney.
  • storm sash (noun) A window outside an ordinary window to protect against severe weather or winter.
  • storm cloud (noun) A heavy dark cloud presaging rain or a storm.
  • debris storm (noun) The sudden spread of dust and debris from a collapsing building.
  • silver storm (noun) A storm with freezing rain that leaves everything glazed with ice.
  • storm cellar (noun) An underground shelter where you can go until a storm passes.
  • storm center (noun) A center of trouble or disturbance.
  • storm centre (noun) A center of trouble or disturbance.
  • storm petrel (noun) Any of various small petrels having dark plumage with paler underparts.
  • storm signal (noun) A signal announcing the approach of a storm (particularly a storm of marked violence).
  • storm troops (noun) Nazi militia created by Hitler in 1921 that helped him to power but was eclipsed by the SS after 1943.
