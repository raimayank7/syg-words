Straight: meaning, synonyms and examples

Straight is used as an adjective (14 senses), adverb (3 senses) and noun (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: Successive (without a break).

In Hindi
सीधा
Synonyms
consecutive, unbent, unbowed, square, uncoiled, true, straightforward, neat, full-strength, directly
Opposites
crooked, curly, curved
Word family
straightness

Straight as an adjective

  1. Successive (without a break).

    “Sick for five straight days.”

    Synonyms: consecutive · Similar: continuous

  2. Having no deviations.

    “Straight lines.” “Straight roads across the desert.” “Straight teeth.”

    Opposite: crooked · Similar: aligned, unbent, untwisted

  3. (of hair) having no waves or curls.

    “Her naturally straight hair hung long and silky.”

    Opposite: curly · Similar: uncurled, unpermed

  4. Erect in posture.

    “Sit straight.” “Stood defiantly with unbowed back.”

    Synonyms: unbent, unbowed · Similar: erect

  5. In keeping with the facts.

    “Set the record straight.” “Made sure the facts were straight in the report.”

    Similar: accurate

  6. Characterized by honesty and fairness.

    “Straight dealing.” “A square deal.”

    Synonyms: square · Opposite: crooked · Similar: aboveboard, guileless, straightarrow

  7. No longer coiled.

    Synonyms: uncoiled · Similar: uncurled

  8. Free from curves or angles.

    “A straight line.”

    Opposite: curved · Similar: trabeated, uncurved

  9. Neatly arranged; not disorderly.

    “The room is straight now.”

    Similar: tidy

  10. Accurately fitted; level.

    “The window frame isn't quite true.”

    Synonyms: true · Similar: even

  11. Without evasion or compromise.

    “A square contradiction.” “He is not being as straightforward as it appears.”

    Synonyms: square, straightforward · Similar: direct

  12. Without water.

    Synonyms: neat, full-strength · Similar: undiluted

  13. Following a correct or logical method.

    “Straight reasoning.”

    Similar: correct

  14. Rigidly conventional or old-fashioned.

    Synonyms: square · Similar: conventional

Straight as an adverb

  1. Without deviation.

    “The path leads directly to the lake.” “Went direct to the office.”

    Synonyms: directly, direct

  2. In a forthright manner; candidly or frankly.

    “He didn't answer directly.” “Told me straight out.” “Came out flat for less work and more pay.”

    Synonyms: directly, flat

  3. In a straight line; in a direct course.

    “The road runs straight.”

Straight as a noun

  1. A poker hand with 5 consecutive cards (regardless of suit).

    A kind of: poker hand

  2. A straight segment of a roadway or racecourse.

    Synonyms: straightaway · A kind of: section · Kinds: stretch

Phrases and compounds with "straight"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • straight man (noun) A performer who acts as stooge to a comedian.
  • straight off (adverb) Without delay or hesitation; with no time intervening.
  • straight pin (noun) Pin consisting of a short straight stiff piece of wire with a pointed end; used to fasten pieces of cloth or paper together.
  • straight-arm (noun) (American football) the act of warding off a tackler by holding the arm fully extended with the hand against the opponent.
  • straight-out (adjective) Without reservation or exception.
  • straight arch (noun) An arch with mutually supporting voussoirs that has a straight horizontal extrados and intrados.
  • straight face (noun) A serious facial expression giving no evidence of interest or amusement.
  • straight hang (noun) A hang performed on the rings or parallel bars with the body erect and the arms at the sides.
  • straight line (noun) A line traced by a point traveling in a constant direction; a line of zero curvature.
  • straight angle (noun) An angle of 180 degrees.
  • straight arrow (noun) A frank and honest person.
  • straight chain (noun) An open chain of atoms with no side chains.
  • straight chair (noun) A straight-backed chair without arms.
  • straight flush (noun) A poker hand with consecutive cards in the same suit.
  • straight flute (noun) A rock drill with flutes that are straight.
  • straight razor (noun) A razor with a straight cutting edge enclosed in a case that forms a handle when the razor is opened for use.
