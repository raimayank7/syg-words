Strip: meaning, synonyms and examples

Strip is used as a noun (5 senses) and verb (13 senses). Its most common meaning is: A relatively long narrow piece of something.

In Hindi
पट्टी; उतारना
Synonyms
slip, airstrip, flight strip, landing strip, comic strip, cartoon strip, funnies, deprive, divest, undress
Word family
stripper, stripping

Strip as a noun

  1. A relatively long narrow piece of something.

    “He felt a flat strip of muscle.”

    A kind of: part · Kinds: row

  2. Artifact consisting of a narrow flat piece of material.

    Synonyms: slip · A kind of: artifact · Kinds: band, band, band, cramp, reef, lead, mullion, ribbon

  3. An airfield without normal airport facilities.

    Synonyms: airstrip, flight strip, landing strip · A kind of: airfield · Kinds: flare path

  4. A sequence of drawings telling a story in a newspaper or comic book.

    Synonyms: comic strip, cartoon strip, funnies · A kind of: cartoon

  5. Thin piece of wood or metal.

    A kind of: lumber · Kinds: batten, cleat, fingerboard, furring strip, jackstraw, picket, slat, spline

Strip as a verb

  1. Take away possessions from someone.

    Synonyms: deprive, divest · A kind of: take · Kinds: disarm, expropriate, dispossess, clean out, unclothe, orphan, bereave

  2. Get undressed.

    “Please don't undress in front of everybody!” “She strips in front of strangers every night for a living.”

    Synonyms: undress, discase, uncase, unclothe, strip down, disrobe, peel · A kind of: take off · Kinds: take off

  3. Remove the surface from.

    “Strip wood.”

    A kind of: remove · Kinds: skin, bark, decorticate, pluck

  4. Remove substances from by a percolating liquid.

    “Leach the soil.”

    Synonyms: leach · A kind of: remove

  5. Lay bare.

    “Denude a forest.”

    Synonyms: denude, bare, denudate · A kind of: clear · Kinds: defoliate, burn off

  6. Steal goods; take as spoils.

    “During the earthquake people looted the stores that were deserted by their owners.”

    Synonyms: plunder, despoil, loot, reave, rifle, ransack, pillage, foray · A kind of: take · Kinds: deplume

  7. Remove all contents or possession from, or empty completely.

    “The boys cleaned the sandwich platters.” “The trees were cleaned of apples by the storm.”

    Synonyms: clean · A kind of: remove

  8. Strip the cured leaves from.

    “Strip tobacco.”

    A kind of: remove

  9. Remove the thread (of screws).

    A kind of: smooth

  10. Remove a constituent from a liquid.

    A kind of: remove

  11. Take off or remove.

    “Strip a wall of its wallpaper.”

    Synonyms: dismantle · A kind of: remove

  12. Draw the last milk (of cows).

    A kind of: milk

  13. Remove (someone's or one's own) clothes.

    “The nurse quickly undressed the accident victim.” “She divested herself of her outdoor clothes.” “He disinvested himself of his garments.”

    Synonyms: undress, divest, disinvest · A kind of: remove

Phrases and compounds with "strip"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • gaza strip (noun) A coastal region at the southeastern corner of the Mediterranean bordering Israel and Egypt.
  • strip down (verb) Get undressed.
  • strip mall (noun) A mercantile establishment consisting of a row of various stores and business and restaurants along a road or busy street; usually opening on a parking lot.
  • strip mine (noun) An open mine (usually for coal) where the seams run close to the surface.
  • taxi strip (noun) A paved surface in the form of a strip; used by planes taxiing to or from the runway at an airport.
  • bacon strip (noun) A slice of bacon.
  • comic strip (noun) A sequence of drawings telling a story in a newspaper or comic book.
  • strip alert (noun) A state of readiness for domestic defense aircraft.
  • strip miner (noun) A miner who does strip mining.
  • strip poker (noun) Poker in which a player's losses are paid by removing an article of clothing.
  • strip steak (noun) Steak from upper part of the short loin.
  • strip-mined (adjective) Mined near the earth's surface by stripping.
  • flight strip (noun) An airfield without normal airport facilities.
  • mobius strip (noun) A continuous closed surface with only one side; formed from a rectangular strip by rotating one end 180 degrees and joining it with the other end.
  • strip mining (noun) The mining of ore or coal from an open mine.
  • barrier strip (noun) A junction unit for connecting 2 cables without the need for plugs.
