Support: meaning, synonyms and examples

Support is used as a verb (11 senses) and noun (11 senses). Its most common meaning is: Give moral or psychological support, aid, or courage to.

In Hindi
सहारा, समर्थन
Synonyms
back up, back, endorse, indorse, plump for, plunk for, hold, sustain, hold up, confirm
Word family
supportive, supporter, supporting

Support as a verb

  1. Give moral or psychological support, aid, or courage to.

    “She supported him during the illness.” “Her children always backed her up.”

    Synonyms: back up · Kinds: sponsor, patronize, help, promote, undergird, second

  2. Support materially or financially.

    “He does not support his natural children.” “The scholarship supported me when I was in college.”

    A kind of: give · Kinds: fund, subsidize, provide, see through, sponsor

  3. Be behind; approve of.

    “He plumped for the Labor Party.” “I backed Kennedy in 1960.”

    Synonyms: back, endorse, indorse, plump for, plunk for · A kind of: approve · Kinds: guarantee, champion

  4. Be the physical support of; carry the weight of.

    “The beam holds up the roof.” “He supported me with one hand while I balanced on the beam.” “What's holding that mirror?”

    Synonyms: hold, sustain, hold up · Kinds: scaffold, block, carry, chock, buoy, pole, bracket, underpin

  5. Establish or strengthen as with new evidence or facts.

    “His story confirmed my doubts.” “The evidence supports the defendant.”

    Synonyms: confirm, corroborate, sustain, substantiate, affirm · Kinds: back, vouch, verify, prove, document, validate

  6. Adopt as a belief.

    Synonyms: subscribe · A kind of: agree

  7. Support with evidence or authority or make more certain or confirm.

    “The stories and claims were born out by the evidence.”

    Synonyms: corroborate, underpin, bear out · A kind of: match

  8. Argue or speak in defense of.

    “She supported the motion to strike.”

    Synonyms: defend, fend for · A kind of: argue · Kinds: apologize, stand up, uphold

  9. Play a subordinate role to (another performer).

    “Olivier supported Gielgud beautifully in the second act.”

    A kind of: act

  10. Be a regular customer or client of.

    “We patronize this store.” “Our sponsor kept our art studio going for as long as he could.”

    Synonyms: patronize, patronise, patronage, keep going · A kind of: foster

  11. Put up with something or somebody unpleasant.

    “I cannot bear his constant criticism.” “The new secretary had to endure a lot of unprofessional remarks.” “He learned to tolerate the heat.”

    Synonyms: digest, endure, stick out, stomach, bear, stand, tolerate, brook · A kind of: permit · Kinds: accept, stand for, bear up, take lying down, take a joke, sit out, pay

Support as a noun

  1. The activity of providing for or maintaining by supplying with money or necessities.

    “His support kept the family together.” “They gave him emotional support during difficult times.”

    A kind of: activity · Kinds: sustenance, logistic support

  2. Aiding the cause or policy or interests of.

    “The president no longer has the support of his own party.” “They developed a scheme of mutual support.”

    A kind of: aid · Kinds: attachment, advocacy, sponsorship, endorsement, blessing, reassurance

  3. Something providing immaterial assistance to a person or cause or interest.

    “The policy found little public support.” “His faith was all the support he needed.” “The team enjoyed the support of their fans.”

    A kind of: influence · Kinds: anchor, lifeline

  4. A military operation (often involving new supplies of men and materiel) to strengthen a military force or aid in the performance of its mission.

    “They called for artillery support.”

    Synonyms: reinforcement, reenforcement · A kind of: operation · Kinds: close support

  5. Documentary validation.

    “His documentation of the results was excellent.” “The strongest support for this view is the work of Jones.”

    Synonyms: documentation · A kind of: validation

  6. The financial means whereby one lives.

    “Each child was expected to pay for their keep.” “He applied to the state for support.” “He could no longer earn his own livelihood.”

    Synonyms: keep, livelihood, living, bread and butter, sustenance · A kind of: resource · Kinds: comforts, maintenance, meal ticket, subsistence

  7. Supporting structure that holds up or provides a foundation.

    “The statue stood on a marble support.”

    A kind of: supporting structure · Kinds: abutment, architrave, basement, buttress, foundation, pedestal, prop

  8. The act of bearing the weight of or strengthening.

    “He leaned against the wall for support.”

    Synonyms: supporting · A kind of: activity · Kinds: shoring, suspension

  9. A musical part (vocal or instrumental) that supports or provides background for other musical parts.

    Synonyms: accompaniment, musical accompaniment, backup · A kind of: part · Kinds: descant, vamp

  10. Any device that bears the weight of another thing.

    “There was no place to attach supports for a shelf.”

    A kind of: device · Kinds: andiron, arch support, back, backboard, baluster, bar, base, bearing

  11. Financial resources provided to make some project possible.

    “The foundation provided support for the experiment.”

    Synonyms: financial support, funding, backing, financial backing · A kind of: resource

Phrases and compounds with "support"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • arch support (noun) A support for the arch of the foot.
  • life support (noun) Equipment that makes life possible in otherwise deadly environmental conditions.
  • life-support (adjective) Of or pertaining to equipment or methods used to sustain life.
  • support hose (noun) Elasticized stocking intended to reduce pressure on the veins of the leg (as in case of varicose veins).
  • child support (noun) Court-ordered support paid by one spouse to the other who has custody of the children after the parents are separated.
  • close support (noun) Close-in firing by one unit against an enemy engaged by another unit.
  • price support (noun) A government subsidy used to maintain prices at a certain level.
  • support level (noun) (stock market) the price at which a certain security becomes attractive to investors.
  • direct support (noun) A mission requiring one force to support another specific force and authorizing it to answer directly the supported force's request for assistance.
  • support column (noun) A column that supports a heavy weight.
  • support system (noun) A network of facilities and people who interact and remain in informal communication for mutual assistance; a network that enables you to live in a certain style.
  • support payment (noun) A payment made by one person for the support of another.
  • logistic support (noun) Assistance between and within military commands.
  • support stocking (noun) Elasticized stocking intended to reduce pressure on the veins of the leg (as in case of varicose veins).
  • financial support (noun) Financial resources provided to make some project possible.
  • interagency support (noun) Provision of logistic (or administrative) support by one or more of the military services to one or more departments or agencies of the United States government.
