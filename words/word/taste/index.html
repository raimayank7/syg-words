Taste: meaning, synonyms and examples

Taste is used as a noun (6 senses) and verb (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: The sensation that results when taste buds in the tongue and throat convey information about the chemical composition of a soluble stimulus.

In Hindi
स्वाद; चखना
Synonyms
taste sensation, gustatory sensation, taste perception, gustatory perception, preference, penchant, predilection, appreciation, discernment, perceptiveness
Word family
tasty, tasting, taster

Taste as a noun

  1. The sensation that results when taste buds in the tongue and throat convey information about the chemical composition of a soluble stimulus.

    “The candy left him with a bad taste.” “The melon had a delicious taste.”

    Synonyms: taste sensation, gustatory sensation, taste perception, gustatory perception · A kind of: sensation · Kinds: relish, sweet, sour, bitter, salt, astringency, flatness, mellowness

  2. A strong liking.

    “My own preference is for good literature.” “The Irish have a penchant for blarney.”

    Synonyms: preference, penchant, predilection · A kind of: liking · Kinds: acquired taste, weakness

  3. Delicate discrimination (especially of aesthetic values).

    “Arrogance and lack of taste contributed to his rapid success.” “To ask at that particular time was the ultimate in bad taste.”

    Synonyms: appreciation, discernment, perceptiveness · A kind of: discrimination · Kinds: virtu, vogue, delicacy, culture

  4. A brief experience of something.

    “He got a taste of life on the wild side.” “She enjoyed her brief taste of independence.”

    A kind of: experience

  5. The faculty of distinguishing sweet, sour, bitter, and salty properties in the mouth.

    “His cold deprived him of his sense of taste.”

    Synonyms: gustation, sense of taste, gustatory modality · A kind of: modality, exteroception

  6. A kind of sensing; distinguishing substances by means of the taste buds.

    Synonyms: tasting · A kind of: sensing

Taste as a verb

  1. Have flavor; taste of something.

    Synonyms: savor, savour · Kinds: smack

  2. Perceive by the sense of taste.

    “Can you taste the garlic?”

    A kind of: perceive · Kinds: savor

  3. Take a sample of.

    “Try these new crackers.” “Sample the regional dishes.”

    Synonyms: sample, try, try out · A kind of: consume · Kinds: degust

  4. Have a distinctive or characteristic taste.

    “This tastes of nutmeg.”

    Synonyms: smack · A kind of: taste

  5. Distinguish flavors.

    “We tasted wines last night.”

    A kind of: identify

  6. Experience briefly.

    A kind of: know

Phrases and compounds with "taste"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • taste bud (noun) An oval sensory end organ on the surface of the tongue.
  • taste cell (noun) An epithelial cell in a taste bud that activates sensory fibers of the facial nerve or the glossopharyngeal nerve or the vagus nerve.
  • taste-maker (noun) Someone who popularizes a new fashion.
  • change taste (verb) Alter the flavor of.
  • taste tester (noun) Someone who samples food or drink for its quality.
  • taste-tester (noun) Someone who samples food or drink for its quality.
  • in good taste (adjective) Satisfying generally accepted social or esthetic standards.
  • acquired taste (noun) A preference that is only acquired after considerable experience.
  • sense of taste (noun) The faculty of distinguishing sweet, sour, bitter, and salty properties in the mouth.
  • taste property (noun) A property appreciated via the sense of taste.
  • taste sensation (noun) The sensation that results when taste buds in the tongue and throat convey information about the chemical composition of a soluble stimulus.
  • taste perception (noun) The sensation that results when taste buds in the tongue and throat convey information about the chemical composition of a soluble stimulus.
