Test: meaning, synonyms and examples

Test is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Put to the test, as for its quality, or give experimental use to.

In Hindi
परीक्षा, जाँच
Synonyms
prove, try, try out, examine, essay, screen, quiz, trial, trial run, tryout
Word family
testing, testee, tester

Test as a verb

  1. Put to the test, as for its quality, or give experimental use to.

    “This approach has been tried with good results.” “Test this recipe.”

    Synonyms: prove, try, try out, examine, essay · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: control, float, field-test

  2. Test or examine for the presence of disease or infection.

    “Screen the blood for the HIV virus.”

    Synonyms: screen · A kind of: check

  3. Examine someone's knowledge of something.

    “The teacher tests us every week.” “We got quizzed on French irregular verbs.”

    Synonyms: quiz · A kind of: examine

  4. Show a certain characteristic when tested.

    “He tested positive for HIV.”

    A kind of: be

  5. Achieve a certain score or rating on a test.

    “She tested high on the LSAT and was admitted to all the good law schools.”

    A kind of: score

  6. Determine the presence or properties of (a substance).

    A kind of: determine

  7. Undergo a test.

    “She doesn't test well.”

    A kind of: take

Test as a noun

  1. Trying something to find out about it.

    “A sample for ten days free trial.” “A trial of progesterone failed to relieve the pain.”

    Synonyms: trial, trial run, tryout · A kind of: experiment · Kinds: field trial, alpha test, beta test, road test, trial balloon

  2. Any standardized procedure for measuring sensitivity or memory or intelligence or aptitude or personality etc.

    “The test was standardized on a large sample of students.”

    Synonyms: mental test, mental testing, psychometric test · A kind of: mental measurement · Kinds: intelligence test, personality test, sub-test

  3. A set of questions or exercises evaluating skill or knowledge.

    “When the test was stolen the professor had to make a new set of questions.”

    Synonyms: examination, exam · A kind of: communication · Kinds: bar examination, comprehensive examination, entrance examination, final examination, litmus test, midterm examination, oral, preliminary examination

  4. The act of undergoing testing.

    “He survived the great test of battle.” “Candidates must compete in a trial of skill.”

    Synonyms: trial · A kind of: attempt

  5. The act of testing something.

    “In the experimental trials the amount of carbon was measured separately.” “He called each flip of the coin a new trial.”

    Synonyms: trial, run · A kind of: attempt · Kinds: double blind, audition, field trial, fitting, pilot project

  6. A hard outer covering as of some amoebas and sea urchins.

    A kind of: covering

Phrases and compounds with "test"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • iq test (noun) A psychometric test of intelligence.
  • mot test (noun) A compulsory annual test of older motor vehicles for safety and exhaust fumes.
  • pap test (noun) A method of examining stained cells in a cervical smear for early diagnosis of uterine cancer.
  • pku test (noun) A test of newborn infants for phenylketonuria.
  • sub-test (noun) One of a battery of related tests.
  • test ban (noun) A ban on the testing of nuclear weapons that is mutually agreed to by countries that possess nuclear weapons.
  • test bed (noun) A place equipped with instruments for testing (e.g. engines or machinery or computer programs etc.) under working conditions.
  • test fly (verb) Test a plane.
  • acid test (noun) A rigorous or crucial appraisal.
  • beta test (noun) (computer science) a second test of an experimental product (such as computer software) carried out by an outside organization.
  • dick test (noun) A skin test to determine your susceptibility to scarlet fever.
  • road test (noun) A test to insure that a vehicle is roadworthy.
  • skin test (noun) Any test to determine immunity or sensitivity to a disease by introducing small amounts on or into the skin.
  • test case (noun) A representative legal action whose outcome is likely to become a precedent.
  • test copy (noun) (printing) an impression made to check for errors.
  • test room (noun) A room in which tests are conducted.
