Thin: meaning, synonyms and examples

Thin is used as an adjective (8 senses), verb (4 senses) and adverb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Of relatively small extent from one surface to the opposite or in cross section.

In Hindi
पतला
Synonyms
lean, slender, sparse, flimsy, fragile, slight, tenuous, dilute, thin out, reduce
Opposites
thick, fat, full, thicken
Word family
thinness, thinner, thinning

Thin as an adjective

  1. Of relatively small extent from one surface to the opposite or in cross section.

    “Thin wire.” “A thin chiffon blouse.” “A thin book.”

    Opposite: thick · Similar: bladed, capillary, compressed, depressed, diaphanous, filamentous

  2. Lacking excess flesh.

    “You can't be too rich or too thin.” “Yon Cassius has a lean and hungry look.”

    Synonyms: lean · Opposite: fat · Similar: anorexic, bony, deep-eyed, gangling, lank, rawboned

  3. Very narrow.

    “A thin line across the page.”

    Synonyms: slender · Similar: narrow

  4. Not dense.

    “A thin beard.” “Trees were sparse.”

    Synonyms: sparse · Similar: distributed

  5. Relatively thin in consistency or low in density; not viscous.

    “Air is thin at high altitudes.” “A thin soup.” “Skimmed milk is much thinner than whole milk.”

    Opposite: thick · Similar: tenuous, rare

  6. (of sound) lacking resonance or volume.

    “A thin feeble cry.”

    Opposite: full · Similar: pale

  7. Lacking spirit or sincere effort.

    “A thin smile.”

    Similar: spiritless

  8. Lacking substance or significance.

    “Slight evidence.” “A tenuous argument.” “A thin plot.”

    Synonyms: flimsy, fragile, slight, tenuous · Similar: insignificant

Thin as a verb

  1. Lose thickness; become thin or thinner.

    Opposite: thicken · A kind of: change state

  2. Make thin or thinner.

    “Thin the solution.”

    Opposite: thicken · A kind of: reduce · Kinds: draw

  3. Lessen the strength or flavor of a solution or mixture.

    “Cut bourbon.”

    Synonyms: dilute, thin out, reduce, cut · A kind of: weaken · Kinds: water down

  4. Take off weight.

    Synonyms: reduce, melt off, lose weight, slim, slenderize, slim down · A kind of: change state · Kinds: sweat off

Thin as an adverb

  1. Without viscosity.

    “The blood was flowing thin.”

    Synonyms: thinly

Phrases and compounds with "thin"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • thin air (noun) Nowhere to be found in a giant void.
  • thin out (verb) Make sparse.
  • wear thin (verb) Deteriorate through use or stress.
  • paper thin (adjective) Thin as paper.
  • wafer-thin (adjective) Very thin.
  • thin person (noun) A person who is unusually thin and scrawny.
  • thin-bodied (adjective) Having a slim body.
  • thin-shelled (adjective) Of animals or plants that have a thin shell.
  • thin-skinned (adjective) Quick to take offense.
  • out of thin air (adverb) Without warning.
  • thin-shelled mussel (noun) Mussel with thin fragile shells having only rudimentary hinge teeth.
  • thin-leaved bilberry (noun) Erect blueberry of western United States having solitary flowers and somewhat sour berries.
  • thin-leaved stringybark (noun) Stringybark having white wood.
