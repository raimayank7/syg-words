Think: meaning, synonyms and examples

Think is used as a verb (13 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Judge or regard; look upon; judge.

In Hindi
सोचना
Synonyms
believe, consider, conceive, opine, suppose, imagine, reckon, guess, cogitate, cerebrate
Word family
thought, thinker, thinking

Think as a verb

  1. Judge or regard; look upon; judge.

    “I think he is very smart.” “I believe her to be very smart.” “I think that he is her boyfriend.”

    Synonyms: believe, consider, conceive · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: hold, rethink, think, think of, feel, see

  2. Expect, believe, or suppose.

    “I imagine she earned a lot of money with her new novel.” “I thought to find her in a bad state.” “He didn't think to find her in the kitchen.”

    Synonyms: opine, suppose, imagine, reckon, guess · A kind of: expect · Kinds: suspect

  3. Use or exercise the mind or one's power of reason in order to make inferences, decisions, or arrive at a solution or judgments.

    “I've been thinking all day and getting nowhere.”

    Synonyms: cogitate, cerebrate · Kinds: puzzle over, rationalize, think, philosophize, brainstorm, chew over, reason, reason

  4. Recall knowledge from memory; have a recollection.

    “I can't remember saying any such thing.” “I can't think what her last name was.” “Can you remember her phone number?”

    Synonyms: remember, retrieve, recall, call back, call up, recollect · Kinds: know, recognize, review

  5. Imagine or visualize.

    “Just think--you could be rich one day!” “Think what a scene it must have been!”

    A kind of: imagine

  6. Focus one's attention on a certain state.

    “Think big.” “Think thin.”

    A kind of: concentrate

  7. Have in mind as a purpose.

    “I mean no harm.” “I only meant to help you.” “She didn't think to harm me.”

    Synonyms: intend, mean · Kinds: design, plan, aim

  8. Decide by pondering, reasoning, or reflecting.

    “Can you think what to do next?”

    A kind of: think

  9. Ponder; reflect on, or reason about.

    “Think the matter through.” “Think how hard life in Russia must be these days.”

    A kind of: think

  10. Dispose the mind in a certain way.

    “Do you really think so?”

    A kind of: think

  11. Have or formulate in the mind.

    “Think good thoughts.”

    A kind of: think

  12. Be capable of conscious thought.

    “Man is the only creature that thinks.”

  13. Bring into a given condition by mental preoccupation.

    “She thought herself into a state of panic over the final exam.”

    A kind of: change

Think as a noun

  1. An instance of deliberate thinking.

    “I need to give it a good think.”

    A kind of: deliberation

Phrases and compounds with "think"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • think of (verb) Keep in mind for attention or consideration.
  • think up (verb) Devise or invent.
  • think out (verb) Consider carefully and rationally.
  • think back (verb) Recapture the past; indulge in memories.
  • think over (verb) Reflect deeply on a subject.
  • think tank (noun) A company that does research for hire and issues reports on the implications.
  • think about (verb) Have on one's mind, think about actively.
  • think piece (noun) An article in a newspaper or magazine or journal that represents opinions and ideas and discussion rather than bare facts.
  • think twice (verb) Consider and reconsider carefully.
  • think factory (noun) A company that does research for hire and issues reports on the implications.
  • think the world of (verb) Esteem very highly.
