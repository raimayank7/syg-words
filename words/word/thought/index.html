Thought: meaning, synonyms and examples

Thought is used as a noun (4 senses). Its most common meaning is: The content of cognition; the main thing you are thinking about.

In Hindi
विचार
Synonyms
idea, thinking, thought process, cerebration, intellection, mentation, opinion, sentiment, persuasion, view
Word family
think

Thought as a noun

  1. The content of cognition; the main thing you are thinking about.

    “It was not a good idea.” “The thought never entered my mind.”

    Synonyms: idea · A kind of: content · Kinds: inspiration, cogitation, concept, preoccupation, misconception, plan, figment, generalization

  2. The process of using your mind to consider something carefully.

    “Thinking always made him frown.” “She paused for thought.”

    Synonyms: thinking, thought process, cerebration, intellection, mentation · A kind of: higher cognitive process · Kinds: free association, construction, reasoning, line of thought, train of thought, mysticism, ideation, consideration

  3. The organized beliefs of a period or group or individual.

    “19th century thought.” “Darwinian thought.”

    A kind of: belief · Kinds: mainstream

  4. A personal belief or judgment that is not founded on proof or certainty.

    “My opinion differs from yours.” “I am not of your persuasion.” “What are your thoughts on Haiti?”

    Synonyms: opinion, sentiment, persuasion, view · A kind of: belief · Kinds: idea, judgment, eyes, preconception, pole, politics

Phrases and compounds with "thought"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • free thought (noun) The form of theological rationalism that believes in God on the basis of reason without reference to revelation.
  • thought-image (noun) A mental image produced by the imagination.
  • second thought (noun) Thinking again about a choice previously made.
  • thought-reader (noun) Someone with the power of communicating thoughts directly.
  • deep in thought (adjective) Deeply absorbed in thought.
  • line of thought (noun) A particular way of thinking that is characteristic of some individual or group.
  • thought process (noun) The process of using your mind to consider something carefully.
  • well-thought-of (adjective) Receiving deferential regard.
  • abstract thought (noun) Thinking that is coherent and logical.
  • food for thought (noun) Anything that provides mental stimulus for thinking.
  • train of thought (noun) The connections that link the various parts of an event or argument together.
  • well thought out (adjective) Resulting from careful thought.
  • school of thought (noun) A belief (or system of beliefs) accepted as authoritative by some group or school.
  • thought-provoking (adjective) Stimulating interest or thought.
  • freedom of thought (noun) The right to hold unpopular ideas.
  • thought transference (noun) Apparent communication from one mind to another without using sensory perceptions.
