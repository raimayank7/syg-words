Tie: meaning, synonyms and examples

Tie is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (9 senses). Its most common meaning is: Fasten or secure with a rope, string, or cord.

In Hindi
बाँधना; टाई
Synonyms
bind, draw, connect, link, link up, attach, bond, marry, wed, splice
Opposites
untie
Word family
tier, tying

Tie as a verb

  1. Fasten or secure with a rope, string, or cord.

    “They tied their victim to the chair.”

    Synonyms: bind · Opposite: untie · A kind of: fasten · Kinds: gag, retie, tie up, loop, cord, lash together, truss, rope

  2. Finish a game with an equal number of points, goals, etc.

    “The teams drew a tie.”

    Synonyms: draw · A kind of: equal

  3. Limit or restrict to.

    “I am tied to UNIX.” “These big jets are tied to large airports.”

    A kind of: restrict

  4. Connect, fasten, or put together two or more pieces.

    “Can you connect the two loudspeakers?” “Tie the ropes together.” “Link arms.”

    Synonyms: connect, link, link up · Kinds: join, ground, join, attach, daisy-chain, tie, interconnect, tee

  5. Form a knot or bow in.

    “Tie a necktie.”

    A kind of: shape

  6. Create social or emotional ties.

    “The grandparents want to bond with the child.”

    Synonyms: bind, attach, bond · A kind of: relate · Kinds: fixate, befriend

  7. Perform a marriage ceremony.

    “The minister married us on Saturday.” “We were wed the following week.” “The couple got spliced on Hawaii.”

    Synonyms: marry, wed, splice · A kind of: officiate · Kinds: solemnize

  8. Make by tying pieces together.

    “The fishermen tied their flies.”

    A kind of: fashion

  9. Unite musical notes by a tie.

    A kind of: connect

Tie as a noun

  1. Neckwear consisting of a long narrow piece of material worn (mostly by men) under a collar and tied in knot at the front.

    “He stood in front of the mirror tightening his necktie.” “He wore a vest and tie.”

    Synonyms: necktie · A kind of: neckwear · Kinds: bolo tie, bow tie, four-in-hand, old school tie, string tie

  2. A social or business relationship.

    “A valuable financial affiliation.” “He was sorry he had to sever his ties with other members of the team.” “Many close associations with England.”

    Synonyms: affiliation, association, tie-up · A kind of: relationship

  3. Equality of score in a contest.

    A kind of: equality · Kinds: deuce

  4. A horizontal beam used to prevent two other structural members from spreading apart or separating.

    “He nailed the rafters together with a tie beam.”

    Synonyms: tie beam · A kind of: beam

  5. A fastener that serves to join or connect.

    “The walls are held together with metal links placed in the wet mortar during construction.”

    Synonyms: link, linkup, tie-in · A kind of: fastener · Kinds: bridge

  6. The finish of a contest in which the score is tied and the winner is undecided.

    “The game ended in a draw.” “Their record was 3 wins, 6 losses and a tie.”

    Synonyms: draw, standoff · A kind of: finish · Kinds: dead heat, stalemate

  7. (music) a slur over two notes of the same pitch; indicates that the note is to be sustained for their combined time value.

    A kind of: slur

  8. One of the cross braces that support the rails on a railway track.

    “The British call a railroad tie a sleeper.”

    Synonyms: railroad tie, crosstie, sleeper · A kind of: brace

  9. A cord (or string or ribbon or wire etc.) with which something is tied.

    “He needed a tie for the packages.”

    A kind of: cord · Kinds: drawstring

Phrases and compounds with "tie"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • tie in (verb) Be in connection with something relevant.
  • tie up (verb) Secure with or as if with ropes.
  • tie-in (noun) A fastener that serves to join or connect.
  • tie-on (adjective) Fastened by tying on.
  • tie-up (noun) A social or business relationship.
  • bow tie (noun) A man's tie that ties in a bow.
  • bow-tie (noun) A man's tie that ties in a bow.
  • cup tie (noun) An eliminating game between teams in a cup competition.
  • hog-tie (verb) Tie together somebody's limbs.
  • tie rod (noun) Either of two rods that link the steering gear to the front wheels.
  • tie-dye (verb) Dye after knotting the fabric to produce an irregular pattern.
  • bola tie (noun) A cord fastened around the neck with an ornamental clasp and worn as a necktie.
  • bolo tie (noun) A cord fastened around the neck with an ornamental clasp and worn as a necktie.
  • tie beam (noun) A horizontal beam used to prevent two other structural members from spreading apart or separating.
  • tie clip (noun) A piece of jewelry that holds a man's tie in place.
  • tie down (verb) Secure with or as if with ropes.
