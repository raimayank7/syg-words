Time: meaning, synonyms and examples

Time is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: An instance or single occasion for some event.

In Hindi
समय, वक़्त
Synonyms
clip, clock time, fourth dimension, meter, metre, prison term, sentence, clock
Word family
timely, timer, timing

Time as a noun

  1. An instance or single occasion for some event.

    “This time he succeeded.” “He called four times.” “He could do ten at a clip.”

    Synonyms: clip · A kind of: case

  2. A period of time considered as a resource under your control and sufficient to accomplish something.

    “Take time to smell the roses.” “I didn't have time to finish.” “It took more than half my time.”

    A kind of: time period

  3. An indefinite period (usually marked by specific attributes or activities).

    “He waited a long time.” “The time of year for planting.” “He was a great actor in his time.”

    A kind of: time period · Kinds: day, dead, hard times, incarnation, wee, while, moment, ephemera

  4. A suitable moment.

    “It is time to go.”

    A kind of: moment · Kinds: high time, occasion

  5. The continuum of experience in which events pass from the future through the present to the past.

    A kind of: attribute · Kinds: geological time, biological time, cosmic time, civil time, daylight-saving time, present, past, future

  6. A person's experience on a particular occasion.

    “He had a time holding back the tears.” “They had a good time together.”

    A kind of: experience

  7. A reading of a point in time as given by a clock.

    “Do you know what time it is?” “The time is 10 o'clock.”

    Synonyms: clock time · A kind of: reading · Kinds: spacecraft clock time, prime time, hour

  8. The fourth coordinate that is required (along with three spatial dimensions) to specify a physical event.

    Synonyms: fourth dimension · A kind of: dimension

  9. Rhythm as given by division into parts of equal duration.

    Synonyms: meter, metre · A kind of: rhythmicity

  10. The period of time a prisoner is imprisoned.

    “He served a prison term of 15 months.” “His sentence was 5 to 10 years.” “He is doing time in the county jail.”

    Synonyms: prison term, sentence · A kind of: term · Kinds: hard time, life sentence

Time as a verb

  1. Measure the time or duration of an event or action or the person who performs an action in a certain period of time.

    “He clocked the runners.”

    Synonyms: clock · A kind of: quantify · Kinds: mistime

  2. Assign a time for an activity or event.

    “The candidate carefully timed his appearance at the disaster scene.”

    A kind of: schedule

  3. Set the speed, duration, or execution of.

    “We time the process to manufacture our cars very precisely.”

    A kind of: determine

  4. Regulate or set the time of.

    “Time the clock.”

    A kind of: adjust

  5. Adjust so that a force is applied and an action occurs at the desired time.

    “The good player times his swing so as to hit the ball squarely.”

    A kind of: adjust

Phrases and compounds with "time"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in time (adverb) Within an indefinite time or at an unspecified future time.
  • on time (adverb) At the expected time.
  • all-time (adjective) Unsurpassed in some respect up to the present.
  • big time (noun) The highest level of an occupation (especially in entertainment).
  • buy time (verb) Act so as to delay an event or action in order to gain an advantage.
  • old-time (adjective) Attractively old-fashioned (but not necessarily authentic).
  • one time (adverb) On one occasion.
  • one-time (adjective) Belonging to some prior time.
  • run-time (noun) The time at which a (software or multimedia) program is run.
  • time lag (noun) Time during which some action is awaited.
  • time off (noun) A time period when you are not required to work.
  • time out (noun) A pause from doing something (as work).
  • time-out (noun) A brief suspension of play.
  • two-time (verb) Carry on a romantic relationship with two people at the same time.
  • at a time (adverb) Simultaneously.
  • face time (noun) Work time spent at the location of or in the presence of other people.
