Tongue: meaning, synonyms and examples

Tongue is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: A mobile mass of muscular tissue covered with mucous membrane and located in the oral cavity.

In Hindi
जीभ; भाषा
Synonyms
lingua, glossa, clapper, natural language, knife, spit

Tongue as a noun

  1. A mobile mass of muscular tissue covered with mucous membrane and located in the oral cavity.

    Synonyms: lingua, glossa, clapper · A kind of: organ, articulator

  2. A human written or spoken language used by a community; opposed to e.g. a computer language.

    Synonyms: natural language · A kind of: language · Kinds: mother tongue, tone language, creole

  3. Any long thin projection that is transient.

    “Tongues of flame licked at the walls.” “Rifles exploded quick knives of fire into the dark.”

    Synonyms: knife · A kind of: projection

  4. A manner of speaking.

    “He spoke with a thick tongue.” “She has a glib tongue.”

    A kind of: manner of speaking · Kinds: sharp tongue

  5. A narrow strip of land that juts out into the sea.

    Synonyms: spit · A kind of: cape

  6. The tongue of certain animals used as meat.

    A kind of: variety meat · Kinds: beef tongue, calf's tongue

  7. The flap of material under the laces of a shoe or boot.

    A kind of: flap

  8. Metal striker that hangs inside a bell and makes a sound by hitting the side.

    Synonyms: clapper · A kind of: striker

Tongue as a verb

  1. Articulate by tonguing, as when playing wind instruments.

    A kind of: play · Kinds: double tongue

  2. Lick or explore with the tongue.

    A kind of: lick
