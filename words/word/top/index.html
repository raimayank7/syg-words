Top: meaning, synonyms and examples

Top is used as a noun (11 senses), adjective (1 sense) and verb (10 senses). Its most common meaning is: The upper part of anything.

In Hindi
चोटी, ऊपरी हिस्सा
Synonyms
top side, upper side, upside, peak, crown, crest, tip, summit, top of the inning, acme
Opposites
bottom, side
Word family
topper

Top as a noun

  1. The upper part of anything.

    “The mower cuts off the tops of the grass.” “The title should be written at the top of the first page.”

    A kind of: region · Kinds: capital, masthead, crown, head, crown

  2. The highest or uppermost side of anything.

    “Put your books on top of the desk.” “Only the top side of the box was painted.”

    Synonyms: top side, upper side, upside · A kind of: side · Kinds: crown, poll, pate, desktop, rooftop, tiptop

  3. The top or extreme point of something (usually a mountain or hill).

    “The view from the peak was magnificent.” “They clambered to the tip of Monadnock.” “The region is a few molecules wide at the summit.”

    Synonyms: peak, crown, crest, tip, summit · A kind of: topographic point · Kinds: hilltop, pinnacle, mountain peak

  4. The first half of an inning; while the visiting team is at bat.

    “A relief pitcher took over in the top of the fifth.”

    Synonyms: top of the inning · Opposite: bottom

  5. The highest level or degree attainable; the highest stage of development.

    “His landscapes were deemed the acme of beauty.” “The artist's gifts are at their acme.” “At the height of her career.”

    Synonyms: acme, height, elevation, peak, pinnacle, summit, superlative, meridian · A kind of: degree

  6. The greatest possible intensity.

    “He screamed at the top of his lungs.”

    A kind of: intensity

  7. Platform surrounding the head of a lower mast.

    A kind of: platform

  8. A conical child's plaything tapering to a steel point on which it can be made to spin.

    “He got a bright red top and string for his birthday.”

    Synonyms: whirligig, teetotum, spinning top · A kind of: plaything · Kinds: humming top, peg top, whipping top

  9. Covering for a hole (especially a hole in the top of a container).

    “He removed the top of the carton.” “He couldn't get the top off of the bottle.” “Put the cover back on the kettle.”

    Synonyms: cover · A kind of: covering · Kinds: cap, lid, manhole cover, screwtop

  10. A garment (especially for women) that extends from the shoulders to the waist or hips.

    “He stared as she buttoned her top.”

    A kind of: woman's clothing · Kinds: blouse, bodice, bustier, halter

  11. A canvas tent to house the audience at a circus performance.

    “He was afraid of a fire in the circus tent.” “They had the big top up in less than an hour.”

    Synonyms: circus tent, big top, round top · A kind of: canvas tent

Top as an adjective

  1. Situated at the top or highest position.

    “The top shelf.”

    Opposite: bottom, side · Similar: apical, crowning, topmost, upper

Top as a verb

  1. Be superior or better than some standard.

    “She exceeded our expectations.” “She topped her performance of last year.”

    Synonyms: exceed, transcend, overstep, pass, go past · A kind of: excel

  2. Pass by, over, or under without making contact.

    “The balloon cleared the tree tops.”

    Synonyms: clear · A kind of: pass · Kinds: hop

  3. Be at the top of or constitute the top or highest point.

    “A star tops the Christmas Tree.”

    A kind of: lie

  4. Be ahead of others; be the first.

    “She topped her class every year.”

    Synonyms: lead

  5. Provide with a top or finish the top (of a structure).

    “The towers were topped with conical roofs.”

    Synonyms: top out · A kind of: supply

  6. Reach or ascend the top of.

    “The hikers topped the mountain just before noon.”

    A kind of: reach

  7. Strike (the top part of a ball in golf, baseball, or pool) giving it a forward spin.

    A kind of: hit

  8. Cut the top off.

    “Top trees and bushes.”

    Synonyms: pinch · A kind of: snip · Kinds: tail

  9. Be the culminating event.

    “The speech crowned the meeting.”

    Synonyms: crown · A kind of: culminate

  10. Finish up or conclude.

    “They topped off their dinner with a cognac.” “Top the evening with champagne.”

    Synonyms: top off · A kind of: complete

Phrases and compounds with "top"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • top-up (noun) An amount needed to restore something to its former level.
  • big top (noun) A canvas tent to house the audience at a circus performance.
  • peg top (noun) A pear-shaped top made of wood with a metal center pin.
  • tip-top (adverb) To the highest extent.
  • top dog (noun) A person who is in charge.
  • top hat (noun) A man's hat with a tall crown; usually covered with silk or with beaver fur.
  • top off (verb) Finish up or conclude.
  • top out (verb) Give up one's career just as one becomes very successful.
  • flat-top (adjective) Having a flat or flattened upper surface.
  • high-top (adjective) (of shoes or boots) having relatively high uppers.
  • tank top (noun) A tight-fitting sleeveless shirt with wide shoulder straps and low neck and no front opening; often worn over a shirt or blouse.
  • top boot (noun) A boot reaching halfway up to the knee.
  • top lift (noun) The bottom layer of a heel.
  • top side (noun) The highest or uppermost side of anything.
  • top-down (adjective) Of an approach to a problem that begins at the highest conceptual level and works down to the details.
  • top-hole (adjective) Excellent; best possible.
