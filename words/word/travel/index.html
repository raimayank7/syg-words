Travel: meaning, synonyms and examples

Travel is used as a verb (6 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: Change location; move, travel, or proceed, also metaphorically.

In Hindi
यात्रा करना
Synonyms
go, move, locomote, journey, trip, jaunt, move around, traveling, travelling, change of location
Opposites
stay in place
Word family
traveller, traveler, travelling, traveling

Travel as a verb

  1. Change location; move, travel, or proceed, also metaphorically.

    “How fast does your new car go?” “We travelled from Rome to Naples by bus.” “The policemen went from door to door looking for the suspect.”

    Synonyms: go, move, locomote · Opposite: stay in place · Kinds: go around, carry, ease, whish, float, swap, seek, whine

  2. Undertake a journey or trip.

    Synonyms: journey · A kind of: travel · Kinds: tour, globe-trot, sledge, voyage, trek, trek

  3. Make a trip for pleasure.

    Synonyms: trip, jaunt · Kinds: junketeer, travel to, travel, ply, commute, peregrinate

  4. Travel upon or across.

    “Travel the oceans.”

    Synonyms: journey · A kind of: travel · Kinds: sail, ship, ride, fly, cruise

  5. Undergo transportation as in a vehicle.

    “We travelled North on Rte. 508.”

    A kind of: travel · Kinds: fly, hop, ride

  6. Travel from place to place, as for the purpose of finding work, preaching, or acting as a judge.

    Synonyms: move around · A kind of: travel · Kinds: itinerate

Travel as a noun

  1. The act of going from one place to another.

    “He enjoyed selling but he hated the travel.”

    Synonyms: traveling, travelling · A kind of: motion · Kinds: walk, circumnavigation, peregrination, traversal, wandering, wayfaring, crossing, driving

  2. A movement through space that changes the location of something.

    Synonyms: change of location · A kind of: movement · Kinds: ascension, circulation, creep, gravitation, levitation, descent, entrance, fall

  3. Self-propelled movement.

    Synonyms: locomotion · A kind of: motion · Kinds: brachiation, walk, step, gait, run, jog, crawl, lap

Phrases and compounds with "travel"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • travel by (verb) Move past.
  • travel to (verb) Go to certain places as for sightseeing.
  • air travel (noun) Travel via aircraft.
  • travel kit (noun) A kit for carrying toilet articles while traveling.
  • travel iron (noun) A small lightweight iron that can be carried while traveling.
  • travel plan (noun) A proposed route of travel.
  • travel time (noun) A period of time spent traveling.
  • travel-worn (adjective) Tired by travel.
  • space travel (noun) A voyage outside the Earth's atmosphere.
  • travel agent (noun) Someone who sells or arranges trips or tours for customers.
  • travel along (verb) Travel along a certain course.
  • water travel (noun) Travel by water.
  • travel agency (noun) An agency that arranges personal travel.
  • travel-soiled (adjective) Soiled from travel.
  • travel bargain (noun) A bargain rate for travellers on commercial routes (usually air routes).
  • travel expense (noun) (frequently plural) expenses incurred by an employee in the performance of the job and usually reimbursed by the employer.
