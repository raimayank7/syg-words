Try: meaning, synonyms and examples

Try is used as a verb (9 senses) and noun (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Make an effort or attempt.

In Hindi
कोशिश करना
Synonyms
seek, attempt, essay, assay, test, prove, try out, examine, judge, adjudicate
Word family
trier, trial

Try as a verb

  1. Make an effort or attempt.

    “He tried to shake off his fears.” “The infant had essayed a few wobbly steps.” “The police attempted to stop the thief.”

    Synonyms: seek, attempt, essay, assay · A kind of: act · Kinds: take a dare, fight, have a go, grope, endeavor, give it a whirl, risk

  2. Put to the test, as for its quality, or give experimental use to.

    “This approach has been tried with good results.” “Test this recipe.”

    Synonyms: test, prove, try out, examine, essay · A kind of: evaluate · Kinds: control, float, field-test

  3. Put on trial or hear a case and sit as the judge at the trial of.

    “The judge tried both father and son in separate trials.”

    Synonyms: judge, adjudicate · A kind of: decide · Kinds: court-martial

  4. Take a sample of.

    “Try these new crackers.” “Sample the regional dishes.”

    Synonyms: sample, try out, taste · A kind of: consume · Kinds: degust

  5. Examine or hear (evidence or a case) by judicial process.

    “The jury had heard all the evidence.” “The case will be tried in California.”

    Synonyms: hear · A kind of: probe · Kinds: rehear

  6. Give pain or trouble to.

    “I've been sorely tried by these students.”

    A kind of: pain

  7. Test the limits of.

    “You are trying my patience!”

    Synonyms: strain, stress · A kind of: afflict · Kinds: rack

  8. Melt (fat or lard) in order to separate out impurities.

    “Try the yak butter.” “Render fat in a casserole.”

    Synonyms: render · A kind of: melt

  9. Put on a garment in order to see whether it fits and looks nice.

    “Try on this sweater to see how it looks.”

    Synonyms: try on · A kind of: wear

Try as a noun

  1. Earnest and conscientious activity intended to do or accomplish something.

    “Made an effort to cover all the reading material.” “Wished him luck in his endeavor.” “She gave it a good try.”

    Synonyms: attempt, effort, endeavor, endeavour · A kind of: activity · Kinds: batting, best, worst, bid, crack, essay, foray, contribution

Phrases and compounds with "try"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • try on (verb) Put on a garment in order to see whether it fits and looks nice.
  • try-on (noun) Putting clothes on to see whether they fit.
  • try for (verb) Make an attempt at achieving something.
  • try out (verb) Put to the test, as for its quality, or give experimental use to.
  • try square (noun) A square having a metal ruler set at right angles to another straight piece.
  • give it a try (verb) Try.
