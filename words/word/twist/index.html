Twist: meaning, synonyms and examples

Twist is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (13 senses). Its most common meaning is: To move in a twisting or contorted motion, (especially when struggling).

In Hindi
मरोड़ना; मोड़
Synonyms
writhe, wrestle, wriggle, worm, squirm, flex, bend, deform, turn, twine
Opposites
untwist
Word family
twister, twisting

Twist as a verb

  1. To move in a twisting or contorted motion, (especially when struggling).

    “The prisoner writhed in discomfort.” “The child tried to wriggle free from his aunt's embrace.”

    Synonyms: writhe, wrestle, wriggle, worm, squirm · A kind of: move · Kinds: wrench

  2. Cause (a plastic object) to assume a crooked or angular form.

    “Bend the rod.” “Twist the dough into a braid.” “The strong man could turn an iron bar.”

    Synonyms: flex, bend, deform, turn · A kind of: change shape · Kinds: indent, incurvate, gnarl, crank, convolve

  3. Turn in the opposite direction.

    “Twist one's head.”

    A kind of: turn · Kinds: crick, quirk, curl

  4. Form into a spiral shape.

    “The cord is all twisted.”

    Synonyms: twine, distort · Opposite: untwist · A kind of: change shape · Kinds: wring, contort, entangle, intertwine, spin, weave

  5. Form into twists.

    “Twist the strips of dough.”

    A kind of: shape

  6. Extend in curves and turns.

    “The road winds around the lake.” “The path twisted through the forest.”

    Synonyms: wind, curve · A kind of: be · Kinds: circumvolute, spiral, snake

  7. Do the twist.

    A kind of: dance

  8. Twist or pull violently or suddenly, especially so as to remove (something) from that to which it is attached or from where it originates.

    “Wrench a window off its hinges.” “Wrench oneself free from somebody's grip.” “A deep sigh was wrenched from his chest.”

    Synonyms: wrench · A kind of: pull

  9. Practice sophistry; change the meaning of or be vague about in order to mislead or deceive.

    “Don't twist my words.”

    Synonyms: twist around, pervert, convolute, sophisticate · A kind of: denote

  10. Twist suddenly so as to sprain.

    “Wrench one's ankle.” “The wrestler twisted his shoulder.” “The hikers sprained their ankles when they fell.”

    Synonyms: sprain, wrench, turn, wrick, rick · A kind of: injure

Twist as a noun

  1. An unforeseen development.

    “Events suddenly took an awkward turn.”

    Synonyms: turn, turn of events · A kind of: development

  2. An interpretation of a text or action.

    “They put an unsympathetic construction on his conduct.”

    Synonyms: construction · A kind of: interpretation

  3. Any clever maneuver.

    “He would stoop to any device to win a point.” “It was a great sales gimmick.” “A cheap promotions gimmick for greedy businessmen.”

    Synonyms: device, gimmick · A kind of: maneuver · Kinds: mnemonic, trick

  4. The act of rotating rapidly.

    “He gave the crank a spin.” “It broke off after much twisting.”

    Synonyms: spin, twirl, twisting, whirl · A kind of: rotation · Kinds: pirouette, birling

  5. A sharp strain on muscles or ligaments.

    “The wrench to his knee occurred as he fell.” “He was sidelined with a hamstring pull.”

    Synonyms: wrench, pull · A kind of: injury · Kinds: sprain

  6. A sharp bend in a line produced when a line having a loop is pulled tight.

    Synonyms: kink, twirl · A kind of: fold

  7. A circular segment of a curve.

    “A bend in the road.” “A crook in the path.”

    Synonyms: bend, crook, turn · A kind of: curve · Kinds: bight

  8. A miniature whirlpool or whirlwind resulting when the current of a fluid doubles back on itself.

    Synonyms: eddy · A kind of: current

  9. A jerky pulling movement.

    Synonyms: wrench · A kind of: movement

  10. A hairdo formed by braiding or twisting the hair.

    Synonyms: braid, plait, tress · A kind of: hairdo · Kinds: queue, pigtail

  11. Social dancing in which couples vigorously twist their hips and arms in time to the music; was popular in the 1960s.

    “They liked to dance the twist.”

    A kind of: social dancing

  12. The act of winding or twisting.

    “He put the key in the old clock and gave it a good wind.”

    Synonyms: wind, winding · A kind of: rotation

  13. Turning or twisting around (in place).

    “With a quick twist of his head he surveyed the room.”

    Synonyms: turn · A kind of: rotation · Kinds: twiddle

Phrases and compounds with "twist"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • twist wood (noun) Vigorous deciduous European treelike shrub common along waysides; red berries turn black.
