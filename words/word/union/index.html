Union: meaning, synonyms and examples

Union is used as a noun (11 senses) and adjective (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: An organization of employees formed to bargain with the employer.

In Hindi
संघ, मेल
Synonyms
labor union, trade union, trades union, brotherhood, coupling, mating, pairing, conjugation, unification, marriage
Opposites
separation, disunion, nonunion
Word family
unionize, unite

Union as a noun

  1. An organization of employees formed to bargain with the employer.

    “You have to join the union in order to get a job.”

    Synonyms: labor union, trade union, trades union, brotherhood · A kind of: organization · Kinds: industrial union, craft union, company union

  2. The United States (especially the northern states during the American Civil War).

    “He has visited every state in the Union.” “Lee hoped to detach Maryland from the Union.” “The North's superior resources turned the scale.”

  3. The act of pairing a male and female for reproductive purposes.

    “The casual couplings of adolescents.” “The mating of some species occurs only in the spring.”

    Synonyms: coupling, mating, pairing, conjugation · Kinds: assortative mating, disassortative mating, hybridization, inbreeding, servicing

  4. The state of being joined or united or linked.

    “There is strength in union.”

    Synonyms: unification · Opposite: separation · A kind of: state · Kinds: coalition, connection, junction, association, marriage, syncretism

  5. The state of being a married couple voluntarily joined for life (or until divorce).

    “A long and happy marriage.” “God bless this union.”

    Synonyms: marriage, matrimony, spousal relationship, wedlock · A kind of: marital status · Kinds: bigamy, common-law marriage, endogamy, exogamy, marriage of convenience, misalliance, monandry, monogamy

  6. Healing process involving the growing together of the edges of a wound or the growing together of broken bones.

    Synonyms: conglutination · A kind of: healing

  7. A political unit formed from previously independent people or organizations.

    “The Soviet Union.”

    A kind of: political unit · Kinds: confederation, enosis, league, customs union

  8. A set containing all and only the members of two or more given sets.

    “Let C be the union of the sets A and B.”

    Synonyms: sum, join · A kind of: set · Kinds: direct sum

  9. The occurrence of a uniting of separate parts.

    “Lightning produced an unusual union of the metals.”

    A kind of: happening · Kinds: fusion, combining

  10. A device on a national flag emblematic of the union of two or more sovereignties (typically in the upper inner corner).

    A kind of: device

  11. The act of making or becoming a single unit.

    “The union of opposing factions.” “He looked forward to the unification of his family for the holidays.”

    Synonyms: unification, uniting, conjugation, jointure · Opposite: disunion · A kind of: combination · Kinds: coalescence, reunion, tribalization, umbrella

Union as an adjective

  1. Being of or having to do with the northern United States and those loyal to the Union during the American Civil War.

    “Union soldiers.” “Federal forces.” “A Federal infantryman.”

    Similar: northern

  2. Of trade unions.

    “The union movement.” “Union negotiations.” “A union-shop clause in the contract.”

    Opposite: nonunion · Similar: closed, organized

Phrases and compounds with "union"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • union army (noun) The northern army during the American Civil War.
  • union card (noun) A card certifying membership in a labor union.
  • union flag (noun) National flag of the United Kingdom.
  • union jack (noun) National flag of the United Kingdom.
  • union shop (noun) A company allowed to hire nonunion workers on the condition that they will join the union within a specified time.
  • union suit (noun) An undergarment with shirt and drawers in one piece.
  • craft union (noun) A labor union whose membership is restricted to workers in a particular craft.
  • labor union (noun) An organization of employees formed to bargain with the employer.
  • trade union (noun) An organization of employees formed to bargain with the employer.
  • cooper union (noun) University founded in 1859 by Peter Cooper to offer free courses in the arts and sciences.
  • credit union (noun) A cooperative depository financial institution whose members can obtain loans from their combined savings.
  • soviet union (noun) A former communist country in eastern Europe and northern Asia; established in 1922; included Russia and 14 other soviet socialist republics (Ukraine and Byelorussia and others); officially dissolved 31 December 1991.
  • trades union (noun) An organization of employees formed to bargain with the employer.
  • union member (noun) A worker who belongs to a trade union.
  • company union (noun) A union of workers for a single company; a union not affiliated with a larger union.
  • customs union (noun) An association of nations to promote free trade within the union and set common tariffs for nations that are not members.
