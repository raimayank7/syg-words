Upper: meaning, synonyms and examples

Upper is used as an adjective (3 senses) and noun (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: The topmost one of two.

In Hindi
ऊपरी
Synonyms
upper berth, amphetamine, pep pill, speed

Upper as an adjective

  1. The topmost one of two.

    Similar: top

  2. Higher in place or position.

    “The upper bunk.” “In the upper center of the picture.” “The upper stories.”

    Similar: high

  3. Superior in rank or accomplishment.

    “The upper half of the class.”

    Similar: superior

Upper as a noun

  1. The higher of two berths.

    Synonyms: upper berth · A kind of: berth

  2. Piece of leather or synthetic material that forms the part of a shoe or boot above the sole that encases the foot.

    “Uppers come in many styles.”

    A kind of: piece of leather · Kinds: toe box

  3. A central nervous system stimulant that increases energy and decreases appetite; used to treat narcolepsy and some forms of depression.

    Synonyms: amphetamine, pep pill, speed · Kinds: amphetamine sulfate, dextroamphetamine sulphate, methamphetamine
