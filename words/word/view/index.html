View: meaning, synonyms and examples

View is used as a noun (10 senses) and verb (3 senses). Its most common meaning is: A way of regarding situations or topics etc.

In Hindi
दृश्य; राय
Synonyms
position, perspective, aspect, prospect, scene, vista, panorama, survey, sight, eyeshot
Word family
viewer, viewable

View as a noun

  1. A way of regarding situations or topics etc.

    “Consider what follows from the positivist view.”

    Synonyms: position, perspective · A kind of: orientation · Kinds: bird's eye view, futurism, vanguard, paradigm, light, sight, straddle

  2. The visual percept of a region.

    “The most desirable feature of the park are the beautiful views.”

    Synonyms: aspect, prospect, scene, vista, panorama · A kind of: visual percept · Kinds: background, coast, exposure, foreground, glimpse, middle distance, side view, tableau

  3. The act of looking or seeing or observing.

    “He tried to get a better view of it.” “His survey of the battlefield was limited.”

    Synonyms: survey, sight · A kind of: look · Kinds: eyeful

  4. The range of the eye.

    “They were soon out of view.”

    Synonyms: eyeshot · A kind of: range

  5. A personal belief or judgment that is not founded on proof or certainty.

    “My opinion differs from yours.” “I am not of your persuasion.” “What are your thoughts on Haiti?”

    Synonyms: opinion, sentiment, persuasion, thought · A kind of: belief · Kinds: idea, judgment, eyes, preconception, pole, politics

  6. A message expressing a belief about something; the expression of a belief that is held with confidence but not substantiated by positive knowledge or proof.

    “His opinions appeared frequently on the editorial page.”

    Synonyms: opinion · A kind of: message · Kinds: adverse opinion, guess, side

  7. Purpose; the phrase `with a view to' means `with the intention of' or `for the purpose of'.

    “He took the computer with a view to pawning it.”

    A kind of: purpose

  8. Graphic art consisting of the graphic or photographic representation of a visual percept.

    “He painted scenes from everyday life.” “Figure 2 shows photographic and schematic views of the equipment.”

    Synonyms: scene · A kind of: graphic art

  9. The range of interest or activity that can be anticipated.

    “It is beyond the horizon of present knowledge.”

    Synonyms: horizon, purview · A kind of: scope

  10. Outward appearance.

    “They look the same in outward view.”

    A kind of: appearance

View as a verb

  1. Deem to be.

    “She views this quite differently from me.” “I consider her to be shallow.” “I don't see the situation quite as negatively as you do.”

    Synonyms: see, consider, reckon, regard · A kind of: think · Kinds: expect, receive, reconsider, reconsider, include, consider, call, like

  2. Look at carefully; study mentally.

    “View a problem.”

    Synonyms: consider, look at · A kind of: analyze · Kinds: contemplate, groak

  3. See or watch.

    “View a show on television.” “This program will be seen all over the world.” “View an exhibition.”

    Synonyms: watch, see, catch, take in · A kind of: watch · Kinds: visualize, spectate, preview

Phrases and compounds with "view"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • view as (verb) Keep in mind or convey as a conviction or view.
  • side view (noun) A view from the side of something.
  • view angle (noun) The angle included by a photographic lens.
  • world view (noun) A comprehensive view of the world and human life.
  • out of view (adverb) No longer visible.
  • view finder (noun) Optical device that helps a user to find the target of interest.
  • angle of view (noun) The angle included by a photographic lens.
  • field of view (noun) The area that is visible (as through an optical instrument).
  • point of view (noun) A mental position from which things are viewed.
  • panoramic view (noun) A situation or topic as if viewed from an altitude or distance.
  • bird's eye view (noun) A situation or topic as if viewed from an altitude or distance.
