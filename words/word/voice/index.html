Voice: meaning, synonyms and examples

Voice is used as a noun (11 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The distinctive quality or pitch or condition of a person's speech.

In Hindi
आवाज़
Synonyms
vocalization, vocalisation, vocalism, phonation, vox, articulation, spokesperson, interpreter, representative, part
Opposites
devoice
Word family
voicer

Voice as a noun

  1. The distinctive quality or pitch or condition of a person's speech.

    “A shrill voice sounded behind us.”

    A kind of: sound · Kinds: androglossia

  2. The sound made by the vibration of vocal folds modified by the resonance of the vocal tract.

    “A singer takes good care of his voice.” “The giraffe cannot make any vocalizations.”

    Synonyms: vocalization, vocalisation, vocalism, phonation, vox · A kind of: communication · Kinds: singing voice, sprechgesang, voice over

  3. A sound suggestive of a vocal utterance.

    “The noisy voice of the waterfall.” “The incessant voices of the artillery.”

    A kind of: sound

  4. Expressing in coherent verbal form.

    “The articulation of my feelings.” “I gave voice to my feelings.”

    Synonyms: articulation · A kind of: expression

  5. A means or agency by which something is expressed or communicated.

    “The voice of the law.” “The Times is not the voice of New York.” “Conservatism has many voices.”

    A kind of: means

  6. Something suggestive of speech in being a medium of expression.

    “The wee small voice of conscience.” “The voice of experience.” “He said his voices told him to do it.”

    A kind of: communication

  7. (metonymy) a singer.

    “He wanted to hear trained voices sing it.”

    A kind of: singer

  8. An advocate who represents someone else's policy or purpose.

    “The meeting was attended by spokespersons for all the major organs of government.”

    Synonyms: spokesperson, interpreter, representative · A kind of: advocate · Kinds: ambassador, flak catcher, mouthpiece, spokesman, spokeswoman, traveling salesman

  9. The ability to speak.

    “He lost his voice.”

    A kind of: physical ability · Kinds: lung-power

  10. (linguistics) the grammatical relation (active or passive) of the grammatical subject of a verb to the action that the verb denotes.

    A kind of: grammatical relation · Kinds: active voice, passive voice

  11. The melody carried by a particular voice or instrument in polyphonic music.

    “He tried to sing the tenor part.”

    Synonyms: part · A kind of: tune · Kinds: primo, secondo, voice part, accompaniment, bass

Voice as a verb

  1. Give voice to.

    “He voiced his concern.”

    A kind of: express

  2. Utter with vibrating vocal chords.

    Synonyms: sound, vocalize, vocalise · Opposite: devoice · A kind of: pronounce · Kinds: chirk, quaver

Phrases and compounds with "voice"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • voice box (noun) A cartilaginous structure at the top of the trachea; contains elastic vocal cords that are the source of the vocal tone in speech.
  • bass voice (noun) The lowest adult male singing voice.
  • give voice (verb) Put into words or an expression.
  • head voice (noun) The higher ranges of the voice in speaking or singing; the vibrations of sung notes are felt in the head.
  • voice mail (noun) A computerized system for answering and routing telephone calls; telephone messages can be recorded and stored and relayed.
  • voice over (noun) The voice on an unseen commentator in a film of television program.
  • voice part (noun) A part written for a singer.
  • chest voice (noun) The lower ranges of the voice in speaking or singing.
  • small voice (noun) An inner voice that judges your behavior.
  • tenor voice (noun) The adult male singing voice above baritone.
  • active voice (noun) The voice used to indicate that the grammatical subject of the verb is performing the action or causing the happening denoted by the verb.
  • passive voice (noun) The voice used to indicate that the grammatical subject of the verb is the recipient (not the source) of the action denoted by the verb.
  • singing voice (noun) The musical quality of the voice while singing.
  • tone of voice (noun) The quality of a person's voice.
  • baritone voice (noun) The second lowest adult male singing voice.
  • in a low voice (adverb) In an undertone.
