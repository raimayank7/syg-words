Walk: meaning, synonyms and examples

Walk is used as a verb (10 senses) and noun (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Use one's feet to advance; advance by steps.

In Hindi
चलना, टहलना
Synonyms
take the air, walking, base on balls, pass, manner of walking, walkway, paseo, walk of life
Opposites
ride
Word family
walker, walking

Walk as a verb

  1. Use one's feet to advance; advance by steps.

    “Walk, don't run!” “We walked instead of driving.” “She walks with a slight limp.”

    Opposite: ride · A kind of: travel · Kinds: tramp down, lollop, tap, stumble, spacewalk, foot, toe, traipse

  2. Accompany or escort.

    “I'll walk you to your car.”

    A kind of: accompany · Kinds: trot, march

  3. Obtain a base on balls.

    A kind of: score

  4. Traverse or cover by walking.

    “Walk the tightrope.” “Paul walked the streets of Damascus.” “She walks 3 miles every day.”

    A kind of: traverse

  5. Give a base on balls to.

    A kind of: play

  6. Live or behave in a specified manner.

    “Walk in sadness.”

    A kind of: behave

  7. Be or act in association with.

    “We must walk with our dispossessed brothers and sisters.” “Walk with God.”

    A kind of: consociate

  8. Walk at a pace.

    “The horses walked across the meadow.”

    A kind of: pace

  9. Make walk.

    “He walks the horse up the mountain.” “Walk the dog twice a day.”

    A kind of: compel · Kinds: parade, march

  10. Take a walk; go for a walk; walk for pleasure.

    “The lovers held hands while walking.” “We like to walk every Sunday.”

    Synonyms: take the air · A kind of: travel · Kinds: constitutionalize

Walk as a noun

  1. The act of traveling by foot.

    “Walking is a healthy form of exercise.”

    Synonyms: walking · A kind of: locomotion · Kinds: ambulation, sleepwalking, gait, march, plodding, prowl, shamble, wading

  2. (baseball) an advance to first base by a batter who receives four balls.

    “He worked the pitcher for a base on balls.”

    Synonyms: base on balls, pass · A kind of: accomplishment

  3. Manner of walking.

    “He had a funny walk.”

    Synonyms: manner of walking · A kind of: carriage

  4. The act of walking somewhere.

    “He took a walk after lunch.”

    A kind of: travel · Kinds: amble, constitutional, foot, hike, last mile, moonwalk, perambulation, turn

  5. A path set aside for walking.

    “After the blizzard he shoveled the front walk.”

    Synonyms: walkway, paseo · A kind of: path · Kinds: ambulatory, boardwalk, catwalk, flagging, promenade, sidewalk, skywalk

  6. A slow gait of a horse in which two feet are always on the ground.

    A kind of: gait

  7. Careers in general.

    “It happens in all walks of life.”

    Synonyms: walk of life · A kind of: career

Phrases and compounds with "walk"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • walk in (verb) Enter by walking.
  • walk-in (noun) Person who walks in without having an appointment.
  • walk-on (noun) Plays a small part in a dramatic production.
  • walk-to (adjective) Close enough to be walked to.
  • walk-up (noun) An apartment in a building without an elevator.
  • walk off (verb) Take without permission.
  • walk out (verb) Stop work in order to press demands.
  • walk away (verb) Go away from.
  • walk over (verb) Beat easily.
  • angry walk (noun) A stiff or threatening gait.
  • space walk (noun) Any kind of physical activity outside a spacecraft by one of the crew.
  • walk about (verb) Walk with no particular goal.
  • random walk (noun) A stochastic process consisting of a sequence of changes each of whose characteristics (as magnitude or direction) is determined by chance.
  • street-walk (verb) Walk the streets in search of customers.
  • walk around (verb) Walk with no particular goal.
  • walk on air (verb) Feel extreme happiness or elation.
