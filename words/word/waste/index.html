Waste: meaning, synonyms and examples

Waste is used as a verb (9 senses), noun (5 senses) and adjective (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Spend thoughtlessly; throw away.

In Hindi
बरबाद करना; कचरा
Synonyms
blow, squander, run off, consume, ware, pine away, languish, emaciate, macerate, lay waste to
Opposites
conserve
Word family
waster, wastage, wasting

Waste as a verb

  1. Spend thoughtlessly; throw away.

    “He wasted his inheritance on his insincere friends.” “You squandered the opportunity to get and advanced degree.”

    Synonyms: blow, squander · Opposite: conserve · A kind of: use · Kinds: burn

  2. Use inefficiently or inappropriately.

    “Waste heat.” “Waste a joke on an unappreciative audience.”

    A kind of: use

  3. Get rid of.

    “We waste the dirty water by channeling it into the sewer.”

    A kind of: discard

  4. Run off as waste.

    “The water wastes back into the ocean.”

    Synonyms: run off · A kind of: run

  5. Spend extravagantly.

    “Waste not, want not.”

    Synonyms: consume, squander, ware · A kind of: spend · Kinds: fritter, luxuriate, lavish, overspend, splurge

  6. Lose vigor, health, or flesh, as through grief.

    “After her husband died, she just pined away.”

    Synonyms: pine away, languish · A kind of: weaken

  7. Cause to grow thin or weak.

    “The treatment emaciated him.”

    Synonyms: emaciate, macerate · A kind of: enfeeble

  8. Cause extensive destruction or ruin utterly.

    “The enemy lay waste to the countryside after the invasion.”

    Synonyms: lay waste to, devastate, desolate, ravage, scourge · A kind of: destroy · Kinds: ruin

  9. Become physically weaker.

    “Political prisoners are wasting away in many prisons all over the world.”

    Synonyms: rot · A kind of: devolve · Kinds: necrose

Waste as a noun

  1. Any materials unused and rejected as worthless or unwanted.

    “They collect the waste once a week.” “Much of the waste material is carried off in the sewers.”

    Synonyms: waste material, waste matter, waste product · A kind of: material · Kinds: impurity, exhaust, body waste, filth, sewage, effluent, garbage, pollutant

  2. Useless or profitless activity; using or expending or consuming thoughtlessly or carelessly.

    “If the effort brings no compensating gain it is a waste.” “Mindless dissipation of natural resources.”

    Synonyms: wastefulness, dissipation · A kind of: activity · Kinds: boondoggle, waste of effort, waste of material, waste of money, waste of time, extravagance, squandering

  3. The trait of wasting resources.

    “A life characterized by thriftlessness and waste.” “The wastefulness of missed opportunities.”

    Synonyms: thriftlessness, wastefulness · A kind of: improvidence

  4. An uninhabited wilderness that is worthless for cultivation.

    “The barrens of central Africa.” “The trackless wastes of the desert.”

    Synonyms: barren, wasteland · A kind of: wilderness · Kinds: heath

  5. (law) reduction in the value of an estate caused by act or neglect.

    Synonyms: permissive waste · A kind of: act

Waste as an adjective

  1. Located in a dismal or remote area; desolate.

    “A desert island.” “A godforsaken wilderness crossroads.” “A wild stretch of land.”

    Synonyms: godforsaken, wild · Similar: inhospitable

Phrases and compounds with "waste"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • body waste (noun) Waste matter (as urine or sweat but especially feces) discharged from the body.
  • food waste (noun) Food that is discarded (as from a kitchen).
  • waste pipe (noun) A pipe through which liquid is carried away.
  • waste-yard (noun) A piece of land where waste materials are dumped.
  • human waste (noun) The body wastes of human beings.
  • toxic waste (noun) Poisonous waste materials; can cause injury (especially by chemical means).
  • waste paper (noun) Paper discarded after use.
  • lay waste to (verb) Cause extensive destruction or ruin utterly.
  • laying waste (noun) Destruction achieved by causing something to be wrecked or ruined.
  • waste basket (noun) A container with an open top; for discarded paper and other rubbish.
  • waste matter (noun) Any materials unused and rejected as worthless or unwanted.
  • waste of time (noun) The devotion of time to a useless activity.
  • waste product (noun) Any materials unused and rejected as worthless or unwanted.
  • waste material (noun) Any materials unused and rejected as worthless or unwanted.
  • waste of money (noun) Money spent for inadequate return.
  • waste of effort (noun) A useless effort.
