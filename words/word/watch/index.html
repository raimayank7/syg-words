Watch: meaning, synonyms and examples

Watch is used as a verb (7 senses) and noun (6 senses). Its most common meaning is: Look attentively.

In Hindi
देखना; घड़ी
Synonyms
observe, follow, watch over, keep an eye on, view, see, catch, take in, look on, look out
Word family
watcher, watching

Watch as a verb

  1. Look attentively.

    “Watch a basketball game.”

    Kinds: witness, rubberneck, watch, observe

  2. Follow with the eyes or the mind.

    “Keep an eye on the baby, please!” “The world is watching Sarajevo.” “She followed the men with the binoculars.”

    Synonyms: observe, follow, watch over, keep an eye on · A kind of: check · Kinds: keep tabs on, guard, invigilate

  3. See or watch.

    “View a show on television.” “This program will be seen all over the world.” “View an exhibition.”

    Synonyms: view, see, catch, take in · A kind of: watch · Kinds: visualize, spectate, preview

  4. Observe with attention.

    Synonyms: look on · Kinds: sit by

  5. Be vigilant, be on the lookout or be careful.

    “Watch out for pickpockets!”

    Synonyms: look out, watch out · Kinds: beware, keep one's eyes peeled, look after

  6. Observe or determine by looking.

    “Watch how the dog chases the cats away.”

  7. Find out, learn, or determine with certainty, usually by making an inquiry or other effort.

    “I want to see whether she speaks French.” “See whether it works.” “Find out if he speaks Russian.”

    Synonyms: determine, check, find out, see, ascertain, learn · Kinds: test

Watch as a noun

  1. A small portable timepiece.

    Synonyms: ticker · A kind of: timepiece · Kinds: analog watch, digital watch, hunter, pendulum watch, pocket watch, stem-winder, wristwatch

  2. A period of time (4 or 2 hours) during which some of a ship's crew are on duty.

    A kind of: shift · Kinds: dogwatch, graveyard watch

  3. A purposeful surveillance to guard or observe.

    Synonyms: vigil · A kind of: surveillance · Kinds: listening watch, spying

  4. The period during which someone (especially a guard) is on duty.

    A kind of: time period

  5. A person employed to keep watch for some anticipated event.

    Synonyms: lookout, lookout man, sentinel, sentry, spotter, scout, picket · A kind of: watchman

  6. The rite of staying awake for devotional purposes (especially on the eve of a religious festival).

    Synonyms: vigil · A kind of: rite · Kinds: agrypnia

Phrases and compounds with "watch"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • day watch (noun) Workers who work during the day (as 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.).
  • watch cap (noun) A knitted dark blue wool cap worn by seamen in cold or stormy weather.
  • watch key (noun) Winder consisting of a key with a square hole; used for winding some watches.
  • watch out (verb) Be vigilant, be on the lookout or be careful.
  • stop watch (noun) A timepiece that can be started or stopped for exact timing (as of a race).
  • watch case (noun) The metal case in which the works of a watch are housed.
  • watch fire (noun) A fire lighted at night as a signal.
  • watch over (verb) Follow with the eyes or the mind.
  • night watch (noun) A watch during the night (as from midnight to 8 a.m.).
  • stand watch (verb) Watch over so as to protect.
  • watch chain (noun) Short chain or ribbon attaching a pocket watch to a man's vest.
  • watch glass (noun) Laboratory glassware; a shallow glass dish used as an evaporating surface or to cover a beaker.
  • watch guard (noun) Short chain or ribbon attaching a pocket watch to a man's vest.
  • watch night (noun) A devotional service (especially on New Year's Eve).
  • wrist watch (noun) A watch that is worn strapped to the wrist.
  • analog watch (noun) A watch that represents time by the position of hands on a dial.
