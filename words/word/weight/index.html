Weight: meaning, synonyms and examples

Weight is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (2 senses). Its most common meaning is: The vertical force exerted by a mass as a result of gravity.

In Hindi
वज़न, भार
Synonyms
free weight, exercising weight, weightiness, system of weights, weight unit, weighting, burden, burthen, weight down, slant
Word family
weighty

Weight as a noun

  1. The vertical force exerted by a mass as a result of gravity.

    A kind of: physical property · Kinds: body weight, dead weight, heaviness, poundage, tare, throw-weight, lightness

  2. Sports equipment used in calisthenic exercises and weightlifting; it is not attached to anything and is raised and lowered by use of the hands and arms.

    Synonyms: free weight, exercising weight · A kind of: sports equipment · Kinds: barbell, dumbbell

  3. The relative importance granted to something.

    “His opinion carries great weight.” “The progression implied an increasing weightiness of the items listed.”

    Synonyms: weightiness · A kind of: importance

  4. An artifact that is heavy.

    A kind of: artifact · Kinds: bob, counterweight, load, makeweight, paperweight, sinker

  5. An oppressive feeling of heavy force.

    “Bowed down by the weight of responsibility.”

    A kind of: oppression

  6. A system of units used to express the weight of something.

    Synonyms: system of weights · A kind of: system of measurement · Kinds: avoirdupois, troy

  7. A unit used to measure weight.

    “He placed two weights in the scale pan.”

    Synonyms: weight unit · A kind of: unit of measurement · Kinds: troy unit, apothecaries' unit, arroba, catty, crith, frail, last, maund

  8. (statistics) a coefficient assigned to elements of a frequency distribution in order to represent their relative importance.

    Synonyms: weighting · A kind of: coefficient

Weight as a verb

  1. Weight down with a load.

    Synonyms: burden, burthen, weight down · A kind of: charge · Kinds: overburden, plumb, saddle

  2. Present with a bias.

    “He biased his presentation so as to please the share holders.”

    Synonyms: slant, angle · A kind of: bias

Phrases and compounds with "weight"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • body weight (noun) The weight of a person's body.
  • dead weight (noun) An oppressive encumbrance.
  • free weight (noun) Sports equipment used in calisthenic exercises and weightlifting; it is not attached to anything and is raised and lowered by use of the hands and arms.
  • lose weight (verb) Take off weight.
  • sash weight (noun) A counterweight for a sliding sash.
  • troy weight (noun) A system of weights used for precious metals and gemstones; based on a 12-ounce pound and an ounce of 480 grains.
  • weight down (verb) Weight down with a load.
  • weight unit (noun) A decimal unit of weight based on the gram.
  • weight-lift (verb) Lift weights.
  • carry weight (verb) Have influence to a specified degree.
  • throw-weight (noun) The weight of the payload of a missile (not including the weight of the rocket).
  • atomic weight (noun) (chemistry) the mass of an atom of a chemical element expressed in atomic mass units.
  • weight gainer (noun) A person who gains weight.
  • weight gaining (noun) Bodybuilding that increases muscle mass and body weight.
  • combining weight (noun) The atomic weight of an element that has the same combining capacity as a given weight of another element; the standard is 8 for oxygen.
  • molecular weight (noun) (chemistry) the sum of the relative atomic masses of the constituent atoms of a molecule.
