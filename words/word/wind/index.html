Wind: meaning, synonyms and examples

Wind is used as a noun (8 senses) and verb (7 senses). Its most common meaning is: Air moving (sometimes with considerable force) from an area of high pressure to an area of low pressure.

In Hindi
हवा; लपेटना
Synonyms
air current, current of air, malarkey, malarky, idle words, jazz, nothingness, tip, lead, steer
Opposites
unwind
Word family
windy, winder

Wind as a noun

  1. Air moving (sometimes with considerable force) from an area of high pressure to an area of low pressure.

    “Trees bent under the fierce winds.” “When there is no wind, row.” “The radioactivity was being swept upwards by the air current and out into the atmosphere.”

    Synonyms: air current, current of air · A kind of: weather · Kinds: airstream, calm air, breeze, chinook, harmattan, crosswind, fohn, khamsin

  2. A tendency or force that influences events.

    “The winds of change.”

    A kind of: influence

  3. Breath.

    “The collision knocked the wind out of him.”

    A kind of: exhalation

  4. Empty rhetoric or insincere or exaggerated talk.

    “That's a lot of wind.” “Don't give me any of that jazz.”

    Synonyms: malarkey, malarky, idle words, jazz, nothingness · A kind of: talk

  5. An indication of potential opportunity.

    “He got a tip on the stock market.” “A good lead for a job.”

    Synonyms: tip, lead, steer, confidential information, hint · A kind of: guidance

  6. A musical instrument in which the sound is produced by an enclosed column of air that is moved by the breath.

    Synonyms: wind instrument · A kind of: musical instrument · Kinds: brass, free-reed instrument, kazoo, ocarina, organ, organ pipe, pipe, post horn

  7. A reflex that expels intestinal gas through the anus.

    Synonyms: fart, farting, flatus, breaking wind · A kind of: reflex

  8. The act of winding or twisting.

    “He put the key in the old clock and gave it a good wind.”

    Synonyms: winding, twist · A kind of: rotation

Wind as a verb

  1. To move or cause to move in a sinuous, spiral, or circular course.

    “The river winds through the hills.” “The path meanders through the vineyards.” “Sometimes, the gout wanders through the entire body.”

    Synonyms: weave, thread, meander, wander · A kind of: travel · Kinds: snake

  2. Extend in curves and turns.

    “The road winds around the lake.” “The path twisted through the forest.”

    Synonyms: twist, curve · A kind of: be · Kinds: circumvolute, spiral, snake

  3. Arrange or or coil around.

    “Roll your hair around your finger.” “Twine the thread around the spool.” “She wrapped her arms around the child.”

    Synonyms: wrap, roll, twine · Opposite: unwind · A kind of: move · Kinds: spool, reel, ball, clue, coil

  4. Catch the scent of; get wind of.

    Synonyms: scent, nose · A kind of: smell

  5. Coil the spring of (some mechanical device) by turning a stem.

    “Wind your watch.”

    Synonyms: wind up · A kind of: tighten

  6. Form into a wreath.

    Synonyms: wreathe · A kind of: intertwine

  7. Raise or haul up with or as if with mechanical help.

    “Hoist the bicycle onto the roof of the car.”

    Synonyms: hoist, lift · A kind of: raise · Kinds: trice

Phrases and compounds with "wind"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • wind up (verb) Finally be or do something.
  • get wind (verb) Get to know or become aware of, usually accidentally.
  • wind gap (noun) A pass in a mountain ridge with no stream flowing through it.
  • wind off (verb) Reverse the winding or twisting of.
  • wind tee (noun) Weather vane shaped like a T and located at an airfield.
  • east wind (noun) A wind from the east.
  • high wind (noun) A very strong wind.
  • west wind (noun) Wind that blows from west to east.
  • wind bell (noun) A decorative arrangement of pieces of metal or glass or pottery that hang together loosely so the wind can cause them to tinkle.
  • wind cone (noun) A truncated cloth cone mounted on a mast; used (e.g., at airports) to show the direction of the wind.
  • wind farm (noun) A power plant that uses wind turbines to generate electricity.
  • wind gage (noun) A gauge for recording the speed and direction of wind.
  • wind harp (noun) A harp having strings tuned in unison; they sound when wind passes over them.
  • wind park (noun) A power plant that uses wind turbines to generate electricity.
  • wind rose (noun) Weather map showing the frequency and strength of winds from different directions.
  • wind sock (noun) A truncated cloth cone mounted on a mast; used (e.g., at airports) to show the direction of the wind.
