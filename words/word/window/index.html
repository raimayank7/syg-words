Window: meaning, synonyms and examples

Window is used as a noun (8 senses). Its most common meaning is: A framework of wood or metal that contains a glass windowpane and is built into a wall or roof to admit light or air.

In Hindi
खिड़की
Synonyms
windowpane

Window as a noun

  1. A framework of wood or metal that contains a glass windowpane and is built into a wall or roof to admit light or air.

    A kind of: framework · Kinds: bay window, casement window, clerestory, display window, dormer, dormer window, double glazing, double-hung window

  2. A transparent opening in a vehicle that allow vision out of the sides or back; usually is capable of being opened.

    A kind of: opening · Kinds: car window

  3. A transparent panel (as of an envelope) inserted in an otherwise opaque material.

    A kind of: panel

  4. An opening that resembles a window in appearance or function.

    “He could see them through a window in the trees.”

    A kind of: opening

  5. The time period that is considered best for starting or finishing something.

    “The expanded window will give us time to catch the thieves.” “They had a window of less than an hour when an attack would have succeeded.”

    A kind of: time period

  6. A pane of glass in a window.

    “The ball shattered the window.”

    Synonyms: windowpane · A kind of: pane

  7. An opening in a wall or screen that admits light and air and through which customers can be served.

    “He stuck his head in the window.”

    A kind of: opening · Kinds: ticket window

  8. (computer science) a rectangular part of a computer screen that contains a display different from the rest of the screen.

    A kind of: display · Kinds: dialog box, foreground

Phrases and compounds with "window"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • bay window (noun) Slang for a paunch.
  • bow window (noun) A window that sticks out from the outside wall of a house.
  • car window (noun) A window in a car.
  • window box (noun) A long narrow box for growing plants on a windowsill.
  • oval window (noun) Fenestra that has the base of the stapes attached to it.
  • rear window (noun) Car window that allows vision out of the back of the car.
  • rose window (noun) Circular window filled with tracery.
  • sash window (noun) A window with (usually two) sashes that slide vertically to let in air.
  • shop window (noun) A window of a store facing onto the street; used to display merchandise for sale in the store.
  • show window (noun) A setting in which something can be displayed to best effect.
  • window lock (noun) A lock attached to the sashes of a double hung window that can fix both in the shut position.
  • window pane (noun) Street name for lysergic acid diethylamide.
  • window sash (noun) A framework that holds the panes of a window in the window frame.
  • window seat (noun) A bench or similar seat built into a window recess.
  • window-shop (verb) Examine the shop windows; shop with the eyes only.
  • oriel window (noun) A projecting bay window corbeled or cantilevered out from a wall.
