Withdraw: meaning, synonyms and examples

Withdraw is used as a verb (12 senses). Its most common meaning is: Pull back or move away or backward.

In Hindi
वापस लेना, निकालना
Synonyms
retreat, pull away, draw back, recede, pull back, retire, move back, disengage, recall, call in
Opposites
deposit
Word family
withdrawal, withdrawer

Withdraw as a verb

  1. Pull back or move away or backward.

    “The enemy withdrew.” “The limo pulled away from the curb.”

    Synonyms: retreat, pull away, draw back, recede, pull back, retire, move back · A kind of: travel · Kinds: fall back, retrograde, back up

  2. Withdraw from active participation.

    “He retired from chess.”

    Synonyms: retire · A kind of: discontinue · Kinds: drop out

  3. Release from something that holds fast, connects, or entangles.

    “I want to disengage myself from his influence.” “Disengage the gears.”

    Synonyms: disengage · A kind of: let go of · Kinds: unlock

  4. Cause to be returned.

    “Recall the defective auto tires.” “The manufacturer tried to call back the spoilt yoghurt.”

    Synonyms: recall, call in, call back · A kind of: take · Kinds: decommission

  5. Take back what one has said.

    “He swallowed his words.”

    Synonyms: swallow, take back, unsay · A kind of: disown

  6. Keep away from others.

    “He sequestered himself in his study to write a book.”

    Synonyms: seclude, sequester, sequestrate · A kind of: isolate

  7. Break from a meeting or gathering.

    “We adjourned for lunch.” “The men retired to the library.”

    Synonyms: adjourn, retire · A kind of: close up · Kinds: prorogue

  8. Retire gracefully.

    “He bowed out when he realized he could no longer handle the demands of the chairmanship.”

    Synonyms: bow out · A kind of: retire

  9. Remove (a commodity) from (a supply source).

    “She drew $2,000 from the account.” “The doctors drew medical supplies from the hospital's emergency bank.”

    Synonyms: draw, take out, draw off · Opposite: deposit · A kind of: remove · Kinds: cheque, dip, divert, overdraw, tap, divest

  10. Lose interest.

    “He retired from life when his wife died.”

    Synonyms: retire · A kind of: tire · Kinds: chicken out

  11. Make a retreat from an earlier commitment or activity.

    “We'll have to crawfish out from meeting with him.” “He backed out of his earlier promise.” “The aggressive investment company pulled in its horns.”

    Synonyms: retreat, pull back, back out, back away, crawfish, crawfish out, pull in one's horns

  12. Remove something concrete, as by lifting, pushing, or taking off, or remove something abstract.

    “Remove a threat.” “Remove a wrapper.” “Remove the dirty dishes from the table.”

    Synonyms: remove, take, take away · Kinds: epilate, harvest, tip, stem, extirpate, enucleate, exenterate, enucleate
