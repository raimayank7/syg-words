Witness: meaning, synonyms and examples

Witness is used as a verb (2 senses) and noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: Be a witness to.

In Hindi
गवाह
Synonyms
find, see, witnesser, informant, spectator, viewer, watcher, looker, attestant, attestor
Word family
witnesser

Witness as a verb

  1. Be a witness to.

    “She witnessed the accident and had to testify in court.”

    A kind of: watch · Kinds: eyewitness

  2. Perceive or be contemporaneous with.

    “We found Republicans winning the offices.” “You'll see a lot of cheating in this school.” “The 1960's saw the rebellion of the younger generation against established traditions.”

    Synonyms: find, see · A kind of: experience · Kinds: catch

Witness as a noun

  1. Someone who sees an event and reports what happened.

    Synonyms: witnesser, informant · A kind of: perceiver, speaker · Kinds: attester, testifier

  2. A close observer; someone who looks at something (such as an exhibition of some kind).

    “The spectators applauded the performance.” “Television viewers.” “Sky watchers discovered a new star.”

    Synonyms: spectator, viewer, watcher, looker · A kind of: perceiver · Kinds: browser, bystander, cheerer, eyewitness, gawker, moviegoer, ogler, onlooker

  3. Testimony by word or deed to your religious faith.

    A kind of: testimony

  4. (law) a person who attests to the genuineness of a document or signature by adding their own signature.

    Synonyms: attestant, attestor, attestator · A kind of: signer

  5. (law) a person who testifies under oath in a court of law.

    A kind of: person · Kinds: adverse witness, character witness, expert witness, lay witness, material witness

Phrases and compounds with "witness"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • lay witness (noun) Any witness who does not testify as an expert witness.
  • witness box (noun) A box enclosure for a witness when testifying.
  • bear witness (verb) Provide evidence for.
  • false witness (noun) A person who deliberately gives false testimony.
  • witness stand (noun) A box enclosure for a witness when testifying.
  • expert witness (noun) A witness who has knowledge not normally possessed by the average person concerning the topic that he is to testify about.
  • adverse witness (noun) A witness whose relationship to the opposing party is such that his or her testimony may be prejudiced against the opposing party.
  • hostile witness (noun) A witness whose relationship to the opposing party is such that his or her testimony may be prejudiced against the opposing party.
  • material witness (noun) A witness whose testimony is both relevant to the matter at issue and required in order to resolve the matter.
  • character witness (noun) A witness who testifies under oath as to the good reputation of another person in the community where that person lives.
  • jehovah's witness (noun) Believer in imminent approach of the millennium; practitioner of active evangelism.
  • bearing false witness (noun) Criminal offense of making false statements under oath.
