Word: meaning, synonyms and examples

Word is used as a noun (9 senses) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: A unit of language that native speakers can identify.

In Hindi
शब्द
Synonyms
news, intelligence, tidings, discussion, give-and-take, parole, word of honor, password, watchword, countersign
Word family
wording

Word as a noun

  1. A unit of language that native speakers can identify.

    “Words are the blocks from which sentences are made.” “He hardly said ten words all morning.”

    A kind of: language unit · Kinds: anagram, anaphor, antonym, back-formation, charade, cognate, content word, contraction

  2. A brief statement.

    “He didn't say a word about it.”

    A kind of: statement

  3. Information about recent and important events.

    “They awaited news of the outcome.”

    Synonyms: news, intelligence, tidings · A kind of: information · Kinds: good word, latest, update

  4. A verbal command for action.

    “When I give the word, charge!”

    A kind of: order

  5. An exchange of views on some topic.

    “We had a good discussion.” “We had a word or two about it.”

    Synonyms: discussion, give-and-take · A kind of: speech · Kinds: argument, deliberation, conference, panel discussion, postmortem, public discussion, negotiation

  6. A promise.

    “He gave his word.”

    Synonyms: parole, word of honor · A kind of: promise

  7. A word is a string of bits stored in computer memory.

    “Large computers use words up to 64 bits long.”

    A kind of: computer memory unit

  8. The divine word of God; the second person in the Trinity (incarnate in Jesus).

  9. A secret word or phrase known only to a restricted group.

    “He forgot the password.”

    Synonyms: password, watchword, parole, countersign · A kind of: positive identification, secret

Word as a verb

  1. Put into words or an expression.

    “He formulated his concerns to the board of trustees.”

    Synonyms: give voice, formulate, phrase, articulate · A kind of: express · Kinds: ask, lexicalize, dogmatize, formularize, frame

Phrases and compounds with "word"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • get word (verb) Get to know or become aware of, usually accidentally.
  • key word (noun) A word that is used as a pattern to decode an encrypted message.
  • good word (noun) Something that recommends (or expresses commendation of) a person or thing as worthy or desirable.
  • head word (noun) A content word that can be qualified by a modifier.
  • last word (noun) The final statement in a verbal argument.
  • root word (noun) (linguistics) the form of a word after all affixes are removed.
  • send word (verb) Inform (somebody) of something.
  • word form (noun) The phonological or orthographic sound or appearance of a word that can be used to describe or identify something.
  • word game (noun) Any game involving the formation or alteration or discovery of words.
  • word play (noun) Playing on words or speech sounds.
  • entry word (noun) The form of a word that heads a lexical entry and is alphabetized in a dictionary.
  • ghost word (noun) A word form that has entered the language through the perpetuation of an error.
  • guide word (noun) A word printed at the top of the page of a dictionary or other reference book to indicate the first or last item on that page.
  • nonce word (noun) A word with a special meaning used for a special occasion.
  • smear word (noun) An epithet that can be used to smear someone's reputation.
  • word class (noun) One of the traditional categories of words intended to reflect their functions in a grammatical context.
