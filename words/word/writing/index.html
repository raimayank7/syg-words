Writing: meaning, synonyms and examples

Writing is used as a noun (5 senses). Its most common meaning is: The act of creating written works.

In Hindi
लेखन, लिखावट
Synonyms
authorship, composition, penning, written material, piece of writing, committal to writing
Word family
write

Writing as a noun

  1. The act of creating written works.

    “Writing was a form of therapy for him.” “It was a matter of disputed authorship.”

    Synonyms: authorship, composition, penning · A kind of: verbal creation · Kinds: adoxography, drafting, dramatization, fabrication, historiography, metrification, novelization, redaction

  2. The work of a writer; anything expressed in letters of the alphabet (especially when considered from the point of view of style and effect).

    “The writing in her novels is excellent.” “That editorial was a fine piece of writing.”

    Synonyms: written material, piece of writing · A kind of: written communication · Kinds: bowdlerization, title, cryptogram, rewrite, literary composition, literature, literature, matter

  3. (usually plural) the collected work of an author.

    “The idea occurs with increasing frequency in Hemingway's writings.”

    A kind of: written communication, oeuvre · Kinds: patristics

  4. Letters or symbols that are written or imprinted on a surface to represent the sounds or words of a language.

    “He turned the paper over so the writing wouldn't show.” “The doctor's writing was illegible.”

    A kind of: written communication · Kinds: orthography, coding system, capitalization, typing, printing, handwriting, hieroglyph, skywriting

  5. The activity of putting something in written form.

    “She did the thinking while he did the writing.”

    Synonyms: committal to writing · A kind of: activity · Kinds: cryptography, handwriting, inscription, notation

Phrases and compounds with "writing"

These combinations have meanings of their own, which often cannot be guessed from the single word.

  • in writing (adverb) As written or printed.
  • writing arm (noun) An arm of a tablet-armed chair; widened to provide a writing surface.
  • writing ink (noun) Any permanent or washable ink used with pens.
  • writing pad (noun) A pad of paper on which messages can be written.
  • writing desk (noun) A portable case containing writing materials and having a writing surface.
  • writing board (noun) Work surface consisting of a wide lightweight board that can be placed across the lap and used for writing.
  • writing paper (noun) Paper material made into thin sheets that are sized to take ink; used for writing correspondence and manuscripts.
  • writing style (noun) A style of expressing yourself in writing.
  • writing table (noun) A desk used for writing.
  • sacred writing (noun) Writing that is venerated for the worship of a deity.
  • secret writing (noun) A piece of writing in code or cipher.
  • writing system (noun) A method of representing the sounds of a language by written or printed symbols.
  • picture writing (noun) A writing system using pictographs.
  • piece of writing (noun) The work of a writer; anything expressed in letters of the alphabet (especially when considered from the point of view of style and effect).
  • religious writing (noun) Writing that is venerated for the worship of a deity.
  • writing implement (noun) An implement that is used to write.
