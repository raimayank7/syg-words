Yellow: meaning, synonyms and examples

Yellow is used as an adjective (6 senses), noun (1 sense) and verb (1 sense). Its most common meaning is: Of the color intermediate between green and orange in the color spectrum; of something resembling the color of an egg yolk.

Yellow as an adjective

  1. Of the color intermediate between green and orange in the color spectrum; of something resembling the color of an egg yolk.

    Synonyms: yellowish, xanthous · Similar: chromatic

  2. Easily frightened.

    Synonyms: chicken, chickenhearted, lily-livered, white-livered, yellow-bellied · Similar: cowardly

  3. Changed to a yellowish color by age.

    “Yellowed parchment.”

    Synonyms: yellowed · Similar: old

  4. Typical of tabloids.

    “Sensational journalistic reportage of the scandal.” “Yellow press.”

    Synonyms: scandalmongering, sensationalistic · Similar: sensational

  5. Cowardly or treacherous.

    “The little yellow stain of treason.” “Too yellow to stand and fight.”

    Similar: dishonorable

  6. Affected by jaundice which causes yellowing of skin etc.

    Synonyms: jaundiced, icteric · Similar: unhealthy

Yellow as a noun

  1. Yellow color or pigment; the chromatic color resembling the hue of sunflowers or ripe lemons.

    Synonyms: yellowness · A kind of: chromatic color · Kinds: canary yellow, amber, brownish yellow, gamboge, old gold, orange yellow, pale yellow, greenish yellow

Yellow as a verb

  1. Turn yellow.

    “The pages of the book began to yellow.”

    A kind of: discolor
