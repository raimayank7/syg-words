Word pages, A to Z

These are the 1000 English words that have a full page on this site. They were chosen in a simple way: we took the words used most often in the body of text that comes with WordNet, and kept those whose entries are rich enough to fill a page with real content, that is, several senses with examples, a set of synonyms, and usually phrases and a word family. Small grammatical words such as "the", "of" and "and" are not here, because a dictionary has little to say about them.

Each page shows every common sense of the word, grouped by part of speech, with a definition, example sentences, synonyms and opposites for that sense, broader and narrower words, phrases and compounds built on the word, and its word family. 1000 of the pages also give the meaning in Hindi. A word that is not listed here can still be looked up in the word finder, which covers about 16,513 words.

Why common words deserve study

It is tempting to chase rare words. But the words that cause real trouble are the common ones, because they have so many meanings. "Set", "run", "take" and "get" each have dozens of senses and appear in hundreds of phrases. A learner who truly knows two thousand common words, in all their senses, reads and writes better than one who half knows ten thousand. Start with the words you use every day and read their pages to the end; you will be surprised at how much you did not know about them.
